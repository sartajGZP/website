Now following is the Right to Recall law-draft I had proposed in 2002, and copies were give to 1000s of IAC/AAP workers since apr-2011 including AK-420 and Anna-840 (see section-40.2 of tinyurl . com / rahulmehta1 for similar RTR-MP, RTR-MLA etc procedures)

1. Say corporator elections have ended. Say there are 100,000 voters in a ward. Say corporator who got elected got 30000 votes.

2. Now after election ends, any citizen of India above age of 21 years can register himself as candidate for deposit of say Rs 10000 in at a corporation ward

3. Any voter can via SMS from his registered mobile or by personally visiting the Patwari , Talati, Village Officer office, can approve 5 or less candidates for Rs 3 fee. The approval will be open, and will come on collector's website with voters' name. And citizen will get SMS feedback for his approval. And citizen will also get recipet for his approval. (*)

4. The voter can cancel/change approvals anyday

5. Now say winner corporator had got 30000 votes. Then if any of the other candidates gets approval = votes corporators got + 10% of TOTAL = 30000 + 10% of 100,000 = 40000 voters, AND the approvals are 10000 more than existing corporator, then there will be recall-elections.

6. In recall election, the existing corporator will be candidate no.1 without any form filling and deposit. And there will be ONLY 8 more candidates --- the 8 persons who have highest approvals. Each must give deposit of Rs 10000 .

7. In recall election,the voting will be confidential.

8. The candidate must get over votes existing corporator got + 10% of TOTAL = over 40000 votes (in this example). Otherwise, existing corporator will stay

9. After recall elections, whther they are successful or failure the approval counts of all candidates will be set to zero.
