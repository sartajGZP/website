AK-420 and paid-media may make gas price issue bigger than Janlokpal. I request nationalists activists to prepare with proper counter-arguments

In apr-2011, I had requested all nationalist activists to expose Anna-840 and AK-420, and expose their defunct "Janlokpal with NO RTR-Janlokpal clauses" draft . But BJP-apex had ordered RSS-apex to order RSS-workers to support Janlokpal movement and so nationalists went ahead and supported Janlokpal, Anna-840, AK-420 etc . Their support is one reason why AK-420 is now 100 times bigger. Then, in sep-2012 , just a month after AAP's formation was announced and it was not even officially formed, I had asked all nationalists to expose AAP by focusing on its structural flaws and AAP's pro-Bangladeshi agenda. But they all just pooh-poohed AAP saying that AAP will get not even 5% votes.

Now AK-420 is making serious preparations on exposing nefarious nexuses BJP-leaders and Congress-leaders have with Mukeshbhai Ambani and Adani. And nationalists are still snoring loud. In fact, the sound of their sooring is louder that noise of AK-420 And please make no mistakes, the nefarious nexus exists at many fronts. eg Many BJP-leaders and Congress-leaders have rented their cars as private taxi to Adani and Ambani, and they make a handsome 30% profit a year on their investment. It is 100% legal. But that does a prove a nefarious nexus between leaders and Adani/Ambani. Many Congress-leaders and BJP-leaders have their relatives making big bucks in Adani and Reliance in executive positions or apex contractors. Again, it is 100% legal. But it further proves nefarious nexus. Congress has lost all middle class and lower middle class anti-corruption votes anyway. AK-420's campaign can ensure that most of these votes will land with AAP and not with BJP.

AK's goal is NOT to reduce gas price. Which is why AK opposed the law-drafts which can improve both private campanies and PSUs in oil-gas business. AK's goal is to cut BJP-votes only and create way for Rockefeller to enter into India and devot all oil wells. But paid-media will project AK-420 as AK-108.

The crux of the question is --- did BJP-leaders and Congress-leaders help Mukeshbhai, ONGC , GAIL etc in ensuring that new gas exploration companies and gas mining companies do not come, and so that prices remain high? Sadly, answer is yes. Well, in Ahmedabad, I have seen BJP-leaders and Congress-leaders of rank and file colluding in legal as well as illegal ways with big land owners in a way that land prices would remain artificially high. When it comes to land business, BJP-leaders in Ahmedabad are only 10% less bad than Congress-leaders. So it is FACT that across India, the BJP-leaders and Congress-leaders are made a huge mess in land business and looted people left, right and center by keeping prices artificially high. So I believe that BJP-leaders have colluded with Reliance in ensuring that more private players dont come up in gas mining business to reduce gas prices

So will AK-420 be able to tarnish NaMo image? He has gathered tons of information. Now it all depends on whether Rockefeller orders paid-Samir-Jain of paid-ToI to print information that AK-420 has. If BJP-leaders' agree to give 350 Loksabha tickets to Rockefeller's agents, then Rockefeller will not ask paid-Samir-Jain to print all this info. Otherwise, all this info about deals between BJP-leaders and Ambani/Andani, with name, place, dates, documents etc will hit front pages and within weeks , crores of votes will shift from BJP to AAP.

Further, now Facebook = CIA owns WhatApp. WhatsApp doesnt send advt. But now WhatApp may start sending advt and can send AK vidoes as advt. Video download on WhatApp is effectively free as most employees download video from WiFi at their offices. So CIA will be able to send AK video to crores of middle class voters across India.

A thunder is coming. But snores of nationalist activists is louder than thunder.

I suggest following method to deal with AK-420 ---- the nationalists should inform voters about structural flaws of "Janlokpal with NO RTR Janlokpal clauses" proposals and structural flaws in AAP abd AAP's pro-Bangladeshi agenda. The nationalists should call paid-ToI as MNC-paid-media and IMO nationalists should NOT call AK as CIA-paid agent because CIA has not paid AK a penny in past 5 years. The ONLY money that AK git from CIA-owned Ford Foundation was some Rs 4 crore, and one cant run campaign from mere Rs 4 crore. So please focus on the truth only and please dont waste time on nonsense given by Minaxi Lekhi and others.

======

Also, pls note that RSS-apex and BJP-apex had helped AK-420 in rising. If was BJP-apex who had ordered RSS-apex to order RSS-workers to attend Anna-840's rallies in apr-2011 and create a crowd so that Anna-840 becomes more popular in people. because of this decision, AK-420 became popular in apr-2011 and later. And now AK-420 is damaging whole nation.

Why did BJP-apex help AK-420 in rising? Because no BJP-leader was able to create anti-corruption movement and tap anti-corruption votes. Just a few months before apr-2011, LKA had taken anti-corruption Rath Yatra across India, and it was a super flop. So BJP-leaders decided to support Ford Foundation's fake anti-corruption movement and "Janlokpal with NO RTR-Janlokpal clauses". And thats where MNC-owners got opportunity to plant AK-420 as a tall leader.

As end note, it is time we nationalists relieve VHP-apex, RSS-apex, BajrangDal-apex and BJP-apex from the tasks of Tridevalaya, expelling Bangladeshi, saving Hindu temples, saving Hinduism, reducing corruption in Supreme courts and other tasks. [b]We must snatch the agenda of Tridevalaya away from VHP-leaders[/b]. Then who should we give it? The activists should DIRECTLY work on this with citizens, and bypass all tall leaders. The ONLY thing I hear tall leaders do for past 25 years is "wait wait wait, wait for timing, wait for next election, wait till we get 360 seats in Loksabha, wait till we get 190 seats in Rajyasabha etc etc". well, no more waiting is needed.
