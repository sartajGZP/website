Reducing corruption in agriculture to non-agriculture (NA) conversion

In order to do construction on a piece of land, several clearances need to be obtained. One is conversion to "non-agricultural" land, if land is not already non-agricultural. This clearance is full of corruption across India. And also, decision to expand city limit is mired with corruption. It is often the case that Ministers, IAS, IPS , judges etc will purchase or get power of attorney over huge plots outside city limit just before city is to be expanded, and then they expand the city limits and seel same land at 10 times the price. The prices becomes burden on the second buyer and loss for the existing buyer. And it also creates speculation

I propose following law-draft to reduce corruption in NA-conversion.

1. All private plots under existing municipal limits should be given NA and taxed at NA rates

2. Increase area of every existing urban are by 20%, and make every plot inside the area as non agricultural

3. And then increase the area of each urban center at rate of 1% a year, OR increase in its population, whichever is higher.

4. Now how to take away discretionary power of officers\Ministers in decide which areas around city should be made non-agricultural and which areas should be kept agricultural? Use step-5 to step-8

5. Using voter list of dec-2013, take the persons who are living in urban area. And calculate center of city based on their addresses.

6. Make a square aligned with North-South-East-West around that center equal to existing area of city

7. Do not remove any area outside square.

8. Now say if city area has to be increased by x%, then add areas not in city but in square so that new city is closer to becoming a square. The new area added should be of size 200 meters * 200 meters , and should be started from North-West-most available block going Eastward and Southward. Add exactly as many blocks as needed so that area increases by x%. The last block will be odd size.

9. If there is any river, then city may or need not skip the block and take next available block

10. All blocks added in the city will have NA permission without asking
