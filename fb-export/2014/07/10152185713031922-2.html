Hinduvaad for BJP is same as what is "garbi hato" for Congress.
.
Since 1998 , I have been asking ALL activists to FIRST focus on Right to Recall, because in absence of RTR, all promises become jokes !! In RTR, a leader knows he can be expelled in 15 days and so will give ONLY promises he intends to keep. In absence of RTR, what we will see is just "vachaneshu kim daridrata" i.e. "why be poor while making promises" i.e. irresponsible promise-making.
.
Absence of RTR creates Irresponsible promise making , which makes honest person lose and less honest person get ahead in race. eg I proposed law-drafts for two child enforcement, law-drafts to reduce Go-hatya to near zero, wiping out terrorism, RJB-devalaya etc. but opposed Hindu Rashtra demand because many panth such as Sikhism oppose this. So many Hinduvaadies told me "well, se, BJP is better than you because they promise all that you promise, and they also promise Hindu Rashtra !!" So I never got support from Hinduvaadies !!
.
Well, BJP , like Congress and AAP makes 100s of promises as they dont intend to keep any !! So CoBhAp make promises by tons. Whereas persons like myself, who want to make only keepable promises , and so lose out.
.
My worry is that all disappointed activists will lose political activism for good. They will start thinking that "all politicians lie, all politicians are cheats and so why waste time". And thats why I ask ALL to focus on law-making and not waste time in neta-bhakti.
