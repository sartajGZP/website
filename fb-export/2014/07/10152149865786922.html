.
Solution I propose is to inform more and more activists to study the law-drafts that improved USA, UK etc. And also ask inform activists that SoNaKe, SoNaKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers oppose these law-drafts and so activists should stay away from them and instead focus on publicizing law-drafts that would improve India.

.
Long long back, the judges, police etc in UK was as corrupt and worse than what we see in India. The activists back then who wanted change didnt resort to some tall-leader-andbhakti. They didnt create a NaMo or AK . But instead they demand what is called Coronor's Jury Inquest . What this law-drafts means is that "if a police officer is accused of injuring a common man, then officers will NOT decide the trial, but 7-12 citizens chosen at random will decide the trial, and if majority of the citizen demand expulsion of that police officer, then that police officer will be expelled " !! This law-drafts came in UK in 950 AD and it was called Coronor's Inquest. and continues till date. Even today, in USA, many European countries, all cases of death by policemen are decided by 5 to 12 citizens chosen at random from the common citizenry.
.
This law-draft drastically reduced corruption as well as heavy handedness in policemen. And so industrial progress of nation started improving. A major lift in growth rate of UK comes right after 950 AD. Later, the Jury System became so popular , that activists demand law-draft that ALL cases will be decided by Jurors only and not the judges. So in around 1100 AD, , the activist forced the king to print law-draft named as Magna Carta, which established that citizens will be punished only after Jury Trial. Later Jury System started spreading across European countries . The USA extended it by law-drafts such as election of Police Chiefs, election of Public Prosecutors, election of judges and Right to recall over all of them.
.
India is facing same problem. Criminals across India are becoming more and more hardened due to corruption in judges, policemen, district public prosecutors, MLAs, Ministers etc. So what do the activists do? First they blame 99% citizens who dont think beyond sports, movies and porn. Well,then same is the case in USA too. If apathy of 99% was reason for India being behind, then USA too should have been in terribly bad shape as India. So "difference" reason is something else.
