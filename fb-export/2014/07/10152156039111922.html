After giving bitter pills of price rise, the least NaMo can do is to raise MP canteen food price in Parliament by at least 5 times , such as Rs 120 for Thali, Rs 20 for tea, Rs 30 for coffee from existing Rs 25 , Rs 4 and Rs 5 !!!
.
I dont blame NaMo for price rise in Railways and many things. Decade of misrule of Sonia\MMS hasnt left much room now. BJP too had its share because all its MPs opposed the law-drafts to reduce inflation , increase growth etc.
.
So NaMo took the only options he had and raised the prices.
.
But least NaMo can do is raise prices in MP canteen as well in parliament House and also North/South etc Avenues. I dont know the prices myself. But I hear that veg thali is Rs 25, tea is Rs 4, coffee is Rs 5 and so forth. IMO, these prices need at least 5 times more. And even then , it is bargain. Tea is at least Rs 40 in and thali is at least Rs 200 in any ac restaurant these days
.
If NaMo cant force MPs to pay even reasonable rates, then all this bravado like "56 nee chaatee" should be dismissed as joke..
.
.-Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta , Right to recall Group
