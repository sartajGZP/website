The difference between "swadeshi" of us at RRG and "swadeshi" of slogan shouters of BST, RSS , BJP etc is ------- we at RRG promote law-drafts to reduce inefficiencies of Indian factory owners so that Indians can make everything from pin to mobile to fighter planes, and thus have high paying jobs.. Whereas "swadeshi' of workers BST, RSS, BJP etc is only tel-sabun , slogan shouting, opposing all good law-drafts and then finally FDI-pooja !!!
.
Today, a big part of Indian youth is pro-FDI and anti-Swadeshi. They think and say that "FDI is good, it will bring jobs, and salary will increase" etc etc. And they are partially right. And they do NOT know about repatriation crisis and they do not know that crisis will force us to sell all minerals etc to foreigners. Why is youth becoming pro-FDI anti-Swadeshi?
.
The biggest reason is "slogan shouting" swadeshi people such as workers in BJP, RSS, BST etc These workers will shout "swadeshi", "swadeshi", "swadeshi" 100 times a day and thus establish themselves as leaders in "swadeshi",. Next these workers will oppose law-drafts such as RTR, MRCM, TCP, Jury, Wealth tax etc.. So the inefficiencies of Indian factory owners remain high and Indian factory owners fail to manufacture goods. So Indian citizens are left with no option but to use videshi goods. And the Indian factory owners also fail to create enough jobs, forget good. And so the unemployed or underpaid youth also becomes pro-FDI !! And finally, in the name of Swadeshi , BST-workers , RSS-workers and BJP-workers will give long lectures using swadeshi tel sabun, as if swadeshi is confined tel sabun. So finally, much of the youth starts seeing "swadeshi" as just a hypocrisy.
.
The solution I propose is to inform the youth about REAL swadeshi i.e. Swadeshi is not tel-sabun and slogan shouting, but swadeshi is promoting law-drafts that will reduce inefficiencies of India's factory owners, enable them to manufacture all goods from mobile to fighter planes and thus enable them to create jobs. And swadeshi is NOT slogan shouting, tel sabun, asking people not to drink coke, asking people not to eat at McDonald and then finally welcoming FDI in railways and also FDI in defence !!
.
So if you want Swadeshi in India to succeed, then I request you to work to spread information on law-drafts that will reduce inefficiencies of Indian factory owners such as Right to Recall law-drafts, MRCM, Jury System, TCP, Wealth tax etc etc. And I request you all not waste time in slogan shouting, tel sabun, neta-pooja, FDi-pooja etc etc.
.
-Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta , Right to recall Group
