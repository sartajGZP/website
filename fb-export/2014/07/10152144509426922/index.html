Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate

Fri Jul 04 2014 03:50:32 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

China shows Arunachal Pradesh on China's map. And yet, NaMo approves four SEZs exclusively for Chinese !! IOW, Chinese are using invasion as a threat to force NaMo to give SEZs to Chinese companies !! Solution : We should now prepare for war. How? Eevery citizen should be required to have guns , delicense all weapon manufacturing except nuclear\chemical\biolgical, JurySys etc..
.
In 1998, ABV refused to give entry to American Insurance Companies , and many other companies. And ABV also did Pokharan-2 against wish of USA. So to teach a lesson to ABV and whole India, USA funded Subramanian Swamy to file cases against Jaylalitha, get Jaylalitha into prison for few days, and forced Jaylalitha to withdrew support to ABV's govt. So ABV's govt fell. Next USA paid Pakistanies to start Kargil war. And finally, war ended ONLY ABV assured iUSA that India will give access to insurance and other companies into India. IOW, it was USA which won Kargil war -- India and Pakistan both lost the Kargil war.
.
Now American threat is as it was. It has only increased. And we have Chinese threatening us. China is openly showing Arunachal Pradesh in China's map. Chinese soldiers intrude into Indian borders at will. And despite this, NaMo has to give industrial parks with SEZ status to Chinese companies !!!
.
Why isnt NaMo cancelling Chinese SEZs for showing AP as Chinese territory? Well, reason is simple --- threat of China attacking India via Pakistan or directly or both is real.
,
Now here are some scariier facts. Pakistan has far far more AK-47-like guns than India has !! India has 4 crore legal/illegal guns, but most are pistons and katta,. Pakistan has 2 crore legal/illegal guns, and most are AK-47-level. So if Chinese break the "wall" of Indian Army, then there is NOTHING that will stop lakhs and crores of Pakistanies from entering India and looting and arsoning.
.
So now whats the solution? The SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers can resume slogan shouting. The rest of us should consider the following :-
.
1. The first part is telling ugly and shameful fact to all of us that years of neglect of Military by Congress\BJP has made Military so weak that it cannot defend us against Chinese. It is NOT fault of soldiers. The SoMo (= Sonia, Modi) and other CoBha MPs always opposed the law-drafts needed to improve manufacturing in India and weapon manufacturing in India. So soldiers are brave, but have no weapons to fight against Chinese.
.
2. Second is telling equally shameful fact to all that USA had won Kargil war and India\Pakistan had lost it.
.
3. So we need to manufacture weapons, The NaMo's proposal of "FDI-made weapon" is fatal --- because it will only make it easier for Western companies and Japan to plant kill switch in complex weapons we buy from these FDI-run western\japanese companies. We need to manufacture our own weapons.
.
4. Now AK-47 or below guns is something we can manufacture today. It is possible to manufacture 80 crore AK-47 level guns in a few months, and enable each adult Indian citizen to have one gun and 100s of bullets. That will be a huge detterent to Pakistanis as well as Chinese.
.
5. The private citizens should be allowed to setup walled weapon trainning campus without any permission, and inside walled campus they should be allowed to keep ALL weapons. The only restriction will be that they cant take the weapons outside the walled area without permission. inside the private walled area , they should be allowed to have all weapons except nuclear, biological and chemical. The campus owners must register and report the weapons they have , and its usage. But no license will be needed.
.
6. All weapon manufacturing except nuclear\chemical\biological weapons should be completely delicensed . Only registration and reporting should be needed.
.
7. TCP, Jury System, DDMRCAM = MRCM, Wealth tax, RTR Police Chief, RTR judges, RTR Public Prosecutor, RTR Pollution Control Board Chairman etc are the law-drafts needed to improve manufacturing in general.. This will also improve weapon manufcaturing.
.
8. Making it COMPULSORY for all citizens to bear arms,. The Swiss have the law that each citizen is required to have guns at his home. And he can be fined if he doesnt have gun at home !! The same law should be applied in India. To start with, everyone with income above Rs 4 lakhs be required to have guns at home. and later ceiling should be brought down.
.
The alternative is that we handover India to USA or Japanese. And thats what FDI in defence is --- we are handing over India to Japanese and Western weapon making firms. That will surely protect us from pakistanis and Chinese. But none of these weapons will work and USA decides to invade India.
.
Now SoMoKe = Sonia + NaMo + AK dont have strength to pass law-drafts such as weaponization of citizens, complete delicensing of all weapon manufacturing, Jury System, wealth tax, RTR, TCP, DDMRCAM = MRCM etc .All CoBhAp MPs and their financiers are opposed to these law-drafts.
.
To get this law-drafts, the method I propose is --- we activists should convince crores of voters to send orders to MPs via SMS to get these law-drafts passed. As the number of voters increase, the possibility of getting this law-draft passed will increase. And more and more voters will come to know that SoMoKe and CoBhApSwaRa are opposed to these law-drafts !! And that will further make activists walk away from SoMoKe-bhakti and CoBhApSwaRa, and so slogan-shouting-activism will decrease, and activists will have more time for legislative-activism. And so chances of getting good law-drafts passed will further increase, and thus Indian Military will improve at a much faster rate.
.
-Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta , Right to recall Group
