.
Let me explain by % occupancy.
.
1. Say a train goes from Ahmedabad to Mumbai and chair car is 50% empty and 50% full all the way from Ahmedabad-M . The vacancy is 50%
.
2. Say a train goes from Ahmedabad to Mumbai and chair car is 50% empty and 50% full all the way from Ahmedabad -Vapi, and 100% from Vapi-Mumbai. And Ahmedabad-Vapi is say 75% of route. Then occupancy will be (75 * 50 + 25 * 100) = 3750 + 5000 = 8750 i.e. 87%.
.
