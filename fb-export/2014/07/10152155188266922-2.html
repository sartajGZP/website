RUMOR --- RBI Chief Raghuram Ghazani announces a scheme by which ex-Ministers and other criminals can now exchange the fake currency notes they have at any bank for REAL currency notes, and RBI = people of India will suffer the burden !!
.
First this is a rumor. The rumor says that RBI has ordered all banks to accept fake currency notes from customers and give it to RBI and in return RBI will give real notes to the banks !! The notification was issued by RBI boss Raghram Ghazani, 25th descendent of Mohamad Ghazani. The cost of fake note will be borne by RBI i.e. people of India. I am not sure of NaMo has approved this notification or not.
.
If such notification exists, then Congress Ministers who as per another rumor, had printed fake notes for themselves worth 1000s of crores of rupees, will feel truly blessed !!! They can now calmly exchange all fake notes using dummy accounts of their men for real notes.
.
All countries do their best to crack down on fake notes. But NO country, not even USA which is otherwise most business friendly country has such law that fake notes can be exchanged for real ones.
.
In case of such law, the real beneficieries are NOT common people, but large criminal. The commons will have a few fake notes. While criminals will have fake notes by toones. .
.
As per RBI, the real currency notes in India are around Rs 12,00,000 crore i..e about Rs 9500 per citizen,. The assorted rumors say that fake notes worth Rs 1 lakh crore i.e. about Rs 800 per citizen were printed during MMS regime from 2004-2014. And bulk of these notes were NOT printed by ISI, but by Indian Ministers themselves !! It is similar to Telgi scam, where fake stamp papers were printed by people like MMS etc and finally they tried to throw all blame on ISI, and when they failed, they threw all blame on Telgi !! ISI does print fake Indian currency notes. But a much bigger share is printed by Indian Ministers themselves.
.
Now if RBI has actually printed such notification, and if Congress Ministers did indeed print Rs 1 lakh crores of fake notes, then we are heading for a royal ripoff !! All these fake notes will bought by RBI for real notes and cost will be passed on to India.
.
Solution I propose is that we should order MP via SMS to cancel the RBI notification to accept fake notes, if such notification exists.
.
