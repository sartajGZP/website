Why Hindivaadies supported BJP\RSS ,over RRG, and how they all got convinced (or fooled?) that RSS-agenda is better than RRG-agenda !!
.
Most Hindivaadies I came across supported BJP\RSS more than RRG. One reason was size ---- BJP\RSS has bigger size. But there was more --- BJP\RSS gave tons of fake promises, which we at RRG didnt.
.
First, we openly stated that we, at least myself, is NOT Hinduvaadi.. The word Hinduvaadi as defined by Savarkar as one important ingredient --- he must believe in Hindu Rashtra. Now many of the law drafts I have proposed - such as law-drafts to reduce go hatya, imposing 2 child restriction , expelling Bangladeshis, stopping Bangladeshis etc are common with Hinduvaadies, but I oppose very concept of Hindu Rastra. The reasons are many -- and most important is -- many Sikhs, Jains and Buddhists oppose Hindu Rastra concept. Whatever one may say and think, Akal Takht has given Hukamnama that Sikhs and Hindus are different, and many Sikhs do accept this Hukamnama, And I am Jain, and consider Jainism part of Hinduism, but many Jains have been saying opposite. And many Buddhist also call themselves non-Hindu. The concept of Hindu rastra offends all of them. Second, putting words "Hindu Rastra" does not add an iota of safety. The final rounds of all wars are fought by weapons and not concepts. And concept of Hindu Rastra doenst improve weapon manufacturing at all. So Hindu Rastra cencept even at best doesnt increase safety.
.
Next, we at RRG proposed law-drafts that would improve temple management, improve education, reduce medicine prices and reduce interest rates. This will reduce conversions, as today, most conversations are because Missionaries run good schools, good hospitals and also run low interest rate loan networks. But ww, at least I, opposed punishments to those who convert,. Where as in very indirect words, BJP\RSS promised anti-conversion laws, which will magically reduce conversions to zero !!
.
And next, we at RRG only promised law-drafts to change Muslim Personnel Law to abolish instant tripple talaq, have alimony and badn polygamy. We didnt support complete dissolution of MPC . Whereas BJP\RSS in low implicit words, promises complete abolition of MPC.
.
IOW, BJP\RSS promised .
.
(1) Hindu Rastra, not explicitly but in very low implicit words
.
(2) complete ban on conversions , using force if needed, and again this promise was made in implicit words and not direct words
.
(3) uniform marriage code
.
(4) Ram Janam Bhoomi Devalaya, KJBD, KVD

(5) complete ban on go-hatya
.
(6) Two child policy
.
(7) ending love jihad
.
(8) ending Islamic terrorism
.
(9) freeing temples from Govt control
.
etc etc.
.
We at RRG opposed (1).
.
The law-drafts to reduce conversions we had proposed were (a)improve govt schools (b) improve govt hospitals (c) reduce interest rates (d) improve temple management, but we opposed use of force to reduce convertion
.
We only supported ending TTT , introducing almony and ending polygamy. and not ban on other clauses of MPC dealing with adoption, inheritance etc.
.
For (4) to (9), we had all law-drafts.
.
So Hindivaadies told us that "see BJP\RSS-apex is making far far more promises than you guys". Well, they didnt know that BJP\RSS-apex doesnt intend to keep any of their promises. When a person doesnt intemd to keep any promise, he will make 100s of promises in explicit and implicit tones. Vachaneshu kim daridrata i.e. "why be poor when making promises" is the typical characteristics of someone who intends to keep no promises.
.
One more reason was that our promises were some 20-25 law-drafts , each law-draft reducing a problem and running to 2-8 pages.. Everywhere,, the statement was "reducing" the problem, and not complete end of the problem. BJP\RSS-apex promised complete end of the problem !!! And they had one-liners instead of drafts running into pages and pages. had one liner HHM-GGM. to solve ALL problems
.
And there was paidmedia working round the clock to create a feeling that BJP\RSS-apex is serious and nit just bluffing or make fake promises !!
.
So all in all, many activists stayed away from RRG-agenda, despite law-drafts to reduce go-hatya to near zero, reduce jeev hatya to not near zero but significantly, reduce love jihad, impose population control and many more. And main reason was that BJP\RSS-apex in implicit words gave far far more promises , much taller promises and had better paidmedia coverage to create a sense of seriousness.
.
Now Sadhvi Pragya is still in prison, there is no shipping out of Bangladeshis , there are no law-drafts to reduce go-hatya even by 1% and so forth. Some Hinduvaadies are now realizing that BJP\RSS-apex had not convinced them but fooled them. Well, better late than never.
.
Solution I propose is that instead of waiting for 2019, and then getting convinced by or fooled by another tall leader, IMO, we all activists should start publicizing law-drafts to that can reduce the problems. Instead of a one tall leader who claims to end all problem in one day, it is better to spend time on law-drafts running into pages and pages which reduce problems at the rate of x% per day. So please focus on law-drafts and please stop looking for tall leaders.
.
-Rahul Mehta
