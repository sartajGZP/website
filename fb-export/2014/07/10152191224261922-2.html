The mechanism is ---
.
1. As of now, each citizen will have vote of value 1
.
2. If a person has zero or one child or two , value of his vote will be 1
.
3. If a person has N = 3 or more children, then value of his vote will decrease become 2/N. i.e. for 3 children , it will become 0.67 , for N = 4 it will be 0.25 etc. For N >5 , it will be zero.
.
4. The children get lesser vote and get delayed voting age. eg if a person's father had N1 children and mother had N2 children (can be from previous marriage), then his sibbling count will be N = (N1 + N2)/2 , which can be fractional.
.
5. If person has one or less sibbling, he voting age will be 18 years and votes weight will be 1. If his sibbling count is N > 2, then his voting age will be (18 + N/2*5 years) rounded off to nearest integer. eg say he has 2 brothers. Then voting age will be 23 years. If he has 3 brothers, then voting age will be (18 + 1.5 * 5) = 25.5 years = 26 years. and so forth.
.
5.And value of persons vote will 1/N. So if he has one sibbling or less, value of of his vote will be 1 . If he has 2 brothers, then value of vote will be 1/2 = 0.5. etc. And if he has no sibbling, then value of his vote will be 2
.
6. If a person is childless and dies, then his vote will be go as per his will or to his next of kin.
.
