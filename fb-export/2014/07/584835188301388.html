Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate:

MNC-led growth : Congress created the problem in 1992, made it worse, and now NaMo is making it further worse.
.
The budget will create superb FDI-led growth !! The growth rate will exceed 8% pa this year and perhaps 10% next year. The magic is simple --- MNC-owners are going to invest billions of dollars in real estate , railways, everything and so there will growth all over. The problem will come when MNC-owners will demand repatriation in dollars.
.
The Congress-bhagats and NaMo-bhagats are full of positive attitude. And they lecture us all that repatriation crisis will never happen. And when it happens , they will blame everyone except the leaders who created the crisis by allowing FDI with without "repatriation by exports only commitment". And then the solution they will propose is massive sell out of mineral mines, everything and more FDI !!
.
The FDI in real estate will fetch zero exports. So where will dollars come for profit repatriation? The answer is --- have a positive attitude. The FDI in railways will create zero exports, So where will dollars to repay profits come from? Again, answer is --- dont show such negative attitude. These were the exact answers given to us by MMS-bhagats in 1992. And now MMS-bhagats are giving exact same answers with sholkas of veda and VM-shouting added.
.
All in all, budget is same as what MMS had given for years --- FDI, FDI , FDI and more FDI. Now there are people who expect different results for same inputs, just because user who is entering inputs has changed !!! And there are people who say that medicine-X can give different results just by changing the doctor who administer it !! As if core properties of program or medicine have less say.
.
NaMo got a bankrupt regime from UPA. He cant be blamed much for it, though some blame does go -- that for years and decades, eha dn other BJP MPs systematically opposed law-drafts needed to fix Indian economy such as RTR, Jury, wealth tax, inheritance tax, DDMRCAM etc. But here, his blame was at most 1/1000, as till now, he was just 1 out of 1000 leaders who opposed this law-drafts. But now, but allowing more and more FDI in non-export areas, he is making the coming problem of repatriation crisis worse and worse.
.
Solution I propose is that.
.
1. First we anti-FDI activists should realize that we are on our own. We should accept that SoMoKe are pro-FDI and remain so. And SoMoKe-supporters will keep supporting FDI and so would CoBhApSwaRa-workers. So we must NOT expect any support from them. We are on our own.
.
2. We should give newspaper advertisements on repatriation crisis that FDI can create and its solution that all FDI must be tied with "repatriation by exports only" commitment.
.
3. We should also advertise the law-drafts needed to fix economy such TCP, RTR, DDMRCAM, JurySys, wealth tax, inheritance tax, removal of GST, VAT, CST, excise, sales tax, LBT etc.
.
4. We should also advertise the law-drafts needed to reduce imports and increase exports to deal with FDI]debt mess previous regime has created and this regime is adding.
.
5. Ask voters to send orders to MPs via SMS to get this law-drafts printed in Gazette.
