How AAPians are helping Japanese bullet train ---- by making bullet train critics look like jokers. And why are AAPians do so? Because they want Japanese MNC-owners to fund media campaign in coming Assembly elections.
.
====
.
Yesterday, I was explaining forex burden bullet train can cause to a small time meeting. And one activist laughed and said "oh yeah, you are a kejrival , the joker who says that bullet train ticket will cost Rs 70000 !!" Essentially, the whole forex issue, and the FACT that Indian engineers can make 4 hr train from Mumbai-Ahmedabad got sidelined.
.
And its not just AK. ALL AAPians are now spreading lies that bullet train ticket from Ahmedabad to Mumbai will cost Rs 70000 !!. AAPians are doing this to make every bullet train critic, such as myself, look like a joker. And thus in reality, AAPians are HELPING bullet train project !!
.
Essentially, in coming Assembly elections, AAPians want paidmedia-coverage. And they want Japanese bullet train owning MNC-owners to pay a big part of it. In return, the Japanese want to cover the facts that
.
(1) GoI has to pay profits bullet train make in dollars !!!
.
(2) Without any FDI except cost of engine, we can make 4 hr train from Ahmedabad-Mumbai.. Existing Shatabdi can run at 140 kmhr speed if tracks are improved and more tracks as put. And that would make Ahmedabad-Mumbai journey 3 hrs . Add 1 hour for stops and some delays, and we will have 4 hrs Ahmedabad-Mumbai train.
.
But due to "Rs 70000 joke, these AAPian jokers are suppressed the spread of the above two arguments.
.
AAPians help Japanese hide these facts by ensuring that EVERY person who talks against bullet train look like a joker !!! Now thanks to AAPians, anyone who talks above 2 points, will get mocked due to AK-420's "bullet train tickets will cost Rs 70000 " i.e. "14 times the flight !!" remark.
.
And in return, the Japanese would pay mediamen to give coverage to AAP for coming assembly elections.
.
=====
.
And here is how AAPians explain why bullet train will cost, thereby making every Swadeshi look like a joker :-
.
project price = Rs 60,000 crore
recovery time = 10 years
number of passengers per train = 800 (WRONG)
number of trains per day = 3 (WRONG , WRONG WRONG )
number of passengers per day = 2,400
number of passengers per year = 876,000
number of passengers in 10 years = 8,760,000
ticket price = Rs 68,493 = Rs 70000 approx
.
Well, the 2 parallel tracks are capable of running 30 trains per day each way !! And train can carry 1200 to 1600 passengers. Once Ahmedabad-Mumbai industrial corridor comes, and once 3 hr train become available, the daily, weekend and fly-by-night traffic will cross 25000 passengers each way per day. . At Rs 4000 per ticket the passenger traffic will fetch Rs 20 crore per day, or over Rs 7000 crore per year revenue. Plus hotels, malls etc connected to station will fetch additional revenue.
.
AAPians beautifully hide two points --- the GoI will need to pay forex for profits, and Indian engineers can anyway make 140 kmhr train !! IOW. AAPians are helping Japanese by coving these two facts for media-coverage in return.
.
So dear activists, I want you all to know that all AAPians in reality work for nefarious interests of MNCs. AAPians goal is to make whole field of anti-corruption, anti-videshi activism look like a joke.. And AAPians want to make every anti-corruption activist and every Swadeshi activist look like a joker.. And thus promote corruption and nefarious interests of MNC-owners.
