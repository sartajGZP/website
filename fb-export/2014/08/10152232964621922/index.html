1. NaMo's decision to suspend talks with Pakistan is welcome. Talks with Pakistan is waste of time. We should prepare for war.
.
2. NaMo;s statements that parents should advice their sons etc is useless. And NaMo's statement that Indians have "mera kya, mujhe kya" is WRONG, it is FALSE insult to every India, and PM should really not make such statements. Please write on MyGoV that he should confine to action, not give sermons and not make false statements, and certainly not make false statements insulting commons of India.
.
=====
.
1. NaMo's decision to suspend talks with Pakistan is very much welcome. The talks were anyway waste of time. Pakistan listens to America, not because America talks, but America has weapons. Pakistan handed over a part of JK to China, not because China talked but because China had weapons. Pakistan has handed over a huge chunk if land near coastline at Gwadar not because China talked, but because China has weapons. Pakistan understands only one language - the language of weapons. And that language will cost us too --- but thats the only language we should speak. Its time we focus on weapon manufacturing (without FDI) and dont waste time with talks in Pakistan.
.
Not only talks, we should suspend ALL trade, tourist relation with Pakistan. No citizen of India should be allowed to travel to Pakistan and vice versa. Only diplomats should be allowed top travel, and that too only in select areas like New Delhi. We should also deny flight passage to all Pakistani flights over India, and we should not fly our planes over Pakistan.
.
The war with pakistan *will* with 100% surily turn into nuclear war. And we should start bracing for that without delay.
.
=======
.
2. NaMo's statements on 15-aug-2014 that parents should tell their sons to behave is uncalled for. Let me ask all readers who have a son. Did YOU follow NaMo's instructions? Did YOU tell your sons that they must not molest, rape etc? Please follow NaMo's instruction and tell your sons LOUDLY and in PUBLIC that "see son, NaMo has asked me to give you a sermon, and here is the sermon I am giving you --- do not rape, do not molest, do not kidnap etc etc".. In fact, please post a status on facebook addressed to your son, and tag him, and so that whole world can see that you indeed gave sermon to your son as NaMo had asked. My point is -- NaMo';s instruction that parents should teach their son is uncalled for.
.
All parents do ask their sons to behave well, because they (a) they want a good son (b) they dont want their son to face punishment . And 99% of sons do NOT commit any crimes. The 1% commit crimes, and most important reason is that Congress and YOUR BJP leaders, including NaMo have been opposing law-drafts needed to reduce crimes against women. It was YOUR Minsietr Arun Jetley who passed law in 2001 that if a "boy" below 18 years murders, rapes, kidnaps etc , then prinsion sentence will be only 3 years !!! and it is YOU who have been supporting this law for 12 years, and even now you are dilly dallying in changing it. It is Congress as well as NaMo's own BJP MPs\leaders who oppose Jury System over crime on women, and it is Congress\AAP as well as NaMo's own BJP MPs who have opposed narco-test in cases of crime against women. So NaMo should better first give a sermon to Congres\AAPs leaders and also BJP leaders, including NaMo himself that they would NOT oppose the proposal to take narco-tests of accused in cases of crimes against women. One of the biggest reason why crimes against women is rampant is India, is because cases get delayed, and they get delayed because we have lesser number of judges. eg China has 200,000 judges, while India has only 18000 judges. IOW, we need 10 times more judges than we have. So when NaMo became CM of Gujarat in 2002 , Gujarat had about 650 judges and needed 6500 judges. How many more seats NaMo added in 12 years? hardly 300 !! So Gujarat needed 6000 extra judges and NaMo gave only 300. Why? Is it because NaMo thought "mera kya, mujhe kya". . well, please ask him why he didnt increase number of courts?
.
And NaMo's statement that (sic) Indians suffer with "mera kya , mujhe kya" mentality is sickening.. If people were thinking "mera kya mujkhe kya" then why did crores of voters came came to vote? why did crores of voters came on street on protests in apr-2011 and again in aug-2011? Crores of voters had supported VHO in Ram Janam Bhoomi in 1990s, and they wwere NOT thinking "mera kyaa , mujhe kyaa". In fact, worst people who think "mera kya mujhe kya" I see these days are Congress\AAP and NaMO's own MPs in parliament, who are refusing to print law to ban cow slaughter in India, refusing to create 200,000 more courts for speedy disposal of cases, refusing to print Right to Recall law-drafts and JurySys law-drafts etc etc. And while NaMo give us sermons, it is BJP CM Manohar Parricker who asked Goa High Court to legalize bullock slaughter in apr-2013 !! And Manohar Parricker had taken permission of NaMo as well as Mohan Bhagwat before he asked Goa High Court to legalize bullock slaughter. IOW, we see more "mera kya , mujhe kya". in CoBhAp-apex than in commons of India.
.
And when NaMo says that we (sic) Indians have "mera kya, mujhe kya" attitude, is NaMo implying that lesser % of Americans o Europeans etc have this attitude? Is Americans and Europeans are better than we (sic) Indians? I am not surprised.
.
And while NaMo said that "corruption is cancer" , which law-drafts did he printed in Gujarat in past 12 years and in center in these 70 days to reduce corruption in supreme court judges, corruption in high court judges, corruption in lower court judges, corruption in Ministers/IAS/IPS etc? NONE !!!! The only law-drafts we saw was TRAI-amemdment to enable ex-IMF-employee to become PMO secretary and NJAC which will allow PM's 3 stooges to have say in appointment of judges, as if PM's stooges are gauranteed to be less corrupt, and judges once appointed will remain so.
.
All in all, NaMo;s sermons that "parents should advice children" , "Indians suffer with mera kya mujhe kya mentality" etc are uncalled for. The mess of crimes against women, or corruption is because CoBhAp leaders including NaMo opposed the law-drafts necessary to reduce crimes against women and corruption. To cover their opposition to law-drafts needed to reduce corruption and crimes, NaMo is pointing fingers at parents, citizens at large etc.
