Following is the proposal one RTR-activists in Delhi took to AK

1. Allow a Delhi voter, whose house doesnt have connection, to register his cell number at local Patwari office or Civic center. It can be one per family.

2. Issue numbers of each tanker provider (they already have it)

3. The slum dweller can specify/change the tanker provider any day via SMS or personally visiting Patwari office

4. Delhi Jal Board will give say 200 liter per day per family who has allocated the water quota for free

5. The slum dwellers can pay the carrier charges to tanker owner , say 25 paise per bucket or whatever.

The fact that citizens can change tanker owner via SMS will ensure that tanker owners will provide optimal service.
