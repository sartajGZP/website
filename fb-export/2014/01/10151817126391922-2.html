AK had asked AAP volunteers to tell to slum dwellers that each slum dweller family will get coupon book of 21000 liters of water every month, similar to some ration card or milk coupons, and tankers will deliver them free 700 liters everyday for each coupon !!!

I always wondered how can one fool a family with no meter that they will get 21000 liters a month of free water. Wont at least 1 out of 100 person ask question? So I asked AAP volunteers how they managed to fool slum dwellers to believe that unmetered connections will also get 21000 liters a month per family for free !! After all, to give any limited quantity for free, one needs a meter. The volunteers tell me that AK had fooled volunteers into believing that each slum dweller family will get some kind of coupon booklet every month, and each coupon booklet will have some 200 coupons, each coupon having value of 100 liters = 5 buckets of water !!! The volunteers were even shown samples of coupon booklets, and they showed such samples to slum dwellers when slum dwellers would ask them this water distribution question !! One volunteer has got election pamphlet with AK's pix, jhadu, candidate name etc etc and promise of this water-coupon booklet and also picture of this water coupon booklet !!! He will dig that pamphlet out and send me scanned copy.

Over 80% volunteers were all unpaid, genuinely prop-poor and concerned citizens. Many took a month's unpaid leave, lost salary of as high as Rs 40000 a month, spent Rs 500 of their own a day in hotel, spent Rs 300 a day for their own food, paid for their travel and also donated money to AAP.

And this is what they ended up doing ---- fooling slum dwellers into believing that slum dwellers will get water coupon booklet of 21000 liters per month per family !!!
