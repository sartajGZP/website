Cross talk is when A and B discuss a topic on wall of X = myself on status post made by X =- myself. I am requesting both A and B to do their cross talk on their walls or msg. WHY?

I have now 4200 friends. And most of them are politically active, which is good thing for my Right to Recall movement. And my wall is also open to friends' friends. So I have to request ALL not to have cross talk on my wall . You are free to write anything for/against my post, replies to your comments etc . And finally, people click "stop notifications" and then they will also stop getting notifications on my comments in their thread. So I lose audience.

Besides, to speak to a specific pastor, you can always start thread on your wall or his wall. So my "no cross talk" rule doesnt curb your freedom of speech.
