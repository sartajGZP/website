Digvijaya’s trusted babu to draft AAP's mohalla sabha law

NEW DELHI: Aam Aadmi Party chief Arvind Kejriwal on Wednesday told TOI that the former chief secretary of Madhya Pradesh, S C Behar, had been invited to the capital to work as an advisor to the government. Behar, who was to arrive in Delhi on Wednesday, would be actively involved in the party's key agenda of Swaraj by helping in the drafting of the legislation for mohalla sabhas.

Fifteen years after his retirement from administrative services, Behar is still considered by state bureaucrats a part of AICC general secretary Digvijaya Singh's inner coterie. It has, therefore, come as a shock to many how Behar could be accommodated as an adviser in the AAP government after Singh has been so vocal in his attacks on Kejriwal. ...
