How those who donate for cow are giving free food to cow meat eaters !! Solution : the donors should focus on law-drafts to imprison
.
Say a cow gives 300 kg of meat. Say price of grass is Rs 5 per kg and khod (wheat etc is Rs 15 per kg). A cow would need at least 3000 kg of khod and 9000 kg of wheat. So price will be 3000 * Rs 15 + 9000 * Rs 5 = Rs 45000 + Rs 45000 = Rs 90000 . If milk is taken, then more grass and more khod would be needed, but cost of that will come. So we are not counting it.
.
So Rs 90000 of khod and grass would be needed for 300 kg of meat. So meat would cost Rs 300 per kg plus profits.
.
But a donor comes, and donates money, and buys almost half the grass and khod for free. So now cost of meat reduces to Rs 150 or even less. Or putting other way, the cow donor gave half the meat to the cow meat eater for free.
.
This is the harsh reality !!
.
Solution is that those who are donating for cows should focus on law-drafts that would imprison cow slaughterers. If cow slaughter goes on, then donating for cow is just giving free cow meat to cow meat eaters.
.
Our sages instutionalized donation for cow as follows
.
1. cow milk is good for health of every man and thus community.
.
2. but cow milk is expensive compared to other milks, as cows give less milk
.
3. so rich donate for cow and thus subsidize NUTRITIOUS food for poor
.
4. so the sages institutionalized donations for cows.
.
All this is well, but cow slaughter creates a HUGE leakage in the system. If cow slaughter starts, then rich by donating for cow are only buying free cow meat for cow meat eaters !!
.
Solution is NOT to stop donations. But solution is that donors should spend money to publicize and bring law-drafts to reduce cow slaughter. Which law-drafts? Please see links in the second comment. Please send orders to MPs via SMS to get these law-drafts passed. Otherwise, by donating for cows, you will only give free food, strength and muscles to cow meat eaters.
