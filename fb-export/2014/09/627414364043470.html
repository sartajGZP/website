==== start of draft ====
.
1. This law-draft is to enable Jurors to order an expert to conduct polygraph or brainmapping or narcotest in private or in public. This draft will come in Gazette after Jury System draft has been printed in the Gazette.
.
2. (instruction for Jurors) With majority vote, the Jurors will decide if polygraph, brainmapping or narcotest should be conducted on the accused. A polygraph may or need not be conducted before brainmapping and a brainmapping may be or need not be conducted before narcotest.
.
3. (instruction for Jurors) The Jurors will decide if the test are to be conducted in private or public. If the name of a female victim has to be withheld, then the polygraph, brainmapping and narcotest must be done in private and Jurors cant make it public, Otherwise, it may be done in public
.
4. (instruction for JA = Jury Administrators) If number of Jurors are below 24, then JA will add Jurors using random selection method given in the Jury System draft, so that number of Jurors are 24.
.
5. (instruction for Jurors) If new Jurors are added, then each lawyer will speak for 30 minutes and may speak more if majority of new Jurors approve to inform about the case for at most 4 hours each. Plus, the Jurors will meet inside a room, away from public , for 1 hour to at most 12 hours, where old Jurors can inform new Jurors about the case.
.
6. (instruction for JA = Jury Administrator) After new Jurors are informed about the case, the JA using random lottery will divide Jurors into two equal groups Group-A and Group-B. If there are odd number of Jurors then the Group-B will have more 1 more Jurors. The Group-A will draft the questions to be asked and Group-B with majority decision will decide if question farmed should be asked.
.
7. (instruction for Jurors) If a person in Group-A wants to swap his position with a person in Group-B, then they may do so. The Jurors with majority approval, can also allow an expert in the panel of experts made by Grand Jurors to frame questions. The framing of questions will be done by Jurors in both Groups only, and will be done confidentially. The judge, lawyers etc cannot be there during their meetings
.
8. (instruction for Jurors) The question framing session will end when majority Jurors agree that question framing is over. The Jury Administrator will take the questions, and ask each Juror in Group-B whether he agrees with the question by only asking the question number, and not reading the whole question. Jury Administrator will give the list of questions approved by majority of Jurors in Group-B to the experts in-charge of polygraph, brainmapping and narcotests.
9 Jurors The Jurors with majority approval will decide which experts will conduct polygraph, brainmapping and narcotest. They will select the experts from panels approved by the Grand Jurors.
.
10. (instruction for Jurors) Based on answers given during the polygraph or brainmapping or narcotests, a Juror in Group-A or the expert can prepare a new question, and if majority in Group-B approve the question, then the expert will ask that question. The question will NOT be asked without approval of majority in Group-B
.
11. (instruction for Jurors) The Jurors will not take the statements made in all three tests as evidences
.
12. ( (instruction for Investigating Officer) The policemen investigating the case may gather more evidences based on the statements made during these tests
.
13. (instruction for Investigating Officer) With permission of Grand Jurors, the investigating officer may take polygraph or brainmapping or narcotest, before the Jury Trial begins. In such case, Grand Jurors may select 6 citizens at random and ask them to supervise the narcotest to ensure that no unrelated questions are asked by Investigating Officer.
.
14. Narcotest will not be taken without consent of Grand Jurors or Jurors. Even if accused consents, then also narcotest will require consent of Grand Jurors or Jurors.
.
CV.1 ( (instruction for DC) If a citizen wants to propose any change in this law, then the citizen can submit an affidavit demanding the change to District Collector or his clerk who will post it on the website of Prime Minister for a fee of Rs 20 per page.
.
CV.2 (instruction for Talati aka Patwari) If a citizen wants to register his opposition to this law-draft or any clause of this law-draft or wants to register any support to affidavit filed in the above clause, then he may register his YES/No for a Rs 3 fee at Patwari’s office. The Patwari will note the citizen’s YES/NO and will also post the citizen’s YES/NO on PM’s website.
.
Note ---- Please see CV.1 and CV.2 clauses at the end of the draft. Using these two clauses, the activists, if needed, can bring the changes in the narcotest procedures. Also, Jury administrator can use his discretion. And if he misuses the discretion, then the citizens can recall the District Jury Administrator. Thus there are sufficient checks and balances over experts in charge of narcotest and Jury Administrators.
