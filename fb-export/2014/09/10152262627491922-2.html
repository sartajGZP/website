Law-draft for weaponization of commons.
.
Dear citizen of India,
.
fi you want weaponization of commons in India, then please send following order to MP via SMS "dear MP, I order you to print http://tinyurl.com/StrengthenIndia in Gazette".
.
Dear MP,
.
If you got the link to this post via SMS, then it is an order from citizen to you to print this text in Gazette of of India.
.
=== start of draft ====
.
1. The Govt of India will put the design of INSAS rifle, 303, 202, .22 revolver and all guns used by Indian police , whose level is "below INSAS" in public domain. (INSAS is Indian version of AK-47)
.
2. Anyone , without need of license, by just registration , can start a factory which makes a gun or its parts or its bullets , with the above design. Anyone may also start factory to make bullet proof jackets.
.
3. If any citizen or govt officer believe that the factory owner is violating any law, then the case will be decided not by the judge , but by the Jury
.
4. Any citizen, unless banned by a Jury, will be allowed to possess the guns mentioned in clause-1 and its bullets, and also bullet proof jackets.
.
====== end of draft ======
.
To citizens,
.
This law-draft will enable every to bear INSAS level guns. This will enable us to deal with coming crisis, be ISIS or whatever.
.
Please keep in mind, that Pakistan has over 2 crore AK-47 level guns, Afghanistan has 1 crore such guns, Iran has factories to make guns etc. And India has only 25 lakh AK-47. And China can make and ship crores of AK-47 guns to Pakistan, Bangladesh etc. So earlier we improve our LOCAL gun manufacturing, without FDI (please note, WITHOUT FDI) the better. So if you like above draft, please send "dear MP, I order you to print http://tinyurl.com/StrengthenIndia draft in Gazette". as order to your MP via SMS.
