===== start of the proposed RTR-DPP Gazette draft =======
.
The exact draft, which will come into effect when CM signs this law is as follows:
.
1. The word citizen would mean a registered voter in the district.
.
2. (procedure for Collector) If any citizen of India wishes to become DPP
= District Public Prosecutor , and he appears in person or via a lawyer with affidavit before the DC (or officer he deputes), the DC (or officer he deputes) would accept his application to become DPP after taking filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election.
.
3. (procedure for Talati or Talati’s clerks) If a citizen comes in person to Talati’s office, pays Rs 3 fee , and approves at most five persons for the DPP position, the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved.
.
4. (procedure for Talati) The Talati will put the approvals of the citizen on district’s website with citizen’s voter-ID number and names of the persons he approved.
.
5. (procedure for Talati) If a the citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
6. (procedure for CM) Later, the CM may implement the above 3 procedures over SMS
.
7. (procedure for Collector) On every 5th of month, the Collector or officer he deputes will publish Approval counts for each candidate as on last date of the previous month.
.
8. (procedure for CM) If a candidate gets approval of over 35% of ALL citizens (ALL, not just those who have filed their approval) in a district, and it is 2% more than approval existing DPP has, then CM may appoint him as DPP
.
9 (procedure for CM ) As long as a DPP has approvals of more than 34% citizens , CM need not replace him. But if a DPP’s approval goes below 34%, the CM can replace him with the officer of his choice.
.
=============end of draft ===================
