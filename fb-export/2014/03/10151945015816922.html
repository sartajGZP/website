To,
The BJP workers who are upset at BJP's Haryana ticket sell out - pls send order/demand to Rajnath Singhji and other BJP-apex leaders via SMS WITH proper URL to give ticket to person of your choice. Pls note that SMS must have proper SMS otherwise, that SMS is a useless noise.

Many Haryana BJP-activists tell me that Sonipat's BJP ticket has been given to some Mr Ramesh Kaushik. I googled and found that Mr Ramesh Kaushik was Congress Minister just 3-4 months back !! And long time BJP worker Pradeep Sangwan, who was BJP district head was not given ticket !! Now I am NOT BJP supporter and so I have no right or business in complaining. But here is my suggestion to BJP-activists

1. Pls ask Mr Pradeen Sangwan to make one facebook status with just one line that "cancel ticket of Ramesh Kaushik and pls give Sonipath BJP ticket to Pradeep Sangpan"

2. The facebook will give URL for that status

3. BJP workers should send the SMS as "cancel ticket of Ramesh Kaushik and pls give Sonipath BJP ticket to Pradeep Sangpan. see url = ..... "

Now BJP leaders may delete the SMS or just throw away SIM in gutter. But if they wish, then they can connect SIM to computer. And the computer will give them totals of each url. IOW, if a BJP leader gets 10000 SMS , then he doesnt need to read 10000 SMS, the PC will give him url and count of SMS containing that url.

IOW, ordering SMS for candidate selection is constructive SMS , not some noise.

This is important for whole India, not just Sonipat or Haryana.

Now, I wont send this SMS, as I am NOT a BJP-worker or even a BJP-voter. I never voted for BJP, nor will I vote in this election. So I should NOT be telling them on what candidate they should select. But IMO those who have worked in BJP in Haryana etc should take some initiative. Rest, it is their decision.
