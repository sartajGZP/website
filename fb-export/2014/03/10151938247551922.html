There is an axiom in politics, that if a person is honest, then he can at worst only be subdued by violence or by fooled keeping conditions in fine print, but honest person cant be fooled by open frauds. But there is a caveat --- the person must not be "greedy". Greedy is someone who wants far far more return for his work or his efforts than what normal market can offer. eg say banks are giving return of say 10% a year i.e. they double the money in 7 years. Now if someone comes and promises that he will double the money in 2 years or 1 years or tripple the money in 1 year, then someone falls for it, then he was not dishonest but greedy. IOW, anyone who is hoping to get too much than his efforts are worth is greedy.
