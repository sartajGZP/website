Varanasi is unsafe seat for NaMo , Hinduvad and Swadeshi. Someone should rush to tell NaMo not to contest from Varanasi and instead take any of the 10-15 safe seats in Gujarat

In 2009, Muralti Manohar Joshi got only 31% votes from Varanasi. The seat has about 30% to 35% non-Hindu voters , and some 10% dalit voters, and 20% OBC voters. Now NaMo has tremendous appeal in dalits and OBCs. But BJP has no network of workers in dalits, non-Hindus and OBC. Amongst non-Hindus,. the criminals like Mukhtar Ansari has network. In Dalits, Congress and BSP has network, and in OBC, Mulayam has network. To make matter worse, AK has appeal in Hindu middle class voters.

So if NaMo contests from Varanasi, then NaMo will become heavily dependent on paid-media-owners to ensure that AK is not heavily projected in Hindu middle class. And BSP\SP will be needed to ensure that they dont push their votes towards Congress. Because if (Dalits + OBC + non-Hindu) votes move to Congress or BSP.

So if NaMo contests from Varanasi, he wont be able to spell even H of Hinduvaad for next 2 months, and wont able to speak even S for Swadeshi for next 2 months. So IMO, Varanasi is bad choice for NaMo, Hinduvaad and Swadeshi.

There are 20 General category seats in Gujarat of which NaMo can win from over 15 with no efforts. Plus there are safe seats in rest of India. Varanasi is NOT a safe seat. Someone should ask NaMo to change his mind.
