I am writing a detailed notes on MNC-owners demand for 100% FDi in defence why NaMo\Sonia\AK accepted it. The outlines are.
.
1. All leaders including NaMo, Sonia, AK, other Congress\BJP\RSS etc leaders in their times, have been opposing law-drafts needed to improve efficiency of factory owners in India.. To be specific , n their entire career , these includinsd, NaMo\Sonia\AK, decided to oppose law-drafts such as Right to recall, Jury System, wealth tax etc , which were needed to improve efficiencies of factory owners.
.
2. So manufacturing in India worsened. Since 1991, we are largely an importer of all manufactured hi tech goods
.
3. So weapon manufacturing also worsened, to a point that now we have to hand over defence to foreigners.
..
Now these Congress, BJP, RSS, AAP etc leaders are screaming at the top of their lungs that (1) see chinese are going to attack us (2) see we are making no weapons (3) and so must allow foreigners to make wepaons. Well folks, how come your manifesto of past 20 years never had any LAW-DRAFTS to improve manufacturing and weapon manufacturing in India? Why were all of them silent on this issue, to the extent that many almost kept saying that "war will never happen".
.
The reason why these tall leaders oppose these law-drafts is because their political career including paidmedia campaigns were funded by nefarious desi\videshi elitemen who oppose wealth tax, Jury syste,m, Right to Recall and many such laws needed to improve efficiencies of factory owners. Now India has degenerated to a point that "best" option is handing over defence to foreigners in the name "100% FDI". The only difference between NaMo and MMS is that if MMS has postire of a meek person, and if MMS has allowed 100% FDI in defence, then RSS-workers, BST-workers etc would have shouted sell out and a cowardly act. Where as NAMo has posture of a firebrand leader, and his act of handing over Indian security to foreigners is touted as an act of bravery . Except differences in postures and perceptions --- the actions are same,
.
Now to save outselves from chinese, we can allow Americans and Japanese.. But if done so, then who will save us when Americans attack us? Again, these tall leaders resume to bold lies that ":USA is never going attack India". . Well, did they say in 1988 that USA is going to loot Iraq and did they forecast in 2011 that USA is going to loot Libya? IOW, they have zero belief in their own forecasting, They say with fake confidence that "USA will never invade India'; because they want to rationalize their decision of opposing law-drafts needed to improve Indian manufacturing and instead invite forigners to run Indian defence.
.
Now solution for us is that allow zero FDI. And we instead make law-drafts that will improve efficiency of Indian factory owners. Which law-drafts? The same law-drafts that Congress-leaders, BJP-leaders, AAP-leaders, NAMo, Sonia, AK etc have been opposing through out their political careers --- namely - Right to recall over all apex positions, Jury System, wealth tax , repeal GST , repeal VAT and service tax etc and many more. These law-drafts will improve efficiency of all factory owners in India, make manufacturing completive, viable and profit making and thus also improve weapon manufacturing. A shorter term goal should be give weapon like guns to ALL adult Indians , which will make it impossible for China to take over India. Giving good 100% made in India guns to all adult Indians is possible within 1 year, if law-drafts such as RTR, JurySys, wealth tax are brought in. And in within 3-5 years , we will become at least 25% as strong as China, and so China will deter from attacking us, and within 10 years we will be about 25% as strong as USA, and so USA will also deter.
.
The decision of NaMo\Congress\AK to hand over Indian Military to foreigners will increase strength of MNC-owners in India, and fasten the rate at which India is getting converted into one or more giant Phillipines. The main goals of MNC-owners across world are --- erasing local religions, finishing maths science education, finishing local manufacturing particularly weapon manufacturing -- so that population becomes dependent on West for forever. The FDi in defence will be one step towards that goal, while law-drafts such as Right to recall on all positions, , Jury System in ALL lower , high and supreme court , wealth tax etc. will be a step towards improving Indian Military without foreign help.
.
And so steps we should take is to inform ALL citizens of India that they should order their MPs via SMS to bring the above law-drafts.
.
