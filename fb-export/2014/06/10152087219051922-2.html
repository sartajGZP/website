There are 4 ways to improve prosperity --- (a) decrease internal inefficiencies, (b) take debt from foreign countries (c) ask foreign countries to invest, which is again a form of debt as there is commitment to pay forex for profits earned (d) loot a foreign country. The hardest way is (a). And (d) is possible ONLY after (a) is done. Now NaMo is ONLY doing (b) and (c) which is what Congress\ABV did from 1991 till today, there is no plan in sight for (a). SOLUTION? See last para
.
Since 1998, I have been asking all activists to focus on law-drafts such as TCP, DDMRACM, JurySys, RTR on all positions, wealth tax, inheritance tax etc so that internal inefficieny decreases. But as per them, country doesnt need good laws, country only needs a chakchak PM be, ABV or LKA or NaMo or AK. So they opposed all law-drafts and resorted to ABV-bhakti in 1998 , LKA-bhakti from 2004-2009 and they resorted to AK-bhakti and NaMo-bhakti in 2011-2014. NaMo was supposed to have some magic wand which will generate wealth without these law-drafts and also without FDI\debt.
.
But now it is clear that NaMo has no magic wand, and these law-drafts are beyond his strength. So only way left to him are FDI\debt. Recetly, India took a debt of $ 1 billion from world bank in the name of primary education. And we are seeing FDI-spree in railways, defence and even in newspaper. Some Rs 60,000 crore are coming, and Japanese are making bullet train in India , and many Japanese companies are likely to get contracts to build flyover in Varanasi and all over India. Even Ganga cleaning projects may go to Japanese companies.
.
IOW, so called development is NOT coming by decreasing our inefficiencies, but only by foreign money and foreign technology. The real development is NOT getting bullet train made by Japanese, but REAL development is when entire train is made and run by Indians only. That real development is nowhere in sight.
.
So there will be massive job growth, massive development and high prosperity --- but foreigners will ghave ON-OFF switch to that. They day they decide to turn it OFF, they can simply ask for repatriation of profits, and there will be serious forex crunch and all will fall flat.
.
And FDI in defence means --- the day USA attacks India, we will have no option but to surrender, as none of the complex weapons made by American or Japanese factory owners will work. The real solution is that all weapons are made by 100% Indian staff, including chips and all code. And we are NOT taking any steps in that direction since 1991 and NaMo regime doesnt seem to be interested in that at all,.
.
