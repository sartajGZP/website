How leaders are capturing credit of work done by SM-activists? example --- Due to SM-activists and SMS, Goa CM Manohar Parikker cancelled Rs 90 lakh project of sending Goa BJP MLAs to Brazil to enjoy FIFA and now paidmedia wants to give credit to NaMo? Why do tall leaders want to steal credit? Why does paidmedia help them? Because elitemen DO NOT WANT ACTIVISTS TO KNOW THAT ACTIVISTS CAN BE SUCCESSFUL WITHOUT TALL LEADERS..
.
BJP Goa CM Manoher Parikker forced Goa sports dept secretary to approv Rs 90 lakh project in which 6 BJP MLAs were all set to go to Brazil to enjoy FIFA. The activists on SM started writing angry posts and many recallists in Goa started asking voters to order MLAs via SMS to stop this trip. Finally, Parikker was forced by SM and SMS to cancel this order..
.
Now paidmedia says that it was NaMo who called Parikker to change Parikker's mind !! IOW, work was done by SMS-activists and SM-activists. And paidmedia wants to give all credit to NaMo,.
.
This is just one another chapter in 5000 year old saga --- activists do all the work, and leaders come in the end to collect the credit using paidmedia. . In fact, mostly, tall leaders oppose the activists , try to suppress them, try to diffuse their movements and try to tire them as far as possible. And only when they fail, and the movement still keeps rising, then at the end moment , they come to collect the credit.
.
The reason why paidmedia helps them is because elietem do NOT want activists to know that activists can be successful without leaders.. The elitemen can control the leaders by bribes and threat of elimination\expose as they are few in numbers. But elitemen can never control activists by bribing or threats as they are simply too large in number. So elitemen promote agents like AK who will pretend that they are agents of commons and not of elitemen. The activists get fooled by paidmedia and start believing that these leaders are agents of commons and not elitemen. So activists follow such leaders. Even Swadeshi activists were fooled by paidmedia to believe that NaMo will work to finish MNC-owners domination in India !!
.
So elitemen use paidmedia to give ALL credit of all good things activists do, to tall leaders, be NaMo or AK or PVNR or MMS etc is to create a myth inside activists that , "activists cant be successful without a tall leader, and so to be successful they must first become andh bhagats of a tall leader" !! That way activists become andh-bhagat of tall leader, and now via threatening and bribing tall leaders, the activists direction can be controlled. .
.
So solution is that activists should realize that they should quit the leader who insists on compromise in the name of timing, vote collection, strategy etc etc. Just as SMS-activists and SM-activists could force Manohar Pariiker to cancel FIFA trip of 6 Goa MLAs, without dharana, anshan and without any tall leaders, they can bring many many changes without dharana, anshan and without any tall leaders
