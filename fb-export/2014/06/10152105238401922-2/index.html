The rise of AAP in Punjab is not one day story. Basically, it is 15 years of rampant misrule of Congress\SAD\BJP which has added up, Solution : see last para
.
To give an idea about how bad the misrule of Congress\SAD\BJP was, please see this Indian express link of 2007
.
archive.indianexpress . com / news/a-big-mac-index-of-tax-compliance-courtesy-ludhiana--------/202800/ (see comment for link )
.
====
... Finance Minister Manpreet Badal ... said that annual VAT paid by McDonald's outlet in Ludhiana, about Rs 1 crore, is greater than the collection from the entire food & beverage industry in Ludhiana. !!!!
====
.
Why was sales tax collection from other restaurants so low? Because Punjab was divided into sectors. And each sector was allocated to some Congress\BJP\SAD leader. And each restaurant owner used to give "sales tax" , not to Punjab Govt, but to that Congress\SAD\BJP leader !!! McDonanld insisted that they will pay tax only to Punjab Govt, and was allowed to do so !! And so McDonald collection exceeded all other restaurants put together !!! (The leaders must have extracted free burgers for that. And McDonald throws away 20 minute old unsold burgers and would have given those unsold burgers to these leaders) Later, computerization etc reduced rampantness in sales tax evasion. But the mafiagiri continued in other areas.
.
IOW, there has been years and years and years of organized loot and misrule in Punjab by Congress\SAD\BJP leaders. The drug problem we see is just one another link in the chain.
.
Punjab Congress\SAD\BJP are gone case. They are in 4th stage of cancer. There is NO way to fix these 3 parties. Punjab Congress\SAD\BJP are like spoilt drunk kids who simply do NOT want to improve. It would be cheaper to end them then mend them. Now if nationalists dont create a good alternative in Punjab, and keep shouting BJP slogans, then Punjab is all set to go into psuedos of AAP. And Haryana could be next. AAP = AK = YY will promote Missionaries, and dalits including dalits in sikhs, may get converted en-masse. Next step would be to drive them towards naxalism..
..
SOLUTION : :I request all nationalist not to waste time in BJP-pooja and focus on creating better nationalist alternatives. Even if you assume that NaMo is seven star good leader, then also except for NaMo, almost whole of BJP is rotten to core. Just because a party had some good past means nothing. For that matter, once SAD was sagely party, and now consists of Badal-mafia. And Congress too once had good leaders like Lal-bal-pal, Bose, Vallabhbhai Patel etc.
.
My request to all nationalists would be to focus on REAL law-drafts that can reduce drugs consumption, liquor consumption, land mafiagiri, corruption etc in Punjab and across India. Focusing on these law-drafts would expose AAP = AK, as AAP=AK would oppose them, and so they would get exposed.
.
