B.
FDI brings down cost of production but NOT the final sale prices. Why? Because weapons are monopoly items, And so price doesnt depend on cost. In monopoly, the item's manufacturing price can be as low as Rs 10, but monopoly owner may sell it for as high as Rs 100. So if a US company makes weapons in India, then cost will be cheaper, but then also US company will keep same high price as it has monopoly. Also, when war starts, it will increases prices by 10 times --- something an Indian company will not be able to do to that extent. So solution is to reduce infficiencies of local manufacturer using JurySys, wealth tax etc and not fall at US companies feet and beg them to make factiries in India.
