Because transmission companies dont have network to carry all the electricity they can produce to all Distribution Companies which need.
.
So Mukeshbhai\Tata and foreigners can very well now make a case to privatize Transmission companies.

Congress\BJP have always supported large scale privatization. In 1998-2004, BJP proudly privatized Hindustan Zinc to an Indian company, which was later taken over by British Company, and privatized 26% of IPCL to Mukeshbhai, and later allowed Mukeshbhai to buy 20% more via market. And BJP is notorious for giving away VSNL to Tata for a price less than 1/10th of plots VSNL owned , forget buildings, equipments etc.

Foreigners bought ICICI , HDFC got "taken overt" by foreigners in 2010, and not many people even know about it !!!

No wonder why AAP = AK is opposing means to improve efficiencies of PSUs, so that demand for privatzation of tansco and PSUs become popular.

Please oppose corrupt people Arun Shourie etc who always bat for privatization of everything and anything.
