Mohan Bhagwat's timewaste speech on Dashahera.
.
My sincere thanks to NaMo for ordering Doordarshan Chairman to show live telecast of Mohan Bhagwat = MB. We can see what timewaste MB is. .
.
Since ages, I have said two things --- almost all RSS-workers are highly committed nationalists which is why they joined RSS and work in RSS for free. But RSS-apex's goal is to waste away time and energy of workers and ensure that no real nationalist alternative come !!! Now , I will show from MB's own speech what a timewaste person he is
.
MB's speech is at tinyurl .com/MohanBhawatE . For Hindi, pls see /tinyurl .com/MohanBhagwat .
.
====
MB says : ... But we must also realize that all this is only a small step in the direction of our goal of making Bharat the 'Vishwa-Guru' of the world, a model nation that runs on the principles of happiness, peace and harmony.
..... Those who want to undertake this effort will have to first completely eradicate the selfishness, fear and absolute materialistic fundamentalism from their own hearts and adopt an integrated and holistic view that looks for happiness of one and all.
=====
.
Eradicate selfishness --- what a timewaste !! Its a good joke, except that enemy is not doing timewaste and so such timewaste can be fatal.
.
I read the speech twice. I didnt find any mention of demand of Central Govt law against gohatya. He demanded a ban on export of meat and export of cow meat. The only statement I saw was
.
====
MB says : We feel it necessary to put a ban on meat exports, beef in particular and cow smuggling in immediate future.
====
.
Well, cow meat export has been officially banned since ages. And as for illegal export, MB has proposed no law-drafts in past decades on how to stop it. Also, I dont think any BJP MP has demanded any law to completely ban meat export. When MB supported BJP in may-2014, MB refused to make a demand to BJP MPs to write in manifesto that "meat export will be banned". MB too has made speeches, but MB has NOT written any formal letter or sms to PM or MP demanding ban on meat export. IOW, there is wide gap between what MB speaks to audience and what MB demands before PM\MPs. Basically, it is just timewaste .
.
Now lets hear MB on Swadeshi.
.
====
MB says : It is necessary that policies of the government should take the nation towards self reliance and should encourage entrepreneurship among the people, but it is equally important for the people to encourage consumption of Swadeshi products. Our tendency of buying daily need goods, even buying foreign made statues of our Gods and Goddesses, simply because they are cheaper in price, needs to be abandoned.
====
.
IOW, he is asking that voters should buy "make in India" goods. But he doesnt resolve the dilemma of "whether goods made by a videshi company in India are swadeshi or not"? eg is McDonand burger made in India swadeshi? Are Dabar goods made in India swadeshi , given that Dabur is now owned by a German company ? Unless one makes a clear statement on "are goods made by videshi in India swadeshi or videshi", the whole swadeshi talk is just timewaste .
.
To give more details, lets say a Chinese company starts oindustrial park in India. It gets all made in China heavy machines into India. It gets all high tech engineers from China. It hires low tech unskilled Indian labor. It makes goods in India. It sells them in India. And takes the profit in dollars back to china. Is that swadeshi or videshi? MB refuses to answer this question. IOW, MB's swadeshi talk is timewaste . (My answer is given in rahulmehta .com/301.htm , search for word WOIC. My answer is --- a product made by Indians made in India and made in company 100% owned by Indians is Swadeshi Rest can be x% swadeshi and 100-x videshi. And I have proposed law-draft to guess the %. But this post is on MB.)
.
There was no demand for law-draft to remove govt control on temples. No demand for law-drafts to reduce LJ. No mention of temple gold moving to SBI vaults and being used to a pledge for repatriation.
.
Now over 1 crore RSS-workers and RSS-supporters spent some 1 hour in listening to this speech. Some went all the way to Nagur spending over 20-50 hours in travel. Many went from homes to neart by shakha, spending 2-3 hours in travel. To that, add cost of fuel. And what information they gained from speech? Nothing. All in all, committed activists ended up wasting time, money and energy.
.
So my thanks to NaMo for ordering DD to show MB-show, so that now all can see what timepass MB is. I would openly and officially request NaMo to order DD to show more and more of RSS-apex , VHP-apex etc etc so that we can know what timepass they all are.
.
Why is RSS-apex so timewaste? Well, RSS-apex in 1925 was started by Indian Kings like Scindhia who were British agents. The goal of British in ordering Scindhia to start RSS-apex was to create organization which will waste away time of committed youth. Later, BJP took control over RSS-apex, and since then RSS-apex has become bigger timewaste.
.
Solution I propose is that RSS-workers should ask RSS-apex to give law-drafts to reduce the mess, increase swadeshi, reduce videshi, reduce gohatya, reduce love jihad etc. And with that, the bubble of RSS-apex will burst. But oif workers dont demand law-drafts, then timewaste will go on and on and on.
