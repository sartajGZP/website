Why US is better suited to fight nuclear, missile, air-force war ---- lesser cities, fewer big dams
.
Cities reduces cost of living because they reduce transportation costs, and transportation time. And sparse living increases transporation time, transportation costs and thus lowers efficiency in a socirt. But if a nation has cities, then damage that can be done on that nation in a nuclear war or a conventional missile war increases. If the population is uniformly distributed across the nation, then the damage during nuclear war, missile war and figther plane war is least.
.
Likewise, large dams increase damage that can be done in in nuclear, missile and fighterplane wars. I have always big supporter of large dams. But after looking at war factor, I have changed my mind. We will have to settle for more expensive sources of power like solar, thermal , CNG etc. And in any case , we need a plan to rapidly empty the dams in case of war threat.
.
USA has been most war ready nation. It has huge amount of duplication in power grid, telephone land line grid , most sparse population, least number of dense cities, least number of dams and so forth.
.
We have a loooong way to walk to make India war ready.
