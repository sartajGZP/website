The early trends look like NaMo plans to give massive tax breaks to Japanese elitemen, the same step that Congress did for USUK elitemen.
.
Now Congress was bad. But a big mess came because of tax breaks Congress gave to USUK elitemen. This forced Govt to raise taxes on other Indian citizens. eg everytime a company in SEZ or Mauritius company gets tax breaks, someone else has to pay a tax at higher rate. Everytime a USUK elitemen gets govt lands at lower prices, some Indian has to pay land at higher price or pay more tax. Tax breaks is always zero sum game --- when X pays lower tax , Y ha sto pay higher tax.
.
So if NaMo plans to give huge tax breaks to Japanese and Korean elitemen, then --

(a) If Japanese elitemen turn out to be less bad than American elitemen, then NaMo will prove to be less bad than Congress

(b) If Japanese elitemen turn out to be as bad as American elitemen, then NaMo will prove to be as bad as Congress
