Many modelling agencies across India are trying to convince college boys and girls that they should stop attending colleges and focus on modelling , acting etc . And many parents of these boys and girls would be trying to convince their boys and girls that they should focus on college education and not modelling etc. With one stroke, NaMo has made lives of modelling agencies easy, and lives of these parents far far more difficult.
.
IMO, the kids should NOT take ANYONE has role models. But if India was a country where people could live without role models. then we would NOT have such dramatic rise of personalities like NaMo, Anna, AK. Sadly, we happen to be a country where not just kids, but even grown adults keep looking desperately for tall role models and wish to follow them without asking any questions. Those who dont need role models, are blessed. But if the kid is likely to fall for a role model, and if parents of those kids wanted their kid to study hard instead of chasing glamour world, then lives of those parents have become more difficult.
