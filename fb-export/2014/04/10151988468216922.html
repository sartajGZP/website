Similarities between BJP, AAP and Congress manifesto

1. All three manifesto are supporting unlevel field foreign companies have i.e. foreign companies in India via FDI dont have to pay income tax and many taxes and all three parties insist on keeping these nefrious laws in tact.

2. All three manifesto are silent on Kashi Vishvanath Devalaya and Krishna Janambhoomi Devalaya.

3. All three parties give tall talks, but none has even one DRAFT which would how the tall promises will be implemented, AAP proposed one Lokpal draft, which was the WORST form of centralization of punitive powers one can see, and a draft that would give India to foreign powers on a sliver platter. And AAP never gave drafts on any issue. And BJP, Congress too dont have any drafts

4. All three manifesto evade questions on expulsion of Bangladeshi

5. All these manifesto are silent on populaton control and rising demographic imbalance

6. All three drafts say "simplify tax rules" without giving an iota of detail

7. All three manifesto are silent about reducing rampant nepotism and corruption which goes in Supreme judges, High judges, lower courts, police etc.

8. All three manifesto ignore away question of rising harassment on women because Juvenile law, which says that criminal below 18 years, no matter how many crimes he committed and how serious they may be, will not be punished above 3 years !! Incidentally it was BJP which made this law in 2001, but Congress/AAP have never opposed it either

9. Certain differences are there, but they end up being equally shocking. eg BJP manifesto supports FDI in defence, which will make Indian weapon business at mercy of foreign companies. And Congress/AAP have no proposals to improve weapon manufacturing at all

All three parties gave manifesto at last minute, just few days before form filling started.

Manifesto have always been useless. But this election has taken uselessness of manifesto to a new height.
