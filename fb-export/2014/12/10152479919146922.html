Those who want to bring change in Govt etc , and ALSO want to use FB as a tool ------ please make two profiles ---- personal and "political". And use "political" profile ONLY for promote political changes you wish to bring and NO personal stuff like pictures, holiday pictures, fancy pictures with friends , gossips etc etc.
.
When you put non-political and personal stuff, the people who come to FB for serious political work simply "unfollow" you !! At least , thats what I do when a postor's over 50% status are non political. And I am sure many activists type people would do the same because they dont want their newsfeed to be flooded with fancy non-political stuff. So when you post politically important stuff, such serious people will simply miss it.
.
FB offers no easy solution. The simplest solution is -- please make TWO profiles. One personal and other to promote and work on political changes you wish to bring.
