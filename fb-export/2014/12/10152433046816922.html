While SoMoKe-andbhagats and CoBhApSwRa-workers are busy slogan shouting and net-bhakti, the activists at Right to Recall Group are distributing information to publicize the law-drafts that can fix courts, reduce inefficiencies of factory-owners etc etc.

Vineet Arora:

Today I distributed 4 copies of 301 book free of cost as a sample to 4 different booksellers of my area. Told them clearly any further copies of the book would be on cost.
Explaining them that we are a group of activists working on political, economic problems present in the country. Various problems along with their possible solutions are present in the book.
I told them about RTR law a how the same if was present in Delhi could have easily evaded any possibility of CWG scam. And how laws like RTR DCP, RTR Atorney General are present in US giving more power to citizens and thus keeping check on administration.
I was very well receieved by all except one where the person insiated that they only sell school books and cannot do anything for political economic cause(this much reluctance is anticipayed and accepted).
Over all it was really great exp. not taking more than an hour in all and I anticipate some book orders coming from them.
