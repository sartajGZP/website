I had met Dixitji only for 5-10 minutes in person and spoken on phone for about 2 hrs. Dixitji in april-2010 had told me that he had drafts for law-change for Vyavatha Parivartan , Swadeshi ready, and would release the drafts when BST has 1 crore members. BST crossed 1 crore marks on around 15-nov-2010 . And then I heard from a BST activist on around 20-nov-2014 that Dixitji has announced that they will soon release the drafts.
.
Only two persons in BST-apex were keen on drafts --- Swami Ramdevji and Dixitji. And only these two wanted to form a new political party. Many in BST didnt want to form a new political party and perhaps wanted to work with RSS / BJP . So they perhaps didnt want drafts to be released.Because drafts of system change would BJP / RSS in negative light as apex of both organization isnt interested in swadeshi or Vyavatha Parivartan
.
BST-workers too didnt want drafts etc. They only wanted slogan shouting. etc. So they too never asked for drafts etc.
.
Also, Dixtji was planning for mass-movement on black money, RTR , swadeshi etc. He had mentioned this in a mass meeting in Chhatisgadh on 28-nov-2010 , two days before he was murdered. While MNC-owners and Fraud Foundation wanted mass-movement on Janlokpal. MNC-agent Arvind Gandhi had held a mass meeting with Swami Ramdevji on around 15-nov-2010. Dixitji refused to come in that meeting because Arvind Gandhi was known MNC-agent. Later, Swamiji agreed NOT to support Janlokpal and moved out most likely due to explanation from Dixitji. And some 15 days later, Dixitji was murdered. Had Dixitji been there, he would have moved ahead with mass-movement for Swadeshi, RTR, Black Money and so mass-movement for Janlokpal would NOT have been so successful.
.
After Dixitji's murder, Swamiji had no one to steer BST. BST-workers too insisted that they should confine to slogan shouting. So Swamiji could not implement Dixtji's dreams. And finally, Swamiji decided to confine to congress-removal, by supporting BJP. But by feb-2014, MNC-owners could force BJP to completely ditch swadeshi and accept FDI in everything. So Dixitji's dreams got completely sidelined.
.
The most important reason why DIxitji's dreams went etc got trashed was because MOST of his followers , particularly in BST, decided NOT work on drafts but confine to tel sabun and confine to slogan shouting. Very few of his followers took his cause of vyavastha parivartan aka drafts etc.
.
Solution I propose to activists to focus on DRAFTS. I am NOT saying that should focus on my drafts. Please note --- I am NOT saying that they should focus on my drafts. They should focus on any drafts that they think will reduce problems of India. Draftless activism will only lead to victory of enemy. And FDI is nothing but a PROOF that enemy is winning and advancing.
