More on non-80G-activism aka true activism
.
Pls see my yesterday's post on activism. This is in continuation with that.

Activism can be paid 80G-activism like NGO paying for activism or fully non-80G-activism. This is about non-80G-activism ONLY.
.
There are 83 crore voters in India, of which say 20 lakh to 1 crore are willing to give say 4 hours a week of time and some 1% of their money for their pet cause. The remaining 83 crore minus those 1 crore to 20 lakhs wont even do that. All they will can do is give vote and/or moral support, which is a lot. But they wont give time or money for their cause, at least not in first round. And all over world, situation is more or less the same. So this isnt the reason why India is lagging.
.
Now some activists may be willing to give lot more time and lot more % of their incomes.
