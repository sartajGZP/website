303.073 : Sant Rampal case ---- how corrupt , nepotic judges and Missionary-paid-media are worsening inter-sect fights in Hindus
November 22, 2014 at 10:06pm

.

How (a) paidmedia \ Missionaries used mess created by three parties ------ (b) corrupt defunct judges (c) Sant Rampal + some of his followers (d) some followers of Dayanand Saraswati ---- to worsen inter-sect strife in Hindus.

.

And moral of the whole story ---- if we dont fix courts, by replacing judges by Jurors, then Hinduism will weaken due to inter-sect clashes.

.

========

.

Some 30 days back, I didnt know who Sant Rampal was !!! All information I gathered is from net in past few days.

.

Lets look at all parties and chain of events.

.

Sant Rampal criticized as well as used abusive language for Dayanad Saraswati. Some of his criticism may be sensible, while and some his statements are pure abuses in foul and inappropriate language.

.

The key thing is --- paidmedia started giving a HUGE coverage to Sant Rampal since year 2000. This obviously aggravated followers of Dayanad Saraswati.

.

Now some followers of Dayand Swaraswati would say "let the mad men shout , lets walk on with life". But some followers of Dayanand Sarawati decided to take some action.

.

=======

.

So there are following 8 people and groups of people.

.

1. SaRa = Sant Rampal

.

2. Followers of SR, who supported his criticism of Swami Dayannnd Saraswati but not his abusive language = SaRaF . This is important group of followers. They will not like abusive language , but if someone physically surrounds SaRa , then they will run to save him.

.

3. Followers of SaRa, who supported his criticism of Swami Dayand Saraswati = SaRaF-anti-SDR.

.

4. Followers of Swami Dayannnd Saraswati , who ignore such abuses --- SDSF-Ingore-SaRa

.

5. Followers of Swami Dayannnd Saraswati , who would propose legal action against Sara under section-295A IPC --- SDSF-anti-SaRa-295

.

6. Followers of Swami Dayannnd Saraswati , who would propose aggressive protests against SaRa outside his ashram

.

7. Corrupt nepotic judges.

.

8. paidmedia

.

=====

.

Now lets see at the long chain of events.

.

1. SaRa = Sant Rampal starts criticizing SDS = Swami Dayand Saraswati.

.

2. SaRa starts using abusive language for SDS along with some valid criticism.

.

3. Some SaRaF = SaRa followers would not like use of abusive language. Some followers cheer up because of their dislike for some specific persons who are SRS-followers.

.

4. Some SRSF would say "ignore SaRa". Some would insist on filing case against him under section-295A . Some insist on aggressive gherao. Here I would quote section-295A of IPC . It says "Deliberate and malicious acts, intended to outrage reli­gious feelings of any class by insulting its religion or reli­gious beliefs.—Whoever, with deliberate and malicious intention of outraging the religious feelings of any class of citizens of India, by words, either spoken or written, or by signs or by visible representations or otherwise, insults or attempts to insult the religion or the religious beliefs of that class, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both. "

.

5. MNC-Missionary-paid paidmediamen give huge coverage to SaRa .

.

5A. If the paidmediamen were 100% paid by Indians, then the paidmediamen would not have given such huge coverage to SaRa unless SaRa drops abuses and confined to criticism. Why? A paidmediamen funded by Indians would have fear of shunning and boycott by a huge section of population. But most of the existing paidmediamen in India are paid by foreigners and so paidmediamen are not worried about boycott. IOW, FDI in paidmedia adds fuel to such clashes. .

.

5B. Now guess who allowed FDI in paidmedia and guess which parties' MPs are supporting FDI in paidmedia as of today?

.

5C. And please note that SoMoKe, SoMoKe-andhbhagats are worsening the situation by supporting FDI in paidmedia. Some SoMoKe-andbhagats may be against FDI in paidmedia. But by supporting SoMoKe , their actions are supporting FDI in paidmedia and this worsending the situation.

.

6. The corrupt nepotic judges took no action for verbal abuses SaRa was throwing.

.

6A. If the courts were run by Jurors, then Jurors would have PROMPTLY imposed punishment on SaRa for using abusive language. I cant speak for all Jurors. If I were in Jury , the punishment I would propose would be (Re 1 fine, 1 day in prison) for FIRST offence, and the double the fine and double the number of days every next time. One can see that by 32 repetitions, even Bill Gates would drop abusive language. I cant say what other Jurirs would do. But across countries in the world, the Jurors have imposed mild punishments on verbal abusers to a point that verbal abuses end. Thats because Jurors want amicable peace , and also dont want end to free speech. So Jurors permit valid criticism and punish foul speech. What is valid and what is foul? That is discretion of each Juror.

.

6B. But we have courts run not by Jurors by corrupt nepotic judges. So no punishments happen under section-295A.

.

7. So some SDSF = Swami Dayanand Saraswati followers resorted to angry protest outside SaRa's ashram. IMO, they had wrongly gheraoed the Satlok ashram. So please note --- that IMO the protest was legitimate , but IMO, gheraoing the ashram was blatantly unethical.

.

7A. Again, if the courts were run by Jurors , then SaRaF = Sant Rampal's followers' would have resort to court complain and not direct fight. If I were in jury, again, my punishments for such gherao would be (Rs 1 fine , 1 day in prison) and double it every time. And such gherao would end.

.

7B. But again, there were no Jurors and we only have corrupt nepotic judges. And so rounds of fights between Sant Rampal followers = SaRaF and SDSF = Swamy Dayanand Saraswati followers grew.

.

8. Now each round of physical fight , unpunished by courts, will get more and more from both sides. eg say there are SDSF who want to ignore SaRa. They will initially stay away from protest. And say there are SaRaF who oppose abusive language for SDS. They will also initially stay away. But if physical fight breaks out between SDSF-protesters and SaRaF-anti-SDS , and courts punish none, then all SaRaF followers will run to support other SaRaF and all non-protesting SDS-followers would run to support other protesting SDS-followers.

.

9. So next round of conflicts were much bigger !!

.

10. In july-2006, many SDS-followers gheraoed SaRa-ashram. They encirled the ashram and no one was allowed to go in, and supplies of food , electricity, water , diesel (to run the generators they had) etc were blocked !! This was an aggressive gherao. For details, google ob "satpal ashram july 2006". This action IMO was wrong

.

11. So SaRa-followers retaliated. Some 1-3 persons died (some reports say 1 person died, some say 3 persons died).

.

12. The judge summoned SaRa in year 2008 in the case of the person who had died. SaRa did appear before the judge. Please note --- SaRa did appear before the judge in year 2008.

.

13. The judge kept SaRa in prison for several months. Please note again. The judge did keep Sant Rampal in prison for some 21 months. And the judge didnt dispose the case.

.

13A. Again. if there was Jury System, the case judgement would have come in weeks. But SaRa remained in prison for several months. And judge didnt resolve the case.

.

14. Then judge gave bail to SaRa. SaRa's supporters rightly lost faith in judges due to the fact that judges didnt end the case in 6 long months. Also, judges across India are highly corrupt and nepotic. So SaRa and his supporters got a valid excuse in saying that "we wont appear in the court". Further, there was no direct involvement of SaRa in the case filed against him. SaRa's followers had attacked those who had encircled the ashram and were blocking people and supplies from going in and out. And police was helping those who had encirled the ashram. So SaRa-followerers had excuse of self defence.

.

15. Sant Rampal's supporters agreed to defy court summons. They surrounded the Ashram. So finally, 25000 policemen were needed to remove them and arrest Sant Rampal.

.

16. paidmedia says that Sant Rampal's disciplies were shotting at policemen and throwing petrol bomns on policemen. If 25000 policemen have surrounded a 12 acre plot, and if people inside are shooting using guns, and throwing petrol bombs, then there must have been many police casulties. Please check how many policemen died or got seriously injured !!!

.

========

.

The allegations on violence on Sant Rampal even if true are indirect "retalitory" i.e. his supported had acted and not himself, and his supporters acted after they were encircled by protestors. Sant Rampal's supporters didnt initiate the violence. Sant Rampal did initiate the verbal abuse , for which followers of Dayand Saraswati could have filed a case under section-295A IPC. The followers of Swamy Dayanand Saraswati were wrong in their action of encircling the Satpal Ashram. So allegations against Sant Rampal will NOT lower image of Sant Rampal in his followers. And the judges who are being used to crush followers of Sant Rampal and his ashram have no legitimacy in society due to rampant corruption and rampant nepotism. "The judges should be respected at all cost" type arguments are baseless when judges are extremely corrupt.

.

For whatever reasons, followers of Sant Rampal took him as Guru because they didnt find any less bad or better Guru. So it is possible to crush Sant Rampal using force via corrupt and nepotic judges. But then , chances are high that followers of Sant Rampal will turn to Missionaries for spiritual counciling !! This will only strengthen Missionaries and further weaken Hinduism

.

Also, the strife amongst various Hindu sects will grow. The FDI-paid paidmediamen will keep on projecting leader of sect-A who abuses figueres of sect-B and so on.

.

======

.

SOLUTION I PROPOSE

.

Solution I propose is that should use Jury Trials to deal with all such disputes. A jury of 1500 voters across India from voters list of India , minus Haryana , should be chosen at random. The age should be between 35 years and 45 years. And they should decide the punishment or acquital in such inter-sect clashes. That will ensure that complaints are promptly and justly addressed.
