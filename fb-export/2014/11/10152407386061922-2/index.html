Some important questions in "activism for law-drafts" , and my answers
.
Following are the questions we should be asking to every activist , and following are my answers :---
.
1. What law-drafts do you propose to reduce the mess. And please give links to the FULL drafts.
.
My answer is --- law-drafts such as RTR, JurySys, TCP, wealth tax, DDMRCAM, labor or agent of labor should deposit PF, TDS , etc and many such law-drafts. The links are in rahulmehta . com /301.htm
.
====
.
2. What activities do you propose 43 crores to 83 crore voters should do, that would convince MPs who wish to listen for 40 hours a week and staff of 2 persons working 40 hours a week, that over 43 crore voters do support and demand the law-draft you propose? Time limit of 40 hours a week and staff limit is important. If one suggests activity that would require MP to spend 100 hours a week and hire a staff of 500, then such activity should be dismissed as useless.
.
My answer --- The activity I propose to 43 crore to 83 crore voters is to send orders to MPs via SMS containing URL to the law-draft .
.
====
.
3. What activities do you propose for 20 lakh activists that would ensure that some 43 crore to 83 crore voters will know about law-drafts you proposed in (1) and activities you suggested in (2) ? And please note ---- most activists have pet causes and they wont work on law-draft unless it is clear to them that their pet causes will be benefitted by the law-draft set you cited .
.
My answer : The law-drafts I have proposed cover some 100+ pet causes such as improving Military , reducing poverty, reducing corruption, reducing conversion without use of force, strengthening hindu sects, reducing higher playing field given to foreign companies, reducing inefficiencies of factory owners, expelling Bangladeshis, reducing crimes against women, improving courts, improving police, go raksha etc etc. All pet causes , except neta-pujan. IOW, there is for something all activists , unless that activist is neta-pujak or that activist has some anti-India goals. So via these 20 lakh activists, I propose to take the information on law-drafts and information on "order your MP via SMS" activity to 43 crore voters to 83 crore voters.
.
Some background to question-3 : There are only two channels to reach --- 43 crore voters --- (a) paidmedia or (b) 20 lakh activists. There is NO third way to reach 43 crore to 83 crore voters . Now SoMoKe have decided to depend on paidmedia to reach 43 crore to 83 crore voters. We dont have that option, as we support proposals like wealth tax, RTR doordarshan chairman , DDMRCAM etc.If an activist is aiming to get attention of paidmedia, then a good activist should shun him ASAP. The only other media is 20 lakh activist. So the activist must have goal to take his proposals to 20 lakh activists in India.
.
======
.
4. What activities do you propose for 20 - 2000 activists you personally know, that would ensure that they will pass the information on law-drafts and activity list to activists they know and then activists they know.
.
Reaching 43 crore to 83 crore voters is possible via 20 lakh activists or via paidmedia. I am not interested in a plan based on paidmedia. I am only interested in knowing plan which goes via 20 lakh activists. And only way to reach 20 lakh activist is via 20-2000 activists you know, who will take it to 20-2000 activists you know, and so forth.
.
So what activity list you propose to activists you know
,
My answer : My request to them is
.
(a) contest elections on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(b) give newspaper advt on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(c) distribute pamphlets on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(d) make important activists as FB close friends and read - share all their status posts
(e) use FB etc to spread info law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(f) hold meeting in gardens , personal offices
(g) make YT vidoes
.
========
.
4. And finally, what activities do you do that would ensure that information on law-drafts and order your MP via SMS campaign passes to activists you know?
.
My answer : I do following activities
.
(a) contest elections on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(b) give newspaper advt on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(c) distribute pamphlets on law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(d) make important activists as FB close friends and read - share all their status posts
(e) use FB etc to spread info law-drafts and order MP via SMS
(f) hold meeting in gardens , personal offices
(g) make YT vidoes
.
=============================
.
IOW, activism on a cause is about taking information about solutions and means to 43 crore to 83 crore voters via activists and NOT via paidmedia.
.
And that is possible ONLY via 20 lakh activists. And no activist-A can fool or convince other activist-B to give away pet cause of activist-B and work on cause of activist-A. To fool activist-B, you would need paidmedia and thats a non-option as far as I go. So the activism program must include causes of at least 20 lakh activists !!! Any activism program that doesnt include causes dear to at least 20 lakh activists will vanish or get eventually hijacked by paidmedia, So I request activists NOT to work on a program that doesnt involve causes that appeal to at least 20 lakh activists across India. And there should be activity list which activist and voters can do, within their limited time and money. And the activity list must have activities that can create a proof to MPs who wish to listen. As per MPs who dont want to listen, there is no need to worry about them.
.
And activist must ask fellow activist to provide his list of law-drafts as well as provide list of activities he proposes for voter and fellow 20 lakh activists
.
I at RRG has given list of law-drafts and list of activities. We need to get answers from others. .
