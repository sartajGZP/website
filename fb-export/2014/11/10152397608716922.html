Dear recallists,

Thos who are following my status posts etc on wealh tax, taxation issues etc.

I have made following changes in the wealth tax proposal.
.
I have added a concept called as WTC = Wealth tax credit.
.
The start value will be taxes a person \ company has paid in past 4 years plus 15% of salaries he has paid in past 4 years. This will include income tax, net service tax, excise, sales tax and vat.
.
The wealth tax payable next year will be = 3% of market value of wealth above 250 sqft of land and 500 sqft of construction per person, minus 30% of actual salaries paid minus income tax payable - minus WTC.
.
If the number is negative , then it will become WTC for next year.
.
So say a person has office worth Rs 10 crore, And in past 4 years, income tax etc he has paid is Rs 30 lakhs. Say this year, his income is zero, The wealth tax credit = Rs 30 lakhs. And wealth tax payable this year will be zero.
.
For rented property, if the person has been staying there for over 12 months, then for 1 year, the wealth tax will be 30% of rent and later it will be 3% of market value of property
.
WTC will ensure that if a person has been paying income tax for long, then for rest of his retired life wealth tax on him will be near zero. WTC will not be inherited. And with 3% wealth tax, there is no need for inheritance tax.
.
But if person has been hoarding land, and generating no income, then wealth tax will force him to sell or rent his rent.
.
This will bring down land prices and will reduce COST OF DEVELOPMENT, and enable India to develop without FDI.
.
