Financial support to poor via mineral royalty vs Financial support to poor via income tax vs Protection to workers from expulsion
.
There are several aspects in so called "welfare state". Two of which are - giving financial support to poor / elders / disabled etc and giving protection to workers against expulsion
.
And there can be two sources of money needed to give financial support to poor --- royalty from natural resources and income tax on employers.
.
So cite positive and negative points of
.
1) Financial support to poor via mineral royalty
.
2) Financial support to poor via income tax
.
3) Protection to workers from expulsion
