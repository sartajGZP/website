(A) Top 8 Law drafts I support most
------------------------------------------
.
(A.01) DdMrcam – Once this law is printed, about 65% of mineral royalties and land rent from GoI plots will DIRECTLY get deposited in the bank accounts of the citizens of India. Poverty will vanish in 3 months. Vote at newindia.in/causes/DdMrcam . Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154642721181922
.
(A.02) WTMITC – Wealth Tax : The proposal is to levy 1% tax on market value of land / construction per YEAR, MINUS 2000 sqft of construction per family, MINUS income tax and other taxes paid , MINUS 15% of salaries paid to employees and minus Rs 200 per year per follower of religious or charitable trusts. This law imo will reduce land prices and increase businesses and decrease unemployment. Vote at newindia.in/WealthTax. Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154511306361922
.
(A.03) CancelGST : GST kills small/ medium traders and manufacturers due to "double taxation on small trader problem", and also due to "missing trader problem" . So GST can be completely scrapped and replaced by Wealth Tax (not excise, VAT, CST). Vote at newindia.in/CancelGST . Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154529102801922
.
(A.04) Right to Recall District Education Officer : This proposed law will enable voters of a district to expel / replace District Education Officer. This law imo will improve education in govt schools. Vote at newindia.in/RtrDeo2 . Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154485412051922
.
(A.05) EIAR – Expelling (Bangladeshi and other) Infiltrators and Allowing Refugees: This proposed law imo will enable citizens of India to expel Bangladeshi INFILTRATORS. Vote at newindia.in/causes/eiar . Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154735766456922

(A.06) Two Child Law : This proposed law will enforce two child restriction on couples. But daughters in many cases of 2+ children get "exemptions" from fines. Vote at newindia.in/TCL . Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154656605436922
.
(A.07) SSDMT - Sanatan Sampraday Devalaya Management Trust : Imo, this proposed law will free Hindu temples from govt control. And the law also has provision that Trustees can have SGPC like structure over temple, in which temple devotees will elect / recall temple heads. Vote at newindia.in/causes/ssdmt . Details fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154735724996922
.
(A.08) Transparent Complaint Filing Procedure : This proposed law will enable any and every citizen of India to put his complaints etc on PM's website. And anyone having same complaint can add his name to complaint via SMS or visiting Patwari office. Vote at newindia.in/causes/Tcp2. Details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154682688171922
