On all fruits/vegetables costing more than say Rs 10 per piece, we should encourage farmers to put labels, which has longnitude / latitude of center of his farm and also farmer's aadhar card number
.
The law should only state --- a farmer will be FREE NOT to put any such label. But NO ONE except farm owner can put such label. And a farmer cannot put label on fruits / vegetables which are NOT grown in his farm. And if a box has label, then ALL items in that box must be from same farm. And no one except farmer can put any such label.
.
So what would be minus points of such labels?
.
One minus point is that patriotic buyers may start boycotting apples grown in farms of secessionist minded farmers in JK.
.
But a plus point is --- each farmer becomes a brand name !!! And brand name has value. Now whatever quality checkes he puts, those who believe in his brand will be benefitted.
