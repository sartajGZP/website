section-01 : Name of the Party , and general note about constitution
.
(1.1) The name of the parfy shall be Right to Recall Party. In case that name is not approved by Election Commission, then the proposed names in order will be (a) Prajaadheen Shashak Party (b) Mahatma Sachindranath Sanyal Party. In case all three names are rejected by Election Commission, then Party Head will decide the name
,.
(1.2) The alternate name "Prajaadheen Shashak Party" is because chap-6 of Satyarth Prakash by Swami Dayanand Saraswatiji says "Raja should be Prajaadheen , and if Rajavarg is not prajaadheen, then it will rob citizens and nation will colapse". And alternate name "Mahatma Sachindranath Sanyal Party" is because Mahatma Sachindranath Sanyal in 1925 had said that "In the Indian republic we wish to form, the citizens shall have right to recall respresentatives, because without Right to recall, democracy will become a mockery".
.
-----------
.
section-02 : Meaning of the word 'may' in this constitution
.
(2.1) The word 'may' in this constitution means 'may or need not' and means ' no binding'.
.
(2.2) The Party here means Right to recall Party or wherever name it gets after registration
.
----------
.
section-03 : Objectives of the Party
.
(3.1) General Objectives

(3.1.1) The Party Shall bear true faith and allegiance to the constitution of India as by law established and to the principle of socialism, secularism and democracy and would uphold the sovereignty,unity and integrity of India
.
(3.1.2) Following the Preamble to our Constitution, the Party envisions an India, free of corruption, which is Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic
.
(3.1.3) The Party Party resolves to secure for all the citizens: Justice, social economic and political - for everyone including the last person, Liberty of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship, Equality of status and of opportunity in all spheres of life, for individuals and communities. And to promote among them all Fraternity assuring the dignity of the individual and the Unity and integrity of the Nation.
.
(3.2) Specific Objectives
.
(3.2.1) The party members will work to inform all 85 crore voters of India about Right to recall laws, Jury System laws, referendum laws and multi-election systems as it is prcaticed in USA and several countries. And members will inform about these laws' merits. The members will also inform all 85 crore voters of India about merits of wealth tax and demerits of indirect taxes such as GST, excise etc. All in all, the party members will work inform all 85 crore voters of India about laws countries across world had from 2000 BC till date , and their merits

(3.2.2) The party members will work to inform voters of India about proposed law-draft which can make India as strong as the strongest country in the world, in all respect --- defence, manufacturing etc. The party members will work to inform voters about proposed law-drafts such as TCP (Transparent Complaint Filing Procedure) , Right to Recall over all positions in govt, Jury System, wealth tax and several such proposed law-drafts.

(3.2.3) The party members will work to obtain cout of number of voters who have supported the proposed laws and also work to inform all voters about this count.
.
(3.2.4) The party members will work to cotest elections at all levels so that these laws can passed in Loksabha / Assembly etc.
.
(3.2.5) The party promises to ask its MPs / MLAs to pass these law-drafts , namely Right to Recall law-drafts, Jury System law-drafts, wealth tax law-drafts etc in Parliament and Assembly.
.

.
section-04 : What party doesnt NOT promise
.
(4.1) The party, till the laws change, does NOT promise voters that Party can force majority MPs / MLAs to keep promises. Thats because existing law allows MPs / MLAs to appoint the party whip by majority, and so majority can appoint the whip as they wish.
.
(4.2) Existing law doesnt empower Party to force an MP / MLA to resign from Loksabha / Assembly. The existing law only enables the Party to expel an MP / MLA from Party. Existing law also enabled the Party to control MP / MLA inside Loksabha / Assembly, by appointing whip and using whip using anti-defection law. But if majority MPs / MLAs change the whip, then Party heads or Party members have no way to control MPs / MLAs in existing law.
.
(4.3) So the Party makes no promise to voters that majority will keep their election promises.
.
(4.4) The party will work to bring laws which will enable voters to expel MPs who refuse to keep their poll promises. But as of today, no such such law exists. And so Party makes no promise that the Party will be able to force majority MPs to keep promises
.
(4.5) The 85 crore voters will have to use whatever non-violent, legal, ethical means they have at disposal to ensure that majority MPs of the Party do keep their poll promises, or resign promptly. The Party members and heads may assist them. within the framework of non-violence, laws and ethics. The sha1hash and bitorrenthashes of the law-drafts that the party supports are given in appendix-1.
.
(4.6) The Party will use whip only when legislation in house is one stated in this constitution or in manifesto, Otherwise the whip will be based on orders issued by citizens via sms / twitter And if majority voters have ordered an MP to act in a specifuic way, then whip will not be issued on him.
.
--------------
.
section-05 : Membership and freedom of members to campaign against Party
.
(5.1) Any voter of India can be become member of Party by declaring that he is member. If he is using Facebook / Twitter etc, then he must mention so on FB / twitter and cite his voter number. If he has mobile, then he must also send SMS to party that he is member and mention his voter number
.
(5.2) Different members will have different Points in decision making. The Points are described in next section
.
(5.3) The Party cannot expel a member. A member may resign. And he may re-join later. His points will NOT be effected.
.
(5.4) The Party will NOT take any disciplinary action against any member for any action. If the member has committed a crime, then any party member may file case against him. But Party will not take any action
.
(5.5) The members will be free to contest election as independent or even any other party and will be free to campaign for other candidates and free to campaign against the Party candidates. The member's points will not be effected or decreased
.
(5.6) The members will be free to form a parallel Party with same of other goals.
.
-----
.
section-06 : Contribution points
.
(6.1) Each member will get points based on items mentioned in this section. The point counting will start from 1-jan-2008 i.e. past constributions will be taken into account
.
(6.2) If a member contests election i.e. his form passes and he doesnt withdraws, then he will get points equal to twice the deposit amount, irrespective of number of votes he gets
.
(6.3) For each vote he gets, he will get points equal to (deposit amount /100) * number of votes
.
(6.4) For each newspaper advertisement, the member will get lower of the two -- 5 times the govt approved rate for that newspaper, page and size and actual rupee amount paid for that newspaper advt by cheque / DD.
.
(6.5) The member will get points equal to money he has paid to YouTube for promotion of video approved by the Party National Head,. The payment must be made via Party or in a way which can be fully verified that said amount has been paid, and for that specific video. The limit on this will be 10000 points per member per year.
.
(6.6) Every voter who puts the FB coverpage as mentioned by the Party will get 1 point per week he keeps that cover page. In such case, the voterid must be written in "about" -> "details" section of his facebook profile, and other details too must be specified. The list of details are in appendix-2
.
(6.7) For every member who puts a profile like above, the member who has guided him will get 20% of FB points he gets. The guide's voterid must be specified in the "about" -> "details" section of the member. And the guide of the guide will get 20% of points the gude gets and so on.
.
(6.8) Every voter who puts the Twitter coverpage as mentioned by the Party will get 1 point per week he keeps that cover page. In such case, the voterid must be written in "about" section of his twitter profile, and other details too must be specified. The list of details are in appendix-3
.
(6.9) For every member who puts a profile like above, the member who has guided him will get 20% of twitter points he gets. The guide's voterid must be specified in the "about" -> "details" section of the member. And the guide of the guide will get 20% of points the gude gets and so on.
.
(6.10) For every law-draft that the Party wants, if a member supports that law-draft on FB in a specific format, then he will get 1 to 5 points, one time (not per week). The points will be removed when he stops support that law on FB
.
(6.11) For every law-draft that the Party wants, if a member supports that law-draft on FB in a specific format, then he will get 1 to 5 points, one time (not per week). The points will be removed when he stops support that law on FB
.
(6.6) On 1st of each month, the points will get multiplied by percentage increase in money supply by RBI that happened 3 months back.
.
(6.6) There will be no points for pamphlet distribution, no points for newspaper coverage other than officially paid advertisements, no points for free or paid news / editorials, no points fo coverage on TV and internet excepy YouTube and so on
