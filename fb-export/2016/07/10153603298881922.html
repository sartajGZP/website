Should we say "SoMoKe-andhbhagats oppose RTR / Jury/ WT / referendum law-drafts" or should we say say "SoMoKe-supporters oppose RTR law-drafts"?
.
(WT = wealth tax)
.
I get too many messages that I should not use andhbhagats and instead use word supporters. So let me clairufy
.
(1) Each recallist is free to use the word he wishes. I dont ask anyone to use word andhbhagat or supporter
.
(2) If a person is a mere SoMoKe-supporter and NOT SoMoKe-andhbhagats, then he would have supported RTR-law-drafts. So IMO, it is WRONG to say that "SoMoKe-supporters oppose RTR-law-drafts".
.
(3) The SoMoKe-andhbhagats oppose RTR-law-drafts, because they know that if RTR-law-drafts become know to too many voters, then image of SoMoKe will fall because SoMoKe have always opposed RTR , JurySys , WT, referendum etc,.
.
So IMO, statement like "SoMoKe-supporters oppose RTR / Jury/ WT / referendum law-drafts" is WRONG, while statement that "should we say SoMoKe-supporters oppose RTR / Jury etc law-drafts" is correct.
.
So depending on context, one has to decide whether he wants to use word andhbhagat or supporter.
.
I generally confine to RTR-law-drafts. So mostly, the word "andhbhagat" is more suited.
.
Each postor can decide on his own.
