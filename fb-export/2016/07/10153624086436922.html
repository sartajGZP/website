My opinions

(1) The recallable CM or PM will decide first compensationj offrer. If reciever wants more, then Jury of 100 voters chosen at random , per care, with max Jury size of 1500 voters should decide compensation. If I am in Jury, my compensation will be linked with INCOMES they can earn from that land, and NOT linked with value of minerals below the ground. eg if family of 5 farmers were earning say Rs 200,000 a year, and five landless can earn rs 120,000 a year, then income form land is Rs 80000 a year. so compensation has to be 20 times the income, and no more.
.
(2) After compensation, if farmers / tribals refuse to evacuate, then TcpState \ TcpIndia should decide final outcome. here, my vote will be YES i.e. go ahead with forceful evacuation
