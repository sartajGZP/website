Proposed complete re-organization of juduiciary
.
(1) pls click my cover picture. pls click link to the group "law drafts I support". And pls see pinned post in that group
.
(2) I have proposed following procedures to fix judiciary in courts
.
(2a) RtrSCCj - Right to Recall Supreme Court Chief judge
(2b) RtrHCCj - Right to Recall High Court Chief judge
(2c) RtrDCPj - Right to Recall District Court Principal judge
(2d) DcJurySys - District Court Jury System
(2e) HcJurySys - High Court Jury System
(2f) ScJurySys - Supreme Court Jury System
(2g) TcpDistrict - Decision of District Court Jury can be cancelled by TcpDistrict
(2h) TcpState - Decision of State High Court Jury can be cancelled by Assembly or TcpState
(2i) TcpIndia - Decision of Supreme Court Jury can be cancelled by Parialment or TcpIndia
.
Juries will consist of 15 to 1500 voters between age of 25 years to 55 years chosen from District / State or India depending on type of court and case
.
====
.
(3) Hierarchy is
.
DCJurySys < HcJurySys < ScJurySys < Parliament < TcpIndia
.
TcpDistrict < TcpState < TcpIndia
.
DcJurySys < TcpDistrict
.
HcJurySys < TcpState

===
.
(4) Chief judges in SC , HC and DC are recallable
.
All junior judges in District / Tahsil Courts are recruited by writtens exams and no interview
.
All HCjs are promoted from District judges by discretion of CM and Assembly
.
All SCjs are promoted from State High Courts by discretion of PM and Parliament
.
No lateral entry in judiciary except chief judges
.
All existing judges from Magistrates to SCCj will be expelled, and pension will be set to Re 1 per month, including medical and ALL benefits. I am serious --- my proposal is to expel ALL existing judges from SCCj to Magistrates.and replace Chief judges by recallable judges and other judges via written exams.
