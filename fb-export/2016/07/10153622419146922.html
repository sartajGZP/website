(1) [ general instruction ] The word citizen would mean a registered voter residing in India. The word 'may' clearly means 'may or need not' and also clearly means 'not binding'. Approval filing will be open i.e. choices given by a voter, along his voter-id and name, will be visible to all, on a designated website.
.
(2) [ instruction to DC = District Collector] if citizen of India wishes to become Minister in-charge of a Dept in Central Govt such as Chemicals, Petro Chemicals, Education, Transportation, Railways or any dept mentioned in the list at the end of this GN, and he appears in person or via a lawyer with affidavit before DC, then the Secretary or Office would accept his candidacy for Minister of that dept after taking filing fee twice as deposit amount for MP election.
.
(3) [ instruction to Talati, or Talati's Clerks ] If a citizen of India comes in person to Talati's office, pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five persons for the position of a specific Ministry, then the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved.
.
(4) [ instruction to Talati ] The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on PM's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences. So approval filing is open.
.
(5) [ instruction to Talati ] If a the citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(6) [ instructions to DC, PM] DC may create a system which will send SMS feedback to the voter. And PM may create a system where approvals can be registered over ATM or over SMS.
.
(7) [ instructions to DC] On every 5th of month, DC may publish Approval counts for each candidate as on last date of the previous month.
.
(8) [ instruction to PM ] If a candidate gets approval of over 43 crore voters, and it is 1 crore more than approval count of existing Minister, then PM may or need not expel the existing Minister and may or need not appoint the person with highest approval count as new Minister of that dept. The decision of PM will be final.
.
