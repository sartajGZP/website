PLD.0034.RtrHCCj - Right to Recall High Court Chief judge
.

(1) [ general instructions ] The word citizen would mean a registered voter in the State. The word 'may' means 'may or need not', and clearly means 'no binding'. HCCj means High Court Chief judge. SCCj means Supreme Court Chief judge. SCj means a Supreme Court judge.
.
(2) [ proedure for DC = District Collector ] If any citizen of India above age of 30 years wishes to become SRJ = State Recognized Jurist, and he appears in person or via a lawyer with affidavit before DC, then DC would accept his candidacy for SRJ after taking filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election. Any existing judge in India may register himself as SRJ candidate for no fee.
.
[ comment - clauses-3 to clauses-5 are similar to RtrPM law-draft ]
.
(3) [ procedure for Talati = Patwari = Village Officer or Talati's Clerks ]
.
(3.1) If a citizen comes in person to Talati's office, pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most ten persons for the SRJ position, then the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he has approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card.
.
(3.2) If a citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(3.3) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter.
.
(3.4) DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(3.5) PM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. PM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM.
.
(4) [ procedure for Talati ] The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences.
.
(5) [ procedure for DC ] On every Monday, DCs will publish Approval counts for each candidate.
.
(6) [ procedure for CM ] If a candidate gets approval of over 20% of registered citizen-voters in State, then CM may appoint him as SRJ for that State.
.
(7) [ procedure for CM ] If an SRJ gets approval of over 51% of all voters in the state and the Approval count is 5% than approval count of existing HCCj, then CM may send the name of the most approved SRJ to the Chief Judge of India asking him if he is appropriate for the position of HCCJ. CM may also send the name to the PM. PM may send the name to SCCj.
.
(8) [ procedure for PM , all Loksabha MPs]
.
(8.1) If the SCCj and every other SCjs recommend that the most approved SRJ should be new HCCj and the existing HCCj resigns or is transferred, within 30 days, then and then only the PM and SCCj may appoint that SRJ as new HCCJ.
.
(8.2) However, if any one of the Supreme Court judge refuses to accept appointment of that SRJ as HCCJ, or gives no response within 30 days, then PM may cancel his recommendation, and PM and all Loksabha MPs may resign and may declare new Loksabha election. Or, PM and MPs may declare an Emergency as per our Magnum Opus Constitution of India, and temporariliy suspend all the Fundamental Rights, of all citizens of India, including all Supreme Court judges. The decision of PM and MPs will be final.
.

(CV.1)[ procedure for DC ] If any citizen wants a change in this law-draft, he may submit an affidavit at DC's office and DC or his clerk will post affidavit on CM's website for Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) [ procedure for Talati or Patwari ] If any citizen want to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to affidavit submitted in above clause, Talati will enter YES/NO and give him a receipt for Rs 3 fee. The YES-NO will be posted on CM's website.
