What is missing trader problem?
.
In VAT = GST, say trader or manufacturer A sells goods to B for say price of Rs 1000 and later B sells to C for say Rs 1050. Now say VAT = GST rate is say 20%. Then A will collect Rs 200 GST from B and B will collect Rs 210 GST from C. Then A will have to deposit Rs 200 to govt and B will have to deposit Rs 10 to Govt.

BUT, if A vanishes, then for all practical purposes, B will have to pay Rs 200 to govt again !!!
