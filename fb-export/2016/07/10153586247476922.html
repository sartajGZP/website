.

There is one more xample where law-passing happened without election contesting, Across Induia, in 1947-48, right after freedom, several communist party activists started agitation for land reforms i.e. taking land from landowners who have 1000s of acres of land and giving it to the landless. To stop them, in many states such as AP, Orissa, UP, Bihar, Kerala, WB etc the Congress leaders decided to massacre communist party workers en-masse. In Gujarat, Congress leaders decided to hijack the agitation by talking about land reforms !!! And so finally Congress leaders got hijacked and they were forced to implement land reforms. In Kerela, the communist party workers won elections and got dismissed by Congress govt in center and then congress started to implement land reforms. In WB, the communist party workers won election in 1960s and started land reforms. In rest of India, communist party workers could not get elected and land reforms didnt happen either.

IOW, this is one more example to show that agitation and mass movement can inded bring REAL change without election contesting.
