"Tax and distribute tax money" vs RRG-law-drafts
.
Several "tax the rich and distribute the tax money" proposals are coming. I at RRG oppose this tax and distribute proposals. We at RRG have proposed following law-drafts to reduce poverty --- DDMRCAM, wealth tax, removal of GST / VAT / Excise , streamlining income tax, deemed auction on land purchase etc.
.
Some of the tax and distribute proposals are
.
1. Thomas Picketty , the crook --- he has suggested wealth tax , and suggested that wealth tax collection money should be distributed amongst worse-off in one or another way. Like all eminent economist, he has given no legal details. And Thomas Picketty the crook opposed wealth tax on charitable trusts !!! Suffices to say, people like Rockefeller and Rothschild will pay zero wealth taxes in Picketty's proposals !!! No wonder why press owned by Rockefeller and Rothschild promote him.
.
2. One "arthik nyay" proposal is --- impose a tax equal to interest rate or half the interest rate on ALL wealth above a limit say Rs 50 crore. And divide all money equally amongst all citizens of India. And other taxes such as income tax, vat , gst, excise etc will be abolished or will be drastically reduced, Please google on "amiri rekha" . Please also google for "Roshanlal Agrawal Amiri Rekha" , "Arthik Nyay" , Bharat Gandhi etc.
.
3. Third proposal is --- impose Rs 100 per liter tax on petrol / diesel / kerosene / LPG , impose Rs 20 per kg tax on coal. And divide money collected amongst all equally. And other taxes such as income tax, vat , gst, excise etc will be abolished or will be drastically reduced,
.
4. Combination of (1), (2) and (3)
.
So at this point, I would like compare and contrast proposed-RRG-law-draft and above "tax and distribute" proposal.
.
In RRG-law-draft, ONLY the money coming from mineral royalties, water royalties, spectrum royalties and rent from govt land is equally divided in population. .
.
Other than that, there will be wealth tax, income tax, no excise, no gst etc but the tax money will be used ONLY for court , police, military and items that courts, police, military need for strong and proper functioning such as ID system, weapon manufacturing, maths education, science education, etc. There is NO RRG-proposal to use money collected from taxes
.
Now as per RRG-law-drafts. 33% custom duties will distributed amongst citizens,. But this isnt tax and distribute because custom duties are meant to reduce import and NOT to earn income. And some 1% of wealth tax and 1% of income tax may be distributed amongst citizens, as a payment to work as voters in the procedures RTR Finance Minister in charge of tax collection. But this isnt same as tax and distribute, where huge % of tax collecvted is used for re-distribution.
