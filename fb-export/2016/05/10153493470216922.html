PLD.0002.DigSignState : Law-draft for registration of hashes of Public Key (Digital Signatures) of voters of a State in India
.
[ Comments :
.
(C1) These comments are not part of the law-draft. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(C2) If you are familiar with PLD.0001.DigSignIndia law-draft, then you need NOT spend time in reading this law-draft.
.
(C3) This law is state level version of PLD.0001.DigSignIndia. Details of PLD.0001.DigSignIndia are as follows
.

(C4) This law-draft will come into effect after CM signs it.
.
end of comments]
.
1. [ instruction to DC = District Collector ] The Governor\CM hereby order DC that if any voter personally comes to Clerk (designated by DC to maintain the voter list), and submits a serial number of 140 digits or less, then the Clerk will take that number, and enter that number in the voter list.
.
2. [ instruction to voter ] The 140 digit number can be sha1 hash or sha2 hash of your public key, or can be any number. If you register the hash of your public key, then any person across India/world can verify your public key, obtained from any source, using this hash from the voter list. And thus, any person across India, can obtain opinions of this particular voter from any website or torrent, and also verify the opinion. The opinion can be your opinion on a law-draft or appointments / expulsions in administration / judiciary / polity.
.
3. [ instruction to DC ] The number given by voter must follow following format - 3 digits , followed by a dot, followed by three digits , followed by dot and so forth. And the last character must be a dot. All characters must be 0-9 or dot. And the last 6 digits must be simple additive checksum of all previous digits. If number doesnt follow this format, then Clerk will not enter the number.
.
4. [ instruction to DC ] DC will allow first registration for free. The citizen can later change the number. For the later changes, DC will charge Rs 10 per change.
.
5. [ instruction to DC ] DC will publish the number to public on the website of DC along with voter-id and the name of the voter. The number will not be put in the print version of the voter list.
.
6. [ instruction to DC ] To reduce cost, the DC may ask voter to bring the number in USB drive and / or mobile phone and Clerk can transfer the file from the voter to Clerk's PC using USB or bluetooth.
.
7. [ instruction to DC ] The Clerk will take picture as well as fingerprint of the voter. The voter must personally come to DC's designated office to register the number.
.
