FBCPBA = Facebook based cover picture based activism -- Part-3
.
( FBCPBA is a campaign method which I , Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta at Right to Recall Party, have invented (with a critical input was from Amar Kumar Ram), to reduce inefficiencies of ALL activists who want to improve India by improving law-drafts..
.
Summary of FBCPBA --- (a) the activist / voter , who decides to follow FBCPBA , has to put text "please click this FB cover picture to know law-drafts I , [name , voter#], support".in his FB cover picture and he must not put any photo or image on his FB cover picture except 1-2 small icons and (b) the description of FB cover picture must have link to his own FB note which has links to FB status posts which has the FULL law-draft-text that activist / voter supports. So FBCPBA has two parts --- cover picture with a text as image, and an FB notes which has all links. BOTH are important. Please see -- the FB-note is also an important part of FBCPBA. And activist must make cover picture and notes all by himself, NO COPY-PASTE of even one line. )
.
======
...
chap-1 ---- what is FBCPBA , see https://fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153344397466922 .
.
chap-2 ---- How FBCPBA will increase influence of ALL law-drafts that can improve India, such as our RRG-law-drafts. --- https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153359386346922
.
chap-3 ---- How FBCPBA will reduce influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagat and CoBhApSwaRa-workers on FB in the eyes of new activists and many voters !! (this post )
.
======.
.
chap-3 ---- How FBCPBA , plus RRG-challenge, can reduce influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagat and CoBhApSwaRa-workers on FB and outside FB in the eyes of new activists !!
.
As of now, mar-2016, IMO, there are some 200 to 500 recallists on FB. May be more, may be less. I dont have exact count. But lets say there are 300 recallists as of now.
.
Say we 300 recallists have fully implemented FBCPBA on our FB wall.
.
After that, we recallists can and will throw an open challenge to ALL SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers on FB as follow . I will refer it as RRG-challenge -----
.
RRG-challenge ----- If you SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers have law-drafts to reduce problems in India, and increase India/US strength ratio, which are better than our RRG-drafts, then why dont YOU SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers put drafts as statusposts, and put links to those statusposts description of your cover page? And why dont you have cover page asking profile visitors to see those drafts.".
.
FBCPBA plus RRG-challenge will reduce influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers on FB, in the eyes of new activists.
.
Now SoMoKe-andhbhagats will have following choices
.
(3.1) SoMoKe-andhbhagats can pretend that they do not know of any such challenge. OR
.
(3.2) SoMoKe-andhbhagats can agree that they know about RRG-challenge, but claim that such challenge is just a nonsense and worth their time. And refuses to put law-drafts on his FB wall. OR
.
(3.3) SoMoKe-andhbhagats will have to put law-drafts that they can think will improve India OR
.
Now lets look at scenario from point of view of a new activist, who is neither an RRG-activist nor a SoMoKe-andhbhagat, but is in touch with many RRG-activists as well as many SoMoKe-andhbhagats.
.
Now in each of the 3 cases, the reputation of SoMoKe-andhbhagats in eyes a new activist will reduce. HOW?
.
Say SoMoKe-andhbhagat-A claim that they dont know about RRG-challenge. But the new activist knows that SoMoKe-andhbhagat does know about the RRG-challenge, So image of X will fall in eyes of new activists.
.
Say SoMoKe-andhbhagat-B claim that "he is aware RRG-challenge, but it is nonsense" and refuses to put any law-draft on his FB wall. But many new activists know that law-drafts are important to important India. So in the eyes of THOSE new activists, the influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats will decline.
..
And what if some SoMoKe-andhbhagat-C decide to put link to law-drafts in cover page? Well, SoMoKe-andhbhagats dont have any law-drafts to begin with, And if they do --- then we get a chance to show that RRG-law-drafts are better than their law-drafts. If things shift from SoMoKe-andhbhakti to comparison on law-drafts, then that too is good for India, And we recallist get chance to ask him "did you first ask SoMoKe to publicly over twitter to pass these law-drafts in Loksabha or Delhi Assembly?" If they ask SoMoKe to print these law-draft over twitter publicly, then "activists' movement for better law-draft" in India will advance. SoMoKe will never be able to support law-drafts like TCP , RTR, JurySys, wealth tax etc.
.
So once we recallists follow FBCPBA and challenge SoMoKe-andhbhagats to give the law-drafts they propose on FB, no matter what step SoMoKe-andhbhagats take, the influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats on new activists will only decrease and decrease.
.
And as influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats decrease, influence of SoMoKe will also decrease. How? Why? Because the influence of SoMoKe on FB is via two sources --- paid-media and also via SoMoKe-andhbhagats. As influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats on new activists decreases, the influence of SoMoKe on FB will decrease.
.
And as influence of SoMoKe decreases, influence of RRG-drafts will further increase. To a consider extent, the reasons why RRG-drafts didnt spread is because SoMoKe-sponors created fake hopes that SoMoKe can reduce the mess. As hope in SoMoke decreases, more and more activists will start looking for alternatives. And so chances that they will become recallists increases.
.
Let me give a numerical example.
.
(3a) say 300 recallists start using FBCPBA. Say these 300 recallists publicly ask all SoMoKe-andhbhagats publicly challenge all SoMoKe-andhbhagats to put the law-drafts they support as their FB-statusposts and put link to those statusposts in their cover picture description.
.
(3b) say there are 1000 new activists who do understand importance of law-drafts and often read FB posts of some recallists , and also know several SoMoKe-andhbhagats .
.
(3c) now these 1000 new activists will see that recallists have published their draft in easiest to reach way namely FBCPBA. And SoMoKe-andhbhagats have refused to put any law-drafts to improve India.
.
(3d) So influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats on recallists will reduce. And so chances that these new activists become recallists increases.
.
All in all, IMO, we will never be able to convert SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers into recallists. IMO, we will only be able to convert NEW coming activists into recallists, and that can happen only if they get introduced to RRG-drafts before they become SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers. Once they become SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers, RRG-drafts will be like "water over stone" on them. "Bhakti ki shakti apaar hoti hei, aur andhbhakti ki shakati to aparampaar hoti hei"
.
So FBCPA will enable us recallists to spread information on RRG-drafts rapidly. And it also enables us to prove that SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers have NO drafts to fix problems of India and increase India/US strength ratio. And that will reduce influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers and thus enable us recallists to convert more and more activists into recallists.
