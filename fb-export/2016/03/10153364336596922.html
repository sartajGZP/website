Say no cannons had come from West.Then how far could have Raja Hemchandra or Shivaji or Bajirawo gone?
.
Please see dates when Europeans came to India --- Portigese, French and then British. And please see dates of Raja Hemachandra, Shivaji, Bajirao-I. I did look at the dates, And after looking at timeline of arrival of Potugese, French , British etc in India and putting it along timelines of rise of Raja Hemchandra or Shivaji or Bajirawo etc, AND seeing the huge benefits they derived from cannons, my conclusions are --- our kings did win wars , but could never improve core strengths.
.
As long as Europeans gave them cannons, they advanced. But as Europeans decided that they themselves now want to rule, and no longer want to supply cannons and guns but want to rule, the Indian kings lost !!!
.
Please look at all dates for yourself. And please look at who got cannons from whom. And decide for yourself.
