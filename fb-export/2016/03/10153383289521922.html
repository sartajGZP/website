KHARAGPUR: Prime Minister Narendra Modi today paused during his speech at an election rally in Kharagpur, West Bengal for over five minutes, on hearing the sound of azan from a nearby mosque. Resuming after the azaan ended, he said "I do not want to interrupt anyone's prayers. So I decided to take a brief rest." ....
