FBCPBA = FACEBOOK COVER PICTURE BASED ACTIVISM
.
(Must read --- This is one of topmost 10 statusposts of mine for Recallists, and topmost post on "campaign methods")
.
SUMMARY :
----------------
.
I have invented this campaign method which I call as "FBCPBA = FB cover picture based activism". It is a campaign method in which activist / voter has to work as follow ----
.
(a) the activist / voter has to make FB cover picture with text "please click this FB cover picture to know law-drafts I , [name , voter#], support".and he must not put any images on FB cover picture except 1-2 small icons and
.
(b) the description of FB cover picture must have link to activist's / voter's an FB notes which has links to FB status posts which has the FULL law-draft-text that activist supports
.
So FBCPBA has two parts --- cover picture with text as image, and an FB notes which has all links. BOTH are important. Please see -- the FB-note is also an important part of FBCPBA. And activist must make cover picture and notes all by himself, NO COPY-PASTE of even one line.
.
FBCPBA = "FB cover picture based activism" has following advantages for us recallists
.
(a) FBCPBA will increase influence of ALL law-drafts that can improve India, such as our RRG-law-drafts. HOW?
.
(b) FBCPBA will reduce influence of SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers on FB and outside FB !! HOW?
.
(c) and thus, FBCPBA will reduce influence of SoMoKe and CoBhApSwaRa !! HOW?
.
(d) and due to (b) and (c) , influence of RRG-law-drafts will further increase. HOW?
.
(e) and FBCPBA fails if we tolerate people to have 2 FB profiles? i.e. we must inist that every activist / activist has one and ONLY one profile on FB. WHY?
.
(f) and now I will make a very TALL claim --- FB was created by CIA to control political opinions of voters in India / world. But "FBCPBA = FB cover picture based activism" can literally turn the table !! As more and more activists use my campaign method of cover picture based activism, FB will become useful to improve laws in India and FB will result into decrease in CIA's control over India !!! OR , CIA will be forced change FB layout to kill FBCPBA . HOW? HOW? HOW?
All these questions are answered in separate status posts. This status post will deal mainly with "what is FBCPBA"
.
===
.
chap-1 --- what do I mean by FBCPBA = Facebook cover picture based activism ?
.
FBCPBA = "Facebook cover picture based activism" is a campaign method , which I , Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta, at Right to Recall Group, have invented. The activist who is following this method has to take following steps ----
.
(1.1) COVER PICTURE ---- an activist / voter who is following FBCPBA has to create a FB cover picture which has ONLY text, and no pictures, except 1-2 small icons. As example, please see my cover picture at https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=10153313342966922 .
.
(1.1.1) The text he has to put is "Please click this picture to see the list of law-drafts I , [NAME , voterid] support on FB.". The line must be in large fonts. The name of voter / activist is MUST to ensure that other activists / voters dont copy-paste his cover picture. And thus each voter / activist who is following FBCPBA makes his FB cover picture.
,
(1.1.2) In the next few lines in FB cover picture, the activist / voter should mention if he is contesting elections or not.
.
(1.1.3) And in next few lines, the activist / voter should state the names (codes) of the law-drafts he supports. It may, or need not, have QR code to law-draft you support and like most.
.
(1.1.4) If the activist is contesting elections, then at most half (not more than half) picture may, or need not, be his election poster.
.
(1.1.5) if the activist is member of any political organization such as Congress, BJP, RSS, AAP, BST, Swaraj Abhiyan, Right to Recall Group etc then a small icon (not more than 10% of cover picture) near corner can be of that political group. It isnt must to put icons.
.
(1.1.6) Summarizing --- the FB cover picture should have a CLEAR TEXT in large font size saying "click this cover page to see the law-drafts I support".. And cover picture should NOT have much pictures except icons of organization icon and / or election banner.
.
(1.2) ONE FACEBOOK NOTE WITH LINKS ---- The voter / activist who is following FBCPBA must make a FB-note that has list of links to FB status posts containing the full law-draft-text that that activist / voter supports. eg The notes I have made is as follows -- https://facebook.com/notes/10152774660951922 .The details are as follows
,
(1.2.1) The title must be "IMP.001: List of law-drafts I , [name of activist / voter , voterid], support; SMSes I have sent to MP". In that FB notes, he should put his name, voter# and assembly / loksabha constituency names. And one line requesting all voters to make similar such FB notes WITHOUT copy-pasting his own notes. The NAME of voter / activist in the title of the FB-note MUST, so that it doesnt get copy-pasted or shared. And each activist who is following FBCPBA makes one such note on his own.
.
(1.2.2) For each law-draft that the activist / voter supports, the activist / voter will need to put following 6 items in notes after taking 4 actions on FB / SMS / Twitter. eg the note I have made is So if the activist is supporting say 25 law-drafts, then that activist will need to put at least following 25 * 6 = 150 lines in this FB note, after taking some 25 * 4 = 100 actions. Say the voter / activist claims that he supports law-draft TcpIndia given at https://facebook.com/ProposedLawDrafts/posts/521586644673979 . Please take a look at this draft to understand steps activists have to take. The activist who supports this law-draft has to take following steps. As examples, pls see "(5) Tcp.India" line in my FB notes at https://facebook.com/notes/10152774660951922 ---
.
(1.2.2.1) Line-1 ---- the shortcode and shorttitle of the law-draft he supports. In this example , the code is "Tcp.India -- Transparent Complaining Procedure , India " You can see that OI have put this shortcode and shorttitle in my FB notes.
.
(1.2.2.2) Line-2 -- the link to the FB status post which has FULL law-draft that that activist supports. The link is https://www.facebook.com/ProposedLawDrafts/posts/521586644673979 .You can see that I have put this link in my FB notes.
.
(1.2.2.3) Line-3 -- the activist must like the status post in (1.2.2.2), and mention that he has liked this status post
.
(1.2.2.4) Line-4 ---- it should be md5 hash of the law-draft.
.
(1.2.2.5) Line-5 --- it should be sha2hash of the law-draft
.
(1.2.2.6) Line-6 ---- the activist must copy the draft on his own wall. (copy, not just share). The copy is needed in case user of facebook deletes the FB status post in (1.2.2.6) . The link to FB status post on activist's own wall becomes Line-3. . eg I have copied this draft on my wall. The link is " https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153344214166922 "
.
(1.2.2.7) Line-7 ---- the activist must share the status post in (1.2.2.2) on his wall. The link to this share-status should be Line-4 . . eg I have shared the law-draft in (1.2.2.2) on my wall, and the link is at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153311598101922
.
(1.2.2.8) Line-8 -- the activist must write a comment in the status in post in (1.2.2.2).. And take link to the comment by clicking the time below the comment. And link to my "I support" is https://web.facebook.com/ProposedLawDraf…/…/521586644673979…
.
(1.2.2.9) Line-9 -- Torrent link --- The activist should also write the torrent link. I am yet to make torrent link. So it isnt there yet
.
(1.2.2.10) Line-10 ---: please send order to MP via SMS to print the law-draft. The order you send must have URL and md5hash of the law-draft. (Later step -- you must also include a torrent link in the SMS). And please put SMS text here. You can also take image of the SMS you are sending, and post the picture on you wall along with these links.
.
