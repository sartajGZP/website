Against Namo's order to give Padma Shri to Priyanka Chopra etc .
.
(A) How NaMo and media-owners benefit eachother by giving Padma award to (undeserving?) people like Devgan , Pruiyanka Chopra?
.
(B) And how do people at large lose?
.
(C) And why do RSS-workers , Congress / BJP / AAP workers and SoGa \ ARke support Padma awards to Devgan , Chopra?
.
Some 100000 guys and gals EVERY year try to join modelling / TV-serial / movie line. Most work for free / cheap for first 1-2 years. And later, most of them end up in high profile "nefarious business". And only a few make money that is can be said as well paying.
.
While stars like Miss Chopra may be making Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore per movie, an AVERAGE model / TV-serial / movie actress makes MUCH LESS than average engineer , much much much less than average doctor and even less than a woman has invested same amount of time in a business. Even a nurse at age of 50 years earns more than an AVERAGE model at the age of 50 years !! The models etc in "nefarious business" do make a lot of money. But that is ONLY for a few years. After that, their value goes down.
.girls Now lets say these 100,000 gals are asked "would you like to work in nefarious business and get so much money? OR, would you like to be modelling etc business where your average income will be MUCH below most careers and after a few years, income will be near zero?". Then over 90% will walk away. And instead they will work on career like teaching, engineering, medicine, nursing , business or plain vanilla office work
.
So a lot of glamor is needed to lure in the young girls. And so paidmediamen pay crores of rupees to some 100-200 of these 100,000 newcoming girlsand raise publicity, That way, an illusion is created in minds of all lakhs newcomer that they will all become very rich. And then they are lured into low paying modelling / acting jobs and nefarious businesses
.
Now what has Padma award go to do with it?
.
Padma award INCREASES the respect and glamor. And so it makes it EASIER for people in movie / TV-serial business to lure in more and more newcomer girls and guys.,
.
IOW, NaMo has given Padma award to Devgan , Chopra ONLY to felicitate producers and financiers in modelling / TV-serial / movie business. They will get more newcomers and for lesser price.
.
And what does NaMo gain from it? NaMo gains HUGE publicity from them. Paidmediamen will raise issues in which NaMo will emerge as winner (eg Rohit Vemulla sucide) and suppress issues in which NaMo loses (eg collapsing of education in Municipal schools in Gujarat).
.
So who loses?
.
Well, a large number of less informed girls will lose. And possibly their parents as well. Due to this Padma award to Miss CHorpra and Devgan, more girls and guys will get lured towards modelling / TV-serial / movie , and reduce their focus on academics. And more of them will end up in low paying work and nefarious businesses.
.
Finally, why so SoGa \ ArKe support NaMo's decision to give Padma awards to Chopra etc? because they do benefit from apex elitemen who sponsor paidmedia
.
And why do ALL RSS / Congress / BJP / AAP / BST workers support NaMo's decision to give Padma awards to Chopra? And why so SoMoKe-andbhagats also support medals to Miss Chopra? Because for them, SoMoke come first and people of India can go to hell.
.
Only we at Right to Recall group have opposed Padma awards to Devgn, Miss Chopra etc
/.
SOLUTION?
.
The kist of people who may be getting awards should be put on net 3 months before award is decided. That way, citizens via SMS can decide send SMS to MP whether award should be given or NOT.
.
Also, right to recall doordarshan charman law-draft should be printed in Gazette. That way Doordarshan will improve and mak programs to inform youngsters that AVERAGE income in modelling / TV-serials / movie is MUCH BELOW most profession involving sincere work. And that most mode / TV-serial / movie actors only end up in "nefarious" business. That way, number of newcomers wasting their life will reduce.
