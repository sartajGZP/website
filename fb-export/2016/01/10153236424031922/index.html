Biggest damage SoMoKe's FDi-FDI-FDI policy creates ---- reduces development of skills in Indian populations
.
There were 100 students in class. All were given some assignments. Some of the "pragmatic" , "smart" , "vyahavarik" students decided to pay big bucks to get all the problems solves and get then written in a nice attractive way. And other "dumb" , "dogmatic" , "khadoos" students slogged and tried to solve all problems themselves. The bright students got 100/100 , and the khadoos students got much poorer marks. Some even failed to get 35/100 .
.
SoMoKe ( = SoGa , NaMo , ArGandhi, one the same when it comes to FDI , ek hi thaili ke chatte batte hein ) are like "pragmatic" "smart" , , "vyahavarik" students. They get people to get best solutions? Need fast train? SoMoKe will call Japanese. Need good cheap mobile? SoMoKe call Chinese. Need weapons? SoMoKe call Americans./ British / French / Israelis. Need large quantity of cheap soaps? SoMoKe would Invite Unilever and ask Uniliver to setup Hindustan Liver in India. Want good ciggerate? Invite British company, Imperial Tobacco Company, and ask them to set up Indian Tobacco Company. Want fast online shopping? Invite Amazon. And want grand social network in India,. SoMoKe would invite Mark Zuckerberg to give freebasics dot com .
.
And EVERY SoMoKe-andhbhagat , every Congress-worker, every
BJP-worker, every AAP-worker, every RSS-worker, every BST-worker, every Swaraj Abhiyan worker supports this "FDI everywhere" mantra of SoMoKe.
.
We at Right to Recall Group have "khadoos" = "dogmatic" proposals. We believe that instead of inviting assorted videshi and asking them to run factories, we should build factories and everything ourselves. The "stupid" way of reinventing the wheels.
.
Now India has imported some 100 crore mobiles in past 10 years. So what did we lose? Not just forex, but we lost engineering ability that manufacturing of thoese 100 crore mobiles would have given us.
.
Now SoMoKe will invite Chinese to bring all machineray , setuo factories etc to make 100 crore more mobiles (the old one will get trashed in 2-5 years anyway). So what do we lose? Well, we need to give then forex for profits they make. But what we will loose is engineering skills that process of setting up factories and making machines would have given us.
.
Likewise, SoMoKe insist on inviting Japanese and Chinese to run bullet trains between Ahmedabad-Mumbai , Hyderabad-Chennal etc etc. What shall we loose? We will never get the engineering ability that train and track manufacturing process would given to us.
.
And all these skills add up in weapon making.
.
I repeat --- all these skills add up in weapon making. We will gain no skills of making mobile phones, making microwaves, making trains etc etc and so we will also not gain skill to make weapons from scratch. So we will end up importing weapons, (eg Tanks are made in Russia , planes are made in Russia / France / UK etc etc) And we will end up making weapons whose all core parts are imported . eg core of Bhramos is Russian, most parts of Tejas LCA are from israel, Radars we are making are made in Israel and so forth, And so when war starts or is about to start, prices of all replacement parts will go 10 times to 100 times !!! And we will go bankrupt and end up handing over whole India to all these assorted countries.
.
So "make in India , made by Indians" and NOT "make in India, made by foreigners" is the solution. But sadly, SoMoKe prefer "made in India, made by foreigners" and oppose "made in India, made by Indians" !!
.
But then it is also a fact that factory owners and labor in India are far far less productive. So how do we "make in India, made by Indians"?
.
We at RRG believe that Indian factory owners and labor are way way behind factory owners of USA , UK , China etc, because
.
(a) factory owners and labor in USA have access to law-drafts like multi-elections, Right to recall, Jury System, referendum
.
(b) citizens in general in UK have access to law-drafts like Right to recall
.
(c) factory owners and labor in China get cheap land, because all land in China is owned by Govt, and China has far far more meritourious officers / judges because all recruitment has less interviews and more written exams, and recruits are from much bigger population.
.
So IMO, it is possible to make India's factory owners and labor as less inefficient as USA , UK , China etc by bringing above law-drafts. And we dont need even $1 of FDI. The biggest benefit is NOT just advoiding the repatriation crisis , but enable Indian engineers to get experience in building things.
.
SoMoKe in their infinite wisdom oppose JurySys , RTR , multi-elections, wealth tax, referendum etc. And so SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers oppose these law-drafts. And so productivity of Indian factory owners and labor will remains low. And so we have to get MNC-owners to come and setup factories in India.
.
All in all, SoMoKe , SoMoKe-andbhagats, CoBhApSwaRa-workers are like "pragamatic" students who just relax, and hire best people to solve their maths assignment, and get best answers written in best possible way. While we are RRG are like students who believe in solving problems using Indians ONLY.
.
So you activists can decide whether you want to work to publicize RRG-law-drafts, or work with SoMoKe-andhbhagats and so NOTHING except slogan shouting, rallying , neta-bhakti and above all, oppose all RRG-law-drafts etc etc.
.
If you decide to work to publicize RRG-law-drafts such as Right to Recall judges, RTR Police Chiefs, RTR PM / CMs / MPs / MLAs , Jury System , TCP (similar to referendum) etc. , then pls download "Right to Recall Manifesto, English, aka 301.pdf " from https://www.facebook.com/groups/righttorecallparty/10152114637883103/ and also "Right to Recall Manifesto, Hindi, aka 301.h.pdf " from https://www.facebook.com/groups/righttorecallparty/10152114645373103/ . And pls send orders to MPs via SMS , listed in https://www.facebook.com/notes/521085451390765
.
