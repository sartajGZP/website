Dear recallists,
.
Here are some questions to wonder about
.
1. Say our goal-1 is fulfilled i.e. we are able to inform ALL 20 lakh to 2 crore activists of India about RTR-drafts, TCP-drafts etc. And say all except say some 2000 to 5000 refuse to publicize these law-drafts. Then what steps do you propose?
.
2. Say goal-1 is met i.e. 20 lakh activists do agree to publicize these law-drafts and take the information on RTR-drafts to over 70 crore voters. But goal-2 i.e. isnt met. i.e. not even 1 crore voters agree to send SMS to MPs. i.e. almost over 98% voters refuse to send SMS to MPs. Then what steps do you propose?
.
3. Say goal-1 and goal-2 are met i.e. 20 lakh activists take the information to 70 crore voters and over 45 crore voters do send orders to MPs via SMS. But say almost MPs / MLAs / CMs / PM refuse to print these law-drafts and say "if voters want, we will resign, but we dont want these RTR-law-drafts, Jury-law-drafts". Then what steps do you propose? Should \ will AMUS force MPs to print the law-drafts?
.
4. Say over 500 recallists agree to contest LS elections and get elected by voters on RTR-law-drafts' promise. Say the new RRP MPs and new RRP PM refuse to print RTR-law-drafts. Then what steps do you propose?
.
5. Say Recallist PM prints TCP , RTR etc law-drafts. Say SCjs cancel that law-draft. And SCjs also rule RTR-SCj-GN as unconsttutional. Then what option do you suggest?
.
6. Say RRP PM prints RTR GNs, TCP GN etc. But say rank and file of IAs , IPS , junior officers , land record officers, bank officers, constables, ALL Govt engineers etc say "we dont want to work under these RTR , Jury etc law-drafts and we are ready to resign". How likely is this scenario? And what steps do you propose? Cancel the RTR GNs ? If not cancel RTR GNs, then how do you propose to run administration? Please note that you do have support of crores of voters.
.
7. Say RRP PM prints RTR GNs, TCP GN etc. But say rank and file of IAs , IPS , junior officers , land record officers, bank officers, constables, ALL Govt engineers etc do not oppose openly. But they start a sabotage from within, How likely is this scenario? And what steps do you propose? Cancel the RTR GNs ? If not cancel RTR GNs, then how do you propose to run administration given their complete un-cooperation and sabotage. Please note that you do have support of crores of voters.
.
8. Say RRP MPs have printed RTR-law-drafts, And foreign powers decide to give massive weapons to Pakistan and Bangladesh to send 10000s of Kasabs into India , and incite Bangladeshis to start massive secessionist movement in Asam , West Bengal and incite Kashmiris to start secessionist movement in JK. And also incite naxals to go on a killing spree. How do you propose to manage this situation?
.
