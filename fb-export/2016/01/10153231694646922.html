Jallikatu
----------
.
Jallikatu is NOT cruel on bull. (Please see video on YouTube on Jallikatu . Pls type "Jallikatu " in youtube searchbox.) . The bulls rarely get injured. It is NOT a bloodsport as wiki says. The bull rarely bleeds. The humans have to stop bull with bare hands, and no weapons. In fact, using nails is also prohibited !! Sometimes, humans get injured, but then so do they in boxing and many sports. No bull has ever died or even bled or even faced any minor injuries.
.
Jalliatu improves race of cows a, bullocks and bull. How? The festival enables all to compare strengths of the bull. and thus best bulls can be selected from breeding. This also enables bull owners to earn income. Ending Jallikatu will only increase number of bulls sent to slaughter houses. This will deplete number of bulls, and so many local desi cow breeds may become extinct..
.
Jalliatu festival increases demand of bulls and thus reduces bull slaughter. Banning Jalliatu will only increase bull slaughter.

.
Belief has been that Lord Ayyappa had vowed to remain brahmachari (celibate) and so he had decided not to see any women in his life. And so women are not allowed in his temples.
