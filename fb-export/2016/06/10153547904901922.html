PLD.0032.RtrCM - Right to Recall CM
.

(C.1.3) This law-draft is SIMILAR to RtrPM, except one more clause, clause-9 is added before CV-clauses. That clause is similar to art-356 of Constituition of India. So if activist or voters is familar with RtrPm, then he needs to see only clause-9 to understand this draft.

end of comments]
.
(1) [ general instruction ] The word citizen would mean a registered voter residing in the state. The word may means may or need not, and clearly means 'no binding'.
.
(2) [ proedure for DC = District Collector ] If a citizen of India above 25 years wishes to be CM, then he can appear before DC. DC would issue a serial number for a filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election and put his name on CM's website.
.
(3) [ procedure for Talati = Patwari = Village Officer or Talati's Clerks ]
.
(3.1) If a citizen comes in person to Talati's office, pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five persons for the CM position, the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card.
.
(3.2) If a the citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(3.3) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter.
.
(3.4) DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(3.5) CM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. CM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM
.
(4) [ procedure for Talati ] The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences.
.
(5) [ procedure for DC ] On every Monday, DCs will publish Approval counts for each candidate.
.
(6) [ procedure for CM ] The existing CM may count his approval count as higher of the following two
.
(6.1) number of citizens who have approved him
.
(6.2) sum of votes obtained by MLA who have supported him
.
(7) [ procedure for CM ] If a candidate gets approval count of approval of CM plus 2% (of ALL registered voters) , then existing CM may resign and may request MLAs to appoint the person approved by the citizens as new CM. Or, Cm need not do so. His discretion will be final.
.
(8) [ proedure for MLAs ] The MLAs may elect the person stated in clause-7 as new CM.
.
(9) [ procedure for CM ] With approval of 45 crore citizen-voters in India, CM may suspend this law-draft in a state for 5 years.
.
[comment : (C.2) - CV sections are similar to TcpState ]
.
(CV.1)[ procedure for DC ] If any citizen wants a change in this law-draft, he may submit an affidavit at DC's office and DC or his clerk will post affidavit on CM's website for Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) [ procedure for Talati or Patwari ] If any citizen want to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to affidavit submitted in above clause, Talati will enter YES/NO
and give him a receipt for Rs 3 fee. The YES-NO will be posted on CM's website.
.
