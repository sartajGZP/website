Dear all recallists,
.
I would request you all to use words "Right to Recall Party" and not words "Right to recall Group" because the word "Group" is giving connotations to many newcomers that we are non-election people and we wont contest elections and we wont form political party !!!
.
So pro-election-contesting and pro-party people stay away. And anti-election-contesting and anti-party people come near. And later , the anti-election-contesting people feel cheated when they find that we do plan to contest elections and do plan to form a political party !!
.
Now we never hid that we will contest election. In the first manifesto I published in 1998 , and in every newspaper advt and in every major document I have published, I have clearly mentioned that I will contest elections to further the RTR , Jury etc drafts. And I will ask as many as possible to contest elections across India --- Loksabha, Assembly, local bodies.
.
So I and other recallists never hid this from anyone. But still, the word "Group" gave wrong connotation to newcomers, and that we are anti-election-contesting etc etc
.
So to clear the confusion to newcomers, I would use ONLY Right to Recall Party henceforth and seldom use word "Group". And each one of you will be on your own --- you may decide.
