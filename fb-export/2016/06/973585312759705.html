how you can filter unwanted posts on your timeline ?
.
if your friends are frequently posting on your timeline and tag you, using this route you can filter such posts.
.
go to : setting --> timeline and tagging ---> you will get two options 1) 'Who can add things to my timeline'? , click "ONLY ME", 2) 'review posts friends tag you in before appear on your timeline' ?, click "ON" ( with using edit option).
.
now when someone tag you with unwanted posts or put useless stuffs on your timeline which you are not willing to add on your timeline, FB will send you such notification -- "X tagged you with a post, do you want to add this on you timeline." this way you will get two options a) hide b) allow.
.
now you can switch your option.
.
this will enable you to filter unwanted posts.
.
