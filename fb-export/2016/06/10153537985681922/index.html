MESSAGE TO City Councilor and City Mayor
--------------------------
.
Dear City Councilor ,
.
If you got the link to this status via SMS , then it is an ORDER sent to you via your voter. The author of this status post has NOTHING to do with this order. The order is to pass the draft as resolution and then ask CM to print this law-draft in the State Govt Gazette. For the filelink and filehashes, pls see the first 5 lines.
.
======= start of the law-draft ====
.
PLD.0009.TcpCity - The draft of proposed Transparent Complaint Filing Gazette Notification at City level
.
[ Comments :
.
(C1) These comments are not part of the law-draft. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(C2) SUMMARY : Any voter can put his complaint / affidavit on Mayor's website and any other voter can add his name to that affidavit.
.
(C3) This law-draft REFERS to a proposed law-draft - TcpIndia. But knowing that TcpIndia is NOT necessary to understand law-draft given here. The fullname and hashes of the law-draft used is given in Appenxid-I.
.
(C4) This law is SIMILAR to TcpIndia. This is City level version of TcpIndia. If you are familiar with TcpIndia law-draft, then you need NOT spend time in reading this law-draft. And if you are already campaigning for TcpIndia law-draft, then you need not campaign for this law-draft.
.
(C5) The City Councilors can pass this draft as resolution and then ask CM to print the draft in the State Govt Gazette. This law-draft doesnt need any legislation from Assembly or Parliament.
.
end of comments]
.
(1) [ instruction to MCEO = Municipal Commissioner or Executive Officer or his clerk ] The City Council , hereby instructs MCEO that : if any citizen-voter in the City submits a Right to Information application or complaint against corruption or any affidavit to MCEO and requests to be put on the website of Mayor , then MCEO or his designated clerk will issue a serial number and put that affidavit on the website of Mayor for a fee of Rs 20 per page.
.
(2) [ order to Talati, Patwari, Village Officer or his clerk ] The City Council instructs Patwari that :
.
(2.1) if a citizen-voter comes with voter ID, and specifies Yes-No on an RTI application, complaint or any affidavit submitted in cluase-1, then Patwari will enter his Yes-No on website of Mayor with his voter-ID and give a printed receipt for Rs 3 fee.
.
(2.2) The Patwari will also allow citizen to change his Yes-No for Rs 3 fee.
.
(2.3) The fee will be Re 1 for BPL card holder
.
(2.4) MCEO may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter's cell phone
.
(2.5) MCEO may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt
.
(3) [ note to all Citizens, Officers, Ministers, judges ]
.
(3.1) This TCP Gazette Notification is not a referendum procedure. The Yes-No count will not be a binding on Mayor, any elected person, any officer, any judges etc. If over 51% of ALL voters in the city, register Yes on a given affidavit, then Mayor may or need not take necessary action on the RTI application affidavit ; or the Mayor may or need not resign. The decision of Mayor will be final.
.
(3.2) Further, MCEO may or need not design and propose a system to collect Yes-No in clause-2 over secured SMS, and implement that system after approval of Mayor.
.
