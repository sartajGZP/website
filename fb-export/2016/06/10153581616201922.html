.

My proposal --- GoI will import the pulse at Rs 50 per kg, and that should be auctioned in open market to whole salers. If retail price is Rs 150 / kg, then GoI will fetch Rs 130 / kg. The money earned, i.e. Rs 130 - Rs 50 = Rs 80 / kg should be divied equally amongst citizens.
.
Essentially, giving to wholesalers at a lesser price , horping that wholesalers will sell at below market price is pointless. And supervising shopkeepers isnt possible and viable.
.
So let market mechanism prevail at retail / wholesale levels. and divide super profit, which not due to anyone's labor equally amongst all.
