section-1 : Registering citizens' Approvals for DLRO candidates
.
(1.1) [ general instruction ] The word citizen would mean a registered voter residing in the District.
.
(1.2) [ procedure for Sarpanch = District Panchayat Sarpanch ] The Sarpanch would appoint an officer as DLRO = District Land Rent Officer.
.
(1.3) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] If any citizen wishes to be DLRO, he may appear in person or place affidavit before Sarpanch. The Sarpanch is hereby ordered to accept his candidacy for DLRO after taking fee same as deposit amount for MP election. Sarpanch will issue him a serial number.
.
(1.4) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] The Sarpanch may assign above task to any class-1 or class-2 officer.
.
(1.5) [ procedure for Civic Center Clerk] A citizen can come in person to Civic Center Clerk, pay Rs 3 fee and approves at most five persons for DLRO position. The Clerk will enter his approvals in the computer and issue him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved.
.
(1.4) [ procedure for Clerk ] The Clerk will put the preferences of the citizen on District website as decided by the Sarpanch with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences.
.
(1.5) [ procedure for Clerk ] If a citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Clerk will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(1.6) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] On every Monday, Sarpanch may publish approval counts for each candidate.
.
section-2 : Replacement of DLRO
.
(2.1) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] If an DLRO candidate gets approval of over 50% of ALL registered citizen-voters (ALL, not just those who have filed their approval) in District, then Sarpanch may expel the existing DLRO and appoint the person with highest approval count as DLRO.
.
(2.2) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] If the existing DLRO has become DLRO by approvals, and the candidate with highest approval also have 2% more approvals than existing one, then and then only the Sarpanch will appoint the candidate with highest approvals for that position.
.
(2.3) [ procedure for Sarpanch] If the person's approval is below 33% of all voters, then Sarpanch may or need not replace DLRO with his appointee. But as long as approval is above 33% , Sarpanch need not replace him with his appointee. Discretion of Sarpanch will be final.
.
section-3 : Ownership of plots under District
.
(3.1) [ declaration ] The Citizens of this District hereby decide and declare the plots owned by District are property of the Citizens of India, residing in the District.
.
(3.2) DLRO will have NO jurisdiction over land plots owned by private persons or companies or trusts or land plots owned by Central Govts Govt or State Govts. He will have no jurisdiction on plots used Military , Police, Courts, Prisons, Railways, Bus Stations, Govt Schools till class XII and tax collection offices. DLRO will have jurisdiction over all vacant plots owned by District.
.
section-4 : Collection of rents from plots owned by District
.
(4.1) Using procedure similar to DdMrcamIndia law-draft, DLRO will frame a procedure to give District plots owned by District at rent, and decide and collect land rent from tenants.
.
(4.2) For parking spaces, DLRO will decide the charges and collect money directly from vehicle owners.
.
section-5 : Distribution of land rent
.
(5.1) DLRO will give 34% of rent collected to Defense Minister for the purpose of strengthening Military and providing weapons and weapon-use education to all citizens.
.
(5.2) DLRO will dispatch rest of rent collected every month to the citizens of India residing in District for over 10 years. The rent to be give will depend on age and number of children, and will be same as in DdMrcamIndia. The place of residence within District will not be a factor.
.
section-6 : Collection of Mineral Royalties
.
(6.1) This law will not cover mineral royalties.
.
setion-CV : Citizens' voice
.
[Comment - (C.2.1) Similar to TcpIndia and TcpDistrict. If you have any of the two law-drafts, then you may skip these two clauses.]
.
(CV.1) [ procedure for Sarpanch ] If any citizen wants a change in this law, he may submit an affidavit at Sarpanch's office and Sarpanch or his clerk will post the affidavit on the website of Sarpanch for a fee of Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) [ procedure for Talati or Patwari ] If any citizens want to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to any affidavit submitted in above clause, and he comes to Talati 's office with voter-ID and pays Rs 3 fee, Talati will enter YES/NO and give him a receipt. The YES-NO will be posted on the website of Sarpanch.
