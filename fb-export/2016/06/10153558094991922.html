Why Vallabhbhai Patel refused to become PM of India despite the fact more than 75% delegates and over 90% Congress workers wanted him and NOT Jawaharlal Ghazi to become PM? Importance of machine spare parts !!
.
Congress back in 1940s had 13 divisions. Each division held meeting of congress activists to decide who should be the first PM of India. Over 90% congress workers supported Vallabhbhai Patel (he was NOT Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, he became Sardar after Police / Military came under him). And only Congress of Kashmir and two Congress of Pakistan region supported Jawaharlal Ghazi as PM. Each region sent two delegates, in case one bribes out. So out of 26 delegates, 20 delegates supported Vallabhbhai Patel and 6 supported Jawaharlal Ghazi. But yet, later Vallabhbhai Patel backed out and agreed to accept Jawaharlal Ghazi as PM. WHY?
.
Many say that it was because Mahaduratma Ghandhi ordered Vallabhbhai to sit down and Vallabhbhai obeyed. well, it is true that Mahaduratma Gandhi did order that Jawaharlal Ghazi shall be the first PM of India and he had also ordered Vallabhbhai Patel to sit down. But why did Vallabhbhai Patel accepted his order? After all, Mahaduratma Gandhi had no guns.
.
And it is NOT that Vallabhbhai Patel respected elders. eg when elder brother of Vallabhbhai Patel , Shri Vitthalbhai Patel died, he had said that his entire property , amounting to over Rs 200 crore by today's price, be given to Rashtrapita Subhashchandra Bose !! And not a penny should be given to Vallabhbhai Patel !!! Vallabhbhai Patel refused to honor the wish of his elder brother even on his own property !!! Vallabhbhai Patel asked court to cancel the will !! And Vallabhbhai Patel made a statement in court, that his elder brother had gone insane at the time of death, and so will should be cancelled !! And so the property should be given to him and not Rashtrapita Bose. Vallabbhbhai Patel also request Viceroy to pressurize the judge to cancel the will !!! The judges were back then told to keep British interest as paramount and so the judge cancelled the will and gave all the property to Vallabhbhai Patel. (Aside --- Vitthalbhai Patel wanted to give his property to Rashtrapita Bose because he saw Vallabhbhai Patel as incapable of getting freedom for India, and he saw more hope in Rashtrapita Subhashchandra Bose !!!)
.
My point is --- Shri Vallabhbhai Patel never respected elders. Then what FORCED Vallabhbhai Patel to sit down, despite support of over 90% of congress workers?
.
Reason was --- importance of spare parts
.
1) The machines of factories of FEF = Five elitemen families = Tata, Birla Dalmia, Sarabhai, Bajaj , were all made in in UK. And all spare parts came from UK. And if UK had stopped sending spare parts, then FEF would have gone bankrupt
.
2) FEF = Five elitemen families were running trusts and ashrams of Mahaduratma Gandhi. Mahaduratma Gandhi was just a show piece. He had no control over accounts of trusts and ashram which ran in his name !!! eg All articles Mahaduratma Gandhi wrote were first edited by men appointed by FEF and only then went to print !!! eg Today, whatever laws Mahatma Arvind Gandhi decies to print are first edited Fraud Foundation and only then sent to Governor for approval.
.
3) British ordered FEF to install Jawaharlal Ghazi as PM of India
.
4) So FEFs ordered Mahaduratma Gandhi to oppose Vallabhbhai Patel, support Jawaharlal Ghazi . Mahaduratma Gandhi did so
.
5) FEF ordered Vallabhbhai patel to back down !!! And so he backed down. And so he backed down
.
In addition, when PM of India were to be decided, Indian Military was under British control. And British had threatened Congress leaders, that if they dont obey British, then they will hand ober India to Indian Military and walk away. It was empty threat, as British found Congress a far far better option than Indian Military. But Congress leaders were NOT willing to take even 1% chance !!!
.
So these were the reasons why Vallabhbhai Patel agreed to back down and let Jawaharlal Ghazi become the first PM of India, despite support of over 90% Congress workers.

And finally, why Congress workers supported Vallabhbhai Patel and not Jawaharlal Ghazi ? Because they saw Jawaharlal Ghazi same was as we see him today !!!
