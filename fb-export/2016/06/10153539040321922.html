======= start of the law-draft ====
.
PLD.0031.RtrPM - Right to Recall PM
.
[ Comments :
.
(C.1.1) These comments are not part of the law-draft. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(C.1.2) This law-draft refers to following proposed law-drafts - TcpIndia. Knowing this TcpIndia is NOT necessary to understand law-draft given here. The details of TcpIndia referred law-draft is given at the end in Appendix-I. Also, the section Citizen's Voice is similar to TcpIndia.
.
(C.1.3) This law-draft isnt similar to any previous draft.
.
end of comments]
.
(1) [ general instruction ] The word citizen would mean a registered voter. The word may means may or need not, and clearly means 'no binding'.
.
(2) [ proedure for DC = District Collector ] If a citizen of India above 30 years wishes to be PM, he can appear before DC. DC would issue a serial number for a filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election and put his name on the PM's website.
.
(3) [ procedure for Talati = Patwari = Village Officer or Talati's Clerks ]
.
(3.1) If a citizen comes in person to Talati's office, pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five persons for the PM position, the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card.
.
(3.2) If a the citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(3.3) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter.
.
(3.4) DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(3.5) PM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. PM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM
.
(4) [ procedure for Talati ] The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences.
.
(5) [ procedure for DC ] On every Monday, DCs will publish Approval counts for each candidate.
.
(6) [ pocedure for PM ] The existing PM may count his approval count as higher of the following two
.
(6.1) number of citizens who have approved him
.
(6.2) sum of votes obtained by Loksabha MPs who have supported him
.
(7) [ procedure for PM ] If a candidate gets approvals 1 crore more than approvals existing PM has, then PM may resign and may ask MPs to appoint the highest approved person as new PM. Or, PM need not take this action, PM's discretion will be final.
.
(8) [ proedure for LS MPs ] The MPs may elect the person stated in clause-7 as new PM.
.
[ comment - CV sections are similar to TcpIndia]
.
(CV.1)[ procedure for DC ] If any citizen wants a change in this law-draft, he may submit an affidavit at DC's office and DC or his clerk will post affidavit on PM's website for Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) [ procedure for Talati or Patwari ] If any citizen want to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to affidavit submitted in above clause, Talati will enter YES/NO
and give him a receipt for Rs 3 fee. The YES-NO will be posted on PM's website.
.
