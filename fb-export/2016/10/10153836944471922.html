.

But he never worked to disclose his citizenship.
.
WHY?
.
Because if this fact had become public to many say 7 years back, then many nationalists would not have become akshay-fans or akshay-andhbhagats.

.
IOW, disclosing it before could have drastically reduced "acquisition" of new fans.

Now if Amir Khan or SRK or Salman Khan had taken US or Canadian citizenship, I am sure Hinduvaadies would have made huge uproar. But given that it is AKaK, they are all silent.
