IOW, it is easy for everyone (except NaMk-andhbhagats) to see that NaMo was hostile to universal water metering in Ahmedabad, and rest of Gujarat. So now despite Narmada dam, Gujarat is facing water shortage than has led to crisis.
.

Because Once Punjab and Haryana were one state. They separate in 1956 and so Haryana got 40% of water of Punjab waters. Due to absence of metering, Punjab started facing shortage of water !! And so some wise guys thought --- if Punjab becomes separate nation, and then ONLY international riparian law will apply, and as per international riparian law, a nation can claim water of river ONLY if river physically flows thru it !! So if Punjab were to become separate nation, then since river of Punjab doesnt flow thru Haryana, Haryana and rest of India cant claim any water !! But if water metering was done in 1950s, then Punjab would have had so much surplus water than no such demand would NOT have taken place !!! But sadly, all Congress / Jansabgh / CPM / Akali etc leaders back then also opposed universal water metering !! And so did all leaders from 1950 to SoMoke !!
