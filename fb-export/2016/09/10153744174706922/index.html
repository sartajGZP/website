NaMo orders his favorite BJP Udit Raj to make "indirect" statement praising cowmeat eating !!! (old news ---- 28-aug-2016)
.
On 28-aug-2016 , as per NaMo's orders, BJP MP Udir Raj tweeted following statement
.
(a) Udit Raj tweeted --- "Usain bolt of Jamaica was poor and trainer advised him to eat beef both the times and he scored 9 gold medals in Olympic".
.
And following were his next statements, all as per NaMo's orders --
.
(b) Udit Raj tweeted --- "I cited circumstances of Jamaica that despite poor infrastructure & poverty Bolt won 9 gold so our players should find ways like that to win"
.
(c) Udit Raj tweeted --- "I intended to convey-players & society to look at ways to win in games rather blaming circumstances & Govt & eating is their choice"
.
(d) Udit Raj tweeted --- "As Usain bolt &trainer found ways and means to win medals similarly our players and trainers should do as per their circumstances"
.
(e) Udit Raj tweeted --- "My tweet is not even remotely connected to advocate beef eating rather it is reproduction of Bolt trainer statement"
.
----
.
All his statements were aimed as praising cowmeat eating. And of course, NaMo and Udit Raj are both very cunning, and so Udit Raj used the word "beef" which includes buffalo meat, So his last tweet was
.
(f) Udit Raj said --- "How educated people can go so wrong understanding beef as cow meet? Beef means cattle meat may include buffalo etc"
.
------
.
So please go the all the six statements Udit Raj made as per NaMo's orders. NaMo had ordered him to put very clever escapes --- use name of Usain Bolt, and use word 'beef' and not 'cowmeat' !!!
.
But lets see one statement that Udit Raj made ".
.
(c) Udit Raj tweeted --- "I intended to convey-players & society to look at ways to win in games rather blaming circumstances & Govt & eating is their choice"
.
see the words --- "Eating is their choice". Means, if someone wants to eat cowmeat, then govt as per our great libertarian leaders NaMo and Udit Raj, should NOT interfere.
.
And nowhere does NaMo ordered Udit Raj to say that "cowmeat should be banned" or that "cow slaughterers should be imprisoned". All the statements that Udit Raj made, as per NaMo's orders, only praise cowmeat eating.
.
----
.
So NaMo and Udit Raj can both claim innocence. NaMo can claim "see, all statements were made by Udit Raj and each BJP MP is free to make statements he likes and I believe in free speech and I dont control MPs etc etc". And Udit Raj can claim innocence and say "see, I was only quoting Usain Bolt, and see I never used word cowmeat and I only said beef" etc etc
.
But lets see what effects statements will have on gullible youth. .

The statements of Udit Raj, made as per order of NaMo, were made ONLY to create above effect.

Second, the next statement of Udit raj says - Govt should NOT interfere in diet !! And next,, he used the word "poor". IOW, the effect that NaMo and Udit Raj wish to create in minds of poor youth is "it is gorakshaks who are stopping them from becoming as muscular and as attractive as Usain Bolt by stopping them from eating cowmeat !!".
