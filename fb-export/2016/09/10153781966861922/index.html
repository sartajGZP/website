NaMo's "mistake" is that he had raised tall expectations by merely becoming PM, he can reduce this problem in a very short time !!! He gave soundbytes and posturing, that created such expectations and NaMo-andhbhagats made it worse by getting carried away and amplying those soundbytes by 1000 times
.

.
(1) convince US-elitemen to force Afghanistan's puppet Govt, that Afghan Govt should to allow India's RAW etc to use Afghan land to ship help Balochistan secessionist to cause maximal damage to pakistan Army

Both support handing over huge portion of Indian economy to US-elitemen via FDI, and also ruin Indian education to keep US-elitemen happy. And both support letting Missionaries run freely and convert to keep US-elitemen happy.

.
All said and done, there are short term measures which we can take TODAY. And long term measures require increasing STRENGTH of India as whole. All this nonsense that "merely by having strong PM, we can fix this problem" . The only way "strong PM" can "weak nation" can solve this problem is by handing over economy to US-elitemen via FDI.

.
Also, IMO, one should note that Pak / JK problem is because help secessionists and Pak gets from USA, UK, China and Saudi Arabia. So we are fighting a proxy war against USA, China, Saudi etc. Saudi is only money rich and has no engineering skills, and can be dealth with with ease. But to deter USA and China from helping Pakistan, we have to gain strength at par with USA / China.
.
Now strength of USA comes from law-drafts like JurySys, RTR, multi-elections, referendum, absence of gst/vat/excise, presence of wealth tax and so on. And strength of china comes from recruitment without interviews i..e recruitment by written exams and low land rent (because all land id govt owned).And China is is much weaker than USA due to absence of RTR, JurySys etc. And China is becoming weaker because of GST it has introduced.

Now, can we become as string as USA without these law-drafts?
.
There where FUNDAMENTAL difference between thinking of Right to Recall Party and SoMoKe-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers comes. SoMoke-andhbhagats and CoBhApSwaRa-workers say that law-drafts like RTR , JurySys, referendum, wealth tax etc are all nonsense and strength can come by neta-puja, neta-andhbhakti, party-andhbhakti, slogan shouting, rallying, cap wearing etc. We at RRP believe that getting these law-drafts is critical for gaining strength and survival of India against adversaries like USA / China and their puppets like Pakistan.
.
So based on this, the solutions we at RRP propose are ---
