Lets say woman A gives implants her egg in the womb of surrogate woman S after paying S whatever was the agreed amount. And say 9 months later, surrogate woman S gives birth to a baby. Then who "owns" the child?

The law before year 2005 said that surrogate woman S would own the child. And that was the law across world before surrogacy laws came.

So egg owner woman A and surrogate woman S would often sign contract before womb renting that "S must give the child for to A". But since there was NO law to legitimize the contract, the contract would get declared as "void from date of signing" aka "void ab initio".So contract becomes void. So in absence of law, the defacto law was --- "child belongs to mother who gave womb and not the mother who gave egg": And pre-pregancy contract would be void.
.
(Voiding pre-pregnancy contracts on handover of baby isnt new. And it is still practiced in non-surrogacy cases. eg say a man M i married to woman A and they are unable to have a child. So so M and A sign contract with woman B, that M and woman B will have relation, and woman B must handover the child borne out of relation to M and A. Now after child birth, if woman B refuses to handover the child, then contract is void).

Now lets come back to surrogacy. pre-2005 situation considered such contracts as void. And so S can now own the child. And S can demand a huge huge money from A to give the child to A.
