.
.2. Third task --- convince over 45 crore voters to send orders to MP via SMS.
.
The second task is --- we need to convince 200,000 activists to convince 45 crore voters to read RRG-drafts. I explained that above.
.
The next i.e. third task is to convince at least 45 crore voters , that each voter should send orders to MPs via SMS.. This task is to be done along with task-2. As recallists explain RRG-law-drafts to the voters, the activists should request voters to send order to MP via SMS.
.
Which orders? The orders to print whatever law-drafts that that voter supports. IOW, we need to convince over 45 core voters to decide which law-drafts he supports and then convince him to send order to his MP via SMS to print that law-drafts. And we recallists need 45 crore voters to send such SMS.
.
So again, we will need some 200,000 activists. It is NOT possible to convince 45 crore voters via paidmediamen.
.
And the SMS must have URL to the law-draft or / and md5hash of the law-draft or sha2hash of that law-draft. And please note order must be sent via SMS and not postcard or email or FB-message. The SMS must have URL and / or md5hash or sha2hash of the law-draft. This is important --- SMS without SMS / hash is useless.
.
And SMS must be sent by voter when activist is NOT around. i.e. no "influence of nearness" and at least 2 says after the activist explains SMS sending
.
To be specific,

(3a) say a recallist activist presented Tcp.India law-draft given at https://www.facebook.com/ProposedLawDrafts/posts/521586644673979 to a voter
.
(3b) say the voter agrees that Tcp.India is good for him and India
.
(3c) Then we recallists need to ensure that the voter sends order to MP via SMS to print Tcp.India draft. The SMS must have this url or must have any url to exactb same draft or must have e8efdfb4bdf7c6ebc4b553515cda28d2 or must have
dbf7887960fa07f203deeccfebdd897e6e45a3777aefe853680b471ba3fb7dfb , or all of them.
.
(3d) and In same way , we need to convince over 45 crore voters to send URL , md5hash or sha2hash of the law-drafts they support.
.
Now, lets say we recallists did manage to convert 200,000 activists into recallists. Say these 200,000 recallists did explain RRG-law-drafts to 70 crore voters. But what if 45 crore voters refuse to send orders to MP via SMS for these RRG-law-drafts?
.
Then IMO, we recallists have lost. We may give up or may keep working and waiting till 45 crore voters have sent orders to MPs via SMS. But as long as voters refuse to send orders to MPs via SMS, we have NO way further. We cant ask MPs to print these law-drafts because we have NOT proved support.
