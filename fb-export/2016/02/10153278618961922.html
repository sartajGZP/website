.
5. Convince over 45 crore voters to send orders to MPs via SMS to resign
.
This task needs to be done ONLY after task-1 to task-4 are done, AND existing MPs have refused to print RTR / Jury drafts.
.
I.e., Task-5 should be done ONLY AFTER --------

(5a) some 543 recallists are willing to contest Loksabha election and some 5000 recallists are willing to contest Assembly elections AND
.
(5b) over 45 crore voters know about some specific law-drafts
.
(5c) over 45 crore voters have ordered MPs via SMS to print one of more of those specific law-draft AND
.
(5d) over 45 crore voters know and agree that over 45 crore voters have ordered their MPs via SMS to print those law-drafts
.
(5e) and MPs have refused to print this specific law-drafts
.
------- THEN , after all 5 things happen, we recallist have moral right to ask voters to order MPs to resign via SMS.
.
Now one alternative is to wait for next elections to come. But time is money , time is life. IMO, wasting time can prove to be very damaging to us. But some voters may decide to wait till next election comes. During that time, they may evaluate RRG-candidates and also work to pressurize existing MPs to print RTR-drafts. Some voters may decide to send "resign" SMS to the MPs.
.
What if over 45 crore voters send orders to MPs via SMS to resign and yet the MPs dont reign? Then AMUS = Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh will convince MPs to resign.. We dont need to worry about it.
.
But if tasks (5a) to (5d) , dont happen, then we racllists have lost because of us or voters, and not because of leaders / elitemen.
