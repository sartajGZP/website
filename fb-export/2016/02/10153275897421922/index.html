RRG tasklist part-4 --- convincing over 45 crore voters that over 45 crore voters have sent order to MP via SMS . (or , convincing Y crore voters that X crore voters have sent order to MP via SMS)
.
First first part at ---- https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153286488891922
.
Previous post at --- https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153278609096922
.
Next part at ---- https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10153278618961922
.
=======
.
3. Forth task --- convincing over 45 crore voters that over 45 crore voters have sent order to MP via SMS . (after 45 crore voters have sent SMS)
.
Lets say X crore voters have sent orders to MPs via SMS. And Y crore voters want to know "how may crore voters in India have sent orders to MPs via SMS". Then we recallists need some methods by which those Y crore voters can decide how many crore voters have sent orders to MPs via SMS.
.
This is necessary to convince voters to ask MPs to "print RRG-drafts or resign". Because if Y crore voters are convinced that over 45 crore voters have sent orders to MP via SMS to print law-draft-A , and yet MPs refuse to print law-draft-A , then these Y crore voters have valid reason to send "resign" SMS to MPs. There is one more condition to sending "resign" SMS --- there must be 543 recallists willing to become MP and print that law-draft.
.
And if --- (a) over 45 crore voters have sent order to MP via SMS to print law-draft-A , AND (b)over 45 crore voters are convinced that over 45 crore voters have sent SMS , AND (c) 543 recallists are willing to become MP , AND (d) existing MPs have refused to print that specific law-draft-A ---- THEN chances that over 45 voters send "resign" SMS to MPs increases.
.
Now if over "45 crore voters have sent SMS to MPs", but if 45 crore voters dont know that 45 crore voters have sent SMS, then chances that they will ask for resignation decreases.
.
=====
.
So how do we convince Y crore voters who want know, that X crore voters have sent orders to Mps via SMS?
.
I have two sets of methods ---- for the voters on FB and for voters NOT on FB
.
Method to prove, for voters on FB
-------------------------------------------
.
Some 10 crore Indian voters are on FB. We need to convince at least 5 crore FB / twitter using voters to take following steps
.
(4a) We need to convince at least 5 crore voters to share the urls , md5hashes , sha2hashes of the law-drafts they support on their twitter \ FB walls.
.
(4b) And on the FB / twitter post of these 5 crore voters, we need to convince each voter to put @HIS_MP_NAME and @PMO in the above mentioned status posts
.
(4c) We need to convince over 5 crore voters of India on FB \ twitter to like \ share the status post which has the law-drafts they support.
.
(4d) We need to convince over 5 crore voters on FB to make an INDEX note in their profile, which has links to all status post which has orders to MPs sent on FB / twitter, as well as md5hashes and sha2hashes of all the drafts he supports
.
(4e) We need to convince 5 crore voters to put the link to above mentioned FB note in the description of their profile as well as coverpage image on FB
.
(4f) If a voter or an activist has access to FB / twitter, and he refuses to take above steps, then he should be assumed as non-supporter. I would request RTR-activists not to waste their time with such non-supporters. If a voter does NOT know how to use FB / twitter , then this doesnt apply on him.
.
Now if and when 5 crore voters have put above information on FB / twitter, we can convince 5 crore voters of India, that 5 crore voters of India have sent SMS to MPs. How? We recallists can ask a voter on FB to see the cover page of their FB-friends who are also real life friends. And see if the description of image has link to notes which has link to an FB-notes, which has list of all URLs , md5hashes, sha2hashes. And he can see / search for law-drafts supported and he is interested in. Later, one may be able to develop FB-app which parses the FB-notes and gives this information.
.
That way, each voter who is on FB, will be able to see how many of his friends support the RTR-lawdrafts. This will give him a good guess of average in whole country. Based on data of his friends, a voter on facebook can decide "whether 45 crore voters have sent orders to MPs via SMS" or not.
..
Now there are about 10 crore voters in India capable of fluently using FB. We need to convince these FB-using Indian voters to check their 100 known friends on FB, if they have support RTR-drafts.
.
Now, what If 200000 recallists activists refuse to ask voters to make FB notes containing links to law-draft they want, then we lost !!
.
Now, what if 200,000 recallists do ask 5 crore voters of India to put SMS text on FB / twtitter etc ; but 5 crore voters refuse to put the SMS text on twitter / FB. and what if 5 crore voters refuse to create one FB note which has list of all URLs , md5hashes , shah2hashes of the law-drafts they have supported? Then we need to call it end. May be, we recallist lost. Or we can keep trying till 45 crore voters do agree,
.
But against all odds, say if we manage to convince over 5 crore voters in India to make a Facebook notes which has links to all twitter / FB posts where he had demanded the law-drafts he wants, then we can convince every FB-using Indian voter the supporter-count. And then based on that, he can decide what is the overall count in India.
.
Method for voters outside FB
-------------------------------------
..
As per voters not on FB , we need to convince every voter to select one trusted person as PROXY who is fluent with FB. That trusted person can create an FB account of the voter , and take the steps I had mentioned above. That way, "common knowledge" about RRG-DRAFTS and supporter-count can increase. And "common knowledge" about those who oppose RRG-drafts can also increase.
.
Also, we need to make some peer-to-peer App which can inform every voter about what law-drafts people in his neighborhood and his phonebook support
.
If over 45 crore voters agree that over 45 crore voters have sent orders to MPs via SMS to print some specific law-drafts, then we have to execute 4th step.
