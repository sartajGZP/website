The task-1 to task-8 deal with bring RTR-law-drafts such as RTR, JurySys , remove VAT / GST , wealth tax , TCP , MRCM etc,
.
Now real success is NOT forcing MPs to print such laws or getting recallist elected as MPs, and getting them to print RTR-law-drafts. The real success is converting India into a place at least as good as (or as less bad as) USA, and may be better or less bad.
.
Once RTR-based regime starts -- two problems will come . Internal and external. This task deals with internal problems -- sabotage by govt employees.
.
Once RTR / Jury , comes, govt staff will have to work with near zero bribe, And once TCP comes, salary etc of govt officers will go to 1/3rd to even 1/4th. So a large number of govt employees across rank and file may try to sabotage the functioning of new administration.
.
How can activists deal with the situation where rank and file of IAS, IPS, judges, junior officers want to sabotage the system and administration, so that RTR / Jury laws can be scarpped and things go back to past?
.
IMO, the activists MUST NOT create a parallel govt which I call as "activistocracy". The efforts must be to improve govt and not create a parallel govt,. Whatever activists want --- it should be done via TCP , Jury and recallable police chiefs, junior police officers under Jury , and other recallable and under-Jury officers. I oppose all direct action by activists , except for self defense in surprise attacks,
.
First see how sabotage may occur and how it can be dealt with.
.
As of today, there are about 700 districts and each district has 25-35 Collector level officers such Collector, additional Collector, District Eductaion Officers , SP , DySP , Land Record Keepers etc.. So we have about 20000 Collector level officers such Collector, additional Collector, District Eductaion Officers , SP , DySP , Land Record Keepers etc. In addition, there are officers who maintain accounts and records and banks and all govt departments. What if they decide to sabotage the system? How can we avoid that situation or deal with that situation?
.
IMO, the activists , using RTR / Jury need to expel / replace as many as possible,. Rule of thumb is --- replace EVERY non engineer in nodal position with any less bad person , who has run a factory with over 100 employees !!! But replacement must be done using RTR and not using just activism,
.
And here, I would request all activists to remember one thing --- Chengez Khan never killed engineers !!! And the word Engineer includes skilled technicians as well as scientists. And IMO it should be extended to include all doctors. And I am NOT talking about killing anyone at all. But I am talking about expelling / replacing those who may be causing sabotage.
.
And in addition to scientists , engineers, technicians and doctors, we also need to ensure that record keepers stay and dont sabotage, These days, across world, in all countries, record keeping has become more important than life saving medicines, Sabotage of records can do more damage than war.
,
Some of the problem can be reduced by making ALL land / flat ownership records public and then making sure that all sale / purchase / lease etc also get posted in public. Next, we would need to make all bank balances public (balances only , not all transaction). This may sound harsh, but stopping bank frauds in times of instability and rampant uncooperation of staff is possible ONLY if weekly bank balances balances are kept public. And IMO we need to implement, what I have proposed as OPEN BITCOIN , where all transactions also become publicly known to all and reach to everyone who want to keep record..So each citizen will have two channels to do banking transactions --- in one, transaction will become public and in another, it will remain confidential. In addition, cash notes will be there, But preventing counterfeit cash will become huge problem. Solution is nacrotest in public of those caught with counterfeit currency.
.
If a govt employee is suspected of causing sabotage, then he should be expelled, but decision MUST be taken by Jurors and NOT by activists. The most activists should do is to guide Jurors,. But final decision must be by the Jury and Jury alone. And the Jurors should be empowered to take narcotest of officer in public if necessary to know the truth.
.
With narcotest in public becoming common, most officers will NOT dare to sabotage the operations.
.
Now a question is --- (a) if existing 20000 Collector and above ranking officers declare that they dont want to work under RTR / Jury laws, and decide to resign, then do we have 20000 people who can replace them? (b) if existing 200,000 Tahsildaar level officers declare that they dont want to work under RTR / Jury laws, and decide to resign, then do we have 200,000 people who can replace them?
.
Answer --- Most engineers and technical specialists in existing govt will decide to stay. If needed their salaries can be raised.
.
And as per generalists, they can be replaced by citizens using RTR. And if existing ones decide to resign enmass or individually,then also within days, citizens using RTR / TCP will be able to appoint one of the local person as district head.
.
As such, chances are high that over 90% of the staff would accept new RTR / Jury law-drafts, accept total loss of bribe and will also accept 50% to 75% reduction in salaries !!! The problem may be other way --- citizens due to hatred against existing staff because of corruption and high salaries they have enjoyed may resort to mass expulsions of govt officers and their replacement using TCP !! And in many cases, operations will NOT damage, So in such case, activists will NOT be able to stop mass expulsion of govt employees and their replacement. If operations are NOT getting hampered, then any non-engineer who can be replaced should be replaced !!!
.
The new setup based on RTR , Jury , Universally verifiable Universal ID system, public crime records, public land ownership record, public share ownership records and OPEN BITCOIN will enable citizens to run all operations with much much less staff in govt and banks.
.
