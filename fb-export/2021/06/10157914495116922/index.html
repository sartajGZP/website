I am against 24-jul-2021 rally - rally dharana must NOT be on a date decided by unknown (possibly foreigner?) people , and should be FIXED day of month !! WHY? Leaderlessness and other reasons.
.
(1) we activists in India should NEVER become part of any network run by foreigner or unknown people. These foreign networks are mostly run by usuk elitemen to waste energy of activists and also destabilize India .
.
(2) Long back , "we" at RRP had decided that if there is rally or dharana or anshan, it will be ONLY on first sunday of the month 10am to anytime and no other start datetime. why? because we DO NOT want a "LEADER" to decide rally / dharana dates. Now indeed , first sunday of month is decided by me and other apex people in RRP. So one may say that "first sunday of the month 10am to anytime" is decided by some leaders. Fine. But at least, leader is now NOT needed to decide second third forth and tenth rally dates. They are now decided AUTOMATICALLY. So now me or any leader have no power to STOP a rally or gathering from happening. And why first sunday? Well, which day is most convinient for people in India and least obstructionist for people in India? Its Sundays. Now second saturday and forth saturday are often holidays in State Govt and many private companies. And so people keep second sunday and third sunday for out of city social gathering so that they can get two days. So two more convinient rally dates are 1st sunday and 3rd sunday. So choosing 1st sunday is not much of "leader deciding" --- its a almost natural choice for people in India.
.
So "rally / dharana on first sunday of month 10 am to anytime" is LESS-LEADERED way than a leader deciding datetimes again and again and again

And leaders can anyday break the movement by not deciding next rally dates !!! AIM leaders could have taken an option of FIXED DATE every month , like we did. But they didnt. WHY NOT?
.
I request all activists NOT to fall for LEADER BASED agitations. We all saw Duratma Anna based leader based andolan of year 2011. We all know how it went. So first, please stay away from ALL foreign organizations. And second, we must have LEADERLESS movement ( or as LESS LEADER based as possible)
