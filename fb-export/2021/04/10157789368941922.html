I think following
.
Many employers will ask employees not to contest elections, and even threaten with expulsion.
.
But if employees take UNPAID leave, and discusses NO politics in office and doesnt involve any work colleague in politics, and if employee is adding good value to company, then employer will come to terms, and NOT actually expel him.
.
So employer may threaten expulsion for election contesting. But if an employee goes ahead and contests election, then now employer has more to lose by expelling him and nothing to lose by keeping him.
.
finally, all employers have to run business with cool heads. They are NOT emotionally attached to any party. And a good employee is very hard to find and replace. And expulsion of an employee, just because he contested election, will make employer look bad in eyes of other employees. Even if other employees are non political, they dont see election contesting as some crime or immoral act.
.
When there is a case of unwanted behavior, employers worry about REPEATING again and SPREAD i.e. possibility that other employees may also do same action. Here, that those two problems dont exist. If an employee has contested election, against warning of employer, then also employer knows that employee will not repeat this for next 2 years to 5 years. And employer also knows that chances that other employees will mimick or copy this action are almost zero. So here, there is no problem of SPREAD / REPEAT.
.
So when finally employer decides to act, its unlikely he will expel election contesting employee just because he contested election.
.
