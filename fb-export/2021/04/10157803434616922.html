One doesnt need 30 day leave to contest elections --- 10 days discontinued leave over 30 days will suffice
.
Say voting of loksabha is on 31-may-2021. Then form filling will start on 03-may-2021 . approx 29 days ago. In this 29 day period, there will be 8 weekend holidays (sat , sun) and 1-2 reilgious holidays.
.
So holidays one may need will be
.
03-may-2021
04-may-2021
06-may-2021
.
3 days to fill form
.
And then two days a week, in next 2 weeks --- Tue / Thus
.
And may be 3 days in one week - Tue Thus Fri
.
Thats all.
.
So all in all , one will need 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 alternate days off
.
Or may be 10 days off in the 20 working days
.
In fact, even on off day, one can work till 8pm and finish urgent work
.
Now, this is NOT fairy tale. This is how I actually contested may-2009 loksabha election and feb-2011 assembly byelection.
.
So yes, no private company will agree for 30 day continuous leave, as this can disrupt project.
.
But if an employee takes 2 alternate days in two week off and 3 days off in 2 week, ONCE or TWICE in FIVE YEARS, then for employer, it is an acceptable loss if the employee is otherwise a good employee.
.
Also, employee is always on mobile. And video call, if there is something urgent , even on day off.
.
Please note --- if such leaves are once or twice in FIVE YEARS , then it is accepatble.
.
As per company "rule" --- private companies have NO "rules". If employee is good, employer will always bend rules for one employee and not for others. eg in very large company, I came across one employee, who in general came at 1pm and left at 7pm, and no day was marked as half day !!! Finally, manager and senior manager have to finish projects with least bugs in codes. Thats what clients pay for. Clients dont pay for "rules"
.
So all in all, election contesting does NOT mean 30 continuous day leaves. There are 8 weekend leaves. And out of 22 working days, one only needs 11 leaves at most
