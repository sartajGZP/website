Inca civilization has made quite tall buildings, and very accurate calanders and star observatories -- but has NO WHEEELS and not many roads. WHY NOT?
.
Why isnt they could not discover wheels?
.
Many people tend to conclude that Incas , Mayans, Aztecs etc i.e. people and civilizations of North America and South America, before Europeans came, were mentally backward !! And the claim is rationalizted by the fact that these peoples had not discovered even wheel, something which Asia discovered / invented in around 3500 BC
.
Imo, it has NOTHING to do with mental backwardness. Incas etc were mentally as sharp as Asians / Europeans and that can be seen from the calnders they made, and buildings they made. Way back in 1100 AD , Incas had predicted when several planets will be in straight line, and they were off only by a few days !!! IOW, they had accurately figured out trajectory and speeds of planets upto Saturn !!! And this was in those days, where Europeans believed that Sub revolve around Earth !!! So mental sharpness of Inca is beyond doubt.
.
Then what can be the reason, for absence of WHEEL?
.
Imo, reason is just NATURAL resource availability !!! Or rather, copper availabuility !!!
.
Imo, reason was --- raw copper was occuring in natural form at Iraq river basins, during 9000 BC. And so copper extracted by then Iraqiees in 9000 BC, This was just a NATURAL phenomenon. So without much smelting, one could obtain copper. And since copper became available to people in Iraq in 9000 BC, the people there could make copper tools. And using copper tools, they could give circular share to stone / wooden blocks, and thus could invent / discover wheel
.
As long as man was confined to stone tools, its very difficult for man to cut cylindrical object from a wooden / stone block, and thus make a wheel.
.
Now Incas, Aztec etc had very very fewer raw copper deposits . So got sufficient copper only ariund 1000 AD !!! So by 1500 AD, they had just started making copper ornaments and tools.
.
Now Asia too took 6000 years to move from raw copper ornaments to making tools capable of making wheels. So Inca too, may have reached from copper tools level to wheel level in next 6000 years or less or more. But before that can happen, Europeans came, and destroyed their civilizations
.
So unavaility of raw copper is NOT something that means lower mental capability. After all, even in Asia , Europe and Africa, why is it that Mespotamia was first to use copper and also wheels? Because availibity of copper was just a NATURAL advantage they got over others, And that enabled them to make better tools and obtain wheel
.
And due to communication and trade, the inventions of Mespotamia became easily available to all across Europe and Asia
.
So imo, it was just a NATURAL and natural resource type difference, that created more differences. Nothing more than that
