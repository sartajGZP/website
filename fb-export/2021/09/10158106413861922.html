Reducing privatization
.
Adding following clause in proposed DhanVapsi Passbook lawdraft will reduce privatization, to almost zero !!!
.
66.7% of net actual incomes of from all companies , boards and all units owned by Central Govt ( including all PSUs, all PSBs , including RBI ) will be distributed amongst citizens of India. The distribution pattern will be same as pattern used for mineral royalty.
.
Net actual income will mean all profits minus all losses of loss making units, plus incomes from capital sales , and minus capital investment and capital expenditures, minus contingency funds. The amount will be calculated on cash basis and not accrual basis.
.
State Govt may or need not distribute 66.7% of similar income amongst domiciles of that state.
.
City , District , Tahsil , Gram may or need not also distribute 66.7% of similar income amongst their domiciles
.
