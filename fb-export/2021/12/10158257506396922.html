As per the exemption, continuing from the British-era rule, the people from the hill district of Kodagu can posses pistols, revolvers and double-barrelled shotguns without a licence

Added later : as such agreement that Coorgies can keep guns was made between Coorgies and second PM Sayyid Jawaharlal Khan and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel in 1948. Law was printed in 1951 and then 1954 and redraft in 1964.
