About raising min marriage age : The question is NOT what age one should marry. Thats a personal decision.
.
Question is --- say a girl of age say 18 years or say 19 years or say 20 years, say marries a boy, then should Govt imprison the boy or parents of girl or boy or both ?
.
And who should decide on this "minimum age of girl or boy" issue
.
-----------
.
My proposal is --- TCP (referendum) . Jury
.
In TCP , each one will vote age he supports.
.
And when a case of underage marriage comes, the Jury of say 25 citizens chosen at randim from list of about 20 lakh voters, between age of 25 years and 55 years, will decide. They will decide whether to apply the law or set aside the law.
.
And if there is a case of underage marriage, then the Jurors will decide whether to apply law or set aside. Thats how all Jurors and judges across world act. The Jurors and judges always apply their will and never go by the law ( eg NJAC was set aside by Supreme judges. And Jurors in UK had in years 1750s had set aside laws, now called as Bloody Code, which had death sentences for many property crimes such as bank cheque forgery )
..
So using TCP and Jury, this minimu age of marriage for girls issue can be resolved
.
