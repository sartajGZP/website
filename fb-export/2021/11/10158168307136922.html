Why planting more trees is good, but it will not reduce CO2 level beyond a point .
.
CO2 level came down millions of years ago, because a few million years ago , plants developed genes to make wood. And bacteria and other animals didnt know how to convert wood back into into CO2.
.
So a lot of CO2 became wood. And when plant died, bacteria could not convert wood into CO2. And so dead wood became coal.
.
Now bacteria know how to digest wood and convert into CO2.
.
So when trees die , carbon in wood again becomes CO2.
.
So if goal is to bring CO2 level back to year 1700 AD level, then planting trees will not suffice.
.
imo only way is to use solar wind energy to convert CO2 into carbon and bury the carbon back
.
So tree planting is indeed good. It will surely improve environment. But lot lot more will be needed to bring CO2 level back to 1700 ad year levels
