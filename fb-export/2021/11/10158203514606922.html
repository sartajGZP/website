The art-328 doesnt NOT give full control over state election to MLAs. But It says that MLAs can make laws about State Assembly elections, and they should NOT be against laws made by Parliament.
.
Now Partliament has NOT made EVM compulsory, It has only give OPTION to ECI to use EVMs. To be specific, ONLY clause in Representation of People Act 1951, which talks about EVM says
.
clause-61A. Voting machines at elections.—Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act or the rules made thereunder, the giving and recording of votes by voting machines in such manner as may be prescribed, may be adopted in such constituency or constituencies as the Election Commission may, having regard to the circumstances of each case, specify
.
IOW, it used word "may". So Parliament does NOT make EVM uses binding. The words "may use EVM" here means "may use EVM" or "need not use EVM".
.
Hence, if Assembly directs by making law to ECI. that ECI shall i.e. MUST use paper ballot and not use EVMs, then Assembly isnt contradicting any order of Parliament. And art-328 makes Parliament as first agency to make election rules, and makes State Assembly as second agency to make rules, and ECI is third agency to make rules neded to implement the rules made by Parliament and State Assembly.
.
So State Assembly can make laws to end EVM in that State.
.
And State Assembly can also make rules to endure that ECI doesnt use black glass and must use transparent glass in EVM.
