Important --- all letters to PM CM should have words "I instruct -- " ( in "मैं निर्देश देता हूँ ) at top, right below date
.
Across India, and across world, in administration, "I instruct" is same as "i order".
.
"I instruct" is a dignified/polite way of saying "I order"
.
So we will not use words "I order", but certainly use "I instruct"
.
in hindi, use "मैं निर्देश देता हूँ -- ". In administion, it means same as
"मैं तुम्हें आदेश देता हूं", but is dignified and polite was to say
