Why negative recall procedure in USA did not create instability, while negative recall procedures in Latin America created instability, and votevapsi (aka positive recall procedure) is must for India, and negative recall procedure must be avoided in India
.
Haryana RSS Govt in oct-2020 printed law which has negative recall procedure for Sarpanch position. I have been proposing positive recall procedure since year 1998, (yes, 1998 ).
.
For difference between positive recall and negative recall, pls see link in comment-3.
.
In this post, I will confine to answering the question – why negative recall procedures didn’t do damage in USA, but did damage in Latin America, and must not be used in India. And reason, is NOT culture !!!
.
Reasons are --- (1) Jury System (2) referendum (3) multi-elections
.
(1) presence of Jury system in USA : In USA, Jury System had started in 1700 AD. And in 1800 AD, JurySys became very strong. JurySys reduced indiscipline in staff and also in nodal officers. And so citizen officer relation in USA had less conflicts compared to all countries.
(2) referendum : most districts and many states have referendum procedure. This further reduces conflicts between officers and citizens. And so demand for recall will be low.
(3) multi-elections and multi-recalls : say an officer such as mayor is recallable and say some other officer such as CM or police chief is not recallable. Then non recallable officers will use or misuse their positions’ influence to create recall for that recallable officer such as mayor. But a recallable officer will not use his positions’ influence to work to recall anyone, because he too can face a counter move. So when many officers are recallable, none will use their position to create a recall others. In USA, most district nodal officers are recallable, and many state apex officers are recallable. And so none use their positions’ influence to recall others.
.
So first two reasons reduce need for recall. And third reason reduces recall attempts accelerated by officers and not demanded by voters per se. And so even though USA uses negative recall procedure, it didn’t create instability.
.
But Latin America does not have Jury System, and doesn’t not have referendum. And so officers and citizens have far more conflicts than USA. And in Latin America, recall is only over Mayor and not State Ministers and other appointed officers in police and administration. And so these ministers and officers will not face counter move if and when they use influence of their positions and govt resources under them to create a recall. So recall polls happen even when demand in citizens is low.
.
Same way, in India, there is no JurySys and referendum. So relation between citizens and officers have too much conflicts. And there is no recall over State CM. So in Haryana, due to negative recall procedure, CM will fully misuse his resources to create recall polls over Sarpanch and so recall polls will happen even when there is no demand in citizens. But if votevapsi aka positive recall procedures are used, then no matter how much push CM applies, he will not be able to have a recall poll, if demand for recall in citizens in low.
.
So RSS workers’ decision to use negative recall procedure in Haryana shows ill motives. And voters and activists across India should work to bring Votevapsi Sarpanch law (i.e. law with positive recall procedure over Sarpanch), and also Votevapsi over all nodal officers.
