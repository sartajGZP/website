How PSU bank privatization increases burden on people of India ? SOLUTION?
.
Say a PSU bank like Central Bank of India is privatized. Say bank has taken deposits from 4 individuals as follows
from A - Rs 1 lakhs
from B - Rs 3 lakhs
from C - Rs 6 lakhs
from D - Rs 10 lakhs
Total --- Rs 20 lakhs
.
Now bank has to keep about 5% say Rs 2 lakhs with RBI and invest about 30% i.e. say about Rs 6 lakhs in govt bonds and rest Rs 12 lakhs , it can lend to anyone.
.
Now say all that Rs 12 lakhs of loans sinks.
.
Then who will pay depositors?
.
Then as per existing laws, Govt will have to cover Rs 5 lakhs of deposits. So Govt will have to come up with Rs 14 lakhs. But only Rs 8 lakhs was deposited by this bank with RBI / Govt. So Govt aka people of India suffer a burden of Rs 14 lakhs - Rs 8 lakhs = Rs 6 lakhs
.
And in many cases, like YES bank as we saw, PM is "convinced" to save all 100% deposits , not just Rs 5 lakhs !!! Whether convincing is done by bribes or media pressure is another issue. So if PM decides to save entire Rs 20 lakhs of deposits, then burden on people will increase to Rs 20 lakhs - Rs 8 lakhs = Rs 12 lakhs
.
So when banks are privatized, the private owners offer high interest and manage to get deposits. They even bribe many govt officers and manage to convince govt officers to have govt accounts in private banks. And then private bank owners will lend recklessly. Then lenders register default, and finally Govt / RBI has to come forward to pay upto Rs 5 lakhs to all depositors or pay whole deposit money to all. And this creates burden on people.
.
How does Govt pay for these burden?
.
There are 10s of ways, such as
.
(1) manufacture more rupees - this increases prices
.
(2) impose lockdown - this weakens self funded manufacturers and so profits of bankfunded manufacturers increases, and they are able to repay their loans, and reduce the burden
.
(3) force PSU banks to lend money to private banks at low interest
.
(3a) step-3 forces PSU banks to reduce interest on deposits and increase fees like fees on minimum balance
.
Using many many ways, Govt i.e. people pay for losses of reckless lending done by private bank owners
.
SOLUTION?
.
Solution I propose is to print law that Govt / RBI / DIC will not insure even Re 1 in private banks. Or, if a customer has deposited amount below Rs 5 lakhs, then bank must use that money in buy only Govt bonds. So in such case, if bank defaults, then bank does have upto Rs 5 lakhs to pay to the depositors. And law that all deposits above Rs 5 lakhs will NOT be guaranteed by govt in any case
.
Above two need honesty in RBI Governor, and that imo will come ONLY by Votevapsi passbook procedure over RBI Governor, To see the draft of Votevapsi passbook over Governor, pls see link in second comment of this post
.
But as of now, such laws not planned. As of now, Govt has promised rs 5 lakh of coverage to all depositors, and not put any conditions that all deposits below Rs 5 lakh of a customer must be deposited in govt bonds,. And in case of YES Bank, Govt worked to save entire deposits of all depositors, and same may happen again in future.
.
