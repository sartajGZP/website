The most fundamental question in activism and politics : say coal mining cost is rs 1000 per tonne and market value at the gate of mine is rs 3000 per tonne. Then in population of 95 crore adults, who should get how much of this rs 2000 difference or profits ?
.
Much of the nonsense we see in politics and activism is because activists are not even aware of this question and they do not know thale importance of this issue !!
.
All major wars were due to this issue only. Long back, issue was water instead of coal. Then coal and iron became important. Then crude oil and now lithium.
.
Next question is --- say in population of 95 crore adults, who should bear guns and who should not. Both questions are related.
.
And third question is --- in 95 crore of populations, every week, say there are 10000 disputes. Who should decide these disputes.
.
Now all three questions are related. The persons with guns will decide disputes and they will also keep much of this rs 2000 per tonne profits.
.
But if importance of guns become known to all, then everyone will try to keep gun and profits will get shared more and more equally. So in case some persons desires to own larger share of this rs 2000 profits , then somehow they need to take focus of activists away from coal and also from guns. And also convince or fool activists to think that only a small portion of population should have guns.
.
Most the thoughts and words and wordnets are manufactured to keep voters and activists away from these three questions.
.
