Malafide game to take sword away from Sikhs has started. I support universal sword ownership (eg sikhs ) along with universal gun ownership ( eg karnataka's koorg district, switzerland, usa)
.
But it seems that Govt has started game to take swords away from Sikhs, by defining Kirpan as something that must have blade length below 6 inches !!!
.
Now swords have blade length of 2,5 feet to 3 feet. And using above definction of Kirpan, sword snatching may start !!!
.
Solution I propose is --- district wide mass movement to have districtwide referendum to add district name in 1963 govt law, which has name of karnataka's koorg district. And RTR PM, so that definition of Kirpan is changed to 2,5 feet blade
