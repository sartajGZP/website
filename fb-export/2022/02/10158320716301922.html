Pls take a look at EVM , before and after vote, with night vision glasses, if you have such glasses
.
WHY?
.
There is a possibility that there may be paper behind black glass before you vote. And there is possibility that program may not cut the paper after you have voted. Since central govt had changed the glass cover in jun-2017 to black glass, the paper behind glass isnt visible after light inside goes off
.
But of you are wearing night vision glasses, then paper may become visible, if it is present
.
So if you have night vision binoculars or glass, pls do take a look at VVPAT before and after you vote .
