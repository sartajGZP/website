labor vs factory owner "class" conflict --- how much fiction and how much real?
.
I first will cite a different case - criminal vs lawabider conflcit. Say number of criminals increase in an area, then harassment on law abiding citizens will increase. So imo, there is a REAL CONFLICT between criminals and law abiding citizens.
.
Now lets come to analysing factory owner vs labor class conflict, which is an important reality as per Marx and Marxists
.
Say there are 50 factory owners and say there are 5000 laborer and say each factory owners has say 100 labor
.
Now as per Marx, there is CLASS CONFLICT between 5000 labor and 50 factory owners. And due to factory owners' profits, labor are poor.
.
So say one more person comes and installs factory. So there are 51 factory owners now
.
Will salary of labor increase or worsen?
.
Since 51st factory owner wants labor, he will have to offer some more salary, and so salaries of all labor will increase.
.
Now, if Govt laws are so, or control over land / minerals is so, that no new person can install factory, then indeed existing 50 factory owners will collude and so labor salaries will reduce
.
But if citizens print laws that minimize entry barriers for factory owners, and citizens make minerals / lands price same for all, then new factory owners will keep coming, unless profits are very low.
.
So every new factory owner, increases salary of labor in general
.
So where is conflict in TWO CLASSES --- labor class vs factory owner class?
.
But if there was much called as CLASS CONFLICT, then increase in number of factory owners should make labor worse off (just as increase in number of criminals ALWAYS worsens situation of lawabiders). But what we see is just opposite !!!
.
So much of the so called CLASS CONFLICT is just a fiction
.
The real enemy of LABOR are legal ENTRY BARRIERS for factory owners, and uneven price of land/minerals
.
