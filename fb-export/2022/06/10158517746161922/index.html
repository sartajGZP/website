Consider a proposal like MRCM in which top 0.01% losses all unethical income and no ethical income. Say next 10% who is earning say rs 10 lakh a year per famiky member loses and gains nothing. And say next 90% gains rs 2000 a month.
.
So now consider a person who was earning say rs 50k per year per family member. So ratio compared to person who was earning rs 10 lakh per year is 20:1
.
Now with proposal like MRCM, the person who was earning rs 50k per family member earns rs 74k per family member. And person who was earning rs 10 lakh per family member earns rs 10.24 lakh per family member. So ratio is now 14:1.
.
Iow, any scheme which raises income of lower class more than upper middle class "worsens" the social height ratio of upper middle to lower. And this is principal reason why over 95% upper middle class will oppose MRCM even though they don't lose a penny, and no one loses even re 1 of ethical income
.
One may have noticed that some poor have jealously against wealth of rich. But many many well off also have jealously against rising income of poor, even if be ethical . And a well off is sometimes less jealous of some Tata Adani trippling his unethical income. But he feels more agrrevated when income of poor increases even by 10%.
.
This can also be seen in one more place. You will see lakh of youth opposing caste based reservation. But of them, not even 1% oppose nepotism based resevation in judiciary and many apex private companies. Because benefit in latter goes to well off. And benefit on former goes go worse off.
