why maratha and sikhs could not unite in 1856?
.
In year 1856, most sikhs were indeed anti-British, but they were more anti-Mugal than they were anti-British, because Mugals had committed a lot of atrocities against Hindus and Sikhs.
.
Sikhs had political animosity against Maratha ( everyone had political animosity against everyone), but Maratha didnt commit Mugal like atrocities, such as abductions, temple breaking, mass killings, arsoning, looting non royals etc. And sikhs also didnt commit any such atrocities. And so people to people anomisty between Sikhs and Maratha was low. The apex level political anomosity gets worked out.
.
In 1857, when Maratha initiated war against British, they had put Mugals as leaders. Most Kings were Maratha, and Mugal King Bahadurshah Zafar was a nobody compared to even a junior level Maratha King. Yet, Maratha had put Mugal King as their Leader. ( WHY?)
.
Sikhs were undecided whom to supoport. Mugals were enemy of Sikhs, and so were British. So meetings were held in every village, and each village decided whether to support maratha or british, Decision was taking by consensus and voting. And 2 to 5 representative from each village assembled at Darbar Saheb Gurudwara , Amritsar ( aka Golden Temple), where thru voting and consesus, it was decided to support British.
.
The main reason was ---- Mugals. The very thought of reinstalling Mugals --- who had done lakhs of religious killings, abductions, cow slaughters, temple breaking etc -- was repulsvive. And British, in Punjab area, had not done religion based atroicities or discriminations.
.
But if Maratha had declared Maratha as Delhi Emperor and not Mugal, then Sikhs may have decided otherwise.
.
And also in past, from years 1700 ad to 1856 ad, both Sikhs and Maratha were warring against Mugals, Afghans and British. But Maratha and Sikhs could never for an alliance of cnovience, forget uniting.
.
So one question comes --- why could not Maratha and Sikhs align against British or Mugals or both? They could have divided the teritories after war. And both would have gained . Such alliance of convience was common in those days.
.
So what stopped Maratha / Sikh alliance for emerging, despite no people to people animosity? Why even if alliance of convinience and spot, if not long term alliance, in years from say 1700 - 1856 , could not come?
