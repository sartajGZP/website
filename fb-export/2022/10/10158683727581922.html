To
Respected Election Commission of India
.
sub : Pls impose necessary restrictions, on FaceBook, InstaGram, TeleGram and all Social Media / Messaging platforms, for conduction of free and fair polls, and maintain law and order during elections
.
(1) Ending FakeIDs : Pls order FaceBook, TeleGram, InstaGram etc that henceforth, all accounts’ voterid or taxid must be visible, or, that account and its pages/groups should not be visible in India
.
(2) Newsfeed should not be decided by Facebook/IG, but user and only user shall decide contents on his newsfeed
.
(3) Ban new posts on FB/TG/IG, if they do not comply
.
( note : FB is short form for FaceBook, TG is short form for TeleGram, and IG is short form for InstaGram)
.
-------------
.
(1) How FakeIDs are used to increase hate in India
.
FaceBook allows users to fake IDs. So many nefarious organizations, such as terrorists organizations, will create 100s of fake IDs. These fake IDs will create pages, attract users and then spew hate
Here is one example, how fakeids are used to increase hate.
.
A terror organization will create many Pages with attractive photos and articles on say Lord Shiva. So lakhs of worshippers of Lord Shiva will follow these pages. The same organisation will also create Pages to attract worshippers of Lord Swaminarayana. Now say some person, who doesnt have any following in Swaminarayan Sect, but is “self declared” “Sadhu” makes adverse comments against Shiva. Now this terror organization will put the video of that “Sadhu” on all their Facebook Pages. Facebook too may be promoting such pages and such video. This will increase rage in worshippers of Lord Shiva.So some of them will make adverse posts and comments against Lord Swaminarayana. And these pages will highlight their comments and posts, and this increase hatred in members of both sects
.
This will make polls unfair, and also violence prone
.
Now if a person’s taxid or voterid (or parents’ voterid or taxid in case of minors) is displayed on profiles, and the groups and pages they own, then their ability to increase hate decreases. Because person can block one such fakeids, and all his pages get blocked. And local policemen can easily track them.
.
------------
.
(2) How Newsfeed should not be decided by FB, but by users only
.
Facebook must be ordered to add features that will allow users to control his newsfeed fully. Say a user-A is following user-B and user-C. Then FB doesnt put all posts of user-B and user-C in newsfeed of user-A. Facebook makes decision on which post of user-B and user-C should be put in newsfeed. This power in gives Facebook owners, power to manipulate political opinion in India. And will damage fairness in polls, and also gives foreigners a control over Indian polls
.
So ECI must order FB to disclose algorithm to public, and make algorithm equal to all users ie if user-A is following user-B, then FB should NOT have discretion on which posts should of user-B should appear in newsfeed and which posts should not
.
--------------
.
(3) Ban FB if FB doesnt obey these orders of ECI :
.
If facebook, telegram etc dont obey these orders of ECI, then ECI must order FB to stop upload of new posts. So users will be able to download all their posts, but not upload new posts. And if FB doesnt obey this order, then ban FB till elections end
.
Regards

( RAHUL CHIMANBHAI MEHTA
National President, Right to Recall Party)
