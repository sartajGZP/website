Assuming Cyrus Mistry death was not a murder, and it was a genuine accident (which looks unlikely though) following are lessons
.
(1) Did road have RUMBLE STRIPS ? ( see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumble_strip ) . Roads with rumble strips reduce accident chances. I am not sure if the billionaire ever worked to improve road safety in India
.
(2) Though he was worth 1000s of crores, but he was in a Rs 80 lakh only car, which did NOT have rear passanger and side window airbags (these cars start with Rs 2 crore). So if you can afford, pls drive better car.
.
(2a) There is one more such case, where death was due to cheap car. Nobel Prize winner Nash, an expert in probability and game thoery, underestimated accident probability !! So instead of Aston Martin Limo Taxi, which he could have easily afforded, he ordered an ordinary taxi. The taxi met an accident and he passed away. Ig he had ordered Aston Martin Limo Taxi, he would have survived.
.
All in all, most people underestimate accident probability.
.
This is assuming that it was not a "car remote control" murder and assuming that it was genuine accident
