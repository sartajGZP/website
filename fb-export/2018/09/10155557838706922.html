Lokpal bill passed by Parliament in year 2013 has following good website related clauses. Can anyone show me this data on Lokpal website?
.
see Lokpal law-draft pdf as passed by Parliament in year 2013 ---- https://dopt.gov.in/sites/default/files/407_06_2013-AVD-IV-09012014_0.pdf
.
The law-draft mentions thrice that Lokpal staff shall make a good website and upload all relevant data.
.
see page-11 "(10) The website of the Lokpal shall, from time to time and in such manner as may be specified by regulations, display to the public, the status of number of complaints pending before it or disposed of by it."
.
see page-18 "(6) The competent authority in respect of each Ministry or Department shall ensure that all such statements are published on the website of such Ministry or Department by 31st August of that year."
.
and see page-22 "(c) the manner for displaying on the website of the Lokpal, the status of all complaints pending or disposed of along with records and evidence with reference thereto under sub-section (10) of section 20"
.
There may be more direct / indirect references to website. I got three only by text search on word "website".
.
The bill was passed in year 2013, some 5 years back.
.
So can anyone show me links where Lokpal staff has uploaded all this data on Lokpal website? How comes these clauses didnt provide a good Lokpal website to people of India?
.
-------------
.
So my point is --- the whole exercise of adding clause that "concerned officer shall put this data in that format on his website" is as useless as above 3 Lokpal clauses. And adding buzzwords like "citizen verifiable website": is also as useless as adding words like "speedy trial", "justice for all", "transparency", and what not.
.
A good website is just one of the 100 services that officer and staff has to provide.
.
If there are RTR/Jury procedures over apex officers and staff, the staff will work to provide all services in best possible way in given budget, WITHOUT any need to mention all these 100 services in draft . And in absence of RTR/Jury procedures, the staff will provide these services in most nefarious possible ways, no matter how many lines are added in draft demanding these services.
.
So whole task of adding website clauses in proposed RTR/Jury dradt is just as attempt of "credit grabbing" and nothing more. These additional clauses add no value that RTR/Jury clauses dont create.
