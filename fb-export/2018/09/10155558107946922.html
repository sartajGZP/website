Most important reasons why I dismiss Dhanvapasi bill proposed by Rajesh Jain is bad / nonsense, and why Minerals3 given at https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 is far far better
.
(1) Dhanvapasi bill talks about SELLING away and not renting govt plots !! My proposal is to collect monthly rent, which is revised every 3 years, and lease can go from 1 year to 15 years, and 30 years in rate cases. So my proposal is inflation proof because if inflation increases, then rents will increase. Also govt owns over a lakh acres of land in urban areas, and even if 5% is sold every year, then prices that will come will be pittance, but after 20 years when all land is sold, the owners can create cartel and raise prices by 10 times !! In MRCM = Minerals3, land is only leased and leased again after lease ends. And rents are revised every 3 years. So handful of elitemen cannot create a market lock
.
(2) Likewise, Dhanvapasi bill talks about selling away mineral mines while Minerals3 talks about collecting mineral royalty and distribuiting amongst citizens. And by maximizing competition between govt mining companies and private mining companies, citizens can get maximal royalties. Dhanvapasi talks about selling away mineral mines. So say all mines are auctioned. Then owners can form cartels, reduce mineral supply and then ensure that mineral prices go sky high. Whereas in Minerals3. citizens can fix a minimum amount that bid winner must extract, and so mineral supply cannot be artificially reduced
.
(3) Dhanvapasi bill creates five to seven bodies, which will manage database of plots/mines, auction , distribution etc. But none of the officials in these five-six bodies are recallable by voters of India. And there is NO provision for Jury Trial against any junior or senior official. So corruption will be rampant as today. In Minerals3 proposal, the apex officer is recallable by voters and other officers can be fined by Jurors. So corruption and nefarious nexuses in MRCM = Minerals3 administration will be less.
.
(4) The auctioning officers can make huge plots of land and mineral mines and thus only few can bid, and so total bid received will be low. In Minerals3, the citizens can expel such officer and replace him by less bad one. So favoring elitemen by creating large plots is less likely in Minerals3 = MRCM proposal, while it will be rampant in Dhanvapasi
.
(5) Some fake recallists have copied ENTIRE existing MRCM = Minerals3 proposal, and merely added 2-3 clauses saying "officer shall create a good website" and added some useless buzzwords like "citizen verifiable" , and added some IT-words like "portal" etc. !! They claim that their version of MRCM is superior just because it has clause that "officer shall create a good website" !! And further claim is that MRCM will damage India because it doesnt have website clause. This is only meant to confuse recallists, and help Dhanvapasi type proposals. In MRCM proposal, the apex officer is recallable/replaceable by voters of India, and staff can be fined/expelled/imprisoned by Jurors. So apex officers and staff will try their best to create a good website, and so I didnt add any such useless clauses. As such, 10s of spurious clauses can be added that officers must address complaint in 15 days, officers must come to office in time and so on. IMO, such clauses are spurious in a law-draft
.
(6) Many fake recallists are calling activists and say "pls send sms to some private phno , register yourself on private website, send voterid xerox to some private office and ask all to take these steps" and MRCM will come in India !! They are only promoting private website and have nothing to do with MRCM or RTR.
.
So Dhanvapasi is just a nonsense. Pls inform all why Minerals3 = MRCM is far better proposal.
