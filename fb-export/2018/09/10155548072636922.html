Why a clause like "officer shall make a citizen verifiable website" is useless clause and also harmful --- because higher judge can use this clause to harass a good officer
.
Words "citizen verifiable" do NOT have universally accepted definition. In fact, I myself dont know what citizen verifiable is. Say X says "Modiji or voter-X supports or opposes RtrDeo3-law-draft". Is it verifiable by citizens of India? In theory, citizens can ask Modiji or voter-X on twitter. But can Modiji or even his staff reply to even 1 lakh question askers? And say Modiji declares his statements on instagram. Then how can those who are NOT on instagram know? And there are 10s of large websites social media website and 100s of small ones.
.
Next say DEO website says "Deo paid Rs X as total salary to N teachers and also gives lists and voterids of all teachers but doesnt give amount paid to each teacher". Then is this statement citizen-verifiable? NO.
.
And lets say DEO also names all teachers and amounts paid to each teacher. Then a citizen calls teacher and teacher refuses to reply.IOW, citizen could NOT verify. So what is next? Should banks be asked to reply to citizens' queries? Has such law has been proposed by person who is putting "citizen verifiable" clause?
.
And lastly, given the COMPLETE ABSENCE of definition of citizen verifiable, a high court judge can interprete the word in his own way, and harass the officer by saying ":why didnt you implement a 'citizen verifiable' website as the law requires you to implement?"
.
So stay away from clauses which ask officers to implement what is NOT defined.
.
Then how can voters "force" officers to implement good websites?
.
Imo, RTR/Jury processes are enough.
.
It is understood that officer has to make good website. Now there are 700 DEO (or district level officers for a given task). Each one will implement some features on his private and official website and each one will put some data. And activists and voters can compare and contrast information/features of DEOs and ask their DEOs to improve website's data/features. And if DEO doesnt fix websites after 2-3 reminders via TCP/CV clauses, then Jurors can impose fines on DEO/staff. And if DEO doesnt improve website even after fines, then voters need to expel that DEO and bring someone who can give better website,. Now course, website is ONE OF THE MANY services and not only one. So finally, each voter will take a weighted decision based what he can get. But at the same time, DEO too will work to implement best of possible.
.
IOW, adding clause like "DEO will give website with this, this that [defined] features and that those [undefined] features" will only higher judge to harass a good DEO and waste his time by calling him in court again and again...
