The RTR-,movement is draft-based i.e. activists read proposed-law-drafts and publicize it and ask voters to DIRECTLY send tweets/postcards/votes to PM/CM to print these law-drafts. And as number of voters who have voted on newindia, sent postcards and tweeted increases, the chances that the law-drafts will get passed will also increase.
.
So now movement can be sabotaged by congress/rss/aap workers and also those who want credit of drafting or want to control movement. They can simply propose "good looking but useless" VARIATIONS, and then a new set of drafts, and ask activists to oppose original drafts and support new drafts.
.
Also, if the SPURIOUS VARIATION PROPOSER has too much time on hand, or he is well funded, then he can pester 100s of activists, and can project as SPURIOUS VARIATIONS as substantial. And the original draft proposer can be serious activist who has 10s of other personal/activism tasks, and may not be able to find time to reply for all such spurious proposed variations.
.
Many drafts are separate additions. eg consider Jury Trial on Theft cases. It is NOT part of JurySys3. JurySys3 deals only with crimes where punishment is over 7 years or serious crimes against women, such as murder etc. The Jury Trial over Theft and several other TYPES of cases will come as LATER additions via TCP/CV clauses.
.
The person who wants sabotage will NEVER propose separate ADDITIONS, and insist that EXISTING ORIGINAL DRAFT should be opposed and new draft should be publicized.
.
So how can activist decide AT INDIVIDUAL level without centralization, that a proposed VARIATION is spurious or substantial?.
.
In this album, I have proposed some thought exercises that activist can do and tests activist can apply on VARIATION and VARIATION proposers , and determine whether proposed variation is SPURIOUS or substantial, and whether it must added in existing draft or can be taken as a SEPARATE addition?
