Ethics of copying, modifying and adding a proposed-law-draft.
.
Proposed law-drafts are legally speaking private properties and copyright laws apply. But ethically speaking, they cease to be private property. But some ethics do apply when a proposed-law-draft is copied/modified
.
And activist needs to work in network. And network comes when activists know about what OTHER activists did, and they refused to do. And from that each activist can guess about MOTIVES of other fellow activists, and then decide to whom to read and ignore. Right information about activists improves activism and benefits nation. And false information or creating a situation that leads to communication of FALSE information worsens activism.
.
So if activist-A writes a 25 page long draft, and then activist-B tells newcomer activist-C (or creates posts that creates such impression before newcomer activist-C) that activist-B wrote whole 25 pages !!! This is unethical and damages activism because activist-C will now not know about works/motives of A or B.
.
So if activist-B wants to oppose draft-A and support his modification/extension of draft-A , then it is NOT ethical, provided his status posts and pdfs BOTH have clear mention of which sections were there in original-draft "as is", and a a separate section showing modification/additions he made.
.
If above is NOT done, his misguiding newcomers by creating impression that he is the original author of WHOLE draft !!! And this damages activism.
.
And activist should never file a case against activist for defamation. And never file case for copying/modifying draft but ONLY IF copying/modification is a CLEAR way that gives FULL information to newcomer in status-post as well as pdfs. .
If status post OR pdf does NOT have separate sections of ORIGINAL DRAFT, and modification/addition, then there is NO way for activist-A i.e. original draft write to file a copyright violation case. And in such case, copyright violation case becomes ethical
