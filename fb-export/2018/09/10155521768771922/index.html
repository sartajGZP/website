How anti-recallists or psuedo-recallists use exaggerated strength of paidmediamen create fear against proposed RTR-law-drafts - by creating a FALSE scenario that elitemen can create thousands and lakhs of paidmedia campaigns !!!
.
One of the arguments of anti-recallists (or psuedo-recallists) use against all proposed RTR-law-drafts is --- (a) RTR-law-drafts and JurySys-law-drafts put power in hands of voters, and (b) paidmediamen can manipulate voter opinions and Juror opinions and so RTR/Jury-law-drafts MUST be postponed till XYZ conditions are met !!
.
Their main plank is --- they show paidmediamen as supremely powerful, more powerful than 10 Gods, who can convince voters that day is night and night is day !!!
.
And THEY IGNORE THE COST THAT PAIDMEDIA CAMPAIGNS NEED.
.
When RTR-law-drafts comes, voters get power to expel / replace / retain / bringback 600 District Education Officers, 600 District Police Commissioners, 600 District judges, 600 District Public Prosecutors i.e. some 25 nodal officers in each district i.e. about 25 * 600 = 150,000 District level offices. And Juries get power to expel all 10000s of Municipal / State officers below the rank of District Nodal Officers such as collectors.
.
And voters get power to replace 30 CMs, 30 High Court Chief judges, 30 State Chief Public Prosecutors, all High Court judges and so on. So there are 30 states, and some 50 to 200 officers / judges in each state (depending on size of State's apex bureaucracy / judiciary ). So at State level , voters get procedure to expel / replace / retain / bringback 30 * 100 = 3000 state level apex officials
.
And at national level they almost all Ministers, apex PSU chairman, SCCj, SCjs, RBI Chairman, RBI Directors, SBI Chairman etc become recallable/replaceable. So at national level, some 20 to 50 positions become recallable / replaceable.
.
Now can paidmediamen manipulate opinion on ALL positions?
.
And if they fail to manipulate opinions even on 10% positions, then the 100% good individuals at apex, using their official powers, can dig out all data and expose remaining 90%. One good judge is enough to expose all nepotism in all courts,. One good Minister is enough to expose murky deals of ALL Ministers.
.
One clean District Education cant get data of other DEOs, but he can surely frame RIGHT QUESTIONS and send to all voters across states, that will expose all DEOs. Worse, a good DEO can also frame right questions that expose ALL police chiefs, all judges, all public prosecutors etc and send these questions to all voters across District / State.
.
So even if paidmedia is successful in putting elitemen's puppets in 90% places, the remaining 10% will do so much expose in 2-3 months, that subsequent recalls will ensure replacement of all their puppets.
.
So once RTR/Jury-law-drafts are printed, elitemen will need to run LAKHS of paidmedia campaign to stop good guys from coming from 100% of 1000s of district level / state level positions.
.
Each paidmedia campaign has to run for several days. And for all those days, 24 hour coverage has to be given. eg consider paidmediamen campaign that was run for Anna during 4-apr-2011 and 11-apr-2011. The campaign had to be run almost 24 hours for seven days. And then weeks and months or coverage had to be given to install ArKe as a leader. The costs of each campaign is 1000s of crores of rupees
.
So when RTR comes over 1000s of district level positions across India, and 10s of State level positions across India, and 10s of National Level positions, the elitemen will need to run 1000s of campaigns every MONTH to ensure that ONLY their puppets come on ALL positions.
.
======================
.
Cost estimates of 1000s of paidmedia campaigns
------------------------------------------------------
.
So lets do how much will it cost elitemen to stop good guys from coning at all positions across India
.
district level paidmedia campaign cost = Rs 50 crore at least. state level paidmedia campaign cost = Rs 1000 cr average
national level paidmedia campaign cost = Rs 20000 crores. .
.
district level positions = at least 30
state level positions = at least 30
national level positions = at least 20
.
number of districts = 600
number of states = 20 (club many small states as one)
nation = 1
.
so cost of all district level paidmedia campaign to get bad guy in and stop good guy
= 30 positions per district * 600 district * Rs 50 crore per campaign
= 18000 * Rs 50 crores
= Rs 900,000 crores
.
so cost of all state level paidmedia campaign to get bad guy in and stop good guy
= 30 positions per state * 20 states * Rs 1000 crore per campaign
= 600 * Rs 1000 crores
= Rs 600,000 crores
.
cost of all national level paidmedia campaign to get bad guy in and stop good guy
= 20 positions across India * 1 nation * Rs 20000 crore per campaign
= Rs 400,000 crores
.
Total = Rs 900,000 + Rs 600,000 + Rs 400,000 = Rs 29,00,000 crores !!
.
This is close to ALL taxes collected by center / state / municipalities across India. And this is almost 15% of India's total GDP !!
.
Well, elitemen cant spend even 1/10th of the money they need to block all good guys and putting all their puppets at even 10% of the district / state / national positions.
.
And say elitemen and paidmediamen managed to put their puppets on these positions. But in RTR-drafts, the voters can replace / expel anyday !!! So elitemen will need to spend crores of rupees PER POSITION every week to hide facts about good guys and promote lies about their bad guys. Now you multiply amounts, by number of positions per state / district and number of states / district . No one has such money
.
Another way to analyse is to take per citizen PER POSITION cost. Each citizen has now power to replace / expel / retain bringback people at 80 positions across distroict / state / India. If paidmedia expense per position per voter is Rs 100, then cost to manipulate opnions of 50 crore voters for 80 positions is
= 80 positions * 50 crore voters * Rs 100 per voter per position
= Rs 400,000 crores AT LEAST.
.
And recall / replacement is possible anyday. So everyweek, paidmediamen will need to print lies about puppets and lies about good guys. So the cost now becomes alnost Rs 400,000 PER WEEK !!! . No one has that kind of money/
.

.
So how do elitemen prevail using paidmediamen today?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
.
A voters wants a good PM / CM. And so he has no choice but to vote for MP / MLA candidate of the party to which hos preferred PM candidate belongs to.
.
So defacto, whole nation is electing only one PM, and only 25-30 CMs only !! The election of 543 MPs and 5000 MLAs is only a formality to elect that one MP and 25 - 30 CMs !!! And election of PM / CM comes only once in 5 years,
.
So number of POSITIONS for which voters EFFECTIVELY vote today are few --- only 2 per state (PM / CM). So today, elitemen and paidmediamen prevail due to lack of voting opportunities that voters have.
.
And at PM / CM level, voters dont have "word of mouth" to know performance,. eg Voters of Ahmedabad can know about performance of DEO of Rajkot thru his friends etc in Rajkot. So PAIDMEDIA campaign will have zero effect in DEO campaigns - because voters have firsthand info from people they know. But in PM / CM, population is so large, and number of tasks are so many --- that one cant evaluate. So one goes by 2-3 major problems like crimes, law-order, islamist problem etc, and casts his votes. And so paidmediamen prevail.

Elietemen dont have money to manipulate so many opnions,.
