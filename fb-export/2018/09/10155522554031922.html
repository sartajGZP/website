Can elitemen via paidmediamen effect even 1% of district level officer recalls? NO. What about state / national recalls? NO.
.
One argument used by pseudo-recallists and anti-recallists is that elitemen using paidmediamen will convince voters to replace good officer with a bad one. So so RTR should not come or should come with several pr-conditions and co-conditions. And pre-conditions and co-conditions are used for stalling. To be specific, many pseudo-recitalists say that #RtrDeo3-law-draft submitted at newindia , in /causes/RtrDeo3 will damage India because elitemen using paidmediamen will manage to convince voters to replace a good officer with a bad person
.
Let me explain, why such claims are nonsense
.
=========
.
(1) First, what is media budget of elitemen? Total GDP of India is Rs 200 lakh crores , march-2018 , at current price levels. The TOTAL advertisment expenditure of ALL media -- print, tv, net etc including govt advts and non-elitemen was Rs 70000 crore i.e. about 0.35% of GDP . Let say elitemen manage to increase it to Rs 200,000 crore i.e. 1% of India's GDP.
.
(2) The cost of a district level campaign to fool voters will cost at least Rs 20 crore. Paidmedia campaigns to promote lies are NOT cheap, because one has to run a media bytes in newspaper and TV again and again and again. State level campaigns cost Rs 200 crore and national level campaigns will cost Rs 2000 crore.
.
(3) At district level, I have proposed RTR over some 30 positions, such as District Education Officer , Rtr Public Prosecutor etc. . There are about 700 districts in India. So we have RTR at at some 21000 psoitions. And I have proposed RTR at some 30 imp posiitions at state level, and 30 imp positions at national. There are 20 major states, and so we have 600 state level RTR.
.
(4) Now say elitemen want their puppets to come at all positions or most positions. To install puppet at one district level position they need to spend Rs 20 crore for a district level paidmedia campaign. For each RTR, each position is separate, each position has separate persons, and so campaign for one will have NO impact on campaign for another. Likeiwise, all state level campaigns, and most national level campaigns are independent.
.
(5) So say elitemen decide to spend all their Rs 200,000 crores to campaign for district level positions. Then also, they can run only at most 10000 campaigns. IOW, for remaining 10000 positions, they cant even afford to have a paidmedia campaign to promote their puppets
.
(6) And its not that their campaigns will be successful all the time.
.
(7) So for half the district level positions, they cant even run a paidmedia campaign. And in other half, they cannot be always successful. IOW, in most positions, elitemen will fail to fool voters from putting good guys at the positions
.
(8) Now even if 10% nodal district level positions occupied by good guys , these good guys can expose remaining 90% within weeks. Today, they cant expose, because if they try to expose, then Ministers will transfer them, But RTR makes it impossible for a Minister or apex IAS to transfer a district level officer which has approval of voters. So good district level officers will expose all other puppets using his own official resources and his influence over voters and activists. And Ministers and apex IAS will NOT be able to transfer or expel them.
.
(9) Now RTR-law-drafts I have proposed make it possible for voters to replace officers anyday.
.
(10) So HYPOTHETICALLY, say elitemen via paidmediamen convinced voters to replace a good officer with bad officer. But once other good officers and activists expose this bad officer, the voters can start approving previous good officers and cancel approvals of bad officers. So again, elitemen will need to run a paidmedia campaign.
.
(11) IOW< to ensure that bad pupper continues, elitemen will have to spend Rs 20 crore on district level paidmedia campaign almost 4 - 6 times year !!
.
So much would cost elitemen to install their puppets at 3000 district level positions? They need at least 4 campaigns a YEAR, for each of these 3000 positions. So they need 12000 district level paidmedia campaign every year. Each district level campaign will cost them Rs 20 crore. So they need = 12000 * Rs 20 crore = Rs 240,000 crores !!! This is more than 3 times the TOTAL advertisement budget of India !!
.
(12) This crux of RTR-law-drafts I have proposed like #RtrDeo3. If paidmedia funding is nil, then good guys will manage to defeat bad guys. And if a paidmedia campaign is used for district level positition, then one campaign will be needed EVERY 2-4 months to ensure that bad guy keep looking good !!! So cost is NOT Rs 20 crores for 5 years, but cost is Rs 60 crore EVERY YEAR !!!! So with existing budget of Rs 70000 crores, elitemen can fund no than 1200 out of 21000 district positions in India. IOW, as many as 95% positions will have non-puppets !! And these non-pupperts using their official resources, influence over voters etc, will expose all puppets at district / state / national levels !!
.
(13) Also, money comes to elitemen from mineral loot and land they own. MRCM decreases their mineral income to 5% of what is now or even lesser, And wealth ta will reduce the value of land to 10% of what is now, and so will also reduce the money they make from land sales. So their abiklity to pay mediamen will DRASTICALLY reduce from existing Rs 70000 crore to 1/10th of what is now !!
.
(14) Suffices to say, when you put following numbers of paper ---
(a) number of districts
(b) number of district level positions on which RTR comes
(c) fact that paidmedia campaigns for bad guys are needed round the year, and NOT once in 5 years
(d) strength of elitemen reduces after MRCM / wealth tax
(e) one good guy at even deputy collector level position can expose 10s of bad guys, if Ministers cant transfer him (and RTR makes Ministerial transfer impossible)

It becomes clear that elitemen cant damage #RtrDeo3 and other procedures.
.
This is ONE explanation. There are several valid explanations for same. In next post, I will explain using other set of calculations, that RTR-procedures I have propposed are immune to elitemen's paidmedia campaigns, due to sheer size

So how do paidmedia campaign suceed today? Because today, we elect PM / CM and so we have NO option but to vote for MP / MLA of that party. eg Those who hate congress have no option but to vote for Modiji, and RSS's MP candidates. So effectively, we have ONLY 2 elections in 5 years --- one for PM and one for CM (or state ruling party). !! So number of elections are small --- only 2, and so elitemen can manage using paidmedia.
.
Also, another major factor is that voter doesnt have option of approving more than one candidate (approval system or range voting or preferential system). So those who hate congress cant approve a good indepenent and have only one option -- RSS . But RTR procedures I have proposed have APPROVAL system and later use range voting. So say paidmediamen convince that puppet-A is good guy, then voter will approve A, but he need NOT cancel approval of guy B whom he thinks is also good. So now paidmediamen will have to run a heavy campaign to malign ALL good guys !!! So costs shoot up to several times.
.
IOW, approval voting (which later changes to range voting where voter can give score between -100 and 100) further makes it impossible for elitemen to use paidmediamen to malign all good guys and project puppet as a good guy.
