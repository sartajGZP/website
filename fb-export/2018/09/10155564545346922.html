Why statements made in video are useless and why tweets or public FB posts are MUST?
.
Because tweet/fbstatus can be archived on archive dot org, with link and date (for fb statuses, pls use mbasic dot facebook dot com link to statuses , and not facebook dot com links to statuses)
.
Whereas archive dot org does NOT archive vidoes
.
Also, on videos. person can say thing in AMBIGOUS way, and listener, and then speaker can say "I said that and not that".
.
Thats why, many times, courts etc ask people to make statement in writing as well as orally. And many govt offices ask for written statements.
.
So if one refuses to make a statement in writing on twitter and FB statuses, then pls ignore him as a non serious person, and just a noise raiser
.

All in all, if a person claims in vague words, but refuses to make clear statements on twitter/FB, and with PROPER hashtags, then pls ignore him as noise makers

.
In video, if a person makes vague ambiguous statement, then chances are high that listener will mistakenly end up deducing what listener wants to infer.And speaker can take advantage of this drawback, and make vague ambigious statements in video
,.
But if same person is asked to WRITE his statements on twitter and FB-status, then most reader will IMMEDIATELY notice that he is making vague and ambiguous statements.
.
Because in video it is difficult to go back exactly to the beginning of statement. In text, there are fullstops and comma, which tell reader where the sentences begin,. There are spaces which tell where para starts and para ends. So one can easily re-read a statement or para. But in video, I cant slide back exactly one sentence or one para.
.
If two statements in my text contradict, then reader can easily dig them out by simply moving eyes, and locating those two statements. But if my two statements in video contradict each other, then listner will have hard time locating my previous statement.
.
So imo, if a person is honest, he will put all his statements in FB posts, and put final action point in twitter. He may make video for explanation. But if a person ONLY wants to put claims in video and does NOT want to put same claims in twitter/FB, then I would request all to see that as sign of malafide motives. In such case, pls ask person to put statements in twitter/FB, and if he refuses, then pls inform as many as possible that he has refused to give specific answer to specific questions on twitter/FB. And call it end.
