How proposed RtrDeo3-law-draft will improve education
.
(RtrDeo3 law-draft is given in pdf at https://newindia.in/causes/RtrDeo3 . see last para is cause is deleted by PmoStaff).

(1) So RtrDeo3 GN is printed in GN by CM
.
(2) There are about 700 DEOs in India as of now. And there are many goo private individuals who have run excellent schools and excellent tuition classes providing education.
.
(3) Many private individuals will register themselves as DEO candidates and will publicize their proposals to improve education
,
(4) Some of the DEOs will start taking steps to improve school education. Today, they dont dare to take such steps, because if the try, then Ministers will transfer them. Because taking such steps require DEO to stop taking bribes from private school owners, and Ministers and apex-education-IAS dont want that to end. But RtrDeo3 give procedures to citizens to replace A by B, thereby expel A, and also retain A and stop his transfers, and also bring back A if A is transferred. So some honest DEOs out of these 700 existing DEOs may take a calculated risk to bring changes that causes improvement in education.
.
(5) Now how will voters of THAT district know --- the voters of THAT district in which DEO is bringing changes will know from their own personal experience, the experience of their own children in schools, and via information shown by DEO on his private website and will send information to parents etc thru postal mails or thru whatsapp. In addition, the candidates who want to become DEO will also put information of their own websites, whatsapp etc.
.
(6) In addition, DEO and candidates can list all public information on their private websites / newsletters in SYSTEMATIC way that can explain problems and solutions to voters. Today, many at apex levels know problems (like ridiculously high salaries of staff, too rigid licensing requirements to start new schools, too much regulation on schools, RTE nonsense, high land prices) that has worsened education. And they have their proposed solutions. But today, there is no incentive to put out such information. So today, only activists work to publicize this information. But with RtrDeo, some of the existing 700 DEOs and 10000s of DEO candidates across India will start to publicize specific information about problems and their proposed solutions, to voters.

(7) The DEO with citizens' approval and popular candidates will now have some direct or via activists communication links with voters. The communication can be via newsletters, pamphlets, DEO's private websites, fb, whatsapp etc.
.
(8) Thru friends and family members in near by Districts, the voters will also come to know about changes DEOs in neighboring districts are making.
.
(9) So Ministers can either transfer such good DEOs or let them work. If Ministers try to transfer them, then demand for proposed Rtr-Education-Minister-law-draft will grow and demand for proposed Rtr-CM-law-draft will also grow. In any case, some existing DEOs or candidates will manage to become DEO with citizens' approval. After that, whole scenario comes in second gear
.
(10) Now DEO will citizens' approval will speed up changes they are bringing and will also work to publicize other proposals such as JurySys overt Education Dept Staff, Saatya System, RTR over State Education Ministers, RTR over Central Education Minister etc.
.
(11) Other DEOs will have to bring changes that one good DEO brings. eg one DEO can implement Saatya System, and publicize on his private website, and later his Govt website (when he gets control over part of the DEO website).
.
(12) So other DEOs will have to bring changes or voters may replace by less bad DEOs / candidates. And this school education in other districts will also improve
.

replies to spurious scenarios
.
(1) Say X is good DEO and A, B, C , D etc are candidates worse than X, The paidmedia will convince voters that A is better than X and get X expelled, and A appointed as DEO
.
Ans : Each district level paidmedia campaign costs Rs 20 crores at least. So installing bad DEOs in 700 districts will cost Rs 14000 crores. Now TOTAL advertisement budget of India in 1-apr-2017 to 31-mar-2018 was Rs 70000 crores. And RTR will come at 20 district level positions , 20 state level positions and 20 central positions. So paidmedia campaigns for even 1% of these positions isnt possible for elitemen. For details, pls see post at http://fb.com/10155522554031922
.
