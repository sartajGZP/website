Proposed amendments to proposed Dhanvapasi bill, to reduce HIGH damages that Dhanvapsi may cause. Analysis and drafts
.
https://www.facebook.com/groups/RcmFiles/permalink/545284939241779/
.
----
.
I oppose the proposed Dhanvapasi law-draft.
.
I oppose the proposed Dhanvapasi law-draft, as proposed on 20-sep-2018, given at the link https://www.dhanvapasi.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/DhanVapasiBill.pdf .
.
Dhanvapasi proposal was proposed by Shri Rajesh Jain in around jun-2018 and detailed Dhanvapasi Bill was given by him in around 20-sep-2018 at the above link. And imo this Dhanvapasi is going to be bigger than Janlokpal Nonsense and it will be more damaging than Janlokpal/Lokpal Nonsenses. Janlokpal was a very bad proposal, but thankfully it never got passed. But Janlokpal did certainly wasted away a huge amount of time and energy of activists and brought bad name to activism. But imo chances are high that Dhanvapasi will get passed and it will do a huge damage to India, over and above wasting activist’s time and energy.
.
Imo, DV bill conceptually has two broad parts. Imo, it proposes that (1) all mineral mines and all “excess” Govt plots shall be SOLD away to private Indian companies or private foreign companies and (2) money obtained from sale will be divided amongst all citizens of India. The part-2 is indeed imo a noble proposal, which I would support. But given that part-1 is extremely damaging to citizens of India, the whole DV proposal is imo damaging and I oppose it.
.
Why is part-1 of DV damaging? Because here, mineral mines will be sold away with all minerals below the ground, and so per tonne royalty after sale will be near zero. And govt plots too will be sold, and not rented, and so income from that plot after sale will be near zero. There are high chances that buyers can be foreign companies, or Indian companies who have already taken loans from Indian banks. And once all mineral mines and plots are sold away to private/foreign buyers, the new owners can form a cartel/nexus, reduce supply of minerals and plots, and thus make the mineral price and land prices/rents go sky high.
.
So Indian citizens as a whole will end up paying much higher price for minerals and land. So imo Dhanvapasi will INCREASE poverty of citizens of India in long term. And Dhanvapasi does NOT necessarily reduce poverty by much in short term because money obtained may be very low when such huge amount of land / mines are all auctioned within say 10 years to 20 years.
.
The MRCM proposal that I have been campaigning for since oct-1999 till date, is completely DIFFERENT from Dhanvapasi. MRCM too has two parts. As per MRCM, (1a) the mineral mines should NOT be sold but highest possible mineral royalty should be collected from private mining companies or govt mining companies using auctions (1b) govt plots should NOT be sold but rented for lease of 1 year to at most 25 years. (1c) so in MRCM, income is forever, and lease holders cannot form cartel and reduce supply as some leases expire every month and so new auctions land/mineral are there every month. (2) and royalties and rents would be almost equally divided in citizens of India every month.
.
My proposed MRCM-law-draft is at https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 . A backup link is http://fb.com/534996790270594 , in case admins at newindia.in delete this Minerals3 proposal. (newindia dot in is central govt website for petitioning). .
.
So part-2 of Dhanvapasi and part-2 of MRCM are alike. But part-1 of Dhanvapasi is so nefarious and damaging to India that comparing MRCM and Dhanvapasi is imo pointless. Also, there are some other laws I have proposed such as WOIC (Wholly Owned by Indians Company) which eventually will prohibit foreign companies from mineral mining and owning land in India. IOW, my proposed MRCM law-draft ensures that all minerals and all Govt plots remain property of Indian citizens forever. Dhanvapasi will do just the opposite – there are high chances that it will end up selling away all govt plots and mines to foreign companies !!
.
Also, Dhanvapasi has no provision for Right to Recall apex officers, it has no provision for internal Jury System and has no system for citizens to make their complaints/opinions public to all citizens across India. So if apex officers in Dhanvapasi become corrupt or nexused, then only recourse citizens have is to complaint before PM and Supreme judges, and we know how non corrupt and how fast these two apex authorities are. Whereas MRCM has provision for Right to Recall apex officer, internal Jury System and also CV-clauses for putting complains/opinions of all voters of India on govt website , in a way all voters of India can see.
.
how can we TRUE activists deal with Dhanvapasi?
-------------------------------------------------------------------
.
So how can we activists deal with Dhanvapasi proposal?
My suggestion are –

(a) Imo true activists should propose amendments to Dhanvapasi, which make it good law. And if law-sponsors oppose these amendments, then activists may be able to expose them in public. And if sponsors accept these amendments, then damages that can come due to Dhanvapasi will not come.

(b) Imo true activists should also propose good proposals wrt mineral royalties and govt plots. Imo, the most important reason why proposals like Dhanvapasi are spreading is because activists did NOT promote good proposals like MRCM !! The issue of minerals mines and govt plots is huge, and ignoring good proposals to deal with this will create vacuum which will be filled by bad proposals. So opposing Dhanvapasi is necessary, but NOT promoting a good proposal dealing with mineral mines and govt plot will benefit Dhanavapasi.
.
And before one proposes or supports amendments, one should make it clear on twitter whether he supports existing Dhanvapasi Bill or he oppose it. I have already put a tweet that I oppose Dhanvapasi Bill. Pls see https://twitter.com/MehtaRahulC/status/1044464404081901568
.
