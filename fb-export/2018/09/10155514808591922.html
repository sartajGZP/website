how/why GST causes most damage to "one man show" (or few men show) type manufacturing units, and wealthtax causes least damage to them?
.
Many highly productive units in manufacturing are one man show or a few men show, where 1 person or 2-3 close friends / relatives manage all workers, they do all accounting, do all reporting to govt etc and also do do all sales / marketing etc
.
Now, such "one man show" or "few men show" may survive in SERVICES, because they dont have a supply chain and inventory to manage.And so inventory investment and investment of unsold goods, and also manage a time bound production schedule are non-issue or lesser issues in services or softwares type "goods". They are also lesser issues in trading.
.
Now in GST, eWayBill and now EPF and many other laws, compliance restrictions are so heavy that such "one man show" or "few men show" will collapse. Because there is no way they can meet compliance burden that GST and eWayBill system imposes on them.
.
And so what does nation lose? If these small "one man show" or "few men show" are shut down? Their business will pass to large companies. So what does nation lose?
.
Well, first loss to nation is efficiency. These one man show or few men show MANUFACTURING units are highly productive . If tax laws kill them, it then it would be a loss to whole economy
,.
Next, these one men show or few men show are also big trainning grounds for many many semi-skilled workers,. These owners supervise workers on hour by hour basis, and try to get best work with least damage,. The workers do feel suffocation under them, but workers learn the trader and gain skills under these owners. IOW, these small units are also big SKILL ADDERS to nation's population.
.
GST and eWayBill System is slaughtering all such "one man shows" and "few men shows" in MANUFACTURING. And as a result, our efficiency in as a economy will go down
.
We are already facing music in textiles and garments. In our DOMESTIC market, share of Indian manufacturers is DECREASING and share of Bangladeshi (i.e. chinese) is increasing !!! And we will face more music in almost all manufacturing !!
.
