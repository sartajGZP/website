(1) Say we upload a proposal on newindia and say we convince 1000 or 1 crore proposal supporters to vote on newindia. Say Pmo staff deletes that proposal. In that case, reputation of Pmo staff and PM himself in the eyes of ALL voters who had seen that proposal goes down for their own crime. And thats a huge gain for activists --- they were able to CONVINCE voters that PM is defunct
.
(2) Say an activist uploads a proposal on a PRIVATE website and activists manage to convince 1000 or 1 crore supporters to vote for that proposal on that private website. Say that private website owner gives copy of who voted for whom to all. Then one day, the private website owner deletes that proposal. Then what does PM loose ? NOTHING. His reputation doesnt go down at all. Who do elitemen lose? Only some money that they paid to website owner. But their reputation doesnt decrease as none can prove that money was given. And private website owner can keep silence on other activists' claims that private website owner deleted the copy. Voter can believe silence of website owner or believe the activist who is complaining about deletion. The activists lose due to their infighting. neither PM nor elitemen lose anything.
.

.
So voting on private website is waste of activists' and voters time. Whereas voting on govt website will add strength to draft's movement OR lessen reputation of PM and PM's sponsors. So I request activists NOT to waste time in publicizing private voting wensites.
