Evaluating a proposed variation in a proposed law-draft - will variation clauses create any difference in efficiency in FIRST week or first month o even first six months? And if NO, then why is the demand to add the clauses TODAY?
.
Our RTR-movement is first DRAFT BASED movement, where I request activists/voters to decide if they wish to DIRECTLY ask PM/CM to print the law-draft, and if they wish to directly ask other activists/voters to do the same.
.
So one way to sabotage the movement is --- propose 10s of "handsome but useless" VARIATIONS. One can propose clause after clauses, along with claims that without these clauses, the draft will being death to India, and these variation clauses are must to deliver all benefits. And all clause will look handsome --- question is what is their use in FIRST week or FIRST FEW MONTHS?
.
If a proposed clause will no value in first few weeks or months, and then it can always be added later using CV/TCP clauses. But still, an activist claims, that "these clauses must be present on day one, or else, hell will break loose and if these clauses are present, then damage will be undone", then whole statement is a WHITE LIE. Because how can that clause prevent damage when that clause will remain materially defaco inactive for months?
.
eg consider proposed RtrDeo3-law-draft. And consider a proposed variation that "DEO shall put all present and past 10 years relevant non-confidential information on DEO website, and implement a user friend interface on DEO website".
.
And now consider two claims that
.
(claim-1) without this website clause, proposed RtrDeo3-law-draft will create damage i.e. India will worsen from what it is today.
.
(claim-2) with this website clause, no damage will happen and all benefits of the law shall come
.
(claim-3) On day one itself, THESE DEO websites and ALL this information, including past 5 years information, will enable voters to compare and contrast different DEOs across India and decide which DEO candidates to approve in RTR-procedure.
.
(show-off) And then one can add fancy name to that website and add fancy buzzwords like "nationalist", "patriotic", "verifiable", "voter-verifiable","justice", "social justice" and 100s of buzzwords that go around.
.
Now lets evaluate these claims i.e lets compare EFFICIENCY of RtrDeo3 and (RtrDeo3 + website-clause).
,
(A) It will take weeks for best DEO to make a good website and months to put PRESENT data and MANY MONTHS to put past data
.
(B) It will take weeks for DEO to create policy that will enable staff to decide which data is automatically non-confidential and which is confidential and which data is in gray area. eg Total salary paid to all teachers is non-confidential, but salary paid to a teacher is as per existing law confidential
.
(C) And as of today, DEO has NO control over DEO-website to begin with !!! DEO website is run by Ministry. DEO has ONLY some user level screens. Now DEO can indeed start putting all relevent non-confidential publish information on his PRIVATE website from day one. But DEO will need to convince voters to get more Gazette Notifications printed via TCP / CV so that he get a portion DE website.
.
One can list several issues here. The point being --- for several weeks/months, a clause like "DEO shall make usefriendly website with all present and past 5 years data" will have no value. And even after that, imo, this clause has NO value. Because good DEO will start making good website and put all public data from day one. First, he will use his private website, and then when gets control over DEO website, he will put data on DEO website.
.
And consider (claim-3) . It claims that proposed variation i.e. "proposed good DEO website" will become useful from day-1, and hides the fact that good DEO website wont be available for weeks !! So how can it possibly become useful - do good or save from bad - on day one?
.
And the next claim can be dangerous. See, "RTR without a good govt websites can ruin India, and so we should FIRST make good govt websites, and ONLY after that we should bring RTR" !!! IOW, the "handsome but useless" clauses can be first added as "saviour" and can then be used as points to delay !!
.
So as time goes, those who oppose RTR or want to fame or money or control or promote their private websites will NOT oppose RTR, but will pose as supporters and give arguments that cause delay. As Lord Krishna said, "in Kalyug, all thieves will wear dress of sadhus" !! Same way, anti-recallists will NOT oppose RTR, but will throw spanners for personal benefits. And one such spanner is -- propose a string of "handsome but useless additions, and claim that RTR-draft will do damage without these additions" !!!
.
So how can you evaluate a VARIATION? Pls do first test as --- does this proposed variation have any significant effect in first few weeks after in case the law-draft is printed? If not, then why is proposer insisting on MUST HAVE on day-1 ? !! And by looking at claims and other behavior, one can later deduce the motives.
