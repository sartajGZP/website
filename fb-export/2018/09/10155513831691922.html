How voting at private website is useless for activist/voters and huge money maker for website owner?
.
Central Govt has made a website named as newindia dot in, on which one can put causes and ask all voters across India to vote. The voters may put their twitterid as username and may put voterid in their twitter profile, and thus viewers can see which voter-ids voted for which causes. But website doesnt reveal voter's identity, except for namde put by the voter himself
.
Now newindia dot in admin can anyday cancel a cause. So efforts of activists will get wasted,. But some advantage will come ---- such deletion of causes will expose PM, because finally, it was his website and whole staff comes under his command
.
But if some private website owners sells the website or takes cash and deletes votes or deletes proposals, then PM or elitemen dont lose any reputation. The website owner can tell all that his copy is correct, and copy of voting long of everyone else is fake and tempered. The voters will blame A or B, but reputation of PM and elitemen remains intact.
.
So in private website too, there can be loss of voting record and loss of uploaded proposals, and same can be on newindia dot in. But on newindia dot in, if that happens, then PM's reputation goes down for his own staff's nefarious actions. And that is a huge plus point of TRUE activists,. Whereas when private website owner sells out, activists wasted their time and PM's reputation of reputation of PM's sponors remains intact
.
Also, many private website talk of transparency etc. And that website does NOT give out name of its owner or voter-id of the owner !! So much for their belief in transparency !!!.
.
So imo, activists should NOT waste time in voting on a private website. By doing so, they are ONLY making the website owner rich.
