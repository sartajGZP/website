Can narcotest be easily faked? NO.
.
In my proposed narcotest law-draft, the panel of MD level doctors (who will give sodium thiopental injection) will be made by CM. The doctor will be selected at random by Jurors.
.
Now say the case is high profile. Say panel has 100 doctors for entire state.
.
Then Jurors can pick 5 to 10 doctors at random just 3 days before narcotest and ask each to come with 20 bottles of sodium thiopental. Then Jurors at last moment will pick one doc at random, and then one bottle at random, and then test remaining 19 bottles for quality.

So now accused doesnt which which of the 5 to 10 doctors, out of panel of 100 doctors, will come, till 3 days ago. And then he doesnt which bottle will get picked at random. And then doctors knows that all remaining bottles will be tested thoroughly.
.
So now having inefrious chemical is too difficult - almost impossible
.
So right amount of sodium thiopental does each inside the body. And so accused will not be able to lie now.
.
Also, when an elitemen is involved in crime, generally, many more will be involved,. eg say a rich person has killed a few people in drunk driving. Then he has to bribe too many witnesses and policemen to get away. So even 2-3 policemen or other staff are chosen at random and narcotested, then truth will come out.
.
So narcotest is quite fool proof.
