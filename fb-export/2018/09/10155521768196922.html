Killing draft based movement by throwing series of spurious ADDITIONS (and variations) to proposed-drafts
.
Ours is a proposed-law-draft-based movement. We have proposed some 200-250 law-drafts. Almost each law-draft has Tcp clauses using which they can extended once passed. In fact, once any law-draft is passed, the Tcp clauses can be used to bring ALL other 200 law-drafts !! eg say #RrtrDeo3 is printed. Then using Tcp-clauses (aka CV-clauses) , activists can work to bring unrelated proposed-law-drafts like DDMCAM as well.
.
The one and ONLY question --- will printing a DRAFT as is, without any addition or variation, without any pre-condition or co-condition ----- will it DAMAGE India or improve India?
.
The same question can be put as ---- will situation of India improve after draft is printed or worsen? Will India / China and India / USA strength ratio improve of worsen?
.
So I will make my stand clear ---- imo, these drafts will improve condition of India i.e.. no draft will worsen condition of India along any dimension - education, manufacturing, weapon manufacturing etc.
.
And improvement is never ending process. More technology will bring more methods to criminals and enemies, and more laws will needed to deal with them.
.
------------------
.
But lately I have come across an interesting way to KILL our draft based movement ---- keep asking for additions of clauses till each 2 page to 10 page draft becomes a 50 page drafts.
.
Their FUD (Fear Uncertainty Doubt) technique is --- each ORIGINAL DRAFT is shown as something that will ruin India if passed, and then (draft + addition) is shown as magic remedy !!!
.
This is not new to me. When I first proposed RTR law-drafts in 1999, the typical congress/rss/aap (aap = cpi back then) claim was " unless India has 90% education of 70% education, these drafts will ruin India" !! And then "unless people of India FIRST become as nationalistic as people of Japan / USA, these RTR-drafts will ruin India". IOW, some pre-condition will be thrown as magic remedy and sentence will have praise of RTR-drafts, but praise will be followed by a "but ..." and then a pre-condition meant to waste time
.
-------------
.
HOW TO DEAL WITH SUCH ADDITION and CONDITION THROWER
.
(1) In each case, first see whether ORIGINAL DRAFT (minus additions proposed) will improve India or worsen India? It goes without saying that there are 100 possible additions and each addition will cause some improvement, Thats why almost all drafts have Tcp or CV clauses so that activists can add clauses or new laws with lesser eforts
.
(2) if you think that the ORIGINAL DRAFT (minus proposed additions) will improve India, then support the draft. And dismiss the persons (who claims that "Original Draft will ruin India , unless my proposed additions are added") as a LIAR and move on
.
(3) If you oppose ORIGINAL DRAFT, then fine. You can decide your own course ...
