Everyone has moral right to IGNORE a proposed law-drafts. I get over 100 proposed VARIATIONS in my draft every year, and if I read and comment on all of them, my 2 lifetimes will go only in reading proposals. And there are 3000 proposals on newindia alone, and lakhs of proposals on FB. So one has moral right to ignore a proposal.
.
But if one is proposing VARIATION of a original proposal, then activists must ask variation proposer to make his stand clear on twitter with proper HASHTAGS about base proposal. If he refuses, then his MOTIVES and PLANS are nefarious. Such person must be considered ONLY AFTER he makes proper statement on twitter/FB/both.
.
Registering opposition on twitter, with PROPER HASHTAG is must, because FB search is poor and FB posts tend to be long, and opposition can be vaguely worded. Whereas on twitter, if one doesnt use hashtags, then it will become clear to reader that he is evading the issue
.
Since ages we have used twitter to communicate support to proposals to @PmoIndia etc, and also state important political beliefs. Stating important political beliefs on TWITTER is important because only that makes it clear whether person is on this side or that side. And same way, declaring opposition to proposals which have become popular in media and proposal which one has created VARIATION is necessary.
.
-----------
.
Rest, opposing a proposal and supporting (proposal + variation) is NOT unethical. Its one's own ethical decision and a political choice. Motives of such person ALWAYS needs to be analysed. But action is NOT unethical. But it is necessary that it is FIRST support/opposition is stated in proper format on twitter and pdf also makes it clear.
