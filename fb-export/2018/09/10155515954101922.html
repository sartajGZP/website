(a) What comes first --- RTR over an officer or good behavior from him? (b) Is there is any to add clauses to make officers properly in RTR-law-drafts?
.
---
.
(a) My answer is RTR-clauses have to come first, and only then one can expect good behavior from him in general. A few officers, say 2% to 5% , may behave properly even in absence of RTR clauses. But they wont be able to do much --- because apex IAS , Ministers and judges, and also fellow IAS officers will block them from going far. And once RTR-law-draft comes, the officers who dont behave properly will be replaced by less bad ones, and so ALL will behave properly without asking. So IMO, clauses to enforce proper behavior , be disclosures or promptly attending complains, are pointless in absence of RTR-clauses,
.
(b) And once RTR-clauses come, imo, there is NO need to print clauses for good behavior, that can be implemented with by officer or his staff within a normal budget. Because officers who dont behave properly will get replaced by citizens using RTR-law-drafts.
.
As an example, consider Saatya System I have proposed for maths science education. This system is NOT part of proposed RTR-DEO-law-draft. Why not? Because imo, it is useless to add all these clauses. In absence of RTR-law-drafts, DEO will implement these clauses in worst possible way. And if RTR-law-draft is there, then DEO who doesnt implement Saatya System within available budget will get replaced by the one who does. So whole task of adding 100s of clauses to implement Saatya System is imo useless. So Saatya System details are NOT mentioned in #RtrDeo3 law-draft I have proposed.
.
-----------
.
So one can propose to add 10s and 10s of clauses in drafts wrt prompt disclosures, prompt deliveries, promote replies to complaints and so on. All these clauses are useless in absence of RTR / Jury. And once RTR / Jury / Tcp come, the Grand Jurors or Jurors or voters can issue these clauses as general instructions to officers. And in most cases, officers will implement them on their own without even asking.
.
So I dismiss whole exercise of adding disclosure clause , promote complain reddressal clauses etc as useless. Those who want to add these clauses can continue their campaign.
