Statistical Verifiability vs Specific Verifiability vs Universal Specific Verifiability vs General Feeling
.
For activism, Statistical Verifiability and General Feeling suffice. Only Law / Punishment / Courts etc need Specific Verifiability
.
(the topic is imp because many activists stall campaign steos by insisting on Specific Verifiability in activism, which is NOT needed at all)

Say I ask you question "what % of traffic constables take bribe - (a) below 20%, (b) between 20% to 40% .... (f) over 80% ". Then most will say "over 80%" or "between 60% and 80%". Now none ever tried to "verify" thus statistically or specifically
,.
Statistical Verification will be --- make list of all 1 lakh traffic constables in India, select say 1000 at random using PRIME NUMBERS and then verify them.
.
And SPECIFIC VERIFICATION will be verifying each and every of the 1 lakh.
.
--------------
.
Next question can be "how many voters in India believe that leader-X is better than leader-Y for the position of PM"?
.
Again, one can make a list of ALL 90 crore voters and tally their opinion.
.
But if an activist is looking for answer, then he doesnt need to get opinions of all 90 crore voters,. He can get list of 90 crore voters, and select say 10000 at random and ask them by contacting them. Or, a group of activists can first select ONE list of 10000 voters , and then each can visit the specific voters selected in his area
.
The key is -- whether selection was truely random or not? And did it assume ensure that each voter is EQUALLY likely to be selected after N voters were selected?
.
And one can keep selection procedure OPEN to all. That can be done by using public domain codes, which generates large random numbers and large prime numbers
.
Law / courts etc do need SPECIFIC VERIFIABILITY. eg lets say that statistical verification show that 80% IAS have assets more than income. Then we can NOT then select 80% of all at random and imprison them !! Here, we need SPECIFIC verification.
.
Likewise, if we want to decide Loksabha Member based on popularity, then again we can NOT use opinion polls, stakes here are so high, that govt officers may get bribed to pick non random and biased sample. So here we need UNIVERSAL POLL.
.
But say activist on his own wants to decide which candidate is more popular. Then he doesnt need to visit all 15 lakh voters in that constituency. He can decide by choosing 1000-2000 voters at random from the list. The activist wants to know for himself and so he has no reason to take a biased sample. .
.
Now what if two activist differ? Non-issue. Activists want to CHANGE opinion of voters and so for them it doesnt matter what is existing opinion of voters.
.
--------------
.
If Mahatma Udham Singh wants to know "what % of voters support law-draft-A", then he can take random sample and verify himself. Or when, % of supporters is high, GENERAL FEELING will suffice. eg Udham Singh didnt do any door to door survey asking 25 crore adult Indian citizens , asking them "shall I execute Governor Dwyer"? He did NOT need to take such survey. The GENERAL FEELING was sufficient that a huge majority of Indian citizens back them wanted execution of Governor Dwyer. So he went by GENERAL FEELING
.
Sometimes general feeling can be fake, and mere creation of paidmediamen. But activist can take a small sample from ENTIRE VOTER LIST of INDIA.
.

.
