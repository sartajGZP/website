common FALLACIES used by anti-recallists and pseudo-recallists to defame RTR / Jury / MRCM etc law-drafts, and how a NUMERICAL analysis can rebut their fake claims
.
Pls see list of fallaices at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fallacies
.
And in particular, pls see fallacy of composition --- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallacy_of_composition .
.
see wiki ---- " The fallacy of composition arises when one infers that something is true of the whole from the fact that it is true of some part of the whole (or even of every proper part). "
.
Fallacies of compsition in extereme is --- a person will claim a statement that may be true in just 1 out 10000 cases ; and then he will apply them on ALL 10000 cases !!!
.
A similar is fallacy of hasty generalization --- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faulty_generalization#Hasty_generalization
.
Hasty generalization usually shows the pattern

X is true for A.
X is true for B.
Therefore, X is true for C, D, E, etc.
.
===========
.
So here is how plot of pseudo-recallists or anti-recallists goes .
.
(1) elitemen will use paidmediamen to influence Jurors --- fine. But in how many of the 3 crore cases, can they create a campaign? Because each case has different parties, different issue and different context. So one campaign cant influence even 5 cases, and all 5 cases may not be running on same day in same district !!!
.
(2) How much money district level paidmedia campaigns, state level paidmedia campaigns and national level paidmedia campaigns cost?
.
(3) how much money can elitemen spare today? And how much can they spare after MRCM and wealthtax weakens them?
.
(4) so what % of 3 crore cases can they influence?
.
It turns out --- in 99.5% cases, they cant even finance a paidmedia campaign to influence Jurors !!
.
And in 5 out of 1000 cases, where they may be able to manage the campaign, in how many cadses will paidmedia campaign effect Jurors? Are they assuming without stating that all Jurors are dumb and will ignore arguments given by parties in court, and will listen only to paidmedia?
.
When all these factors are put , it will show that paidmedia can change judgment of Jurors in less than 1 in 10000 cases and will have NO effect in 9999 of the 10000 cases
.
And the possible effect in 1 out of 10000 cases is mentioned, and then applies on ALL 10000 cases
.
rebuttal is to do a NUMERICAL ANALYSIS of how much money elitemen can spare on paidmediamen, costs of paidmedia campaigns at district / state / national levels and how much money elitemen have today, and will have after MRCM , wealthtax and RTR/JurySys comes. And apply SUBJECTIVE part --- in how many cases can paidmedia wash effect of statements made by both parties.
.
When all numbers are put on paper, it becomes clear that they may be true in at most 1 out of 10000 cases, or even less. ...
