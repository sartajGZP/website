How RTR-law-draft such as #RtrDeo3 forces all DEOs to improve their own DEO websites
.
There are about 600 to 700 DEO = District Education Officers in India. I have proposed lawdraft https://newindia . in/causes/RtrDep3 which will create a procedure by which voters of district can replace DEO. Pls download draft from the above link.
.
The procedure enables a DEO to become DEO of upto 5 districts across state and 10 across India, if they have approvals of voters. And if they are DEOs of N districts, then their salaries will N times. And also, their chances of getting appointed State Education Minister by voters increases later
.
So now , how will DEO's websites will improve once RTR-DEO comes?
.
Say one DEO A improves website. Then ALL voters across India will be able to see, and will ask their DEOs to improve their respective website. And if he doesnt, then chances that citizens will approve A and replace their DEO with A increases
.
Now website will NOT be only factor. There are many factors, like maths / science exams, teaching material provides, how payments to teachers / students etc is scheduled and so on.
