Why proposal to add clauses like "officer must put past 10 years data or all current data on his govt website" etc in EXISTING law-drafts is imo spurious, and HARMFUL?
.
(1) many tasks have HUGE costs and need many skilled engineers which most district level officers dont have it today
.
(2) when RTR on an officer comes, imo he will automatically implement many such features in administration WITHOUT asking
.
(3) see other reasons below
.
=============
.
How about adding a clause that in RTR-PM draft that "PM shall ensure that every youth in India is employed"? Or how about printing a clause in RtrDeo3 that "DEO must ensure that every kid gets proper education". Or, why not add clause like "Officer shall bring heaven on earth" !! After all, God just said "Let there be light" and there was light. So maybe, we can write "let there be food for all" or "PM shall ensure that everyone has food" and there will be food.
.
My point is --- activists should confine to PROCEDURES and not make DECLARATIVE demands in drafts, unless there too simple and objective, and procedure to carry them out is known since ages and already existing in existing administration.
.
And activist should NOT add "wishlists" declarations in drafts. The drafts should have procedure that implement wishlist,
.
---
.
Now let me talk specifically about the clause "DEO must post past 10 years data on DEO website" Now lets say this is written in existing proposed RtrDeo draft. Say the draft passes, but RtrCM, Rtr-Education-Minister, RtrHccj draft and JurySys in High Court is yet to come.
.
So following will be problems
.
(1) Sat RTR DEO has come. Say voters bring a new DEO
.
(2) DEO website is run by State Education Ministry and NOT by DEO !! DEO is just a user with a few screens. DEO today has NO screens to put data not authorized by Ministry on DEO website. So how will he put ALL current data on DEO website? He cant. And there is no way he can put past data either
,.
(2) Now DEO can make a private website and put some all important public information that will be useful to students, parents and voters,. But he cant put past all current or past data of DEPARTMENT on his private website.
.
(3) IOW, clause like "DEO will put all current data and all past 10 years data on DEO website" is a clause that new DEO cant implement
.
(4) So now HCjs can harass him by sending notices saying "see, RtrDeo draft says that you must put all current and past 10 years of data on DEO website. Did you do that task?". DEO will surely give explanation. But harassment can be endless. eg One HCj used to call IAS to appear personally, and so IAS would come as 10 AM. And then HCj wont give any hearing till 4pm and then throw next date at 4pm !!
.
(5) Next -- what is IT budget of existing DEO? Almost zero. All he has is few data entry operator. He has zero coders of his own and no server of his own. All servers are owned by Ministry and all coders are under Ministry. So DEO has no way to implement a new better website
,.
So solution?
.
Say RtrDeo3-law-draft is passed. Then voters approve new DEO. Then that DEO can make a PRIVATE website and put all public information useful to students, parents, teachers, voters etc. And he can inform voters about bad laws and constraints , and make proposals using TCP / CV clauses, and send messages to voters to YES those proposals. And new proposals will get him power to make his own parallel OFFICIAL DEO website, along with website of Ministry, and power to put department's data on OFFICIAL website. Next, he has to get budget for servers and coders passed using public pressure using TCP. And ONLY after that, he can start putting data and webpages with meaningful code on website. And it will take him months to digitize the PAST data and enter it. One can simply scan the pages and upload JPG. That can be good start, but doesnt enable user to SEARCH on text. To search on text, one needs to convert all JPG into text using OCR and lot of manual work. And given that district offices have all papers in English as well as local language, it is a VOLUMINOUS task
.
So if a clause like "DEO shall put all current and past 10 years data on website", then till above tasks are done, that clause has no real value. Because no DEO will be able to put even current data on official website (except data permitted by Ministry) .
.
Next, even if such clause is there --- unless there is RTR-DEO and JurySys over him, he will put data in useless ways. And if RTR/Jury comes, then there is NO need for such clauses. The small fines by Jurors on DEO or staff for not taking proper steps on website and fear of expulsion will prompt DEO/staff to improve website as well other functions.
.
Anyone who has made websites or any code knows that GOOD websites and good code never come from law-drafts. The customers (here voters) make detailed proposals (thru TCP or other means), DEO and his coders will implement, activists will see mistakes or DELIBERATE fouls, will inform codes or Jurors or both about mistakes and/or fouls, and then they will get fixed and will evolve. This is VOLUMINOUS process involving DEO, coders, activists and voters, which punishments where need be, and also funding.
.
And lastly, who will decide punishment if DEO has uploaded only 50% of current data? And only 25% of past 10 years data? Judges or voters? If judges are to decide punishments, then RtrDeo3 draft will be useless. And if voters decide punishment uding RTR or Jury, then NO SUCH clause demanding "good website" is needed. One out of 700 DEOs will make a good website giving maximal disclosures, and all DEOs will be forced by their voters to implement that.
.
---
.
So imo adding a clause "DEO shall make a good website showing all current data and past 10 years data" , and then telling everyone that "see, this clause will create a good website, and without this clause, DEO will never make a good website" are TWO LIES rolled into one statement.
.
Such clause have no value on day-1 and no value till funds are given to /powers on DEO website are given to DEO. And even after that, 100s of actions need to taken by DEO, his coders, activists etc to report bugs or fouls, and then Jurors punishing coders or staff for mistakes or fouls. And such clause can be misused by Hccjs, if RTR-Hccj has not yet come. And above all, there is NO need for such clauses. RTR / Jury ensures that DEO will improve ALL facilities including websites, testing, teaching within his budget.
.
=============
.
So I want to restrict EXISTING proposed-law-drafts to clauses which can be implemented within few days or weeks after printed and clauses that can give real powers to citizens within days and weeks after passed. And tasks given to officers in EXISTING proposed-drafts should either within their existing powers or new powers needed should be EXPLICITLY stated in proposed-law-draft. And any positive change that RTR/Jury will bring or force need NOT be stated --- that only makes draft longer and adds no benefit.
.
So adding a clause that "DEO will put all current data and all past 10 years data on DEO website" is imo useless clause.
