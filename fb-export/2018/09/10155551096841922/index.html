A a nefarious minister/officer with nexuses will start putting data where mineral royalties are taken properly, and then he will claim "see, I dont have time, money and staff to put all data on web all in one day, and so pls give me some time to put all data" . .

So it is necessary that officers upload more important data first.

All this is HIGHLY subjective.
