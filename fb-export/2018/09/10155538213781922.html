Why proposal to add clause like "DEO should put put entire cheque-book passbook on website" in existing proposed RtrDeo law-draft is spurious
.
Existing law says that salaries of Employees are confidential i.e. they cant be made public. And similarly, existing laws make many expense details confidental.
.
DEO can disclose total salary paid. He can also give list like
.
number of clerks paid Rs X to Rs Y per month = N1
number of clerks paid Rs Y to Rs Z per month = N2
..
number of teachers paid Rs X to Rs Y per month = N5
etc etc
.
IOW, he can give totals, and sub-totals and sub-sub-totals in meaningful way.
.
But today he has NO power to disclose salaries and names
.
And proposed-RtrDeo3 is a GN to be signed by CM. So we should NOT put any clause that violates state/central law.
.
Now I have proposed a separate law that makes ALL salaries of all govt employees (except police/military) including PSUs and govt-funded bodies public, But till such is passed, DEO cant make salaries public.
.
Now if CHEQUE-BOOK is to be put on website, then following details will be needed to be put
.
(1) bank name, branch name, cheque number, date
(2) amount
(3) reciever name , reciever's panid or some id, account# of reciever, his bank name , his bank branch name
(4) ACTUAL reciever name, actual reciever's account details
(5) date on which cheque was given
(6) date on which cheque was cleared
.
And pls note (3) and (4). Without pan-id or some-id of reciever, the name has no use. And if reciever is company, then owners' name is also needed. And reciever name on chque and actuall name where cheque was deposited can be different. And sometimes, it is legal, and sometimes illegal.
.
Now does DEO have powers to put all this info on DEO website? NO.
.
Worse, existing laws PROHIBIT him from making salaries, name etc PUBLIC,
.
So till separate laws are printed that empower DEO to put some or all expenses on website, adding such clause in RtrDeo will have no value till those separate laws are passed
.
And once RtrDeo3 is printed, one out 700 existing or new DEOs will do expense reporting in proper format on DEO website or his own private website. On his own private website, he can put all information that can be made public as per TODAY's laws. And once one DEO does that, RTR procedure will force all DEOs to do the same.
.
And DEOs approved by voters or activists can put more disclosure related proposals via TCp/CV clauses. And so more expense related and other information will come.
.
So imo, adding a clause like "DEO will show all cheque details of all cheques on DEO website" will have zero effect on day-1. And expense reporting will improve WITHOUT any such clause once RTR/Jury comes over DEO and other officers. So many such clauses can be added using TCP today or later. But statement like "without such clauses, RtrDeo will do damage or will become defunct" are imo wrong statements.
