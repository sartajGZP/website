Making all RELEVANT govt information available to all --- why this needs RTR / JurySys first
.
Can we have setup where each voter gets access to relevant information within govt , without RTR / Jury?
.
My answer is --- NO.
.
Because unless there is threat on officers that citizens can PUNISH them if they were later caught hiding relevant information, the officers will hide as much as they can.
.
And most disclosure rules can evaded
.
eg when audit of Govt book improved, the Ministers / officers / judges promptly started giving more and more contract work to private companies, whose accounts are prima-facie onfidential !! And then they started en-masse privatization
.
eg some 10 years back, it was possible for Railway Ministers and Officers to siphone funds allocated for station maintenance and hide it. Today, since all books are on computers, it is difficult to hide. So what did Ministers do? They calmly started PRIVATIZING railway station. So now books are maintained by private companies, which will further
give contract to private comp[anies,. and this most details never come on govt records.
.
And consider the cases of MISUSE of discretionary powers. The officer can always cook data that will look use of
discretionary power bonafide, until a Jury looks into it.
.
To give one example --- in many areas, 1000 workers are hired, and 500 work and 500 sit. The sitters take money and just give 75% to 90% back to Govt officers !! Now all 1000 entries are valid real humans with aadhar and with finger prints, and all money goes via bank. Unless Jury or voters actually do a TRIAL, they will never come to informed
conclusion that 1000 people were hired to do a task where only 500 were needed.
.
So first, unless there is RTR / Jury on officers, they will put information in English only as far as possible, and in clumsiest possible way that meets the written criteria, making information practically inaccessible, overloaded and useless. As a software developer, I can say that one can never codify "user friendliness" and it comes ONLY from market competition where users expel a bad product and promote a good product, So only way to make officers put information in USER FRIENDLY way is --- citizens need to expel the officers who dont put all relevant information in user friendly way, and citizens need to promote those who do display maximal relevant information in maximal user friendly way. IOW, imo,
without RTR / Jury, the dream that officers will display all RELEVANT information in user friendly way is just a pipe dream. Thats why, I have made RTR primary. ...
