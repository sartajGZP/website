My general answer to "why I kept this clause in proposed law-draft" and "why I didnt keep this clause in proposed-law-draft"?
.
I have proposed over 10s of major law-drafts (and over 200 if one counts of all specific RTR as different) such RTR, JurySys, WT etc. In addition, 10s of drafts are half finished. And doing several tasks to raise campaign - pamphlet printing , election contesting, party registration etc.
.
And each time I upload a law-draft, I get 10s of questions like (a) why I have put such as such clause and
(b) why I didnt put such as such clauses
.
IOW, per proposed-law-draft, I may gets many many such questions
.
My general answers to these questions are
.
(1) Pls decide your existing opinion on whether if this law-draft is printed today, then whether it will improve India or worsen India.
.
(2) If it will worsen India, then pls ignore the WHOLE draft and move on
.
(3) if you havent ask PM to print the draft, then pls give me link to any law-draft that you think is less bad or less inefficient wrt the problem that this draft reduces
.
And this also applies for almost all things in life. Say a person wants to own a home. Then he can build it himself or you buy one of the homes in market. If the buyer asks 10s of question to the builder on why he made the flat in the way he made, then the chances are high that the builder will NOT answer most of his questions because the builder has 100 tasks in life. And this is the case where builder is going to get money from buyer. Here in activism, the draft proposer has no goal to get any money from draft supporters, and draft supporters ask PM directly and draft proposer doesnt stand to benefit/
.
And the best way for question asker to get answers about any draft any proposed law-draft is to work to get better law-draft !! As he works to get better law-drafts, answers will come to him.
.
And if any draft proposer keeps answering 10s of questions he gets on 10s of drafts, for all his life, he will be answering questions only.
.
Finally, activism is about acting and not about QA or spending time to answer all questions of gyaan pipasu.
,
When they try to get/frame better or less better drafts, they may get answers to their questions
