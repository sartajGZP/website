(for recallists only. others may skip)
.
Dealing with spurious or substantial proposed VARIATIONS in proposed-law-drafts ---- (a) is variation proposer clearly showing ORIGINAL draft and variation separately of claiming whole draft as his own? (b) why/when analysis of motives of variation proposer is necessary and unnecessary?
.
(pls post comments only after completion. comments posted before will be deleted)
.
The RTR movement is based on proposed-law-drafts. And asking voters and activists to DIRECTLY ask PM/CM/MP/MLAs etc to print these proposed-law-drafts. And also publicizing and explaining these proposed-law-drafts to all. As more voters know about these law-drafts and more voters DIRECTLY ask PM/CM to print these law-drafts, the chances that these law-drafts would get printed will increase.
.
So one way to improve / sabotage are ----- propose valid or spurious/trivial/petty variations to ORIGINAL proposed-law-drafts , and insist that original proposed law-drafts should be opposed and only new proposed variation draft should be publicized.
.
How how can a recallist decide whether variation is spurious or substantial?
.
My stand is as follows
.
(A) Say activist-A is proposing a variation to original law-draft
.
(B) The FIRST thing activist-B should see is --- is variation proposer CLEARLY showing original draft and variation separately? Or in his writing, is he clubbing together and IMPLICITLY showing that he wrote WHOLE draft?
.
Imo, if a person is showing variation, he should let reader first see the ORIGINAL draft as is, and then show his variation in same or different post/pdf, but clearly marked as variation. The variation proposer is sincere, he will also give sha1 has of original draft and also a link to status post or pdf which has ONLY the original draft.
.
When a NEW COMER sees a draft on wall of activist-A, one impression he will get is --- whole draft is written by the proposer. Unless it is public knowledge that there is an original draft.
.
eg say a person comes to my wall, and sees proposed addition to GST namely STAS. Then he knows that GST isnt my draft, and only STAS is my proposed addition.
.
But if a person goes to activist-A's wall, and sees a 25 page PDF , and it is posted as new proposed law-draft, unless indicated clearly, the newcomer will think that ALL 25 pages are written by that person or his group. This is IMPLICIT communication. And one way law-draft proposer can AVOID this mis-communication is --- by clearly mentiioning in his PDF as well as status posts, that it is variation of some other original proposed draft, and give sh1hash of original draft and then also list his variations.
.
If variation-proposer is deliberately NOT communcating this, then analysis of MOTIVES becomes necessary.
.
One possible MOTIVE behin proposing motive can be --- he can now put a new sh1hash, and then IMPLICTLY create an impression that he wrote ALL 25 pages of DRAFT, where his addition was perhaps only 1 page
.
So GOOD variations are always welcome. And thats why I have TCP = CV clauses in almost all drafts. And there may be VALID reason to say "waiting till TCP=CV is used to add variation will damage India, and so I want this clauses added now". But imo author has NO valid reason to hide the fact in his status post and his PDFs, that his draft is a variation. And there is NO valid reason for not clearly showing which clauses are original drafts and which clauses are additions !!
.
So anyone who sees a VARIATION, must ask varaition proposer to clearly show two parts in his new draft SEPARATELY. If a person has all the time to put variations, surely, he can repost the statuses and PDFs. And if variation proposer refuses to show two parts -- original and his variation -- separatelty in his PDFs / status, then imo, activist-B should analyse the motives behind proposing variation. And one possible ill motive can be --- he wants to go to newcomers, and claim credit of writing WHOLE draft !!
.
And if VARIATION proposer activist-A refuses to edit post/pdf showing original draft and variations separately, and adding link to to original draft, then activist-B should inform ALL about the FACT that draft proposed by activist-A is mix of some original draft, and his variation, and activist-A is deliberately creating an image that would make newcomer think that activist-A wrote whole draft !!! IOW, activist-B should inform all that activist-A is trying to fool newcomers using IMPLICIT means.
.
============
.
As a note, I came across several drafts in which first 25 pages were drafts I had written to implement WealthTax. And the last few para were "concerned officer will ensure that all complaints submitted by citizens and tax payers will be addressed within 90 days" !! And some other draft had a clause "officer will ensure that reply to complaints are posted on website within 7 days" !!
.
And the status post as well as PDF had no mention that first 25 pages were draft written by someone else. It was clear that person was trying to create impression on NEW COMERS, that he himself wrote all 25 pages of the wealth tax draft !!
.
If that person was committed to cause, he would clearly marked ORIGINAL DRAFT and his 2 line additions separately. !! But he deliberately didnt mark it that way.
.
Later, one activist messaged me --- why did you steal Mr. A's draft, and removed last two para !!! IOW, the newcomer was told by Mr. A on phone, that Mr. A had written whole draft himself and I stole his draft and removed two para !!!
.
All this can reduce if ALL activist-B inform as many as possible that VARIATION proposer has only made a few petty changes, and first 2 to 25 pages of the draft are all taken from someone else's draft.
