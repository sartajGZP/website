List of IMPORTANT newindia dot in links for law-drafts I have proposed
.
I request ALL to make a folder named as "Law Drafts I asked PM/CM to print" and in that folder, pls make a status post which has all NEWINDIA DOT IN links to all law-drafts that you have asked PM/CM to print. Also given is FB link which describes the proposed law-draft.
.
(in approximate order of importance as per my opnion)
.
(01) Minerals3 -- https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3
Additions to Minerals1. 67% Mineral Royalties will equally distributed in citizens, and 33% are used for Military. And law-draft also has RTR over apex officer, and JurySys over staff and disputes.
.
(02) Minerals1 -- https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals1
Mineral Royalties will equally distributed in citizens. A cut down version of Minerals3-law-draft. Minerals1-law-draft has core of Minerals3, but no RTR/Jury/CV. Minerals1 simply has statement asking PM to directly deposit Mineral Royalties in citizens accounts.
.
(03) WealthTaxs2 -- https://newindia.in/causes/WealthTax2
WealthTax is 1% of land value, per year of land above 1000 sqft per family, minus income tax paid, minus about 5% to 15% of salaries paid, minus Rs 500 or year per citizen beneficiary of Trust minus previous years' wealth tax credits. Imo, this WealthTax will fall only on vacant land. Also, all lands of all trusts including religious trust will be taxed. Along with land, most forms of wealth also get taxed.
.
(04) RtrDeo3 -- https://newindia.in/causes/RtrDeo3
Right to Recall District Education Officer
.
(05) JurySys3 -- https://newindia.in/causes/JurySys3
Jury System in Magistrate courts and District courts for cases in murder, serious crimes of violence and serious crimes against women. And later, AFTER this law is printed, other crimes can be added in this law, and that will enact JurySys over these crimes as well.
.
(06) CancelEvm -- https://newindia.in/causes/CancelEvm
Cancel EVMs and bring ballot papers back.
.
(07) TempleLaw5 -- https://newindia.in/causes/TempleLaw5
Proposed law-draft that creates JurySys over temple trustees. This will end govt control over temples. And also, this law enables temple devotees to create an SGPC like structure to manage their temples. This may strengthen the devotees.
.
(08) TwoChildLaw7 -- https://newindia.in/causes/TwoChildLaw7
Financial and later imprisonment like punishment for more than two children, except in cases where person has daughters and no sons.
.
(09) EIAR -- https://newindia.in/causes/EIAR
Expel Bangladeshi Infiltrators and allow in refugees from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka etc. The law enables officer to do DNA test of suspected Bangladeshi with DNA of person n Bangladesh and prove whether suspect is Bangladeshi or not. So proposed law is more effective than so called NCR
.
(10) TcpIndia7 -- https://newindia.in/causes/TcpIndia7
Transparent Complain Filing Procedure. Any voter can personally appear at collector office and submit his complaint/affidavit which will come on PM's website. And any voter can visit Patwari office and submit YES/NO on any affidavit. And YES/NO filing can be done later via sms/app/at, as well. And all yes/no of all will be visible to all on website along with voterids of person who files yes/no. Yes/No count wull NOT be binding on any officer/minister/judges
.
(11) NarcoTestInPublic1 -- https://newindia.in/causes/NarcoTestInPublic1
Courts can order narcotest in public in SPECIFIC serious crimes like murder, serious crimes against women etc. And later, more laws can be passed to add other crimes.
.
(12) RaiseTataCaptiveCoalRoyalty -- https://newindia.in/causes/RaiseTataCaptiveCoalRoyalty
British in 1946 gave a coal mine in Jharkhand to Tata, and royalty is Re 1 per year per ACRE !! Yes, not even per tonne, but per acre !!! My proposal is to take royalties as per market rate. It should be made Rs 3000 per tonne asap and may come to Rs 12000 per tonne.
.
(13) WaterMeter -- https://newindia.in/causes/WaterMeter
Law to put water meters at ALL water connections. This will reduce water wastage and also enable WHOLE population across India to have 24 hours water a day.
.
(14) PLOD -- https://newindia.in/causes/PLOD
Public Land Ownership Data of ALL land owners. So name of ALL land owners along with all plots they own wll come on net, including land owned by Trusts.
.
(15) RtrPm2 -- https://newindia.in/causes/RtrPm2
Right to Recall PM law draft
.
(16) RtrCm -- https://newindia.in/causes/RtrCm
Right to Recall CM
.
(17) RtrSccj -- https://newindia.in/causes/RtrSccj
Right to Recall Supreme Court Chief judge
.
(18) RtrHccj2 -- https://newindia.in/causes/RtrHccj2
Right to Recall High Court Chief judge
.
(19) STAS -- https://newindia.in/causes/STAS
Separate Tax account System. It can be used for GST to end so called Invoice Mismatch Problem aka Missing Trader Problem. It can be also used for simplifying administration of TDS, PFs etc
.
(20) RrpRtrMp -- https://newindia.in/causes/RrpRtrMp
Right to Recall MP law-draft proposed by us as Right to Recall Party. The draft imo is far far less bad that RtrMP law-draft proposed by RSS MP Sri Varun Gandhiji.
.
(21) Nhdmt2 -- https://newindia.in/causes/Nhdmt2
The law creates National Hindu Devalaya Management Trust. This trust will be managed like SGPC.
.
(22) CancelEWayBillSystem -- https://newindia.in/causes/CancelEWayBillSystem
Cancel eWayBill System of GST. Transporters are made to pay fine of thousands/lakhs of rupees for petty mistakes.
.
(26) NoOneNationOnePoll -- https://newindia.in/causes/NoOneNationOnePoll
Modiji's proposed 'one nation one poll' will reduce power of voters
.
(27) DontReduceCbseSyllabus -- https://newindia.in/causes/DontReduceCbseSyllabus
Due to pressure of usuk-elitemen, SoMoKe are cutting down CBSE syllabus by almost 50% so that students' maths/science skills reduce. Pls vote on above petition to stop that
.
(28) DigSignIndia -- https://newindia.in/causes/DigSignIndia
Any voter can create a pair of public/private keys and submit sha1 hash to the public key to Tahsildaar who will put the sha1 hash in the voterlist.
