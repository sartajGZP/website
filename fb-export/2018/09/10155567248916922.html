(1) Good DEO website is useful/needed DOES NOT mean "clause in law stating DEO shall make good/useful website" is must/needed/useful
.
(2) "clause in law stating DEO shall make good/useful website" is absent does NOT at all mean "DEO will NOT make good/useful website". Whether DEO will good website or not depends on punishments he is likely to face and powers he has to put information, and funds/skills he has.
.
(3) Only a MALICIOUS activist will say "see this, this law has clause that DEO shall make a good website and hence if this law is printed, then good website will come".
.
(4) Only a MALICIOUS activist will say "see this, this law doesnt have clause that DEO shall make a good website and hence if this law is printed, then good website will never come".
.
The presence/absence of a clause may or MAY NOT have some effect or zero effect. One needs to analyse how much effect mere addition of clauses can make. If mere addition/absence makes zero effects , then addition of such clauses is only spurious.
.
-------
.
One of the biggest fallacy in law and legal reasoning is --- assuming that mere presence of clause will ALWAYS change behavior or change reality and mere absence of clause will NEVER change behavior or change reality.
.
And such claims are often exploited by malicious leaders and fake activists across the world, to promote their useless variations and stall others' useful proposal
.
This can be STRAWMAN FALLACY or FALSE DILEMMA FALLACY.
.
For other fallacies prevelent in law and legal reasoning, pls see https://thebestschools.org/magazine/15-logical-fallacies-know/
.
