Opposing RTR-law-drafts by putting pre-conditions (or co-conditions)
.
Ever since I proposed RTR-law-drafts in oct-1998 , I have faced these litanies by those who oppose RTR that "RTR is bad without my law-drafts / pre-conditions and RTR is good but ONLY with my law-drafts / preconditions " !!
.
I dismiss their statements as nonsense. They are as bad as people who say "RTR is bad. PERIOD" !!!
.
Lets say just one RTR-law-draft that I have proposed is printed. Then almost all RTR-law-drafts have TCP-clauses aka CV-clauses. And those CV-clauses aka TCP-clauses are as powerful as standalone TCP. The power of TCP-clauses dont reduce or increase whether they are embedded in a larger draft or standalone. And TCP-clauses alone can ensure that MRCM-law-draft gets printed within a few months and so on.
.
And lets take any RTR-draft munus TCP-clauses. That draft alone too will force every nodal officer (on whom RTR-draft has been printed) in district / state / India to improve his department and improve his staff
.
All these claims that "RTR-drafts without my preconditions" are nonsense. And there are too many people putting 10s of conditions. I wont find time to address even one of them
.
But all of you may notice --- for the new draft they give (copy of original + conditions) , they refuse to mention what steps 90 CRORE voters of India should take to COMMUNICATE to PmoIndia to print their proposed law !! If anyone is serious about getting any law-draft printed, the FIRST thing he will ask law-draft-supporter to COMMUNICATE TO PmoIndia to print those law-draft. Absence of this step shows that they have no intention of getting their own pet law-draft printed !! IOW, they only want badmounth existing RTR-law-drafts by giving weird scenarios in which RTR-law-draft will fail, and their magic preconditions and magic additions will work as savior !!!
.
So there are 40 lakh RSS-workers who oppose RTR-drafts, some 10 lakh congress-workers who oppose RTR and 2 lakh AAP-workers who oppose RTR. A few dozen more --- makes no difference.
.
Now why I wont rebut all 1000 "RTR-law-draft is bad arguments"? Because I have 10s of work to do. In RTR, I have to upload 10 main drafts, upload 50 other drafts, REGISTER PARTY, work to train candidates in form filling etc, write 40 pamphlets, on 10 main law-drafts etc. And I have to earn money to give newspaper advertisements, and also pay for my own expenses. And I have plenty of other personal work.
.
Overtime, I will write on how recallists can address the FAKE scenarios that RTR-draft-opposers give. In general, one should take QUANTITATIVE approach in all the steps in their scenario. One the opposer is asked to give QUANTITIVE DETAILS, the nonsense of his scenario that claims that RTR-law-drafts are bad will become clear. And this way, his scenario can be falsified to the audience. ..
