A good article against GST - written by a US economists in year 2010 !!
.
(GST is known as VAT in Europe, and its the same thing)
.
In USA, district's parents' committees decide syllabus (as opposed to apex experts in state/central govt in India). And so a large number of district schools in class-9 to class-12 have economics textbooks which explain regressiveness in taxation, and make 12th class students familiar with taxes such as wealth tax, income tax, sales, tax and their comparison. So a large number of activists in USA know why wealth tax is less bad compared to VAT = GST. And so some economists do write against GST to cater to this audience.
.
