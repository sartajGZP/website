#RtrFso - Right to Recall District Food Safety Officer, to reduce food adulteration
.
The proposed law at https://newindia.in/causes/RtrFso creates a procedure by which voters of a district can replace Food Safety Officer. And all cases of food adulteration will go to Jury and not judges. Imo, this will reduce food adulteration.
.
