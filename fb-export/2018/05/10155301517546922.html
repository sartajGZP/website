About - sending SMS to MPs and sending SMS to private numbers - why I consider this TOTALLY USELESS now
.
Previously, I had supported this method. Now imo, following methods are far far better than SMS and so I no longer support sending SMS to MPs and sending SMS to private phno is complete waste
.
(1) Govt of India has created website newindia dot in, on which anyone in India can vote. Also, we can send tweets to @PmoInIda. So sending SMS to MPs is no longer needed.
.
(2) newindia.in has problem that it allows voting by email and so one can create 1000 fake email ids and add 1000 votes !! So we should ask @PmoIndia to allow only those mobiles which are linked with aadhar to allow cast voting on newindia.in (see proposal https://newindia.in/AadharLoginOnly ) .
.
(3) private websites will be less reliable than twitter / fb and twitter is less rliable than govt websites. newindia dot in is govt website and so I give highest priority to newindia dot in, followed by twitter / fb.
.
(4) next option is to ask people to declare support on etherium. One can make a small Rs 1 transaction on etherirm and in message one can write "@PmoIndia https://newindia.in/AadharLoginOnly " if he supports this cause. Later, we can develop a better blockchain based OPEN messaging system, which can be OPINION MEDIA , which can be extension to SOCIAL MEDIA
.
So as of today, I oppose sending SMS to MPs and completely oppose sending SMS to private phno - to declare support and prove public opinion. Today, I support voting on newindia dot in, sending tweets to @PmoIndia , and sharing on FB. and next step will be writing on blockchain based serverless opinion media.
