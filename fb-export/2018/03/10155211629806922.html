Dear all recallists
.
It was Ahimsamurti Mahatma Chandrashekhar Azad who had proposed "Indian republic with Right to Recall" in 1925 , NOT Ahimsamurti Mahatma Bhagat Singh !!!
.
So if you have picture or quote of Bhagat Singh saying so, pls remove it and instead put picture or quota of Mahatma Chandrashekhar Azad
.
The statement was made in 1925 in the MOST important document of Hindustan Republican Association -- its first founding manifesto !!! And Ahmisamurti Mahatma Bhagat Singh joined the organization in 1928. And one reason why he had joined was because he believed in the proposals of the organization. So one may be right in saying that Mahatma Bhagat Singh agreed with this proposal. But is WRONG to say that he made that statement.
