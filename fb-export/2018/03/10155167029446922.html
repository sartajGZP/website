#AadharLoginOnly

Pls vote here to ensure that fake IDs cant cast vote on newindia dot in

NewIndia dot in is a Central Govt website to collect public opinion. But sadly, it allows logins by created FB/twitter and email to vote !! IOW, fake users can vote !!!

Pls vote on following proposal if you want to make newindia dot in cleaner. The proposal at https://newindia.in/causes/AadharLoginOnly says

" Pls ensure that VOTING on newindia dot in can be done ONLY by a user who has logged in using his mobile phone (and not email), and his mobile phone is linked with aadhar.

And one phone per aadhar should be allowed to vote on the proposal. Also, voter should have option of making his name public.

And for each cause, the list of voters will show voters who have wished to make their name public and show count of voters who have decided to keep names private. "

.
