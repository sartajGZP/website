Australin ball tempring shows that match fixing is trivially possible

One argument often given against match fixing is that match fixing needs too many players to co-operate and someone will rat and whole scam will come out
.
Well, australian ball tempering shows that now one will rat. And in case of ball tempering ratting was easy, and yet no one ratted. And it got proven due to video. But matchfixing is done all orally in close dark smoke filled rooms. And all actions on grounds are legit. So even if one wants to rat, he will never have proof.

So a common argument given against match fixing that "it is impossible get illegal co-operation from too many players" falls flat. Its now clear that ALL players may be willing to co-operate as well as hide match fixing.
