SSC scam --- (a) solution to existing scam (b) log term solution to reduce cheating etc .

(a) Solution I propose to existing scam is Jury Trial and narcotest in public of main accused such as examination center owner where mass cheating and impostering happened (impostering means X giving exam on behalf of Y) and SSC officers who were supposed to supervise these centers

(b) And long term solution ise. how can exam taking be improved, so that cheating, paper leak , impostering etc happen much less in future?

====
.
(B) Long term solution

I will first put my views on LONG TERM solution. The problems in exam are paper leaks, impostering, cheating, giving seat number close to a learned candidate so that cheating becomes easy and so on. But mother of all problems is INSIDER TEMPERING at server level where admins replaces answers !!! Or, in examination center, the owner installs remote login software on PC which will allow someone else to click answers !!

So first, I am against ONLINE recruitment exams in govt. Online exams do better job at protection from paper leaks etc but enables apex insiders by colluding with server admins to run a very large scale fraud without candidate impostering, without cheating at terminal level and without any outsiders seeing !!! So I oppose online exam for GOVT recruitment and support paper methods. I have put one paper based method ( see today's other FB posts) .
.
Now To improve exam taking methods, each activist needs to decide which exact paper method or which exact online method should be used to take recruitment exams. After that, activists should upload DRAFTS of these methods on govt website such as mygov dot in or newindia dot in. And then activists need to ask citizens to support these drafts. And AFTER sizeable number of ciizens have supported the drafts, activists need to see if Minister\Chairman adopt these methods. And if they dont or fail to adopt it, then activists need to ask citizens to replace these Minister\Chairman in-charge. To enable citizens to change Ministers\Chairman etc, I have proposed RTR Minister and RTR Chairman law-drafts

I will put exact draft of RTR-Minister and RTR-Chairman drafts later. What can you do to promote these drafts TODAY? If you have decided to support this RTR drafts, then you can work to support RtrDeo3 draft TODAY. RtrDeo3 draft is already uploaded on newindia dot com . The drafts I will propose for RTR-Minister and RTR-Chairman will be similar.

======

(A) short term solution

As a short term solution, I propose that activists should demand a Jury Trial on SSC Chairman and Examination Center Owners. And Jury can do narcotest in public if the need be.

=====

(C) Useless "solutions"

I do NOT support investigation by Cover Bureau of India alias Cleanchit Bureau of India CBI or investigation by some SIT headed by some corrupt IAS/IPS/judges or trial by some corrupt judge. These people ONLY know how to coverup and give clean chits. eg when Sri Telgiji had printed fake stamp papers of thousands of crores of rupees in 1990s, Cover Bureau of India alias CBI finally closed the case by saying that fake stamp papers were only Rs 150 crores !! And not even one IAS/Minister was even accused !! And we also saw 2G clean chits given by CBI and then a corrupt judge. So I am sick and tired of handing over case to Cover Bureau of India alias CBI or SIT under some corrupt IPS/IAS or trial by some corrupt judge.

So (A) and (B) are the solutions I propose. And (C) are useless solutions I oppose. I will give drafts and links in 2-3 days
