UP elections and casteism --- RSS-workers' complaint that RSS lost UP byelection due to caste is fake. All SP/BSP/RSS get caste votes --- its equal equal

Many Dalit /OBC voters dont vote for RSS/Yogi , and thats partly due to casteism. But at the same time, many upper caste voters also dont vote for Mayawai/Akhilesh, and that is also partly due to casteism. So its equal-equal

Will voters always vote by caste? imo NO. If a WELL KNOWN candidate has huge MERITS over other candidates, then caste becomes lesser issue, But when all known candidates are corrupt and defunct, and other candidates are are unknown, then the caste factor becomes important.
.
====
.
Many RSS-workers have now started blaming casteism for defeat of RSS in two UP local byelections (RSS = BJP, just as shaktimaan = gangadhar). As per them, nothing was/is wrong with Modiji, Yogiji, RSS-leaders and RSS-workers !! Everything that is wrong is with these casteist voters only !!

Let me put this in capital. .
.
ALL THREE LEADERS -- MAYAWATI , AKHILESH, YOGIJI ARE ALL CORRUPT FROM TOE TO HEAD. BUT MAYAWATI WAS BETTER ADMINISTRATOR THAN YOGIJI. Then also why did upper caste voters voted for RSS?
.
Mayawati had more kills than Yogiji in her first year. Law order situation had vastly improved in Mayawati days and so did roads and electricity supply. This was all despite rank and file of IAS were hostile to her due to her caste. She heavily depended on muslim votes, but then also she decided to encounter many islamist criminals. She was hoping that when she fixes law-order situation, some upper caste voters and OBC voters will vote for her. But paidmediamen kept writing only against her again and again. The judiciary also started frivolous cases against her, So she didnt get any upper caste votes in 2012, and Muslim voters shifted to Akhilesh. And so she lost 2012 Assembly election and also lost may-2014 loksabha seats.
.
She did have a flaw -- statues. She did spend a lot of money on all sorts of her own statues, elephant stutues and what not,. But all that was not even 1% of UP budget. Her infatuation was grossly over stated by bikau mediamen more loyal to RSS than BSP.

,
And a sizeable number of SC/OBC voters did vote for RSS = Modiji in may-2014 and even in feb-2017. But within months, it was clear that RSS and congress are one and the same, except high decibel BMKJ and VM slogans. No decrease in corruption in policemen, no decrease in corruption in education, and only changes that compuetrization brings since 1986 (when Rajiv Gandhi compuertized railway reservation and corruption in railway reservation drastcailly reduced within months). .
So my point is --- all parties RSS/ BSP/SP use casteism. RSS gets upper caste voters by creating a real or fake claim that once RSS gets 67% majority in both houses, RSS will remove caste based reservation !!! RSS had worked day and night in Gujarat in 1980, 1984 and 1990 to create anti-reservation movement to take away upper caste voters away from congress towards RSS. So a huge number of votes that RSS gets in UP/Bihar is also because casteism, and OBC/SP also gets caste votes. So RSS-workers's complaint that "RSSy lost UP byelectuons due to casteism" is fake,. A huge number of votes they get is also due to casteism only. Its equal-equal.
