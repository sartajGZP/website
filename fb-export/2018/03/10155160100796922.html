IMO, this is VERY BAD --- Over 30,000 farmers reach Mumbai, to gherao Vidhan Sabha Bhavan tomorrow, Monday 12-mar-2018. . Their demand is loan waiver.

If their demand is accepted, then this can lead to 100s of groups across coming to state capitals across states to gherao Vidhan Sabha for their demand . And 10s of groups may also come to gherao Parliament. And if their demand isnt accepted, then tomorrow can be a bloody showdown.

But first, why the demand of loan waivers?

soga/namo/arke etc take bribes or politicla favors from super-elitemen and write-off their debts. In the process, congress/rss/aap workers earn big bucks and so they support such write-offs.

And so lakhs of people start agitation for write-offs and waiver

Temporary solution is --- loan waiver of at most Rs 5 lakh per person and no more, and ONLY to farmers whose (wealth minus one home minus 5 acre per family) is below Rs 50 lakhs. And put ALL information of loan waivers public with name , aadhar#, amount waived, wealth the farmer has etc. And same information should be also made public for all non-agricultural NPAs as well.
.
Long term solution I propose is that PSU banks should STOP giving loans without collateral so that situation of write-offs arise rarely. And so the demand of loan waivers will be also low.

What can we do to bring such "no collateral no loan" law?

Imo, you can start with RTR over RBI Governor. RBI Governor is the main authority deciding loan rules. I am yet to upload RBI Governor law-draft on net, But RTR PM law-draft us very much similar, and will also have same effect because PM appoints RBI-Gov in India and he can expel RBI Gov anyway, So you can work to publicize RTR-PM-law-draft for now. Or, pls wait for a few weeks -- I will upload RTR-RBI-Gov law-drafts and also law-drafts to ensure that banks dont issue collateral free loans.
