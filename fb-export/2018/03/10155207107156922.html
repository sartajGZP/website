----------------------------------
.

--------------------------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
--------------------------------------------------
.
(1) Approving DAAO candidate, or approving existing DAAO:
.
(1.1) If you (i.e. a voter) wish to replace DAAO (i.e. District Anti Adulteration Officer) of his District/City, then that voter can visit office of Patwari (or Talati or Village Officer), any day to approve a candidate. There will be no specific date, time duration or time limit. Using this, votes of district can replace DAAO anyday.
.
(1.2) DAAO can ensure that setup labs, make network of informers, and setup food testing labs, and raid and prosecute the food sellers who sell adulterer food. He must try to do this in a way that causes minimal harassment to the food sellers who are NOT adultering the food. If DAAO doesn’t do his tasks, then you (i.e. voters of district) may replace him.

(2) You (i.e. voters) may be called for Jury Duty i.e. called to decide complaint against a food seller in case of food adulteration or Govt staff involved in detecting food adulteration. If you are called to decide fine etc, then you will have to appear at Municipal Office, hear the complainers and accused, and decide the fine. If you decide not to appear, then a Jury may fine you.
.

Part - II : Summary of the procedure give in the draft
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
.
(3) comment : summary of whole law-draft
.
(3.1) The voters will have Right to Recall District Anti Adulteration Officer, and voters will conduct Jury Trial over anti-food adulteration staff, and cases of food adulteration.
.
(3.2) For details of RTR DAAO procedure, pls see For details, you may see ‘newindia dot in / causes/RtrDeo3 ’
.
(3.3) The voters of State can suspend RTR DAAO procedure and Jury Trial procedure in a district for 4 years using explicit majority of voters. And the voters of India can suspend RTR DAAO procedure and Jury Trial procedure in any District / State using explicit majority of voters.
.
----------------------------------------------------------------
Part –III : Instructions for Officers and comments
----------------------------------------------------------------
.
(4) what is comment : The comments are not part of actual code of this legislation draft. The activists and voters may use comments to understand the draft. And Jurors may use comments as a reference to decide a case. But the comments are NOT binding on anyone.
.
(5) The word “may” does not imply any moral-legal binding. It clearly means “may” or “need not”.
.
(6) Candidature for DAAO
.
(6.1) CM will appoint an officer as DAAO i.e. District Anti Adulteration Officer for every City./District. If the district/city is large, then CM may divide it into 2 or more zones and assign each zone to same or different officer.
.
(6.2) If any person has a graduation degree, and is eligible to contest Loksabha election, then he will be eligible to become DAAO. If such a person comes to DC and wishes to be registered as a replacement of existing DAAO, then the DC shall accept form, with a fee equal to deposit of MP election. DC will issue a serial number and post replacement candidate’s name and details on CM’s website. The candidates can get their names removed by applying in writing.
.
(6.3) DC will register existing DAAO, as candidate for free. The serial number of existing DAAO will be 1.
.
(7) Citizen approving replacement candidate or existing DAAO
.
(7.1) If a citizen comes in person to Patwari’s office, pays fee as decided by CM, and approves at most five replacement candidates for DAAO’s position and / or if he approves existing DAAO, then the Patwari would enter his approvals in the computer and would give receipt showing voter’s voter-id#, date/time and the candidates he approved.
.
(7.2) If a voter comes to cancel his Approvals, Patwari will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee. Cancellations will also come on CM’s website.
.
(7.3) comment : to explain approval – (i) The voter can approve anyday, and there is no time limit. And different voters can file approval anyday they like – not necessarily on same day or during any specific time interval. (ii) say a voter approves candidates A , B and C. Then approval counts of A, B and C will increase by 1. (iii) now anyday, voter may cancel any of the approval he has filed. Say he cancels approval of C. Then approval count of C will decrease by 1 and approval count of A and B will remain unchanged (iv) the voter can also approve any other candidate anyday. Say he approves D, in which case, approval count of D will increase by 1, and approval count of A and B will remain unchanged
.
(7.4) The Patwari will put approvals given by every citizen on CM’s website with citizen’s voter#, name and names of candidates he approved.

(7.5) Approval filing is open and public i.e. each voter’s approval of candidates will come on CM’s website in a way anyone in world can see
.
(8) Condition for replacement of DAAO : if
.
(8.1) a replacement candidate gets approval count over 35% of all (35% of ALL) voters in District AND
.
(8.2) his approval count is 2% (2% of all voters) more than approval count of existing DAAO AND
.
(8.3) his approval count is highest of all
.
then, and then only, CM may or need not, remove existing DAAO and appoint that candidate as the DAAO.
.
(9) comment :
.
(9.1) The “35%” and “2%” mentioned in clause above is “35% and 2% of all voters in the voter list” respectively. e.g. if a district has say 20 lakh voters, then 35% and 2% would mean 700,000 and 40,000 respectively regardless of number of voters who have filed approval. And all three conditions, (8.1) , (8.2) and (8.3) should be fulfilled.
.
(9.2) Example - 1: Say a District has 20,00,000 voters. Say existing DAAO has approval of say 500,000 voters. Then if one candidate has approval of 35% of 20,00,000 = 700,000 voters, then CM may or need not appoint him as DAAO.
.
(9.3) Example - 2 : Say District has 20,00,000 voters. And say existing DAAO has approval of say 800,000 voters. Then a candidate must have approval of (800,000 + 2% of 20,00,000) = (800,000 + 40,000) = 840,000 to for CM to appoint him as DAAO.
.
(10) One person can become DAAO of upto 5 districts in a State and upto 15 Districts across India. If he has become DAAO via approvals of voters i.e. in each district he has approval of over 35% of all voters, then his salary and perks will be proportional to number of district he is DAAO of. E.g. if he is DAAO of say 7 districts in India, and he has approval of over 35% voters in 5 districts, then his salary will be five times the salary of DAAO. But if a person is DAAO of say 5 districts and in each District he has approval of less than 35% voters in 4 or all 5 districts, then his salary will be of one DAAO only.
.
(11) Term limit : One person can be DAAO of one District for atmost 8 years in a District in his lifetime. After that, he may become DAAO of any other District or may serve on any other position in that District.
.
(12) No retirement benefits : if a person has become DAAO via approval of voters, and is not part of Govt cadre, then he will not be eligible for any retirement benefits after his term ends due to term limit or expulsion. If any other law or resolution passed by District / State / India may provide him retirement benefits. But this law doesn’t create any provision of any retirement benefits.
.
(13) Salary of DAAO brought in by approval of voters : Salary and perks of DAAO brought in by approval of voters will be same as median salary of District Collectors in the Districts of the State irrespective of seniority and other considerations. DAAO will not be eligible for any government housing or personal staff at home or government car or car driver, but he will get equivalent monetary amount. Decision of CM on this issue will be final. But all DAAOs brought in by approvals of voters must get same salary from the State Government. And voters of District thru explicit majority may give additional salary to a DAAO.
.
(14) How can CM appoint person approved by voters as DAAO : The decision will be left to CM. CM may appoint him as OSD (OSD = Officer on Special Duty) and then appoint him as DAAO. The decision of CM will be final
.
(15) Setting up testing labs, sniffing dogs, network of informers and running website/app for anonymous or open complains
.
(15.1) DAAO will setup food testing labs, can buy or hire sniffer dogs and can also setup network of informers, or can take information from honest competitors. DAAO will also install websites/app where users can report complaints openly or anonymously.
.
(15.2) Using reliable information, DAAO can raid the premises of a food seller and do testing on spot in front of Jury or take samples to lab. And in case of violations, DAAO will submit the complain to Jurors.
.
(15.3) Adulteration will include the cases where a chemical was used more than limit prescribed.
..
(15.4) DDAO will also suggest standards to CM or citizens of District/State. Using CV-clauses listed in this draft, DAAO can publicize these standards and may issue notification of their implementation.
.
(J) - Jury Trial over food adulteration cases and complaints against the staff
.
(J.1) comment : To know details about proposed Jury System, pls see ‘ newindia dot in /causes/DJSM ‘
.
(J.2) Wherever there is dispute between DAAO or his staff and a food seller, or decide fine on food seller, DAAO will use Jury System to decide the case. This section gives rules of formation the Jury.
.
(J.3) DAAO will appoint a Jury Administrator for each District called as DJA = District Jury Administrator. DAAO may replace him anyday.
.
(J.4) Resolving a dispute
.
(J.4.1) For every dispute, the DJA will randomly select voters from voter list of District between age of 30 and 55 years. The person must not have appeared in any Jury in past 10 years and must not have faced any conviction in past.
.
(J.4.2) Number of Jurors : The number of Jurors will be at least 12 and at most 100. The number of Jurors will be 12, if the applicable fine as claimed by DAAO is below Rs 1 lakh. And there will 1 extra Jurors for every Rs 1 lakh of fine. The highest size will be 100 Jurors. If the complain is not about fine, but about misbehavior or corruption of officer, then Jury size will be 50.
.
(J.4.3) The Jurors will hear the both sided and each Juror will announce his fine. DAAO will impose the find approved by 67% of the Jurors
.
(J.4.4) If over 67% of Jurors announce that the officer accused should be expelled, the DAAO will expel him from the staff.
.
(S) Suspension of RTR-DAAO by people of State or of people of India
.
(S.1) The voters (all voters, not just voters) of a State can suspend this RTR DAAO procedure in a District, for 4 years, using a procedure, established by CM. The procedure should show that explicit majority of ALL voters (not just voters who voted or said YES / NO) in the state have approved the suspension. And suspension can be extended by voters for 4 more years, several times.
.
(S.2) The voters (all voters, not just voters) of India can suspend this RTR DAAO procedure in a District or a State, for 4 years, using a procedure, established by PM. The procedure should show that explicit majority of ALL voters (not just voters who voted or said YES / NO) in India have approved the suspension. And suspension can be extended by voters for 4 more years, several times.
.
(S.3) comment : “S” stands for sovereignty. Any District level procedure can be suspended by explicit majority voters of State / India. And any State level procedure can be suspended by explicit majority voters of India.
.
(RV) Non binding directive for Range Voting :
A few weeks or months after this law has passed, ECI may or need not allow voters to submit marks between (-100 to 100) for each candidate or just submit approvals. If voter is submitting approval and not marks, then no approval will mean 0 marks and approval will mean 100 marks. But if voter is giving marks, then actual marks given by him will be counted. The candidates total marks will be sum of all marks he got. And if candidate has 1 or more marks from over 51% of all voters, then system will ignore all his negative marks. The total marks of sitting MP will be higher of the two ---- [ (votes he got * 67) , score he got after election ] . And recall poll will happen when a candidates score is (MP’s score + voter population * 100 / 10). And recall will happen as per clause-15. ECI may or need not implement this clauses, only after over 51% of all voters of India has specified YES using clause-CV.2. This procedure is called as Range Voting, and it is immune to so called AUIT (= Arrow’s Useless Impossibility Theorem) . Range Voting is better than Approval Voting used here.
.
(CV) Citizens’ Voice:
.
(CV.1) If any citizen residing anywhere in India wants any change in this law or has any complaint under this law, then he may submit an affidavit to DC’s designated clerk and clerk will scan the affidavit on put on CM’s website along with photo and details of the citizen. The clerk will charge Rs 30 per page. The clerk will issue serial number for the affidavit.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen residing in the area covered by the Patwari wants to register his YES / NO on the affidavit submitted in above clause, then he can go to Talati’s office and provide the serial number of the affidavit and his YES/NO on a form, and Patwari will enter his YES/NO for a fee for Rs 3 . The YES/NO shall come on CM’s website along with citizen’s voter-id, name, date etc
.
