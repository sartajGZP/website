How TRIVIALLY easy it is manipulate VVPAT !!! SOLUTION? See last para.
.
Pls see any video on how BU = ballot unit, CU = control unit, PU = Print unit are connected
.
BU is connected to PU = VVPAT's printing unit
PU is connected to CU = Control Unit, thru a cable
.
CU has memory and CPU where candidate's vote-count is stored.
.
So voter presses candidate# N on BU and BU transmits N to PU which prints the candidate name etc and then PU sends the candidate# to CU
.
Once can put a remote control accepting circuit in cable and send the preferred candidate# to that circuit anytime after candidate number is issued and before polling.
.
The circuit in the cable connecting PU and CU will work as follow
.
(a) for first 100 votes - transmist count honestly
(b) after 100 votes, give every 3rd vote to candidate P, i.e. preferred caniddate
.
So by changing cable between PU and CU, and putting a circuit in cable which can change votes, one can change votes in CU
.
====
.
No wonder why ECI didnt allow hackers to change even cables during their challange !!!
.
Now cables are stored in BEL warehouses or collector's warehouse or in truck which ships EVMs. One has 5 years of time to change one box of cable with another box of tempered cable which look the same. The cables dont have serial number.
.
The only challenge is --- ensure that some EVMs are NOT rigged and only one of that EVM is selected for manual counting.
.
Manual counting is done in ONLY 1 out of about 250 EVMs in Assembly. How is that booth chosen? The booth numbers are printed on pieces of paper of size postcard. And Then it is folded 4 times And slips are shaken in a bucket and then one slip is selected by RO
.
Say there are 1 to 250 booths in Assembly, Say booth from 1 to 99 are NOT tempered. Some how, one needs to ensure that slip selected is between 1 to 99. This can be ensured in many ways, There countless magic tricks One is to print numbers from 11 to 250 with ink is a way that last digit will disappear when papers are shaken. So number elected will be always one digit or two digit and so between 1 to 99. And another is to use different types of papers to print numbers from 1 to 99 and different type of papers for 100-250. And on shaking the numbers between 0 to 99 will come at top .

All in all, changing PU-CU cables is TRIVIAL and so changing votes is trivial . One needs to figure out how to ensure that only UNTEMPERED get selected for manual counting.
.
===========
