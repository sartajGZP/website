Each citizen will get equal water quota. He can use it or transfer whole or part of the quota to any other water user for a price in cash or kind. The price will be mutually agreed by water user and water quota owner. Govt will not fix any price. .
Water quota will be issued to human citizens only, and none to non person entities such as trusts, companies, associations, housing societies etc. The non-person entities who want Municipal Water or Underground Bore water will need to obtain water quota from human residents of the district.
.
Examples :

(1) Say water quota is say 5000 liters per person per month. Say a person has family of 4 and so his quota his 20000 liters per month. He may allocate 12000 liters to his own water connection and allocate the rest i.e. 8000 liters to any water buyer. The water buyer will directly pay the person. He may also allocate 5000 liters to one water buyer and 3000 liters to another water buyer and obtain money from both.
.
(2) Say a factory needs 500,000 liters of water per month. Then it will need to obtain that much quota of water from persons living in the district.
.
(3) Say DWDO decides Bore Water Quota as say 5000 liters per person per month. Say a bore owner wants to draw 100,000 liters per month. Then bore owner will need to 20 full quota from 20 persons in that district. Or, he may obtain parts of their quota which adds to 100,00 liter. The bore owner will need to pay money directly to the quota owner
