(* 4 non-Nehru-PMs (i.e. PVNR, ABV, MMS, Modiji, because others didnt get much time and the 3 Nehru PMs (namely Jawaharlal Ghazi, Devi India Amma, Rajiv Gandhiji)

But the impression one may get that non-Nehru-PMs did something much better is wrong

i.e. skills of people in India has increased compared to 1950, but so has skills across world. Has skill of people in India increased MORE than that of world? i.e. is RATIO better. The ratio is case of china/usa is far far better, But the ratio of India/World SKILL level has not made much improvement, We get false impression that "lot has changed in India" because we dont know that "mot more has changed in world"
