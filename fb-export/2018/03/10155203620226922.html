Australian ball tempring shows that match SCRIPTING (*) is trivially possible

One argument often given against match SCRIPTING is that match scripting needs too many players to co-operate and someone will rat and whole scripting drama will come out

(* I use word scripting and NOT fixing because I consider match scripting ethical, like scripting of movie, and I use word fixing when it is illegal/unethical eg judge fixing)
.
Well, Australian ball tempering shows that no one will rat. And in case of ball tempering ratting was easy, and yet no one ratted. And it got proven due to video. But match scripting is done all orally in close dark smoke filled rooms. And all actions on grounds are legit. So even if one wants to rat, he will never have proof.

So a common argument given against match scripting that "it is impossible get illegal co-operation from too many players" falls flat. Its now clear that ALL players may be willing to co-operate as well as hide match scripting
