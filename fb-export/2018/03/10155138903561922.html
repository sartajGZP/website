FBCPBA vs meetings --- meetings need DOUBLE COINCIDENCE of free available several hours of CONTINUOUS time i.e. two or more persons must be free for 1-3 hours at THE SAME TIME interval, while FBCPBA needs only free time for both at time of their choice and time intervals .

And when meeting has more than two persons, then meetings not only need double "coincidence of time", but COINCIDENCES OF CAUSES !!! Whereas in FBCPBA , causes of all participants need not be same.
.
=====
.
In FBCPBA i.e. "facebook coverpage based activism", one activists put law-drafts he supports and information on law-drafts, and how iho (iho = in his opinion) activists related to that cause can work. The start point of ALL links has to be coverpic description. And law-drafts he supports must be in photoalbum named as "law-drafts I supports (top 50)" OR "law-drafts I supports (after top 50)" .
.
Meetings cannot be completely wished away. But imo, one should use FBCPBA activism as far as possible for bulk information exchange, and solve doubts etc over phone (when BOTH persons have PC in front of them) . So imo meetings should be reduced.
.
Lets say 2 persons decide to meet. Then for each commute time will be say 1 hour from home/office to meeting place and 1 hour back. And say meeting is 2 hours. So both need same 4 hours of free time INTERVAL. It is not same as "both need 4 hours", but both needs "4 hours at the same time interval" !!! This is what I call as DOUBLE COINCIDENCE of free time interval, harder to get these days.

In FBCPBA, the writer can write at his own free time, and time interval need NOT be continuous 4 hours. And reader can read at the same time
.
=====
.
Also, when a writer needs to communicate information to 2 or more persons,. then we need MULTIPLE COINCIDENCE of TIME INTERVAL i.e. each must have 4 hours of free time on the same day same time interval. Getting 2 persons agree on same time is difficult, Imagine how 3-5 people can find same time slot

.
====
.
And say W writer is promoting law-drafts on 10 causes. Now X is interested in (C1 , C2, C3) and Y is interested in say (C4, C5, C6). Meeting of W with both X and Y will make both X and Y feel that meetings didnt go as they wanted. And if more than 5-10 persons are meeting, 1 person may feel that he spent time and his causes were completely left out !!!

Whereas in FBCPBA, W can put links to all causes and law-drafts and make one index which has list. X, Y Z etc i.e. readers can read the index and read status related to their causes in detail and ignore the rest or just browse thru the rest. That way, none of the listeners i.e. readers will feel that they spent time, and yet got left out.
.
====
.
So consider two groups G1 and G2 each having say 1000 activists, and in each group 1000 with varied causes, And each each activist in both group has decided to spend 400 hours a year. Say activists in G1 decide to use FBCPBA afap (afap = as far as possible) and use phone calls and meetings in ratio of say 320:40:40 i.e out of 400 hours , they spend 320 hours in writing on FB in ORGANIZED way , 40 hours in phone calls when both has PC and 40 hours in meeting including commute time. And G2 spends exclusively in meetings. Then imo G1 will expand at a faster rate, and will have MORE INFORMATION. Whereas G2 will work mostly at local levels and information exchange will be minimal, and will not expand across India because even though each has decided to spend 400 hours, they wont be able to find nundred 4 hour intervals on same days and same time intervals. ..
