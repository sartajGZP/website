SERIOUS public notice --- ALL law-drafts we have proposed have been labeled as LEFTIST/COMMUNIST by over 95% of the 40 lakh RSS-workers in India. So may be, these drafts are so !!

And over 99% RSS-workers have called us leftist/communist. And they have called us traitors as well, and that we are NOT ,,, and I will ell a few things about their pseudo-hinduvaad and their FDI-nationalism or rather pseudo-nationalism
.
So my request to all recallists is --- whenever you present RTR-law-drafts to others, BEFORE giving description, pls mention CLEARLY that over 95% of all 40 lakh RSS-workers have labeled our proposed law-drafts as communist/leftist proposals and so they have labeled as leftist/communist as well !!
.
====

Consider law-drafts we have proposed, such as Right to Recall PM/CM/MP/MLA/judges or Jury System or "Direct Deposit of Mineral Royalties in Citizens' Account" or "scrap GST, levy wealth tax" etc etc.

How do other political parties' workers react?

ALL congress/rss/aap/bsp/etc workers have opposed these law-drafts.

Now congress/aap workers dont put labels ---- they merely oppose these law-drafts but dont label them as right or leftist or capitalist or communist. . Whereas over 95% of the 40 lakh RSS-workers across India have labeled these law-drafts as communist/leftist law-drafts !!!

So are these law-drafts communist/leftist proposals?

The labels like communist/leftist have been ill defined since ages. And the concept of capitalism/communism/leftism/rightism etc are older than 5000 years --- long before these labels came into existence. I dont go into ancient history. But consider Thomas Paine's proposal in "Agrarian Justice" in year 1790. He says that "all citizens have equal ownership over all land" , which is clearly as communist/leftist concept. And this was some 30 years before words "leftist/rightist" came and some 50 years before marx coined the word "communism". And next Thomas Paine in 1790 said that inheritance tax of about 10% should be imposed on land, and that money should be equally divided into all citizens of USA !!! Again, this is very much a communist/leftist concept, Pls google on "Agrarian Justice" for more.
.
But at the same time, people keep changing meanings of these labels !! eg till 1935, word socialism and communism meant same. Which is why Bolshivieks in 1917 named the new country as "Union of SOCIALIST Soviet Republic" and renamed their party as "Communist Party of Soviet Union". . And after 1935, "intellectuals" started saying that "socialism and communism mean different concept" !! And the meanings must have changed 5 times in past 50 years !!!

So exact meaning of these labels are NEVER objective. But these labels are used in every second sentence by RSS-workers !!! And so I presume these labels are very much important.

And since these labels are IMPORTANT to them, I do NOT want to avoid this labeling business.

So here is what I say now , and pls note my exact words "we are working to bring law-drafts such as RTR, JurySys, scrap GST, levy wealth tax etc" and over 95% of the 40 lakh RSS workers across India have labeled ALL these proposed laws as leftist/communist proposals, and they may be so, as they say so".

So I make it very clear that labels have come from them, and I am NOT disputing their labels. And then I also make it clear that "congress/aap workers have also opposed these proposals, though they havent given any labels".

So imo, we should NOT avoid these labeling business. And imo, we should inform all newcomers etc right AT FIRST that over 95% RSS-workers have labels all our law-drafts as leftist/communist, If this effects their opinions, then so be so. I see no point in hiding these truth.....
