Loksabha MPs thru majority vote can bypass courts and directly imprison anyone (including supreme judges) thru majority vote.

One precedent is --- Loksabha in dec-1978 had voted on motion to suspend Devi Indira Amma from parliament for a few days and also IMPRISON her . The motion passed with simple majority. And after that, Devi Indira Amma was imprisoned. And due to heavy protests across India, she had to be released.

Supreme judges cannot void imprisonment order issued by Loksabha. Because Supreme judges can void ONLY an order of Supreme Court or Lower Court, not Parliament. Also, Loksabha can imprison ALL supreme judges. So supreme judges better not clash against Loksabha. If worst comes to worst, PM can ask Military to imprison SCjs and Military is outside the jusridiction of court. i.e. any case against soldier can be moved to court ONLY after Military approves !!
