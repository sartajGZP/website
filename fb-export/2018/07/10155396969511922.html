Useless entity called as "Constitution of Political Party" --- part-2
.
ECI says that every political party should have a constutution. And next it says that party officer bearers should be elected by members, and should be elected democratically.
.
So lets say some 10000 people form a party. Now say that party gains momentum. Then some 20000 congress/rss/aap/etc workers can become members of THAT new party, and then expel the Party president , and appoint some congress/rss/aap/etc leader as party president and ruin the party !!
.
This is also possible in public limited companies, where shared are openly traded. Here, newcomers and outsiders can take control. But here, newcomers will have BUY shares and share prices will increase as more shares are bought. IOW, newcomers will be able to buy company and control over it, but they will need to pay a huge price to existing shareholders first. No such mechanism is possible within ECI guidelines.
.
Suffices to say, ECI has put pretty nonsensical guidelines about what party constitution must have.
.
So what options do those who want political party registration have?
.
ECI leaves them with only one choice. Write the constitution as ECI wants, and then make changes that founders want !!! Or , party apex can simply ignore the party Constitution !!!
