Useless entity called as "Constitution of Political Party"
.
Many people ask me about Constitution of Right to Recall Party, that I am working to get registered. My reply is --- Constitution of a political party is a nonsense concept as of today in India. I made a constitution because election commission has a nonsense rule that a political party which needs registration MUST have a constitution, And then I give them two links --- first is constitution that I will give to ECI and another is the real document which has rules I will implement. (pls see comment for links)
.
Now why is Constitution of political party useless?
.
A rule is useful if govt implements it. Today, no court and no govt and even ECI doesnt refer to Party Constitutions when members of party have dispute. There is NO law in PRA which gives primacy to party constitution.
.
The most important issue in political party is --- who will get loksabha tickets. The ECI goes by list signed by President of the party !! ECI doesnt even see if the list was made by the process mentioned in constitution. IOW, most important issue is settled by signature of one person and no process is DEFACTO required to be followed.
.
Next --- ECI says that party officers bears must be elected by party members democratically. But ECI puts NO restriction on how LS candidates should be appointed by party !! IOW, ECI focuses on issue of petty appointments and ignore main issues
.
IOW, as of now, ECI allows parties to be run by discretion of party's president or apex body of 2-5 leaders and thats all.
.
IOW, ECI never enforces constitution of a political party on a party, But ECI has long list of good things that must be present in the party constitution at the time of registration !!
.
In cases of companies, Company Law has long list of guidelines that companies must follow. ECI guidelines are all "suggestive" and not binding. They become binding ONLY when polls come, and these binding guidelines are only wrt election and not how parties are to be run.
.
So finally, why have two constitutions? I will reply this after party registration is over.
