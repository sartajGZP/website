How YOU can decide which activists to work with on FB\internet and outside internet
.
THESE ARE ONLY GUIDELINES
.
(A) decide whom to work with outside internet

(A01) does he repeatedly phonecall you? IGNORE / BLOCK him. I am against pestering
.
(A02) ELECTION CONTESTANTS --- has he contested elections to publicize RTR? If yes, imo you may prefer him
.
(A03) Has he sent postcards to PM demanding important law-drafts? Sending POSTCARDS to PM is the most important PRIMARY campaign step. Does he have xerox copies of the post cards he has sent or pictures of the postcards he has sent? If he has NOT sent postcards to PM demanding imp law-drafts, pls dont spend time with him.
.
(A04) Does he distribute pamphlets? Then pls BUY some pamphlets from him and also get DOC files. You may rewrite pamphlets or distribute his pamphlets.
.
(A05) Does he hold meetings? If yes, pls attend meetings, provided discussion is on LAW-DRAFTS and campaign steps, and not poems and slogans
.
(A06) Does he focus too much on campaign steps or 1-3 law-drafts? Imo, he is timewaste. RTR movement is about 100s of law-drafts, and surely an activist may decide that some law-drafts are more important than others. But no law-drafts have any monopoly.
.
(A07) does he, his pamphlets, his FB posts, his meetings etc ask voters to take PRIMARY campaign steps on MULTIPLE law-drafts? Or law-drafts that VOTERS SUPPORT? Primary campaign steps are "voter communicating to PM / CM / MP / MLA directly to print law-drafts.. If not, imo, he is timepass.
.
(A08) I strongly oppose fulltimers.. So pls work only with an activist who has job, income etc Even if he iw working only 4 hrs a week (a week), it is better to work with him, that one who has no job etc. If someone has given up job and working full time on RTR campaign, then I dont support this method. (exception - students, those who are between two jobs and looking for job)
.
Based on above guidelines, pls decide whom to work with and whom not to work with outside FB / internet
.
----------
.
(B) decide whom to work with on FB / internet
.
(B01) Does he put all non political posts in specific albums or puts them on wall outside all albums? If he puts non political posts on wall outside albums, the PLEASE DO NOT WORK WITH ON FB \ INTERNET and even outside internet. Most basic etiquette on FB for an ACTIVIST is to put ALL non political personal posts in specific albums, so that voters can UNFOLLOW that album, but FOLLOW that person. If person does NOT want to put his non political posts in specific albums, then working with him will be waste of time.
.
(B02) Look at his cover picture. Does it ask reader to click cover picture so that reader can see description of law-drafts? This is very important.
.
(B03) Pls read cover picture's TEXT description. Does it inform reader about law-drafts he supports? Does the text description give links to law-drafts he supports and other imp links? If yes, then he is imo worth following on FB
.
(B04) Look at list of albums. Do albums provide information on various imp political topics?
.
