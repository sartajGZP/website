(003) My request to management of SocialChains dot io to allow account with verification of aadhar linked mobile only ....
.
This is MY first suggestion to owners of socialchains dot io website.
.
Currently, website asks for aadhar card scan, and then deletes the data after verification. So chances of misuse are zero, UNLESS, an insider employee ILLEGALLY saves the data before deletion !! And that employee later misuses it. The company is US based and US laws are so strict that employees rarely smuggle general end user data. So chances are very very low.
,.
But India, most users will be afraid of giving aadhar card scan to website.
.
So any solution?
.
One solution I have proposed to management is - ask user to provide only mobile number linked with aadhar, and then use OTP to verify that he is possessing that mobile number at the time of opening account.
.
And later, make aadhar card scan giving OPTIONAL
.
So website will have TWO TYPES of account
.
(1) BlueTick --- only mobile phone number verified
.
(2) GreenTick --- mobile phone number and aadhar card scan verified
.
So most people will open BlueTick account, and later some will migrate to GreenTick.
