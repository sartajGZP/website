"Evidence" that one has posted postcard to PM \ CM etc demanding a law-draft --- if A claims that A has sent postcard to PM demanding CancelEVM or MRCM or TCP or WealthTax or CancelGST etc , how can B verify it?
.
(1) A can post scan of postcard, but then may be A wrote the postcard, scanned it and never sent it.
.
(2) next, A can write postcard, and make a video showing postcard showing postcard and putting postcard in postbox. Thats good enough --- but still not very conclusive.
.
(3) The proof I put as --- when B meets A, B can give postcard bought by B to A, and write it again, and then post in front of B !! The cost is borne by B. That way, B can be now fully convinced that A did write postcard demand that law-draft. And here B has to write postcard on B's cost as well. IOW, he who wants proof has to pay for TWO or more postcards !!
.
----------
.
So full scheme I propose is as follows
.
(step-1) A has to give list of all law-drafts A supports and list of all causes for which A has sent postcard to PM, CM etc. Say A supports L001 to L100 and has postcards for L001 to L020
.
(step-2) B has to give list of all causes he supports and list of all causes for which he has sent postcard to PM, CM etc Say A supports M001 to M100 and has postcards for M001 to M010
.
(step-3) Say B wants to verify if A did write postcard to PM on say L005.
.
(step-4) Then B can buy postcard and give to A, and A has to write postcard to PM demanding law-draft L005. And L can post it himself. This B is convinced
.
(step-5) In return, A can ask B to write 2 postcards on any law-draft M001 to M010. Cost will be paid by B and not A.
.
IOW, B wants proof has to pay thrice the cost, and B also helps causes dear to A !!
