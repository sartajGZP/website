How can A ensure that time in communicating with B and (C's time in refering B to C) will not be waste?
.
Say an activist-A is true supporter of DDMRCAM = MRCM and activist-B meets him, and B says "he too is a true supporter of MRCM". Now how can A ensure that B isnt fake? Because if B is fake, then time spent with B got wasted away, and one has only limited time in life.
.
Also, say A is in one city say Banglore for example. And he meets some guy C, who asks "do you know any recallist in Chennai whom I can meet and get info"? So now if A gives name of a TRUE activist to C, then movement will benefit. But if A ends up giving name of a fake activist, then movement will get damaged. So every activist A will need list of true activist B
.
So imo, A should ask B to do 5 simple things
.
(1) ask B vote at https://newindia.in/causes/DdMrcam , and check on his mobile that B has already voted
.
(2) ask B to send postcard to Pm with only few lines, like "sub : DDMRCAM , pls pint https://newindia.in/causes/DdMrcam law-draft - name, voterid" and signature as optional
.
(3) ask B to tweet "@PmoIndia #DdMrcam https://newindia.in/causes/DdMrcam ".
.
If B doesnt take these 3 steps, then A can presume that B is timepass. And imo, if A spends more time with B, then A will be wasting his time.
.
Utilizing time properly is very essential in activism.
