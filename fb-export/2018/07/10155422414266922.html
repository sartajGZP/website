Should we ask @PmoIndia to print a law or MP or both ; and is asking ONLY @PmoIndia "bad"?
.
My answer :
.
(a) communicating to @PmoIndia is must. Method I propose to communicate is @PmoIndia are sending postcard, voting on newindia and tweeting @PmoIndia .
.
(b) after that, sending postcard to MP may be sufficient. One may send SMS, tweet to MP, but after sending msg to PM, I dont see that necessary
.
WHY?
.
(1) Most laws we have proposed are GNs (GN = Gazette Notifications) to be printed by PM. So MPs' permission isnt needed at all
.
(2) If over 50 crore voters have asked PM to print a law, and even when Parliament's permission is technically needed, it is politically feasible for PM to bypass MPs. (see fb.com/10155421147466922 )
.
So imo, communicating to @PmoIndia is sufficient. After that, one may communicate to MP via postcard, SMS, tweeter etc. But imo, now it isnt that important
.
------------------
.
The three main purposes of communicating to PM / MP are
.
(1) purpose-1 : seeing whether supporter is fake or real -- say a person says he supports MRCM, Say he refuses to ask PM / MP to print MRCM. That that convinces us activists that that activist is a fake. IOW, primary purpose is to differentiate between real and fake. And so if a person has communicated to PM via newindia dot in, postcard and tweet, then he has already showed that he is NOT fake. So we need NOT ask him to prove again, by asking him to tweet MP (on his personal handle) and so on.
.
(2) purpose-2 : creating a count before PM / MPs --- by sending postcards to PM, voting on newindia, and tweeting to @PmoIndia , he has created count in PMO. When count in PmoIndia reaches in crores, the MP will come to know thru their unofficial communication channels. And when count is in few hundreds, creating count at one place like Pmo is sufficient.
.
(3) purpose-3 : convincing all and convincing Udham Singh that count is N --- this is needed ONLY after count is in crores. eg today, less than 10000 voters across India support MRCM. So we get NOTHING by telling 90 crore voters and telling Udham Singh that "see, 10000 voters in India support MRCM !!" Because count is so low, the Udham Singh cant act. When crores of voters have communicated to Pmo, ONLY after that convincing crores and convincing Udham Singh that count has crossed crores will be necessary
,.
--------------
.
So communication to @PmoIndia via postcard, tweet and newindia is PRIMARY. Communication to MP is secondary, and I have taken it as optional.\
.
---------------
.
So finally --- I will restate the question -- should we ask @PmoIndia to print a law or or should we ask MP or should we ask both ; and is asking ONLY @PmoIndia "bad"?
.
My answer is : If a person has asked MP, but refuses to ask @pmoIndia via ALL three channels (postcard, newindia and tweet) then I will dismiss him as fake and walk away. Asking MP is a plus, but I wont insist it as condition to decide real or fake.
