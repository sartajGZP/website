(Q) why did I end SMS to MP campaign method. A campaign method in 2011 was "instruct your MP via SMS" . So why is it now "instruct PM via newindia dot in and postcard"
.
my reaosons ::
.
(1) A large number of our proposals can be implemented by GN, and there is no need for legislation. eg MRCM, RTR-PM, RTR-SCCj are all mere GN that can be printed by PM with no permission from MPs needed. Also, if over 50 crore voters have asked PM to print a GN, that violated existing law printed by MPs, then PM can print the GN and only court can cancel it, And if RTR-SCj law-draft is printed, the courts will never dare to cancel a law-draft supported by 50 crore voters. So MPs can be bypassed by PM as well as voters.
.
(2) Most MPs now clearly and OPENLY say "ask high command and dont bother me, and I will vote as per whip they issue". So we only need to tell PM, and may be leaders of apex parties. So your MP belongs to RSS = BJP, you only need to ask Modiji and no one else. Also, MPs can asked for AADHAR COUNTS of each issues votes for voters in their constituencies. So Pmo can ignore all non-mobile votes, take aadhar# of all votes given by all mobiles, and get pincodes, let loksabha constitiencues for that pincodes and give to MPs
.
(3) Most MPs have defunct mobile numbers of Parliament House website. And newindia keeps and shows LOG of votes casted, SMS are stored in records. So voting on newindia PLUS postcards is better than SMS
.
(4) And One early campaign method was "postcard to PM" (see group - https://fb.com/168513566493928 ) . I left that group because one admin was "Janata Mat" which is a fake profile, and later I decided to end all communications with all fake profiles, unless I personally know that person. But I have always advicated method "postcard to PM" and "postcard to MP". This method was before "SMS to MP". Later in 2013, SMS became cheap, and so "SMS to MP" method was added.
.
(5) And "SMS to PM" was preferred to "SMS to MP", but PM had NOT released any mobile number on net. The Only numbers of PMO were landline and SMS dont reach to such landline number. So "SMS to PM" was better than "SMS to MP", but technically not possible. So "SMS to MP" was started, ALONG with "postcard to PM", and "postcard to MP".
.
(6) Now PM has newindia dot in, which allows voting by mobile and voting by fake email-ids. So vote-count on newindia dot in is partly-useless but partly-useful. But PMO's servers knows which votes came from mobiles and which votes came from fake email ids. So PMO does have accurate voter-count for each proposal. And one of my proposal is to AllowAdharLoginOnly so that count become genuine
.
(7) So count is partly useless , partly useful. So now SMS to MP is less needed if person knows how to use OTP based website. So now voting on newindia is more important than "SMS to MP". Also, SMS of most MPs are NOT on Parliament's website. And most mobiles on Parliament's website are defunct. And SMS has no record, while voting on NewIndia is recorded, and user can take printscreen and post on FB
