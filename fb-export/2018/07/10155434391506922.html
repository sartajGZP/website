step - 01: using English for NAME on FB profile
.
step - 01 : pls put first two or all words in the NAME on your FB profile in ENGLISH. And in case you want to use hindi or local language, then you may put first two letters of your name in English and next two words in name in local language.
.
---
.
my reason in short : FB search doesnt work well when name is NOT in English
.
my explanations in detail
.
(1) FB search doesnt work well when your name is in non-English language such as Hindi, Gujarati etc.

(2) So say an activist who just knows your name wants to reach to your profile to say read law-drafts you have proposed or to communicate to you.
.
(3) Then most common way and sometimes only way that activist can use to reach your profile, is by using SEARCH feature of FB
.
(4) so if your name is written in English, then that activist will be able to see your profile in search result and read your posts etc. Otherwise, he will end up spending a lot of time in just searching for your profile and may give up
.
So pls keep the NAME of FB profile in English
