(A) Why imo sending SMS to MP is useless (as of now)

From 2011 to 2014 , I used to request all to send instructions to MPs via SMS to print necessary law-drafts. Today, imo, sending tweet to @PmoIndia is sufficient and sending SMS to MP is timepass. why?
.
(A.1) MP can download all tweets sent to @PmoIndia. MP can also get voterids of voters from tweet sender's profile. And he can keep tweets of his voters and ignore rest. So there is NO need for voter to send separate tweet to MP .
.
(A.2) One can send tweet to @PmoIndia via non-smartphone by sending SMS to 9248948837 = 92489-48837 , ( pls see my tweet at https://twitter.com/MehtaRahulC/status/1019537854676885504 ) . So option of sending tweet is now available to everyone in India who has smartphone or dumbphone
.
(A.3) tweet to @PmoIndia is visible to all and a better record than SMS to sent to MP or anyone.
.
(A.4) So now imo sending SMS to MP is timepass and imo not needed at all.
.
===================
.
(B) Why imo sending tweet to MP is also NOT needed?
.
(B.1) MP can download all tweets sent to @PmoIndia. MP can also make a PUBLIC request to PM to give all tweets sent to @PmoIndia. MP can also get voterids of voters from tweet sender's profile. And he can keep tweets of his voters and ignore rest. So there is NO need for voter to send separate tweet to MP .

(B.2) So I see no need for a voter to send separate tweet to MP
.
(B.3) Also, if a voter has sent a tweet to MP, then a friend of voter cant send same tweet to his MP. He will have to edit it before sending and replace MP's twitter id. And so spread decreases.
.
(B.4) So imo voter should send tweet ONLY to @PmoIndia and not MP or CMs. If tweet is for CM, then he may had hashtag such as #CmStates to communicate that CMs should pay attention to the tweet, But imo a seprate tweet to #CmStates isnt needed
