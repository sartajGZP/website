A general rebuttal to a general anti-RTR-draft argument --- RTR-drafts are good but should be BROUGHT ONLY or after XYZ law is passed or XYZ conditons are fulfiled, or an RTR-law-draft must be brought with XYZ clauses or else it will create damage and not good.
.
my ans ---- Imo, there is NO PRE-CONDITIONING needed for any of the Rtr-drafts. And there is no CO-CONDITION either. I have proposed in my albums "Law draft I support". I dismiss all such pre-conditioning arguments as pointless.
.
(CO-CONDITION means --- saying that X is good ONLY if Y comes along with X or else X is bad. i.e. a person insists that X and Y must be glued)
.
Imo, if ANY RTR-law-draft posted in my albums "law drafts I draft is passed today, without any other RTR-draft or any other draft or any other conditions, then also situation of commons will IMPROVE from existing, and not worsen.
.
eg one may say "RTR-PM , JurySys etc are good, but only if people are educated . If people are uneducated, then RTR-PM , JurySys etc will do damage. So lets not bring RTR-PM , JurySys till 90% people are educated" !!
.
I hear such arguments all the time, and dismiss them.
.
Say ONLY one of the law comes, and everything else is same. Then also, that law will ONLY cause IMPROVEMENT, and no damage from existing situation.
.
Now a SPECIFIC rebuttal will need to me go thru PRE-CONDITION one is putting. And there are too too many people who oppose RTR-drafts, and each one has several such pre-conditions. So I wont find time to write SPECIFIC rebuttal against their SPECIFIC pre-conditions.
