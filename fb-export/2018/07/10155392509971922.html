One more way to send message to PmoIndia , which cant be faked
.
One can send contribution of Rs 102 exact to (PM relief fund) using BHIM, and send 40 character long message. The message can be links to newindia dot in , which has the law-drafts you want.
.
Now BHIM is linked with aadhar, So fake messages can NOT be sent.
.
Using RTI, one may be able to obtain list of all messages sent to PM relief fund where donation amount was Rs 102.
.
The recipet can be kept on phone or uploaded on FB as proof.
.
Currently, one cant send amount below Rs 100. .
.
So this method cant be used for all 1000 law-drafts that a person supports, But he can surely use it for 1 to 10 law-drafts he supports most.
.
Also, one can send at most 40 characters. So he can send 5 to 8 hashtags of newindia dot in labels he supports.
