Those who filed income tax returns early may get notice from income tax dept !!!
.
WHY?
,
Because many PSU as well as private banks have filed TDS and interested earned between 01-apr-2017 to 31-mar-2018 information as late as 02-july-2018 !! And so they will get reflected in person's so called 26as on 09-july-2018. So if that person filed return based on a 26as which he had downloaded before 08-july-2018, then the interest earned data he gave will mismatch with final data given by the bank !!!
.
And so a notice will be sent to him !!
.
On one hand, income tax dept sent be 10 emails asking me to file return asap. And on other hand, banks are given interest earned data and TDS data on as late as 02-jul-2018, which becomes available to person on 09-july-2018 !!
.
And notice may result allegation of under-reporting income and a scrutiny as well !!
.
GoI should first force PSU banks to ensure that all TDS and interest earned information is filed before 15-apr-2018. And update all 26as within a wek. And only then ask persons to file income tax returns !!
