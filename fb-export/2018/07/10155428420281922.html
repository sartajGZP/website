Re : suggestion that "allow only those who voted to participate in recall process" in RTR-MP-law-draft. Imo, a useless suggestion.
.
In the RTR-law-draft I have proposed, one suggestion that I got and I dismiss as useless, is -- in recall process, only those who voted in initial election should be allowed to participate.
.
So as per this useless suggestion, say there 20 lakh voters in Loksabha, and say 15 lakh voted, then in recall process, which may or may not occur, only these 15 lakh should be allowed to participate.
.
Here are reasons why I dismiss this as nonsense, Following are my reasons.
.
(1) The people who made this proposal are yet to give complete RTR-MP-law-draft that adds this suggestion. I have general tendency to dismiss all draftless wonderful proposed laws as useless. So this is one reason why I have dismissed this as useless suggestion.
.
(2) A voter didnt vote because he saw all candidates worthless and he also saw NOTA as worthless. But in recall process, a better option has come, and so now he is willing to participate for recall process. So I am against blocking that voter.
.
(3) Each booth officer keeps record of who voted on paper. And these papers are destroyed after 90 days unless there is a court case and court order. So to implement "allow only those who voted to recall", ECI will need to keep this paper record for 5 years and will also need to make xerox copies and may also need to enter it electronically !! Imo, its a HUGE useless expenditure.
.
(4) Say candidate-C or supporter of C knows that voter-V is likely to recall C if C wins election. And there is voter-S who supports C, but is unable to vote on that day. So chances are high that C will bribe booth officer to mark "S voted and V didnt vote" !!! To avoid that, one would need to ask booth officer to give receipt to every voter who voted, and collector staff or ECI staff will need preserve copy of that receipt for 5 years !! And a whole layer of security will be needed to ensure that booth officers keep correct record of those who voted and those who didnt. So again, costs shoots up, And there will 1000s of complaints and whole layer needed to resolve them
.
(5) Finally, a voter could not vote because he didnt get voting card even though he had applied. But now he has card. I see no reason to exclude him in recall process now. If he is to be included as per this proposal, then another layer of record keeping is needed. And imo, its another huge useless expense.
.
(6) A voter didnt vote because he was below 18 years of age on voting date. But now he is eligible. I see no reason to stop him from participating in recall process. Does this proposal include him? I cant say without seeing the draft, and the proposal maker doesnt give draft !!
.
(7) Say candidate-C knows that voter-V is likely to recall C if C wins election. So chances are now HIGHER that C will use force to stop V from voting. So again, costs of policing election increases
.
And I will write more after those who want this feature give DRAFT of implementing the restriction they have proposed.
