sending booklets / pamphlets by post
.
main advantages --- (a) the sponsors doesnt have to TRUST the worker (b) pamphlet goes to home and so 3-5 person may read, and readership increases before it its thrown away
.
disadvantage - cost is higher than hand to hand distribution
.
What worker can do is ---
.
(1) get 1000-2000 pamphlet printed
(2) put them in A4 size envelope
(3) put address of voters from voterlist
(4) put postage stamp
(5) may or need not put sender's address on pamphlet or envelope (I wont put my address, I will only put my second whatsapp number and fb address)
.
What sponsor can do - the worker can show the bunch to the sponsor. The sponor may or need buy 0 to more enevlopes with all costs --- pamphlet, envelope, postage, labor and then put in mailbox himself
.
