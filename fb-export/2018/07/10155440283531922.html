(1) what does govt do with mineral royalties , spectrurm royalties and land rent from govt plots as of today?
.
Today, Govt deliberately does poor collection of mineral royalties !! eg consider a coal mine in Jharkhand owned by Tata. Tata were Britishers' agents and collaberators and as a gift, British gave a very good coal mine to Tata in 1946 for 999 years lease. The condition was that coal can be used only for making steel in Tata's steel factories and tata cant sell the coal. But who is watching. So the royalty on that coal from 1946 till 2015 (and may be even now) was a princely sum of Re 1 per YEAR per ACRE !! Yes, Re 1 only, that too, not per tonne, but per ACRE and per YEAR. And no minister raised the coal royalty. Last I got information in year 2015, and it was still Re 1 per year per acre. I dont know if it changed after some activists like myself started internet campaign, which didnt go far.
.
Likewise, when coal mines were auctioned, some coal blocks went for as low as Rs 300 per tonne !! The reason given was "coal was to be used for electricity generation and raising royalties will raise electricity prices and worsen economy" !! IOW< some or other excuse is cooked by bikay economists and bikau Ministers. And in many cases, govt officers will simply take bribe and let illegal miners do all the mining, and collect zero royalties
.
What is "right" price of royalties? Say coal fetches Rs 20000 per tonne in market and say coal digging and transport cost is Rs 3000 per toone including interest on investment and normal profits of say 10%. Then a fair auction will enable govt to get royalty of upto Rs 16000 per tonne or even a bit higher But auction rules are rigged to the extent that royalties are never more than Rs 3500 per tonne, and mostly below Rs 1000 per tonne !!
.
This was about coal. Situation is worse in iron, aluminum and worst in crude oil !! Crude oil PSUs like ONGC also pay very low royalties. And so do private companies like Reliance.

So suffices to say --- mineral royalties are deliberately under-collected by using criminals ito stop bidding n auction or rigging auction rules. And the reason is bribery that goes all the way from Patwari to PMO and supreme court judges. And most bikau economist and bikau jornalists too are neck deep in throwing false knowledge and suppressing information.
.
So in that, how will MRCM create any gains? If Royalty from Tata's coal mine is Rs 1 per year per acre, and royalty from coal mine given to Jindal is Rs 300 per tonne, then what do commons get when such peanuts is divided by 90 crore?
.

And how will citizens know that NLRO is corrupt? Well, when royalty is as low as Re 1 per year per acre or Rs 300 per tonne, one doesnt need second thoughts. And in doubtful cases, Jurors may take narcotest of staff in public and extract all truth. Sant Tulsidas once said, that In administration and politics, there is no love without fear. And RTR / Jury installs a "fear from voters" and thus installs a "love for voters". in NLRO and staff. And so they will keep auction process open and less unfair.
