(Q) Can PM print any law, that 50 crore voters of India supported and asked PM to print, and goes against existing laws printed by MPs , without getting new law prinited by LS / RS MPs?
.
My answer : YES. But HOW?
.
PM can first print RTR-SCj-draft i.e. Right to Recall Supreme Court judges as GN. Now RTR-SCj is a GN and so it doesnt need permission of MPs.
.
Now PM can print a GN that 50 crore voters want, without permission of MPs. Now once PM prints a GN. it is law , even if it against laws passed by MPs, unless court cancels the law or court stay orders it. So if 50 crore voters want that law, then judges will NOT dare to cancel that GN printed by PM, after RTR-SCjs-GN has been printed.So Parliament can be bypassed
.
Added later --- any GN printed by PM is "law" and binding on voters unless a court cancels it or stays it. If a voter or an MP thinks that GN is against law printed by MPs, then they will have to ask court to cancel it OR ask MPs to replalce the PM. But till that happens, that GN is binding.
.
So if for whatever reason court refuses to cancel or stay the GN printed by PM, it is binding on voters like all other laws.
.
So Jury System as such will need approval of Parliament. But say 50 crore voters ask PM to print JurySys law, and PM prints it as GN without going to MPs. Then till MPs replace PM (and new PM cancels it) or court cancels JurySys, JurySys start.
.
Now JurySys-law-draft is supported by 50 crore voters, and printed by PM as GN, and NOT approved by MPs, then will SCjs dare to cancel that JurySys GN? If there is law-draft like RTR-SCjs, then imo judges wont dare to cancel any GN supported by 50 crore voters.
.
So if 50 crore voters have asked PM to print a law, then imo we need not worry about what MPs think and do, what judges think and will do etc. We do need to worry about whether desi-videshi elitemen will work to create armed revolt or not.
