How GST will reduce employee efficiency in large company as well, and how RERA will further reduce efficiency of even large builders
.
I will first give example of one large builder I happened to know. He had staff over 100 civil engineers, along with 10s of other professions. One remark he made, which all business owners will see --- (a) some of his civil engineers were 9 am to 5 pm , just do work assigned to them and never take much deep interest even in their own work (b) but some 10% of his engineers were hyperactive --- they would do not just their work perfectly, but would also be always willing to assist others work such as plumbing, electricity, tiles fitting, chasing labor and almost everything, even assisting CAs !!
.
Not only these hyperactive staff was prompt, perfectionist, always trying to ensure that their and others' work is error free, but they were putting more hours without asking. And their total output would be several times normal, thought salary would be same or sometime may be just 20% to 30% more.

What makes such hyperactive employees tick?
.
In almost all cases, their goals was --- to start their own business !! To start their own construction firm or contractor.
.
So they were willing to learn ALL disciplines' and all agencies' works because their goal was to become builders themselves. So they would try their best to get experience in everything. So these employees were not just productive, but they would also increase others' productivity. And they become benchmark for other employees to perform.
.
One can see this across industries, across countries across time. In software business, an engineer is more motivated than in civil engineering. Because in software, starting new business is easier. In automobile engineering, the engineers are far less motivated than civil engineering, because starting own motor making company is far far more difficult than becoming a builder.
.
But now say that Govt rules become so, that it is almost impossible for a newcomer to start business. In such cases, such hyperactive employees would see that there is no way they can become master of their own business. So many of them will eventually start behaving like mediocres as well.
.
Thats what GST does, and RERA does worse
.
GST is making MORE difficult for newcomer to start business. Not just CA costs and accounting headaches have increased, but buyers insist on buying only from large sellers due to "buyer will bear burden of missing trader" clause i.e. clause-16.2.c of CGST act . So newcomer doesnt get clients easily.
.
So now newcomers are finding it more and more difficult to start manufacturing business. And so they have no option but to remain employee. Even the active ones see that they will never be able to run their own business, and will remain as employee for whole life. And so their motivation will start decreasing.
.
And RERA is killing existing small builders, and making it far more difficult for new small builders to come up.
.
So all these factors will further reduce active employees' enthusism even in large companies. And so over productivity and INNOVATION will decrease
.
And meanwhile USA has no excise, no GST, no VAT, no service tax and has only state level sales tax on end sales of goods (and not on services). And they have less RERA like laws. So motivation of employees will be higher in US companies in USA> So they will be able to accomplish more innovation and higher productivity. And so US companies will become more competitive than Indian companies,. and will be able to defeat Indian companies in almost all fields
.
SOLUTION?
.
Solution I propose is to scrap GST and instead implement wealth tax. And RERA should be replaced by Jury System, where Jurors can punish an errant builder, but not impose suffocating restrictions on him.
.

.
