Possibility of replacing income tax by proposed "wealth increase tax" and proposed "wealth tax"
.
I have discussed proposed wealth tax in previous posts and wont discuss here. The new concept, yet undrafted, I am proposing here is "wealth increase tax" and imo that can replace income tax, but not wealth tax
.
And for the purpose of this tax, "Wealth" will exclude money in current accounts and cash in hand !! So all citizens will have a 0% current account, in which he may keep zero balance for no charge. And wealth will include FDs, savings, land etc
.
Now proposed wealth tax on FDs / savings will be zero. But wealth increase tax will apply on FDs / savings , but not cash in hand and current account
.
How does this proposed "wealth increase tax" apply?
.
First, it applies on book value and NOT market value. (note - proposed wealth tax applies on estimated market value, and not book value).
.
Say tax starts on say 01-jan-2018;. Say a man's BOOK VALUE of wealth on 01-jan-2018 is say Rs 1.00 crore
.
Now say his
wealth on 02-jan-2018 is Rs 1.20 crore
no change till 05-jan-2018
wealth on 06-jan-2018 is Rs 1.50 crore
no change till 12-jan-2018
wealth on 12-jan-2018 is Rs 1.10 crore
no change there after for 4 years
.
So he will pay
(a) wealth increase tax on 20 lakhs for 3 days
(b) wealth increase tax on 50 lakhs for 7 days
(c) wealth increase tax on 10 lakhs for next 4 years
.
So say tax rate is 5% a year.
.
So wealth tax increase tax will be

(a) 20 lakhs * 3 / 365 * 5 / 100 = Rs 822
(b) 50 lakhs * 3 / 365 * 5 / 100 = Rs 822
(c) 10 lakhs * 4 * 5 / 100 = Rs 2 lakhs
.
Now for exact definition, I am using wealth owned on daily basis. But for implementation for lower values of wealth, one may use wealth at monthly basis. .
.
The basis --- whatever has been increase in wealth, it will be taxed at say 5% a year, for at most 4 years, but NOT if increase disappears in a smaller time duration.
.
The plus point over income tax is --- one doesnt need to store all and match all transactions while calculating and verifying taxes. One has to show only his books. And unlike income tax, here (wealth increase + wealth decrease) gets taxed at a lower rate. So it will be less damaging for a risk taker.
.
