(5) Why communicating to PMO is necessary?
.
Many laws we have proposed dont need MPs' approval and PM's signature will suffice. eg MRCM, RTR-PM, one version of RTR-MP, RTR-SCCj, RTR-SCj, RTR-HCCJ etc. And even if MP's approval is needed, PM can always print the law as Ordinance and then ask MP to approve or reject.
.
And "should I communicate to PM or not", is subjective.
.
eg if X says that I support law-draft-A, then first request I will make is "did you tell PmoIndia that you want law-draft-A"? And after several reminders, if X refuses to communicate to PmoIndia, then I will NOT take X as supporter of law-draft-A. He may or or may nor be, But I wont be spending my time with him. After all, my time is important (and so is everyone's time). And no activist-A would like to wok with an voter-B or activist-B if voter-B or activist-B doesnt meet certain criteria. And one NECESSARY condition is communicating to PMO in a manner that doesnt disrupt civil life of other citizens..
.
So imo, steps to communicae PM are important.
.
