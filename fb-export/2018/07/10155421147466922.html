(Q) Can PM print any law, that 50 crore voters of India supported and asked PM to print, without permission of LS / RS MPs?
.
my answer :
.
YES. But HOW?
.
Any GN (GN = Gazette Notification) printed by PM is a law on citizens till a court cancels it, The officers who implement law take orders from PM and judges, and not from MPs.
.
So if for whatever reason court refuses to cancel or stay the GN printed by PM, it is binding on voters like all other laws.
.
So PM can first print RTR-SCj-draft i.e. Right to Recall Supreme Court judges as GN. Now thats a GN and doesnt need permission of MPs.
.
Now PM can print a GN that 50 crore voters want, without permission of MPs. Now once PM prints a GN. it is law , even if it against laws passed by MPs, unless court cancels the law or court stay orders it. So if 50 crore voters want that law, then judges will NOT dare to cancel that GN printed by PM, after RTR-SCjs-GN has been printed.So Parliament can be bypassed
.
Consider an example. Say Jury System law-draft. As such, this needs to be passed by Parliament. But say 50 crore voters ask PM to print JurySys law, and PM prints it as GN without first asking MPs to print the law. Then till MPs replace PM (and new PM cancels it) or court cancels JurySys, JurySys start.
.
Now JurySys-law-draft is supported by 50 crore voters, and printed by PM as GN, and NOT approved by MPs, then will SCjs dare to cancel that JurySys GN? If there is law-draft like RTR-SCjs, then imo judges wont dare to cancel any GN supported by 50 crore voters. And if judge still dares, then voters can expel the SCj using Right to Recall Supreme judges GN and next judge can cancel previous judges' order, and that would restore JurySys.
.
So if 50 crore voters have asked PM to print a law, then imo we need not worry about what MPs think and do, what judges think and will do etc. We do need to worry about whether desi-videshi elitemen will work to create armed revolt or not.
