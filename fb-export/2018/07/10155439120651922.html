which of 500+ law-drafts to pick and which to ignore?
.
There are over 500 law-drafts in RTR-movements and 5000 or even more may come. eg we have law-drafts on RTR-over-100-positions, which can be grouped as 10-20 RTR-law-drafts, then JurySys law-drafts, MRCM, wealth tax, many on taxation and black rupee in land issue, bangladeshi, weaponization, freeing temples, goraksha , two child law etc etc. And more will keep coming
.
No recallists, not even myself, can even click "vote" on all these law-drafts on newindia. I will send at most 300-500 tweets to @PmoIndia, and no more. And will write some 50-100 postcards and no more. And when it comes to newspaper advt or pamphlerts, I will confine to 20-30 law-drafts only. Thats based on my existing resource limitation. Now if I get 10 times more resources, then I may add more law-drafts.
.
So eventually, all recallists will be facing this questions
.
--- out of 500s of proposed-law-drafts
(a) which 500 shall I support on newindia and which I wont?
(b) which 300 shall I tweet @PmoIndia and which I wont?
(c) which 100 shall I write postcards and which I wont?
(d) which 50 shall I write pamphlets on which I wont?
(e) which 20 shall I give newspaper advt and which I wont?
and so on
.
So each recallist has to decide.
.
My criteria is --- arrange these proposed law-drafts in some order, then take first ones for which you have time.
.
But then, how to decide which law-draft is more important than others? It is HIGHLY SUBJECTIVE and I leave it as each one on his own.
.
For me MRCM is most important, because imo poverty or poor is most important, and this law-draft can reduce poverty at fastest possible rate. But then MRCM doesnt appeal to middle class and support of middle class is needed to take any movement in second gear,. In middle class, small traders and small manufacturers may find wealth tax and Jury System more attractive than MRCM. But poor may not find much appeal in JurySys or wealth tax, as tax laws dont directly effect them, and as of now, they have no dealing with courts. Likewise, someone very much worried about demographic problem will find two child law more attractive and some worried to save Hinduism may find law-drafts to end govt control over temples more appealing. RTR / TCP are general enabler. RTR reduced tendency and possibility that Ministers / judges will try to sell away commons to serve his elitemen friends. And TCP spreads pro-common information at much much rate than paidmedia or even social media, and so nefarious benefit elitemen get by blocking / dispersing information reduces, But unless some law-draft like wealth tax or two child law or MRCM comes, RTR / TCP dont reduce physical problem at hand. So many who support wealth tax may say "I want wealth tax, and so I will do activism to directly bring wealth tax , and I dont want two step process of doing activism to first bring TCP and then use TCP to bring wealth tax, and so I will work on wealth tax alone, and not TCP and then wealth tax via TCP route. " And many may prefer opposite route i.e. first TCP and then wealth tax via TCP, and some may work on both law-drafts. And imagine someone in Asam or West Bengal, whose house is now only 500 meters away from Bangladehies and Bangladeshies just took over near by area. He may say "hell with TCP , MRCM, JurySys etc, I only want to work on law-draft to expel Bangladeshies" !! And consider a place which is running out of water and forced migration threat is looming due to water shortage and wastage. That person might say "hell with all law-draft and I want to work on WaterMeter law-draft only !!". And many worried about diseases like cancer etc may insist on ReduceAdulteration law-draft only, and many parents worried about kids education may insist on RtrDeo only.
.
In short, each one has priority and urgency,.
.
So each activist has to decide. On "which drafts to select", I have zero suggestion to someone who has picked up which drafts he wants to work on.
.

and many more
.
And there is album "law-draft I support (top 50) which has 20-30 law drafts for now and will have some 50-60 law-drafts down in a month,
.
Now one may say --- can you add xyz law-draft say CancelEVM in top 10? Well, that means I have to first remove one of the above ten proposed law-drafts !! And if I dont think that CancelEVM is more imp then anyone of the above, then I wont add CancelEVM in top-10 and it will be in next 40 list only
.
But it may happen, than someone proposed a law-draft ABC, and I think " yes, this is more imp than one law-draft DEF in top 10 and then I will remove DEF and instead put law-draft ABC in top-10 list.
.
Also, as SITUATION around changes, the priority may change. eg National DI was high in my list in year 2008. But after Aadhar System, its now lesser priority than it was. Similarly, priority I give to WaterMeter law is now more than I gave in year 1999 !!
.
But whatever may be the case --- one will never find time to read all 500 law-drafts and 1000s more to come. I will NEVER ask activist "dont promote your favorite law-draft ,because we have too many law-drafts" !! Who knows, his law-draft may be far better than most of the law-drafts we have? So new law-drafts will keep coming from existing and new activists, and I wont be work to end the supply. But at the same time, I wont be able to even see the complete list, forget read all drafts and decide which to keep where in my priority list.
.
And this will be also be situation with most recallists. They too will keep getting new law-drafts and they will have to decide which to read and which to ignore, which to work on, and which to ignore as they will never find time to process all. Imo, this is a PERMANENT problem to stay.
