Dear recallists - pls dont allow anyone to write on your wall , and other FB tips
.
(1) pls dont allow anyone to write on your wall and pls dont allow anyone to tag you. --- pls keep settings so that ONLY you can write on you wall. And pls dont allow anyone to tag. Pls google to see how to block tagging and how to ensure that only you can write on your wall
.
(2) pls ensure that those who want to see your political posts see ONLY political posts --- so if you are putting personal posts like some party pictures, you can put in album and set "who can see" to close friends, family etc. That way, when a person interested in politics comes on your wall, he sees only political posts
.
(3) avoid post on breaking news that all know, unless you are writing solution
.
(4) if the post is on problem, pls ensure that post has links to drafts and a short summary of drafts you propose as solution,. You can copy-paste from a standard status post which has summaries and links to drafts. And MOST IMPORTANT, pls add 1-2 line which tell reader what HE can do get those law-drafts printed.
