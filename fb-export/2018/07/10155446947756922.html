What is Right to recall in short?
.
Right to Recall means law-drafts using which citizens can recall / replace PM, supreme court judges, replace CM, replace high court judges, replace MP, replace MLA, replace District Police Chief, replace District Education Officer, replace Mayor, replace City Councilor, replace RBI Governor, replace SBI Chairman etc, if and when and only if the need be.
.
Psudeo-recallists and anti-recallists both inist that RTR is confined to MP / MLA. Well, RTR is NOT just over MP / MLAs. eg In California, High Court judges are appointed by Governor/MLAs, but voters can recall that High Court judge. And voters in USA also have right to recall over District Education Officer, Sheriff i.e. District Police Commissioner, Governor etc .
.
One of my goal is to get ALL these RTR law-drafts over 10s of apex nodal positions at national / state / district levels printed in India. And in addition get several law-drafts like MRCM, Jurysys, referendum (TCP) etc printed.
