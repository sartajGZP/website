Allow hotel customers to pay IGST instead of SGST
----------------------------------
.
Respected Finance Minister,
Govt of India,
New Delhi.
.
sub : allow a hotel to issue IGST bill instead of SGST, if the customer has registration in a different state
.
(1) Say a service provider from state-A provides services in state-B, and has to stay in a hotel in state-B. In such case, hotel will give bill with 9% SGST (or 14% SGST) of state-B, which the service provider coming from state-A can NOT defacto use as input credit.
.
(2) So now please consider situation of a small service provider vs a large service provide.
.
(2a) The large service provider will have business across India in almost all states, and so will have registration in all states. While the small service provider will have registration in only one state or two states
.
(2b) So a large service provider will be able to use hotel’s SGST as input credit. But a small business provider will NOT be able to use SGST of other states, because small service provider has registration only in one state
.
(2c) so defacto, this causes DOUBLE TAXATION on services of small service provider. And makes small service provider LESS competitive. So customers may have to prefer a large service provider even when small service provider is more efficient. This is a loss to India’s competitiveness in economy.
.
(3) SOLUTION : we request you to make changes in GST laws, so that hotel may issue IGST bill when customer is from other state.
