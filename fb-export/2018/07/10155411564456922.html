Verifying whether X has sent postcard to PM or not demanding law-draft-A
.
Consider a law-draft namely https://newindia.in/Ddmrcam .
.
If a person-A says that he supports MRCM, then first question I will ask him is "did you send postcard to PM citing '#Ddmrcam https://newindia.in/Ddmrcam ' with your voterid / name ? If not, you may be a supporter or may not be, but I will not take you as DdMracam supporter, and I dont want to spend time wrt to activism for DdMrcam with you".
.
Now say he says he did send this postcard. Then how would I know that he is speaking the truth?
.
Solution is --- I will give him blank postcard and ask him to write again, and send that postcard myself !!!
.
If a person has send the postcard once, hen he should have no problem in sending it again.
.
So when activist-A meets activist-B, A can ask for list of causes B has supported by sending postcard to PM. From those 10-20-100 causes, A can pick any 5, and ask B to write 5 postcards with those 5 causes again !! If B writes, then A can send those postcards again, and so now A is convinced. And B may or need not do the same process for 1 to 5 causes that A supports. That way, A may verify B or B may verify A or both may verify each other.
.
------------
.
How is verification useful?
.
These days, I run into too many people who claim that they support RTR !! How can I convince neutral audience that they are fake? Well, I can ask the question "did you ask PmoIndia to print which RTR-drafts"? They give no clear answer and that convinces the audiences that they are fake. And if they say "RTR-PM-law-draft" or some such RTR-law-draft, then my next question is "pls tell me how you communicated to Pmoindia to print this Rtr-X-law-draft you claim you support". And then finally, if they say they did write a postcard, then by asking him again, I can either increase support for Rtr-X-law-draft, or I can convince audience. At least one good purpose is served !!
