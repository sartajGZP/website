How to use facebook effictively

Completely ignore newsfeed !!!

Make following lists of profiles/pages

(1) Daily
(2) OddDayList, EvenDayList
(3) MondayList, TusedayList, WedList, ThusList ..
(4) 1st_of_month, 2nd_of_month, 3rd_of_month ...

Daily are people whose posts you wish to read everyday

OddDay and EvenDay -- people whose posts you want to read every alternate day
.
MonList, TueList, WedList ,,, - people whose posts you want to read once a week

1st_of_month, 2nd_of_month, 3rd_of_month ...--- people whose posts you want to read once a month
.
====
.
