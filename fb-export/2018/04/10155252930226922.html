.
Lets say a you are contesting election, and a voter says that he will vote for you. And say ballot papers and not EVMs are used. The voters says that he will vote for you, but he will NOT put stamp on your symbol. he wants to support and vote for you but NOT in the way govt orders him to vote and support. he will vote and support in his OWN way !! Instead of stamping on your symbol, he will write a poem on ballot paper supporting you !! How is this FORMAT of his support?
.
There are times when things are useful ONLY they are in UNAMBIGUOUS accepted form, when they are counted. And counting lakhs and crores of items is possible only when items are in FORMAT. An item not in format will need be rejected, and and cannot be counted.
.
So the voter who refused to stamp, but wrote a poem on ballot paper supporting you, may be useful in real war or may not be useful in real war. But he is of no use in election.
.
The activism consists of several activities. And many activists may not like to confirm to put their activities in a FORMAT . WHY? Because sometimes they feel that format is crushing their individuality or may be some other psychological reason. Another reason is --- many activists will believe that their format is better and so they might want all other activists to follow their format.
.
Here, one aspect is counting, and AUTOMATED counting. eg say each activist and voter write on twitter "Evm should be cancelled" or "EVM should be banned" "or EVMs should be replaced by ballot paper" etc etc. There can be 1000 such statements. If say each activist promotes a DIFFERENT statement, then no programmer can automate the counting. So lets say activist decide to use hashtage. But again, one may prefer to use hashtag #CancelEVm , anotner may use #BanEVM, hird may propose to use #BanEvmBringBallotBack and so on. I know NO solution to this disagreement. I leave it to each activist to decide the hashtags he wants for his causes
.
Next is tabulating information. Say information is put on facebook status in albums. Say there are 1000 activists promoting law-drafts to reduce poverty. Say all agree to write posts on poverty in an album on their profile. Say each one prefers a different name as album title. One prefers "How to reduce poverty". Another prefers "law-drafts to reduce poverty" and so on. Now say a NEW activist decides to read posts on this topic and he knows 10 activists who are working on this cause. Then has to go to each activists profile, and look at entire list of album, see each album's title and see which album will have posts on poverty reduction. But if each activist agrees that title will be "(B029) Reducing poverty" , the new activitist will have to spend less time. A new activist can go to profile of existing activist and see if that album exists, and if that album doesnt exist, then he can be sure that that activist is working that specific cause. And he may even be able to write an FB script to which he can give list of 100 activists' FB IDs, and the script will pull out all posts in public albums of all these 100 activists, where album title is "(B029) Reducing poverty" or where first five letters are (B029). . But again, activists will have differences. Why put number to begin with? Why put prefix B and not A? and why shouldnt number be 29 and not 01? So even if all activists see that point that using same album titles will enable automation and may increase speeds of newcomer activist, the activists still may not be able to agree on exact text of the title. So so automation may never happen.
.
Variation reduce speed and automation in short term. But variations often lead to qualitative improvement. All in all, there is no end to variations. So different religions and legal systems have their own sub systems to ignore, ban or promote variations. eg Islam solved the problem of variation (bidah) by classifying them into two groups - good bidah and bad bidah. eg1 use of technology is good variation. eg2 But if someone says "lets shift time of most important weekly namaj from friday 2pm to thus 4pm" then it is bad bidah. And there is punishment for bad bidah. And who decides whether bidah is good or bad and who decides punishment for bad bidah? Maulvi, gathering and hierarchy of maulvies . And Shia and Sunni have different hierarchies of maulvies and so bitter fight starts. Other religions and legal systems have their own ways.
.
Also, variations can sometimes co-exist with old form. eg New version of word has docx files, but old doc files are supported. But this doesnt go indefinitely. eg Apple supports old iPhones even after releasing new iPhones, but not forever. So a person who is proposing variation in campaign method has to decide whether he wants to support BOTH methods --- original and variation or not. And for how long?
.
I have not proposing any system etc at this stage to deal with variations in campaign methods in us recallists. I will be putting by my methods, as I develop them. Sometimes, I will be able to give reasons, but I wont find time to write reasons all the time. here is no end to variations, and sometimes a variation can be be indeed better.

SO A GENERAL RULE I put for each activist is --- a variation reduces counting, reduces speed of newcomer and makes automation almost impossible. So each time a variation is proposed, pls see if the advantage it offers is higher than losses. And whether both methods - original and variation can be supported or not and for how long.
.
So one thing is --- without format, newcomers cannot count, will have low speed and cannot automate. And whenever there is a suggested format, there will be variations.
.
So each time there is a SUGGESTED FORMAT, and there is a variation, I request activists to decide on his own, what benefits the suggested format has, and whether the benefits are more than losses for newcomers - decrease in speed, decrease in counting, and possible loss of automation.
