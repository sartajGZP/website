Cash crunch --- Possible reasons and not possible reasons
.
Cash crunch is when

(a) X withdraws cash, and doesnt later deposit back in bank and and hoards it

(b) X withdraws cash, buy goods/service or land from Y, and Y hoards it
.
So why would people suddenyly start hoarding cash? Or, what are they buying so that seller is hoarding cash and not depositing it?
.
--------------
.
Possible reason - 1 : Desies or Foreigners are buying a huge amount land
.
When FMCG goods are bought, cash is used. But most retailers deposit x% of cash in bank and keep rest. And this x will not suddenly increase. But when land is sold, the seller will NOT deposit cash in bank as cash is off the book. He will slowly spend it or he will pass it when he is buying land. So if suddenly number of land deals shoot up, then demand of cash will increase.
.
So one reason can be --- some apex elitemen are buying huge amount of plots. But desi elitemen are all cash starved. So may be some desi elitemen are buying lands, but most large buyers will be videshi elitemen.
.
So a large number of desi/videshi elitemen on buying spree for land can be reason for increase in currency demand
.
--------------
.
Possible reason - 2 : More and more cash transactions to evade GST
.
Its possible that more and more traders and manufacturers are moving to cash to evade GST. So demand of cash is increasong
.
----------------
.
Possible reason - 3 : Fear of bailin and FRDI
.
Modiji was about print one of the most corrupt and defunct law in history of India --- bailin over PSU banks as well as private banks. Bailin over private banks is fine. But bailin over PSU bank is a HIGHLY corrupt and defunct proposal. But if Modiji is bringing bailin over PSU banks, and if apex people know about it, then they are withdrawing all the deposits, This can cause cash crumch
.
-------------------------
.
Not possible reason - 1 : cash withdrawals due to Karnataka election
.
Total cash supply in india Rs 18,00,000 crores and amount of cash used in KN election will NOT be more than Rs 100 crore per constituency, there are less than 250 constituency in KN and so below Rs 25000 crore. and all of hat will NOT be cash, Many payments to media houses are by cheque. So election cant be major reason
.
-----------------------------
.
Not possible reason - 2 : economy is growing
.
Growing economy needs more cash. But unless there si sudden growth, it can cause cash crunch. And if economy is growing. then electricity consumption should also grow and so should sale of several basic goods. That isnt happening. So it is unlikely reason
.
