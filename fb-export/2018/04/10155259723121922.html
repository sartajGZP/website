#AllowVotingOnNewIndiaViaPatwariOffice -- Allow voting on NewIndia via patwari office

NewIndia. in website is a great step by GoI. Here any voter can put a proposal, and any voter across India can support it by voting on it.
.
But right now, one needs internet to give support. And over 70 crore voters are NOT capable of using net - lack of net or lack of training.
.
So I have requested @PmoIndia to make a system, where a (1) voter can go to local Patwari / Talati / Village Officer’s office (2) show his voter-id (3) give proposal HEADLINE (4) vote on the proposal. That way, crores of voters who cant use SMS or net will get connected to NewIndia dot in.

THIS PROPOSAL HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH VOTING IN ELECTIONS (no pdf)
.
