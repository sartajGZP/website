A tale from Buddha's life
-----------------------------
.
A mother took his son to Buddha. And she said to Buddha "My son eats too much sugar. Pls advice him not eat too much sugar". Buddha thought for a while. And then he said "Pls come to me after 1 month".
.
After 1 month, the mother came. And Buddha told the boy not to eat too much sugar. And the boy gave up his bad habit.

Buddha's disciple saw that. So the disciple asked "why didnt give him the advice on the first day? why did you ask him to come again after 1 month?".

Buddha replied "One month back, I myself had this bad habit of eating too much sugar. So if I had given this advice not to eat too much sugar to the boy, the advice would not have worked , whether the boy knows about my bad habit or not. . So 30 days back, I gave up eating sugar completely, and decided not to eat much sugar ever again in my life. And only after that, then I gave the advice. "
.
