(B070) Recruitment by exams - abolish interview, change subjects
.
One of my goals is to defacto abolish interviews i.e. reduce its weightage to almost zero, and change subjects in recruoment exams. Subjects like political superstition (aka political science) , economics, sociology, history, psychology etc should be removed. And suvjects like maths, logic, sciences etc should be added.
.
Another goal is to add subjects like cooking, dance, music, modelling (ramp walking), script writing etc in UPSC IAS exam selection. Because these subjects are "important". My point is --- do you see why subjects like political superstition (aka political science) serve no purpose in UPSC exams?
