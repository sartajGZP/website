If you want lakhs of activists and crores of voters to take a step --- then FIRST thing you should do is -- (a) do that activity yourself (b) put the activity description etc on FB , Twitter, NewIndia as applicable
.
And make sure , that THAT post is REACHABLE thru some "table of contents" post pointing to some other "table of contents" post finally pointing to the posts
.
Because others may or may not do, if they SEE that you have taken that step. But if you havent taken that step, then there is NO way they can see on your FB profile that you have taken that step.

In such case, if you want them to take a step, then you will need to SPEAK to all of them to take that step. And that will be too time consuming. And also you will have to explain why you want them to take step while you yourself havent taken that step yet. And that will be too clumsy.
.
Where as if description of your step along with posts showing how you took that action step are on FB, then all 10 lakh activists and 5 crore voters of India who have access to internet will be able to see how that step can be executed.
.
But of you think that "others should take that step, but I will not take that step" for whatever reason, then you need to write or videotape a whole explanation on why you want others to take that step, while you wont take that step.
.
So imo, best way to ask lakhs of activists and crores of voters to take an action step, is to first take that action step and then put description of that text in an FB post. And then make sure that Link is in "Table of contents" list, and link to that "Table of content list" is in the MAIN "table of contents" list. , so that that link is reachable..
