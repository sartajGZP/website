NarcoTest in Public Act – Narcotest in Murder and serious violence cases
.
comment : Once this act is passed, the judges or Jurors will be able to ask accused or complainers to go on narcotest, with their approval. And the test will be optional for them. But in cases involving extreme violence, the judge or Jurors may decide to force accused but not others to take the test. The judge or Jurors will decide whether narcotest will be in public or confidential i.e. done before court in camera. When done confidentially, the portion disclosing name of victim will be removed and rest of the audio/video will be made public. The questions will be decided by judge or Jurors, with help of policemen and experts. The answers given in narcotest will NOT be taken as evidence.
