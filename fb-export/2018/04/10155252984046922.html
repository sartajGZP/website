Deciding album's titles and numbers
.
I have proposed that we activists should put posts inside albums on walls and not directly on wall. So now question is what should be names of the albums and whether albums should have numbers, and if yes, what should be the numbers.
.
Here I will use following guideline for myself --
..
(1) I will put first letter an alphabet and next three letters will be number between 000 to 999. And I will put them in brackets and then put a space character after that. So first SEVEN letters of my album titles will be "(XDDD) " where X is alphabet , DDD are digits and there is a space after ")".
.
(2) After several letters I will put title
.
(3) I will use prefix "A" for albums which deal with activism
,
(4) I will use letter B for albums related to causes.
.
(5) For activism, I have decide the numbers which one can see in list of album in album "(A001) Table of contents" after 5 days from today
.
(6) For numbers of albums related to causes, I have used following methods
.
(7) Each cause is close to a govt department. eg cuase of "poverty reduction" is imo conceptually closest to Finance Ministry. Now I took EXPENSE budget 2018-2019 pdf from govt website. The first two pages in that pdf has LIST of all departments. So to decide number for cause "Reducing poverty", I decided to give it same number as "Finance Ministry". But FinMin doesnt have serial number but departments in FinMin do have serial number. And first entry in FinMin is #29. and so I have decided to give #29 to cause "reducing poverty". So title of my album to put posts on reducing poverty is "(B029) Reducing poverty" .
.
Examples
.
(7.1). "Improving agriculture" is "(B001) Improving Agriculture" , because serial #1 in expense budget is given to dept of agriculrue
.
(7.2) "Goraksha" is "(B003) Ending cow slaughter and ending unfair subsidies to meat" because serial #3 in expense budget is "3. Department of Animal Husbandry, Dairying and Fisheries" and it is closest to this cause.
.
So using this method, I have decided album numbers for most of the causes.
.
