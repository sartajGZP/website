First JurySys Act – District Jury System for Murder and other violent crimes
हत्या औरअन्य हिंसक अपराधों के लिए जिला जूरी सिस्टम ड्राफ्ट
( हिंदी अनुवाद अंग्रेजी ड्राफ्ट बाद दिया है )
.
Once this act is passed, punishments / fines / acquittals, in cases such as murder, abduction, rape, beatings, death due to negligence etc will be decided by 15 voters to 1500 voters chosen at RANDOM from District / State or India, between the age of 25 years and 55 years. And later, PM / CM may add types of crimes and types of civil disputes that will be decided by Jurors and not judges
---------------------
.

-----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : summary of the law
.
(1.1) comment : This law creates Jury System in Magistrates’ Courts District Courts and Sessions Courts, for crimes such as murder, beatings, threat, rape, abduction and all crimes where physical force or threat of using physical violence is used.
.
(1.2) comment : once this law passes, you (you means registered voter in India) can be called for Jury Duty. In Jury Duty, you will have to listen to facts and arguments as presented by accused, victim, witnesses and lawyers of both sides and decide the punishment/fines or acquittal.
.
(1.3) comment : The jury system will be supervised by an officer called as District Jury Administrator or DJA. DJA will be appointed by CM ad you (you means voters of District) can replace him using “(RTR) Right to Recall DJA” clauses given in this law.
.
(1.3.1) comment : In “Right to recall District Jury Administrator”, you can approve at most 5 candidates anyday and he may cancel approval of any candidate anyday. And you need not file any approval if you don’t wish to.
.
(1.3.2) comment : The candidate which has highest approval and approval of over 35% of all voters in district, will replace existing DJA and become new DJA.
.
-----------------------------------
Part - II : Instructions for Citizens, Officers and comments
------------------------------------
.
(2) This law will cover only following cases, regardless of age of accused and victims
.
(2.1)murder, attempt to murder, any human death due to accident or negligence or any unnatural human death
(2.2) any crime involving violence, such as beating, or threat of violence or accident or negligence that caused bodily harm or could have caused serious injury
(2.3) abduction / rape / molestation / harassment / stalking of woman of any age, including harassment at workplace.
(2.4) any type of crime or any type of civil dispute mentioned by PM or CM using Gazette Notification in this list
(2.5) any crime or any type of civil dispute mentioned by majority voters via using “(CV) Citizen’s Voice” clauses, and approved by PM / CM.
.
(3) If a type of crime or a type of civil dispute is added by CM, then it will apply only for that state. If it is added by PM, then it will apply for whole nation or States specified by PM in that GN. The CM / PM may specify whether Jurors will be chosen from District / State or neighboring Districts/ States or whole of India.
.
(3.1) comment : This law, at start, will only cover some crimes where the argument that - “Jurors don’t understand law about such and such crime” – has been rejected as outright lie by majority voters. Later, PM / CM or voters can add types of crimes as well as types of civil disputes where they believe that argument that “Jurors don’t understand law involving such and such crime” has been rejected as outright lie by most voters of India. eg PM / CM / voters may add crimes like gohatya, corruption, nepotism, theft. fraud, cheque bounce, loan defaults, forgery of land sale documents and so on
.
(3.2) comment : PM / CM may also add types of civil offences .such as tenant / owner dispute, labor disputes and so on, where PM / CM / voters see that laws and matter are simple enough that Jurors do understand, and the argument that argument that - “Jurors don’t understand law involving such and such crime” has been rejected as lie by most voters of India.
.
(4) Temporary suspension of Jury System in a District
.
(4.1) With approval of over 51% of ALL citizen voters in that State, CM can suspend all above clauses and appoint his own DJA for 5 years in one or more districts. CM may change DJA anytime during these 5 years. And CM may move a case of that district to any other district of selected at random
.
(4.2) With approval of over 51% of ALL citizen voters in India, PM can suspend all above clauses for any districts in India and appoint DJA for 5 years in those districts. PM may change DJA anytime during these 5 years. And PM may move a case of that district of a neighboring state’s district selected at random
.
(5) The CM will appoint an officer as DJA for each District., which citizens can replace using Right to recall DJA clauses given later
.
(6) Formation of First Grand Jury - Using the voter list, DJA will, in a public meeting, randomly select 50 citizens from the voter-list between age of 25 years and 55 years. From that, he can exclude any 20 after interview, so that finally there are 30 Grand Jurors. If DJA is appointed by CM or PM under clause-(4), then DJA may select up to 100 citizens from District / State / India and exclude 70.
.
(7) Formation of subsequent Grand Juries - In the first set of Grand Jurors, DJA will retire the first 10 Grand Jurors every 10 days. And after first month, term of each Grand Juror will be 3 months, and thus 10 Grand Jurors will retire every month and 10 more will be selected. For selection of 10 Grand Jurors, DJA will select 20 voters at random from voter list of District or State or India, as in clause above, and select any 10 after interview.
.
(8) Method to select voters at random -
.
(8.1) The DJA will not use any electronic device to select a number randomly. He will use the procedure detailed by CM or PM. If CM or PM have not specified the procedure, he will select as follow.
.
(8.2) Say DJA has to choose a number between 1 and a four digit number say ABCD. Then DJA will do 4 rounds of dice-throw for each digit. In a round if the digit he needs to select is between 0-5, then DJA will use only 1 dice and if the digit he needs to select is between 0-9, he will use 2 dices.
.
(8.3) The number selected will be 1 less than the number which comes in case of single-dice throw and 2 less in case of double-dice throw. If throw of dices exceeds the highest digit he needs, he will throw the dices again.
.
(8.3.1) Example - Suppose DJA needs to select a page in a book, which has 3693 pages. Then DJA will execute 4 rounds. In the 1st round he will use 1 dice as he needs to select a number between 0-3. If the dice shows 5 or 6, he will throw the dice again. If the dice show 3, the number selected is 3-1=2. And DJA will proceed to second round. In the second round, he needs to select a number between 0-6. So he will throw two dices. If the sum exceeds 8, he will throw the dices again. If the sum is suppose 6, the second digit selected is 6 - 2 = 4. Like that, suppose the dices in 4 rounds show 3, 5, 10 and 2. Then DJA will select digits as (3 - 1), (5 - 2), (10 - 2), (2 - 1) i.e. page number 2381. The DJA should use different citizens to throw dices. Suppose the voter-list has B books, the largest book has P pages and all pages have N entries. Then using above method or method described by CM, DJA will select 3 random numbers between (1 to B), (1 to P) and (1 to N). Now suppose selected book has less than that many pages or the selected page has fewer entries. Then he will again select a numbers between (1 to B), (1 to P) and (1 to N).
.
(8.3.2) Another method can be - DJA can prepare list of all voters, and select two large prime numbers at random. Say there are N voters. Then DJA can select two prime numbers between N/2 and 2N say n and m. And the voters selected can be (n mod N), (n + m) mod N, (n + 2m) mod N to (n + (k-1)*m) mod N, where k is number of persons to be selected.
.
(8.3.3) If Jury System is suspended in District, and DJA is appointed by CM or PM , then he may select voters from whole State or India. The age will be between 25 years and 55 years.
.
(8.4) The Grand Jurors will meet on every Saturday and Sunday. They may meet on more days if over 15 Grand Jurors approve. The number must be over 15, even when less than 30 Grand Jurors are present. The meetings, if happen, must start at 11 am and last till at least 5 pm. The Grand Juror will get Rs. 500 per day he attends plus travel expense. The CM , PM may change the compensation as per inflation or other conditions such as travel distance. The money will be give 60 days after his term ends.
.
(8.5) If a Grand Juror is absent in a meeting, he will not his daily payment. And may loose up to thrice his amount to be paid and maximum fine of 0.05% of his wealth and 1% of his annual income may be imposed. The individuals who are Grand Jurors 60 days later will decide the fine.
.
(9) If any person or company or District Prosecutor or any entity, has complaint against any other person or entity, and crime is covered under clause-2 or GN issued under clause-2, then he can write to some or all Grand Jurors or ask Grand Jurors to give hearing or he may upload the complain on website using (CV) clause. The complainer may specify the remedy he wishes. The remedy can be obtaining possession of a property or obtaining monetary compensation from the accused or imprisoning the accused for certain number of years/months or his execution. The remedy asked must be legal, as per the law interpreted by Jurors and Grand Jurors.
.
(10) If over 15 Grand Jurors, in a meeting, issue an invitation to a witness or complainer or accused, then that person may appear. The Grand Jurors may or need not invite the accused or complainer. They may allow lawyers and experts to speak or not to speak.
.
(11) If over 15 Grand Jurors declare that there is some merit in the complaint, then the DJA will call a Jury consisting of 15 to 1500 citizens, between the age of 25 years and 55 years, to examine the complaint. And if 15 or more Grand Jurors say that “case has no merit”, then the complain will be dismissed
.
(12) if majority Grand Jurors think that the complaint is absolutely frivolous then they may impose a fine of upto Rs 5000 per hour they spent in hearing and deliberation, within one hour minimum, and no more. Each Grand Juror will declare a fine, and the median fine will be imposed and executed by DJA. The Jurors will also decide how much of that fine should be given to wrongfully accused as compensation. The accused may file a separate case to recover additional compensation for damages such as loss of time, reputation and other damages he suffered.
.
(13) Deciding number of Jurors for a case - Each Grand Juror will propose the number of Jurors needed for the case, and DJA will take the median as the decision. In case number of Grand Jurors are even, then DJA will take the higher median. Each Juror will propose a number between 15 and 1500. The guideline for Grand Jurors are –
.
(13.1) if the accused has higher economic or social or political standing, then the number of Jurors may be higher.
.
(13.2) if the crime is serious, the number of Jurors should be higher. eg if crime is theft of say Rs 100000 or less, then number of Jurors may be 15. If amount is more, then it will be higher. And if the alleged crime is murder, then number of Jurors may be 50 or 100 or more.
.
(13.3) if the person is accused of multiple crimes in past, and Grand Jurors see merit in most complaints, then they may put higher number of Jurors. eg if a person appears as career criminal and has been accused for several beatings or murders in past, then number of Jurors against him can be 100 or even 1500.
.
(13.4) if amount involved is large, then number of Jurors may be more. Number should be 15 at least. And 1 more Juror for every Rs 1 crore may be added. The maximum can be 1500 Jurors.
.
(13.5) The decision of Grand Jurors on number of Jurors will be final.
.
(13.6) The Grand Jurors will decide whether the Jurors will be from District, State or India.
.
(14) Jury selection - The DJA will randomly select number of citizens equal to twice the number of Jurors required from the voter lists. Then DJA will then send them summons to appear. And of those who arrive, DJA will excuse the Jurors who are relatives or neighbors or colleagues of either party or their lawyers. And he will also excuse Jurors who have served in the District in past 10 years. Then DJA will select required number of Jurors at random, not using any interview. The decision on excusing will be taken by DJA and can be changed only by majority of Grand Jurors.
.
(14.1) DJA will inform each Jurors about academic scores of each Jurors, and their profession and wealth / income levels. The Jurors who are less informed and less skilled in logic / maths, may take advice from Jurors who are more skilled in logic / maths. The discretion of each Juror on which Juror’s advice he will take will be final.
.
(15) DJA will ask CDJ = Chief District Judge to appoint one or at most 3 Judges to advise the Jurors and administer the case. The Chief Judge's decision on number of judges will be final. DJA will appoint Trial Administrator , TA henceforth, to run the trial.
.
(16) The trial will go from 11am to 5pm. The trial will start only after all Jurors are present or over 75% of the Jurors selected (not just present) vote for hearing to start. In all clauses, the majority means majority of Jurors selected, not just present, and “x% of Jurors” means “x% of all Jurors”, not just Jurors present.
.
(17) TA, i.e. Trial administrator appointed by DJA, will allow the complainer to speak for 1 hour, during which no can interrupt. Then Judge will allow the accused to speak for 1 hour and 30 minutes during which no one can interrupt. Like this, the Judge will alternate the speakers. The lunch break will start between 1 pm and 2pm and will be for 1 hour. The case will go on like this on every day. The Jurors with majority vote can change the duration for which one may speak.
.
(18) The case will go for at least 2 days. On the 3rd day or later, if majority Jurors declare that they have heard enough, then the hearings will go on for 1 more day. If on the next day, majority Jurors declare that they would like to hear more arguments, then the case will go on till majority Jurors say that case should end.
.
(19) On the last day, after both parties have presented the case for 1 hour each, the Jurors will deliberate for at least 2 hours. If after 2 hours, majority Jurors say that they need no more deliberation, then TA will ask each Juror to publicly declare his verdict.
.
(20) In case of fine, each Juror will state the fine he thinks is appropriate, and must be less than the legal limit. If it is higher than legal limit, then TA must take it as legal limit. TA will arrange the fine amounts stated in decreasing order, i.e. highest first and lowest last. And TA will take the fine approved by over 2/3rd majority. eg if there are 100 Jurors, then 67th fine amount in decreasing order will be taken as the fine collectively imposed by the Jury. So if 34 out of 100 Jurors have declared zero fine, then fine will be zero.
.
(21) In case of prison sentence, TA will arrange the imprisonment lengths cited by Jurors in decreasing order i.e. highest first and lowest last. If sentence length is higher than legal limit then maximum sentence as stated in the Law will be taken. And TA will take prison sentence approved by over 2/3rd of Jurors as prison sentence collectively decided by the Jury. So if 1/3rd Jurors have declared zero months of prison, then and then only accused will be declared not guilty and set free. eg if there are 100 Jurors, then 67th imprisonment length in decreasing order will be taken as the imprisonment. So if 34 Jurors say zero months, then punishment will be zero months.
.
(22) In case punishment of execution, over 75% of all Jurors must approve execution. In such case, there will automatic second trial. The second Jury will only decide if the convict should be executed or not. In second Jury too, at least 75% of all Jurors must approve execution.
.
(23) In case of any other remedy asked, the Jurors will ask both parties to submit at most 5 proposals or less. The Jurors may take proposal from judges or TA or any person approved by Jurors. Each Juror will give marks from 0 to 100 to each proposal. Not giving any marks would mean 0. The proposal with highest total marks would be taken as proposal approved by the Jurors.
.
(23) DJA will give same compensation to Jurors as Grand Jurors
.
(24) Fining a Juror for being absent or late - in case a Juror or a party does not show up or shows up late, the Grand Jurors after 3 months will decide the fine, which can be upto 0.1% of his wealth, or 1% of his annual income. The Grand Jurors decision will be final.
.
(25) If over 50% of Jurors think that complain was frivolous, then each Juror may impose a fine of Rs 1000 per day per Juror and no more. Each Juror will disclose a fine, and median fine will be collected by DJA. But limit on fine will be 2% of the wealth of complainer or 10% of his annual income, whichever is higher. Of this, accused will get amount equal to number of days he spent multiplied by his daily income in past income tax return. If the accused wants more compensation, then he may file a separate complain.
.
(26) In complex cases, each party or TA may ask any technical / legal expert , who is willing to give information, to explains facts and law to the Jurors. DJA will decide his daily compensation, which will not exceed his daily income.
.
(27) DJA can create a Grand Jury at tahsil level and also run Jury Trial in tahsil courts, if that District has tahsil Courts. The Tahsil Court Grand Jury and Tahsil Court Jury will hear the cases that fall within them. DJA will appoint a TJA = Tahsil Jury administrator for Tahsil Courts. And operations will run in same way as described in above clauses.
.
(28) Appeal : Either party will have 30 days to appeal against the verdict in the State's High Court or the Supreme Court of India. The verdict of Higher Court will prevail over verdict of District Court.
.
(28.1) comment : This law makes no imperative on whether High Court or Supreme Court will use judge system or Jury System. If MPs or voters want Jury System in High Court or Supreme Court, then they may print a separate law.
.
(29) If citizens of district or state or India order District Principal Judge to cancel the verdict of District Court Jury using CV clauses at District / State or India level, DPCJ may cancel the verdict of Jury.
.
(30) The District Police Chief or policemen designated by him will execute the fine or imprisonments as written by the Judge and approved by the Jurors.
.
(31) In cases listed in clause-2 or GN issued under clause-2, no Govt employee will impose any fine or prison sentence without consent of over 2/3rd of Jurors of the Lower Courts, unless approved High Courts or Supreme Court or majority voters of District / State / India as decided by (CV) clauses. No Govt employee will imprison any citizen for more than 48 hours without approval of over 2/3rd of the Jurors or 50% of the Grand Jurors or majority voters of District / State / India using (CV) clauses
.
(32) The Jurors will decide the facts as well as intensions, and must also interpret the laws as well the Constitution, and natural justice. If Jurors think that the law is unjust or inapplicable, then the Jurors can give “not guilty” verdict even against the law.
.
(RTR) Right to Recall District Jury administrator
.
(RTR.1) If any voter of India above age of 30 years wishes to become DJA, then he can appear in person before any DC (DC = District Collector where he wishes to become DJA). DC would accept his candidacy for DJA. DJA will take filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election And DC will issue a serial number unique across India.
.
(RTR 2) Any voter can visit Talati office in person. pay Rs 3 fee, and approve at most five persons for DJA position. The Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on PM's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences
.
(RTR.3) If a the voter comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee
.
(RTR.4) If a candidate gets approval of over 35% of ALL voters in District, and 2% more than existing DJA, then CM may or need not expel the existing DJA and may or need not appoint the person with highest approval count as DJA. The decision of CM will be final
.
(RTR.5) If existing DJA has approval of less than 35% of ALL voters, then CM may replace him.
.
(RTR.6) PM may create a system whereby citizen can file\cancel approvals using SMS or mobile phone apps or via ATMs.
.
(RTR.7) comment : Later, PM may create a system where voter can give score of (-100 to 100) to any candidate. If a voter doesn’t give any score, it will be assumed as zero. The voter will have choice --- he can give score of simply approve / not-approve, and in that case not-approving will mean score of zero and approving will mean score of 100. If a candidate has positive score from over 33% o ALL voters, then negative scores will be ignored. The candidate with highest score, and who has over 51% of highest possible score may be appointed as DJA
.
(CV) Citizens’ Voice:
.
(CV.1) If any citizen residing anywhere in India wants any change in this law or has any complaint under this law, then he may submit an affidavit to DC’s designated clerk and clerk will scan the affidavit on put on PM’s website along with photo and details of the citizen. The clerk will charge Rs 30 per page. The clerk will issue serial number for the affidavit.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen wants to register his YES / NO on the affidavit submitted in above clause, then he can go to Talati’s office and provide the serial number of the affidavit and his YES/NO on a form, and Patwari will enter his YES/NO for a fee for Rs 3 . The YES/NO shall come on PM’s website along with citizen’s voter-id, name, date etc The YES / NO count will not be binding on PM or any officer etc. PM may enable citizen file YES / NO using SMS or APP.

भाग -1: नागरिकों के लिए सामान्य निर्देश
----------------------------------------------

(1) टिप्पणी : कानून का सारांश
.
(1.1) टिप्पणी : यह कानून हत्या, अपहरण, बलात्कार, मार-पिटाई, धमकी और अन्य सभी ऐसे अपराधों जिनमे शारीरिक बल या शारीरिक हिंसा का प्रयोग किया गया है, के लिए मजिस्ट्रेट अदालतों, जिला अदालतों और सेशन अदालतों में जूरी सिस्टम की स्थापना करता है |

(RTR.4) यदि किसी नए उम्मीदवार को प्राप्त अनुमोदनों की संख्या अमुक जिले में दर्ज मतदाताओ की कुल संख्या के 35% (सभी दर्ज मतदाताओ की कुल संख्या के 35%) से अधिक हो, तथा उसे मिले अनुमोदनों की संख्या पदासीन जिला जूरी प्रशासक को मिले अनुमोदनों की संख्या से भी 2% अधिक हो तो मुख्यमंत्री पदासीन जिला जूरी प्रशासक को पद से हटा सकते है और ऊपर दी गयी शर्ते पूरी करने वाले नए उम्मीदवार को जिला जूरी प्रशासक नियुक्त कर सकते है, या उन्हें ऐसा करने की ज़रूरत नहीं है। इस संबध में मुख्यमंत्री का फैसला अंतिम होगा।
.
(RTR.5) यदि पदासीन जूरी प्रशासक के अनुमोदनो की संख्या जिले में दर्ज कुल मतदाताओ की संख्या के 35% से कम हो जाती है तो मुख्यमंत्री उसे बदल सकते है।
.
(RTR.6) प्रधानमंत्री ऐसा सिस्टम बना सकते है जिससे नागरिक अपने पंजीकृत मोबाइल से SMS भेजकर या इन्टरनेट द्वारा या एटीएम द्वारा या मोबाइल फ़ोन एप्प द्वारा अपने अनुमोदन दर्ज करवा सके।
.
(RTR.7) टिपण्णी : बाद में प्रधानमंत्री ऐसा सिस्टम बना सकते है जिससे नागरिक किसी उम्मीदवार को अंक ( -100 से 100 के बीच ) दे सके। यदि मतदाता द्वारा सामान्य अनुमोदन किया जाता है तथा कोई अंक नहीं दिए जाते है तो इस अनुमोदन को 100 अंको के समकक्ष माना जाएगा, तथा यदि कोई मतदाता अपना अनुमोदन दर्ज नहीं करता है तो इसे शून्य अंक माना जाएगा। इस तरह मतदाता के पास दो विकल्प होंगे। या तो वह सामान्य अनुमोदन कर सकता है या अनुमोदन नहीं कर सकता है, अथवा वह उम्मीदवार को अंक दे सकता है। किन्तु यदि मतदाता उम्मीदवार को अंक देता है तो मतदाता द्वारा दिए गए अंक ही मान्य होंगे। यदि किसी उम्मीदवार को जिले में दर्ज कुल मतदाताओ के 33% से अधिक अंक प्राप्त हो जाते है तो अमुक उम्मीदवार के नकारात्मक अंको को खारिज कर दिया जाएगा। प्राप्त किये जाने वाले सबसे अधिक संभाव्य अंको के 51% तथा सबसे अधिक अंक प्राप्त करने वाले उम्मीदवार को मुख्यमंत्री जिला जूरी प्रशासक के पद पर नियुक्त कर सकते है।
.

(CV.1 ) [ जिला कलेक्टर या उसके द्वारा नियुक्त कर्मचारी के लिए निर्देश ]

यदि देश के किसी भी क्षेत्र में निवास करने वाला कोई भी नागरिक मतदाता इस विधेयक के किसी अनुच्छेद में बदलाव चाहता हो अथवा वह इस विधेयक से सम्बन्धित कोई शिकायत दर्ज करना चाहता हो तो ऐसा नागरिक मतदाता जिला कलेक्टर के कार्यालय में उपस्थित होकर एक शपथपत्र प्रस्तुत कर सकता है। जिला कलेक्टर या उसका क्लर्क इस शपथपत्र को 30 रूपए प्रति पृष्ठ का शुल्क लेकर दर्ज करेगा तथा एक सीरियल नंबर जारी करके इस शपथपत्र को स्कैन करके अमुक मतदाता के छाया चित्र (फोटो) एवं अन्य विवरण के साथ प्रधानमंत्री की वेबसाईट पर रखेगा।

(CV.2 ) [ तलाटी अथवा पटवारी के लिए निर्देश ]
