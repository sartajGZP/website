Why make 50 IMPORTANT albums each containing 50-100 IMPORTANT posts and also make 10 "UNIMPORTANT" albums which may have practically unlimited number of "UNIMPORTANT" posts? ---- so that you can give RELEVANT information, to NEW activists you may meet AFTER 1-2 years from today, from YOUR wall !!!
.
IOW, classifying posts into albums is also for NEW activists you will meet 1 year later, less for people you already know today !!!
.
---------
.
We activists are writing/sharing two types of posts ---
.
(a) some posts deal with current issues and these posts may become less relevant after a few weeks or few months
.
(b) some posts are dealing with core issues and proposals, and they will be relevant for months and may be years to come.
.
(c) some posts are relevant for long term, but they get SUBSUMED by other posts and so after subsumption, they are now irrelevent. So if you see that or locate that subsumed post, and delete that post, then no damage is done to your cause and reduce irrelevant post count.
.
(d) And LONG TEM RELEVANCE is also subjective. Say you are working on 50 causes C1 to C50 and have posted / copies / shared 100 posts for each cause. Say post P1 to P100 for cause-1. Say P101 to P200 for cause-2 and so on. Say you meet a new activist who is interested in cause-1 but NOT in cause-2. Then posts P1 to P100 are relevant to him, and post P101 to P200 are NOT relvant to him.
.
---
.
Now say you have written 10 posts a week, copied 10 posts and shared some 20 posts a week, totaling 40 posts a week, or say 2000 posts a year and say 1000 posts in 5 years. Out of these 10000 posts, say some 2000 posts deal with core issues and laws you are campaigning for, The rest were with current issues or got subsumed by other posts. Say these 2000 posts were on 20 causes C1 to C20. Say P1 to P100 were for cause-1, say P101 to P200 were for cause-2 and so on. Say 5 years have passed now.
.
Now say you meet a 10 new activists say A1 to A10. A1 is interested only in cause-1 and not in other causes. And A2 is interested in cause-2 only and not other causes.
.
Now if you had never classified posts into albums, then you cant ask activist A1 to A10 to read posts from your wall, because they cant read all 10000 posts. So only option for you is to explain 1000s of posts to him on phone.
.
But say you had posted 2000 long term posts into 20 important albums by topics and posted other less relevant 8000 posts into 10-20 less important albums. Then when five years later, when you meet activist A1, you can simply request him to read 100 posts in album meant for cause C1. And to A2, you can ask him to read posts in album for cause C2.and so on.
.
IOW, if you post posts into albums categoried by causes and issues, then when you meet a NEW activist months or years later, you can give all posts related to HIS cause in one go !! That will save your time and his time too
.
Please note that movement's speed depends on how in LEAST possible time, one more informed activist can communicate to other NEW less informed activists. And so classifying posts into album improves efficiency,. And we at Right to recall do NOT have funds. So we better improve our efficiency.
.
So please make 50 albums, based on causes and major issues. And pls put posts in those albums as applicable. And make some 5 - 10 general albums and make one album as "Unclassified posts". And put less relevent posts in those album or "unclassified posts" album. That way, your ability to communicate to activists after 2-3 years will be MUCH higher
