2-april-2018 bandh : Reasons behind babdh and riots of march-2002 and reasons behind bandh call and disturbance of 2-april-2018 are SAME ---- rampant corruption, rampant nepotism and rampant nexuses in india's supreme judges, high judges and lower judges
.
Why did large number of Hindus across Gujarat went on rioting when 60 Hindus were burnt alive in Godhara on 27-feb-2002? The MAIN reason was --- they KNEW that judges would take bribes and NOT punish the guilty and so they decided to do what they could. If they were sure that judges would punish guilty, then riots' inensity would have been much less. But since Hindus KNEW that judges would take bribes and NOT punish guility, they supported Gujarat Bandh call given by VHP on 28-feb-2002;. And then riots started.
.
Same is the core reason why lakhs and crores of Dalits came out for demonstration on 2-april-2018 They KNEW that corrupt, nepotism and nexused judges will NEVER run dalit atrocity cases properly and so diluting the law will only increase atrocities.
.
Here is one example of how corrupt / nexused our judges are ---
.
----
.
. (see Indian express 3-apr-2018 , link in comment )

In November 1995, while dismissing Bhanwari Devi’s plea for justice, the district sessions judge remarked that the upper-caste men (the five accused included a Brahmin and the rest Gujjars) could not have raped a Dalit woman at the cost of defiling their caste purity. ....
.
----------

The solution I had proposed in march-2002 was same as solution I propose today -- (a) that one should STOP giving BHASHANS on damage bands cause and imo one should also stop his cacophony that supreme judges are institutions and are NOT corrupt, not nepotic and not nexused. (b) and one should work to print to force MPs to print laws that would reduce corruption, nepotism and nexuses in courts
.
The primary cause why MPs were forced to bring one sides law like dalit atrocity act was corruption/nepotism/nexuses of judges. Each time a well off upper caste would harass a Dalit, the well off would manage to influence judges thru nefarious means and judges will cause endless delay and junk the case. And due to this, fear of CONVERSION of Dalits was rising. So Devi Indira Amma added some provisions in IPC which made crimes against Dalits more serious. And that proved to be insufficient and so later in 1989 Rajiv Gandhiji had to make a much stronger law. And so some false cases also started.
.
Now instead of solving the problem of corruption , nepotism and nexuses in judges, Sri Modiji and product of nepotism, Honorable Justice U U Lalit decided to dilute the law.
.
RSS-workers and right wingers too insist that one should ONLY focus on one sidedness of in the said law, and one MUST assume that no supreme judge, no high judge, no lower court judge is corrupt , nepotic and nexused, and all of them are showing utmost fairness in dealing with cases involving Dalit atrocities !! SInce judges are fair and FAST, there is no need for any one sided law.
.
There are plenty of one sided laws that RSS-workers and right wingers support. The best example is GST law, where if buyer has paid GST to seller, and sellr doesnt deposit the collected GST in Govt, then Govt will charge buyer again !! (It applies only when buyer is normal registered dealer, NOT when buyer is unregistered or composite). The law is heavily one sided i.e. Govt-sided because even if buyer has paid GST once to seller, as dictated by GST law itself, due to sellers; crime or fault, buyer will have to suffer. All RSS-workers and right-wingers has OPENLY supported this clause-16.2.c of CGST Act. But they have immense pain when they see one sidedness in Dalit Atrocity laws.
.
Now there is indeed some damage due to false cases. But damage isnt even 1% or even 0.01% of the damage that corruption / nepotism / nexuses of judges cause on Dalits. But you will never see an RSS-worker or right wingers or Modiji himself, even working to reduce corruption / nepotism . nexuses of judges,. But they all celebrated U U Lalit's "landmark" judgement. And yes, the nepotic judge Deepak Mishra was the brain behind it --- he is the one who decided to give the case to U U Lalit. These judges have done NOTHING to increase speed of cases and reduce corruption in judges in general. But sure they acted very fast to give reliefs to accused.
.
Dilution of law was NOT accompanied by improvement of courts,. So obviously, cases of atrocities against Dalits will only increase.

The Dalits first asked Sri Modiji to immediately bring Ordiance to cancel the judgment. Modiji gave no response, which same as saying that he refused. Next, Modiji did NOT even ask Govt lawyer to file case before bigger bench to cancel this 2 corrupt/nepotism judges' judgment. (That was done AFTER Dalits gave call for Bharat Bandh). And nothing was done to reverse the judgment till 1-april-2018 !! And meanwhile, RSS-workers and right wingers across India started distributing sweets and celebrating the judgment, calling it as "most egalitarian landmark judgment in history of India" !!!
.
Essentially, Modiji, judges, right wingers and RSS-workers were thinking that Bharat Bandh will fail. BUT DUE TO SOCIAL MEDIA AND WHATSAPP, Dalits were able to organize within a short span. Finallly, Modiji came to know via IB / RAW etc about level of preparation. So he asked Govt lawyer to file case against judgment before larger bench, But nothing was done to get an early hearing , again hoping that 2-april-2018 bandh will be a dud.

Now this is first time there has been widespread violence in Dalit aandolan. Till date, Dalits andolans have been peaceful. But now there is VALID feeling that if aandolan is 100% peaceful, then paidmediamen will give zero coverage and judges, leaders and party workers will simply laugh at the peaceful demonstrators. The paidmedianmen want money or violence, or else there will be no coverage. Thats their rule. And judges, leaders and party workers too listen only when there is a fear of loss of law order, otherwise they only mock at demonstrators calling them weak. And so now Dalit youth has decided to go on a warpath
,
=====
.
So finally, whats the solution from this mess?
.
Solution I propose for this mess is to fix courts. "Punish the criminals RAPIDLY and cause minimal harassment to innocents accused" and thats ALL that is needed to end Dalit atrocities, domestic violence, dowry violence, defunct tax laws and so on. How can that be done? Fix courts so that criminal cases take 1-2 months and judgments are not getting sold by bribes, or nepotism or nexuses. And that should be done BEFORE before complaining against onesidedness in laws.
.
And how can we fix courts?

Courts work on WRITTEN procedures codes and laws. So I would request you ALL to ask congress/rss/aap workers you know to give you the DRAFTS that THEY have proposed to fix courts across India. .
.
The drafts I propose are Jury System and Right to recall judges, and narcotest in public in Dalit atrocity cases. For drafts and what can YOU do to bring these drafts in India --- pls click my cover picture and search for words "Jury" and "judge". And pls click index link in cover page descripion and search for same two words.
.
And here, pls note that all RSS-workers have called Jury law and Right to recall judge law as LEFTIST proposals. Are these proposals leftist? Well, may be if they are saying so.
..
IMO, these laws will fix our courts and with that need for one sided laws will decrease. Bhashans against bandh are useless, when judges, apex leaders and party workers love to mock at peaceful demonstrators. So pls work to fix laws. And pls dont waste time with congress/rss/aap workers.
