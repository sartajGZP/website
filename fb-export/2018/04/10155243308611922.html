NarcoTest in Public Act – Narcotest in Murder and serious violence cases
.
comment : Once this act is passed, the judges or Jurors will be able to ask accused or complainers to go on narcotest, with their approval. And the test will be optional for them. But in cases involving extreme violence, the judge or Jurors may decide to force accused but not others to take the test. The judge or Jurors will decide whether narcotest will be in public or confidential i.e. done before court in camera. When done confidentially, the portion disclosing name of victim will be removed and rest of the audio/video will be made public. The questions will be decided by judge or Jurors, with help of policemen and experts. The answers given in narcotest will NOT be taken as evidence.
.

(1) The judge or Jurors can ask accused, complainer, witness to undergo (a) polygraph test or (b) brain-mapping test or (c) narcotest or more than one of these 3 tests or all of these 3 tests in any order. If it is Jury, then all decisions will be taken only if over 75% of ALL Jurors agree. The test will be optional, except in cases of (2)
.
(2) In cases of extreme violence, the judge or Jurors may make one or more of these tests compulsory on the accused without his permission, but not on witnesses or complainer.
.
(3) The judges or Jurors will decide whether the test (i.e. lie detection or brain mapping or narcotest) will be conducted in public or confidentially. If done confidentially, then after test, the portions disclosing names of female victims will be removed and rest of audio/video will be put in public.
.
(4) The judge or Jurors will NOT ask him irrelevant questions, not connected with any crime committed. The judge or Jurors will decide the question using help from policemen and experts.
.
(5) If Jury System is used, then Jurors will be divided into two groups. One group will write questions, and second group will accept or reject question with majority vote
.
(6) The judge or Jurors will NOT use the statements as evidences. But policemen can use the statements to discover more evidences.
.
(7) The test will be conducted by a doctor chosen by judge or Jurors , from a list of approved qualified doctors. The list will be prepared by CM.
.
(8) comment : A nonbinding guideline for judge or Jurors is – (a) before test, they can listen to witnesses, complainer, accused, see material evidences and decide if test needs to be taken (b) if they decide to take test, then first they should take polygraph test, then brain mapping and then narcotest (c) also, judge or Juror may decide to first take narcotest confidentially, then again publicly, or may decide to take just one narcotest.
.
(9) Appeal : The decision of judge or Jurors in a court can be cancelled by judge or Jurors in a higher court.
.
(10) PM thru Gazette Notification can suspend this act for a group of district or group of state for 4 years.
.
