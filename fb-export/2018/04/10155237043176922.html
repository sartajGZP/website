#ReduceAdulteration - proposed law-draft to reduce food adulteration. The proposed law-draft at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155236998786922 will, IN MY OPINION, reduce food adulteration.
.
The law-draft be printed by State CMs in Gazette,. No permission of Assembly or PM is needed. PM can print this as non-binding directive to states and also make it binding on Union Territories.
.
The law creates a procedure of Right to Recall over DFSI i.e. District Food Safety Officer and Jury Trial over those who are suspected to be selling adultered food.
.
Adulteration can be of several types - (1) selling outright fake food like milk made up of urea (2) selling stale food (3) selling food with far more pesticide , ripener etc chemicals than permitted limited (4) selling brand counterfeit etc etc. This law covers all such cases. A separate law that I will propose later will cover adulteration in medicine. This law covers only food adulteration.
