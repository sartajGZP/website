How privatization monopoly reduces efficiency of NATIONAL economy
.
Say there is railway station. Say there are slots for 100 shops and say all have equal value to vendors i.e. for any vendor, he will pay same rent to any slot.
.
(a) Station master decided to keep rent as Rs 10000 per month. There are 110 applicants.
.
(b) And so he decided to raise rent to Rs 13000 per month. And he found that there were only 90 applicants.
Total rent = 90 * Rs 13000 = Rs 11,70,000 per month
.
(c) And so he decided to raise rent to Rs 12000 per month. And he found that there were only 95 applicants.
Total rent = 95 * Rs 12000 = Rs 11,40,000
.
(d) And so he decided to raise rent to Rs 11000 per month. And he found that there were exactly 100 applicants.
Total rent = Rs 11,00,000 per month
.
What is best option for owner? (b)
.
What is best option for society? (d) !!!
.
Say rent left after expenses of station is to be divided equally amongst all citizens. Even then, (d) is best option for society, because it maximizes employment , competition, total output etc.
.
So any private company will opt for (b) and not (d).
.
This is why you see expensive coffee at airport and yet large amount of vacant space. The private company which maintains airport deliberately keeps space unallocated so that number of slots for shops are less. And so higher rent can be collected !! To society, that is lose lose situation. More and more customers will bring food from home adding to inefficiency, And employment at airport will be below maximal.
.
But if company is govt owned, and citizens have RTR/Jury over apex/staff, then citizens can force thet apex/staff to take option (d).
.
Now here, monopoly like Railway Station or Red Fort or Taj Mahal are NOT created by private companies. In fact, except patents, there is no way private company can have a long lasting monopoly because employees of the that company themselves will quit and soon become new suppliers !!
.
Monopolies like Taj Mahal or Red Forts are due to historical reasons or rather historical wrongs.Monopolies like Railway Station are created by Govt's power to confiscate or acquire land to build railways. Unlike companies like Microsoft, who once had monopoly in Operating System, Railway Stations or Taj Mahal are NOT any private individuals' own hard work or risk.
