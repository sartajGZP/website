Importance of "99% non-interactive communication thru posts in FB albums" in activism
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
.
Imo, most important part in activism --- communication WITHOUT phonecalls, chat, comments, meetings etc i.e. communication thru "99% NON-INTERACTIVE" means such as FB small albums, printing your own pamphlets/books, whatsapp broadcast groups, newspaper advt etc
.
Most important reason imo is ---- because out of every 100 persons whom you explain law-drafts to, ONLY ONE will take it forward !!!
.
There are more gyaan pipasu passivists and 80G-activists than non-80G-activists in this world.
.
===========
.
For purpose of activism - I do NOT divide communication into verbal and written. But I divide into interactive, semi-interactive and largely non-interactive. And also divide as recorded interaction and non-recorded interaction.
.
(a) interactive --- two or more persons are communicating at the SAME time eg phonecall, physical meetings, interactive chats etc
.
(b) semi-interactive --- a person has sent a message, and other will reply when he is free and communication goes on. like 2-person threads we use
.
(c) largely non-interactive --- say you have posted 5000 posts in 50 albums, 100 post per album. And also uploaded PDFs etc. And you ask people you meet to read them and then answer questions via two person threads chat or sometimes on phone. So here 99% communication is non-interactive and meant for all. Only a small time is given in SPECIFIC questions asked by one person
.
IMO, (c) is most optimal for activism. If you are in sales business, then (a) may be most optimal. But not for activism.
.
WHY?
.
(A) out of 100 persons who explain law-drafts to and answer questions, ONLY ONE will take it forward. And some may work less than you can, and some may work more. But in activism, many are gyaan pipasu (knowledge hungry) passivists, many are already tied with some group, many want YOU to work on their cause and not other way round and so on. So those willing to work and those whom you may be able to convince to work on RTR drafts will be very small % of people whom you will be talking to. May be, only about 1%
.
(A1) This is unlike sales, where you know that person does want to buy and isnt just gyaan pipasu. And he doesnt want to sell, but he wants to buy. He may noy buy from you and buy from someone else. but chances are much higher than 1% than he may buy from you.
.
(B) Second, how many hours do you have in a day? How many hours in a week? And how many hours could you spare for activism in past 1 year? And how many can you spare in this and coming years? As life progresses, more issues may keep coming.
.
(C) now add the problem of DOUBLE COINCIDENCE OF FREE TIME. i.e. say you want to hold a meeting and you are free on say Sunday between say 3pm and 6pm. Then of those willing to spare 3 hours a week, how many will be able to spare EXACT same those three hours , sunday 3pm to 6pm? And, add time to commute.
.
(D) Lastly, we need both supporters and forwarders. A forwarder will have tendency to read. So if you ask people to "read first, ask question later", then you may be turning away many supporters, but not many forwarders.
.
====
.
So now you can compare and contrast phonecalls vs "putting 5000 posts in 50 albums, 100 posts each" methods. .
.
If you FIRST spend hours and hours in putting "5000 posts in 50 albums, 100 posts per albums, and each album having 'table of contents', and posts are linked like pages in wikipedia", then a person who doesnt mind reading will get huge amount of information, And later, you can answer specific questions thru 2 person thread or phonecalls.
.
Now the posts you have put can be read by 10s or may be 100s or even 1000s of activists. So this ONE TIME COST that you put in putting all 5000 posts will enable you to give STRUCTURED information to 100s of activists. You can never achieve this efficiency using phonecalls or even chats or even 2 person threads.
.
Thats because thru FB albums, you can implement "one writes, different hundred people read at DIFFERENT times , each one reading at his own time". That imo is the most effective way to spread information in YOUR LIMITED TIME.
