self financed medical colleges are causing far far more damage to "merit" that caste based reservation.
.
For an MBBS seat, you need just 5% in physics, 20% in biology
.
Before NEET was mandatory, the cut-offs were 50% marks for the general category, and 40% for the reserved
.
From 2016, these were changed to 50th and 40th percentile, respectively
.
This opened the doors to candidates with just 18-20% marks in the NEET aggregate
.
