How to put information on FB wall - presuming that hundreds less than 1% will visit wall once a day, some 10% will visit once a week, some 20% will visit once a month and rest will visit once a year?
.
And each visitor will spend say 10 minutes on your wall and spend more time ONLY if he sees something that meets his cause.
.
So if you have written a post related to your cause, then it should be placed so that he can see in 10 minutes or less !!
.
And none will get notifications of what you post. Because most people become immune to notifications as it has too much noise. And so if you a write a post, less than 10 persons will read within next few hours !!!
.
Most people often write posts on FB with a false assumption that everyone will read it right after reading and so there is no need for them to ORGANIZE the links to posts for someone who is visiting the wall a week or month or year later,
.
FB doesnt offer any auto indexing and search on walls' post is poor. That means that if you want 'once a week" or "once a month" or "once a year" visitor to see posts relevant to HIS cause, then YOU better make tables of contents i.e. statuses having headings of other posts and links to other statuses . And your posts better have a heading. And so on.
.
You can decide how you wish arrange your contents. But pls keep one thing in mind all even if you have written a post that is useful to X, X will NOT see it in next hour or even next week. He may come to wall after week or month. and if post is "reachable" and "locatable" withoin 5 minutes to 10 minutes, then only he will be able to see it
.
So pls decide how you want to arrange the links in your profile, and what should be STARTING STATUS for visitor, and how do would you tell the visitor where that starting post is located?
