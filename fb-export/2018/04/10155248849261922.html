Making albums inside albums in FB -- make groups and put links to groups inside post inside album
.
i.e.
wall profile -> posts + albums
albums -> posts + links to groups
groups - > posts
.
One way to organize files is to make folders and put files in folders. And sometimes, you need to make folders within a folder, and put files in those folders. One often needs 3-4 level of nesting to organize all files.
.
Same way, we recallists have 1000s of posts on 100-200 issues. And so I have suggested tat we all make 50-100 IMPORTANT albums and put IMPORTANT posts 5000 posts in those albums, about 50 to 100 posts per album. And make 20-30 unimportant albums, and put all 100s of unimportant posts in those albums
.
But you often need folders inside folder. But facebook doesnt allow albums inside albums. How can nesting be achieved?
.
Solution I propose is to create album for TOPICS and create GROUPS for subtopics. And place links to groups inside album. And put posts inside the group.
.
IOW, group allow us to INDIRECTLY create levels of nesting on FB and organize our posts
