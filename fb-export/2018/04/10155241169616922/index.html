Organizing important posts in FB albums and important albums' headings
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
,
Dear recallists,
.
Pls make following albums on your FB profile. I have also given what should be the approx number of posts in the album. And what should that album contain
.
The albums with number (A---) are compulsory. Those with number (B---)
.
And at the end, pls see naming convention.
.
chap-1: Compulsory Albums
----------------------------------

Pls keep these albums with exact same name. You can put the posts as you wish. But the names and numbers should be exactly as follows.
.
(A001) List of my Facebook photo albums ---- less than 10 posts.
.
(A002) My core political beliefs (top 100) --- less than 200 posts
(A003) My political beliefs (after 100) --- less than 400 posts
.
(A004) Whom did I vote for in past" --- less than 50 posts
(A005) Whom do I wish to vote for in future" --- less than 50 posts
.
(A006) Campaign methods and activism" --- less than 300 posts
.
(A007) Steps YOU activist can take on FB, twitter to publicize law-drafts YOU support" --- less than 100 posts
.
(A008) Steps YOU voter can take on FB, twitter to publicize law-drafts YOU support" --- less than 100 posts
.
(A009) Important maths/logic exercises to understand RTR etc
.
(A011) Law drafts I support (top 50) -- less than 300 posts
(A012) Law drafts I support (51 to 200) -- less than 300 posts
(A013) Law drafts I support (after 201) --- no limit
.
(A016) Proofs that I supported law drafts on NewIndia dot in (top 50)
(A017) Proofs that I supported law drafts on NewIndia dot in (51 to 200)
(A018) Proofs that I supported law drafts on NewIndia dot in (after 201)
(A019) Proofs I supported law drafts on NewIndia dot in (all jpg)
(in all above 5 albums, number of posts should not be more than 5-8 posts per law-draft you support)
.
(A021) Proofs that I supported law drafts on Twitter (top 50)
(A022) Proofs that I supported law drafts on Twitter (51 to 200)
(A023) Proofs that I supported law drafts on Twitter (after 201)
(in all above 5 albums, number of posts should not be more than 2-3 posts per law-draft you support)
.
(A026) Proofs that I sent postcard for law drafts I support (top 50)
(A027) Proofs that I sent postcard for law drafts I support (51 to 200)
(A028) Proofs that I sent postcard for law drafts I support (after 201)
(in all above 5 albums, number of posts should not be more than 1-2 posts per law-draft you support)
.
(A031) Elections I have contested so far : 2 - 5 posts per election you contested
(A032) Elections I may contest in future : 2 - 5 posts per election you will contest
(A033) Election campaigning activities : campaigning you have done for others
(A034) Newspaper advt I have given to publicize RTR etc

(A041) Election contributions I have taken
(A042) Newspaper advt contributions I have taken
(A043) Other contributions I have taken
(A044) Election contributions I have given
(A045) Newspaper advt contributions I have given
(A046) Other contributions I have taken
.
(A061) Important videos
(A062) Important PDFs
.

---------------------------------------

Following are the norms I have proposed in naming FB albums so that album names are constent across all recallists
.
Those who wish to follow my naming system, should take the names I given which begin with A such as
.
(A001) List of my Facebook photo albums
