Many debit card frauds happen because international transactions dont use OTP over SMS over mobile phone.
.
This can be changed --- the banks can implement software that will put OTP over international transactions as well, unless a person specifically asks to make it OTP-less. OTP-less transactions may be needed when person is out of India and not in position to get OTP via SMS. But as long as person is in India, he can live have OPT-only transactions on his debit card. The choice and risk can be left to debit card owner.
.
But banks are not willing to bear this "cost" of implementing this software !!!! So so customers are losing money left right and center !!!
.
We have a sick FinMin, who cant force banks to make OTP-less transactions optional for end user !!!
.
What is worse is that Ministers have issued guidelines that local police stations will NOT take complain, and so person has to go from one head office to another to another to register his complaints. So most people give up !! And so number of complaints are not even 1% of frauds !!!
