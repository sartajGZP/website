One possible way to improve internet speed in your mobile/dongle --- change and upgrade SIM card !!

Dear all vodaphone users in ahmedabad --- if you are facing net problems, pls change SIM card. And this advice may apply to many many users of other cellphone companies too across India !!!
.
===
.
My net connection in Vodaphone dongle/phone was terrible and after 23-mar-2018, it went terrible. Just to test, I kept dongle on for whole day, and it didnt download even 500 MB video over bitorrent (which normally takes 2 hrs only). And while using FB, I would get "no internet" every minute !!! The connection was so bad that I had decided to change company. I called their 198 three times, registered complains and thrice I got phone calls, I explained the problem, and then thrice I got SMS that complain had been fixed. But problem was same.
.
Finally, I went to their service center on saturday 7-april-2018 to personally register complain and guy there requested me to change SIM and give a try. He said that new SIM is upgrade may work.

I got both SIMs changed and within 1 hour problem was 100% fixed !!!
.
After googling I found one possible reason (I am still not sure) that some frequencies are hardcoded in SIMs and old SIMs use frequecies when quota was 5 GB for Rs 700. Now quota is 20 GB for Rs 400 and unlimited calling and so usage in that band has increased. And old SIMs dont use new frequency bands. And so there is crowding in old SIMs and so the net problem !!
.
Sadly, even their own tech support didnt know that changing SIM can work. And the person whose job was only to note down complains knew the solution !!!
.
And this problem may be there across India. So if you are facing net problem in mobile or dongle,. see if upgrading SIM helps.
