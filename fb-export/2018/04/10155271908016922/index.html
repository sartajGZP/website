steps I suggest you to take to increase POSSIBILITY that Right to Recall over Food Safety Officer law may come in India
-----------------------------------------------------------
.
List of chapters
.
(1) introduction
(2) primary steps for voters
(3) campaign steps for voters
(4) steps for activists
.
-----------
chap-1 : Introduction
------------
.
This post is for those voters/activists who wish to give some hours their life in bringing some laws to REDUCE FOOD ADULTERATION in India.
.
In this post, I have put steps that 10 crore net-enabled voters and 80 crore net-less voters can take. And imo, their taking these steps will increase the chances that “Right to Recall over Food Safety Officer” law and “Jury System in Food Safety cases” law will come in India.
.
Food adulteration is one of the major problems in India --- not as serious as poverty , rising domination of usuk-elitemen, weakening manufacturing, weakening weapon manufacturing etc. But still imo, food adulteration is surely in top 20 problems of India.
,
Imo, food adulteration can be reduced by Right to Recall over FSO i.e. Food Safety Officer and Jury System over Food Safety Staff, and Jury System over those accused of adulteration.
.
SoMoKe ( = SoGa/NaMo/ArKe) and ALL CoRaAp ( = congress/rss/aap) workers have opposed RTR over FSO and JurySys over FSO staff and those accused of food adulteration. As per them they have FAR superior solutions to this problem.
.
So I request all voters who have faith in SoMoKe or congress/rss/aap workers to obtain law-drafts SoMoKe have proposed and also see what campaign steps they suggest
.
This album is for THOSE who oppose SoMoKe’s solutions and instead wish to bring RTR-FSO law and “JurySys in adulteration cases” law.
.
Now pls note --- the steps I suggest imo only increase POSSIBILITY that RTR-FSO etc laws will get printed. Pls note that I am NOT giving any "100% guarantee". There are leaders like SoMoKe and there are workers like congress/rss/aap workers who give 100% guarantee of everything, be Rs 15 lakh per family or reducing corruption by useless Janlokpal law or whatever. I am NOT giving any such 100% guarantee.
.
I will give an analogy. Is there any doctor in world who would give 100% guarantee of curing a cancer patient. NO. Most doctors would only say "if you take these medicines, then possibility that patient will get cured increases". No honest doctor will give 100% guarantee except may be in small diseases like cough and cold. I dismiss guarantee giving SoMoKe and congress/rss/aap workers as dishonest people. I am only giving steps, which imo, INCREASES possibilities that this law will get printed. Nothing imo gives 100% guarantee. Those who want "100% guarantee" and want to hear statements like "if such and such steps are taken, then RtrFso law will come with 100% guarantee" should imo look elsewhere.
.
I will be putting more information on this food adulteration and other issues on my FB profile and my youtube channel. So pls follow
(a) my FB album on RtrFso etc at https://fb.com/mehtarahulc/media_set?set=a.10155269662571922
(b) my youtube channel at https://youtube.com/RightToRecallGroup
.
-------------
chap-2 : List of PRIMARY steps I suggest to voter, and reasons behind steps
-------------
.
To bring a law, imo, voters/activists need (a) written law-draft (b) campaign steps to get that written draft printed in law-book aka Gazette.
.
So following are three PRIMARY steps I suggest all voters/activists of India to take to decide law-drafts. The campaign steps are given in next chapter
.
Pls read this post twice. Please read REASONS in second reading, and NOT in first reading.
.
(step - P.1) Pls ask congress/rss/aap workers via twitter or FB public post to give copy of written law-draft etc they propose to reduce food adulteration problem. And pls also ask them what steps 90 crore voters of India should take to bring these laws and policies in India. Pls ask them to put law-draft pdf on newindia dot in, and ask them put campaign steps on their FB profile.
.
Reason : Congress/rss/aap workers say that they have BETTER law-drafts and better action steps than what I have proposed !! So imo, you must ask them to upload the DRAFT on newindia dot in. And also ask them to upload the steps etc on their FB profiles. So I request you ask congress/rss/aap activists to give the law-drafts etc to you.
.
(step - P.2) To see the draft I have proposed, pls see pdf at https://newindia.in/causes/ReduceAdulteration/ or see https://fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155236998786922 . If you dont have time to read whole draft, then pls ask one or more activists you trust to decide if the draft is worth supporting.
.
(step - P.3) Now the pls decide yourself, or decide by opinions of the activists you trust, WHICH draft you wish to support. Whether you to support RtrFso and JurySys drafts I have given, OR any draft given by congress/rss/aap workers or some other draft that you know.
.
Reason : Imo, one should support a law-draft ONLY after reading it himself OR after taking opinion of 2-5 TRUSTED activists you personally know. There may be chains or networks of trust i.e. activists you trust may not have personally read the draft but may be depending on some other trusted senior activists. That too is fine. Because there are 10000s of drafts and one cannot read all of them. So one has to depend on network of trusted activists. But without a word from trusted activist or reading yourself, I request you NOT to support my law-draft or any law-draft.
.
-------------
chap-3 : List of CAMPAIGN steps for voters, and reasons behind steps
-------------
.
If you decide to support the law-draft congress/rss/aap etc have proposed, then you need to execute the campaign steps they have proposed.
.
Following are the list of steps I request YOU to take, if you decide to support RtrFso and “Jury over adulteration cases” law-drafts.
.
Please read reasons in second reading of this post
.
(step - C.01) pls send two postcards - one to PM and second to CM. On postcards please write following in english or any local language - " Respected PM/CM, #ReduceAdulteration, pls print law given at newindia dot in/causes/ReduceAdulteration” and pls write your name, address, voter#. And if you decide NOT to send postcards, then rest of the steps imo are useless. I request you not waste your time any further. Execution of following steps without sending postcard imo will be useless and waste of time.
.
Reason : Imo, each postcard increases the possibility that the RtrFso law will get passed. And over 70 crore voters in India don’t know how to use whatsapp and most don’t have net. So they cant take other steps. So sending postcard and inland letter are ONLY steps they can take. And so I want ALL to take this step – whether one has net or not. And putting xerox on
.
( following steps are to be taken ONLY by those, and all those who know how to use internet or can afford to hire a person who can use internet )
.
(step - C.02) before sending the postcards/letters mentioned above, pls take photo or scan the postcard/letter and pls put the picture on FB in an album named as “Law drafts I support”. Pls make sure that photo or scan is clear. Pls make the album named as “Law drafts I support”. And put the picture in that album. Pls make album public. Pls note --- it is MUST that you the picture in to a SEPARATE album and not directly on wall !!
.
Reason : if you put scan of postcards etc you have sent in your FB album, then chances that your friends will also send postcards increases. And as number of people who send postcard increases, chances that this law will get printed also increases. And it is MUST to put this picture in a separate album dedicated for law-drafts. Because if it is outside wall or in some album with 1000s of pictures, then after a few days, neither you nor your facebook friend will be able to see or retrieve it. But if it is in a separate album dedicated to law-drafts, then it can be retrieved anyday.
.
(step - C.03) pls click "Vote" button at https://newindia.in/causes/ReduceAdulteration .
.
reason : Imo, chances that this law will get passed increases as more and more voters support this proposal on newindia dot in. newindia dot in is central govt website for India’s voters to submit petitions to Govt of India.
.
(step - C.04) pls click "share on facebook" button after voting at above link. After sharing on fb, pls move this post to “Law drafts I support” album OR pls put link to the shared post in “Law drafts I support” album
.
reason : Sharing this on facebook will inform your friends about this RtrFso law. So possibility that more of your friends will also know, support and take steps to promote these laws will increase.
.
(step - C.05) pls write following tweet " @PmoIndia #CmStates #ReduceAdulteration pls pint this law to reduce food adulteration https://newindia.in/causes/ReduceAdulteration . Law creates Right to Recall procedure over District Food Safety Officer, sh1hash = 96a162ae2319a85e5a1a1132b06dc6fcc556f5bf " . Pls see my tweet at https://twitter.com/MehtaRahulC/status/984366009367875584 . You may copy-paste my tweet.
.
reason : as more and more citizens send this tweet, the chances that this law will come increases
.
(step – C.06) pls put link of the above tweet of yours in the “Law-draft I support” album.
.
reason : a post in album with proper name is visible for weeks and months, and years. Whereas a post on wall outside albums becomes defacto unreachable and invisible after a few days. So imo, if one wants political change in India, then all his posts related to political change should be placed in albums, and there should be table of contents post in the album
.
(step - C.07) Put a status post in album "Law drafts I support" , and in that post, pls copy-paste or share the post given at https://fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155271640286922 . I would suggest copy-paste over share.
.
Reason : not all, but possibly 1 out of 100 friends of yours may read this full draft. And so draft will spread at a much faster speed.
.
(step - C.08) Pls put link to the album “law-draft I support” in cover picture description.
.
Reason : again, a post in album with proper name is visible for weeks and months, and years. Whereas a post on wall outside albums becomes defacto unreachable and invisible after a few days. So imo, if one wants political change in India, then all his posts related to political change should be placed in albums, and there should be table of contents post in the album
.
(step – C.09) In addition, you may publicize the contents of THIS post over WhatsApp, FB, instagram etc . And ask people to over whatsapp etc to take these steps
.
(step – C.10) [OPTIONAL] In “law-drafts I support album”, pls put a post that you will vote for MLA / MP candidate who will support this RtrFso law. This is optional step. Taking this step will vastly increase the chances that the law will get printed. But a voter has 10s of other factors to think of while voting. And so he may not support this step.
.
(step – C.11) This step is MOST important. Pls put link to law-draft I support album in cover picture description and in “about me” in the facebook profile.
