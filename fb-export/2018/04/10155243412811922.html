ReduceDebitCardFrauds1 - Measures to reduce debit/credit card frauds and other online payment frauds
.
---------------------
.
[ comment : These measures can be printed by PM in Gazette, after which RBI can print them in Gazette, and after which banks will have to implement them. No legislation is needed.
.
Important clauses are –
.
The “comments” listed here are not binding on any Officer / Minister / judges. The Jurors , Citizens and all can use it as guidelines. But they are not binding. ]
.
RBI Governor is hereby requested to issue notifications to implement the following measures :-
.
(1) When a bank gives a debit card to a customer, at first, it should be enabled only for ATM withdrawals and nothing else. Only after customer requests in writing, on a form provided by bank, the bank will add other features such as payments, online payment etc. The form must be in English as well as local language.
.
(2) For all credit/debit cards, the bank will provide option of “non-online payments only”. “Non-online payments only” will mean the card must be physically presented, and PIN must be entered, and then only payment will be made.
.
(3) For all credit/debit cards, the bank will provide option of “OTP only online payments” option as well. Here, payment will be made only after OTP sent on his registered mobile is entered on the website etc from where he is buying goods or services. If the user has asked for “OTP only online banking”, then payment on systems which cant send OTP will be NOT be made.
.
(4) when OTP is sent, the SMS must inform the person, in English as well as local language, that money will be deducted from the account.
.
(5) If the bank offers non-OTP payments, for say international payments, then bank must offer separate daily/weekly/monthly limits for non-OTP payments which will be initially set to zero. The user must increase limits by OTP or by physically presenting the card at a bank branch or at any designated center. And user may specify limit for a few hours, few days or at most one month, after which limit will be set to zero.
.
(6) The bank must provide a separate 1-800 number for customers to report fraudulent payments. And a human operator must pick the phone right away without going thru key presses, except the card number. And if only one card is connected to the mobile the customer is calling from, then entering card number should be optional. The operator should take down the fraudulent payments’ details from customer and later report steps it took to stop or undo the transactions to RBI and customer within 1 week
.
(7) For every fraud that customer reports, the bank must gather and provide all details to card owner and police, including account number of final receiver and name of the final receiver, for a fee of no more than Rs 50
.
(8) If a customer wants his credit card or debit card payment fraud complain to become public, then bank must put the complain on bank’s website for a fee of no more than Rs 25. And bank cannot take it down unless RBI or Sessions court permits bank to take down the complaint.
.
