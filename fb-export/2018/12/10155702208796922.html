Can you make a wall if every brick says --- I will decide my own shape and size, and why should I become a cuboid of exact size 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm?
.
A wall can be made of any brick size.
.
And there is no hard logic on why brick should be 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm. In fact, this is standard followed in India, and different countries follow different standards on sizes. And India too has several "standard" sizes. All we know is that bricks cant be too large and bricks cant be too small. But no maths or engineering calculations say why size should be 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm and not 5 mm to 20 mm more or less on each size.
.
And bricks dont need to be cubical or cuboid either. One can have hexagonal bricks with height i.e. upper surface and lower surface can be uniform hexagons and each surface on side can be a rectangle or square. Such bricks too would make a wall. In fact, hexagonal bricks are cooler and used by many.
.
So say you want to see a wall come up
.
And what if all bricks say --- I will decide my shape and my size, and nobody will decide for me, my life my rules and all brainy TV (or brain TB?) serial dialogs that we have heard 10s of times (well, we minus me, because I stopped watching TV channels since 1996).
.
And what if all bricks say "who said that brick size must be 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm ? who proved that this most optimal? and I can show that size A * B * C is better than 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm"
.
And next brick can say "A1 * B1 * C1 is better than 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm as well as A * B * C " ? And so on till A99 * B99 * C99?
.
And next brick can a say "cubodial bricks are boring and thing of past, why not go for hexagonal shape? . And why not octagonal or spherical shapes and so on?
.
So if you wish to see a wall come, will you like to see each brick decide its shape and size ?
.
So whats the solution?.
.
Solution I propose is that --- each brick which wishes to be different from 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm should first propose exact shapes and sizes, and then show how wall can be made using THOSE shapes and sizes.
.
IOW, variations should not be stopped because they bring evolution. And imo variation can NOT be stopped in age of communication. But all variations need not be good. That is seen in genetics too --- that most DNA mutations cause damage and only 1 out of 10000 mutations cause stable better version.
.
So if you want to see a wall come up, then one responsibility that MUST to be THROWN on anyone who proposes a different brick shape and size is --- show how you propose to make walls and buildings using the shape/size you propose. A variation which doesnt give complete picture must be shunned , or else, one will go on making 1000s of variations in brick shape/size, and never see a wall.
.
After seeing the new wall design that uses new brick shape/size, see if that wall offers any substantial benefit over walls made using bricks of size 215 mm x 102.5 mm x 65 mm . If not, then the variation is just a SPURIOUS variation and nothing more
