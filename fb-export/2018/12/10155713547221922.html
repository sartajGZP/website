Rally in AHMADABAD to stop export of meat and animals
.
An organization, NOT KNOWN TO ME, is holding a rally to stop EXPORT of meat and live animals for slaughter. This is legal and within govt powers. Govt may not have powers to stop animal slaughter completely. but export is NOT a fundamental right. And export of meat and live animals can be legally stopped, and should be stopped
.
Also, India is a food / water deficient country. And export of 1 kg of meat is same as exporting 10 kg to 20 kg of wheat and same as export of 20000 liters of water !!! IOW, it is a bad economic policy as well.
.
So if you are in ahemdabad, pls attend this rally
.
