PARTIAL Confidentiality in Grand Jury hearings - because witnesses fear defamation from accused and so Grand Jurors often keep promise to keep testimonies secret
.
In USA, Grand Jurors decide whether a person should be charged with criminal charges or not. For CIVIL cases, this is not needed --- one can directly ask for civil jury trial.
.
The Grand Jury decides whether hearing will be confidential or open. IOW, it is NEVER always pre-decided. Grand Jurors decide so. And later, during or after hearing, Grand Jurors can release whole or some of the hearings. It is decision of Grand Jurors.
.
WHY GRAND JURORS OPT FOR CONFIDENTIALITY?
-------------------------------------------------------------------
.
Jury Trial are always open, except in crime against women where victim identity may have to be kept confidential
.
Long back, Grand Jury hearings were always open. But since it was open, it was possible for accused or anyone to file a defamation case on a witness who is giving testimony . Many times, witnesses or policemen or experts are giving theories or giving doubts or describing possibilities about what may have happened. IOW, they are giving OPINIONS or PATTERNS of behavior, which are useful for Grand Jurors to decide guilt or acquittal.
.
Now if hearing is held in open, then accused can immidiately sue the testimony giver for defamation. And due to this, many witnesses etc refused to make full statements before Grand Jurors
.
Openness is a double edged sword. It i\gives information to voters, but ALSO reduces information that Grand Jurors will get from witnesses !! Because if proceedings are kept open, then many witnesses will simply not put information about PATTENRNS of behavior of accused, or their opinions etc and they may be useful to decide if trial should be conducted or not
.
In law-draft I have proposed, the District / State / National voters will decide whether District / State / National Grand Jury procedings will be secret or open. The voters can decide using CV clauses. If voters have not decided, then Grand Jurors will decide using majority votes and can vary from case to case.
