How would you counter lies like "over 90% judges are NOT casteist, not nepotic and dont have nexuses with lawyers etc etc"

Say you are debating on FB or in person JurySys etc and other person is anti-RTR and say there is audience of 5 to 100 persons. .

Anti-RTR-people at the drop of hat make statements like "less than 5% judges are corrupt, less than 5% judges are nepotic etc etc". "They are institutions and we must put faith in them'; and so on.

How do you propose to convince the AUDIENCE that they are "liars"? And their statements are "false".

=============
.
Now pls note the whole context
.
Say the audience has 100 persons. Now bikaumediamen have run stories that judges are all non-nepotic, unbiased, non-corrupt, non-nexused etc. And bikaumediamen as well as all bikau political science professors, bikau socialogis etc have run the theme that 95% voters in India are foolish, casteist, they are tempramental nutcases, rioteers, unethical, greedy, vote-sellers etc etc. So you stand NOT just against anti-RTR-judge speaker, but also against the starting image that audience has in his mind
.
Also, mediamen have run stories that corruption cases in judges are on-off, two-off or hundred-offs and not pattern. One-off means some exception that happened once but will not happen again and isnt part of pattern or core feature. So if you merely give examples of corruption in judges, then the paidmedia stories that "such corruption exists, but it is one-off" will reduce the effect of the corruption stories you cite.
.
In this case, and all the cases, it is first necessary to guess the picture that paidmediamen and bikau political science professors have created in the minds of audience. You starting point MUST be fully consistent with the image they have mind.
.
So now what will be your statements to convince audience that "judges are corrupt and nepotic'? And would that be your statement? If notm what will be your statement?
.
=======
.
Added later - on 6-feb-2018
.
First lets see statement S1 of us recallists (S1 = "over 80% judges are corrupt, nepotic, nexused" ) vs statement S2 of anti-RTR-supreme-court-judges procedure people that S2 = ("less than 5% judges are corrupt or nepotic or nexused".)
.
We want to convince audience that S1 is true. Now many in audience may think that judges are corrupt, But the paidmediamen and bikau-political science professors have run a campaign that "judges are not corrupt"
.
And they have a powerful argument on their side --- what evidence do you have that 80% SC-judges are corrupt? We can given some evidences against some judges. But neither we have time to get and tabulate facts against 100s of judges, nor audience has time to go thru each facts we mention
.
So imo, first step should be drag the issue from domain of "UNDERIVABLE UNVERIFIABLE statements posed as facts" to the domain of "statements cited as beliefs". !!
.
So we should put our stand as S1A = "I believe that over 80% supreme judges are corrupt, nepotic, nexused". Pls note difference between S1 and S1A.

S1 = over 80% judges are corrupt
S1A = I believe that over 80% judges are corrupt
.
Pls note that it is not just wording --- whole connotation changes !!
.
Now inform audience that ----
.
"(a) judge-worshipper should have put his statement as S1B = 'I believe that only 5% judges are corrupt'. If he has simply misphrased his statement then he and we should correct it (b) and if he actually literally means what he says, then he is not honest.
.
If he thinks that 'he believes that only 5% judges are corrupt', then fine --- I only request him to state his belief on his twitter profile so that whole may know. And if he is actually saying that "only 5% judges are corrupt" then he is dishonest,. Because no one has every done any exhaustive checking of judges, nor it is possible within existing laws to find how many judges are corrupt or nepotic. Because judges practic nepotism using cross-nepotism and take bribes via relative lawyers and never leave paper trail. Only way to know if 100s and 1000s of judges are corrupt or nepotic or not is to take narcotest. So unless he has taken narcotest of 100s of judges, he can ONLY have a belief not knowledge of the facr".
.
IOW, imo, the recallist should first word HIS statement as belief. And then inform audience that if judge-worshipper is putting his belief as fact. Whatever is the level of corruption in judges --- one can never derive forget verify using legal/objective means. One can ONLY form a belief based on his biases, his interests or his experience.
.
And once you have convinced audience that BOTH statements are beliefs, imo, we can request audience "pls decide your own beliefs about corruption in judges. Dont let others FORCE beliefs on you by giving weightages to their beliefs dishonestly put as facts" !!
.
Its not just my sentences --- its also campaign method. Essentially, I dont intend to convince anyone that "80% judges are corrupt" or "only 5% judges are corrupt". I want EACH activist and EACH voter to know that all statements on this topic made by all are their mere beliefs or statements tempered by their self interests or biases. And they too have right to form a belief.
.
And what belief they decide to harbor whether "90% judges are corrupt" or "80% are corrupt" or .... " or "only 5% are corrupt" is upto them
.
And if they think that "80% judges are corrupt",. then solution I propose is Jury System !!
.
So imo, we should first bring the whole issue in the domain of beliefs, by explaining that none has any objective way to deduce objective facts on this issue of level corruption in judges. So some people put their beliefs as beliefs, and some people put their beliefs as facts or some people put statements best to their self interest as facts !!
.
And once audience knows that they should form their own beliefs on issue of corruption of judges, and dont give a damn about what bikau political science professors say or what bikau mediamen say, the chances that they will form BELIEF that "over 80% judges are corrupt" are quite high.
