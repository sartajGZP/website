Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate

Wed Feb 07 2018 09:43:10 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

An important question came as --- Japan doesnt have much RTR/Jury etc. Then why did it progressed some much?
.
One of my core belief is that only reasons why USA is far far ahead of India are "good" law-drafts that USA has and India doesnt and some "bad" law-drafts that India has and USA doesnt. The so called political culture theory and cultural traits are useless thoughts. And amongst law-drafts, most important are Right to Recall, Jury System, multi-elections, referendum, and absence of GST/CST/Excise/ServiceTax and presence of wealth tax.
.
So now question comes as --- Japan\China and many country dont have such law-drafts, but they made progress. So how could they make progress
.
I had answered the question for China. I need to dig out that post. That will take some time --- I am putting all my posts in one Excel sheet. But that will take time. But I havent answered the question for Japan.
.
Now along with these five law-drafts, there are some more law-drafts which help a nation such "recruitment in lower/middle administration by written exam". China was the first country in world which mastered recruitment by written exams. So kings and lords etc didnt have discretionary powers to appoint lower/middle officers. This system started in 700 AD in Japan, in very very weak form - much weaker to China. But concept had taken roots. This system never came in India till British rule.
.
Recruitment by written exam creates bureaucracy which is far far more intelligent than country where officers are recuited purely by discretion.

(Aside : In India, we have recruitment by written exam, but subjects we have chosen for IAS are braindead subjects like political science, sociology, history, psychology, literature, economics etc and subjects like maths, science, engineering etc have near zero value in IAS exams. Whereas in China, maths is defacto compulsory subject and a large number of science/engineering graduates get recruited by taking their own subjects. Also we have interviews after written exams. So choice of useless subjects, ignoring useful subjects and interview and language issues --- these factors have ensured that median of selectees remains very poor).
.
Later, in 1750s, Japanese realized that West was way way ahead in technology and administrative methods. This was important difference between Japan or 1950s and India of 1750s. Because Indian elite and intellectuals of those days always took "lack of unity", "cultural traits", "law of moral values" etc as primary factors and never considered technology or administrative methods as important issue. This difference imo came because intelllectual level of the then Japanese bureaucracy was better because some of them were coming from written exams, compared to India, where in 1750, recruitment by written exam was near zero.

So this created an important difference between Japan of 1750 and India of 1750.
.
So right around 1750s, Japanese elitemen, administrators as well as commons saw the importance of manufacturing technological goods, and also fight against Western elitemen,
.
Next Japan thru some internal reasoning --- decided that they will legally ban or defacto ban import of finished goods and import of cheap Bangladeshi like labor.
.
So now country's elite, administrators, commons have made three decisions

(1) Manufacturing, science, engineering is important
(2) Minimal import of finished goods
(3) No import of cheap labor from china or anywhere

So now elitemen have no option but to promote maths/science education in commons. Because without that, manufacturing is not possible and import of complex goods is non-option
.
Next. no import of cheap labor. So low skilled labor's wages will also start rising. because manufacturing increases demand of labor, and if supply of cheap labor from other country is banned, then employers have no option but to pay more wages. So situation of lower class will improve - they will be able to spend more money for their kids education
.
But even after all that, Japan is way way behind USA in weapon manufacturing and ultra-high tech. Even today, the first version of new CPU first comes in USA, and only later Japenese catch up. In very few areas, japan is ahead of USA
.
And Japan does have some Right to recall over judges. Pls google on "Judicial Retention Japan" . And it had used Jury System in early 1900s , but was ended in 1930s . And now Japan has started Jury System in year 2009
.
But we recallists have given primacy to law-drafts like (a) RTR (b) JurySys (c) multi-elections (d) referendum (e) absence of GST and presence of wealth tax etc as important factors. And we have also emphasised on recruitment by written exams using maths/science subjects. So it becomes important for us to explain why Japan is not as worse off as India, because it didnt have these laws.
.
Imo, main reasons were
.
(a) Japanese bureaucracy in before 1700 was much better than India, because Japan did use some practice of recruitment by written exams
.
(b) So right in 1700s, Japan accepted that they are behind west in manufacturing, and accepted that reasons are their being behind in science and administrative methods

(c) They took two decisions -- no import of manufactured goods and no import of cheap labor

(d) So maths/science education for commons improved and wages of commons also increased.
.
So Japan progressed.
.
But again, today in India, if we want to ban import of manufactured goods and import of bangladeshi labor, it wont be possible without referendum. Because western elitemen have bribed Indian apex leaders such SoMoKe to promote import and "manufacturing in India, made by Westerners" model. So they see no need to improve science/engineering education. And leaders like SoMoKe also allow import of Bangldeshi labor. And so wages of poor doesnt rise. And so progress remains poor. So Japan of 1700 progressed without RTR etc. Today's India cant.
.
====
.
Added later --- some QA from comments that came below
.
(1) comment : China and Japan have education in their national language and mother tongue
,
My reply --- There was no English in India in 1750. But even in that year, Japan was significantly ahead of India
.
(2) comment : One question on this, if India never had written exams in Bureaucracy, then how come we were advanced in science between 1 CE and 8 CE (primarily the Gupta period)?
.
My reply :
.
Advancement in maths/astronomy can happen by brilliant men , whom rulers give ample resources, in living in less efficient administration. And some advancements in phsyics, chemistry, biology, medicine can also happen by brilliant men , whom rulers give ample resources, in living in less efficient administration. But ENGINEERING and TECHNOLOGY, and manufacturing needs efficient/fair courts/admin.
.
So we were behind manufacturing and weapon manufacturing by 300 BC and more so by 700 AD. You can compare and constrast weapons we had in 300 BC with weapons of Alexander. And you can also compare and contrast weapons we had in 700 AD with weapons of Arabs. Imo, better manufacturing in Greece in 300 BC was due to Jury System and a weak procedure of election that they had started since 800 BC. And imo, Arabs manufacturing improved after 500 AD due to procedure of gathering they follow. Gathering means - each person living in an area must come to one designated place once every week at a fixed time. The place was mosque and time was 2pm every Friday. Imo, gathering reduces inefficiencies and unfairness amongst those who come to gathering.
.
-----
.
Jury System is imo superior than gathering. So when UK started JurySys in 950 AD , and other countries followed it, they became stronger than Arabs /Turks and stopped their advancement and by 1500 AD Europeans were pushing them back. And by 1600 AD Europeans were taking over the world.
.
-----
.
Today,. leaders such as SoMoKe and congress/rss/aap leaders are opposing JurySys as well as refusing to improve "recruitment by written exams". The IAS exams have so hard questions in maths, science, engineering that maths, science, engineering stuidents have to take subjects like political science !! And subjects like polsci, history, sociology, litreture etc are useless and braindead subjects, and IAS are selected from those who can excel in such braindead subjects !!! Whereas China gives emphasis on maths, science etc in recruitment in general administration as well. So Chinese administrators are less inefficient than ours
.
Chinese Communist Party also gives emphasis to written exams inside their party for second/third promotions. The initial recruitment is almost open for all, and 1-2 promotions comes by attendance and doing activities. But after that, written exams are taken, and matter for promotions. Some nexuses operate here - the exam isnt as water right as Chinese Govt exams. But no political party in India takes ANY exams for promotions. Everything is by nexuses only
