Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-1
pls do this experiment - need 5 or more players, and one host.
.
To do this maths exercise, you will need 5 or more players. You can do with just 2 players too, But understanding the Common Knowledge behind is difficult with just 2, 3 or just 4 players.
,
You also need some caps say 5 white caps and 5 black caps. Then you should repeat the game with 4 white, 1 black etc
.
Now lets call these players A, B, C, D and E
.
Put white caps on head of some players and black on remaining. If possible, host should put caps in a way that no one sees whats the color of his cap.
.
Each player may know the color of his cap because host was bit careless in putting cap.

IMPORTANT --- But each player must NOT cite the color of his hat till he has given LOGIC to decide what his color is.
.
No player can /ask tell about the color his cap
.
Each one sees the color of others' cap.
.
======

step-1
.
Now host has to ask each player A to E in front of all --- "what is color of your cap , and how did you decide it"
.
(Now if a player can give answer. "black or white , and I knew it already" . But thats NOT the right answer, because due to flaw in game playeing, you may be knowing color of your cap. But in the REAL game, you dont know color of your cap till information you have and logic applies says so.)
.
Host has to ask this question twice , thrice , 10 times to each player in front of each player
.
see if players can cite color of their caps along with reasons
.
