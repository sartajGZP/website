Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-3
.
Pls see post "Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-1" "Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-2" in this album titled as "Maths problems imo useful in understanding politics".
.
There are 4 games in both parts and you must play these 8 games several times before playing games mentioned here.
.
pls do this experiment - need 7 or more players. You can do with less players, but results will be clearer if you have 7 or more player.
.
There are 4 games here, Following are the steps for 7 player version of this game-1 to game-4 ...
.
Game - 1
---------------
.
(1.1) One player will become Host H and Host will give labels of P1 to P6 to other six players
.
(1.2) You need 12 white caps and 6 black caps. Each player will be given one white cap which he needs to keep in his pocket. Host will keep other 6 black cap and 6 white cap with himself.
.
(1.3) IMPORTANT : Host must put caps on heads of players in a way that one doesnt know what the color of his cap.
.
(1.4) Host will put 2 black caps on heads of two player P1 and P2 and 4 white caps on heads of other four players. In next rounds of the game, put 2 black caps on different players. And in next round of the game, take 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 and then 0 black caps, and white caps on rest of the players.

(1.5) Then host will say -- if (a) X has logically deduced X has black cap AND (b) X knows that every white cap wearer knows that X knows that X has black cap --- then X must remove his black cap and put his white cap. But if your cap is white or if X dont know color of cap or if there is one white player Y who doesnt know that "X knows that X has black cap" then X must NOT change color of his cap.And X stands for any of you.
.
eg if (a) P1 has logically deduced that P1 has black cap AND (b) P1 knows that all 4 white cap wearer (P3, P4, P5, P6) each know that P1 knows that P1 has black cap, then and then only P1 shall remove his black cap and put white cap.

(1.6) Each player must say one of the three statement --- (a) I know I have black cap and so I will change, OR (b) I know I have white cap and so I wont change OR (c) I dont know the color of my cap and so I wont change. And then he can change cap in case-a
.
(1.7) Host will make the statement in (step-1.5) about eight to nine times and players will take step-1.6 everytime.
.
======
.
Game - 2
---------------
.
(2.1) to (2.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(2.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
(2.5) to (2.7) --- same as (1.5) and (2.7)
.
=========
.
Game - 3
---------------
.
(3.1) to (3.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(3.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will ask one player with black cap to leave room for 2 minutes. That player, say P1 must NOT hear what host has said. And all players must note that statement made by host is NOT heard by that P1. Host should explain these points to all players - that exiting player P1 has not heard the following statement. Each player must note that P1 had left the room and P1 should note that all players saw him leaving the room.
.
(3.4.2) Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
(3.4.3) Now the player with black cap who had left the room will be brought back in room
.
(3.5) to (3.7) same as (1.5) to (1.7)
.
========
.
Game-4 : same as game-3, except that in that Host will ask player with WHITE cap to leave room for 1-2 minutes, and the statement (3.4.1) will be made in his absence. And the he will be brought back. Other steps are same
