Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-2
.
Pls see post "Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-1" in this album titled as "Maths problems imo useful in understanding politics". There are 4 games and you must play. 4 games several times before playing games mentioned here.
.
pls do this experiment - need 7 or more players. You can do with less players, but results will be clearer if you have 7 or more player.
.
There are 4 games here, Following are the steps for 7 player version of this game-1 to game-4 ...
.
Game - 1
---------------
.
(1.1) One player will become Host H and Host will give labels of P1 to P6 to other six players
.
(1.2) You need 12 white caps and 6 black caps. Each player will be given one white cap which he needs to keep in his pocket. Host will keep other 6 black cap and 6 white cap with himself.
.
(1.3) IMPORTANT : Host must put caps on heads of players in a way that one doesnt know what the color of his cap.
.
(1.4) Host will put 2 black caps on heads of two player P1 and P2 and 4 white caps on heads of other four players. In next rounds of the game, put 2 black caps on different players. And in next round of the game, take 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 and then 0 black caps, and white caps on rest of the players.

(1.5) Then host will say --- those who have logically deduced that they have black caps must remove their black cap and put on white cap and those who white cap must not change. But if your cap is white or if you dont know color of your cap, then pls dont change the cap.

(1.6) Each player must say one of the three statement --- (a) I know I have black cap and so I will change, OR (b) I know I have white cap and so I wont change OR (c) I dont know the color of my cap and so I wont change. And then he can change cap in case-a
.
(1.7) Host will make the statement in (step-1.5) about eight to nine times and players will take step-1.6 everytime.
.
======
.
Game - 2
---------------
.
(2.1) to (2.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(2.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
(2.5) to (2.7) --- same as (1.5) and (2.7)
.
=========
.
Game - 3
---------------
.
(3.1) to (3.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(3.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will ask one player with black cap to leave room for 2 minutes. That player, say P1 must NOT hear what host has said. And all players must note that statement made by host is NOT heard by that P1. Host should explain these points to all players - that existing player has not heard the statement. Each player must note that P1 had left the room and P1 should note that all players saw him leaving the room.
.
(3.4.2) Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
IMPORTANT QUESTION -- Now pls note that each player with black cap knew that at least one player has black cap. And each player with white cap also knew that 2 players have black cap. So has host added any new information? And the player P1 who had not heard this statement also had this information. So did he miss anything?
.
(3.4.3) Now the player with black cap who had left the room will be brought back
.
(3.5) Host will ask once --- which players have black caps? and then host will wait for 1 minute. Then host will ask same question again, and wait for 1 minute. Like that, host will ask this question 7-8 times.
.
(3.6) Each player must say --- black, white or I dont know the color of my cap everytime host asks the question
.
========
.
Game-4 : same as game-3, except that in that Host will ask player with WHITE cap to leave room for 1-2 minutes, and the statement will be made in his
.
