Circulating proposed law-drafts
.
If any recallist has any law-draft proposal, I request him to take following steps , which I do
.
(1) Put the law-draft in a pdf file, and upload the pdf on newindia dot in. If law-draft is simple (like CancelEVM or CancelBailIn or CancelFRDI etc), then no need for pdf -- just put a post in newindia dot in . The law-draft pdf cannot have link to any private website or any person name or any party name. It can only have link to newindia dot in.
.
(1.1) This step is important --- if the draft is NOT on newindia dot in, then I wont read it and I wont ask anyone to read either, Even if draft isnt final, you can make new draft later and start a new campaign. But first step should be uploading the pdf on newindia dot in
.
(2) Then vote on this proposal on newindia dot in .

(3) Then share the proposal from newindia on your FB wall.
,
(4) Then tweet @PmoIndia to print the law. The tweet must have hashtag, link to proposal on newindia , (optional - sha1hash of pdf) and may be 1-2 line
.
(5) Then share the tweet on FB in photo album called as "Law drafts I support" or "Law drafts I support (top 50)" or "Law drafts I support (after top 50)". write 5-10 line summary
.
(6) Put FULL text drafts as FB status.in above album
,.
And later you can add video , links to pamhlet PDFs , put proposed newspaper advt etc.
,.
But above 6 steps are must
.
And thats all --- no need to approach others. People read the drafts/posts and will decide what to do.
