Why you need to ask all?
.
This gameset will need 6 persons. You can do with less, but results will be less obvious
.
(1) One person will act as stranger, and other 5 will be person who know each other.

(2) The stranger will have to step out of the room for a while.

(3) Now 5 persons in room will randomly decide which two will act as LIAR and which three will act as TRUTHFUL
.
(4) Liar has to always lie and Truthful will always speak truth. They all know about themselves. The STRANGER outside doesnt know who is Liar and who is Truthful.
.
(5) Now Stanger has to walk inside, and ask questions and decide who all are Liars and who all are Truthful
.
But he can ask ONLY type of question - he can ask A whether B is Truthful or Liar. eg he cant ask "which direction sun rises".
.
So now Stranger has to guess how many questions will ask to whom and how will he decide
