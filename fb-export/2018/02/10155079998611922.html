Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- part-4
.
Pls see posts "Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge" part-1 to part-3 in this album titled as "Maths problems imo useful in understanding politics".
.
There are 4 games in all 3 parts and you must play these 12 games several times before playing games mentioned here.
.
pls do this experiment - need 7 or more players. You can do with less players, but results will be clearer if you have 7 or more player.
.
There are 4 games here, Following are the steps for 7 player version of this game-1 to game-4 .
.
Game - 1
---------------
.
(1.1) One player will become Host H and Host will give labels of P1 to P6 to other six players
.
(1.2) You need 12 white caps and 6 black caps. Each player will be given one white cap which he needs to keep in his pocket. Host will keep other 6 black cap and 6 white cap with himself.
.
(1.3) IMPORTANT : Host must put caps on heads of players in a way that one doesnt know what the color of his cap. Each one can see others' caps' colors.
.
(1.4) Host will put 2 black caps on heads of two player P1 and P2 and 4 white caps on heads of other four players. In next rounds of the game, put 2 black caps on different players. And in next round of the game, take 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 and then 0 black caps, and white caps on rest of the players.

(1.5) Then host will say -- (a) if X has logically deduced X has black cap AND (b) if X knows that every white cap wearer knows that X knows that X has black cap --- then and then ONLY, every white must point finger at X !! But if X's cap is white or if X does know X's cap's color or if there is one white player Y who doesnt know that "X knows that X has black cap" then NO ONE will point finger at X !!
.
eg1 if (a) P1 has logically deduced that P1 has black cap AND (b) P1 knows that all 4 white cap wearer (P3, P4, P5, P6) each know that P1 knows that P1 has black cap, then and then only every (P3, P4, P5, P6) will point finger at P1
.
eg2 if P1 has not deduced that P1 has black, then NONE should point finger at P1
.
eg3. if P1 has logically found that P1 has black cap, and if P1 has also deduced that all 4 white cap wearer (P3, P4, P5) each know that "P1 knows that P1 has black cap", but white cap wearer (P3, P4, P5) know that P6 doesnt know that "P1 knows that P1 has black cap", then NONE will point finger at P1 !!!

Explanation : Each of P3 to P6 who have white cap, give benefit of doubts to P1 (or P2) that (a) P1 doesnt know that P1 has black cap. (b) P1 doesnt know that that each of (P3 , P4, P5, P6) know that "P1 knows that P1 has black cap". And once benefit of doubts for BOTH is completely removed, each points finger at P1. And till then, NONE will point finger at P1.

(1.6) After Host says the statement in (1.5), each player will decide whether he wants to point finger at anyone or not.
.
(1.7) Host will make the statement in (step-1.5) about eight to nine times and players will take step-1.6 everytime.
.
======

(2.5) to (2.7) --- same as (1.5) and (1.7)
.

Game - 3
---------------
.
(3.1) to (3.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(3.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will ask one player with black cap to leave room for 2 minutes. That player, say P1 must NOT hear what host has said. And all players must note that statement made by host is NOT heard by that P1. Host should explain these points to all players - that exiting player P1 has not heard the following statement. Each player must note that P1 had left the room and P1 should note that all players saw him leaving the room.
.
(3.4.2) Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
(3.4.3) Now the player with black cap who had left the room will be brought back in room
.
(3.5) to (3.7) same as (1.5) to (1.7)
.
