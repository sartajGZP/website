Why Sri Vallabhbhai Patel declined to become PM of India in 1946?
.
The British wanted Mr. Jawaherlal Ghazi to become PM, The British communicated their decision to the elitemen such as Tata, Birla, Godrej, Bajaj, Saravhai etc. These elitemen asked Sri Vallabhbhai Patel to step aside and let Mr. Jawaherlal become PM. Duratma Gandhi also sided with the British and the elitemen on this issue.
.
The tale we hear that is that it was Duratma Gandhi who decided. Nonsense.
.
Why didnt Sri Vallabhbhai Patel said GTH to Duratma Gandhi? Lets not pay much attention to "respect". In apex politics, everyone respects everyone to a point, and then no more.
.
He had supported of over 80% of congress workers. But back then, congress was funded by elitemen and so was media. Patel was not willing to against mediamen, elitemen and British.
.
The elitemen decided to obey British because their factories' machines' spare parts came from British. Without British help the factories would have shut down within weeks.
.
Sri Sardar Patel was far far better than Mr. Jawaherlal Ghazi but he was no Rastrapita Bose. Rastrapita Sushashchandra Bose would have said GTH to British, elitemen, Duratma Gandhi etc and would have went ahead with decision of congress workers. But Sardar Patel didnt have history of giving head on collision against British or elitemen.
.
Such was the might of British even in 1946 even after decision of giving "freedom" to India was taken, and decision of PM was to be made. They could not only have PM of their choice, via elitemen, they could force no. 2 to withdraw, and make sure that no one in public knows !!! So may be, this was the weakness of India.
.
The weakness was mainly because elitemen's factories machines parts came from UK. This was weakness of elitemen NOT India. Had India stopped import of parts, then Indians would have learnt to make them. But in the process, REAL engineers would have taken over economy, and elitemen would have lost their elitemen status.
.
Thats why, one of the FIRST orders Chairman Mao have in 1949 was burn down all European machines !!! Na rahega baans , na bajegi bansuri. Burn down European machines so that dependence on Europe ends !!! And this decision forced China to make their own machines. And so manufacturing started improving, though initially there was a deep cut in their manufacturing as their machines were way to inferior than European machines
.
So Sri Vallabhai Patel could NOT become first PM because Indian Ministers/elitemen had no guts to free India from Western machines. And activists didnt understand how British controlled Indian apex using their dependence on British machines.
.
And today, Modiji is making SAME factor much worse by FDI !!!

The reason why Mr. Jawaherlal Ghazi appeared less good compared to Sri Patel was that back then paidmedia was not as powerful as today. There was no TV and only 2% population read newspaper. If today was the case, thru TV channels, British could have created an image that Jawaherlal Ghazi is better than Vallabhbhai Patel. Thats how usuk-elitemen today have created lionlike image of Sri Modiji.
.
IOW, while Modiji does cite the story about Sri Vallabhbhai Patel being sidelined, but he is deliberately giving incomplete plot. Modiji refuses to mention WHY this happened. Back then, Jawaherlal Ghazi was NOT PM. And Congress didnt depend on his money either. So why Duratma Gandhi favored Mr. Jawaherlal Ghazi - is important factor in story, that Modiji conveniently misses. And Modiji also misses to mention the FACT that RSS back then too depended on money from Kings and large landlords, , and Kings and landlords were bankrolling RSS only because British has ordered. And Modiji also doesnt mentions the MAIN part that back then, India's dependence on British machines made us opt for a weaker PM. And today, he is making that situation much worse by FDI.
