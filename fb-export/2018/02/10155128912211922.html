Putting items like coverpic etc on FB profile that can enable OTHERS to spread information on law-drafts at a faster rate

Your FB profile has items such as coverpic, profilepic, nickname, photo albums, status posts. etc. The audience will see them. And these items communicate two things --- (1) they inform audience what laws you support/oppose and ALSO (2) they inform audience how they can organize their coverpic, nickname, status posts, photo albums etc to inform their audience what laws they support/oppose

So what you display on FB informs audience what laws you want and also informs request for others on how they can propagate information further.
.
Now some ways your readers can start putting coverpic, albums, status posts etc is by

(a) copying your items and using without any editing
(b) copying your items and using after editing
(c) not copying, but looking and writing items from scratch.

So each time you put an item on your profile, you need to keep this in mind

(a) do you want audience to copy that item or want him to write it from scratch
(b) do you want audience to copy and edit the item before he puts?
(c) do you want audience to copy and uses without editing?
.
And also, each item you put an item on profile, you will need to decide
(a) should that item be copied or written from scratch by reader?
(b) if copied, then should it be used after modification
(c) if copied, then should it be used without modification

If (C), then you need to keep that item in a way that it can be used without modification.
.
So pls note following --- if the item such as coverpic, nickname , status etc is so that it should be copied and used without modification, and you should keep THAT item in a way MAXIMUM number of readers can copy and use it without modification
.
So pls keep this in mind while making your coverpic, nickname, status posts, albums etc ..
