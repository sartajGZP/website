Why Kalki Modiji and Arvind Gandhi dress the way they dress
.
A key factor is "suspension of disbelief". Pls google on it and also wiki it.
.
In action movies, the directors and script writers have to create a lot of fast action and mystery so that viewers get so mesmerized that they dont analyze plausibility of events or how realistic the actions/plots are.
.
And costume helps in elavating the audience as well as distracting the audience. The costume takes away some seconds of brain in analysing costume and so brain gives that much LESS time to analyse the realism or logic or lack of logic or lack of realism behind statements actors are making or the events shown
.
So both can wear normal cloths normal office going managers or owners wear. But depending on metaimage they want to create, they dress too carefully
.
This fancy dress competition was first started in India politics by Duratma Gandhi. He chose fakir dress so that people will become so bhakt that they stop analysing his statements
.
Kalkipurush came at a time, when Gujarat was facing too much harassment from islamist criminals and Godhara arson had happened right after when 60 Hindus were killed. So image needed was that of superman or KalkiPurush. And so Modiji chose gaudy dresses.
.
Mahatma Arvind Gandhi wanted to project himself as ":anti-establishment" and establishment is corrupt and he is anti-corruption, So he deliberately bought an expensive shirt and expensive pants which appears as very very inexpensive !! (special expensive designers are needed to ensure that cloths look unexpensive !! ).

All in all, they dont dress like normal company managers or normal company owners. But they dress very elegantly in their own way to distract the audience and enable audience to create suspension of disbelief
