A "lie"-- If we can get 50 MPs elected, then these MPs can force remaining 490 MPs to pass the laws that these 50 MPs want by creating a huge noise and rukus, and stop Parliament till these laws are passed
.
I came across a video where a gentleman was saying --- "we are not in position to contest all 540 seats or get 270 MPs elected. But we are in position to contest some 200 seats in ,may-2019 and we can win 50 seats. So how can we get swadeshi laws passed using only 50 MPs?"

Answering this question, he further said "hamare 50 MP jab tak swadeshi kaanoon paas nahi hote tab tak parliament chalane he nahi denge". i.e. "our 50 MPs will NOT let Parliament to run till Govt passes the swadeshi laws we want".
.
IMO, his method is flawed.
.
Getting 50 MP or even 5 MP elected will create a huge influence. That I agree, But these 50 MPs cannot overpower remaining 490 MPs. Because if these 50 MPs try to stop Parliament against the wish of PM, then PM may tolerate their noise etc for 2-3 days. But after that, if and when PM wants to get the laws he needs passed, he will simply ask Speaker to ask Parliament Policemen (aka Marshals) to forcefully catch the noise making MPs and drag them out of Parliament building. The speaker can also order the gate police to ensure that these noise making MPs not be allowed in Parliament campus for weeks. And meanwhile, the PM can get the laws he wants passed
.
IOW, if these MPs or activists behind these MPs have mechanism to raise mass movement, and invoke Udham Singhs then yes, PM and these 490 MPs will bow down. But just because 50 MPs start pushing and making noise --- they cant bend the will of PM without getting lakhs of activists and crores of voters involved.
.
So first, contesting 200 LS election constituency in may-2019 will be a huge achievement,. And winning 50 MPs or even 5 MPs is a great achievement. If any new political party can win even 5 MPs, in first election they contest --- that be a new record. But statement that "50 MPs can get law passed against will of PM and will of 490 MPs without mass movement, by merely raising hell in Parliament Hall" is a lie....
