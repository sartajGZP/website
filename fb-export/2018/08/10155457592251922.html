About argument that law-draft -X is good ONLY with "law-draft-X + clauses-Y" and without clauses-Y, law-draft-X will devastate India !! - activist needs to decide for EACH X and Y separately.
.
The first question is to ask the activist to make CLEAR claims. Is he saying
.
(a) "if law-draft-X is passed WITHOUT clauses-Y. Then EXISTING situation will WORSEN"? Or
.
(b) is that activist saying that ""if law-draft-X is passed WITHOUT clauses-Y situation will indeed improve, but clauses-Y will cause more improvements".
.
Then will EXISTING situation WORSEN"?
.
eg my claim were "if Janlokpal without Right to Recall Janlokpal clauses is passed, then situation of India will WORSEN" and "if Janlokpal is passed with RTR-Janlokpal-clauses then situation of India will improve". whether one agrees or disagrees, at least I was clear about Janlokpal-law-draft and RTR-Jalokpal-clauses.
.
eg2. my claim is "CancelGST is good but ONLY with WealthTax, and CancelGST in absence of WealthTax is bad". IOW, if demand CancelGST isnt accompanied with WealthTax demand, then I oppose it. That actual happened, when some texttile traders in ahmedabad in aug-2017 wanted me to give talk in their meetings against GST, but requested me NOT to speak about wealthtax !! I decided not to attend the meeting !!
.
But consider NRC =National Citizen's Register. Here, my claim is "NRC-law-draft is positive even without RTR-NRC-head but NRC-law-draft with RTR-NRC-head-clauses will be far far more effective".
.
So I never opposed "NRC with no RTR-NRC-head-clauses", while I did opposed "Janlokpal with no RTR-Janlokpal-clauses" and do oppose "CancelGST without WealthTax".
.
So when an activist says about addition of clauses-Y in law-draft-X, then FIRST, pls do ask him to make CLEAR claims about need/effect of clauses-Y's additions.
.
----
.
Now whether is claim that "law-draft-X will do damage without clauses-Y, and law-draft-X is good ONLY with clauses-Y", each activist has to evaluate on his own and case will be specific to X and Y.
.
As such there are endless claims like "law-draft-X is bad without clauses-Y, but good only with clauses-Y", then "law-draft-X is bad without clauses-Z, but good only with clauses-Z", "law-draft-X is bad without clauses-W, but good only with clauses-W" and so on. Each activist on his own, each claim on its own, each X and Y, ,W are on their own
.
My general case is --- "if X is good without Y, then I doubt MOTIVES of people who oppose X without Y and insist on X + Y".
