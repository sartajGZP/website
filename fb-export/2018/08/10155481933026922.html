Amiri Rekha proposal by Roshanlal Agrawalji vs (MRCM + Wealth Tax) proposal by RRP
.
(1) What is Amiri Rekha proposal? (as I understand)
.
The proposal imo is WRONGLY named as Amri Rekha and should have been named as Wealth Tax. But I wont go into shabd-vivaad.
.
The Amiri Rekkha proposal as per Roshanlalji (NOT SUPPORTED BY ME) is ---
.
(AR.WT.1) All wealth below say Rs 20 crore will be tax exempt. This Rs 20 crore amount is decided by a guideline given by them, which can be drafted, This limit is called as AMIRI REKHA.
.
(AR.WT.2) High tax rate, almost 4% to 5% or 6% on wealth above say Rs 20 crore. The rate will depend on interest rate and rent rates. So rate will be high.
.
(AR.WT.3) Money will be used to fund all govt expenses
.
(AR.WT.4) Leftover money will be divided equally amongst all citizens of India !! So wealth tax rate on wealth is HIGH because a huge amount has to be collected to given monthly payment to ALL 90 crore adult citizens across India.
.
(AR.WT.5) Valuation of wealth --- each person will have to disclose wealth and value. And anyone who offers twice the price can obtain it without consent of owner.
.
(AR.WT.6) All records of private and govt owned wealth will be kept public.
.
And pls note, this is MY UNDERSTANDING of AR.WT i.e. Armiri Rekha Wealth Tax, as proposed by Roshanlal Agrawalji, Bharat Gandhi etc. They have given details, but not a draft. So for all details, pls ask them
.
So though proposal is undrafted, it is clear enough that it can be drafted. So imo, we should NOT use reason as "show the draft" as a big minus point. Any draftable undrafted proposal can be discussed based on details given, and absence of draft imo should be treated a minor minus point ONLY.
.
So the proposal is "wealth above Amiri Rekha limit will be taxed". But when many hear words "Amiri Rekha", then take proposal as "wealth above a limit will be confiscated" !! So many end up opposing this proposal for wrong meaning conveyed by the LABEL or name of the proposal. And imo, reason is bad naming. I has suggested 2-3 times that name should wealth tax. But after suggesting 2-3 times, I didnt suggest again as I dont like shabd vivaad.

How does Wealth Tax of AR i.e. WTAR differ from RRP-WT?
.
We have distinguished wealth in to 5-8 types
.
(RRP.WT.01) Types of wealth --- we have broadly divided wealth into following categories
.
(RRP.WT.01.A) Minerals, Bandwidth
(RRP.WT.01.B) Waters
(RRP.WT.01.C) Govt lands
(RRP.WT.01.D) Private lands, construction
(RRP.WT.01.E) Equipment, gold etc
(RRP.WT.01.F) Intellectual property
(RRP.WT.01.G) shares, bonds etc
.
And wealth tax rules for them differ, For minerals/bandwidth, we have royalties and govt land we have rent. And 65% of this is divided is distributed amongst all citizens, and 35% is used for Military expenses. And tax rate on private land is low, nowhere close to rent.
