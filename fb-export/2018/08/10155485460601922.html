Avoiding bank frauds at your own personal level
.
My suggestion are ---
.
(1) keep three savings accounts preferably in 3 different bank, not just three different branches of same bank
.
(2) One savings should have NO ATM (yes, not even ATM) , no net banking, and give a letter in writing to have NO outward RTGS, SWIFT or any e-transctions from that account
,.
(3) One account should have no ATM and only net banking. Keep ONLY money you need
.
(4) second account should have ATM but no net banking. Keep only money you need
.
(5) bl;ock all international transactions on debit card. Use only credit card for international transaction
.
Some pain will be there in moving money between accounts
