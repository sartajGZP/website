Managing SPURIOUS proposed-variations in proposed-law-drafts
.
One thing we recallists worked is --- we gave DRAFTS and so those who were giving blue sky arguments were on backfoot. .
But draft based movement can be sabotaged by throwing VARIATIONS after variations on all proposed-law-drafts. And person who is proposing variation can keep throwing claims Original Draft is bad and only his draft with variation is good. And then next person can claim that Original draft is bad, draft plus first variation is also bad, and only draft with first variation and his proposed second variation is good. That way, recallists, will remain confined to law-draft-analysis and will never be able to execute campaign steps. So anti-recallists win !!
.
After all, activism is law-drafts PLUS campaign steps. If all recallists decide to confine to law-draft-analysis and dont spare time and energy for campaign-steps, then law-drafts will never get printed.
.
----------
.
So how can recallists deal with proposed-variations on proposed-law-drafts and decide whether to accept / reject variation WITHOUT centralization?
.
Solution I propose is following
.
(1) Consider proposed-original-draft-A and proposed-campaign-steps-B. Say recallists are executing campaign-steps-B.
.
(2) Say person-X is proposing (draft-A + variation-V). Variation can be addition, deletion or modification of clauses. Or, it can be mere re-ordering of clauses.
.
(3a) If person-X says "draft-A is good and draft-A + variation is better" , then there is no BLOCKING. The recallists who agree can continue campaigns steps for (A + V). And rest may ignore V
.
(3b) But say person-X claims that "draft-A is bad and A+V is good", then essentially, he asking all recallists to STOP taking campaign-steps-B. THIS CAN BE DUE TO INTENTION OF SABOTAGE. OR CAN BE BONAFIDE.
.
[e.g.1 When Janlokpal Nonsense Draft was proposed, I had said that "Janlokpal Draft " is bad and only "Janlokpal with RTR Janlokpal clauses" is good. So was asking all to stop campaigning for Janlokpal only and campaign ONLY for "Janlokpal plus RTR Janlokpal". Now we know that Janplokpal was a nonsense, and just a drama.
.
e.g.2 When aadhar system was proposed in year 2011, I mentioned that system has NOTHING to stop even NEW-COMING Bangladeshies !! So I had proposed aadhar plus amendment of linking one's aadhar with relatives' aadhar . But I did NOT oppose standalone Aadhar, because Aadhar alone to reduces fund siphoning, criminal tracking, finding lost children etc. So my stand was "support standalone aadhar" and "support aadhar plus amendments".]
.
So if situation is (3a), then there is no issue to be addressed. But if situation is (3b), then

------------
.
SOLUTION?
.
Then following questions a recallists can ask to decide whether he should accept person-X's demand to stop campaign for draft-A or continue.
.
(Q1). Ask person-X to give details of campaign steps he proposes. i.e. what steps does he ask say 80 crore voters to take in case say these 80 crore voters decide to support (A + V). If campaign steps are absent, then chances are high that his sole goal is only to stop draft-A , not promote force PM to print (draft-A + V)
.
(Q2) Apply quantitative analysis to decide whether draft-A alone will IMPROVE from EXISTING situation we have TODAY or worsen from existing situation. If draft-A alone can improve situation which we have TODAY, then his motives behind opposing ORIGINAL DRAFT A needs to be speculated
.
(Q3) person-X will give some scenarios in which draft-A worsens existing situation and (A + V) improves. See if his claim of WORSENING is valid / invalid in what % of possible cases.
.

So QUANTITATIVE analysis of his campaign methods and (A + V) will enable a recallist to decide without centralization, whether person-X's proposal to stop campagn for law-draft-A is malafine or bonafide.
