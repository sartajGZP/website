What does the words "print law-draft-X" need, before and after printing the law-draft-X?
.
After part is --
.
(a) expelling officers who dont want law-draft-X or dont follow law-draft-X
.
(b) fining citizens who dont follow law-draft-X
.
(c) setting us a system to decide and collect fines
.
and many more items
.
So now first question comes is --- are there enough people willing to become officers who agree to work as per law-draft-X? eg say we say "print Right to recall District Police Commissioner law-draft". Say now next day, say 600 out of 700 DPCs in India submit resignation, saying that "they dont want to work under the new law". Well, they have right to resign and activists or recallists cant stop them from resigning. So now, are there 600 people capable of becoming District Police Commissioners, and also willing to work under new law?
.
And the "AFTER law-draft is printed" part effects "BEFORE law-draft is printed" part.
.
In "before law-draft is printed" part - one needs support of crores of voters or apex elitemen. The law-drafts I have proposed wont get elitemen's support and so getting support from crores of voters is the only option.
.
So one factor that most voters will see is "ok, the proposed-law-drafts you activists have proposed look good, but do you activists have 1000s of officers willing to work under proposed-law-drafts in case existing officers decide to resign under new law-drafts?".
.
Many voters may put this question before they decide to support law-draft.
.
A common example -- consider machines in factories. Many factory owners didnt buy certain machines, because repair manuals were ONLY in Chinese !!! And there were skilled repairers. They started buying these machines ONLY after repair manuals in English and repairers became available.
.
So activists who want new proposed-law-drafts need to convince voters , that in case , existing officers were to quit en-masse, they have means to find good replacement, and that they are capable of running the new administration with all problems. In absence of proof of ability, many voters will will deter from supporting proposed-law-draft. After all, for some voters, STABILITY and CONTINUITY are very important issues.
.
So this is one issue to be kept in mind in activism.
