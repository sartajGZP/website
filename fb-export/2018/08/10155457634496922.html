Reading the proposed-law-drafts and network of trusts
.
Each voter should read the proposed-law-draft. But then, there are over 3000 proposed-law-drafts on newindia dot in alone and 1000s may come, and 1000s may be on change dot org etc and so on. So how can one decide whether to read or not to read, and whether to vote YES, vote NO or ignore?
.
Thats why, every voter MUST network with activists and make a list of TRUSTED activists. And each time, an acivist suggests a law-draft, the voter should ask him "did you read it yourself or is it based on trust of someone". And he should be asked to give his chain/network of his trusted fellows and seniors.
.
And, at random, trust should be verified. This is IMPORTANT. Voter should look at some important law-drafts himself, and then see if activist's suggestion was right or wrong. And then voter should guess motives of activist, if he was wrong. And if motives appear as malafide, then voter needs to find another activist to rely upon.
