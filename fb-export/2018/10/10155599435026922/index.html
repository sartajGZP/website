Mirage clause or ILLUSION clause - a clause that gives no procedure, makes tall AMBIGUOUS declaration, and so almost every reader will get fooled to like and support it
.
solution -- think from HONEST officer point of view -- is it implementable?
.
While analyzing clauses in draft, an activist or voter should see if the clause is REALISTIC clause or just a MIRAGE clause.
.
A realistic clause is something that officer/staff can implement in reasonable time and funds, and it will become CLEAR to everyone in booth, city, state or whole India, that clause was deliberately not implemented
.
While MIRAGE or ILLUSION clause looks tall and nice, but in practice, it is very vague, undefined, something that almost no officer can implemented for weeks and months, or no officer can ever possibly implement it. But the VAGUE words in clause create an ILLUSION before voter/activist that the clause will deliver exactly what he wants.
.
----
.
EXAMPLES :
.
(e.g.01) consider clause that "court shall deliver fair judgment within 90 days after case is filed, and all pending cases' fair judgment will be delivered within 90 days after this law is printed".
.
Imo, this is mirage / illusion clause. A reader will be very impressed by a law which has such clause, because he will think that all his pending cases and new cases etc will end after this law-draft is printed. But even if honest officer comes, there is no way he can clear all pending cases or new cases in 90 days. But to many readers, this clause will appeal a lot.
.
(e.g.02) consider a clause 'Patwari will enter YES/NO of the voter and serial number of the proposal that the voter gives, and it shall come on PM's website. The voter will pay a fee of Rs 3"
.
Imo, this clause is a real clause. Because with reasonable funds and skills, an officer can setup hardware and software that will implement this. Also, the voter and persons around him can see whether Talati is entering YES/NO sincerely or whether he is not. And Talati isnt entering YES/NO, or if it doesnt come on website, then it will become visible to all. So this clause imo is a real clause and not a mirage clause
.
(e.g.03) consider clause "officer shall maintain a good website and will put ALL the details on the website"
.
Imo, this is a mirage or illusion clause. A reader will be thrilled to see such clause --- he will think that all information HE NEEDS or he thinks is important will now come on website. But the number of "details" in any district level office or even Tahsil level office are so many, that no honest officer will be ever able to put all details on the website !! And what is worse is -- bad officer can misuse this clause and start putting useless information first. never put critical information, and then say "see, I am putting all details and so it will take me some time". IOW, while reading this clause gives a rosy beautiful but FALSE picture in mind of reader. But when it comes to implementation, no honest officer will be able to implement it
.
(e.g.04) consider clause "Officer shall put complete bank passbook, all cheque details and cashbook details on website"
.
Imo, this is mirage clause. Because payments made to many individuals are confidential as per PRESENT law. Unless a systematic law is FIRST made, citing which salaries or contract payments can be fully disclosed, and which shall be kept confidential, officer simply cant implement this clause
.
(e.g.05) consider a clause "voter shall provide marks between -100 and 100 to each candidate and Talati will enter the marks in his computer which will come on PM's website. Or, voter may specify -1, 0 or 1 or simply 0 or 1 to each candidate".
.
Imo, this clause is real, but some details are needed. How is "1" interpreted. And what if a voter specifies score like 150? And so on. But once these cases are all worked out this clause can be implemented.
.
(e.g.06) Consider Right to Recall University Vice Chancellor. Which clause is better "VC will put all work details on univ website" or
.
(a) VC shall upload all degree information of all persons who are Ministers in Central Govt or State Govt across India, and have claimed to have obtained degrees from his univ. He will obtain the list from affidavits put on ECI website.
.
(b) VC shall upload all degree information of all persons who are MPs or MLAs across India, and have claimed to have obtained degrees from his univ. ECI shall publish the list on ECI website.
.
(c) VC shall upload all degree information of all persons who are MPs candidates or MLA candidates across India, and have claimed to have obtained degrees from his univ. ECI shall put the list on ECI website
.
(d) VC shall put names and aadhar numbers and voter numbers, and degree details of all Govt employees who have claimed to have obtained degree from his univ. Secretary of Dept of Personnel of Union Ministry and each State Govt shall put lists.
.
(e) VC shall put degree details with names, voter-number, aadhar number of all students who have graduated from univ, starting from last year first. He will go as back as possible, upto year 1950 but not before.
.
(f) VC shall also upload marksheets of all students after degrees are uploaded. eg marksheet of students of year 2018 will be uploaded after degree upload of students of year 2013 is done.
.
(g) VC will provide interface for ALL univ passouts to upload their degree information using a PC. Website should show that information is uploaded by student and not by univ. The website will also show whether it is verified or not verified by univ staff.
.
(h) If a pass out wants early verification of his degree, then he can pay a fee of Rs 300. After paying the fee, VC will ensure that verification of his degree details are done within 2 weeks.
.
(i) If a pass out wants early verification of his marksheet, then he can pay a fee of Rs 200 per semester or Rs 400 per year. After paying the fee, VC will ensure that verification of his marksheet details are done within 4 weeks.
.
(j) Any THIRD PARTY can give Rs 500 and information about degree of any student. Within 6 weeks, registrar shall verify whether degree information is correct of false.
.
.....
and so on a
----
.
IOW, if one wants good website, then that activists would put list of all IMPORTANT details that should come first. And it will vary from dept to dept. eg consider university. What is more important -- details on how money is spent on building or details of whose degree is real and whose degree is fake? And also, degree information of "important persons" should come first.
.
So if clause like "Vice Chancellor will put all work details on website" is put in draft, then that clause adds ZERO value and more confusion. The VC can spend all time in adding petty details, and never put important details like degree information. Instead , if the second set of clauses are added, then citizens will be better served.
.
============
.
Out movement is DRAFT based, and so one way to sabotage the movement is give drafts loaded with MIRAGE/ILLUSION clauses. These mirage clauses will be vague, ambiguous and can create an illusion in each reader's mind that the clause will deliver him what he wants, deliver him heavens and there will be zero cost on tax money.
.
So activists will need to CULTIVATE SKILLS to decide whether a clause is real or a MIRAGE / ILLUSION clause. A general guideline is
.
(A) to put yourself in the officer seat and see whether you can implement the clause. And id clause well defined to begin with or vague.
.
(B) has anyone in world implemented such feature till date?
.
(C) does it serve important issues first?

... and so on. In general it will vary from dept to dept
