(A079) About Rajesh Jain's Dhanvapasi proposal
..
(section-25 of the proposed DhanVapasi draft. which I refer as "DhanVapasi-part-1" involves selling away all govt plots and also mineral mines to private individuals using auctions. Now Indian elitemen are all in debt (except Indian elitemen controlled by usuk-elitemen). And so if Dhanvapasi-part-1 auction is held then most of the govt plots and mineral mines will become property of usuk-elitemen or Indian elitemen controlled by usuk-elitemen. With that, India and Indians will get ruined after 5 years to 10 years.
.
Imo, my proposal Minerals3 aka MRCM is far superior than this DhanVapasi-part-1. Here mineral mines and govty plots are NOT sold. The mineral mines are given to one who can give MAXIMAL possible royalties for a duration of few years. And govt plots are rented for 1 year to 25 years. .
.
The second part of DhanVapasi and my proposal MRCM is same --- the money is equally divided into citizens of India.
