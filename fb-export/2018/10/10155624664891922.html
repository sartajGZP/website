Why RTR has "failed" in Peru?
.
Many fake recallists use Peru as example to claim that RTR will fail in India because it "failed" in Peru
.
First, it didnt fail in Peru. There has been spurious recalls causing instability at local instability, but administration vastly improved after recall system came in Peru. But still instability has become issue
.
(1) The reason why recall procedure is creating instability is because NEGATIVE recall condition is used and NOT POSITIVE recall.
.
In Peru, a Mayor or city councilor faces recall when over 50% voters demand his recall. So says sitting Mayor X has support of say 35% voters, and no one has support of 36%. Then in MY proposed procedure, X will continue, till some Y proves that he has support of at least 35% + 10% = 45%. But in Peru's recall code, if 51% demand recall of X, X will be recalled, and in next election X may not contest and then Y comes who again doesnt have 51% votes. And so negative recall can happen against him as well,.
.
(2) Another reason is -- Peru recall needs signature gathering, which takes a long time and huge costs. And it enables existing Mayor to sabotage signature gathering by bribing signature gathering activists and signature verifying officers. Thats why I always opposed voters signing on private activists petitions or sending SMS to privet websites. Because such activists mostly want to sell out. And such signatures or SMS have no value because govt officer has to re-verify all. And thats why I didnt have signature gathering or sending SMS to private phone numbers or showing voterids on private website NONSENSES from day one. In my proposed procedures, a voter will register approvals DIRECTLY on govt website which become visible to all, by personally visiting Talati office or sending SMS from his registered mobile. IOW, signature gathering has weakened RTR procedure in Peru, and no such signature gathering is there in my proposed RTR procedure
.
(3) Another reason why corruption didnt come down to as low as US level in Peru despite RTR, is lack of Jury System. Peru has almost non existent Jury System. And there is no NARCOTEST IN PUBLIC procedure in Peru. And so Mayors have nothing to worry except recall if they are corrupt. This isnt the case in USA. in USA, after recall, Jury can imprison a corrupt officer, And I have added Narcotest in public procedure to make punishment more likely in corruption cases.
.
IOW, instability caused in Peru is due to NEGATIVE recall procedure and SIGNATURE gathering, not due to RTR per se. And corruption did decrease due to RTR, but not to USA levels, because of lack of Jury System.
.
But many psuedo-recallists are claiming that instability in Peru recall is because of lack of clause that "officer must create a good website" etc etc and NOT because Peru has negative recall, signature gathering and no Jury, and no narcotest !!
.
IOW, they want to derail campaign for law-drafts such RtrDeo3, JurySys3, RtrPM etc by creating FAKE analysis, blaming RTR and not negative recall signature gathering, and also want to promote fake UNIMPLEMENTABLE clauses. So when activist takes a draft with UNIMPLEMENTABLE clauses (like officer must put ALL details on website, which is unimplementable for several years in India), the anti-recallists will immediately convince audience that "see, their RTR-draft requires a website which is infeasible in India" !!
.
So all in all, psuedo-recallists have a master method. Blame RTR and not negative recall or signature gathering for failure of RTR in Peru, and then propose an UNIMPLENTABLE FAKE SOLUTION !!!
.
SOLUTION?
.
Solution I propose is that activists need to understand that their analysis is WRONG, and their proposed solutions are also wrong. So recallists need to show to audience that their analysis is wrong.
.
