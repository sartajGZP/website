The nonsense proposal of making CBI independent
.
Almost all opposition leaders say they want CBI to become independent body like ECI. eg when Arun Jetley was in opposition, he too supported his useless nonsense proposal that CBI should be independent and Modiji supported this proposal by keeping silence when Jetley made this nonsense proposal. And congress made same "make CBI independent" nonsense demand in 1998-2004,
.
The proposal is as nonsense as saying "police dept in gujarat or ahmedabad or any state or any city should be made independent of any ministerial control".
.
Or as nonsense as saying "every police inspector should be independent of all ministers, judges and commissioners". .
..
Citizens hold ministers responsible for law-order. So if Ministers dont have powers over police (or CID or CBI), then there is no way ministers can work to maintain law-order and reduce crimes. We have problem of corruption and solution I propose is RTR Police Commissioner, RTR Home Minister etc. Making CBI or police dept are nonsense proposals.
.
With law-draft I have proposed, namely Right to Recall District Police Commissioner ( see https://www.facebook.com/notes/10150424068741922 ) , it makes District Police Commissioner semi independent of CM, but the same procedure makes him dependent on voters, because voters can expel him anyday. But when "make CBI independent" nonsense proposal is floated, these nonse proposal promoters dont put any mechanism by which voters can control CBI chief or staff
.
CBI should remain under PM. In fact, today, the mess is because PM doesnt have power to CBI chief which PM had in past. In feb-2014, law was printed that CBI Chief can be expelled only of at least 2 out of 3 people (PM, Leader of Opposition, Chief judge of India) agree. And thats why we see CBI director misusing his power to harass CBI special director.. If PM had power to expel CBI Director or Special Director, which PM had before feb-2014, then PM would have asked both apex officers to do their job and not waste time in fighting each other.
.
We should print law-drafts such as Jury System over CBI staff, using which citizens can expel a CBI officer below rank of Assistant Director if he misuses his powers. And PM should manage all CBI officers directly or indirectly above the rank of Assistant Director. And using RTR PM, citizens should expel/replace PM if PM misuses CBI or doesnt punish errant apex officer. And there should narcitest in public procedure in which Jury can take narcotest in public on any CBI officer below the rank of Assistant Director, if there is sufficient evidences of him misusing his powers.
.
But whole proposal of "make CBI independent" is a nonsense, Making CBI independent means "make CBI puppet of Supreme judges, who are puppets of usuk-elitemen anyway". .
.
So pls dont fall "make CBI independent" nonsense. Instead, pls vote for Right to Recall PM proposal given at https://newindia.in/causes/RtrPm2 . This proposal will enable voters to replace PM if he misuses CBI and will reduce the chances of misuse. ....
