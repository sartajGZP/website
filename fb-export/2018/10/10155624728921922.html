Killing RTR-movement by adding UNIMPLEMENTABLE clause in the draft.
.
SOLUTION --- recallists should ensure that EVERY clause in draft is CLEAR to every reader, unambiguous, and implementable with YESTERDAY's technology, and within given time and budget and existing laws or existing proposed laws. Otherwise, by adding a clause which is vague and needs time man-power and budget that officers will never get will only help anti-recallists.
.
-----
.
Our RTR-movement is draft based, and so there is an easy way for anti-recallist to kill the movement. First, the anti-recallists can pose as recallists. Then he can create campaign based on lies, and convince fellow recallists that "see, take this RTR draft to people", and that draft will have an UNIMPLEMENTABLE clause-A.
.
So when recallists take THAT draft with clause-A to audience, other anti-recallists will be easily able to convince audience that "see , they themselves admit that their draft will ruin India without clause-A and clause-A cannot be implemented for several years". And so chances that audience will agree with anti-recallists increases !! And thus campaign will die.
.
-----------
.
Example-1
.
(1) Consider RTR-draft #RtrDeo3 given at https://newindia.in/causes/RtrDeo3 .
.
(2) Now a psedo-recallist can come and create an analysis based on lies, that
.
(2a) RtrDeo3 will fail unless people are educated
.
(2b) so lets add a clause-A that "Deo shall educate all adults as well along with children".
.
(3) say recallists end up falling for his fake analysis and agree to campaign for RtrDeo4 which is copy of #RtrDeo3 plus clause-A given above.
.
(4) now when recallist goes to audience with draft RtrDeo4, the anti-recallists will have easy time convincing audience that both #RtrDeo3 and RtrDeo4 are bad . How? Anti-recallists' argument will be "see, the recallist himself agrees that RtrDeo3 will fail without clause-A . And clause-A is unimplementable because adults are too busy and will never show up as adult leaning school". And this campaign for RtrDeo3 will suffer
.
========
.
example-2
.
(1) Consider RTR-draft #RtrDeo3 given at https://newindia.in/causes/RtrDeo3 .
.
(2) Now a psedo-recallist can come and create an analysis based on lies, that
.
(2a) RtrDeo3 will fail unless all details of all tasks of all clerks and all officers are on website that all can see in english as well as local languges, and download using torrent and it is blockchained etc etc
.
(2b) so lets add a clause-A that "all details of all tasks of all clerks and all officers are on website that all can see in english as well as local languges, and download using torrent and it is blockchained etc etc "
.
(3) say recallists end up falling for his fake analysis and agree to campaign for RtrDeo4 which is copy of #RtrDeo3 plus clause-A given above.
.
(4) now when recallist goes to audience with draft RtrDeo4, the anti-recallists will have easy time convincing audience that both #RtrDeo3 and RtrDeo4 are bad . How? Anti-recallists' argument will be "see, the recallist himself agrees that RtrDeo3 will fail without clause-A . So first clause-A should be implemented and then only RtrDeo3 or RtrDeo4 should be printed". And clause-A is impossible to implement unless there is RTR over officers and JurySys over officers to begin with. IOW, without JurySys and without RTR, officers will never make a good open website, and so clause-A will never get implemented !!
.
(5) Also, try this for one month, Pls put details of ALL work you did in PRIVATE SECRET or PUBLIC (as you wish) FB group . You will see that putting "ALL" details will take up all your time !! Next, ask all people below you to put all work details they did in whole day on PRIVATE SECRET or PUBLIC (as you wish) FB group . After sometime, you will see that "clause like put all work details" is useless. One has to sit down, think for days and days, and decide which details details can be put within given workhours and given budget. And one has to spend hours and lakhs of rupees in making user interface that will enable clerks/officers to put these details, enable senior to verify and approve (sign off) these details and enable citizens to search/sort/download meaningfully. And it will vary from department to department, section to section. eg travel expenses can be non issue in many departments and can be huge in other department. And so if you are making website for first department, you may decide not to add some fields wrt travel expense which you may need to add in other department where travel expense is huge. eg consider a policeman who has to deliver 50 court summons a day, If you ask him to enter travel expense for each summons, he will be wasting hours doing useless work where amount is tiny. But consider officer in foreign affairs ministry who has to travel abroad. Here, travel expenses are huge and details per trip are needed.
.
(5a) Thats why, when a website is made in any govt dept, 100s of hours go preparing what is called as SRS i.e. System Requirement Specification.
.
(6) IOW, clause-A like "officer will put all expenses on website" , "officer will put all work details on website" will only enable anti-recallists to convince audience that
,
(6a) "see dear audience, recallists himself agrees that without clause-A, RTR draft will ruin India
.
(6b) clause-A need years of work and are unimplementable today because DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS are needed.
.
(6c) So his drafts will ruin India, if they are printed today
.
----
.
So solution I propose to recallists --- dont add any clause that is vague and need huge amount of specification for implementation. Adding such clauses only help anti-recallists
