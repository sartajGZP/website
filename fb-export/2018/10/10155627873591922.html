Nonsense that "without 'officer must maintain good website clause', hidden corruption will increase if proposed RtrDeo3 , Minerals3 = MRCM law-draft, JurySys3 law-draft are passed !!!
.
In proposed law-draft RtrDeo, voters get Right to Recall District Education Officers, thru which, they can replace, retain, bring back, expel etc DEOs. There is a separate draft which gives voters JurySys over staff. And there are other RTR drafts which give RTR procedure over other officers. And other drafts, that create JurySys over staffs of other departments and ministries. In addition, there is narcotest in public procedure for serious crimes.
.
Due to recall, jurysys and nacrotest , the officers will be prompted to improve disclosure of expenses, working, decisions, progress reports etc using websites, whatsapp, fb and also sending printed summary reports to all voters. So I see no need to add 10s of clauses in recall/jury/narco drafts saying that officer/staff must make a good website, must make a good whatsapp group, must make a good FB page, or must implement blockchain on website to ensure integrity etc etc, Using available means and technology, RTR/Jury/Narcotest will prompt officers/staff to disclose all relevent informatuion, or else, voters will replace them, with officers who are willing to disclose properly.
.
But some fake recallists, in order to reduce support for Right to Recall law-drafts, have started a campaign that "RtrDeo3, JurySys3 etc drafts will INCREASE hidden corruption" i.e. hidden corruption in India will INCREASE if these drafts are printed !!
.
This is nonsense.
.
First, there whenever corruption grows even into few lakhs of rupees, it becomes known to 10s of people in staff and never remain hidden. eg say hospital staff has to buy oxygen cylinder. Then say he buys 100 cyliners. Then it is possible for 1 person to smuggle out 1-2 cylinders or get 98 cyliners and clear invoice for 100 cyliners. One person can hide 1-2 cyliners and get away once in a while. But if he does several times, or trues to smuggle out more cyliners, then some graud or inventory checker will notice and report. And unless he is given cut, he will not remain silent.
.
And problem is worse for people at top. They have to get all decisions implemented using staff.. So their corruption will immiditale get noticed by staff.
.
So very tiny corruption (like someone smuggling biscuits or tea bags or notebooks out of office) can remain hidden. But large corruption will never remain hidden. Now there is general law that if officer X is doing something wrong, and his collegue Y notices that wrong doing, then he must inform senior officers and senior is required to take action. (This rule doesnt apply for private citizens). So if anyone X notices a large corruption by Y, and X doesnt report, then he knows that later , Jury can impose fine on him or even expel him. So unless Y is given cut, he has no reason to remain silent.
.
IOW, one time small corruption or theft (like taking office stationary home) may go unnoticed. But large or repeated small corruption will always gets noticed by many in staff as well as public. And using JurySys and narcotest in public, citizens will force the officers to disclose information, and also punish them
.
IOW,. all these claims by people (who call themselves recallists !!) that proposed law-drafts such as RtrDeo3 , JurySys3 etc will INCREASE corruption are false. They are making such clause only to derail RTR movement.
