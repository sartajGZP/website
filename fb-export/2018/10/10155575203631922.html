Why RTR-president-procedure in Venezuela didnt convert Venezuela as low-corrupt as USA?
.
(1) RTR-president procedure does NOT use positive recall but uses negative recall. So many voters dont call for recall, because they fear "what if new person is worse"? The recall procedure I have proposed are positive recall i.e. a voter can approve upto 5 candidates and also approve existing PM. Also, later, the approval is replaced by marks-cum-approval i.e. voter can either approve/ignore or give marks between -100 to 100 to candidates (ignore = zero marks),.
.
(2) Recall procedure in Venezuela is further weakened by condition that at least 190,000 signatures have to be gathered first. (Venezuela has about 1.90 crore voters) Then there is a long process of signature verification. So many times group-A will gather 100,000 signarure , group-B will gather one lakh signature, but supporters of A wont sign on B's petitions etc. Also, many times signature gatherer gets bribed or pressurized. In recall process I have proposed, there is NO signature gathering and so no verification is needed. Each voter has to personally appear at Patwari office or vote via secure APP/SMS, and so verification is done right at the time of approval filing. And there is no possibility of groupism or possibility that some intermediate activists / leaders will sell out
.
(2a) This is called as SYNCHRONOUS RECALL PROCEDURE vs ASYNCHRONOUS RECALL PROCEDURE . In SYNCHRONOUS procedure, several voters must act TOGETHER to go to next level. Whereas in ASYNCHRONOUS procedure, each voters works at his own time on day of his choice, and when certain number is accumulated, change may happen. SYNCHRONOUS procedures are good in small electronics parts, where each component has to do only specific tasks, and each component is connected with others. But some voters may be free on monday, others may be free on tuesday, and so dealing with crores of voters, ASYNCHRONOUS procedures are better. eg Election is synchronous i.e. all voters must vote on same day. But recall procedures we have proposed are NOT synchronous --- any voter can file approval anyday he is free.
.
(3) Next, in Venezuela, Election Council, equivalent to our Election Commission can suspend / cancel recall procedure !!! The Election Council can even suspend signature gathering !!! This makes RTR more cumbersome
.
(4) And finally, Venezuela has RTR over Nation's President, but has no Jury System, no RTR/elections for police chiefs, district education officers, district judges, district public prosecutors etc. And Venezuela has no district level referendum system, and has only national level referendum procedure.
.
So while RTR did help voters in controlling President (eg President was forced to cancel NoteBandi i.e. Demonetization). But it doesnt help voters in ensuring proper policy making, because at PM level, only choices are large political groups, and all large political groups are dependent on paidmedia, and so paidmedia sponsors dictate their policies
.
Due to absence of Jury System, the manufacturers in Venezuela suffer heavy handedness and corruption of officers. And so industrial growth in Venezuela is poor. And so unemployment is high and so is crime
.
