Say yes to fire-crackers to control dengue
.
By Lalit K. Jha

NEW DELHI OCT. 15. Say yes to firecrackers to get rid of mosquitoes and the dreaded dengue! This seems to be the new ``mantra'' of the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, particularly its Health Department, which is busy fighting the dengue fever. It has requested the Delhi Government's Environment Department to put its "anti-firecracker campaign'' on hold or keep it low key this year in view of the fast spreading dengue fever.

It has officially informed the Delhi Government about its inability to participate in the anti-firecracker campaign arguing that it was already involved in a massive mass awareness campaign on dengue and concentrated its entire energy in preventing this disease from spreading further.
