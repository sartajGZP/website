How do YOU suggest should crores of voters should communicate to PM/CM about law-drafts that these voters want?
.
Two of the most important parts of "recallism" is that (a) voters should directly communicate to PM for law-drafts that the voters want. (b) and voters should directly communicate to voters about law-drafts each voter wants.
.
#TcpIndia7 combines both . But we dont have #TcpIndia yet.
.
And not just about proposed-law-drafts, Recallism means that as far as possible, on as many issues as possible, voter-to-PM and voters-to-voters communication should be direct and should involve zero or as fewest possible activists, media, and discretionary power of govt.
.
So lets take some important law-drafts such as #Minerals3 , #RtrDeo3, RtrPm, JurySys3, ExpelBangladeshies, TcpIndia7 and so on.
.
Say some N voters have decided that PM should print one or more of these law-drafts in Gazette. N can be few hundred or few thousand or few lakhs or few crores.
.
Then in your opinion, which PHYSICAL SPECIFIC means these N voters should use to communicate to PM that they want these specific law-drafts?
.
And what steps these N voters take to inform maximal number of voters that they have communicated to PM to print these law-drafts?
.
-----
.
Pls avoid useless answers like "voters can use whatever means they want". If every voter uses random means, then it cannot create a CAMPAIGN. A CAMPAIGN or a MOVEMENT needs voters to take specific actions.
,
So what steps do YOU suggest to voters to take?
.
-------------
.
Many people laugh at my suggestion that "all MRCM-activists and all anti-DhanVapasi-part-1 activists should send postcard to PM and , and all activists should work to convince lakhs and crores of voters that each should send postcard to PM asking PM to print #Minerals3". There are 10s of flaws once can find with ( postcard + newindia + twitter + show on FB//WA/twitter ) method. But the importance becomes clear ONLY after one works to complete well defined propose alternatives.
.
So pls suggest the PHYSICAL actions voters should take to communicate to PM .
