Why DhanVapasi-part-2 i.e. every citizen of India getting Rs 20000 a year will NOT cause much long term improvement in India? Because it will only increase imports and not manufacturing skills in India.
.
DhanVapasi-part-1 is 10 times to 100 times bigger disaster than Janlokpal and Lokpal combined. Because DhanVapasi-part-1 involves SALE and not renting/royalty of mineral mines and govt plots for good. If maximal possible royalty is taken from mineral mines, then a good amount can come for decades and possibly for centuries. But if all mineral mines of India are sold off within 5 years to 10 years, then money will paltry and after 10 years, it will be zero. And after 10 years, mineral mine owners will drastically increase the prices by reducing supplies. And so all money that commons got for 10 years will evaporate in 2 years !!
.
Likewise, if all govt plots are sold off in 5 years to 10 years, then price fetched will be paltry. And after 10 years, the new landowners will increase the land prices and rents. And so all money that commons got for 10 years will evaporate in 2 years !!
.
But even if we dont count huge damage that DhanVapasi-part-1 gives Rs 20,000 (twenty thousand) per year to every voter for 5 years to 10 years, then also, benefits India and Indians will get will be near zero. WHY?
.
Because DhanVapasi group has NO proposal to bring JurySys or RTR or any law that will improve Indian manufacturers' efficiency or maths/science education. So Indian manufacturers efficiency will remain low, much lower compared to USA, China, SoKo, Taiwan etc. And so almost all money will get used up in IMPORTS or goods manufactured by foreign companies in India. And in REAL manufacturing in India will be low, and skill increase in Indians will be almost near zero.
.
This disadvantages are NOT there in MRCM. Because MRCM has CV clauses which will make it easier for activists to bring RtrDeo3, other RTR-law-drafts, JurySys3, WealhTax2 etc law-drafts which will reduce false cases against manufacturers, will reduce land prices, and also improve maths/science education and also improve law/weaponuse education. Pls MRCM-law-draft itself has RTR-NLRO-clauses (NLRO =
National Land Rent Officer) and JurySys over staff clauses. IOW, MRCM = Minerals law-draft takes India closer to RTR and JurySys on its own.
.
IOW, MRCM not only ensures that mineral mines and govt plots remain with Indian citizens for ever (unlike DhanVapasi-part-1), MRCM and related law-drafts create real manufacturing growth, while DhanVapasi will only increase imports. ..
