How activists may inform other activists/voters about campaign steps
.
(incomplete)

Campaign steps are NOT informing voters about law-drafts. Informing voters about law-draft is a necessary first step, and comes before campaign steps. But it isnt campaign step. Campaign ( = movement , aandolan ) step is step meant to create a give a message from crores of voters to all existing PM/MPs/elitemen to print these law-drafts and also create an alternative PM/MPs/elitemen who would be willing to work with these law-drafts.
.
So how can an activist inform fellow activists and voters about what campaign steps he wants 2 lakh activists and say 50 crore voters to take to get some specific law-drafts printed.
.
Following are the steps I suggest
.
(1) The first step I suggest is --- every activist should make an FB album named as "(A008) how activists iyo sud inform voters laws activists want". And in that album, he can put status posts that inform fellow activists/voters about campaign steps
.
(2) Next step is -- activists should himself decide what campaign steps he wants 50 crore to 80 crore voters to take. He needs to spell these physical steps in clear language that if 1000 people read and execute these steps, then all 1000 do take same action. In IOW, steps cant have phrases like "do whatever you wish" because in such case, 1000 persons who read will all take different actions and in such case campaign is unlikely to arise. So activist should make list of campaign steps he proposes for 80 crore voters to take and put them
.,
(3) Next, activist should himself execute all steps that he wants 50 crore voters to take. And he should put proofs of those steps as far as possible. eg if activist wants 50 crore voters to send 20 postcards per voter to PM, then imo activist should send 100 postcards himself.
