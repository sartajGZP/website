Saving Somnath Temple gold and how history may or may not repeat itself
.
summary -- All the somnath temple gold could have been saved by giving away 1 gram gold to each of the lakhs of Hindus living nearby. But temple owners didnt give away the gold, and Ghazanavi looted all. Now using Dhanvapasi-part-1, usuk-elitemen are going to rob all mineral mines and govt plots of India. History may or may not repeat again.
.
----------
.
When Mehmud Ghaznavi was coming to loot Somnath Temple, imo there was an easy way to save about 2 tonne of gold (as many historians say) that Somnath temple had. That easy way imo was --- give away 1 gram of gold to each of lakhs of the Hindus living in the near by area. Ghaznavi could not have searched lakhs of homes and fought with lakhs of Indians to get that 2 tonne of gold. It was not viable and cost effective for him.
.
But Somnath temple owners back then did not want to give away that gold to commons. Many say that somnath temple owners had created a myth that Somnath temple was built by Lord Shiva himself and so Lord Shiva will come and protect it. The myth was that Lord Shiva will personally come and open his third eye, and Mehmud Ghaznavi and all his soldiers will burn to ashes !! May be such myth was there, may be no such myth was created - we dont know for sure. But we do know for sure that temple owners did NOT give away 1 gram of gold to all lakhs of hindus living near by, And so when Ghaznavi came, all 2 tonne of gold was at one place --- no way to hide and so easy to loot and easy to collect. And millions of people had no reason to fight back because it was not their gold anyway. So finally, Ghaznavi looted all the gold.
.
And worse, he could use that gold to build a bigger military and rob more temples and kill more Hindus and convert more Hindus back then. He reduced the taxes in Iran, and so Iranian economy grew much bigger and he could build much bigger army. And so on and on
.
That 2 tonne of gold by today's price will be Rs 660 crore.
.
Now come year 2018, and usuk-elitemen are coming to rob all India's mineral mines and govt plots worth 100000 times more using DhanVapasi-part-1 proposal.
.
So what is thsi Dhanvapasi-part-1 proposal, that will imo ruin India?
.
As per DhanVapasi-part-1, mineral mines will NOT be given for royalty and govt plots will NOT be rented some years. But as per DhanVapasi-part-1 , these mineral mines and govt plots will be SOLD off via auction to desi/videshi elitemen. And desi elitemen are all in debt - we see everyday that their companies are being taken over by videshi elitemen one after another. The handful of apex "Indian" elitemen are actually usuk-elitemen-sponsored Indian faces only (eg Tata, Ambani, two Mittals, etc are all controlled by usuk-elitemen). So when such a huge amount of mineral mines and govt plots will be auctioned, the prices will drop rock bottom and almost all plots and mines will get sold within 5 years. Since Indian elitemen dont have money, the buyers will be all usuk-elitemen; After that, using a cartel, usuk-elitemen will increase the prices of minerals and plots to skyhigh level and all money that came in 5 years will get washed away within 2 years. All in all, this will be thousand times worse than Somnath loot
.
Why will DhanVapasi-part-1 will get executed? Because Dhanvapasi-part-2 says money collected by this auction held in Dhanvapasi-part-1 will be equally distributed amongst citizens of India. And the proposed bill makes a real or false promise that every Indian citizen will get Rs 20000 a year. So due to this promise, and power of FB, DhanVapasi will become hugely popular across poor of India, unless a better proposal is put. And so crores of poor voters will end up voting for DhanVapasi and thus DhanVapasi-part-1 may happen. And thru DhanVapasi-part-1, all mineral mines and govt plots may end up becoming property of usuk-elitemen.
.
SOLUTION?
.
So solution I propose is the law-draft I have been campaigning for since oct-1998 --- MRCM aka Mineral Royalty for Citizens and Military aka #Minerals3 given at https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 .
.
This #Minerals3 draft is similar to giving 1 gram of Somnath gold to millions of people living near by.
.
As per this proposed MRCM-law-draft, the mineral mines will given to govt pr private miners for maximal royalties and govt plots will be rented for 1 year to 25 years. And royalties and land rent collected will be equally distributed amongst all citizens of India. This way, mineral mines and govt plots will remain as property of Indians for decades and may be centuries.
.
So I request all activists of India to see that now there are ONLY two choices
.
(1) If proposed MRCM-law-draft is NOT presented before poor of India, then poor of India will support DhanVapasi-part-1 and all mineral mines and govt plots of India will go auction and will become property of usuk-elitemen
,
(2) If proposed MRCM-law-draft is presented by activists to crores of poor voters of India, then all will see that renting is better than selling for ever, and so imo many poor voters will support proposed MRCM-law-draft
.
So imo, what activists can do is --- run a campaign for proposed MRCM-law-draft, then voters will prefer MRCM to DhanVapasi and in such case mineral mines and govt plots will remain with Indians. Otherwise, except activist-led-campaign to promote MRCM, I dont see any way to stop DhanVapasi.

So lets see whether history will repeat Somnath or not. Imo, if activists campaign for proposed MRCM, then Somnath may not repeat, Otherwise, Somnath will repeat with multiplier of 100000.
