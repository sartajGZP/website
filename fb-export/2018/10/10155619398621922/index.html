Campaign (Aandolan , Movement) vs Information spreading
.
Informing lakhs and crores of voters / activists about proposed-law-drafts is MUST NECESSARY and important step. And it is must to create a CAMPAIGN. But it is NOT same as campaign. And information spreading ALONE cant cause a CAMPAIGN.
.
So what do I mean by campaign (wrt to proposed law-draft)?
,
Campaign is where almost every voter / MP / elitemen feel that if these proposed law-drafts are not passed, then voters and activists may start working to REPLACE MPs and also replace elitemen. i.e. if election procedure is blocked.
.
eg Janlokpal movement was a CAMPAIGN which did go far, but not very far. Dhanvapasi will be campaign, which imo will go very far
.
Now both Janlokpal and DhanVapasi are elitemen sponsored campaign created to fool people. Our #Minerals3 campaign are different.
.
But what I mean to say is --- mere information spreading is NOT same as campaign.
.
eg say activists inform crores of voters about #Minerals3-law-draft. But if no activists ask each supporter to communicate to all his WhatsApp friends, that the supporter supports #Minerals3, then most will not know that #Minerals3 has sizeable number supporters. And so a CAMPAIGN or andolan will not start
.
------
.
so imo, our whole movement has 5 parts
.
(1) writing drafts
.
(2) informing voters about drafts - pamphlets, video, explanations, also informing about FAKE variations in drafts,. countering fake arguments against drafts, holding meetings, newspaper advt , whatsapp messages, FB status post etc
.
(3) campaign - asking law-draft supporters to create a CAMPAIGN / MOVEMENT / AANDOLAN for these law-drafts
.
------
.
And imo, campaign has 5 parts
,
(3a) all supporters communicate to PM that they want those specific law-drafts. I define a person as supporter of law-draft ONLY if he has communicated to PM to print the law-draft
.
(3b) each supporter informs all his friends, family members, colleagues via WhatsApp/FB etc that that supporter has asked PM to print these laws
.
(3c) some supporters of law-drafts start contesting elections and each supporter looks for election candidate who is most likely to print these law-draft
.
(3d) each supporter asks Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh to takeover -- if and only if candidates who had promised have won and are in majority and still havent printed these law-drafts in 24 hours
.
(3e) each supporter communicates to all his friends etc , that he has asked Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh to takeover -- if and only if candidates who had promised have won and are in majority and still havent printed these law-drafts in 24 hours
.
----
.
All these steps from (1) to (3c) i.e. all steps except (3d) and (3e) can work in parallel without previous step crossing any threshold. eg. imo, we should not put condition that "communication to PM will start only after 1 crore pamphlets have been distributed or at least 10 lakh have supported" . The first letter I wrote to PM to print 1999-version of TCP was in year 1999, when I was the only supporter of TCP !! (back then TCP had a different name and code was completely different). And my book 301.pdf written in year 2009, printed in dec-2012 has chapter "Letter to PM", and back then there were only a few tens supporters. So all steps from (1) to (3c) imo should start on day one.
.
I will discuss all this later, But point I want to make in THIS status post is --- CAMPAIGN STEP is step voter takes to create an andolan / movement and give message to PM/MPs/elitemen that "we voters may decide to replace you with other PM/MPs/elitemen if you decide not to print these law-drafts". Whereas spreading information about law-draft only means --- one activist/voter informs another activist/voter about plus points of a proposed-law-drafts. Strong campaign needs good information spreading,. But information spreading alone wont automatically create campaign = aandolan = movement.
