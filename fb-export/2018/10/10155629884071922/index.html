Why negative recall procedures didnt create Peru like instability in USA? Ans : Jury System !!
.
All recall procedures including the first one I had proposed in oct-1998 always used POSITIVE recall and never used negative recall. Negative recall is when voters vote to expel an expel position holder be judge or PM or police chief. Whereas positive recall means, that voters can approve any candidate along with existing position holder, and replacement of existing person in power happens when a candidate has 1% or 5% or 10% more approvals than existing person in position.
.
Many times, some 60% people vote and winner gets say 40% of those who voted. i.e. only 24% of all voters, So a sizeable portion of the remaining 76% doesnt like that winner and so getting signature of 51% is viable, And so negative recall can cause instability, and this was known since ages and ages.
.
But in recall procedures I have proposed from day one i.e.. oct-1998, I always proposed positive recall, i.e. replacement will NOT happen whether 51% or 67% or 75% oppose existing candidate, but will happen when alternative as 1% or 5% or 10% more approvals than both - 24% as well as current approval of the existing person in power. So UNNEEDED recalls wont happen in my proposed procedure code.
.
-----
.
But a question comes ---- USA had negative recall procedure in most districts/states. So why didnt it create instability?
.
Imo, the reason is Jury System . And second reason was absence of indirect taxes and presence of wealth tax. And another reason was multi-elections and recall over many positions.
.
The JurySys over District / State employees is strong. So corruption in those position is low. So elected district/state level official doesnt face a blame for staff's actions. And staff is less inefficient and less corrupt, and so decisions of people and apex leaders get carried out properly
.
Next reason is --- lack of indirect taxes and presence wealth tax.. USA has excise only on a few goods, there is no interstate tax, there is no service tax, though there is now state sales tax on sale of goods at final level. (And this state sales tax is now indeed creating many problems). Lack of indirect taxes prompted regimes to have wealth tax, which kept property prices low. (later because of a law that bank owned empty property isnt taxes, the property prices started rising). And low land prices and lack of indirect taxes kept real growth rate high and so population has lesser crisis and problems, and so lesser chances of recall.
.
And lastly, in USA, citizens directly elect Mayor, Police Chief, District Eductaion Officer etc and can recall them. So Mayor isnt blamed when police chief does something wrong and vice versa. In places like Peru, Mayor is recallable, and no one else is recallable, and he ends up facing expectations of fixing everyone and ensuring that all officers work properly !!! Same way, if in India , say RTR MLA comes and RTR Police Chief doesnt come, then MLA will face expectation of fixing police dept, and MLA cant fix police dept because he has no power and all public wrath due to wrong actions of a police officer will also get channelized against MLA !!
.
IOW, negative RTR procedure didnt create much damage in USA because of JurySys, absence of indirect taxes, presence of wealth tax, presence of state/district level referendum, multi-election etc
.
Many fake recallists claim that just because negative recall procedure in Peru created some instability, the positive recall procedure codes like RtrDeo3, JurySys3, Minerals3 etc will also worsen the situation in India !! In there analysis, these fake recallists hide the facts that Peru doesnt have strong or even namesake Jury System, it has negative recall procedure, doesnt have multi-election or multi-recall and no mention of tax system. And then to confuse the audience, they will pull some unimplementable ultra-hitech software proposal, claiming that unless govt officer FIRST start maintaining good high tech website, RtrDeo3, JurySys3 etc will ruin India !!! All this is done only to derail RTR movement.
.
Coming to point, USA had OTHER good laws due to which damage of negative recall remained low, and plus point of recall did come. But whole issue is inapplicable to RtrDeo3 etc drafts, because these drafts use POSITIVE recall and not negative recall procedure
