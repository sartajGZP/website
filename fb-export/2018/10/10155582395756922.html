Why good govt websites and expense reports will be pretty much useless in taking some MOST imp decisions
.
Many life and death issues in governance are core ideological. And voters need to electa person to take some of these important decisions. And here, voters need to guess what ideology/mentality he has, and then keep/replace him. And in many life-death issues, information on official websites or integrity in expense reporting will be least of needed points
.
eg consider level of MSLW ( = maths/science/law/weapon-use) education in children of poor. The elitemen would always want Education Minister and DEOs who would ruin maths/science/law/weapon education in poors' kids as far as possible. While commons would need to have a Minister/DEO who wishes to promote maximal maths/science/law/weapons education.
.
Now elitemen will always manage to find a fame hungry person, who is willing to ruin maths/science/law/weapons education of kids of poor. And fame hungry persons are always very systematic about expense reporting (eg Duratma Gandhi). Plus, he will get ample support, staff help and 100% while legal funds from elitemen to promote his campaigns.
.
And such fame hungry people are also always very prompt in ensuring that all details are given in books and disclosures correctly.
.
So if one look at BOOKS , be expenses books or other books, or websites that show these books, then books/websites of elitemen-agent will look very neat and clean. Whereas at core, that person will be completely defunct, fame hungry and anti-common, pro-elitemen
.

Here too, elitemen will manage to find a fame hungry person who is willing to support GST and find many creative arguments against wealth tax. This where TRUE ACTIVISTS and true activism helps and TCP can help. And this is where psuedo-activism will be also at highest !!!

The true activists will need to prepare proposals which improve MSLW education of poors' kids, and using TCP prove supporter-count of the proposals.
