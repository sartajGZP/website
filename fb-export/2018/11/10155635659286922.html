But lie-1 is important. Their claim that oxygen supply ended due to HIDDEN corruption is lie.

The oxygen supply didnt end because staff was stealing 2 out of 100 cyliners or because staff was paying for 100 cyliners for 98 delivered. The oxygen supply ended because company which was supposed to supply liquid oxygen in bulk in large tanks stopped supplying. That company stopped supllying because hospital stopped paying them for months and months. The payment had stopped because the hospital chairman was asking for too much bribes. The liquid oxygen supplying companies many notices to hospital, press, health ministers, apex MLAs,MPs, CM etc. So IT WAS OPENLY KNOWN TO ALL that hospital isnt paying for oxygen delivered.

Finally, the oxygen supplying company stopped sending oxygen.
.
But in court, the company was forced to give statement that he had never stopped supply !! WHY? because law says that even if a govt hospital doesnt pay, the vendor cannot stop services !!! The vendor can approach courts and collect payment, but vendor is committing serious crime if he stops supply. If the oxygen supplying company were to admit in court, that it had stopped supplying oxygen, then owner will face case under IPS section-304 etc w, which says "homicide due to negligence" and he can face 10 years in imprisonment !! And so oxygen supplier gave a statement in court that he never stopped oxygen supply !!!
