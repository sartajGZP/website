GoRaksha / GoSeva are most probable reason why India never had slave system since 1500 BC !!
.
India had caste system which was a big minus point of Indian society. But ALL other countries had slavery which India never had since 1500 BC. Imo, reason was -- use of bulls, but imo, that became possible due to Goseva / Goraksha
.
All other societies just slaughtered cows and bulls, And so they needed a huge low paid low skilled labor for farming and so they resorted to slavery.. But India mastered use of bulls for farming. And so there was almost no need for slavery
.
Cows and bulls are very intelligent and emotional beings. The bulls work poorly if they treated poorly and fellow bulls and cows are slaughtered. So Indian culture created beliefs that gives a lot of respect for cows and bulls, And this makes cows and bulls feel connected to humans around them, and makes them easy to use in farms.
.
So imo , reason ancient India didnt had to resort to slavery was because bulls were productive and easy to domesticate, and that was because culture installed beliefs of goraksha, goseva and also respecting cows and bulls.
