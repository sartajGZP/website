Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate

Sun Nov 11 2018 07:39:06 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

Why DhanVapasi claim that "DhanVapasi law-draft will enable govt to give Rs 20000 per year to all 135 crore adult Indians for FIFTY YEARS" is false? And why DhanVapasi will cause HUGE LOSSES to all Indians after all minerals/plots are sold away?
.
And why #MRCM = #Minerals3 is much better proposal?
.
------------
.
chapters
-----------
.
(1) What is DhanVapasi Nonsense Claim?
(2) One explanation why DhanVapasi Nonsense claim is false
(3) Second explanation why DhanVapasi claim is false
(4) How DVN is a huge NET LOSS after payments end?
(5) why MRCM is better than DVN?
(6) why MRCM is now must
(7) solution?
.
============
.
chap-1: What is DhanVapasi Nonsense Claim ?
----------------------------------------------------------
.
DhanVapasi Nonsense proposed law-draft DhanVapasi (see pdf draft link in first comment) proposes that .
.
(part-1) all minerals mines and all govt plots will be sold off (not rented, but SOLD OFF) . I oppose it -- my proposal is renting the govt plots and giving mineral mines on maximal royalty basis.
.
(part-2) And then proceeds will be equally distributed amongst all citizens of India --- I support this part with some small variations
.
Now CLAIM DhanVapasi makes is --- thru this sale , all 135 crore Indian citizens will get inflation adjusted rupees twenty thousand every year for next 50 years !!
.
Say Govt decides to give Rs 20000 per person per year to each of 135 crore persons , then, Govt will have to raise Rs 135 crore * Rs 20000 = Rs 27,00,000 crore = Rs 27 lakh crores every year...
.
So DhanVapasi says that every year some 2% to 3% of mineral mines and govt plots will be sold off, and this sale will be sufficient to generate Rs 27 lakh crore every year !!
.
This imo is a FALSE claim. I will explain how.
.
====================
.
(2) One explanation why DhanVapasi cant give Rs 20000 per year per person for even 20 years
.
(2.1) To raise Rs 27 lakh crores in first year, Dhanvapasi claims that selling 2% of Govt wealth will be sufficient.
.
(2.2) But moment govt sells even if 1% of mineral mines and plots, the prices of mineral mines and plots will fall below 25% of present value. The present value of mineral mines and plots is due to artificial shortage. And each 1% extra sale will further drive down the price. And each % sale will further drive down the price. And next year, prices will be further lower.
.
(2.3) And so in order to obtain Rs 27 lakh crores, govt will need to sell as much as 5% to 20% of the mineral mines and plots.
.
(2.4) So to obtain Rs 27 lakh crores, Govt may end up selling as much as 5% to 20% of the mines and plots. And so within 5 years to 20 years at most, Govt will run out of all mineral mines and plots !!
.
(2.5) And after that, the new private owners (most of whom will be usuk-elitemen) will create a cartel and will raise the mineral prices, raise land prices and raise land rent to an extent that ALL money that citizens of India got in 5 years to 20 years will get sucked back !!
.
my conclusions :
.
(2.a) IOW, DhanVapasi Nonsense may create some gains for 5 years to 20 years to poors. But once, all minerals and all plots are sold out, within next 10 years, the net losses will be HIGHER than all gains.
.
(2,.b) So if one takes 5 years to 10 years into account, then DhanVapasi may be gainful to poor. But over a 20 years to 30 years, DhanVapasi is a NET HUGE LOSS CAUSERS to all poors and all citizens of India !!!
.
===========
.
(3) A second explanation why DhanVapasi cant give Rs 20000 per year per person for even 20 years
.
(3.1) India's coal reserves are 300,000 million tonne. India's consumption including imports is 800 million including imports. Lets say we end imports and mine all coal we need. So at current rate, it would last for about 370 years.
.
(3.2) Currently, average price is Rs 6000 per tonne. Extraction cost plus transport is Rs 1000 per tonne. So maximal value of coal royalty can be is Rs 5000 per tonne. So total price of all coal reserves is Rs 1500,000,000 million i.e. Rs 1500,00,000 crores = Rs 1500 lakh crores. DhanVapasi document puts estimate at Rs 850 lakh crores. But I wont fault them for difference in estimate. Estimates on such volumes can always vary.
.
(3.3) But if Govt decides to sell all coal mines and reserves within 50 years, then that means Govt is selling mineral rights to 300,000/50 = 6000 million tonnes a year. Whereas market needs only 800 million tonnes. So coal royalty will fall drastically. And so govt will not get even Rs 500 per tonne of royalty or may end up getting even less.
.
(3.4) So if Govt decides to sell away all coal reserves within 50 years, then Govt will NOT get Rs 1500 lakh crores or but end up getting mere Rs 150 lakh crores or even less !!
.
(3.5) IOW, DhanVapasai expects to get Rs 850 lakh crore rupees from sale of all coal mines. But this estimates are based on CURRENT price where supply is 800 mt. If Govt starts selling coal reserves at 5 to 10 times this demand, then prices will fall and total proceeds will be much much less --- can be far far below even Rs 150 lakh crores.
.
(3.6) That was case with coal. Same is case with all minerals. If Govt plans to sell all mineral mines within 50 years, then prices will go down so low, that to get the stipulated amount, more amount will have to be sold and so mines will get sold off in just 5 years to 20 years.
.
My conclusions:
.
(3.a) All prices in DhanVapasi document are calculated at present market rates, where supply is so low that supply will last for 100 years to 400 years. But if sale is to be done in 50 years, then effective supply goes more than 2 times to 8 times. If supply doubles, then prices may go down by much more than 50%. And if supply goes more than 4 times, then prices will drop much below 25%.
.
(3.b) So in order to get Rs 20000 per year per Indian citizen, Govt will have to sell much more than 2% of its assets --- as high as 5% to 20%. IOW, govt will have to sell away all its assets within 5 years to at most 20 years to raise a sum of Rs 27 lakh crore per year
.
===============
.
(4) How DhanVapasi is a huge NET LOSS 5 years after sale of govt plots/mines end?
.
For 5 years to 20 years, DhanVapasi will enable all Indian citizens to get Rs 20000 per year. Fine. But in these 5 years to 20 years, all mineral mines and all govt plots of India will get sold off to private and mostly foreign elitemen. So after all sale ends, these desi/videshi elitemen will form a cartel and reduce the supply of minerals, and increase rents or selling price of the land. So price of minerals and land can literally go 5 times to 10 times, and so all the money obtained during 5 years to 20 years of sale period will get SUCKED BACK within next 5 years !!
.
So DhanVapasi is indeed all profits for some 5 years to 20 years, But after that, it is huge loss. So much that when losses and gains are added, DhanVapasi , 10 years after sale ends,m will be a huge net LOSS MAKER for all citizens of India, except super wealthy who have bought all mines and govt plots
.
=====================
..
(5) Why MRCM is far far better?
.
In MRCM, mineral mine is NOT sold off, but given to private or govt company on maximal royalty basis. i.e. say coal's market value is Rs 6000 per tonne and mining cost including interest, transport and normal profits is Rs 1000 per tonne. Then using good auction methods, it is possible to get royalty of Rs 5000 or at least Rs 4500. And this will come till coal comes out of mineral mines, till minerals last or mineral has value - whichever first. So proposed-law-draft Minerals3 is better than DhanVapasi in this respect.
.
Likewise, if govt plot is rented, then rent for will come for decades and centuries. Whereas, if all govt plots are sold off with goal to raise at least Rs 5000 per year per person, then all plots will get sold off within 5 years to 20 years. And after that, the new desi/videshi land owners will form cartel and take the land prices and land rent to sky high level - make it 5 times to 10 times the price. So all money that came in 5 years to 20 years will get sucked back !! And after that poverty of commons will increase
.
So MRCM is RENTING the govt plots for 1 year to maximum 25 years, but no more. And hence its better proposal than DhanVapasi Nonsense.
.
IOW, in both cases, minerals as well as plots, MRCM is far far better than DhanVapasi Nonsense.
.
===============
.
(6) why is MRCM now must
.
I started publicizing MRCM proposal in oct-1998, and except for 10-20 activists across India, most activists decided to ignore it and not forward it systematically, Most activists disliked the proposal that poor should be given money for free !!! And many activists preferred law-drafts like TcpIndia7, JurySys3, WealthTax2 etc. Working on those law-drafts was not problem. But their decision to complete ignore MRCM has created this DhanVapasi disaster.
.
Now all pro-India-activists have no option but to support MRCM. Because DhanVapasi Nonsense is making promise of giving Rs 100,000 per family per year, and in the process it will sell away all mineral mines and govt plots to usuk-elitemen.
.
So now do you have any proposed-law-draft that can appeal more than DhanVapasi, to 60 crore poor voters of India? And a list of campaign steps that can match DhanVapasi's campaign? IMO, only answer is Minerals3 aka MRCM. But you may look for any answer you wish.
.
So if you wish to stop DhanVapasi, then imo, you consider MRCM seriously
.
================
.
(7) Solution?
.
Solution I propose is that activists will need to take TWO steps
.
(7.1) activists need to inform all about how DhanVapasi is a NET LOSS causer after 10 years to 25 years, and
.
(7.2) activists need to inform all about proposed Minerals3-law-draft. and work to raise a CAMPAIGN for Minerals3-law-draft aka MRCM-law-draft.
,
And IMO, second set of steps i.e. MRCM-campaign is more important.
.
So what can you do to raise MRCM campaign? To start with, you can vote at https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 , and you can also take steps mentioned in post "Campaign steps I propose for #Minerals3" at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155651225586922 .....
