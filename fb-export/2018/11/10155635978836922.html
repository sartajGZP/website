Campaign (movement, andolan) steps are NOT publicization !! Publicization is must for campaign steps to happen. But campaign steps are something else.
.
So what do I mean by campaign steps or aandolan or movement, in context of campaign steps for RTR/Jury law-drafts?
.
Proposed campaign steps have imo three parts
.
(1) steps that N = 5 crore or 50 crore voters need to take, in YOUR onion, to create an ALTERNATIVE to PM/MPs if they make it clear that they wont pass the law !!!
.
(2) steps ensure that existing PM/MPs do pass the laws.
.
(3) how do propose to communicate these steps to N crore voters and train them to take these steps?
.
----
.
