Why Sardar Patel's 182 meter tall statue is costed 8 times to 10 times the 128 meter tall Buddha statue ? Ans : (1) foreign imports (2) corruption of Modiji and RSS-workers (RSS = BJP)
.
First, I support this the SVP statue. And we need 4 more such statues ---
.
(a) Rashtrapita Subhashchandra Bose who was MOST imp person behind freedom (not Mahaduratma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Ghazi, SVP or Shashtriji etc),
.
(b+c) Mahatma Bhagat Singh and Mahatma Udham Singh --- due to whose works British didnt dare to kill Rashtrapita Bose when he was in India, and
.
(d) Baba Saheb who against the wish of Jawaharlal Ghazi, SVP, Shashtriji and Rajendra Prasad forced all to accept 'one man one vote' and caste based reservation, the two factors that has saved India and Hinduism.
.
So what about costs of this statues and 4 more?
.
Well, if the SVP statue was 100% made in India and made by Indians, and corruption was as low as China, then cost of statue would NOT have been Rs 3000 crore, but much below Rs 700 crores !!! So 4 more statues will not cost Rs 12000 crore but less than Rs 3000 crores.
.
Pls look at following two wiki links on Budda's statue in China and India's SVP statue
.
----
.
see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Temple_Buddha
.

.

.
Buddha statue is 128 meters and was made in year 2008, and statue cost was $18 million and total project cost was $55 million. And a huge amount of copper was used to give it a golden finish look from outside. So much copper was NOT used in SVP statue. And SVP statue is 50% taller i.e. 182 meters. And yet statue cost is 10 times i.e. $180 million and total cost is some 8 times i.e. about $400 million.
.
Inflation of 10 years, and 50% more height will atmost make the statue twice expensive. And imo less than twice, because Buddha Statue uses huge amount of copper and has golden color finish.
.
The cost of SVP statue if due to corruption of Sri Modiji, RSS-leaders and RSS-workers. That isnt new. In Ahmedabad, BRTS busstands are costing Rs 10 crore each, not counting land cost. In Rs 10 crore, one can make three grand 300 sq yard 4 BHK bungalows (not counting land cost) with batthrooms, plumbing, electricity, tiles and paint and all. But thanks to sri Modiji, RSS-leaders and RSS-workers, all we get for Rs 10 crores is a busstand which is just elevated platform with roof and 1-2 rooms. The reason is commons sense --- Sri Modiji, RSS-leaders and RSS-workers as all as corrupt as SoGa, congress-leaders and congress-workers.
.
So first, statue is a good thing, Cost went high due to corruption and foreign imports. Solution is NOT to oppose statue, but see why costs went so high and how can reduce imports.
