This is nonsense. In JurySys3-law-draft I have proposed, (see link in first comment) , each Grand Juror can use his own DISCRETION and say YES/NO on whether case should be started or not. And a Grand Juror an decide based on statements of either parties or material evidence or based on his reasonable guesses. This is because , many times, statements and evidences come only after trial starts.

Also, in my proposed-law-draft, if Grand Jurors have reasonable doubt, they can issue non binding "request" to parties for polygraph or brain mapping or narcotest. Polygraph can be fooled, but not always, If 10 people are involved in a crime, then at least 6 to 7 will not be able to fool polygraph. And brain mapping cant be fooled by even 2 out of 100, Brain mapping isnt useful in forcing a person to tell truth,. But brain mapping is very much useful in knowing that person is hiding truth or speaking lies. And if one party accepts the request for polygraph or brain mapping and comes clean, and other party refuses the request even for polygraph, then Grand Jurors can use this to decide whether Jury Trial should be ordered or not.
.
And not just my proposed JurySys3-law-draft, across world, Grand Jurors as well as judges have ordered Jury Trials or judge trials based on reasonable doubt, based on chain of events, on mere statements and in absence of material evidence. This also results in spurious trials many times, but chances of spurious trials are high in judge system due to nexuses judges have with lawyers and elitemen.
