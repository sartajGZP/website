Fight between Modiji and Urjit Patel is about Rs 9 lakh crore reserves that RBI has . Its all about money only !!! Why imo PM is wrong.
.
RBI "earns" income profits by printing notes, but also ends up spending a lot of money in paper currency management and cheque clearing system. Some profits are kept in form of reserves. And RBI has been transferring all its net profits after reserves to GoI since 1951.
.
The reserve is in form of gold, forex and some cash deposited by banks. Since ages, RBI keeps about 12% of assets as reserve in form of gold, forex etc. It has been about 12% since ages, and except It fell down only during 1962 war.
.
The issue is that --- no previous PM except in 1962 war ever asked RBI to spend this reserve,
.
So why is Modiji asking Urjit Patel to hand over that cash to govt?
.
The reason is --- Govt finances are in bad shape, despite huge petrol taxes. And Modiji wants to spend a lot of money in public schemes to win may-2019 elections
.
Now what if reserves are spent and then crisis comes?
.
IMO, these reserves should be kept for crisis. If PM wants to spend money, he is welcome to reduce expenses and/or raise taxes. But Rs 9 lakh crores of reserves are needed for unforseen emergencies
