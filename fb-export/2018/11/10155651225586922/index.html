Campaign steps I propose for #Minerals3
.
This status is NOT about plus / minus points of #Minerals3 aka MRCM. This status is applicable AFTER you have decided to bring #Minerals3 in India. To get information about MRCM = Minerals3 , pls see first comment
.
(aside but important : My first request to all is --- pls WRITE list of CAMPAIGN steps you suggest that crores of voters should take to ensure that PM either prints this law, or a replacement PM emerges.
.
My second request is --- pls write steps you suggest that hundreds or lakhs or crores of voters who support MRCM, should take, to communicate to PM that those voters want PM to print MRCM law. Pls note -- many activists suggest that there is no need for voters to communicate to PM, whereas, imo, voters' communication to PM on what law-draft voter wants is PRIMARY.
.
And for each step you mention, pls also mention - how many crores of voters in India have SKILLS to take that step. .
.
Because activism is always about creating better campaign steps NOT just about finding faults in proposed campaign steps, And thats true for all cases in general --- activism is always about finding better solutions, not about citing flaws in existing or proposed solutions. )
.
========================
.
CAMPAIGN STEPS I SUGGEST - with reasons
------------------------------------------
.
Following are some of the campaign steps I propose for #Minerals3-law-drafts to the SUPPORTERS :
.
(A) Imo, supporters must communicate to PM, that he wants PM to print #Minerals3 law-draft
.
If a person claims he supports Minerals3 and doesnt wish to communicate to PM that he wishes PM to print Minerals3, then I dont take him as a supporter.
.
Now question is --- what are the exact physical means to communicate to PM?
.
THIS IS MOST IMPORTANT --- and so I will repeat. What are the exact PHYSICAL MEANS DO I (AND YOU) SUGGEST TO COMMUNICATE TO PM?
.
I suggest three means - postcard, vote on newindia and tweets
.
(A.1) so imo, any voter who claims to be supporter of MRCM aka Minerals3 must first communicate to PM via postcard that he wants PM to print Minerals3-law-draft link to my postcard is https://fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155617604576922 . If a MRCM = Minerals3 "supporter" has not sent postcard to PM to print Minerals3, then I dont take him as a supporter.
.
(A.1.1) The postcard must have newindia dot in link where Minerals3 draft has been uploaded, namely https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 .
.
(A.1.2) The voter need not out his signature on the postcard, but must write his voter#, name and cityname,

(A.1.3) Over 80 crore voters are in position to send such postcard.
.
(A.2) The supporter can vote YES on this Minerals3 proposal at https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 . About 25 crore voters in India have skills to vote there. If a person can knows how to use OTP ans has not voted on https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 or a newindia link which has SAME law-draft-pdf , I dont call him supporter.
.
(A.3) The supporter can tweet "@PmoIndia #Minerals3 https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 " . About 5 crore voters across India have ability to send tweet
.
(B) Imo, supporters must communicate to all, to whom he is PERSONALLY known, that he wants PM to print #Minerals3 law-draft. He need not communicate to strangers or people to whom he is not know. And he should also display on facebook so that newcomers know that he has asked PM to print #Minerals3
.
So what are the physical means he should use to communicate to people to whom he is known?
.
(B.1.1) He can send scan of postcard to all to whom he is known via WhatsApp. He may also send his video putting the postcard is postbox.
.
(B.1.2) He can put scan/video of postcard on FB in PUBLIC ALBUM named as " Proofs- I asked PM\CM to print imp laws via postcard"
.
(B.1.3) He can put link https://newindia.in/causes/Minerals3 in FB album named as "Proof I asked PMO to print laws via newindia"
.
(B.1.4) He can put whole Minerals3's description from etc from https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155624176761922 in FB album "Law drafts I support"
.
(B.1.5) He can make a video on Minerals3 and send it over WhatsApp and also share in FB in album "Law drafts I support"
.
IMO, (A) and (B) are sufficient
.
Imo, as more and more voter take steps-A and steps-B, the chances that replacement PM will print the Minerals3-law-draft or existing PM will print the Minerals3-law-draft will increase. ..
