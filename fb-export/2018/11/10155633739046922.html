We recallists need to grow up --- from *(law-drafts + pamphlets) to (law-drafts + pamphlets + campaign steps), because we need to decide campaign steps, and no one else will do for us.
.
Many recallists insist on confining to law-draft analysis and some have grown up i.e. along with law-draft analysis , they also work for publicizing i.e. pamphlets, newspaper advt etc
.
But each recallist MUST answer the question now -- what does he want 5 crore or 50 crore voters to do to ensure that PM/MPs do print these law-drafts in Gazette?
.
This is important next step.
.
eg in year 2007, Apple could capture a big smartphone market with iPhone-1. But if apple had stuck itself with iPhone-1 and never moved to iPhone2 to iPhone10, it would have lost all the share to samsung.
.
Same way. we did move quite a bit ahead with proposition that "law-drafts are important, kalki puja is useless". But now many seem to be stuck with (a) ignore all law drafts, propose only TCP and (b) ignore the need for campaign , publicize only law-drafts.
.
Many who show tendency that "ignore campaign steps , focus only on law-draft publication", are less bad than those who say "ignore all law-drafts , focus only non TCP".
.
Law-drafts make campaign creation easy, but provided one does work to create a campaign. If one wishes that others should create campaign, then others are not going to show up
.
-----
.
The first step in campaign is blue print. Just as "proposed law-draft" provides a proposed blue print for lakhs of officers and crores of voters, the "proposed campaign steps" give a blue print for lakhs of activists and crores of voters.
.
To start with --- ask the question --- what do YOU want 5 crore or 50 crore or 80 crore voters to do so that PM/MPs are forced to print the proposed laws or get replaced , and new PM/MPs are more likely to print these law-drafts.. Pls write a status in album titled as "(A016) campaign steps".
