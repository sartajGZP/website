Using Slovakia 'example' (a country of population mere 55 lakhs, which was highly advances even in 1910s and 1991) to claim that good websites are more important than recall/Jury procedures , and making port wrt to India !!!
.
The nonsense of pseudo-recallists will not find any limit. Many times, they will take NEGATIVE recall procedures and signature based recall procedures of Peru to show that RtrDeo3-draft, JurySys3-draft etc will ruin India. Next, they will pick a country of population mere 55 lakh, Solavakia to show that "good websites can fix society" and "good website can come without recall/jury" to show that Rtr/Jury systems are not needed !!
.
Well, first I request all to see data of Slovakia in year 1920, 1991 and 2010 before good website started. Way back in 1920, Slovakia was a highly industrialized society. WHY?
.
Now that is in a way a contradiction to my central RTR/Jury hypothesis. As per my Rtr/Jury thesis, RTR/Jury or at least recruitment by written exams is must to create a US-like industrialized society. So how could Slovakia become an industrialized society way back in 1910s?
.
Well, it was industrialized, but NOT US-like.
.
The real industrialization started in UK in year 1200 AD after JurySys came in two phases - Coronor's Jury System in 950 AD and "Trial by Peers" in Magna Carta in around 1200 AD. So manufacturing in UK started improved,
.
Now whole Europe was facing massive aggression from Turkey and Arabs.Turkey had become powerful due to "gathering" prpcedure of Islam. And it had captured Greece and many parts of Eastern Europe. And Turkey was advancing.
.
So enable Eastern Europe to fight back, UK, Germany etc had no option but to give weapons to all Eastern European countrues (back then, they were kingdoms). And also teach them how to repair them and make some parts. Thus foundations of industry started in Slovakia due to UK/Germany's need to stop Turkey. And later, Slovakia improved manufacturing with trainning it got from Germany and UK
.
But Slovakia could never become a good weapon manufacturing country. That is evident from fact that when Hitler's Army attacked Slovakia, they had to surrender within a few days.
.
Later, when USSR took over Czek and Slovakia, USSR provided a massive maths/science education base. For all plus and minus points of USSR and Communism, whereever Communism went or whereever USSR gained influence, it massively improved maths/science education. Pls google on top 100 mathematicians --- a large number of them come from Hunary, Czek and Slovakia. And USSR also promoted recruitment by written exams in basic entries in Govts, though later promotions were all discretionary.
.
So Slovaia was highly educated country in year 1940. And by 1991, it had population became as educated as western countries, with maths/science base better than USA. But due to absence of JurySys/RTR, it could not become a good manufacturer of core engineering. So it had to confine to services and providing accessories.
.
Next, Slovakia never had multi-language problem. It never had ethnic crisis. we see in India in every city. Czekoslovakia did have ethinic problems, but never had violence or street level fights. There were two linguistic groups, which peacefully separated with a referendum without throwing a stone, and without anyone losing any life or limb or property. The govt bought wealth tax above market prices of the people who wanted to shift from Czek to Slovenia and vice versa.
.
------------------
.
IOW, it is nonsense to say that "good website clauses" that came in around 2010 to 2015 make remarkable difference in Slovanei. First, Slovenia does NOT have independent weapon manufacturing industry and so usuk-elitemen are NOT working to destroy it. usuk-elitemen are also not working to change religion there because they already have western religions. And country population was highly educated - more educated than usuk, with better maths/science base, in 1991 itself.
.
And presence of "good website" is cited as important factor in "improvement" by many anti-recallists or psedo-recallist (p-recallist is anti-rtr person who falsely poses as recallists , and then creates a false negative campaign against RTR-drafts).
.
-------------
.
IOW, Slovenia is better than India, and slovenia has no RTR/Jury. But thats because it was far better than India, even in 1920 and far far better in 1991 in terms of industry as well as maths/science education. And Slovakia was satellite to Germany in 1910 to 1945, then became satellite of USSR from 1945 to 1991 and then became satellite of usuk-elitemen. It never even tried to become a full weapon manufacturer, and had no "need" to protect its religion/culture etc
.
And such claims are NOT new. Since 2004,. there has been movement in India which is claiming that "TRANSPARENCY is more important than EMPOWERMENT, and once we have TRANSPARENCY , we dont need TRANSPARENCY , and TRANSPARENCY can come without EMPOWERMENT". Thats why, we have International organizations like TRANSPARENCY International, but no EMPOWERMENT international !! And all academicians write volumes and volumes on TRANSPARENCY and not on EMPOWERMENT . And thats why both Lokpal and Janlokpal law-drafts have several clauses on WEBSITES and TRANSPARENCY , and none on EMPOWERMENT of the votes.
.
Entire RTI movement and Lokpal/Janlokpal movements came from that movement only, Basically, RTR/Jury talks about EMPOWERMENT of voters, and the movement assumes that TRANSPARENCY will AUTOMATICALLY start improving the day EMPOWERMENT comes using Rtr/Jury clauses. And so RTR/Jury movement makes REAL EMPOWERMENT by Rtr/Jury as primary issue and MRCM/WealthTax as equally important to fix economy. Whereas Transparency people always try to belittle RTR/Jury and even MRCM/WealthTax, and focus on websites, disclosures, publicizing govt contracts etc.
.
But Slovakia example PROVES that without Jury/RTR. the country will always remain as satellite of some major country. A small country can prosper despite being a satellite --- say Singapore or Hong Kong or Macau or Slovakia etc.
.
And btw, did Slovekia used 1 page draft to improve its websites? Or is 1 page draft ever possible to force offficer to create a good website? Answer to both questions is - NO. I will later explain why 1 page RTR draft and 10 page Jury draft is sufficient. But why a good website law will need 100s of pages.
.
IOW, the anti-recallists (or psuedo-recallists) have all these TACTICS. One tactic is to claim --- see transparency and good website can come without RTR even in large non-satellite countries like India , by giving examples of small and SATELLITE countries like singapore or slovakia. And next, their claims will be "transparanecy is sufficient to make India into heaven, and so RTR/Jury are not needed, and activists should focus only on good website clauses" !! .
.
All these campaigns are funded by Transpancy International and such nefarious organizations only.
.

.

Pseudo-activists will always give examples or SATELLITE countries like singapore or slovakia, and claim that "see RtrDeo3 , JurySys3 etc are NOT needed, and will ruin India if they are printed in India" !!
