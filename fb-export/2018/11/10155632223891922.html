Indo Japan $75 billion currency swap -- what it means ? And why it is not a bad or good news, but just routine news?
.
In simple language, it means a low interest loan and nothing more. And it also means that interest will apply only on forex amount India takes from Japan and not whole $75 billion. IOW, if GoI opts not to take any forex, then defaco burden will be zero
.
But the contract PDF is NOT uploaded on website of FinMin or RBI (at least, I could NOT find it).. And I am sure that none of Modi-andhbhagats who claim that this currency swap is a historical achievement has read that Indo-Japan contract.
.
What that contract has, one hing is sure from basic defintion of currency swap ---- whatever forex India takes from Japan, India has to return that forex back to Japan when contract ends. So key question is --- whatever forexr we get, we will blow it away in importing cars, mobile phones and petrol. And India isnt making anything worth exportable except buffalo meat. So how the hell we will return this forex?
.
So all in all, it is a low interest loan and nothing more. And basic problem remains --- India's exports are NOT rising and imports are rising, and non-petrol imports are rising. So forex situation will worsen. Currency swap only postpones the repatriation crisis and will make it bigger when it comes.
.
