My answers to the key questions in PSU banking
.
I had posted some key questions at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10155026325831922 .
.
PLS SEE ABOVE POST, and write YOUR answers before you see my answers !!
.
Many moons ago, Aristotle told me - in politics, answer the questions before you ask questions.
.
So I will answer my questions.
.
(Q1) Despite lower interest, why did lakhs and crores of depositors kept money in Govt banks ?
MY ANSWER : people in India had TOTAL faith that their principal / interest is 200% safe in govt bank and not 100% safe in private bank and so they deposited money in govt bank despite lower rate of interest
.
--
.
(Q2a + Q2b) Did I hear statements in society that "money in PSU bank is safe" ? Did I hear statements that "money in private or co-op bank is also safe"?
MY ANSWER : I have always heard from elders, other depositors as well as PSU bank managers that "100% money in govt bank is 200% safe, while money in private bank is NOT safe". I heard it from countless elders and people from all walks of lives - from vegetable selling illiterates to PhDs in real sciences.
.
---
.
(Q3a + Q3b) Why didnt Govt save all private banks' depositors? Why didnt Govt allowed PSU banks to default on deposits and saved their deposits?
MY ANSWER : Govt wanted people to have COMPLETE faith in PSU banks. And so Govts did not let any PSU bank collapse and saved all of the deposits thru any means. But Govt never felt that obligations towards private banks. And so Govts allowed them to sink when burden of saving was too huge.
.
---
.
(Q4) whom do I blame or hold responsible for this depositors' faith in Govt banks?
MY ANSWER : Citizens' faith in PSU bank is due to statements, actions and deliberate inaction of Govt as a whole. I hold Govt as a whole responsible for this faith.
.
---
.
(Q5) what is the max AMOUNT insured by Govt in PSU bank (not private bank but PSU bank) since 1950s?
MY ANSER : Infinity - even if one deposits in PSU bank is Rs 1000 crore, it is imo insured by Govt’s unspoken unwritten promise.
.
---
.
(Q6) What was the max amount insured by Govt in private bank
MY ANSWER : rupees one lakh only
