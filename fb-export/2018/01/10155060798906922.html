Derivable facts vs Underivable facts , Provable vs Unprovable facts and Refutable vs Irrefutable facts - stating them as facts or opinions.
.
There are be certain facts, but they are NOT Derivable in existing legal/technological setup, And sometimes, they are Derivable, but not provable/refutable or unprovable/irrefutable for the other person.

imo if anyone makes such UNDERIVABLE aka UNPROVABLE fact as his OPINIONS or BELIEFS should be welcomed as non-dishonest., but anyone who puts those statements as FACTS should be marked as dishonest person. This notion imo helps in activism and debating with hostile adversary before an audience.
.

.
chap-1 : Derivable facts vs Underivable facts
.
Consider following statements
.
(S1) over 80% judges in India are nepotic, corrupt or nexused
(S2) only between 60% and 80% judges are nepotic etc
(S3) only between 40% and 60% judges are nepotic etc
(S4) only between 20% and 40% judges are nepotic etc
(S5) less than 20% judges are nepotic etc
.
Now one and only one statement is fact i.e. true. And others are false i.e. lies
.
But is it possible to ever know the truth? There are 20000 Lower Court judges , 1000 High Court judges and 30 Supremes. And if one adds alive retired judges - add some 20000, 3000 and 100 to these numbers. Now even if you ask question to all judges, will judges give right answer? Will any judge who is nepotic going to admit that he nepotic? One way is to take narcotest. But such test isnt legal as per today's law. And no one has money etc to do narcotest on 1000s of judges and retired judges.
.
IOW, there is NO known viable way to know the truth.

Now consider five persons D1 to D5 who says
(D1) it is fact that over 80% judges in India are nepotic etc
(D2) it is fact that only 60% to 80% judges are nepotic etc
(D3) it is fact that only 40% to 60% judges are nepotic etc
(D4) it is fact that only 20% and 40% judges are nepotic etc
(D5) it is fact that less than 20% judges are nepotic etc
.
How did D1 to D5 derive the facts when such facts are simply NOT derivable or even provable?
.
Now consider five persons H1 to H5 who says
(H1) it is my belief that over 80% judges in India are nepotic etc
(H2) it is my belief that only 60% to 80% judges are nepotic etc
(H3) it is my belief that only 40% to 60% judges are nepotic etc
(H4) it is my belief that only 20% and 40% judges are nepotic etc
(H5) it is my belief that less than 20% judges are nepotic etc
.
Each person here is NOT dishonest. He is simply stating his BELIEF or OPINION. Whereas each person D1 to D5 is dishonest. He knows that statements he is speaking are NOT provable or even derivable.
.
Now consider following statements
.
(S11) Over 80% voters in India consider caste over merit
(S12) Between 60% and 80% voters consider caste over merit
(S13) Between 40% and 60% voters consider caste over merit
(S14) Between 20% and 40% voters consider caste over merit
(S15) Less than 20% voters consider caste over merit
.
And consider persons D11 to D15 who .say
(D11) it is fact that over 80% voters vote by caste and not merit
(D12) it is fact that only 60% to 80% voters vote by caste ...
(D13) it is fact that only 40% to 60% voters vote by caste ....
(D14) it is fact that only 20% to 40% voters vote by caste. ...
(D15) it is fact that less than 20% voters vote by caste. ...

And consider persons H11 to H15 who .say
(H11) it is my belief that over 80% voters vote by caste and not merit
(H12) it is my belief that only 60% to 80% voters vote by caste ...
(H13) it is my belief that only 40% to 60% voters vote by caste. ...
(H14) it is my belief that only 20% to 40% voters vote by caste. ...
(H15) it is my belief that less than 20% voters vote by caste. ...
.
Now a voter has several factors in mind before he goes to voting. And a simple question like "do you consider caste" gives no conclusion. And what if voter saw all candidates and meritless? Or what is voter didnt know about merit levels of the candidates?
.
IOW, given the subjectivity and complexity of the question, it is impossible to know the truth.
.
So statements (S1) to (S5) are what I call as UNDERIVABLE facts.
.
