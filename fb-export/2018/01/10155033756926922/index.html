When discussing with FRDI with pro-FRDI-folks, pls discussion to confine to bailin
.
The real and only reason why SoMoKe want FRDI is to bring bailin nonsense. and FRDI has other 140 pages of text along with bailin. So when discussing FRDI law, what I saw is that congress/rss/aap workers will go on and on and on and talk about everything but say nothing about bailin !! And then they will say --- see FRDI is harmless !!
.
Clauses other than bailin in FRDI law are neutral i.e. niether harmful nor harmless. eg FRDI sets up a new central govt body called FRC i.e. Financial Resolution Corporation. Well, nothing good or bad, because today we have RBI which does that task and if one more body is setup, then there is nothing good or bad or outstanding, Next FRC will have regulatory power over banks -- well so do FinMin and RBI. Again nothing wrong or good. FRC will be committee of 15 men appointed by PM --- well someone has to appoint people in Govt and all chains eventually end with PM or MPs or SCCj. So again, there is nothing outstanding or new or different.

IOW, there is pages and pages of nothing new, nothing good and nothing harmful
.
But inside the FRDI law is bailin clauses, and thats what is real part. It says that with permission of FRC can trim down the fixed deposits even in PSU bank !!

And next part is congress/rss/aap workers will hide the fact that as of today, since 1950s, Govts had given unpsoken commitment of giving FULL protection to PSU FDs upto any amount be Rs 1 lakh or Rs 10000 crore. They cleverly hide this fact, and immidiately say that "existing DIC insured only Rs 100,000 and FRDI will have higher limit and so despite bailin, FDRI is improvement" !! We need to inform all citizens and activists that congress/rss/aap workers and SoMoKe are liars --- as of now IMPLICIT Govt protection over PSU bank deposits is infinite.
.
So when discussing FRDI with congress/rss/aap liars, pls confine to bailin and fact that as of now, implicit FD protection in PSU bank is infinite and not Rs 100,000. Other than that, there is nothing to discuss about FRDI
.
As per solution - solution is NARROW banking or SAFE banking. SoMoKe and CoRaAp-workers oppose safe banking because safe banking will mean that the elitemen such Ambani, Ruia etc will not get bank money to plunder. These elitemen give part of bank money to SoMoKe and CoRaAp and so that will also end. So SoMoKe and CoRaAp oppose safe banking or narrow banking. Thats one more point we ineed to inform voters about
