Using someone as camel to break the castle's gate
.
In Gujarati, there is a saying "using someone as camel to break the gate". Here is history behind this saying
.
Many moons ago, kings used to form castle to protect key parts of their kingdoms. And castle would have strong walls and gates.
.
The gates would be weaker parts. And enemy will try to break gates than walls.
.
Two methods used to break the gate was
.
(1) Use elephant to crack the gate or the wooden log which locks the gate
.
(2) Use a huge wooden log, and men will carry that log and ram into the gate
.
So to make gate breaking difficult, kings' engineers started putting large long sharp nails - as long as 3-4 feet long over the gate. So elephant which tries to break the gate will get hurt by the nails, and will die or give up.
.
And if men are running towards the gate with wooden log, and if that log hits a nail instead of gate, then wooden log will get rupture
.
So enemy starts using camels. HOW?
.
The camel will be placed in from of gate. And enemy's soldier will hold camel tightly with ropes so that he cant even move. The elephant will now press the camel, and the nails of the castle gate will piercecamel and elephant will not get hit by the nails. So elephant presses camel, nails piece the camel, camel dies, but via camel's body and bones, the force imparted by elephant on camel gets transmitted to gate, and gate will start wrecking
.
The trick was effective. But some castle owners also figured out a way to deal with this. They installed buckets filled with acid etc. So when elehpant comes near gate, the castle's soldiers will start throwing this buckets on elephants, and that will hurt elephant and/or mahavat.
.
So some enemy invented one more trick
.
The camel underwent a training program
.
The camel will be kept hungry and given some liquor. Then camel will be shown a curtain which has picture of castle and gate. Then trainers will pinch camel with spears and make came run towards the curtain. Moment camel touches the gate on the curtain, camel will be given some food. And harder the camel hits the curtain, more food camel will get. And hitting curtain wont hurt the camel at all.
.
So camel will be trained to think "let me hit the gate hard, there is no damage, and harder I hit the gate, more better food I will get"
.
Now on the actual war day, the hungry drunk camel is put before the castle gate. Due to his past training, camel thinks that if he runs fast and hits the gate hard, he will get food. But this time, it is no picture on curtain, but a real hard gate with long iron nails. And this will be the end of the camel.
.
This is how Gujarati saying that "using him as camel to break the gate" come into existence.
