Concepts of implicit promises made by Govt, and “abandoned” laws
.
In my worldview, the relation between Govt and citizen isnt just WRITTEN laws. It also includes UNWRITTEN DEFACTO UNSPOKEN publicly known promises that Govts had given to citizens since decades and decades. These promises are not mentioned in any written law. And these were NOT promises given by leaders in their election campaign. These are unwritten promises.
.
Now many times, many citizens conveniently say "oh !! show me the law where GoI made such and such promises" !! And they will discard all unspoken promises that Govt has given since decades. And in many cases, they will show a law text, that was defacto abandoned and unenforced since long back, and say “see, here is the law.”
.
And concept of unwritten promises isnt new. For that matter, UK has written laws, but no written constitution. Hence, UK has no WRITTEN fundamental rights !! That doesnt mean that UK doesnt have fundamental rights. In fact, except USA, fundamental rights are most respected in UK, more than any Western country or any Eastern country across world. Likewise, before “right to life" was written in law, did Govts have unlimited power to kill? NO. So unwritten promises made by Govt has existed since time immemorial.
.
In most cases, written text was added AFTER implicit promises came into existence. So there has always been time when promises were formed and remained unwritten for a time, and then they were written down to reduce ambiguity. Despite this, there are "intellectuals" who in order to support FRDI go to extent of saying that - there is NO concept of unspoken promises between Govt and citizens across world across time !! All I say to them - lets agree to disagree. There are times when discussions are neither needed nor useful. Decisions are useful, not discussions.
.
Likewise, imo, there has always been concept of “abandoned written law”. i.e. many laws exist on paper, but Govts didn’t implement them or even sent notices and public legitimacy also sided with non implementation
.
So imo relation between govt and citizen includes written laws, but excludes laws that Govt has defacto abandoned and deliberately unenforced since long back. IOW, imo, it is unethical for men in Govt to suddenly pullout a written law that was defacto abandoned long back, and say “see, this was the law”.
.
And when a written law comes that deals with promise, that implicit promise ends, unless the written law fully captures it. IOW, an implicit unspoken promise is not eternal – it will end when written law ends it or replaces it
