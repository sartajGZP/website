Doxastic Logic and "Doxastic way to discuss proposed law-drafts and campaign methods and politics"
.
DL = Doxastic Logic means applying logic on beliefs. See wiki page on Doxastic Logic. There are many ways Doxastic Logic is applied. eg a person states his beliefs which may or may not be facts. And then one applies logic to see if there is any inconsistency AMONG the beliefs, and so on
.
So in DL, the goal is to analyse beliefs, and NOT check beliefs with "reality". And goal is also to compare and contrast beliefs of two or more persons. eg one can ask question like "is belief X held by A consistent with his belief Y". Or, "is belief X held by A consistent with belief Y held by B". But a question like "is belief X held by A consistent with so called 'reality'" is outside the scope of the field DL.
.
For more, pls see the wiki page (see link in first comment)
.
Based on this concept of DL, I have formulated what I now call as "Doxastic way to discuss proposed law-drafts, campaign methods and politics" or "Doxastic Way to Debate or Discuss" pr DWTD for short. And associated with DWTD is a new concept - which is
.
In DWTD , there can be two or more opposing persons X and Y, and there can be audience of 1 to 20 to 100 to million people. If X and Y oppose each other completely, then it is best they discuss in front of audience and not in absence of audience. And X and Y should state their statements and also state which of the statements are beliefs or facts. And then X, Y and audience can analyse their statements - be facts or beliefs.
.
Most important things are
.
(1) Is X or Y putting a statement as FACT or BELIEF?
.
(2) Are some facts stated possibly DERIVABLE at all, forget VERIFIABLE or PROVABLE? If they are not derivable, then how did X or Y obtained them?
,
(B) are statements being are statements being made "honest"
(B) are his statements consistent with his own statements
(C) are his ACTIONS in politics consistent with his statements?
(D)
.
----
.
(A) Honest vs Dishonest statements/persons
.
Here, there can be two opposing persons X and Y, and audience of 1 to 20 to 100 to million people. And A and B should state their statements and also state which of the statements are beliefs or facts. eg A can say
.
(1) I am making statement "Earth is round" and I consider it as fact
.
(2) I am making statement that "over 95% supreme judges are nepotic" and this is my belief
.
So both debaters should clarify whether their statements are something that is their beliefs or something the consider facts
.
Now audience should first evaluate HONESTY of speaker. eg if a person says "over 95% of judges are nepotic and this is my belief". Then if I am in audience, I would say that person is possibly honest. But say a person says ""over 95% of judges are nepotic and thats a fact". Then I will outrightly dismiss that statement as well as that person as DISHONEST. Because no has ever gathered data about 30 existing SCjs plus 500 dead or alive SCjs. And no one has data about 20000 existing lower court judges or 500,000 retired dead or alive judges. So if a person is putting something that is UNDERIVABLE, forget UNVERIFIABLE as fact, then that statement and person are both dishonest. The statement may be right or wrong, but the statement maker is surely a liar
.
IOW, audience as well as both debaters should evalate the honesty of the A and B as they put their statements. If any person is putting an UNDERIVABLE fact as fact, then that statement and person should be flagged as dishonest
.
So here, an important concept is DERIVABILITY. There can be a statement which can be fact. But it can be impossible to conclude whether it is fact or not !!! eg "Majority voters in India vote on the basis of caste". To derive such fact, one will need to ask over 45 crore voters in India, and no one has time or money to ask this question to 45 crore voters. If one takes a sample, then it is more than likely that he excluded the answers which went against his statements from the report. A statement that "it is my belief that majority voters vote base on caste" is possibly an honest statement. But a statement that "it is fact that majority voters vote based on caste" is imo a dishonest statement, unless that person has actually asked over 45 crore voters in India.
.
One more example of UNDERIVABLE fact is "over 90% judges dont consider caste while giving judgment". Lets say one has time and money to ask question to all the existing 20000 lower court judges, 900 high court judges and 30 supreme judges. And the question was asked "do you consider caste while appointing judges or while giving judgement" and say 90% of judges had said NO. But such surveys are useless, because a seasoned and polished person knows how to lie and when to lie. IOW, may be 90% judges are NOT casteist, But whether they are casteist or not, is UNDERIVABLE.
.
Similarly, consider a statement that "it is my belief that 80% voters are casteism and 80% judges are not casteist". Such person is who says so is imo NOT dishonest. But say a person makes a statement "it is fact that 80 voters voters vote on caste but over 80% judges DO NOT consider caste in appointment or judgment". And say he had actually asked the question "do you consider caste while vote or making appointment or giving judgment" to 90 crore voters and also 20000 judges. And answers agree with his data. But the method he has used is useless. Because a common man has less tendency to lie, while a judge is polished and trainned, and so very likely to lie. So if a common voter uses caste while voting, he will say YES to the question, because he doesnt see any reason to lie. But if a judge is casteist, then he is trainned and he knows that saying YES will damage his career, and so he is going to say NO !!
.
So if a person puts an UNDERIVABLE fact as his belief, he is not dishonest. But if a person puts UNDERIVABLE fact as fact, then audience should mark him as dishonest.
