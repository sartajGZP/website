A reply to liars who claim that Govt ever made implicit promise of protecting unlimited amount of deposits in PSU
.
FRDI supporters say ---- see , Govts never made any such promises that all Rs 1000 crore deposit in PSU bank is safe !! They will show links on RBI website which say that only Rs 1 lakh was insured. And they ask for links and proofs where Govt said that " 100% deposit in PSU bank was insured".
.
Here is a newspaper quote from FinMin Arun Jaitley (see link below) , In this Indian express link, Arun Jaitley says “as far as public sector banks in India are concerned, there always has been, and will remain, an implicit guarantee of the government as far as supporting depositors in those banks are concerned”
.
There are also other physical reasons. If a large deposit in PSU bank was as risky as deposit in private bank, then why did that depositor forgo higher interest? and why did he opted for lower rate of interest in PSU bank?
.
And many times, when a person has say Rs 10 lakh, why did he deposit all Rs 10 lakh in one bank and not Rs 1 lakh each in 10 banks? Surely, one reason was to save hassles. But no hassle is worh risking all deposits. In USA, people always do deposit splitting i.e if a person wants to deposit $1 million, then he will split into 4 FDs of USU$ 250,000 each, because USA's FDIC insures only US$ 250,000.
.
And this Rs 1 lakh promise was given in around year 1993. Then why didnt depositors ever asked Govt to raise the limit to Rs 10 lakh or more? Lets take a different example. Every year, we see income tax payers asking Govt to raise income tax exemption limit. It was some Rs 20000 in 1990s and now it is Rs 250,000 and tax payers repeatedly request it to raise every year. Every now and then, it, income tax exemption limit also becomes an election campaign issue. But citizens never asked Govt to raise Rs 1 lakh deposit protection limit. And no party ever bothered to make it an election issue. Why? IImo, it was only because, all depositors just presumed that 100% deposits are 200% safe in PSU banks, and so why waste time in bothering about Rs 1 lakh limit written in some law no one reads.
.
But many pro-FRDI people are now shouting that - "See , Govt never made any unspoken promise of protecting deposit above Rs 1 lakh in PSU bank. And so pls dont complain if deposit in PSU bank is used for bailin" !!!
.
Despite statement of FinMin and MATERIAL BEHAVIOR that show that such unspoken promise to protect ALL unlimited deposits in PSU bank existed, and was created by Govt's own actions, they deny that such promise ever existed !!
.
All I can say to them is --- lets agree to disagree , and lets not debate further. You are welcome to support the bailin law that Modiji is proposing. I will continue to work to oppose this bailin nonsense and also promote proposed law-drafts such as execution by majority approval and right to recall PM and so on. At the end of the day, decisions are important. Discussions are not important.
