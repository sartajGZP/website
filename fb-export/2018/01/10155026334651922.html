How concepts of unwritten promises and abandoned law apply on FRDI?
.
Some such implicit unspoken unwritten promise has been –
(a) Govt will never allow PSU bank to sink
(b) Govt will protect 100% deposits in PSU banks of ANY large amount
(c) Govt may allow private bank to sink
(d) Govt will NOT protect more than Rs 1 lakh in private bank
.
And two such abandoned and unenforced laws has been – (a) PSU bank can be liquidated and (b) only Rs 100,000 of deposit is to be protected by Govt in case PSU bank sink.
.
Till now, Govt never let PSU bank liquidate, and all deposits of PSU bank has been protected.
.
The supporters of FRDI claim – pls show link where Govt made such promises !! And they promptly show link on RBI website which shows that only Rs 100,000 are insured by RBI !!
.
Here I will quote one statement given by Finance Minister Arun Jetliey – (see link below ) “As far as public sector banks in India are concerned, there always has been, and will remain, an implicit guarantee of the government as far as supporting depositors in those banks are concerned.”
.
If concept of implicit guarantee is oxymoron or void, as many FRDI-supporters say, then they should first PUBLICLY ask Arun Jetliey to retract this statement, and make this claim that “implicit guarantee is oxymoron” after Arun Jetley publicly retracts this statement !!
.
Now FRDI is a written act which deals with deposit insurance. And so FRDI ends this implicit promise of deposit protection in PSU bank, because FRDI explicitly talks about bail-in in PSU banks.
