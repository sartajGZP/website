Some key questions wrt PSU banking,
.
Pls note that these questions are NOT some objective questions, that you will get answers in some factsheets. These are SUBJECTIVE questions, and you have to decide YOUR OPINIONS on these issues/questions. With each question, some OBJECTIVE facts are tied, but the main question is subjective.
.
(Q1) Some objective facts : Govt banks offer 1% or rather 11% LESS interest than private banks. (eg when Govt bank offers 8% per annum rate, private banks often offer 9% ; so it is 1% lesser , or rather (9 - 8)/9 = 11% lesser).
.
question : Despite lower interest, why in YOUR OPINION, did so many many depositors still kept the money in Govt banks and not in private banks? Pls decide your OPINION.
.
(Q2a) I grew up hearing "money in Govt bank is all safe" !! Did YOU hear such statements from elders etc in society?
.
(Q2b) I never heard anyone say that money in private or co-op bank was safe. So did YOU ever hear statements that "money in private or co-op bank is also safe"?
.
(Q3a) Some facts : Many times, after 1960 as well as before, borrowers who borrowed from co-operative banks' or private banks' went bust. And in those cases, Govt allowed those banks to sink and Govt did NOT pay full money to depositors. eg in borrowers of Madhopura Bank , Ahmedabad in year 2001 defaulted. Many many depositors lost part of the money above Rs 1 lakh.
.
Question : so why didnt Govt saved all private banks or its depositors? Pls decide your opinion
.
(Q3b) Some facts : but in all cases when lenders of Govt bank failed, Govt rescued those banks and depositors lost zero paisa of principal or interest.
.
question : Why didnt Govt allowed PSU banks to default on deposits? Pls decide your opinion
.
(Q4 = q1 + q2 + q3) some facts : many FRDI supporters say that “PSU bank depositors are to be blamed for their faith that Govt will protect 100% of PSU bank deposits”.
.
question : Do you blame depositors by saying that "depositors were irresponsible and so they had this false faith" or do you hold Govt responsible for creating such faith in PSU bank?. Pls decide your opinions
.
.
(Q5) in YOUR opinion, what was the max AMOUNT insured by Govt in PSU bank (not private bank but PSU bank) not just as per written law, but as per unwritten unspoken implicit promises given by govts since 1950s? It is numerical question. Pls state the amount best to YOUR opinion
.
(Q6) In YOUR opinion, what was the max amount insured by Govt in private bank? Not PSU bank but private bank. And not just as per written law, but as per unwritten unspoken implicit promises given by govts since 1950s. Again, it is numerical question. Pls state the amount as in your opinion best to your opinion .

So pls re-read above questions/issues. Imo, they are important while deciding ethics and effects of FRDI bill
.
