Alternative I propose to FRDI
--------------------------------------
.
(1) We activists and voters should ask PM to print law that says "100% of all deposits of all in all PSU banks will be fully guaranteed by Govt”
.
(2) We activists should ask PM to print law that says no more than Rs 1 lakh of deposits in private banks will be guaranteed by Govt, and later also remove this Rs 1 lakh and make private bank insured by NO govt body. Later, the “bank” word in all private banks should be replaced by “non banking financial companies”.
.
(3) SAFE BANKING or NARROW BANKING in PSU banks --- The money in PSU bank should be lent only in very specific areas, and ONLY to individuals and not companies, such – (a) home loans to individuals (b) student loans (c) medical loans (d) vehicle loans to individuals (e) farming loans to individuals . And PSU banks can lend to Govt bodies..
.
(4) So interest in PSU bank will be low. And is someone wants more interest, he should go to private financial companies.
.
Also, only depositors of Indian citizens and companies wholly owned by Indians should be protected.
