.

.
Vegetarians will also suffer, even though they are least responsible for this damage

Solution I propose is TRANSFERABLE QUOTA SYSTEM OVER FISHING. Each person across world, will have right to fish x kg of fish per year, and he can transfer to any company for a price or he can choose not to give it to any company. The company or person who has obtained Y kg of fishing rights can fish ONLY Y kg in a year. This will impose global restriction on fishing and will increase the fish price. This will need a UN agreement along with national laws
