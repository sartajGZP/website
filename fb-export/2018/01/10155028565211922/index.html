Lies about FRDI and bailin ---- Lies thrown by Modiji and RSS-workers about bailin – (a) that bailin will be "sparingly" used (b) bailin improves protection of depositors more than existing levels !! etc etc
.
Modiji and RSS-workers across India are throwing 10s of lies about FRDI to misguide activists and voters.
.
Lets us look at some lies thrown my Modiji and RSS-workers
.
Pls see the newspaper link given below . Here are some statements
.

(1) RSS-leader Arun Jetley says "… Most certainly, it ( = bailin ) will not be used in case of a public sector bank as such a contingency is not likely to arise .... "

What does he really mean? Pls see that RSS leader Arun Jetley does NOT say that bailin will NOT be used at all on PSU bank depositors. He makes it more than clear that “the law does allow bailin procedure to used on PSU depositors”. And his words “that most certainly wont be used ..” are lies. He will use bailin on PSU bank depositors and then say “see, I had never said that bailin will never be used … all I said is that it wont be used most certainly. Well, this was a case other than most certain cases” !! And of course it will not be frequently used – such as once a week. It will be sparingly used, say just once a year !!
.
If bailin is not to used for PSU banks, then why are Modiji and RSS-workers hell bent on putting bailin clauses in the bill for PSU banks? The fact that RSS workers are vocally demanding that “FRDI law must have bailin clauses over PSU bank deposits” prove that
.
(2) Next, RSS-workers are saying that as of now only Rs 1 lakh is insured and FRC may insure a higher amount !! Well, till day, IMPLICIT UNSPOKEN commitment of Govt was to protect ALL PSU deposits of indefinite amount. Here is quote from same link –--- “ [Arun Jetley says] : As far as public sector banks in India are concerned, there always has been, and will remain, an implicit guarantee of the government as far as supporting depositors in those banks are concerned.”
.
IOW, till date, 100% of deposits in PSU bank, be Rs 1 lakh or Rs 10000 crore were FULLY protected thru unwritten unspoken commitment from Govt. With FRDI and bailin, this unwritten commitment is gone, because FRDI act clearly spells out how bailin can be used. IOW, RSS-workers’ claim that only RS 1 lakh was insured till date is a lie.
.
All in all, Modiji, Jetleyji and RSS-workers are spreading tons of lies about FRDI and bailin provision. Reality is that once bailin provision get passed in Parliament, small depositors till Rs 10 lakh will face no loss, but large deposit holders will get ripped off royally !!

So many Indians will start hoarding deposits in foreign banks – legally or illegally. And many will hoard currency notes or gold or silver or bit coins, legally or illegally. And hoarding of land will also increase. All in all, the bailin provision brought by Modiji will ruin Indians in India, and increase strength of usuk-elitemen in India.

So I request all to oppose this bailin nonsense.
And pls note that Modiji is publicly supported bailin clauses and SoGa/ArKe are also supporting it. And every congress/rss/aap worker is supporting this bailin nonsense.

So pls work to create a POLITICAL ELECTORAL alternative to congress/rss/aap that will work to stop this bailin nonsense, or cancel this bailin nonsense if Modiji manage to bring it
.
