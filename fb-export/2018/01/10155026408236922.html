How FRDI's bailin provision will ruin Indians and strengthen usuk-elitemen in India
.
Once FRDI is passed, with bail provisions, there is no way for Indian upper middle class to save savings in deposits safely. No matter which bank he takes, PSU or private, he will have bailin sword hanging over him.

So confidence in PSU banks will corrode and people will resort to holding gold or paper currency or bitcoins or foreign currency or other means, legally or illegally.

This will increase number of Indians who will hoard savings in foreign banks, legally or illegally, where money is insured by Govt. eg US banks have safety of US$ 250,000 (about Rs 1.75 crore) and Australian bank have safety of AU$ 250,000 (about Rs 1.25 crore). So US citizenship, AU citizenship or legal/illegal ways to hoard money in those banks become safer / better option
.
Also, people will do deposit splitting i.e if Govt insures banl deposits of say Rs 10 lakhs per person per bank, then a person who has Rs 1 crore will open account in 10 banks !!
.
But in India, we have only 20 PSU banks !!! So even if Govt insures Rs 10 lakhs of deposits, then also, one can get only Rs 2 crore insured from Govt. Compare this with USA, where there are 6500 banks insured by FDIC, and each person is insured upto US$ 250,000 per bank. So by FD splitting he can get insurance over US$ 250,00 * 6500 = US$ 1.6 billion. IOW, a person with US$ 1.6 billion can get all his deposits fully insured by US Govt using FD splitting.
.
But in India, once FRDI comes, even if Govt insures Rs 10 lakh of deposits, only Rs 2 crore will be fully insured in PSU banks using FD splitting

IOW, upper middle class men and upper class men who have crores of rupees will deposit will simply find it better to legally or illegally save money in USA. If this means changing citizenship, then so be it.
.
So how this bill strengthens usuk-elitemen in India?
.
As ability of India’s upper middle class to safely store bank deposits decreases, their ability to complete against companies of usuk-elitemen will decrease. And bail in provisions will ensure that many loose their savings and this their ability to invest there after.
.
So due to above reasons, I oppose FRDI.
.
Now here, pls note again, that supporters of FRDI claim that FRDI is improvement over existing Rs 1 lakh insurance !!! Listen to their lie carefully --- they say “as of now, only Rs 1 lakh is insured”. Defacto reality is – today, in PSU bank, UNLIMITED amount has been insured by govt thru unspoken implicit guarantee.
.
So how can bailin provisions be stopped from coming?
