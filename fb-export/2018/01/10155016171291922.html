Deciding what should be MY OPINION on an issue/question, rather than look for "FACTS"
.
In activism, many times, one has to decide what his decision is and what is the decision of others rather than look for FACTS.
.
There will be some FACTS which will enable one decide what his opinion should be and what is possibly other's opinion - but finally , it will be OPINION and not facts.

----
.
eg1. consider the RAW FACT that interest rate in PSU bank on deposits is 1% less than interest rate in private bank. Many times, PSU bank will offer some 8% interest , while private bak will offer 9% pa. So actually, PSU bank is offering (9% - 8%)/9% = about 11% lesser interest, not just 1%. But this maths isnt the question,
.
The question is --- despite this lesser interest rate, there are crores of individuals who put money in PSU bank and NOT in private bank. WHY?
,
Now how will you possibly find FACT behind their choice? You will never find time and money to collect answers from crores of individuals. If you decide to take a small sample, then may be you can find. But of you depend on answer of a BIKAU JOURNALIST or a BIKAU ECONOMIST , who claims he had taken a RANDOM sample, and taken their answers, then well, you are only letting yourself getting fooled. Because it has been proven again and again and again that every journalist is bikau and every economist is also bikau (more expensive than journalist in general).
.
So in case you have to decide ANSWER to this question "WHY despite this lesser interest rate, there are crores of individuals who put money in PSU bank and NOT in private bank", you better decide what is YOUR opinion on this issue. If someone is telling FACTS about this, then you are only letting him make a fool out of you !! Because he too never asked all the crores of individuals who took this decision
.
----
.
eg2. You will often hear all BIKAU political science professors than voters in India sell votes. Well, there were some 85 crore registered voters in may-2019 loksabha election of which some 70% i.e. about 44 crore had voted.
.
So lets put a question --- how many crores of voters had sold the votes?
.
Now say --- do YOU have time and money to ask even 1 crore voter or even 10000 voters whether they sold their vote or not? IOW, when one is putting numbers like "X crore voters sold their votes", one is ONLY stating his OPINIONS and not really stating any facts. He may be fooling you to by putting is paid opinion as fact, but he is NOT putting any fact. And you will never find time to ask crores of voters on why they voted and whom they voted for (in case you dont trust EVMs).
.
Now some questions and counter-questions may enable you to form your opinion. eg if over 20 crore voters are willing to sell votes for highest bidder, then why dont Rockefellar or Rothschilde or Tata or Godrej directly become PM ? They have more money that congress / rss / app combined to buy votes. Why do they act via proxies like SoMoKe , if vote selling is such a fact? Such questions can guide you in forming opinion. But finally, you will need to form an opinion - and stop reading bikau political science professors articles which claim that "they have factually found that X crore voters in India sell votes".
.
-----
.
So in general, in countless cases, when you hear a bikau political science professor, or bikau historian or bikau economist or bikau opinoion poll gatherer or an activist citing some FACT about billion voters of India --- pls note that he is merely giving his paid or unpaid, andh or learned, opinion and not really citing any fact. No one has time to ask billion voters of India and no one has money to do such questioning. And if one claims that one has chosen X thousand voters at RANDOM, then there is no way to verify if he speaking truth or lies , and there are 100 times more liars in the field of political science, economics, journalism etc.
.
So, in activism, it is better to decide your own opinion, rather than depend on his paid or unpaid opinion, his lies that he has "FACTS".
