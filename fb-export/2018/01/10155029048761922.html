FDI in Patanjali's case may NOT harm India --- serious no sarcasm
.
(1) Lets say a foreign company invests with following condition - they will do sales in India and from income they earn, RBI will have to give dollars back. In such case, such FDI creates forex burden on India.
.
(2) But say a foreign company invests in an Indian company with following condition -- the joint venture will do zero sales in India, but take goods made by that Indian joint venture company and sell it ABROAD in USA or Europe etc, and keep some of the profits earned from it . Now this FDI arrangement does NOT create forex burden on India
.
To the best of my information, Patanjali may enter into SECOND arrangement, if at all they do. So FDI in their case does NOT create forex burden on India.

If Patanajali is doing type-1 arrangement, then it may create forex burden on India. But I dont think thats the case. Patanjali doesnt need money to expand LOCAL sales. They need money and partner ONLY for foreign sales.
