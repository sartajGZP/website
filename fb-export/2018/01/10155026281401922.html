As of today, DEFACTO coverage of deposits is Rs 1000 crore NOT Rs 1 lakh as liars say !! FRDI will reduce coverage to few lakhs.
.
To promote truth, understand the lies, the liars and the motives of liars/
.
Moment discussion on FRDI starts, some liars will crawl out of woodwork and rocks, and cite some link which say "Govt today insures only Rs 100,000 in PSU and private banks", and so if FRDI puts insured amount as Rs 10 lakhs, then FRDI improves the situation, not worsen it !!
.
These liars do quote the written law correctly, but these liars push the fact under the carpet, that since 1950s, there has been an IMPLICIT UNWRITTEN UNSPOKEN commitment by GoI that ALL deposits in PSU banks, no matter what is the amount be Rs 1 crore or Rs 10000 crore, are 200% fully insured by Govt.
.
Here is a statement made by Fin on 1-jan-2018 , reported in Indian Express on 2-jan-2018 (see link in 1st comment). The FinMin Jetley says --- "As far as public sector banks in India are concerned, there always has been, and will remain, an implicit guarantee of the government as far as supporting depositors in those banks are concerned."
.
And as per pro-FRDI people, such unwritten commitment to fully protect all PSU deposit never existed !! Then well, is FinMin Jetley a liar in their opinion?
.
And once FRDI passes, this unwritten commitment OFFICIALLY ends, and they are explicitly replaced by BAILIN nonsense. The FRDI act clause-52 clearly spells out that a PSU or private bank with permission of FRC (a body consisting of 10-15 men appointed by PM) can do bailin - i.e. it can reduce the amount of FD above certain amount by any amount !! eg it can convert Rs 50 lakh FD into Rs 30 lakh FD by using bailin !!!
.
So pls understand the lie that "as of now only Rs 1 lakh is insured by Govt". As of today, Govt insurance over PSU bank deposit is DEFACTO Rs 1000 crore or even more. FRDI will make that cover go, and reduce it to few lakhs or may be Rs 1 crore. But FD and savings and current deposits above that amount will be used for bailin.
