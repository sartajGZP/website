If you are in Mumbai on 30-jun-2018 saturday, pls attend event on Dhan Vapasi https://www.nayidisha.com/community-leadership-program/.
.
The questions you can ask is
.
(1) Say NewDisha gets 400 MPs in may-2019 elections. Then when will new NewDisha govt start delivering Rs 8000 per family PER MONTH, totaling Rs 200,000 crores a MONTH across India? starting 01-jul-2019? or starting 01-aug-2019?
.
(2) which law-draft will NewDisha pass as in 01-jun-2019 so that payment of Rs 8000 per family per MONTH start as in ?
.
(3) what plan NewDisha has in case SCjs put stay order on those laws or cancel those laws passed to deliver Rs 8000 per month per faimily?
.
(4) NewDisha also claims that it will end all 3 crore pending cases in 3 years. What laws has it proposed to deliver that? How many new courts will be needed? Will the courts use judge system or Jury System? And what if judges refuse to end all these cases in 3 years? What laws do they propose to deal with judges who refuse?
.
Pls put all these questions on paper, and make copies. And after taking permission, give copy to everyone who is attending the meeting.
