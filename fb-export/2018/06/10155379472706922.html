The most important problem in activist --- betrayal by timepass by new elected MP. And solution I propose is --- "from day-one, activists should promote DRAFTS and candidates whose manifesto or party manifesto and/or affidavits have these drafts". So how do drafts help?
.
summary
------------
.
Imo, the most important problem in politics is that newly elected MPs do timepass to implement their promises by giving reasons after reasons for delay. These MPs will NOT outrightly refuse, But they will delay and delay and never leep the promises. There are countless examples in past and recent past.
.
Solution I propose is --- (a) from day-1 activists should promote DRAFTS (b) then activists should promote ONLY those election candidates who or whose parties or both have supported these drafts. (c) and if possible, the support for draft should be in concrete form such as citing newindia dot in links and sha1hash in manifesto, election affidavits etc
.
How does these draft reduce the problem of betrayal?
.
Consider two parties - say RRP or any party such as congress/rss/aap/others. Say all parties have promises RTR-SupremeCourt-judge law but only RRP has given draft.
.
Now in which case, possibility of not keeping promise is LESSER? and why?
.
The congress/rss/aap/other candidates gave promise for some laws, but didnt give DRAFTS. So after election, they can form committees for drafts and delay and delay. They will never refuse, they will simply delay.
.
But in case of RRP elected members, drafts are given before elections, and so they cant delay in the name of draft writing. So if they want to default, only option they have is to outrightly refuse.
.
So RRP elected candidates delay, then Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh can act.
.
So why can Ahimsamurti Udham Singh can act on RRP MPs and not on congress/rss/aap/other MPs?
.
Because one IMPORTANT pre-condition for AMUS to act is --- OBJECTIVE breach of OBJECTIVE promise . If the promise is vague, then there is no breach and certainly no objective breach.
.
IOW, force of AMUS is applicable on RRP MPs, while force of AMUS isnt applicable on congress/rss/aap/others MPs.
.
So imo, chances that activists will suffer betrayal are less if they support election candidates who have promised drafts in their manifesto or in their party manifesto.
.
And what can be FORMAT of promise? Imo, best format is newindia dot in links in manifesto along with sha1hash. Along with manifesto, it can also be in their election affidavit.
.
Now can other parties adopt our methods? NO. because congress/rss/aap/other parties have flatly refused to give DRAFTS. You mention LAW-DRAFTS and they immidiately insist on delay !! So AMUS's counter gurantee will not apply on them.
.
So whats the solution to problem of "breach of promises by newly elected MPs"? Solution I propose to REDUCE the possibility of breach is -- from day-1 , before election, activists should publicize drafts and then support only those party/candidates who have supported drafts on their manifesto
.
So, my MOST IMPORTANT point is
-------------------------------------
.
In my proposed solution, the activists have to act BEFORE the betrayal problem comes. I repeat --- the activists must act BEFORE the problem of betray comes. My solution is NOT that --- "if new MPs start betrayal, then after activists should next start publicizing law-drafts". The solution starts BEFORE problem comes !!! The activists must publicize law-drafts BEFORE betrayal or fulfillment comes. "Before, and not after", is one important point in method I suggested
