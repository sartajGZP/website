comparing two proposals --- better along all dimensions vs "better along most dimensions only, and looking bad on some dimensions"
.
consider GST vs wealth tax. Then wealth tax is superior that GST along ALL dimensions. And so when a recallists is arguing against congress/rss/aap worker on GST vs WT topic before audience of say 1000 voters, the recallists will have easy time
.
But now consider judge system vs Jury System. JurySys is superior along many dimensions such as lack of nexuses, no fear of suspension, expulsion or unfavorable transfers, no enticement of promotion or favorable transfers etc etc. But on dimension, namely "KNOWLEDGE OF LAWS" , a congress/rss/aap worker can make JurySys look bad. Also, on other dimensions that such as temperament, biases, caste biases etc, they can make some noise.
.
Similarly, compare "weaponization of we the commons". The chances of mass slaughter by foreign armies and chances of large scale govt atrocities drastically reduce by weaponization of commons. But deaths due to random violence does increase with weaponization of commons.
.
IOW, a large number of recalist proposals are better than congress/rss/aap proposals along ALL dimensions. And so recallists can convince audience easily.
.
But in many cases, along one dimension, the recallist proposal can be made to look bad. So imo, what recallists will need to do is to make a LIST OF ALL dimensions / factors, and then show that along N dimensions, recallist proposal is better and along some useful or useless dimension, congress/rss/aap proposal may be better. Then one needs to give some SUBJECTIVE weightages to each dimension. And then show that recallist proposal is better when WEIGHTED SUM is taken. And finally, weightages are subjective. So solution I propose is "referendum for the proposed recallist law-draft" and not law-draft !!
.
eg on "weaponization of we the commons", my proposal is "REFERENDUM on proposed law-draft to enable all voters of India to bear guns". that way subjectivity of weights is decided by each voter himself. But GST vs WealthTax , I dont emphasis on word referendum, though eventually most laws we have proposed will come via newindia dot in or TCP only, which are similar to referendum.
.
So my point is --- it is important to first KNOW that along 1-2 dimensions, some recallist proposals can be made to look bad !! And in such cases, recallist should first make list of ALL dimensions, and then work to show that recallist proposal is better than congress/rss/aap proposals.
