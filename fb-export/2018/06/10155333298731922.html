Its not always "what were the wrong things did country (that lost) do"? Its often "what were the right things that that country didnt do?" !!
.
Nokia once had over 50% of share in mobile phone. And within a few years, it was below 5%. And many opined that it was NOT because Nokia took any "wrong actions". But there were 10s of "right actions" that samsung , apple etc did take and nokia didnt take
.
Likewise, when I read about present / histotical events, my conclusion is that nation A often looses against nation-B, and reasons are less about what "wrong" nation-A did. But more often "what right actions nation-B did take and nation-A could not take or did not take".
.
Today, India is losing and losing before China and USA. And imo, it is less about what "wrong steps" we are taking. And imo, it is more about what "right steps of less wrong steps that china / usa are taking and we are NOT taking". !! (And imo, china too is losing and lose against usa because right steps china is not taking, but thats china's problem and I will not spend more time on that).
.
And differences of opinions of what rights or less wrong steps usa/china are taking and india isnt taking varies from widely.
.
As per me, right steps usa has taken and we didnt are law-drafts like (1) RTR (2) jurysys (3) referendum (4) multi-election (5) wealth tax (6)no gst/vat/excise/servicetax and much less salestax, and (7) universal gun ownership. And many more law-drafts.

But most congress/rss/aap workers have opposed this, and they consider all seven steps that usa has taken and India didnt as wrong steps.
.
And reason why we are losing against china is due to steps they took such as (1) low weightage to interviews in recruitment (2) higher weightage to maths / science and lower weightage to useless subjects like political superstitions (aka political science) , dis-economics, sociology etc in recruitment exams (3) more level field in recruitment exams in (4) state ownership of all lands and minerals which has created lower land rent and mineral charges for NEW COMERS
.
Again, all congress/rss/aap workers have opposed this steps and they have considered these steps as wrong steps. (I also dont support step-4 taken by china but do support step-1 to step-3. On land, I have supported DDMRCAM = MRCM on govt land and WealthTax , and for mineral I propose MRCM = DDMRCAM (see my album 'law drafts I support top 50' for these two law-drafts).
.
Imo, each activists should spend time in deciding what steps India is NOT taking . Imo, BIGGER reason are steps we are NOT TAKING and not in steps we are taking.....
