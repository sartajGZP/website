How GST will increase overcrowding in cities --- solution cancel GST and levy wealth tax. see last para
.
(excise/vat/servicetax/cst were killing business and so two alternatives were GST or wealth tax. SoMoke and congress/rss/aap workers decides to oppose wealth tax and instead use GST. Now GST has 10s of problems, and one is --- it will increase overcrowding in cities. I have explained here how).
.
Let me give one numerical example
.
(1) Say 100 persons in Mehasana some 75 km away from ahmd and all their customers are in ahmd. Say each is making ghee of say Rs 2 crore a year
.
(2) Say 100 persons in ahmedabad are making ghee of Rs 2 crore a year EACH and their customers are also in ahmd.
.
Now if a ghee manufacturers insists on paying GST then his customers may walk to GST evader
.
Now due to eWayBill system, it is difficult for Mehasana manufacturers to evade GST. They face risk of confiscation of transport vehicle and all goods, and serious fines. While eWayBill system law doesnt apply on manufacturers in Ahmadabad if customer is also in Ahmedabad
.
So Mehasana manufactures may decline to send goods without eWayBill in which case they will end up paying charging 5% more (GST on ghee is 5%).. Whereas Ahmedabad manufacturers, if buyer is also in ahmd, can easily evade GST because eWayBill law doesnt apply on him.
.
So many customers of Ahmedabad will switch to Ahmedabad manufacturers
.
So many ghee manufacturers will have to shift close to their sellers. Or in any business of the manufacturers in cities will grow and those outside cities will shrink.
-
----------------
.
Apply this on 100s of goods. GST is anywhere from 5% to 18% to 28%. Say a tiles manufacturer is in Morbi (a city in Gujarat which has highest tile manufacturing). And buyers are builders in Ahmedabad, GST on tiles is 28%, So if manufacturer is in ahmd, then GST evasion is easy, but if manufacturer is in Morbi, then GST evasion is difficult due to eWayBiill system.
.
Now most buyers in cities and many sellers are in cities and off city areas or small cities. So many manufacturers will try to shift to city. And so overcrowding in cities will increase
.
Now this wont apply to manufacturers where there is steep economy of scale.
.
All in all, GST law DEFACTO says
.
(a) if manufacturer and buyer are in same city , then evasion is GST easy because eWayBill system legally doesnt apply if both are in same city.
,.
(b) if manufacturer and buyer are in different city , then GST evasion is DIFFICULT because eWayBill system applies !!
.
So many buyers may switch to sellers in same city. Now buyers are more in cities and sellers are in off city areas or remote areas. So sellers in cities will grow and many sellers will try to shift to cities. And so crowding in cities will increase !!!
.
-------------
.
Solution I propose is to COMPLETELY scrap GST and levy wealth tax.
.
If you support the proposal of scrapping GST and levying wealth tax, there there is ONE 10-second step you can do TODAY to SUPPORT wealth tax --- pls vote at https://newindia.in/causes/WealthTax . To know more about WealthTax , pls see fb.com/10154511306361922 .
.
Pls note that congress/rss/aap workers across India have opposed wealth tax and supported GST. And SoGa / NaMo / ArKe too have opposed wealthtax and supported GST.
.
So if a person is congress/rss/aap worker or a SoMoKe-andhbhagat and he says that he opposes wealth tax and supports GST, then at least he is speaking truth. But if a person is a congress/rss/aap worker or a SoMoKe-andhbhagat, and he says that he supporters wealth tax and opposes GST, then I request all to dismiss him as a liar.
