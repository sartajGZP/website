a valcano of digital payment frauds may erupt --- one trojan or insider fraud in phone company server , and 1000s and 1000s of our bank account owners may get robbed by digital payments
.
The trojan or insider fraud can send OTP to fraudster and not end user, and fraudster can siphon out money. Account owner will know after days and weeks !!!
.
Say there are thousand users A1 to A1000. Say X is a fraudster who knows their account# and ph#.
.
Say X manages to hack phone company server. And server will send all SMS to A1 to A1, except OTP !! OTP will be sent to X and not A1 !!
.
So now X can rob A1 and A1 will come to know days or weeks later after he sees list of all transactions in the statement !!!
.
And X can rob A2 to A1000 in same way !!!
.
IOW, India is sitting on a valcano of possible large scale digital payment frauds.
.
Solution I propose is to end OTP nonsense. The rules I propose are
.
(1) end use of OTP as conclusive proof
.
(2) if payer says within 60 days that he didnt make payment, the reciever looses and now reciever can file police complain

(3) receiver should physically call payer on a landlind and record the voice as a proof
.
(4) all should be given SMS enabled landline at cheap rates

(5) OTP can be sent on landline too as an evidence but not proof.
.
False routing of SMS / call on landline is too too difficult
