An interesting invention to look into. Some 12 kg of weight is hanged along a rope which goes through pulley. The weight is manually take to a height of some 10 ft. The pulley is connected to a custom electricity generator. The weight falls at very very slow rate --- takes some 20 minutes to climb down from 20 feet to ground.
.
And as weight comes down, the pulley moves and genarator creates electricity that runs a bulb. So for 20 minutes, one can get light without external electricity, solar panel, battery etc.
.
And excellent must have , it it is working fine

Interesting Engineering:

These lamps powered by a pulley system are design to tackle energy poverty..
