(1) say X supports a law-draft, but X says "I will NOT ask PM to print this law"!!. Then should A take X as "supporter"? IMO , NO
.
(2) say X supports a law-draft, but X says "I will surely ask PM to print this law, but NOT using most reliable channels"!!. Then should A take X as "supporter"? well, lets analyse
.
My stand on (1) is clear. I take a person as supporter of law-draft ONLY if that person "tells" PM to print that law-draft. He may have VALID reason to oppose / ignore law-draft Thats fine. I dont mind people who oppose proposed law-drafts. I only want to ask activists to stay away from FAKE supporters
.
Now say a person agrees to tell PM. Now there are 10s of methods to tell PM. Some are
.
(1) Take an aadhar link mobile phone of yours, and VOTE on newindia dot in --- when a person votes on newindia. PMO KNOWS (audience doesnt know, but PMO knows) that vote came from aadhar-linked mobile and not from some emailid or twitterid or fbid
.
(2) person votes on newindia using emailid, or fbid or twitterid
.
(3) person writes postcard
.
(4) person calls PMO's landline and gives message to staff
.
(5) person sends tweet to @PmoIndia .
.
(6) I will write on FB status "@PmoIndia , I support ...#HASHTAG etc etc"
.
(7) I will write "@PmoIndia #HASHTAG " on Ethereum
.
Now which is LEAST unreliable and least inexpensive method ?
.
IMO, least unreliable method is (1). Because when you call from PCO or mobile, the staff has to manually write down message and that can create typo-spelling error. Also, cost of keeping staff wiil be huge if lakhs of voters start calling. And calling leaves no record. Whereas on newindia, PMO as well as user (nut not thirdparty) can see which causes he has voted for
.
Likewise, when tweet is sent to @PmoIndia, the tweet reaches reliably, and inexpensively. But PMO has no way to know whether tweet sender is fake or real. Whereas on newindia dot in, if a person votes via his aadhar linked mobile, then PmoIndia knows that a real Indian voter is voting
.
And postcard is good if and only if person has no internet.
.
The plus point of tweet and fbposts is that your friends will come to know. So it has value in activism. But fbpost has zero value in communication to PM, and tweet has some value , but less than newindia dot in
.
Now each method has some plus points which other method doesnt. eg when I send tweet to @PmoIndia, my friends can see that I am supporter, but PmoIndia cant see that I am a real person. And when I vote on newindia dot in, PmoIndia can see that I am a real mobile using person or fake email using person, but my friends cant see.
.
So finally, I request ALL to use MULTIPLE methods ----
.
(1) vote on newindia dot in
.
(2) tweet PMOIndia.
.
(3) send postcard to PM
.
ALL of above 3 steps are must. If one step is missing, and I DO NOT take him as supporter of law-draft. Now course, if person has NO internet, then first two steps are NOT needed.
