Reducing true activists' possible timewast ---- so just because some step is "good", doesnt mean that that step must be done , if there is a much better step because time, money and energy are limited. And which step is better --- it is SUBJECTIVE.
.
One action item in activism is to take steps that reduce TRUE activists' possible timewaste.
.
And it will be subjective. Examples to show subjectivity are ---
.
(a) an activist-A may think that "spending time on cause-X will be useful, spending time on cause-Y is time waste ". While activist-B may think other way
.
(b) an activist-A may think that "spending time on cause-X is useful, but law-draft-P is useful, while law-draft-Q is time waste". And activist-C may say "spending time on cause-X is useful, but law-draft-P is timewaste, while spending time on law-draft-Q is useful"
.
(c) an activist-C may think that "spending time on cause-X is useful, but law-draft-P is useful, while law-draft-Q is time waste". And activist-C may say "spending time on cause-X is useful, but law-draft-P is timewaste, while spending time on law-draft-Q is useful"
.
(d) and an activist-D may agree with cause, law-drafts of activist-A, but oppose the campaign steps , because he thinks that A's campaign steps are timewaste.
.
---
.
Some specific examples:
.
(1) I at right to recall party, and many recallists, support RTR, Jury etc law-drafts. while congress/rss/aap etc workers oppose these law-drafts because as per them, they are timewaste. and they consider SoMoKe-puja as geat activity and we consider it as timewaste.
,.
(2) within 'recallists", many consider some proposed law-drafts as good, and other proposed law-drafts as timewaste.
.
(3) and there is sharp division on campaign steps. eg I consider election contesting and newspaper advt as most effective while many consider these methods as useless. And many like myself consider newindia dot in, as highly useful, while some consider newindia dot in as highly timewaste.
.
----
,
So even if something is good, a person may not agree to do it, because he has VALID reasons from his point of view that something else is far far more time efficient. .
.
So each activist has to make a list of steps he wishes to take and NO take. And it will be subjective --- each one on his own.
