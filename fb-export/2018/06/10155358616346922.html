Bhaiyuji's maharaj's suicide and decision of Modiji, Devendra Ghazini and rss-workers' to loot temples in Maharashtra
.
Bhaiyyuji Maharaj , who committed suicide some 1-2 days back, was a spiritual leader. He had a large following in MH and MP.
.
He was only 50 years old, and was in perfect physical and mental health. He was an ace sportsman (and a modelling actor) in his youth, and had a daughter from first marriage. His first wife had passed away a few years back. And he had recetnly re-married some 1-2 years back. From what I read, his second wife had given birth to a baby boy some 3 - 4 months back. So if this be the case, a man has joy as well as reason to live. and a man feels that he has duty to live, no matter how harsh the conditions he is facing.
.
There were some domestic problems -- disputes between family members. But NO legal problems, they were only household problems. And who doesnt have such problems? People dont sucide due to such petty fights in family members. And he had NO financial problems --- all in family were well off. When one has problems amongst family members, but enough money, he can easily solve problems by buying separate flats or bunglaows for each family member. And he had no shartage of flats or bungalows. IOW, family dispute does NOT add to a reason as big as suicide.
.
One possible reason was ---- for past 10 years hBhaiyyuji Maharaj was bitterly fighting against congress's decision to loot temples. And he had supported Modiji, Devendra Fadnavis (now known as Devendra Ghazini) and RSS-workers across MH / MP , with hope that Modiji, Devendra Fadnavis and RSS-workers will end temple loot unleashed by congress leaders and congress workers.
.
Bhaiyuji Maharaj had personally worked to convince lakhs of his followers to vote for RSS = BJP, and convinced them that once rss-leaders and rss-workers come into positions, temple loot will end,.
.
But as we all know, Modiji, Devendra Ghazini (aka David Fadnavis) and RSS-workers are now more aggressive than congress/aap in temple looting across MH and India. With fear that Modiji may loose may-2019 elections, the loot has become more aggressive.
.
So Bhayujji had a huge loss of face before all his followers. His followers were upset against him.
.
For a man who is spiritual leader, loss oi face is more severe than financial loss. When there is financial loss, people at least dont doubt integrity. But when there is open breach of promises , people start doubting integrity of promise makers and some people will start suspecting and shouting against.
.
In my opinion, this was the principal reason that forced Bhaiyyji Maharaj to commit sucide.
.
Otherwise, domestic problems are non issues --- they come and go and for a man of this stature, they dont matter.
.
I have come across cases where people suicided due to severe financial loss, but never heard of case where a wealthy man committed suicide, that too when he had a daughter and also a three month old son !! No matter how depressed he is, the joy of newborn son will reduce the depression and moral duty to take care of newborn will force him to live against all suicide. So family reasons are not adding up.
