FAQs about newindia dot in

.
One of the principal stand I have taken as of today is --- if you have NOT supported a law-draft on newindia dot in, I dont take you as supporter of THAT law-draft or proposal to govt (except in cases where person does NOT know how to use net all). And if you havent sent postcard to PM demanding that law-draft, then also I dont take you as supporter of that law-draft.(be netizen or non-netizen)
.
My stand may change if PmoIndia who own newindia dot in change functionality of newindia or he takes down newindia dot in. So my stand is wrt to today's newindia dot in
.
Newindia needs mobile or PC some rudimentary familiarity with net and needs skills to use OTP. Out of 90 crore voters, some 30 crore voters are capable of using it. And over 70 crore can send postcards.
.
So some FAQ about newindia dot in come. Here are my answers
.
---
.
(1) votecount on newindia is useless
Ans : imo, it is semi-useless, and not totally useless,. eg if a proposal has 50 votes, then it proves that less than 51 REAL voters in India supports it. But say votecount is 10 lakhs, Then all 10 lakh can be fake and so it doesnt prove that proposal has even one supporter, but it does prove that proposal has less than 10,00,001 REAL supporters . So votecount on newindia does prove UPPER BOUND of real-supporters.
.
-----
.
(2) PM has no way to know how many real voters voted YES and how many YES-sayers are fake
.
Ans : PM i.e. websiteowner does know, though people outside PMO dont. Because website stores whether vote came from mobile or SM. SM can be fake, but mobiles are now aadhar linked,. So duplicate votes -- i.e one person with 10 mobile but one aadhar, can be counted as one vote by PM.
.
Newindia dot in allows fake voting. But it DOES NOT mix fake votes and real votes like EVMs or ballot boxes.
.
---------
.
(3) Newindia dot in allows fake voting and so we should boycott it
.
Ans : sadly, NRIs who are Indian voters living outside India cant access newindia website using mobile phones because international SMS are expensive. So NRIs can access newindia only via SM account or emails. Hence, PM has valid reason to allow SM based logins,. Now PmoIndia could have shown votecount as two votecounts -- one from mobiles and second from SM accounts. But PmoIndia can justify absence of two separate votecounts as "oversight, work in progress etc". So PM has a valid reason to allow SM based logins.
.
Now say A is victim and B was witness. B says to A "I support you, and I will tell ALL that you were victimised. I will tell ALL at pan galla, all on char rasta, in all meetings, but I will NOT go to court and I will NOT become witness in court because judge is allowing fake people to become witness" !!!
.
I will dismiss as non reason. the judge can be corrupt, the judge may have already taken bribe and decided the judgement, the judge may be allowing fake wintnesses --- but all those are non-reason for B for NOT becoming witness for A
.
If B says A "I oppose you and I wont be your wintess in court" , then its fine. But if B says to A " I support you , I tell ALL everything,m but I wont be witness in court", , then B's stand should be dismissed as nonsense
.
So lets say "PmoIndia has kept SM logins on newindia dot so that fake votes can come". But if a person refuses to support your proposal on newindia dot in, then THAT reason is not the reason. There may be VALID reasons why he refuses to support that proposal. But presence of fake voters cant be so
.
------------
.
(4) why a person (a) who knows how to use newindia dot in (b) refuses to vote on newindia --- is NOT a supporter of that proposal?
.
Ans : If a person is a TRUE supporter of a law-draft, then THE FIRST thing he will do is tell PM and his MP that he supports the proposed law-draft. How can both be told? Newindia dot in is CHEAPEST and simplest way to RELIABLY send information to PmoIndia !! Next is twitter. And for non-netizen, postcard is only cost effective way. And so imo, ALL three MUST be used. So if a person has NOT used ALL three, then I dont take him as a supporter of that proposal.
