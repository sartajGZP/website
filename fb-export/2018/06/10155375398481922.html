How can elitemen destroy a "good" proposal like MRCM-law-draft? --- by sponsoring a pseudo-activist like Rajesh Jain who publicizes a "bad" proposal like Dhan Vapasi which looks like the good proposal MRCM-law-draft !!! ---- part - 2/2
.
I described "HOW" part of "how bad proposals like Dhan Vapasi ruin campaigns for good proposals like MRCM-law-drafts at - fb.com/10155375385696922
.
In this part-2, I will explain possible solutions I propose.
.
Lets say a person knows MRCM-law-draft with details. In that case, no matter how much he sees about Dhan Vapasi nonsense, he will never get impressed by Dhan Vapasi and in his eyes, reputation of MRCM-law-draft will NOT decrease.
.
But if a person doesnt know MRCM-law-draft in detail, and he comes to know about Dhan Vapasi nonsense, and then he hears about MRCM-law-draft, then chances are high that he will dismiss MRCM-law-draft as nonsense
.
This is similar to RTR-MP-law-drafts. If a person knows RTR-MP-law-draft as proposed by myself at RRP first and then he reads Varun Gandhi's RTR-MP-law-draft, then he will dismiss Varun Gandhi's draft as nonsense and reputation of RRP's RTR-MP-law-draft will NOT decrease. But if a person first reads Varun Gandhi's nonsense RTR-MP-law-draft, then he will get a feeling that RTR is a nonsense concept, and so later he is very likely to even refuse to read RRP's RTR-MP-law-draft.
.
So whats the solution?
.
First, this shows why drafts are very important, far far more important than we think or I thought. The reason I Took "draft first" approach way back in 1995, was to ensure that reader gets full details of the CONCEPTS as well as how drafts actually and do implement these concepts. But it turns out DRAFTS are needed also --- to stop elitemen from maligning the concepts using fake drafts !! IOW, in absence of good drafts, a good concept can be shown as bad concept using a bad draft !! So the "draft first" approach I took 1995 is more right that I thought !!
.
Second, imo, we should see that spreading DRAFT is more important than anything else !!! So translation in local languages and distribution of pamphlets , PDFs etc are imp
.
And third --- writing drafts on 100s of issues and concepts !!! More the issues we have have, more difficult it will be for elitemen and psuedo-activists to malign these concepts. eg Mr. Rajesh Jain has Dhan Vapasi nonsense proposal to malign MRCM. So we can question him on how he plans to fix courtsm how he plans to reduce problems of factory owners, what alternative does he propose to GST's missing trader problem and "double taxation on small trader's problem" and so on. Using this, we can convince activists that he proposals are too incomplete to fix the govt. And once we get ear of the activists, we can convince him why Dhan Vapasi is nonsense and why MRCM-law-draft is good !!
.
So consider an activist who believes Dhan Vapasi is good. So if we talk about MRCM-law-draft, he wont listen to us. But if we talk about fixing labor courts or courts or Bangladeshi problem, then he will listen to us because Rajesh Jain is big zero on these issues. So once that activist listens to us, we can also convince him that Dhan Vapasi is nonsense and MRCM-law-draft is good
.
IOW, "100s of law-draft" is more useful than we think. Many times, I get suggestions that "one should focus on 2-3 issues" whereas from day one, I considered this suggestion useless. "If there are 100 problems, then how can any problem be ignored or wished away" was my counter argument. It may be good politics to focus on 2-3 issues and ignore other 98, but it is surely bad activism. Now again, it turns out "having 500 drafts to solve 100 problems": is far better than we though. If elitemen try to malign one good draft using one bad draft, then other 99 good drafts can be used to to protect that law-draft !!!
.
