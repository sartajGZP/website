N voters supports proposed-law-draft-A and "M voters know that N voters support proposed-law-draft-A" --- imo, if N is too too small, then number M is useless..
.
Consider law-draft I have proposed "Right to Recall District Education Officer" I have uploaded at newindia dot in / RtrDeo3 . Some 33 real or fake voters have voted (as on 10-jun-2018 morning). Actual number of supporters can be 33 or zero, because newindia allows fake voting. Fake voting problem can be reduced by showing two votecounts --- votes by aadhar linked mobiles and votes via other means. But thats a separate proposed law-draft. But we do know that not more than 33 voters in India support this specific Right to Recall DEO proposal.
.
Now one may say "well, some 80 crore Indian voters support RtrDeo3, but they didnt vote because of a, b, c etc reasons". who knows.
.
Now say real count is 10 or 15 or 30. 33. Then how many voters in India know that "only 33 or less support RtrDeo?". Imo, it is useless question. WHY?
.
Now there are million proposals like RtrDeo3 . There are some 3000 on newindia dot in only. So if billion people of India decide to notice supporter-count of each of these million people, then they will end up spending whole of their 100 years' lifetime in just gathering this information !!
.
So as long as supporter-count for a proposed-maw-draft is extremely low, imo, it is waste of time for majority to know "how many voters support a proposed-law-draft".
.
working to ensure that "majority knows that majority supports proposed law-draft", is useful ONLY after majority has supported a proposed law-draft, or, number of supporters are more than supporters of leaders who oppose these law-drafts. eg NaMo has over 22 crore proven supporters or 25 crore proven supporters across India (RSS = BJP got some 22 crore votes in may-2014 elections, and NDA got some 25 crore votes). So unless RtrDeo3 has over 22 crore or over 25 crore supporters, I see no point in knowing "how many voters know that 33 voters support RtrDeo3"? .
.
So imo activists should work to inform all that N voters support proposed-law-draft-X after N is at least above sat 10 lakhs or say 1 crore. Till then working to inform "N support proposed-law-draft-X" is waste of 90 crore voters' time.
.
