What campaign method do I propose to gather votes on proposals and prove votecounts? (nothing to do with elections)
.
Campaign method is steps that activists is asking fellow activists and voters to publicize proposed law-drafts and gather votecounts inform voters about votecounts.
.
This post is NOT about publicizing means such as pamhplets , whatsapp etc. This post is ONLY about knowing votecounts and proving votecounts to as many as possible.
.
The steps I propose are
.
(1) Proposal maker has to put proposal in PDF and upload on newindia dot in. Newindia dot in is a central govt website. It allows anyone to upload the proposal but allows no one to later edit / update (unless insider fraud).
.
(2) Proposal maker, activists, voters take following steps
.
(2a) all vote on newindia dot in/causes/proposal . and thru newindia, the proposal is shared on FB wall.
.
(2b) all tweet "@PmoIndia #HashTag LINK #Sha1Hash .. some comment"
.
(2c) all put tweet-text, tweet-link, newindia link, draft-text in FB album called as "(A002) law-drafts I support (top 50)" or "(A003) law-drafts I support (after top 50)"
.
(2d) all take bitmaps of "causes I have voted for" from newindia and put bitmaps in album named as "(A026) Proofs I supported law on NewIndia.in twitter etc (top 50)" or "(A028) Proofs I supported law on NewIndia,twitter etc(after top 50)". see http://fb.com/10155176951001922 as example .
.
(3) Getting upper bound on support --- pls see votecount in newindia. If say 1000 persons have supported it, and then it proves that 1000 or less persons support it.
