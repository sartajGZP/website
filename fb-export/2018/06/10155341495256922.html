Implementing open voting (a) by blockchain (b) by Govt website (c) by well known private websites (d) by less know private websites --- why best option is (b), and (b) > (a) > (c) and (d) is almost useless.
.
---
.
I defined open public voting wrt India means --- a method in which
.
(1) any voter can submit one or more proposal
.
(2) any voter can cast his YES/NO vote on any proposals he wishes and ignore proposals he wishes to ignore
.
(3) his vote becomes visible to all 90 crore voters of India
.
(4) one voter can cast ONLY one vote (and he he casts more votes, then all his votes will get clubbed and only one vote say last vote will be counted)
.
(5) all voters can see vote-counts
.
(6) all voters can feel assured that none has tempered others' votes or absents
.
Now voters too would NOT like to be bothered too much. eg if everytime he votes, then if 1000 people call him, them eventually, most voters may quit this platform.
.
----
.
METHODS?
.
(1) best method is govt website implementes above. See TCP at fb.com/10154682688171922 . if you support TCP, pls vote at newindia.in/causes/Tcp2 . But till govt doesnt implement TCP, what to do?
.
(1a) Govt has implemented newindia dot in, which has flaws that it allows email based IDs to vote and so one voter can cast 1000s of votes !! So solution is that we voters should ask @PmoIndia to allow ONLY mobile based voting on newindia dot in . and that too from aadhar linked mobile only. So bogus voting on newindia dot in will end. see newindia.in/causes/AadharLoginOnly
.
(1b) another proposal is to make it OPTIONALLY open --- see
newindia.in/causes/OptionalOpenVoting
.
----
.
(2) But what if Govt doesnt implement TCP or above improvements in newindia dot in? Then next option is BLOCKCHAIN . In blockchain, each person will put his votes on blockchain. And each person, thru persons he trusts, will get list of NON DUPLICATE Ids of some of 90 crore voters or all of 90 crore voters. Now all votes are on public ledger which can not be tempered by anyone. So every person can download all votes or some of the votes on his PC if he wants and also verify counts.
.
Possibly, Etherium too can be used for this task. One can make payment of Re 1 on Etheirium and put his vote as message. And those who wish to verify can collect all messages on blockchain. I dont know if ethroum allows all this. If not, I plan to write such blockchain code to have OPEN VOTING, but for next few months, I am too too busy with other tasks.

(3) Using twitter , fb .i.e. well known PRIVATE websites
.

A list of nationwide twitterids of 10 crore Indian voters (or more and more come on twitter) can be made by Govt or by private persons

(4) voting on less known private websites
.
The problem of sellout is known. Say 10000 activists work and spend 100s of hours each to make a private website well know amongst say 1 crore voters. And then any eliteman can purchase that website. So each of these 10000 activists ended up wasting their 100 hours and only 1 person, namely owner of the website encashed.
.
We have problem of sellout in FB / twitter too. But activists dont have to make FB/twitter well know. They are already well know. So if FB/twitter sell out, then voters' time got wasted. But activists' loss of time was LESS than loss compared to problem of selling out of less known private website.
.
