Maharashtra govt to take control of Shani Shingnapur temple
.
21-jun-2018
.
MUMBAI: The state cabinet has approved a proposal to take over control of Shani Shingnapur temple in Ahmednagar district.
.
This came after an agitation by activist Trupti Desai two years back. The state will frame an Act for management of the temple. A bill will be table before the monsoon session, scheduled to commence on July 4 in Nagpur.
Desai welcomed the move but demanded 50% trustees and a priest should be women. The cabinet decision also came after several complaints about irregularities and corruption in the management system and process of appointment of committees.
.
Desai said it was a victory of her protest started in January 2016. She added that they plan to meet chief minister Devendra Fadnavis next week to demand appointment of female trustees. For over four centuries, women had been barred from stepping on to a high platform on which stands a black stone-symbolizing Lord Shanidev, the personification of planet Saturn. However, the temple trust in April 2016 took a decision to end the age-old discriminatory practice and allow women inside the sanctum sanctorum. The Maharashtra government had said that it will not allow any discrimination between men and women devotees.
.
