NO
.
This is called lip service.

Same way, jawahar ghazi and most congress leaders from 1915 to 1947 demanded universal voting. But these were just words. When they came to power, they showed least interest in universal suffrage and started promoting people in media who were opposing universal suffrage . Rajendra parasd, who opposed universal franchise, was made head of constituent assembly. It was hoped by all apex leaders, that they will be able to maneuver and postpone universal franchise.
.
In 1948, times of India was paid to write editorials portraying universal voting as dangerous.
.
All wanted to bury the issue of right to bear weapon and universal voting under the carpet. And none raised issue that jury trial be made fundamental right, which was case in USA constitution and magna Carta.
.
So apex congress rss cpi leaders were successful in burying two issues --- right to bear guns and jury trial.
.
They could not bury third issue due to ambedkar. But ambedkar also could not find even 10 members to support his proposal to make right to vote as fundamental right.
