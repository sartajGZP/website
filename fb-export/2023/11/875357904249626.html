This week in 2021, House of Gucci starring Lady Gaga and Adam Driver premiered in theatres.

The movie earned over $150 million at the global box office, becoming the highest-grossing drama of the year, received nominations for an Academy Award, a BAFTA, a Golden Globe, a Screen Actors Guild Award, and a Critics’ Choice Award, and established Gaga as a “butts-in-seats movie star” (Forbes).
