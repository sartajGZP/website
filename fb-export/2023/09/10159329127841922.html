How to ensure that paper ballots are not changed in paper ballot voting --- counting immidiately after voting ie very next minute
.
(01) In the paper ballot voting system I have proposed, voting will end at 3pm and counting starts immidiately on same table at 3.01pm. ie the ballot box will NOT be moved even an inch. This is MUST to ensure that ballot boxes are NOT changed.
.
(02) Later, there will be CONFIDENTIAL voting on whether voter should be given option of open ballot.
.
(03) In the system I have proposed, there will be one booth per 20000 to 50000 voters. The booth size will be large --- 100 feet * 100 feet hall , with pillars, no walls, on first floor, with ground parking. The table will have 1 ballot box per 1000 voters.
.
(04) Each voter will be given (kilometer distance between booth and his home * 2 * Rs 30) as presumed travel cost,. irrespecitive of actual cost.
.
(05) The voting time will be 9 am to to 3pm next day ie 30 hours. Voting will continue even during night. The booth have live camera
.
(05) Counting will start at 3.01 pm on same table
.
Its important to ensure that ballot papers are not swapped.
