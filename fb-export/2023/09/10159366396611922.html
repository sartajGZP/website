why I never invested a penny in share market till date

I offer NO investment advices to anyone. I am just mentioning my reasons

In share market, there are players like Soros, Buffet, Bill Gates etc who can create crisis like lockdown, wars etc when they want. Whereas I cant create such crisis. And they have advance knowledge of when interest rates will chaneg and by how much.

So they have advance knowledge of MAIN govt events to come. And I dont have that advance information. So I will lose against them

eg say today is jan-2022. I have no idea that Putin will start war in feb-2022. Now say a company is good. So I will decide to buy shares in jan-2022. But Soros etc know in jan-2022 that Putin will start war in feb-2022. So they will short this company. And they will win against me.

Likewise, say its oct-2019. Say a company is good and I decide to buy share. Now I have no knowledge in oct-2019 that Govt of Bharat is going to impose a year long lockdown in apr-2020. But Bill Gates, Soros know in oct-2019 that Govt of Bharat is going to impose lockdown in apr-2020.

Further, if I face cash crunch, then RBI or Fed Banks will never help me. But if bank owners face cash crunch, then RBI or Fed Banks will come and rescue them

Now as such, same applies in software. Some of them are far far ore intelligent than me, and more experienced and more resourceful. But they cannot write all code market needs. So there are many areas, where they will simply never come and compete with me. So my work is protected there

But in share market, since all decisions are just a few clicks away, thee apex are always competing against me and vice versa. So I am not likely to win

eg what is fate of an investor in Ukraine today if he had invested in the "best" companies of Ukraine in years from 2002 till jan-2022? Of course, if he had sold all shares in ukrainian companies in jan-2022 and bought shares in USA companies in jan-2022, then he would be fine. ( assuming he is not dead in war). But only soros, bill gates have such advance infiormation.

I sensed this issue in year 1994, when during my job in Morgan Stanley in New York City in USA. And next 5 years of coding in Finance companies further confirmed it. That these biggies can create crisis, and create wars.

So way back in year 1995, I decided to stay away from share market. And confine to real business like coding.

I have some real eastate, but I was NOT investing. I just bought some flats and one office for myself and for my parents and elder brother in year 1996, 1998 and 2004. It was real need for myself, my elder brother and my parents not for investment purpose. And prices went six times after year 2004. It was just good luck and not any "smart" decision.

And real estate is good, if you invest and rent. Otherwise, imo mere real estate is not always much beneficial in general.

Now are shares in general beneficial? One can answer after getting ALL sale purchases made by all individuals in say past 20 years. If this data is made available, then I can show this or that way. In absence of UNIVERSAL data, one will be dependent on SELECTIVE data, and conclusions will be pointless.
