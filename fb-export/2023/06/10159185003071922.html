why boiled water (heat at 100 degrees for 5 minutes and then cool it) has "more" vitamin/mineral than non boiled water? c coment
.
hint : this is may not be a chemistry question and may be a biology question !!
.
my answer : The vitamins and minerals in water are mostly trapped in bodies of microbes and other larger organisms, some as large as mosquitoe larve and tapeworm. Water is also full of insect eggs.
.
When these organisms are alive, and go in stomach, they can cause harm. Harm or no harm, human stomach cannot digest them much. Some microbes also create a TOUGHER cell wall around them to increase protection. So all in all, stomach is NOT able to extract the vitamins, minerals which are inside the cell walls and cell membranes.
.
But boiling kills all these organisms. So they are harmless. And their cell walls are broken or weak. And they are dead and cannot make cell wall tougher inside stomach. So the vitamins and minerals in their body ( so called cell cytoplasm) become avaiable to our digetive system
.
As a saying goes --- jinda haathi lakha kaa, maraa savaa lakh ka". This is more true for microbes !!! A dead microbe is worth more than alive in most (not all) cases.
.
So as such, some vitamins etc get decomposed due to boiling. But TOTAL supply increases.
.
Thats why, those who drink cooled boiled tap water seldom face vitamin B12 deficiency !! Because they get ample dead bacteria in their water !!!
.
