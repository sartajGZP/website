Air India passengers stuck in Russia forced to sleep on floor
.
( news report , 07-jun-2023)
.
Some Air India passengers have been kept in schools and some in the hostels due to non-availability of accommodation.

The passengers stuck in Russia’s Magadan are facing many problems after an engine glitch of the San Francisco-bound Air India flight led to its diversion on Tuesday. The stranded passengers are facing problems such as the availability of food and lodging and are not even able to get the medicines required.
