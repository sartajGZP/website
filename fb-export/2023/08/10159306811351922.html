Subjective declarations are 100% useless. Procedures are useful. Declarations r useful if 100% objective, which is never a case in politics. So why political science professors insist that students be confined to declarations?

In softare programming, declarations like "select all lines with string 'xxx'" are useful because they are 100% objective. But in sociology, politics etc, almost everything is subjective. So declarations like fundamental rights, 100s of princiiplaes and doctrines are all 100% uselss for people. But PROCEDURES like exact procedures of referendum, election of judges, election of police chief, election of CM, their recall procedures are useful.

Political science professors always insist on keeping students busy with declarations and insist on keeping away from procedural details. Imo, because they are paid by elitemen to do so
