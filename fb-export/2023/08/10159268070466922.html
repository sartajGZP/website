Why some inter activist meetings have to ve NO TOPIC MEETING?
.
10 lakh activists, 200 causes, 20 solutuons in each cause.
.
And to need mass to move for one specigic solution, at least 10000 activists are needed to support that one specific solution
.
This means that a large number of activists will need to spend less time on his solution and his cause.
.
So ctivism today needs a process, via which one activist A can possibly convince another activist B, that B should spend less time on his cause and his proposal and B should spend more time on cause of A and solution of A. And vice versa ie same process should also allow B to possibly convive A that A should be spend less time on his proposal and his cause and spend more time on cause of B and proposal of B.
.
Now if a meeting has TOPIC, then that itself will turn off 90% activists !!! Because TOPIC will not be of his proposal. When a meeting has TOPIC, it means -- no one will be allowed to speak on other topic even if that other topic is 100 times more important !!
.
So may be, all meetings need not be topicless. But at least some meetings must be topicless
