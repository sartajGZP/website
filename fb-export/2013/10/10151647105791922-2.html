Cow slaughter is BANNED in most all states of India, except WB, Kerala, Asam. So if you see cow slaughter on bakri-id on 16-oct-2013 or any day , pls do call police at 100 number at that place. If the local cop is non-corrupt, he may take necessary action.

Bull slaughter is banned in many states like Gujarat etc but legal in many states. Pls check law for you state at http://www.dahd.nic.in/dahd/reports/report-of-the-national-commission-on-cattle/chapter-ii-executive-summary/annex-ii-8.aspx

For details pls google "[state name] cow slaughter" and you will get links to PDFs .

Pls note that AAP, AK etc have demanded legalization of cow slaughter across India (if you wish to verify , then pls ask AK and get his statement on his issue on YT) . Anna was once opposed to cow-sluaghter once, but now keeps silence on it. Congress till 1980 opposed cow slaughter, but how supports it. BJP has opposed cow slaughter , but may soon say "pahale manav raksha aur manav vikas, baad me go raksha" . Those scientific people who oppose superstitions , for some vague reason, support cow slaughter on bakri-id etc. So IMO, those who want cow-slaughter to end are on their own. IMO, they should request voters to send necessary orders to MPs via SMS to print necessary laws to reduce cow slaughter. Which laws? See http://rahulmehta.com/301.htm , and search for word "cow"
