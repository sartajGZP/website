Christianity is rapidly replacing Hinduism in tribal areas in southern Gujarat, and perhaps whole of tribal India for that matter. Why? Because of rising costs of medicines, falling levels in govt schools, high interests rates from money lenders and superb education/medicine, credit societies and other charity works by missionaries. SOLUTIONS -- (1) pls order MPs via SMS to print MRCM-draft and RTR-District-Education-Officer-drafts in gazette (2) pls order MPs via SMS to print law to cancel product patents so that prices of medicine falls (3) pls order MPs via SMS to print SGPC like HMPC = "Hindu Mandir Prabanhdak Committee" act in gazette. Detils are given below.

Now pls note that all political leaders including AAP-leaders and Anna have opposed the proposal , that crores of citizens should directly send necessary orders to MPs via SMS, and they have also opposed the proposed RTR-DEO, MRCM draft. I dont know their stand on HMPC draft

===

A friend of mine visited about 5-8 southern gujarat tribal villages. The trip was sponsored by an NGO run by Missionaries. The NGO was looking for volunteers to work in poor tribal areas. I am writing what my friend told me.

===

All the villages he visited were now over 80% Christian. Most homes had bibles, jesus statutes, not many hindu murties etc.

The main reasons in his opinions, and I agree with him from what I have heard , are

(1) Missionaries are running very good schools for free. The kids in first class were able to speak simple english like "one two three upto hundred", "a b c d ... z" etc. The schools were free and were better than schools of ahmedabad which charge Rs 500 per month fee. Of course , the schools wont compare with DPS. And govt schools in those areas dont exist or are shabby.

(2) Missionaries are running hospitals for very low prices. Govt hospitals are far away and expensive due to corruption and are of poor quality.

(3) Money-lenders' heavy rates and heavy handedness ended after Missionaries came !! Tribals were poor and money lenders used to charge 10% to 15% interest per month and made them poorer !! Missionaries started credit societies etc and so interest rate came down to just 2% per month, and sometimes even less.

(4) Missionaries are also running employment providing small scale industries.

(5) Slowly, beef is being made a staple diet !!!

As per villagers, the villages were hell holes before Missionaries came in year 2000. Some villages still dont have electricity !!! The Missionaries setup hospitals, schools etc and cleaned up the villages.

Now this is part of Gujarat (southern Gujarat), a far far better off state. You can imagine the scene in Orissa, AP, Northern KN, Northern TN, Eastern MH , Jharkhand etc

A possible future scenario is that MNC-owners may use these tribals to create widespread insurgencies in India. It has happened across world, and also in several parts of India.

====

Solutions I propose

1. The Hindu temples have 10 times more money than Missionaries . But Hindu temples dont spend it on charity work. Why is that Gurudwara spend it on chairty and weapon-use-trainning, while temples dont? Becaue temple wealth is transferred by inheritance or guru-prataha. While in Missionaries, it is community property or owned by Vatican (and Vatican uses election, not inheritance or guru-pratha). So pls order MPs via SMS to print HMPC = Hindu Mandir Prabhandhak Committee act similar to SGPC = Sikh Gurudwara Prabandhak Committee act . I will soon publish full text of the act. The act creates one National Hindu Temple Trust, several State Hindu Temple Trust, several Sect Hindu Temples Trusts - one for each sect. Every Hindu will be member of National/State Hindu Trust and exactly one sect Hindu trust. The head of National/State trusts will be elected/recallable and the sect will specify where the head is elected or heridtary or via will. You may order MP after reading the act I have proposed, if you like the act

2. Pls order your MP via SMS to impose taxes on incomes\donations\wealth of ALL temples, mosques, churches and exemption will be Rs 500 per member per year. This has NOTHING to do with religion. But tax exemption to trusts is wrecking Indian military due to lack of funds.

3. Pls order your MP\MLA via SMS to print Right to Recall District Education Officer in Gazette. The biggest reason for change has been poor quality of Govt schools. And the quality will NOT improve unless we print RTR-DEO-draft in the Gazette.

4. Due to patent act, the prices of medicines have gobne 10-20-50 times. The Missionaries get medicine for free from MNC-owners. So pls order MPs via SMS to cancel patent act. This will reduce medicine price

5. Industries in tribal areas are NOT coming because Supreme Court judges have banned development in the name of environment and forest protection. Solution -- order MPs via SMS to print RTR over Supreme judges draft in Gazette and print Jury System in Supreme Court draft in Gazette.

6. Another reason industries are NOT growing in tribal areas is because of law-order problems. So pls order MPs via SMS to print Right to Recall Police Chief draft in Gazette, Jury System draft in Gazette, and RTR judges draft in Gazette so that law-order in tribal areas improve and more industries come.

7. Give free mobile-phones to all tribals, free solar charger, fee 500 minutes of talk time per month and free 500 SMS per month . This will enable contrators in urban areas to appraoch them and give them labor work.

8. details of above law-drafts are in http://rahulmehta.com/301.htm . In addition, pls download and seach the pdf on word "usury" "interest" and you will also see law-drafts I have proposed to reduce high interest rates money lenders charge.

====

Solutions I do NOT support

1. I oppose using state laws on non-violent-tribals or non-violent-Missionaries ---- Why? Following reasons

(a) Those such as VHP-apex who want state-laws to stop Missionaries give NO DRAFTS. And I dont support their draftless proposals. If someone wants to use state laws to curb Missionaries, pls first provide drafts

(b) If state laws are used against Missionaries, the MNC-owners will threaten PM of India that MNC-owners will give huge weapons to Pakistan, and reduce Military help to India. CIA can also enable 1000 of Kasabs across India.

(c) I see NO legally possible definition of "conversion". The person i.e. tribal or dalit is exercising his free choice of wroship. What can police\court do if one worships Jesus and not Indian Gods? Beat him? Imprison him? It doesnt work. Even Romans who used to throw Christians to hungry lions could not stop that

(d) It can be successful in short run eg Saddam Hussein of Iraq ended conversions using raw state power. But when US Military came, Iraqis could no longer use private violence or state power to stop conversion. And yes, US Military can also come in India, unless we improve our Military. And to improve Indian Military, we will need to improve courts/police etc. And of we can do that, then conversions will end anyway.

2. I oppose private violence on Missionaries and tribals --- if private violence is used, then by showing vidoes of private violence in USA, the Missionaries will manage to get 10 times more money and also weapons. eg when VHP killed Missionaries and tribals in Orissa , the Missionaries managed to get huge funds to buy media-support for Biju Patnaik and also got weapons from private citizens in USA which were given to Naxals. The Missionaries can always use Naxals as violent front. In private violence, naxals will have upper hadn as they can get huge weapons supply from MNC-owners

====

All in all, as of now, Missionaries are advancing because they have certain plus points. My porposal is to create BETTER PLUS points in Indian Govt and Hindu Temples. This can be done by the law-drafts I proposed. So pls give newspaper-ads to request over 40 crore voters to send orders to MPs via SMS to print these law-drafts.
