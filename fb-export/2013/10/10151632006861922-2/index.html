When you die, at the gates of heaven and hell, questions Chirtagupt will ask you will be "did you do your religious moral duty of sending necessary orders to MPs via SMS, to keep rajaa prajaadheen? which orders did you send via SMSes? none? why not? didnt have time? didnt have money?" . And if you havent sent necessary orders to MPs via SMS , you will lend in hell.

Atharvaved says (see Satyarth Prakasj chap-6 page-2) that "raajaa aur sabha dono prajaadheen hone chahiye" i.e. king=PM=CM and parliament=MPs\MLAs ought to be both dependent on citizens. And "chihiye" or "ought" means --- it is YOUR all citizens' religious duty to ensure that the king and MPs is dependent on us citizens. And so you must send necessary orders to MPs via SMS.

And what if you fail to do this religious duty?

Well, when you die, at the gates of heaven and hell, the first question Chitragupt will ask is "did you do your religious moral duty of sending necessary orders to MPs via SMS, to keep rajaa prajaadheen? And next questions he will ask is

2. which orders did you send via SMSes?

And if you answers is "none", then next questions will be

3. Why not?

4. didnt have time? didnt have money?

And answers like "my leader arvindbhai told me not to send orders to MPs via SMS" wont count.

eg Vedas say that it is your religious duty to protect cows. This means it is your religious duty to ensure that MPs print law-drafts in Gazette to protect cows. And so it is your religious duty to order MPs via SMS to print law-drafts to protect cows. If cows are saved, you get punya. And attempt to save cows also bring punya. And so your order to MP via SMS print laws to protect cows will bring you punya. And not sending this order to MPs via SMS is failing your religious duty, and so will bgring you paap.

And Chirtagupt has all records. He has copy of every SMS you sent and copy of every SMS you deliberately decided not to send. Chirtagupt will know whether you had time or not. And Chirtagupt also knows whether you had money to send order to MP via SMS or not. He will ask you, only so that he can convince you why you should be sent to hell or heaven.

And if you havent done your religious duty of sending orders to MPs via SMS to keep "raajaa prajaadheen", then you will be sent to hell. And if you have sent orders to MPs via SMS, then you will find place in heaven

===

And if you dont send orders to MPs via SMS, then you may reach hell before you die. i.e. it is very likely that India will become hell in few years to come. And if you send orders to MPs via SMS, then you may reach heaven before you die. i.e. it is very likely that India will become heaven in few years to come.
