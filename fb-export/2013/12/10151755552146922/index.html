Why AK is worse than Congress\BJP leaders? Because BJP\Congress leaders are NOT wasting true-activists time. While AK is wasting away crores of activist hours.

My point has been that AAP is worse than Congress\BJP\CPM and AK is doing more damage to India than Congress\BJP\CPM leaders . Why? Because Congress\BJP\CPM leaders never waste time of activists who want system change and thus the Congress\BJP\CPM leaders Congressi\BJPians are NOT wasting resources of movements for system change. So while Congress\BJP leaders indeed do harm to India, they are not blocking the remedy. Whereas AK is wasting time and resources of movements who want system change, and thus AK is blocking the remedy.

A criticism of AAP which proves that AAP is as bad as Congress\BJP is IMO completely correct but still time-waste. And a criticism that AK is as hopeless as any Congressi is also correct but still time-waste. Why? Because AAPians will validly refute this argument as "see, if we are as bad as Congress\BJP then why shouldnt you vote for us? If we are as bad as Congress\BJP, then pls throw a dice and give us equal chance". And their argument is valid. If you have to pick one person for the position of security gaurd, and all three applicants are thieves, then you would pick the least dishonest thief, and in this case it would be AAP = AK. So argument that #AAPisCongress3 and #AKisCongressi3 doesnt go far. Instead we should show facts that convince activists that AK is worse than worst of Congress\BJP leaders !!

I would give one example of correct but time-pass argument against AK. Many rightly say that AK had cut deals with BJP and Congress leaders which ensured that he will never get transfers outside Delhi. Not only it is fact, but story is worse. In early 1990s, AK developed contacts with Missionaries, who gave him posting in Delhi to begin with !! To get posting in Delhi as deputy commissiner , one needs to give bribe of Rs 1 crore today (so it would be Rs 10 lakhs in 1990s), and AK didnt have that kind of money back than. So AK developed contacts with Missionaries in early 1990s and promised Missionaries to work as their agent, and so Missionaries got him posting in Delhi. And from 1990s to 2005. he kept good contacts with Congress as well s BJP leaders which ensured that he and his wife get posted in Delhi or vicinity only. But still this fact is timepass. Why? Because all it proves is that AK is just another Congressmen. So AAPians gives a valid argument "Sheila Dixit is corrupt, Harshvardhan is corrupt, AK is compromised" so why vote for first two and not for us?

So I ask ALL to focus on the FACT that AK is worse than worst of Congressmen and worse than worst of BJP leader. And that FACT is ---

(1) remedy to our ills is system change i.e. laws that improve India such as Right to recall, Jury System Congress leaders and BJP leaders are NOT wasting time

(2) the most important resource to bring system change is activist and his time aka activist-hours

(3) worst Congressmen leaders and worst BJP leaders are NOT wasting time of activists who mwant system change. The leaders flatly say now, and show them the door !!

IOW, Congress/BJP leaders are problems, but they dont block the remedy by wasting activist-hours.

Whereas AAP = AK lies to activists that AK = AAP is committed to Right to Recall , tax reforms, reduce corruption in supreme judges, reduce nepotism in supreme judges etc. and due to paid media, true activists who want system change start believing in him. And AK drains away their ebergy by making them do useless tasks like naarebaazi, rallying, banner holding, leader pooja etc etc. AK tell activists NOT to study drafts, not to study even 40 page Janlokpal draft , not to ask questions on law/administration etc and thus wastes away whole time.

For the movement of system change the ONLY resource the true activist and his time. The movement for system change will never get paid-media coverage. It will get get support from PIL as most High and Supreme judges are corrupt/nepotic. And so movements for system change depend ONLY on how many activist-hours it gets from true activists. And AK using his image created by paid-media, is consuming away and wasting away of all activist-hours.

AK is like a weed on steroids. How do weeds kill plants like wheat etc? The weed will consume all water, minerals and the wheat plant will starve due to lack of water and minerals. Some weeds also release toxins that damage roots of wheat plants. And weed doesnt give any fruit or seeds to eat. A weed simply consumes water and evaporates it away. AK is doing the same thing. He consumes TRUE unpaid activists who want system change and he wastes away all their hours in vote cutting, naare baazi, banner holding etc.

And AK is more effective in blocking system change. eg some 3 years back, a Congress leader had said that "Right to recall needs to change in Constitution" thre by making RTR look like expensive remedy. And no true activist believed as he was a Congresssi. But now AK, Yogendra Yadav and Sanjay Singh mutter same lie that "Right to recall will require Constitutional Amendment" and activists believe him !! And thus AK delayed RTR-movement in India by discouraging activists !!

And thus, AK is blocking remedy to India's evil.

Other leaders are diseases. AK is worse --- he is someone who burning medicine shops.

====

So I request ALL to focus on the point that "AK is worse than worst of Congress leader and worst of BJP leader" and NOT use that arguments that ONLY prove that "AK is as bad as Congressmen". Say #AAPisWorseThanCongressBJP and #AKiisWorseThanCongressBjpLeaders
