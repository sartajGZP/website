Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate

Mon Dec 23 2013 17:27:22 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

Dear folks, AK is 100% fraud. But lets NOT criticize AK for wrong reasons , because in such case right issues will lose focus. To be specific, lets NOT criticize on his forming Govt with passive support from Congress, but lets focus on the RIGHT issues that (1) AK is refusing to put Right to Recall Delhi Corporator and RTR Delhi Mayor draft in Delhi Assembly, and that (2) AK is now opposing SMS-voting by keeping counts confidential !!

BJP decided not to form Govt for valid reasons. Now AAP was given second option. If AAP doesnt form Govt, there will be Presidential rule. Who gains? No one (except PM). And who loses --- may be AAP loses, but so would people of Delhi. So whatever step AAP took by forming Govt, AAP has reduced the "otherwise losses" of people of Delhi.

Besides, AAP got 28% of polled votes and 40% of seats. We in India have seen Govts with lesser votes and lesser seats. The solution is to have Governor style system where people DIRECTLY elect Governor, and also have Preferential Voting like Ireland or Two Round Voting so that final winner has clear 51% approval. But we dont have such system today. Today, we have system where CM needs 36/70 MLAs, or repoll followed by may be another repoll and so forth.

And people of Delhi did say that they want change from BJP\Congress. Otherwise, AAP would got 5% voted only. Now we all know that it is manipulation of paid media which got him middle class votes, and Congress paid its social workers to transfer Congress's slum\dalit\muslim votes to AAP. But then whatever may be the case, we must respect the final vote count. And final vote count says --- give BJP first chance and give AAP second chance. Now if BJP cant form Govt, then there is nothing wrong in AAP forming Govt. Besides, AAP gave no Ministry to Congress. AAP has so far said that it will NOT show any favor to Congress even if Govt were to break. i.e. they will run Govt without fear of losing support of Congress. Time will tell what they do. But so far, they have NOT made any substantial compromises on "taking/giving support" issue.

===

And finally, I had sent SMS to AAP to form Govt. So I should be last one to criticize now.

And main reason I want all NOT to focus on this minor issue, is that we are missing major backstabbing by AK !!

1) AK has back stabbed all voters by refusing the proposal to bring Right to Recall Delhi Mayor draft and RTR Delhi Corporator drafts

2) AK asked all to vote on SMS number, and now he is refusing to give counts of vote. Now what use is voting, if counts are NOT disclosed?

And his continued support for Bangladeshis, juvenile criminals etc etc is still there.

IMO it is time, we FOCUS on these issues, and not on issues where "good choice or bad choice" was still the "only choice left for people of Delhi".

===

And now lets prepare for next round of drama. IMO, we should convince as many activists as possible to ask AK to disclose the counts it gets on SMS-server. And if AK doesnt, then more and more activists will realize that AK is fraid.
