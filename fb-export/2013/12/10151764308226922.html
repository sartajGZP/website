ECI says NOTA will be counted as invalid votes

8-dec-2013

Election Commission of India has issued directives about of counting of votes polled under the option “None of the Above” (NOTA). According to the commission NOTA votes will be counted as invalid votes. The commission has directed to computation of forfeiture of security deposit on the basis of candidates having secured total valid votes. The CEO office has apprised all collectors and district election officers and returning officers about commission”s directives.
