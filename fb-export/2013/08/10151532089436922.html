FAQ-002 : What if my MP is in other 20% i.e. my MP hasnt put his no on Loksabha website?
Ans : If your MP's cell no is NOT on Loksabha website, then pls send order to near by MP of his own party with a line at top "please fwd this SMS to shri XYZ". Likewise , if your MLA's cell no is NOT on website, then then pls send order to near by MP\MLA of his own party with a line at top "pls fwd this SMS to shri XYZ".
