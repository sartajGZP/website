A great business opportunity --- putting camera in front and back of two-wheelers to deter rash rich drivers.

Now two-wheeler drivers are scared and there comes a good win-win business opportunity. One can make a full solution, with rugged camera in front and back, and this decreases the chances that driver can get away.

Even if 1% two-wheelers buy this solution, this can be great business opportunity.
