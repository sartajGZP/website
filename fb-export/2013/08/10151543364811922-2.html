Say an activist-A has read 1000 books on economics, politics, administration etc including mine, with hope to reduce problems of India. And say an activist-B , who wants to reduce problems of India, asks activist-A "pls recommend me 5 books to read", then my book should be one those 5 !! This was the second goal I had in my mind when I started writing in 1996. And I think, I have finally met this goal.

The first goal was to of course, to reduce problems of India, not to write a good book. But writing book and solving problems of India go together. Why? Because unless one writes, 1000s cant read, and so more clone-activists cant come. So good writings are necessary these days, though in past many brought monumental changes, good or bad, without writing anything (*1). So after years and years of reading 1000s of articles and 100s of books on economics. politics, law, administration, sociology (and of course maths, science, computer engineering etc), I started writing in around 1996. The first book came out in oct-1998. And since then, I have been adding to it. And at that time, my goal was

1. Say one activist-A who wants to reduce problems of India has read 1000 books including mine

2. Say another activist-B who also wants to reduce problems of India asks activist-A "pls recommend me 5 books to read"

3. Then my book should in the list of five !!

And today, from the feedbacks I get, I think I have met this goal.

===

The first version of the book came out in oct-1998. It some 112 pages (*2) . My friends asked me to make a summary, so I made 20 page summary. And some friends asked for shorter summary and so I made 12 page summary. And then some asked for even shorter summary --- and I made 550 page (*3) volume with plan to write 4 more such 500 page volumes. The first volume came out in jan-2013. the remaining are in pipeline. Why? I decided to cover those activists who were willing to spare time to read 50000 pages to reduce problems of India. And thru them, I believed I could reach more and more activists. Now it looks like I was perhaps on right track.
