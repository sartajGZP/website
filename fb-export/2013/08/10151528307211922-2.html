Make every dairy to print "we may sell our old cows/buffalo to slaughter houses" mark OR print "we NEVER sell our old cows/buffalo to slaughter houses" mark on every milk bag.

The dairies etc which dont sell animals to slaughter houses will have more expensive milk. But using this label, those who want to reduce cow\buffalo slaughter can buy that expensive milk, and thus cattle going to slaughter house will reduce.

So pls order your MP via SMS to enact a law, that would require dairy to print "we sell or do not sell our old cows/buffalo to slaughter houses" mark on every milk bag.
