Tue Sep 03 2013 16:02:40 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

To those who wish Kiran Bediji to become Delhi CM from BJP , I request you all to order/request Delhi BJP District heads via SMS to announce Kiran Bediji as Delhi BJP CM candidate

Better late than never.

Even since Kiran Bediji retired, she had wished to become BJP-leader, and also Delhi CM, a position she is very much qualified and deserving for. In fact, the chair of Delhi CM would be honored to have her, and citizens of Delhi would feel honored to have her as CM. But people like Goel, Sushma etc stopped her from entering !! Why? Well, why not? Goel didnt want any CM rival inside BJP. And Sushma didnt want any competitor for top woman slot in BJP. But despite BJP's refusal, Kiran Bediji didnt join Congress of any party despite many parties inviting her. She started NGO etc and did social-political work.

When I saw Bediji in Ariel advertisement , I felt bad. Why should a lady of such high stature in India market a videshi product? But then whom do you blame? What would a person do if so called Nationalist party keeps an unwelcome stand. When I saw Kiran Bediji like class-A traitors and PIL-traders like Prashant Bhushan (I have known him since 1990s , as he has been supporter of independent Kashmir since 1990s), I felt bad --- why a patriotic lady is working with such class-A traitors? But again, what do you expect when so called Nationalist party gives no space?

But thankfully, she didnt convert. She retained her pro-Swamy-Ramdevji stand, and attended jun-2012 anshan of Swami Ramdevji. She got show cause notice for that. But she didnt bother. Later, she was asked to say "Congress corrupt, BJP corrupt", but she refused to say "BJP corrupt" , and in a way, rightly so, because the statement was fabricated only to create a third party to cut NaMo votes. And when Arvind Ghandy, adopted son of Soorpankha Ghandy, formed party to cut NaMo votes, so that Bianca Ghandy can become PM, Bediji didnt join that party. She was welcomed to join that party several times, and offered no. 2 position but still she didnt join.

Despite all this, Delhi-BJP-apex is opposing her entry in Delhi-BJP.

What is appaling is that Delhi-BJP-members and Delhi-BJP-voters are still sleeping.

Well folks, if a deserving person is ignored for long, dont take things for granted and assumed. Under such unjust treatment, one may end up joining wrong side. If you dont respect merit, then dont complain if merit joins the other side. What good do you see in Goel and company? Not that Bediji is best (she is pro-MNC, anti-Swadeshi and I have one more reason against her --- she is anti Right to Recall). But pls do consider her with people like Goel who are completely corrupt from head to toe.

All in all, enough of actionlessness. I request all BJP-supporters and BJP-members-members of Delhi to order/request BJP-district-head via SMS to offer CM-candidate-position to Kiran Bediji

===

Disclaimer --- I am anti-BJP, and I have requested all to vote ONLY candidates who have supported Jury System draft, Right to recall draft, wealth tax draft etc or use 49-o. And Congress, AAP, BJP all have opposed RTR-draft, Jury-draft etc.AAP only talks about RTR, but opposed all RTR-drafts it got and insists on time-pass on matter of RTR-dfraft. But if BJP gets a better CM-candidate, I think, it is better for Delhi and for India)
