Questions to AAP-activists

1. Situation : Say on the advice of you all AAP-activists, some of us 80 crore voters vote for AAP and elect 10 or 50 or 100 or 200 or 300 or 400 AAP MPs in may-2014. Say in jun-2014, some 75% of AAP MPs sell out the way 75% of Janata Party MPs sold out in 1977. And suppose that these 75% sold out MPs refuse to pass RTR-MP-draft OR print utterly useless signature based RTR-draft .

Question : Then what suggestions you AAP activists will give to us citizens in july-2014?

===

2. Delhi has 1.23 crore voters. Say AAP gets majority assembly and say AAP elects a CM. Say some days later, say 62 lakh Delhi voters order AAP-CM to resign via SMS. Will AAP-CM resign right away?

3. Say AK wins from New Delhi which has about 200,000 voters. Say after few days, some 101,000 voters order him to resign via SMS. Then does he promise to resign next day.

===

Plato told me some 2500 years ago, that in politics , one MUST answer the questions he asks, or he should not ask questions So here goes my answers at Right to Recall Party

1. There is NOTHING party-chief, party-members etc can do if 75% or even 51% MPs sell out. The citizens will have to force PM\CMs to get RTR-MP, RTR-MLA, RTR-PM, RTR-CM drafts in gazette.

2. RRP promises that if majority of state voters order via SMS to resign then RRP's CM will resign next day. But again, if CM sells out , pls see answer-1

3. RRP promises that if majority voters of MLAs order via SMS to resign then RRP-MLA will resign. But of MLA sells out, then see Answer-1
