Today, it is Soorpankha Ghandy (* - see footnote), tomorrow it may be NaMo or any other Indian diplomatic passport holder. So pls order your MP via SMS to order Foreign Minister to expel any one US diplomat as retaliation.

If an Indian , and that too a senior political leader is treated against international norm, in a foreign country, we must retaliate against that country in same proportion. And our views against that leader should NOT come in that decision.

Soorpankha Ghandy has committed no crime in USA. Whatever crimes she has committed are in India, and we Indians have to decide it using Jury System, Right to Recall, narco-tests in public etc whenever that comes.

Today, US court is bullying Soorpankha Ghandy. Tomorrow, it can be any other diplomatic passport holder. And I would tolerate such summons if that Indian diplomat had committed a crimes in USA. But thats not the case here. This summons are outright insult to India.

So we must retaliate against US court's decision to issue summons on her. So we should expel a US diplomat for their action.

So I would request ALL to order MPs via SMS to order Foreign Ministry to expel one US diplomat.
