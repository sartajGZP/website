Attn all recallists : A modification in ordering MPs via SMS --- add words at end "or else I will not vote for you" . Arvind Gandhi opposed this modification as well as proposal to order MPs via SMS

(eg1) I have asked voters of Ahmedabad to order MPs via SMS as follows "I am ordering you to cancel law which makes digital cable compulsory. Cancel the law or else I will not vote for you"

(eg2) I have asked voters of Ahmedabad to order MLAs via SMS as follows "I am ordering you to print Right to Recall District Police Chief procedure in Gazette. Pls print thsi procedure, or else I will vote against you"

Arvind Gandhi has opposed this SMS for several reasons. first he supports law which makes analog cable illegal and makes digitall cable compulsory as MNC-owners want digital cable as it is easier for large companies to control it. Second, if voters send orders to MPs directly , then people like him will lose all business. And lastly, of voters add a threat line that " ... or else, I will not vote for you", then ordering via SMS may have strong effect !! All AAP activists have been asked to oppose this "request 80 crore voters to order Mps via SMS" campaign !!!!
