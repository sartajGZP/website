I came across interesting question --- say in 1940 if 15 crore Indians out of population of adult 28 crore had sent postcards to viceroy to quit India, would they have quit? If not, then why would today's PM obey orders via SMS from 40 crore voters?

The question is valid, and very interesting.

My proposal to convince 40 crore voters to send order to MPs via SMS is to get one of the two things done --- (a) EITHER get that order printed in Gazette OR (b) prove to 40 crore voters that "so called PM is America's viceroy to India, and NOT PM of India".

And is (b) is proved, that moral basis for Mahatma Udham Singh to visit Viceroy of India is established. If (b) is proved, then Udham Singh will visit American Viceroy to India, and ask him to act like "non viceroy real PM" or make space for "non viceroy real PM"

IOW, SMS campaign is either to get things done, or prove to over 40 crore voters that Indian PM is in reality is American Viceroy.

And why is this proof important? Because Udham Singh will never visit a person who is PM of India. He will visit so called fake-PM of India, only if majority of Indians call him as "American Viceroy to India". Now if over 40 crore voters send same order to MPs via SMS to print some law in Gazette, then if so called PM still refuses, that it proves that he is NOT PM of India. In which case, it is now established that is American Viceroy to USA. And then and then ONLY it is OK for Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh to meet so called fake-PM of India.

Now in 1940, there was NO need to ask 15 crore Indians to send postcards to viceroy to quit India. Why? Because the then viceroy never lied that he is PM of India. He openly called himself as viceroy of India and had openly said that "hell with you all 28 crore Indians' opinions". Today, the confusion is because so called PM says "I respect opinions of 80 crore Indian voters". And unless we prove this notion as a lie, the confusion would prevail. And so asking 40 crore voters is necessary to end the confusion.

All in all, either we use freedom, or lets prove that freedom doesnt exist, so that we can OFFICIALLY ask Udham Singh to bring freedom.

So where do RTR-drafts, Jury-drafts, wealth-tax drafts, TCP-draft etc fit in?

Mahatma Udham Singh needs to be convinced that he is NOT just fighting against evil, but things may become good when all the fight ends. A possibility of better India is the REAL motivation, not just faults in existing state of affairs. So the SMS-texts should be so, that they convince Udham Singh that it can improve India if printed in Gazette, and so it is worth his time to meet the American Viceroy to India, and ask him to act like "real PM" or make space for real PM. IMO, RTR-drafts, Jury-drafts, wealth-tax drafts, TCP-draft can improve India. So IMO, these drafts may convince Mahatma Udham Singh.
