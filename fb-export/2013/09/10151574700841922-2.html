The court has give mere 3 years light print sentence to the juvenile. Juvenile thanks Arvind Ghandy for opposing proposal that activists should order MPs via SMS to reduce juvenile age to 16 years effective 1-1-2010

On 30-jan-2010, I had requested activists to request all 80 crore voters of India to order MPs via SMS to reduce juvenile age to 16 years, and make it effective from 1-jan-2010 .

see my status link at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10151233673441922

The juvenile has sent sincere thanks to Arvind Ghandy, Anna and Subramanian Swamy who opposed my proposal at Right to Recall Group, made on 30-jan-2010, and repeated several times.

My proposal was that activists should request 80 crore voters of India to order MPs via SMS to reduce juvenile age to 16 years, and make it effective from 1-jan-2010 .

The activists liked the proposal, but they all said that their leaders namely Anna, Arvind Ghandy and Subramanian Swamy has opposed the very idea that voters should send orders to MPs via SMS. And Subramanian Swamy insisted on court drama only, and Anna\Aravind instead that juvenile age must not change !!

Shame on activists who follow such leaders
