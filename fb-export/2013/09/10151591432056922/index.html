Congress IT cell head has asked all congress volunteers across all communities, to create a pro-AAP profile , using their own real name or some Hindu name and convert pro-NaMo voters into pro-Arvind-Ghandy, by saying "BJP bad, Congress bad , AAP good" etc . Solution? Pls see last para of the post.

Basically, Congress gameplan on facebook is as follows

1. Facebook will have some 10 crore to 15 crore voters out of 80 crore voters across India by march-2014

2. Facebook users are 80% pro-NaMo, anti-Congress. Less than 10% are pro-Congress. And Congress has realized that there is NO way they can convince them to vote for Congress .

3. So Congress IT cells have asked its volunteers to wear AAP cap, and also use Hindu male\female fake profile or use their real profile, and approach all NaMo-fans.

4. And try to convince them that AK is better than NaMo, and thus cut NaMo votes and make Congress win.

5. Even if Congress volunteers, posing as AAP-activists, get 10% success over facebook, that would over 1 crore NaMo votes less.

Congress IT cells has asked them give following arguments ----

(a) "Congress bad , BJP bad, so AAP good". "Sonia bad, NaMo bad, so Arvind Ghandy good". Something like "males bad, females bad, hence middle-sex good, no proof needed"

(b) NaMo is good, but BJP is all bad, One man cant do much. So vote for AAP

(c) NaMo is murderer, Godhara godhara, riot, riot etc etc

===

So folks, do you see an AAP-cap wearing profile in your friend-list chanting one or more of the above 3 mantra? Then his profile may be fake. And even if real, he is a Congress-agrent, who cant convince you to vote for Sonia and so he is asking you to vote for AK. On election day, that AAP-cap guy will wear AAP cap, but vote for Congress.

====

SOLUTION

1. Refer to Arvindbhai as "Arvind Ghandy, adopted son of Soorpankha Ghandy, who has formed party to cut NaMo votes so that her sister Bianca Ghandy can become PM".

2. Mock their "Congress bad , BJP bad, so AAP good" slogan and "Sonia bad, NaMo bad, so Arvind Ghandy good" slogan with "males bad, females bad, hence middlesex good" counter-slogan.

3. Ask him that every officer who fights against corruption gets transferred dozen times a year. How come Arvind Ghandy was never transferred outside Delhi even once?

4. Ask him that every officer who fights against corruption, his wife, if officer, also gets transferred dozen times a year. How come AK's wife who is also income tax officer, has never been transferred outside NCR area?

5. Ask him that if AG wants system-change, then why is he delaying RTR-MP-draft he had promised in apr-2011? and why did AK opposed the proposal to print RTR-Janlokpal-clauses in Janlokpal-draft?

6. If Arvind Ghandy wants better India, why did he oppose death penalty for juvenile murderer? Why did he insist on keeping juvenile age as 18 yrs? why did he oppose the proposal to put all land-ownership records on net? why is he opposing narco-tests in public on corrupt Ministers ? why is he opposing narco-test on murderer suspects of Santosh Koli? Why is he opposing narco-test in public on rape accused and even rape convicts?

IOW, put arguments that Arvind Ghandy is opposing all positive reforms. And if AAP-cap wants proofs, then ask him to visit Arvindbhai, ask question, and put Arvindbhai's answers on youtube.

===

Also, put statuses on following FACTS , so that NaMo-fans dont fall in argiments of "NaMo bad, Sonia bad, AK good"

(1) Arvind Ghandy opposed death penalty for juvenile murderer

(2) he insisted on keeping juvenile age as 18 yrs

(3) he opposed the proposal to put all land-ownership records on net

(4) AG opposed narco-tests in public on corrupt Ministers

etc etc and so AG doesnt want any system change at all.
