Gujarat Samachar YESTERDAY (29-nov-2013, friday) in PLUS supplement page-2 has given 100% unpaid coverage to "order your MP via SMS" method to reduce problems. Apparently a person known to press reporter had problems in his society. So all 1000 persons in his and near by society ordered their municipal corporator via SMS to solve that problem or they will not vote for him and his party !! And the problem got fixed in 2 weeks !! So the reporter tried to locate source of the idea and came across my assistant Harshad Solanki , who had suggested this "order your MLA via SMS" method . And 100s of voters are now sending orders to MPs\MLAs via SMS. The article doesnt mention my name because newspaper has policy not to put politicians' name in PLUS section of its newsspaper. So pls download this JPG and read it. And if you have access to 29-nov-2013 Gujarat Samachar, then pls read it and show it all.

The good thing is that despite opposition of ALL MPs, all MLAs , all party leaders and despite opposition of activist leaders like Anna, Arvind Gandhi etc, the activists and voters have started sending orders to their MPs, MLAs etc via SMS.
