Surajit Dasgupta:

How the AAP misleads people by exaggerating facts, misrepresenting its constituency and manipulating news:

1. There was a concert at Jantar Mantar last evening apparently to celebrate the Aam Aadmi Party. When the programme was on, the party's enthusiasts began tweeting attendance figures of anything between 10,000 and 100,000 sitting at homes across the world or from the venue. The fact: The maximum capacity of Jantar Mantar is 3,000.

2. Bollywood actress Amrita Rao happened to endorse the AAP today through a tweet. And the party's gang of fans went ecstatic! Why does a party that claims to be of the aam aadmi start jumping up and down in joy whenever a Khaas aadmi or aurat endorses it? This is the very mentality that made them invite a serial abuser of an entertainment channel to campaign for the party, which went on to embarrass them and they had to apologise for it. Story 1 was related. What does Vishal Dadlani understand about politics and nation building?

3. The EC has said the CD of the purported sting operation on some members of the AAP does not violate the model code of conduct. It means it was not wrong to do the sting or air the footage. How does it translate to a clean chit to the AAP? But that is what they are claiming it to be!
