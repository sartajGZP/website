The plan I propose to improve India is

1. activists should explain law-drafts to activists and 80 crore voters via newspaper advt etc

2. activists should ask activists and 80 crore voters to send orders to MP via SMS with #hashcodes to print those draft. #hashcodes are so that MP\PM can create totals if they wish to

3. if over 40 crore voters send order to MP via SMS then, [b]AMUS[/b] = Ahimsamurti Mahatma Udham Singh will visit and convince PM to print those drafts in Gazette.

4. Pm of next PM :) will agree

Now your questions and corresponsing asnwers are

===

Q1 : If the whole idea is 'Mahathma Bhagath Singh', then what is the need for sms?

Ans : If AMUS visits PM before 40 crore voters send SMS, he would get labeled as criminal, and will not invoke more AMUS if he fails. The number is justice and justice is number. Unless a proposal has PROVEN majority, or at least SEMI-PROVEN majority, it has no moral force and PM who refuses to print a proposal NOT backed by majority it is NOT a Bharatdrohi. But once 40 crore bhartiaya voters has issued orders , and yet PM doesnt print, and then it is proved that PM is Bharat-drohi.

Before 1947, there was NO procedure of election, and so AMUS needed no SMS or anything to establish that British were Bharatdrohhi. But today, due to procedure of election, PM is NOT Bharat-drohi unless proven so. So only way to prove that PM is bharat-drohi is to have proof that he is disobeying order of 40 crore voters. And for that , 40 crore voters have to send an order first. Now SMS is cheapest and least errorprone way to send order to MP.

===

Q2. Bhagath Singh didn't need any sms campaign.

Ans2 : Because in those days, it was established that Viceroy is Bharat-drohi. And so no need for SMS.

===

Ans3 : In case of candle\charkha, there is no method to obtain count of proof. Where as SMS with #hashcode can be counted. And SMS doesnt drain away time and energy, which charkha, bhaja, rally, slogans do. SMS preserves the energy for next round :) of "talks".

(1) NO voter gets feedback from any MP's server that "SMS was recieved and count of your hashcode was N", that itself becomes a POSITIVE proof that ALL MPs are chor" . eg Anna and AK both refused to implement SMS server which will send feedback to voters SMS with #hashcode. So many activists are now convinced that AK\Anna are just like Congresssi.

(3) Once counts of #hashcode become publicly known and crosses 40 crore, the MPs will lose courage to say NO and AMUS will gain courage to visit PM.
