Does Indian Evidence Act, 1872 says that facts stated in Laws\Gazette written in London be taken as fact even today ?? !!

Folks, I wont get time to gather more information on this. But this connected with art-147 debate. Here is section-37 of Indian Evidence Act . Pls read and get more info and form opinion.

==== see indiankanoon . org/doc/1525862/

37. Relevancy of statement as to fact of public nature contained in certain Acts or notifications.-

When the Court has to form an opinion as to the existence of any fact of a public nature, any statement of it, made in a recital contained in any Act of Parliament 3[ of the United Kingdom] or in any 1[ Central Act, Provincial Act or 2[ a State Act] or in a Government notification or notification by the Crown Representative appearing in the Official Gazette or in any printed paper purporting to be the London Gazette or the Government Gazette of any Dominion, colony or possession of his Majesty is a relevant fact]. 3[

1. Cf. the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Act 5 of 1908 ), Sch. I, Order VII, Rule 17. As to admissibility in evidence of certified copies of entries in Bankers' books, see the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 1891 (18 of 1891 ), s. 4.

2. Subs. by the A. O. 1948 for" any Govt. in British India".

3. Ins by the A. O. 1950.

========

IOW, words "[of the United Kingdom]" were inserted in 1950 !!!
