Patanjali Gomutra / Panchgavya etc does NOT have label of "desi cow" !!! BST workers are telling us all that all Patanjali products use Desi Cow milk , Desi gomutra etc. But then label on product is NOT "desi cow".
.
So it is POSSIBLE that it is made of Jersey cow gomutra and Jersey cow milk
.
In that case, it may NOT have any advantage that Ayurved texts say
.
So my point is --- if you are buying ANY cow product with hope of advantage that it will have benefits as given in Ayurvedic texts, then pls make sure that it has "desi cow" label.
