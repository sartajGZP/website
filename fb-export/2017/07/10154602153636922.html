GST will make accounting simple - says SoMoKe and congress / rss / aap workers. Then why is Centraol Govt training 500,000 youth for GST accounting?
.
GST seems to be a NAREGA for accountants.
.
With GST, excise \ vat \ service tax \ local body taxes and entertainment tax, octroi etc are all gone. So lakhs of CAs and non-Chartered-accountants who were previously working on excise , VAT , service tax etc can now work on GST
.
And GST is "simpler". Plus GSTN should make all accounting much easier. And there are 10s of GST software to do things more simple.
.
So we should be having a surplus in accountants.
.
But Central Govt wants to train some 500,000 more youth in GST accounting !!
.
