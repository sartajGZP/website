The Union Ministry of Minority Affairs has decided to introduce a scheme to offer a financial assistance of Rs 51,000 towards the marriage of girls, who availed the ministry’s scholarship for education.
.
“We decided in a meeting day before yesterday, like ‘Shadi Mubarak’ in Telangana, the girl child who does graduation by availing our scholarship, we will give Rs 51,000 as ‘Shadi Shagun’,” Union Minister of State for Minority Affairs (independent charge) Mukhtar Abbas Naqvi said in Hyderabad on Saturday.
