Steps law-proposers need to take
---------------------------------
.
WARNING - if you are a non-activist voter (someone who can give less than 4 hrs a week) then pls do NOT this post. This post has cumbersome details for law-proposing activist. These details will only unnecessarily confuse you. If you are a non-activist voter, and not an activist, then pls directly see fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154842453071922
.
And if you are an activist, but not a law-draft-proposer, then pls read this post and these see (LinkToBePut). This post is ONLY a reference for you. The main post for you is in previous link.
.
ALL REASONS BEHIND MAKING THESE STEPS ARE EXPLAINED IN SEPARATE STATUSES. Pls see link in first comment to see logic and reasons behind the proposed steps.
.
=======
.
There are two types of steps I suggest law-draposes to take
(A) onetime steps - steps you will need to take once
(B) everytime steps - steps you will need to take everytime you propose a new law-draft (or make translation)
.
(A) One time steps :
.
(A.1) Pls keep profile cover picture which says --- "please click this cover pic, read description, to see law-drafts I support" in any 2-3 languages you decide. The cover pic MUST not have any name, any voterid, any links, any phno, any law-draft heading or any law-draft description. And it MUST NOT have any name of political party, political group etc. It should simply say "please click this cover pic, read description, to see law-drafts I support". You may download my coverpic. (new term – coverpic)
.
(A.1.1) ONLY EXCEPTION is, when and if you are contesting election, then you may have an election related cover pic.
.
(A.2) Create a public FB group called as "My Files". Pls change settings so that no one except you can put posts. Dont add anyone in this group.
.
(A.3) Create TWO public FB album in your profile called as "(002) Law drafts I support (first fifty)" and “(003) Law drafts I support (other, after first 50)”. Later, you may need to create one or two more such albums. I will refer this as LawDraftAlbum (new term – LawDraftAlbum).
.
(A.3.1) Why give numbers (002) and not (001)? Because I have decided to keep album# 001 for “My core political beliefs”. I will describe that album and its posts in some other status.
.
(A.4) Create two FB statuses on your wall. Put first line in the status as "Top 50 Law drafts that I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print". And in second status, pls put "Law drafts (after top 50) that I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print". I will call both these statuses as IndexStatus. Every time you support a law-draft, you will need to edit one of this, and add some links/description etc of the law you support. (new term = IndexStatus)
.
(A.5) In the text description of cover picture, pls put following 6 lines, plus some other lines
(a) line1 = “Top 50 Law-drafts I tweeted @PmoIndia to print – “
(b) line2 = link to first IndexStatus
(c) line3 = link to the album “Law-drafts I support”
(d) line4 = “Law-drafts (after top 50) I tweeted @PmoIndia to print – “
(e) line5 = link to second IndexStatus
(f) line6 = link to the album “Law-drafts I support”
(g) in addition, pls request reader to copy download your coverpic, make it his cover picture, and also put links to FBCPBA.
.
(A.6) Pls put above lines in description of profile picture as well as description of both LawDraftAlbums i.e. “Law draft I support” albums.
.
-----
.
(B) Everytime steps --- i.e. steps you will need to take whenever you propose / support a law-draft
.
(B.1) create doc --- pls put law-draft you are proposing in a text file or ms-word doc file.
.
(B.2) make pdf and get sha1hash --- pls make PDF of law-draft. Pls google on "getting sha1 hash online". Pls obtain sha1 hash of PDF from two different websites. Pls make sure that sha1hash is from both website is same. If different, then make minor changes in doc file, and make PDF again. I will call this file as Law-Draft-Pdf or simply as pdf, and its hash as sha1hash. (new terms – pdf , sha1hash)
.
(B.2.1) what is sha1hash? - sha1hash is a 20 character long word obtained by applying some complex mathematical operations on bytes of the file. As of now, you need not get into what those mathematical operations are. Simply get two websites which enable you to upload a file and obtain sha1hash.
.
(B.3) make a hashtag --- for each proposed law-draft, the draft proposer MUST think of a hashtag. It should not be very long, less than 10 characters. Pls note – hashtag has NOTHING to with sha1hash. They only sound like, but are completely different.
.
(B.4) create cause on newindia --- create a cause on newindia dot com. Pls upload the PDF on that cause. I will call this as Law-Draft-Cause or newindia cause. And I will call link to cause as causelink. Pls use title of cause same as hashtag. pls vote for your cause on newindia dot com (new term = causelink)
.
(B.5) upload pdf on FB --- pls also upload the law-draft PDF FB group "My files". Pls get link to the status post which shows the pdf file. I will call this as PdfLink. (new term = pdflink)
.
(B.5.1) for simple proposed law-drafts --- if the proposed law-draft is simple, eg "Cancel Mauritius Treaty", then one may not create law-draft-doc or law-draft-pdf and may simply create a cause on newindia.
.
(B.6) TWEET @PmoIndia --- pls tweet @PmoIndia to print the proposed law. The tweet must have 3 things (a) hashtag (b) causelink (c) sha1hash. And in addition, you may put a small text msg. If proposed law-draft-text is simple, and there is no pdf, then sha1hash wont be there. I will call this tweet as TweetToPmo. I will call link to this tweet as tweetlink and text to this tweet as tweettext. (new terms - tweettopmo , tweettext, tweetlink).
.
(B.8) Draft in FB status - pls put entire law-draft text as FB status. I will call this as FullDraftStatus. Post this status in “Law drafts I support” album, as well as post this on wall. Posting on wall is backup incase while “Law draft I support” album gets accidentally deleted or deleted by a hacker. (newterm = fulldraftstatus)
.
(B.9.1) Law draft text longer than 8000 characters : If draft text length is below 8k, then ignore this clause, But if draft is longer, then you may need to divide the text into 2 more statuses by making it part-1 , part-2 etc. Pls keep length below 16k, even though FB allows 64k. Thats because most mobiles dont show status more than 16k. You can find out length of text by putting that text in notepad. Pls put links to all status in each status.
.
(B.9.2) Summary\Example statuses – pls create a status which give summary of the law-draft and few more status which give examples, if needed. Such examples will be needed for complex law-drafts like Wealth Tax etc. Simple law-drafts may not need status giving examples. The links to all can be put in the MainStatus described below. I will call these as SummaryStatus.
.
(B.10) Making 2 MainStatuses !!
.
(B.10.1) pls make a status post in the album "(002) Law drafts I support (top 50)" OR album “(003) Law drafts I support (other)” as you decide. The choice of album will depend on whether the law in top 50 laws you are proposing. The post should have (a)at top, hashtag and title of law-draft (b) request to voters to send tweet to @PmoIndia (c) tweettext (d) tweetlink (e) a 10-20 line description of the law-draft (f) link to law-draft-status (g) link to summarytatus. This way, all your friends on FB know that you have supported the law-draft along with its description. I will refer this as MainStatus1. Pls do NOT put any picture in this status. If you cant put pictureless status in album, and then pls put the status on wall. I will call this as MainStatus1. You may put a copy on wall outside album as backup, in case album is accidentally deleted.
.
(B.10.2) After putting status post in above album, pls post same post again in the album "Law Draft I support", with a picture. Pls use picture with plain text, and last line in pix must be “see description of this pic to get details”. I will call this as MainStatus2.
.
(B.10.3) Repeating above 2 clauses ---- same text of MainStatus1 is getting posted twice - in MainStatus1 and MainStatus2 !! MainStatus1 is post in the album "Law drafts I support" without pic. MainStatus2 is same text in album "Law drafts I support" with simple pic. This repetition is due to FB problems !!! If a person is using mbasic dot Facebook dot com, he doesn’t see pictureless status posts in album !! And so we need two statuses – one with picture and one pictureless.
.
(B.11) Make two empty "." comments in both MainStatuses
.
(B.12) In first comment, pls put all links - tweettext, tweetlink, links to cause on newindia, link to MainStatus1, link to MainStatus2, link to LawDraftStaus, PdfLink. This is ready reference for all links for you in future
.
(B.13) In second comment , you can later put FAQa and answers that can serve as ready reference for you.
.
(B.14) Editing IndexStatus - In IndexStatus you will need to add following lines for every law-draft you support (
(a) line1 = hashtag without # and title of law
(b) line2 = link to MainStatus1
(c) line3 = link to MainStatus3 (mention as backup status)
(d) line4 = tweetlink
(e) line5 = tweettext
(f) line6 = a small description of the law (for 5-20 lines for some 10-15 law-draft you like most, but not more for other law-drafts).
.
(B.15) If you edit this status again and again, then FB may delete it !! So after editing it say 10 times, pls create new status, and put copy of new status in old status top line. And copy contents of old status into new status , and then add the lines you wish to add.
.
(B.16) In case you lose the above status, because FB deletes it, then you can recreate the status using links in your album "(001) Law Drafts I support"
.
(B.17) Next, pls create album called as "(001) My Core Political Beliefs". This album will have tweettexts , tweetlinks, and full text of your core beliefs about judges, IAS, IPS, SoMoKe, congress / rss / aap leaders, congress/rss/aap workers. journalists, editors, columinists, political science professors, economics prof, sociology prof, RBI guv etc. The tweets must have specific hastags. I will explain that in a different status
