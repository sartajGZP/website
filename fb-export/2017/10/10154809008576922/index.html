We Recallists are commons of India, not members of civil society, who believe that we commons of India must force the PM to print Right to Recall procedures in Gazette next month. .
My goal is to raise mass-movement, where citizens are asking Mahatma Udham Singh to ask PM\CMs to print Right to Recall Drafts in the Gazette.
.
RTR is mentioned in Atharvaved and Satyarth Prakash as well. Swami Dayanand Saraswatiji in Satyarth Prakash chap-6, page-3 says that "Raajaa must be prajaa-aadheen, or else he will rob citizens". In modern India, we Recallists have been around since 1920s. eg Consider Shri Sachendra Nath Sanyal, Guru of Bhagat Singh and the founder of Hindustan (Socialist) Republican Association, the organization that Bhagat Singh joined in 1927. HSRA did the heroic task of giving phansi to Sanders, who had murdered Lala Lajpat Rai so that Mohanbhai get focus. Shri Sanyal wrote in manifesto of HSRA on 1-jan-1925 "In this Republic (that we want to create) the electors shall have the right to recall their representatives, if so desired, otherwise the democracy shall become a mockery." (see : shahidbhagatsingh.org/index.asp?link=revolutionary ).
.
So RTR demand in India is almost 90 years old. We recallists are not saying anything new, except that we are campaigning using The Draft as main tool. Today, we recallists are struggling against anti-recallists such as L K Advani. But more damage is coming from psuedo-recallists such as The Anna and The Team.

What is Right to Recall PM, RTR Lokpal etc
----------------------------------------------------
.
So what is this "Right to Recall"? Well, let me ask you one question first.
.
Say you own a factory with 100 employees such as managers, laborers etc. And suddenly Govt makes 2 new rules –
.
1. you can NOT expel managers till they are 65 years and cant expel any workers for next 5 years
.
2. every month, money from your account will be deducted and salary will go to your employees.
.
My question to you is : then after three months, will the level of indiscipline increase or decrease?
.
Please read further only after answering the above question. I will re-ask this question : three months after these new rules come, will level of indiscipline increase or decrease?
.
IOW, if we the commons of India, can not expel judges, MPs, Ministers, IAS etc they will all become indisciplined, corrupt, nepotic etc. This is what Shri Sanyal, Guru of Bhagat Singh said in 1925. And Satyarth Prakash also says that “Raajaa must be Prajaadheen or else he will rob citizens”. It was implemented in Greece in 600 BC and made Greece so powerful that Greece could win up to the river Yamuna. And has been implemented in US since 1800s and is main reason why US has been able to conquer Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Pakistan, Libya and India may be next. But one doesn’t need scriptures nor history nor examples of Greece and US to get the idea behind of Right to Recall --- it is plain vanilla common sense. Most problems of us commons in India are not different from the factory owner who can not fire workers for 5-35 years. And the solutions to our problems is also trivial --- procedures by which we may expel officers, Ministers, judges.
.
For that matter, why do you think corruption in US policemen and judges is less than corruption in India's policemen and judges? Are policemen and judges in US so dumb that they dont know how to squeeze money from citizens? Or are they all saints and dont want any extra money? Surely, not. And if we say education is the reason, then are policemen/judges in India illiterate? All this are non-reasons. The real reason why policemen/judges in US take much less bribes than policemen/judges in India is only because citizens in US have procedures by which they can expel/replace District Police Commissioner and District Judges. Why do judgments in US courts come in 6 months in over 99% cases? Because citizens have Right to Recall District Judges, High Court Chief Judges, District Public Prosecutors etc.
.
Yes. This may be surprise to many of you - that there are countries in the same planet where we live, where people can expel Governors, MLAs, District Police Chiefs, District Judges, even High Court Chief Judges, District Public Prosecutors, District Education Officers etc. And this is the reason why corruption in these offices is very low in US. (And in same US, citizens cant expel Senators, Federal Officials and Federal judges and so corruption is sky high in all these offices in US. )
.
This is RTR aka Right to Recall. RTR means procedures by which we citizens can expel/replace an officer, Minister, judges by proving majority in citizens and without approaching any higher authority. RTR and its variant Jury System, are the only known way mankind has found in past 5000 years to reduce corruption in officers, Ministers, judges.
.
My goal is to get RTR-PM, RTR-judges, RTR-Lokpal, Jury System and many such drafts printed in the Gazette of India and its States. I have proposed Right to Recall over PM, CM, Supreme Court judges, High Court judges, District Police Chief, Reserve Bank Governor, MP, MLA, Mayor and 250 such positions.
.
In India, almost all intellectuals (ku-buddheejeevies) have opposed RTR. The intellectuals claim that we citizens of India are communal, casteist, stupid, gullible etc and so we citizens must not get RTR over judges, CM, PM etc. The ku-buddheejeevies have lied that and claimed that the RTR drafts I have proposed are unconstitutional and these ku-buddheejeevies have also stooped low and claimed the the drafts, if printed in Gazette, will create instability. IMO, the ku-buddheejeevies are opposing RTR because they are all on payroll of elitemen and elitemen hate RTR as after RTR comes, bribing away judges, Ministers and officers will become difficult. So ku-buddheejeevies oppose RTR to keep their elitemen masters happy. RTR is opposed by ALL parties except Bharat Swabhiman Trust. BST's leader, Late Shri Rajiv Dixit had openly supported RTR openly and all its volunteers are openly campaigning for RTR.
