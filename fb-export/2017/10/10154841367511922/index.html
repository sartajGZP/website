steps non-activist voters may take to publicize the law-drafts that THEY support
.
ALL REASONS and LOGIC BEHIND MAKING THESE STEPS ARE EXPLAINED IN SEPARATE STATUSES. Pls see link in first comment to see logic and reasons behind the proposed steps )
.
Background
----------
.
I define “non-activist voter” as someone who can spend no more than 4 hrs a week on all activities -- FB / twitter, pamphlet distribution, giving newspaper advt, election campaigning etc -- to publicize the law-drafts they support.
.
Now if a voter -- (a) knows how to use internet, and (b)that voter refuses to publicize law-drafts (those law-drafts that that voter supports) on FB / twitter (c) using his FB cover picture as start point -- then I take him as person who is NOT interested in improving law-drafts of India.
.
I will repeat : if a voter -- (a) knows how to use internet, and (b)that voter refuses to publicize law-drafts (those law-drafts that that voter supports) on FB / twitter (c) using his FB cover picture as start point -- then I take him as person who is NOT interested in improving law-drafts of India.
.
And I request recallists to spend time ONLY with those voters who are interested in working to improve laws of India.
.
To a voter who doesn’t know how to use internet, but does want to improve laws of India, I request him to send postcard to PMO. The postcard should have two requests to PM (a) request to print law-draft title as given on newindia dot com, (b) request to put the scan of postcard on PM’s website. I will give details in some other status.
.
And pls note – there is NO precondition that voter must support RTR and law-drafts I have proposed. The voter may be anti-RTR, which is fine with me. I only request a voter to publicize law-drafts, that THAT voter supports, on FB / twitter and using his cover picture as entry point.
.
Now say a net-using voter believes that certain law-drafts needs to be printed. And the voter also agrees to publicize law-drafts on FB / twitter. Then I request him to consider the steps I suggest him to take. My method uses FB cover picture as starting point. And that’s why I call it as FBCPBA = “Facebook cover picture based activism”.
.
Or, I request voter to give link to complete list of steps that he thinks lakhs and crores of voters in India should take on FB / twitter to publicize the law-drafts they are supporting. The voter may ask any congress / rss / aap leader or worker to provide list of steps voters should take on FB / twitter and outside internet.
.
IMO, every voter MUST ask congress / rss / aap leaders and workers to give list of steps that lakhs and crores of voters should take on FB / twitter and outside internet, to bring law-drafts that voters support. And ask them to put the list of steps they propose on FB and put link in their cover picture description. And the voter should also keep links of law-drafts he supports.
.
So this chapter describes - steps that I request ALL lakhs/crores of net using voters of India to take to publicize the law-drafts that THEY support, not necessarily law-drafts we propose. Pls note again – there is NO precondition that you must support law-drafts we have proposed. You can use this method to publicize the law-drafts that congress / rss / aap or any political party has proposed.
.
-----
.
Steps I request net-suing citizens to take
------------------------------------------
.
There are two types of steps ---
.
(A) one time steps i.e. steps you need to take only once and
(B) everytime steps i.e. steps you need to repeat everytime you decide to support one new law-draft.
.
(A) One time steps : steps you need to take only once and
.
(A.1) Pls keep profile cover picture which says --- "please click this cover pic, read description, to see law-drafts I support" in any 2-3 languages you decide. eg pls see – fb.com/photo.php?fbid=10153491466351922 . you may download this , upload this and make it your coverpic.
.
(A.1.1) The coverpic MUST not have any photo, any name, any voterid, any links, any phno, any law-draft heading or any law-draft description. And it MUST NOT have name of any political party, any political group etc. It should simply say "pls click this cover pic, read description, to see law-drafts I support". Because if coverpic has name etc, then newcomer may not like to download, upload and make it his coverpic and so spread reduces
.
(A.1.2) The coverpic MUST be this and nothing else ONLY EXCEPTION is -- if you are contesting election, then you may have an election related cover pic.
.
(A.2) Create TWO public FB album in your profile called as "(002) Law drafts I support (first fifty)" and “(003) Law drafts I support (other, after first 50)”. I will refer this as LawDraftAlbums (note the new term – lawdraftalbum).
.
(A.2.1) I have decided to keep album# (001) for FB album “(001) My core political beliefs”. And so I have decided to use album# (002) and (003) for “Law drafts I support”. I will explain nature of posts in album “(001) My core political beliefs” in some other status.
.
(A.3) Create two FB statuses on your wall.
.
(A.3.1) Put first line in the first status as "Top 50 Law drafts that I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print". And in second status, pls put first line as "Law drafts (after top 50) that I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print". I will call both these statuses as IndexStatus. (note the new term = IndexStatus)
.
(A.3.2) Every time you support a law-draft, you will need to edit one of these two IndexStatuses, and add some links/description etc of the law you support.
.
(A.4) In text description of your FB profile cover picture, pls put following 7 lines and a small description
(a) line1 = your full name , and your voterid (optional)
(b) line2 = “Top 50 Law-drafts I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print – “
(b) line3 = link to first IndexStatus
(c) line4 = link to the album “(002) Law-drafts I support (top 50)”
(d) line5 = “Law-drafts (after top 50) I have tweeted @PmoIndia to print – “
(e) line6 = link to second IndexStatus
(f) line7 = link to the album “(003) Law-drafts I support (after top 50)”
(g) and you may add some description, and request to reader to download the coverpic and make it his coverpic.
.
(A.5) Pls put above lines in description of profile picture as well as description of both LawDraftAlbums i.e. “Law draft I support” albums.
.
(A.6) Next, pls create album called as "(001) My Core Political Beliefs". This album will have tweettexts , tweetlinks, and full text of your core beliefs about judges, IAS, journalists, editors, columnists, political science professors, economics profs, sociology profs, RBI guv etc. The tweets must have specific hashtags. I will explain "(001) My Core Political Beliefs" in a different status
.
(A.7) Pls add some congress / rss / aap / rrp activists in your “follow” list. You can do so by visiting their profile, and clicking follow. If you know them personally, you may add them in the friend list.
.
-----
.
(B) Everytime steps -- i.e. steps you will need to take whenever you decide to support and publicize a law draft.
.
(B.1) Pls see the congress / rss / aap / rrp activists that you “follow” on FB. Pls click their coverpage. Do you see any link which says “list of law-drafts I support”. If not, pls move to next activist. If yes, then pls see the list of law-drafts he supports. You may ask him question etc on these law-drafts. If you decide not to know about these law-drafts, then ignore following steps. But if you decide to know about these law-drafts, then pls take following steps
.
(B.2) For the law-draft you wish to know, pls see the newindia dot com link in the activists’ status post. You may download pdf from newindia dot com, and may read the pdf which has the law-draft. The status in activist’s post may also have links to summary.
.
(B.3) After discussing the law with congress / rss / aap / rrp / other activists you know, or after reading the draft, pls decide whether you want to support this DRAFT or not. If you decide not support the law, then you may ignore the following steps. If you decide to support the law-draft, then pls take following steps.
.
(B.4) Tweet to PmoIndia --- Pls send the tweet to @PmoIndia. THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP. Pls note that tweet MUST have (a) a hashtag (b)link to for that law on newindia dot com (c) sha1hash of the pdf. And may be a short text. Sometimes, sha1hash may be absent. But hashtag is must. And link MUST be link to newindia dot com.
.
(B.5) Making a MainStatus for each law-draft you support --– for every law-draft you support, you need to make one FB status, which I call as MainStatus.
.
(B.5.1) Pls go to your profile, and select Photo and after that select albums. And then select album "(002) Law drafts I support (top 50)" OR album “(003) Law drafts I support (other)” as you decide. The choice of album will depend on whether the law in top 50 laws you are supporting or not.
.
(B.5.2) Then click FeedView. You will be able to see a textbox below where you can post new status.
.
(B.5.3) In the MainStatus, i.e. the one status per law-draft you support, pls write following lines
(a) at top, hashtag and title of law-draft
(b) a request to voters to send tweet to @PmoIndia
(c) the full text of the tweet you sent to @PmoIndia
(d) link to the tweet you sent
(e) a 10-20 line description of the law-draft – should be what YOU think
(f) link to a status which has summary
(g) link to a status which has law-draft in text form (optional)
.
(B.5.4) When you post this status, it may appear in the newsfeed of your friends. Or your friend or anyone can see if and when anyone visits your profile and clicks your coverpic and follows links.
.
(B.5.5) This way, many voters in India will know that you have supported the law-draft. And some may read about this law-draft and may decide to support. So chances that more people will come to know increases.
.
(B.5.6)If you cant put pictureless status in album, then pls put the status on wall. You may put a copy on wall outside album as backup, which will be useful in case album is accidentally deleted.
.
(B.6) Adding a few lines per law-draft in IndexStatus - In the IndexStatus (see 3.A.3) you will need to add following lines for every law-draft you support
(a) line1 = hashtag without # and title of law
(b) line2 = link to MainStatus1
(c) line4 = tweetlink
(d) line5 = tweettext
(e) few lines for very imp law-drafts , not all law-drafts
.
OPTIONAL STEPS
--------------
.
(B.7) Draft in FB status - pls put entire law-draft you support as FB status in the album. You will need to obtain draft from an activist.
.
(B.8) Making second MainStatuses with image - After putting MainStatus, pls post same line again in the album "(002) Law Draft I support" or (003) Law draft I support”, with a picture. Pls use picture with plain text, and last line in pix must be “see description of this pic to get details”. You can obtain pic from wall of an activist.
.
=====
.
If you want to do more, then you can see chap-2, meant for people who wish to spend more than 4 hrs a week in publicizing law-drafts using FB/twitter, pamphlet distribution, newspaper advt, election campaigning, election contesting etc.
