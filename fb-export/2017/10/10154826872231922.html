Proof that FB activism does work
.
We at Right to Recall Party request all to tweet link to law-drafts that they support to @PmoIndia. And then we request ALL to share the tweetlink in FB one album, and write a few sentences about the law-draft.

Rank and file of congress / rss / aap workers tell all that activism on FB is useless and such actions will have NO effect.
.
Here is one REAL proof that FB-activism work.
.
Consider GST. When Modiji printed GST law-draft in jul-2017, it had clauses that
.
(1) unregistered service provider cannot physically go outside state to provide service, though he may ship his services out of state. eg say a CA or software developer is unregistered and say located in State-A. Then as per law-draft printd in july-2017, he can indeed stay in state-A and services to state-B..But he cannot physically travel to state-B and give his services.
.
(2) unregistered trader or manufacturer cannot go out of state with his goods and sell goods nor can he ship goods out of state
.
On oct-15, NaMo changed law as follows - unregistered service provider CAN now physically go outside state to provide services. But law was NOT changed for traders and manufacturers !!!
/.
So why did Modiji do favor to service provider and NOT to traders and manufacturers? as such, manufacturers provide MORE employment than services people.
.
Reason was ---- a large number of service providers like software people, CA, CS, lawyers etc are active on FB. Whereas very few traders and manufacturers are active on FB !!
.
So if service providers were hurt, then they would have started writing anti-NaMo and anti-RSS posts on FB, and that would have done a huge damage to NaMo / RSS (RSS = BJP, one and same just as Shaktimaan = Gangadhar).
.
And so service providers were given relaxation.
.
So this is a proof positive that FB does impact politics.
.
