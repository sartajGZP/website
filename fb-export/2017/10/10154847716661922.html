Unitists (ektaavaadi) activist --- an activist whose main goal is to FIRST "unite" all activists ; he often insist that ALL activists should forget their goals and first thing all activists should do is to unite !!! Why dont all unitists (ektawaadi) first unite?
.
Many activists are unitists i.e. their primary goal is to unite all activists !! And they often say that --- all activists must forget their goals first and first thing they should do is to unite !!!
.
To any activist with a cause, this may be a bouncer.
.
The issue is --- which cause should that union of activists take and in which cause should it drop and what should be the order in which causes should be taken?
.
eg I met an activist who said that he supports RTR, Jury. But his next statements were --- "RTR gives power to people, but today people are not intelligent and mature enough to use this power. And so BEFORE we give RTR like powers to people, we should educate them !! And existing education system is made by Macaulay is wrong. And so we should education people using the ancient Gurukul method. And so we should FIRST establish several Gurukuls, educate people, and THEN AFTER we should bring laws like RTR, Jury !!" And he claimed that he supports RTR , Jury etc.
.
So in case I unite with him, then my first goal will have to help him in getting grants to establish 1000s of Gurukuls across India. And number of Gurukuls will have to reach million before 500 million kids can be given "good" education !! And then RTR, Jury etc laws will be brought !!
.
My take is to bring RTR District Education Officer and RTR State / Central Education Minister laws TODAY. And then using these laws, those voters who want Gurukul System can force these officers to let Gurukul Trustees form Gurukuls OR voters can force them to improve existing public / private schools. So if parents see merits in Gurukuls over existing private / public schools, then his cause will be served. But his "cause" says that RTR DEO etc should come some 10 years AFTER millions of Gurkuls are formed. Obviously, I wont unite with him.
.
And neither would/should he unite me.. Because if he unites with me, then he does NOT need to do any fund gathering, but he will have to support RTR , Jury etc DRAFTS on twitter / FB etc, and that too - TODAY !! But he believes that such power should be given ONLY after people are "educated" as per Gurukul system !! So he cant support RTR etc today..
.
My point is --- unity between us too is NOT possible,
.
Same way, a gentleman I know says that (a) royalties from minerals (b)rent from govt land and (c) rent from private land should be distributed amongst citizens !! I agree with (a) and (b) but I oppose (c). i.e. he says that PRIVATE land too should be taxed at rent rate, and money be divided amongst citizens. Whereas in my proposals, the private land is taxes ONLY to run govt expenses such as court, police, Military etc and the money is NOT distributed amongst people !! And he cant agree with my proposal and I wont agree with his.
.
But all this is NOISE to unitists. The unitists doesnt want to get into details of proposals of activists.A true unitists wont waste even 1 minute in understanding details and points of conflicts. His one-liner --- we ALL activists must forget our causes first and first we must "unite" !!! And after "uniting" we should decide the proposals !! But after uniting, the proposal will either say "cause of A first or cause of B first" And in any case, A or B will depart.
.
