Details about proposals
------------------------------
.
So there will be a govt website, where you can type name of a person and his voterid etc and the website will show
.
(1) How much land he owns
.
(2) How many flats he owns and how much land he owns via flats he owns
.
(3) How much land / flats he owns via HUFs
.
(4) How many companies and trusts he is partner / director / trustee etc of, and how much land / flats these companies / trusts etc own
.
And all data of all 130 crore citizens and all million companies / trusts of India will be also available on DVDs for a charge, and will be in downloadable form on torrents. So all land ownership data can come on your PC if you want.
.
