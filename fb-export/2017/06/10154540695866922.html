Normally, large unit is intergral multiple of smaller unit. eg 1 km = 1000 meters etc and multiple is factor of 10.
.
But when it comes to time, we have 1 year = 12 months, 1 month = 30 or 31 days, and 1 year = 365.24 days, and we have 1 day = 24 hours, and 24 hours = 60 minutes and 1 minute = 60 seconds.
.
Why are ratios in timeunits not multiples of ten?
...

(Ans added after 2 days --- thats because day and year are two natural units and these to units are NOT nice multiples of each other. Day is unit defined by sunrise and next sunrise. And year is time unit defined by one summer to next summer (or one winter to next winter). And these two units are NOT good multiples of each other. Now day was divided into 12 parts and not 10 parts, because dividing circle into 12 parts is easy, while dividing circle into 10 parts is NOT easy !! And dividing circle into 16 or 32 parts is also easy. So hour could have been defined as 1/24th of a day or 1/32th of a day. i dont know why 1/24 was chosen over 1/32).
.
Next to divide hour into minute, again dividing circle into 60 parts is easy while dividing circle into 100 parts is NOT easy. So so 1/60th of hour was natural choice for next small unit and so on).
