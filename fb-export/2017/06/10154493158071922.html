We at Right to recall Party has proposed this law that will put all details of wealth, salary, perks, allowances (except allowances consumed by work duties) and other incomes including personal incomes of all Govt employees except Military) and all PSU employees on web. This will include all Policemen as well. And this will also include pension income of those who have retired. The website will also show salaries / perks they have recieved since they started the job (as far as the data is available).
.
And the data with comes with their names, employeeid and photo. So if you walk into any Govt office, their name tags will have their employee ids. And if you enter the employee id in your mobile app, then APP will show his current salary and wealth etc , and also show past salaries as far as data is available and how much salary he got till date.
.
Govt employees have complained that they are being underpaid and exploited. So it is necessary that ALL activists and ALL citizens get this information so that constructive suggestion to reduce their "exploitation" can come from citizens !!
.
Congress / RSS / AAP have opposed this proposal. SoGa / NaMo / Arke have also opposed this proposal..
