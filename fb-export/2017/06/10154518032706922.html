#STAS - "separate tax account system" to solve a serious problem in GST
.
GST has a SERIOUS problem. GST law says that "buyer must pay GST to seller. and if seller doesnt deposit that GST amount collected to Govt, then Govt as per CGST act clause-16.2.c, will defacto collect that GST amount from buyer once again !! It applies when buyer is regular i.e. non-composite dealer. It doenst apply when buyer is composite or unregistered. Its called Missing Trader Problem or MTP, or "buyer shall bear the burden of the Missing Trader ".
.
STAS i.e. Separate Tax Account System is a proposed aw that can solve the problem.
.
For more information, pls see STAS details at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154517884606922
.
