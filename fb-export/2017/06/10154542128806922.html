Electricity generation data says electricity growth rate between apr-2016 and apr-2017 was 5.5%. And economic growth rate was stated as 5.1% . .
.
electricity per capita consumption of India is very low.and electricity is a basic necessity right after food / cloth / medicine etc, .
.
So growth rate in electricity consumption should be at least 1% more than total growth rate. if growth rate in over economy was 5.1%, then electricity consumption should have grown by some 6.1%
.
