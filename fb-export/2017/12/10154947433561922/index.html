Law draft I propose as replacement of FRDI - Financial Resolution and Depositor Insurance - law
.
(the full details of law-draft I had proposed to fix NPA problem in year 2008 is in chap-9 and chap-23 of https://fb.com/groups/ProposedLawDrafts/1881857995364862 and hindi version is https://fb.com/groups/ProposedLawDrafts/1881857995364862 . Pls see link in comments for FB status version)
.
Kalkipurush Sri Modiji wants to print a law named as "Financial Resolution and Depositor Insurance " to deal with problem of NPA. Essentially, following is how NPA is created
.
(1) Depositors deposit money in bank owners and bank managers B
.
(2) Bank owners and bank managers give loans to L
.
(3) Later, L refuses to pay. And in many cases, bank owners and bank apex managers know beforehand that L is NOT going to pay back. But still bank owners, apex bank managers, apex bank directors, Finance Ministry IAS, Finance Ministers, PM, RBI Chairman, RBI directors etc give loan to L because L has given bribes to bank owners , apex bank directors etc
.
(4) So how can B give money back to depositors?
.
One solution that Govts till date followed was --- Govt would print money and give to B, and B will pay to depositors. And money printing causes inflation. And apex bank directors keep taking bribes and giving more loans and creating more NPAs. IOW, take bribes - give loan - create NPA - print money to pay for NPA - again tae bribe - again give loans - again create NPA - again print money - .... goes on and on and on
.
This method isnt sustainable.
.
SoGa / ArKe and Congress / AAP workers have proposed no solution. And neither did NaMo and RSS-workers till date. But now that NaMo is on chair, he cant ignore the problem. So the "solution" Modiji and RSS-workers have proposed FRDI bill which says --- bank owners will be empowered to tell depositors that --- we made losses in lending, and so we are trimming away say 50% of your interest earned and trimming away say 20% of your deposits !! SoGa / ArKe have started hoarse cries, but have proposed no solution.
.
----
.
The solution I propose for existing NPA - print money and pay depositors or PSU bank, and let depositors in private banks and co-op loose their deposits.
,.
The solution I propose for now on is
.
(1) Depositors will be given two choice - deposit in PSU bank or private financial company
.
(2) In PSU bank, the interest will low and PSU bank will NOT give any money as loans except (a) student loans (b) home loans below Rs 10 lakh per person (c) medical loans below Rs 5 lakhs per persons (d) vehicle loans below Rs 5 lakh per person (e) loans to govts etc. These loans have low risk. And interest need not be high. And so depositors will get very low interest as well. And customer will have to pay all banking charges. PSU banks will completely exit high risk lending businesses such as giving loans to private industrial projects.
.
(3) In private financial institutions, deposit will be completely uninsured and returns may be high - but all money may evaporate if the company looses. And Govt will be prohinited from insuring or bailing out private companies.
.
-----
.
The key difference is --- depositor will have an EXPLICIT zero risk low return option, which can be used mainly for fund storage and fund transfer.
.
Now pls note that SoGa / ArKe and congress / aap workers have NOT proposed any answer to the question of how NPA should be handled. All they shout is "adani, ambani " etc etc. And Modiji and RSS-workers are now shameless suggesting that depositors MUST NOT be given any "zero risk, low return" option and banks must be allowed to wipe out the deposits in case of losses !!
.
Only we at Right to Recall Party are proposing sensible way out in which depositors can be protected..
.
I will soon publish full draft. After that, we will start twitter / fb and pamphlet campaign....
