Guidelines to newcomers about where can they get answers on RTR , JurySys etc
.
Since I dont have time to answer questions to newcomers and so I request newcomers ask questions to some other recallists.
.
Which recallists?
.
I wont give any names - you find out !!
.

.
(2) Of this, those whose who have contested elections are worth asking questions to. But so far, there are only 5 who have contested elections - myself, Rakesh Suri, Suhel Pathak, Kamal Monga and Neha Monga. And I have strict policy --- female should ask questions to females only and males should ask questions to males only. So you may need to ask questions to non-election contestants
.
(3) Amongst non-election contestant, look for following -
.
(3a) if nickname says "pls click cover picture". then he may be worth asking questions to. Otherwise, imo, he is not worth asking questions to

(3b) if cover picture image says "see this coverpic to see the law-drafts I support", then he may be worth asking question to
.
(3c) If cover picture has paragraphs and links to law-drafts he supports, then he may be worth questions to
.
If neither (3a) to (3c) are fulfilled, then pls move to next recallist
.
(3d) Look at the posts --- do posts talk about solution law-drafts? do posts give imp news that paidmedia doesnt report? YES? Then pls ask him questions. NO? Are the posts just about contradictions in leaders, common news, mocking without giving any solution etc? Thenignore him and more to next person
.
And I request all to ask as many questions as possible by FB posts only and not spend more than 1-2 hrs in phone in first week and then not more than 1-2 hrs a month in first 2-3 months. And after that, QA should be done via FB only
.
These are the guidelines I give to newcomers.
