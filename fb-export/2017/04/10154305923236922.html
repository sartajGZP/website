.
But in 1950, all leaders of Congress, RSS (*) , CPI etc unanimously agreed on one thing --- thee should be NO referendum law in India !!!
.
(* RSS = RSS + BJP, just as Gangadhar = Gangadhar + Shaktimaan. And back in 1950s, BJP was known as Jansangh).

Apex leaders like Congress, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, Duratma Gandhi, Shyamaprasad Mukherjee, Namboodripadd etc all opposed proposal to print referendum procedures in Indian Gazette.
.
And today, we have SoGa / NaMo / ArKe opposing the proposal to print referendum laws in Indian Gazette.
,
Even in late 1980s, when demand to make TriDevalaya grew, Congress / RSS / CPI leaders opposed proposal to print referendum law.
