say you are kaarigar (craftsman) such as locksmith or cannon maker or gunmaker etc
.
say you have skills but you are poor. And say you need money to start a unit of your own
.
Say A and B are two rich people,
.
A says " I will give you X gold coins, And you gave to pay me y% interest a month , and y can be large. And you have to pay interest till whole X and interest is paid. And if you cant pay monly interest , then compoud interest will apply on unpaid interest. And if you cant pay, then along with your shop, I will take over your house. And you son will have to pay debt and interest, if you fail to pay back before you die"
.
B says "I will give you X gold coins, and ALL money must be put ONLY on shop. From gross profits, you can only take small amount for yourself. And you pay me y% (as high as 67%) of your PROFITS every month. And one you pay back 2X or 3X, the parnership will end and you will keep ALL the profits. And if whole business fails, then I will take over ONLY shop (but not your home), and auction shop and its goods and recover as much as I can. And in can you die, then your son will need to pay balance amount ONLY if he wants the shop, and not otherwise.".
.
So which deal would YOU take?
