RtrCM – Right to Recall CM
------------------------------------------
.
[ comment : This law-draft can be printed by CM as Gazette Notification in State Govt Gazette. No permission of Assembly or Parliament is needed. And this draft can be also printed by PM in Central Govt Gazette. No permission of Loksabha or Rajyasabha is needed,
.
This draft has 2 parts -
.
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
.
Part - II : Instructions for citizens and officers
.
Important clauses are – (6) , (7) , (13) , (14) , (15) ]
.
----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : this law will enable you (you means any voter of India) to specify at most five persons whom you consider appropriate for the position of CM of India. You can file approvals over SMS or APP or ATM by personally visiting Patwari (Talati) office.

(2) comment : you can cancel the approvals anyday and you can approve anyone else anyday.
.
(3) comment : If any candidate gets 10% of higher approvals (10% of all registered voters in State) than votes CM’s supporter MLAs had got in the election, then that new person may become CM. And later, if his approval count decreases and some other candidate’s approval count is 1 crore higher than him, and then other candidate shall become CM
.
-----------------------------------
Part - II : Instructions for officers and citizens
------------------------------------
.
(4) comment : Comments are not binding on any officer etc. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(4a) comment : Understanding RtrPm draft given at newindia.in/RtrPm will be useful in understanding this draft.
.
(5) (5.1) The word citizen here means a registered voter in the State.
(5.2) The word may means may or need not, and clearly means 'no binding'.
.
(6) If a citizen of India above 30 years wishes to be CM, he can appear before DC ( = District Collector). DC would issue a serial number for a filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election and put his name on the CM's website as CM candidate.
.
(6a) comment : in case this law-draft is printed by PM, then instead of CM’s website mentioned above clause and later clauses, DC and officers may have to put the information on PM’s website
.
(7) If a citizen comes in person to office of Talati (or Patwari or Village Officer), pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five candidates for the CM position, then the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card.
.
(8) If a citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, then the Talati will cancel one or more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(9) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter. DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(10) CM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. CM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM or internet or mobile app.
.
(11) The Talati will put the approvals of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his approvals.
.
(12) DCs across India will publish Approval counts for each candidate.
.
(13) The existing CM may count his approval count as higher of the following two
.
(13.1) number of citizens who have approved him
.
(13.2) sum of votes obtained by Assembly MLAs who have supported him
.
(14) If a candidate gets approvals 10% (of all registered voters in state) more than approvals that the existing CM has, then the existing CM may or need not resign and may or need not ask MLAs to appoint the highest approved person as new CM. Or, CM need not take this action, CM's discretion will be final.
.
(14.1) comment : say a state has 4 crore registered voters. Then 10% in above clause will mean 40 lakh, irrespective of number of voters who have filed for approval.
.
(15) MLAs may or need not elect the person stated above as new CM.
.
(CV.1) If any citizen wants a change in this law-draft, he may submit an affidavit at DC's office and DC or his clerk will post affidavit on CM's website for Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen want to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to affidavit submitted in above clause, Talati will enter YES/NO and give him a receipt for Rs 3 fee. The YES-NO will be posted on CM's website.
.
------ end of draft RtrCM law-draft -------
