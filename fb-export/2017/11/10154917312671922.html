Increase election deposits for wealthy candidates - #ElectionDepositHigherForWealthyCandidates
.
If candidates gets 15% votes, then all deposit is refunded. And this threshold should be reduced to 5% and partial refund should be made possible,. (eg if a person gets 1% votes, then he gets 20% of deposit back)
.
But one and only way to reduce spurious election candidates is to increase election deposit.
.
So deposit structure I propose for MLA election is
.
For candidate wealth (liquid plus market value minus one home) above Rs 2 crore , deposit = Rs 200,000 .
.
For candidate wealth (liquid plus market value minus one home) below Rs 25 lakhs , deposit = Rs 25,000 .
.
For rest, deposit = 1% of wealth
.
So if a candidate has say wealth of Rs 1 crore, then deposit he will need to pay will be Rs 1 lakhs.
.
And fine for underpaying deposit will be 5 times the amount evaded.
.
And while calculating wealth, he can remove one home, and need to take market value of all his land / home etc
.
The deposit I propose for Loksabha candidate = Twice the MLA candidates

If you support this, then pls vote at .newindia.in/causes/ElectionDepositHigherForWealthyCandidates
.
And pls tweet " Respected PM, . – – – . pls make MLA election deposits as follows . – – – . For candidate wealth (liquid plus market value minus one home) above Rs 2 crore , deposit = Rs 200,000 . – – – . For candidate wealth (liquid plus market value minus one home) below Rs 25 lakhs , deposit = Rs 25,000 . – – – . For rest, deposit = 1% of wealth . – – – . And fine for under-reporting wealth = 5 times the deposit evaded. And no fine if deposit paid is Rs 200,000 . – – – Deposit for Loksabha = Twice the MLA deposit amount . – – – . This will reduce number of spurious candidates. (no pdf) "
.
