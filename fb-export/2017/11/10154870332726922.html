Digital payment frauds are because banks are giving ATM card which come with (a) physical retail payment option and
(b) net payment option with OTP, and also
(c) come with no OTP international net payment option
.
The customer has no way to specify that I want ONLY ATM or I ONLY want ATM plus net physical retail (i.e.goods can be sold ONLY to card owner in person and not on net). And he cant even disable international non OTP payments !!
.
And worse, benks start net baking without asking !!
.
I got letter from bank that net banking has been enabled on my account and I had to personally go and give letter that I dont want it !!
.
So problem is with debit / credit card system. Not with Aadhar,. Now of course, aadhar linking is making digital paynents frauds worse,. But solution is to enable customer to have
.
(a) ATM only card
(b) ATM + physical retail ONLY card
(c) ATM + physical retail + OTP based india only net banking card
(d) international transaction credit card (NO DEBITcard option)
(e) credit card must have 2% charge to cover frauds only
(f) credit card company and NOT card owner should be asked to file FIR
.
Above will solve the digital payment fraud mess. I see no need to remove aadhar system
