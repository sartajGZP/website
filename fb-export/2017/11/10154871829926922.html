Voting for a proposed law-draft on PM's website newindia.in and sharing your support on FB increases the POSSIBILITY that the law-draft will get passed. There is no guarantee
.
And for that matter, NO amount of campaigning gurantees that a law will get passed with 100% guarantee
.
Each step increases probability
.
eg say
.
(1) you vote on newindia.in/causes/DdMrcam - possibility that DdMrcam will pass increases
.
(2) you share your vote casting on FB. The possibility that DdMrtcam will pass will further increase
.
(3) you share your vote casting on twitter. Then possibility that DdMrtcam will pass will further increase
.
(4) You add more description on FB when you share vote casting on FB. Possibility increases
.
(5) You make ONE FB album which has link to ALL vote casting FB posts and tweets --- so any user who comes to that album will NOT see all posts but see ONLY posts related to votes !! Then possibility that ;laws will pass further inccreases !!
.
(6) You unfollow congress / rss / aap etc activists who refuse to support these laws on newindia.in, FB and tweeter --- then possibility that these laws will further increase !!
.
(7) You work for election campaign for those who are publicizing DdMrcam and other such laws you support,. Then possibility that these laws will pass further increases
.
So each step increases possibility. And as more people take these steps, possibility further increases
.
And NOTHING takes possibility to 100% !! Nothing gives 100% guarantee. If one says that --- take these and these and these campaign steps, then possibility that a law will get passed becomes 100%, then I dismiss that statement as errorneous
.
Finally, newindia.in gives ONLY voter count. It does NOT give names, ph# of addresses. It allows login using email and so it allows fake voting. We should ask PM to make sure that voting can be done ONLY via accounts which have mobile which are linked with aadhar. That will reduce spurious voting.
.
Bit even with fake voting, newindia helps in publicizing the law-drafts amongst friends YOU personally know. And while votecount can be useless, by knowing that a person DID NOT vote for this cause, you know that that person is NOT committed to the cause.
.
Now votecount can be fake, but "NOT VOTING" also provides useful information about lack of commitment in a given activist.
.
So, pls do vote for causes you support on newindia dot in. And pls share that on FB / twitter
