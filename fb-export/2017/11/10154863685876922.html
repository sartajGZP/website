Activists who need mega-funds vs activists who need far LESS funds
.
All activist methods will need some funds -- and some can be self funded i.e. activists are using their own incomes only or depend on contributions of open supporters. I would compare how much funds are needed in different methods for same cause.
.
Activism is generally about improving one or more or all sectors. eg some activists may wish to improve education, some wish to improve health, some would wish to reduce corruption in one or some or all depts, and so on.
.
Now some activist want to improve a sector WITHOUT any law-change as first step while some activists such as recallists FIRST want to bring law-change , and then improve the sector using govt employees, govt contractors and tax money only. IOW, activism of us recallists and many such LEGISLATIVE activist is confined to bringing law-change and then law and govt do rest of the work.
.
I will take "education activists" as example i.e. activists who wish to improve education in India's students.
.
There are several methods proposed by various education activists
.
(a) DIRECT ACTIVISM - Many activists propose methods in which 100s of activists will DIRECTLY teach thousands of kids and improve their education. Some activists take one more step, and are working to setup several Gurukuls or private schools or granted schools to give education to kids. So to teach crores of kids, one would need lakhs of activists working for free or need tens of crores of rupees of donations or govt grants to pay the teachers !!
.
(b) PETITION ACTIVISM - Another set of activists make complaints etc in govt dept, or file petitions / PILs in courts and try to fix the problems.
.
(c) LEGISLATIVE ACTIVISM : some activists such as us recallists confine to raising political campaign to get some laws printed that will improve education. eg Right to Recall District Education Office, RTR State Education Minister, RTR National Education Minister, Saatya System (* not draft, see later) and so on. But we DO NOT believe in doing activist in fixing problems within EXISTING laws, and nor do we suggest that activist should directly teach kids
.
(d) ELECTORAL ACTIVISM -- some activists insist that problem can be reduced merely by replacing CM or PM. So their confine to election campaigning and oppose wasting time in publicizing law-drafts etc. And they do some social work etc, but goal is to collect votes, eg congress / rss / aap workers mainly believe in ELECTORAL ACTIVISM. Their goal is to ensure that their leader becomes PM / CM and nothing else. They do some social work etc. But goal is ONLY to collect votes for their leaders,
.
In (c) i.e. LEGISLATIVE ACTIVISM, we propose to use tax money to pay salary to teachers and there is no donation to pay teachers. The activists' money and cause supporters' money is NOT used to pay teachers,. The money from activists and supporters us used ONLY to publicize ppeffort is ONLY in law-drafts such as RTR-DEO-law-draft , RTR-State-EduMin-law-draft, RTR-National-EduMin-law-draft and so on. And to request activists and voters to send necessary tweets and put necessary posts on FB to publicize the law-drafts using their FB profile and so on
.
In (b) i.e. PETITION ACTIVISM, the activists dont directly teach kids, and they work to improve teaching by filing complains etc in respective dept. So expense is less than direct activism. But finally, taking action on complaints is at discretion of apex GoI officers or judges. So it all boils down what GoI officers and judges will do
.
In (c), there is only information distribution. So there are costs like internet, printing / distributing pamphlets , newspaper advertisements etc. Other than newspaper advt, costs are small. And even if all newspaper advt are included, then also costs PER VOTER is much less than (a). The reason is --- the method (c) only aims at improving efficiency of tax money. and improving efficiency of officers. Neither activists nor contributors are directly paying for education.
.
The ELECTION .ACTIVISM has known problem - the CM / PM often behave like same previous CM / PM.
.
So method (c) - namely LEGISLATIVE activism, is LEAST expensive.
.
But there is also pitfall. (a) will give small output for small input. And so may (b). But in (c),, output is zero till the law-draft comes, no matter how much is input !!
.
So LEGISLATIVE activism has high threshold. And thats one reason why many activists prefer DIRECT activism or PETITION activism or ELECTORAL activism
.
So one cant say that Legislative Activism is "best" - because though it is least expensive, it has high threshold i.e. effects will come only after law-drafts are printed. But Legislative Activism is LEAST expensive of all - because activists and supporters are using their money ONLY to spread information and the physical expenses are done using tax money, and efficiency of tax money increases after laws change.
