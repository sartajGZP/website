Putting posts and pictures of campaign activities in one FB album vs putting all over wall - if you decide to put pictures etc all over all wall, then chances are high that your viewers may unfollow whole of the profile !!
.
Say activist X puts pictures / videos / posts on campaign and also puts 10-20 posts a month on law-drafts . Now say some activist / voter Y reads his posts.
.
Now say X decides to put several pictures wrt campaign on his profile. Now A has imo 3 choices
.
(1) X can put pictures and posts all over his wall - so B will get all pic on his newsfeed
,
(2) X can create 2-5 albums and put all putposts / pictures in albums.
.
eg X can create album called as "My election campaign" and put election campaign related posts and pictures in that album. And X can create an album called as "Law drafts I support" and put his law draft related posts in that album.
.
(3) X can create 2-5 album and put all posts in album and also put some 2-5 posts only on the general wall.
.
Now decision of X will effect whether B will read his OTHER posts or not !!!
.
Because say Y likes to read law-draft related posts but not see 10s of campaign pictures. But if X insists on putting all his posts and pictues on wall without separating them in albums, then Y may (or may not) decide to unfollow whole profile of X. But if X is dividing posts / pictures into albums, then Y can unfllow specific albums and follow other albums.
.
So finally, X has to decide. And his decision will effect how far his posts will reach.
