Electricity consumption imo shows that economy is in very poor shape, - consumption increase in oct-2017 compared to oct-2016 was only 3% !!!
.
see page-4 of http://www.cea.nic.in/reports/monthly/executivesummary/2017/exe_summary-10.pdf
.
total electricity production of oct-2016 = 099.785 billion units
total electricity production of oct-2017 = 102.617 billion units
% increase = 2.84% oi.e. below 3% !!
