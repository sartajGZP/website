#RtrPm2 - Full law drafts of Right to Recall PM .
.
RtrPM – Right to Recall PM
------------------------------------------
.
[ comment : This law-draft can be printed by PM as Gazette Notification. No permission of Loksabha or Rajyasabha is needed.
.
This draft has 2 parts -
.
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
.
Part - II : Instructions for citizens and officers
.
Important clauses are – (6) , (7) , (13) , (14) , (15) ]
.
----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : this law will enable you (you means any voter of India) to specify at most five persons whom you consider appropriate for the position of PM of India. You can file approvals over SMS or APP or ATM by personally visiting Patwari (Talati) office.

(2) comment : you can cancel the approvals anyday and you can approve anyone else anyday.
.
(3) comment : If any candidate gets 1 crore higher approvals than votes PM’s supporter MPs had got in the election, then that new person may become PM. And later, if his approval count decreases and some other candidate’s approval count is 1 crore higher than him, and then other candidate shall become PM
.
-----------------------------------
Part - II : Instructions for officers and citizens
------------------------------------
.
(4) comment : Comments are not binding on any officer etc. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(5) (5.1) The word citizen here means a registered voter.
(5.2) The word may means may or need not, and clearly means 'no binding'.
.
(6) If a citizen of India above 30 years wishes to be PM, he can appear before DC ( = District Collector). DC would issue a serial number for a filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election and put his name on the PM's website as PM candidate.
.
(7) If a citizen comes in person to office of Talati (or Patwari or Village Officer), pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five candidates for the PM position, then the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card.
.
(8) If a citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, then the Talati will cancel one or more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(9) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter. DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(10) PM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. PM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM or internet or mobile app.
.
(11) The Talati will put the approvals of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his approvals.
.
(12) DCs across India will publish Approval counts for each candidate.
.
(13) The existing PM may count his approval count as higher of the following two
.
(13.1) number of citizens who have approved him
.
(13.2) sum of votes obtained by Loksabha MPs who have supported him
.
(14) If a candidate gets approvals 1 crore more than approvals that the existing PM has, then the existing PM may or need not resign and may or need not ask MPs to appoint the highest approved person as new PM. Or, PM need not take this action, PM's discretion will be final.
.
(15) MPs may or need not elect the person stated above as new PM.
.
(16) Non binding directive for Range Voting :
.
(16.1) A few months after this law has passed, PM may or need not allow voters to submit marks between (-100 to 100) for each PM candidate or just submit approvals.
.
(16.2) If a voter is submitting approval and not marks, then no approval will mean 0 marks and approval will mean 100 marks.
.
(16.3) But if a voter is giving marks, then actual marks given by him will be counted.
(16.4) The candidates’ total marks will be sum of all marks he got.
.
(16.5) And if a candidate has 1 or more marks from over 51% of all voters, then PM will ignore all his negative marks.
.
(16.6) The total marks of sitting PM will be higher of the two ---- [ (votes that MPs that support him got * 67) , marks he got after election ] .
.
(16.7) Existing PM may resign and invite highest marked person to become PM if highest marked person has 67 crore more marks than existing PM has. And MPs may elect the highest marked PM as new PM.
.
(16.8) PM may or need not implement this clauses, after over 51% of all voters of India has specified YES using clause-CV.2.
.
(16.9) comment : This procedure is called as Range Voting, and it is immune to the so called AUIT ( = Arrow’s Useless Impossibility Theorem) . Range Voting is better than Approval Voting used here.
.
(CV.1) If any citizen wants a change in this law-draft, he may submit an affidavit at DC's office and DC or his clerk will post affidavit on PM's website for Rs 20/- per page.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen wants to register his opposition to this law or any section or wants to register YES-NO to affidavit submitted in above clause, Talati will enter YES/NO and give him a receipt for Rs 3 fee. The YES-NO will be posted on PM's website.
.
