In activism politics, it is LESS important what one believes --- it is more important whether he PUBLICLY acts per his beliefs or not
.
eg1. I hear many saying that over 80% supreme judges are honest and brave, while over 90% MPs are corrupt. But, I dont see them PUBLICLY requesting retired SCjs to contest elections. And I seldom see retired BRAVE SCjs contesting elections to replace dishonest MPs !!
.
eg2. one says that RSS is nationalistic and RSS and BJO are not same. Well, fine. But do you see him PUBLICLY asking RSS-workers and RSS-leaders to either fully publicly support BJP or Congress or AAP or create a better nationalistic political party?
.
So never dispute political beliefs of a person. When it comes to political beliefs, imo it is best to agree to disagree. But do see if his political actions on FB / twitter and outside internet are in synch with his beliefs and in sych with urgent needs of India. Only that is important.
