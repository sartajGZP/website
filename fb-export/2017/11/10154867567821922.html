Essentially, there are financial transactions and non financial transactions. Financial transactions are where money is moving, but there is no material moving below. And matrial or non-financial transactions are when material or land is moving along with money. eg say X buys vegetables for Rs 100 and so some material moved. But say Central Govt paid Rs 1000 crore to some State Govt. Then material moved = 0. A bordeline case is interest on loans - X loans Rs 1 lakh to Y and Y gives Rs 1.03 lakh after 1 month, where Rs 3000 was interest. Here material moved = 0, money moved = Rs 2.03 lakhs. But there was risk - and so one can take Rs 3000 as cost of risk or value of some material wealth generated from loan.
