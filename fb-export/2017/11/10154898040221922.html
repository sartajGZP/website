#RtrHCCj2 –
.
Full draft of Right to Recall High Court Chief judge
------------------------------------------
.
[ comment : This law-draft can be printed by PM as Gazette Notification. No permission of Loksabha or Rajyasabha is needed.
.
This draft has 2 parts -
.
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
.
Part - II : Instructions for Citizens, Officers and comments
.
Important clauses are – (6) , (13), (14) , (15) , (16) , (NS)
(NS) clause given at end is very important ]
.
----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : This law will enable you (you means any voter of India) to specify at most five persons whom you consider appropriate for the position of High Court Chief judge in your state. You can file approvals over SMS or APP or ATM by personally visiting Patwari (Talati) office.

(2) comment : You can cancel the approvals anyday and you can approve anyone else anyday.
.
(3) comment : If any candidate gets over approvals of over 50% of ALL voters in the state, and it is 10% (of all) higher than approval count of existing HCCj, then that new person may become HCCj. And later, if later his approval count decreases and some other candidate’s approval count is 10% higher than his approval count, and then other candidate shall become HCCj.
.
-----------------------------------
Part - III : Instructions for citizens and officers
------------------------------------
.
(4) comment : Comments are not binding on any officer etc. They are only to provide information to the activists, voters and Jurors.
.
(4a) comment : Understand -- (a) Right to Recall PM draft given at newindia.in/RtrPM2 and (b) Right to Recall Supreme Court Chief judge draft give at newindia.in/RtrSCCj will be useful in understanding this draft
.
(5) (5.1) The word citizen here means a registered voter in that State.
(5.2) The word may means may or need not, and clearly means 'no binding'.
.
(6) If a citizen of India above 30 years wishes to be SRJ ( = State Recognized Jurist), then he can appear before DC ( = District Collector). DC would issue a serial number for a filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election and put his name on the PM's website as SRJ candidate.
.
(7) If a citizen comes in person to office of Talati (or Patwari or Village Officer), pays Rs 3 fee, and approves at most five candidates for SRJ position, then the Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The fee shall be Rs 1 for those with BPL card. A citizen can also approve existing HCCJ or any existing HCJ.
.
(8) If a citizen comes to cancel his Approvals, then the Talati will cancel one or more of his approvals without any fee.
.
(9) DC may create a system of sending SMS feedback to the voter. DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(10) PM may add means to enable citizens to register approvals via SMS. PM may create a system where by citizens can register approvals via ATM or internet or mobile app.
.
(11) The Talati will put the approvals of the citizen on district's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his approvals.
.
(12) DCs across India will publish Approval counts for each SRJ candidate.
.
(13) If a candidate gets approval of over 35% of all voters in a State, then PM may appoint him as SRJ for that State. (comment : if a state has say 10 crore voters, then 35% will mean 3.5 crores irrespective of number voters who have filed for Approvals)
.
(14) If an SRJ gets approval of over 50% of all voters and the Approval count is 10% more than approval of existing HCCj, then PM may send the name of the most approved SRJ to Supreme Court Chief judge of India if he it is Constitutionally appropriate to appoint him as new High Court Chief judge in that State.
.
(15) If SCCj recommends within 7 days that the most approved SRJ should be new HCCJ and the existing HCCj resigns or existing HCCj is transferred by SCCj within 7 days, then and then only the PM may appoint that SRJ as the new HCCJ.
.
(16) However, if SCCj opposes or gives no response within 7 days, then PM may cancel his recommendation. And PM and Loksabha MPs may all resign and may declare new Loksabha election. Or, PM and Loksabha MPs may declare an Emergency. And as per our Magnum Opus Constitution of India, during Emergency, PM may temporarily suspend all the Fundamental Rights of all citizens of India, including the Supreme Court judges. The decision of PM and MPs will be final.
.
(17) comment : PM or CM may print a clause similar to clause-16 in RtrPm2 law-draft to implement Range Voting for SRJ and HCCJ candidates. Range Voting will allow each voter to give marks from (-100 to 100) to each candidate. And Range Voting procedure is immune to so called AUIT ( = Arrow’s Useless Impossibility Theorem). For more details, pls see claise-16 of RtrPm2.

(NS) (NS means “Nation is Sovereign”) With approval of 46 crore voters of India, PM can suspend this procedure in any State for 5 years. And suspension can be done again with approval of 46 crore voters. During these 5 years, HCCj will be appointed by PM / SCCj as per prevailing rule and will not be recallable by voters of the State.
.
(CV) CV : Citizens’ Voice:
.
(CV.1) If any citizen residing anywhere in India wants any change in this law or has any complaint under this law, then he may submit an affidavit to DC’s designated clerk and clerk will scan the affidavit on put on PM’s website along with photo and details of the citizen. The clerk will charge Rs 30 per page. The clerk will issue serial number for the affidavit.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen wants to register his YES / NO on the affidavit submitted in above clause, then he can go to Talati’s office and provide the serial number of the affidavit and his YES/NO on a form, and Patwari will enter his YES/NO for a fee for Rs 3 . The YES/NO shall come on PM’s website along with citizen’s voter-id, name, date etc
.
