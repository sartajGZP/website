#RtrHCCj2 - Right to Recall High Court Chief judge
.
Once this law is passed, state voters will be able to change High Court Chief judge. Each voter can approve one or more candidates via SMS / APP for the position of State Recognized Jurists. and can change approvals anyday. If approval of 55% of State voters and 10% more than approvals of existing HCCj, then PM / SCCj can appoint the highest approved person as HCCj.
.
And with approval of 46 crore voters in India, PM can suspend this law in any State.
.
