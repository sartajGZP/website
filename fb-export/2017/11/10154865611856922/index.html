Many activists may be interested only in creating an asset-rich NGO and interested in any national cause AFTER THAT (or may be never) !!
.
I came across many "education activists" (i.e. activists working to improve education in poor kids across India) , goseva-activists (protect cows across India), health activists (i.e. those who wish to improve health of citizens of India) etc etc
.
Now over 90% of SENIOR education activists refused to support Right to Recall District Education Law-draft on their twitter / FB walls. And they also refused to support RTR-State-Education-Minister law-draft and RTR-Central-Education-Minister law-draft on their FB/twitter walls !! And they did NOT propose any law-draft to improve education across state / India.
.
And over 90% of SENIOR gosevaks refused to support "narcotest in gohatya cases" law-draft or "Jury Trial in gohatya cases" law-draft or "right to recall over state cow welfare minister" etc on thwir twitter / FB wall. Nor did they propose any law-drafts that can reduce cow slaughter across state / India.
.
And same way, over 90% of SENIOR health activists , i.e. who wish to improve health refused to support Right to Recall over District Food Anti-Adulteration Officer, RTR State Health Minister etc etc.
.
Some of their JUNIORS do support these law-drafts,. But as is case with many NGOs etc, the juniors depend on approvals of the seniors
.
The reasons can be following
.
(1) NGO owner doesnt see any need to change laws. Or doesnt believe that activists and activism can do any law-change.
.
(2) NGO-owners may be interested in FIRST building a huge NGO, and then only after he wishes to serve the cause at state / national level !! So he first wants his NGO to have a few crores of assets or a few 10s of crores of assets, and then only AFTER that he wishes to serve the cause at state / national level.
.
(3) And once he meets the goal of having an NGO of say assets worth Rs 10 crores, he may decide to serve the cause ONLY after NGO has asset of Rs 100 crores and so on. i.e. he may never be able to decide to work for the cause at state / national levels !!
.
So there are many valid or "valid looking" reasons.
.
So my request to junior activists is -- if YOU wish to spend time and energy in bringing changes in laws in India, chances are high that your senior NGO-owner or party-owner will ask you to work on law-change AFTER his NGO / party has acquired assets worth crores !! So you need to decide whether you wish to wait or not. My suggestion will be --- do NOT wait for his NGO to acquire assets of crores ; pls start supporting drafts on FB / twitter / pamphlets etc asap
.
----
.
(Aside :
.
In RTR-movement, we never ask volunteers to donate or get donations except for newspaper advt.
.
So in newspaper advt, the senior will NOT be accumulating any material assets in his name or in name of party or any NGO. So one may say -- the senior activist will get some fame. Well, he may, get fame. But primary fame goes to the law-draft and the senior recallists ONLY gets secondary fame. But compare this to other NGOs. Here the NGO-owner converts donation into some land / building etc owned by that NGO and the NGO-owner also gets primary fame !! IOW , donations to most NGOs add assets and fame to the NGO-owners whereas advertisement contributions in Right to Recall only add "fame" to the law-drafts and ONLY some fame to senior recallists and zero assets to senior recallsts or RRP. So this is an important difference when it comes fund gathering in RRP and others)
