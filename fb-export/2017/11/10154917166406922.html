Empower Election Returning Officer to attest candidate's election affidavit (not other affidavits) - #EmpowerROtoAttestElectionAffidavit
.
As of today, if there are over 100-200 fields in election affidavit. And if by mistake the candidate forgets to fill a field, then he has to submit a whole new affidavit again. This wastes away a lot of time of candidate and also of election staff. And increases cost of conducting elections.
.
So law can be printed specific to election affidavits, that RO server as attesting officer for election affidavits (and election affidavits only). And if there are blank fields in affidavits, and RO points them out, then candidate can fill those fields.
.
This will save a lot of time of RO and candidate
.
