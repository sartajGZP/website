Should a recallist contribute to NON LOCAL pamphleteer and NON LOCAL election candidates?
.
A chicken and egg situation must be understood. A candidate gets local contrivution AFTER he contests election 2-3 times and so for first 2-3 times, he needs remote funding. But remote funding must eventually end.
.
In RTR movement, I have proposed following campaign methods
.
(1) sending necessary tweets @PmoIndia and putting necessary #MyCorePoliticalBeliefs , #iBelieve , #MyPastVoting , #MyPossibleComingVotingPreference tweets
.
(2) create albums of FB and share tweets and put other posts in albums in organized manner and put links in cover page description
.
(3) fill sample election form (if you are NOT contesting election)
.
(4) make videos (optional)
.
(5) distribute pamphlets
.
(6) newspaper advt
.
(7) contest elections
.
The steps-1 to step-4 cost Rs 0 i.e. costs are negligible. And if a person has NOT done step-1 to step-3, I dismiss him as non serious activist, unless he has contested election !! (election contesters are VIP in my world view and imo election contesting washes away all sins).
.
Now step-4 to step-7 are expensive, But newspaper advt can be measured, So question of LOCAL or NON LOCAL doesnt arise, The neswspaper advertiser will need to publish list of contributors
.
So question is --- should a recallist contribute NON LOCAL pamphlet distributors and non local election contestant?
.
My proposed method is
.
(1) If a person on your area is contributing, then assist him. But if no one in your area is contesting, then you may need to look in other areas
.
(2) If a person on your area is distributing to him, then assist him. But if no one in your area is distributing pamphlets then you may need to look outside
.
My point is --- one can always make a valid (or valid looking) argument that "I will contribute pamphleteer in my area and election contester in my area and not other areas. Because if a person is in other areas, then I I and no one can supervise. But question is -- what if NO ONE in your area is contesting election? What if no one in your area is a pamphleteer? So a valid reason which leads to complete inaction is useless. Local supervision is welcome, but sometimes it is non option
.
But at the end of the day, each recallists must fund himself OR he must get funds from people in his local areas or people who are able to supervise his actions. It is necessary to ensure that money is spent PROPERLY (eg if I give a newspaper advt which no one can understand, then money is NOT well spent. if I distribute pamphlets which has layout and language which no one can understand,m then again, money isnt well spent. So who decides if advt is proper? Who decides is pamphlet is well made? Well, the person who is spending money is the best person to decide !! ).
.
But at the same time, if a recallist is not well off, he cant contest elections and he cant distribute pamphlets. with his own money. And if he doesnt contest elections, then he cant get local contribution. This chicken and egg situation must be understood. A candidate gets local contrivution AFTER he contests election 2-3 times and so for first 2-3 times, he needs remote funding.
.
So a contributor should NOT cancel funding a remote contestant just because he is remote. Because if that candidate is NOT well off and is contesting first or second time, it is impossible for him to get local funding
.
So following is the decision making methods I propose
.
(A) Decision making method I propose for contributor
.
(A.1) Contributor should decide how much he wants to contribute.
.
(A.2) If there are local pamphleteer or local election contesters, then contributors should fund them and ensure that money is well spent
.
(A.3) But if there are no local pamphleteer or local election contesners, the bimo, pls support a remote person 2-3 times.
.
(A.4) Summary --- give funding where you can supervise best. But if all spots where you can give money have zero supervision, then pls dont make "I cant supervise" as reason from not funding.
.
(A.5) Whatever contribution you give, pls write a status post in your FB album called as "My election contribution"
.
---
.
(B) Decision making method I propose for pamphleteer and election contester
.
(B.1) Whatever contributions you get, pls write a status post in album "Election contributions I got".
.
(B.2) Take any contribution by cheque / RTGS / paytm i.e. paper / electrobnic only (you make take very small chillar by cash, but avoid as far as possible)
.
(B.3) Take contributions ONLY from individuals (NOT trusts / companies etc) and ONLY those who are Indian citizens or Persons of Indian Origin or Overseas Citizens of India. No foreign contributiomns. NO EXCEPTIONS.
.
(B.4) You may take contributions from those who are NOT in your area. But remember, when that remote donor finds a local activist in his area, he may very much stop contributing,. So pls work to get local contributors. I repeat --- PLEASE WORK TO GET LOCAL CONTRIBUTORS
.
-----
.
(C) How can you convince locals to contribute?
.
(C.1) Frankly, I have zero experience !! I am fincially a very well off (well off by Indian standards, by IIT standard, I am THE poorest of all 28 IIT delhi computer science students who passed out with me in 1990 and I am perhaps the poorest in ALL 260 IIT delhi students who passed out with me in 1990 in all branhces. But by Indian standards, I am quite well off !!) Since I am well off, people may not like to contribute to my pamphlets and elections. They have a fallacy that I have enough to win election -- and a fallacy which cant removed easily. But more important reason is -- I am spening more than 95% of my time in law-drafts and proposing campaign methods. So I never got time to do fund raising etc.

(C.2) IMO, best person to talk is Pawam Kumar at https://www.facebook.com/pawan.jury . He may be able to explain how to work so that local contributors can be obtained
.
My hunch is that along with pamphlet distribution, one needs to spend a lot of time in explaining concepts,. I am unable to do this because (a) need to spend time in law-drafts (b) right now, I can go in self funded more
.
So I request every pamphleteer and election contestant to note one thing -- pls work to create local contributors. You cant depend remote contributors all the time.
.
