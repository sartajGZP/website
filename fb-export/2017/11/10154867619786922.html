The status at fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154867555946922 is what I call as Law-Draft Index or INDEX status.

That way, probability that a person, who is visiting my FB profile (be newcomer or person who has already been to my profile) will come to know about these law-drafts increases. And imo, it is fastest POSSIBLE way on FB to spread the information.

Whereas your FB profile can "talk" for 24 hours a day, and your profile can talk to 1 to 10 to 100 to 1000 persons a day. So if you keep descriptions and links, to law-drafts you support, on your FB profile and FB posts properly, then speed at which information about law-drafts will spread via you will increase.
.
IMO, each person can ONLY increase probability that these laws will get passed thru his efforts. By keeping links and description etc in an "organized" manner on your FB profile, you can increase the rate at which information will spread amongst those who visit your FB profile. And thus you can increase the probability that the law will get passed.
