At the end, each activist / voter can ONLY increase the probability that a law will get printed. No voters / activists is all powerful God that he alone can get it done, even if he works 1000 hours a day !! And no number alone , can give 100% guarantree.
.
So question like --- "how long i.e. how many days how many activists need to work - will it take to get this law passed" or rather "how many activist man-days are needed to get this law passed" , and non-questions in activism. Because many lot depends on how those who oppose these law-drafts act and how far they are willing to go , and that is not possible to estimate
.
And many events leave effect. eg Freedom fighters like Mahatma Bhagat Singh started a movement and pace increased.. But Duratma Gandhi and RSS-leaders were able to keep activists busy, and effective slow down the freedom movement. But second world war, an unexpected external event, happened and weakened the British. And that enabled freedom movement to grow, and Rashtrapita Subhashchandra Bose's military actions gave huge boost.
.
IOW, activists efforts aka activism increases possibility , and it may or may not take it to 100%. But say, activists increase the information level, and then some unexpected event can drastically increase pace of movement

And unexpected event can also dampen the movement. So unexpected event need not always favor the ,movement
.
So numbers alone dont create success. After all, less than 120,000 British were able to rule over all 40 crore Indians for over 100 years !! These 120,000 British were backed by 4 crore British back in UK, but UK was ruling over 100 crore people across world. So if one takes pro-rata, then some 1.5 crore British from distance of 1000s of miles ruled over India.
.
So numbers alone dont decide, but increase in numbers does increase probability.
