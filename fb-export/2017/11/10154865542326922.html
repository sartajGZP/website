Competition in causes (and proposed law-drafts) such as
.
(a) one may feel is his cause is done first, then other’s cause will automatically get done afterwards or will get done easily".
.
(b) one may feel that other cause is good, but only after his own cause is first implemented , and if other cause is implemented before his own cause, then there it will harm India !!
.
and many more valid or “valid looking” reasons.
.
----
.
(1) Two activists may be opposing each other
.
Many times, activists will be opposed to each other. eg many activists’ cause is to have strictest implementation of anti-opium law i.e. they want a legal / police system where ALL opium users and sellers would get long prison sentences asap. Whereas, I want complete legalization of opium. And the law-draft I have proposed, namely "Jury Trials in cases of opium growing, selling, buying, reselling and using" is more likely to legalize opium. So clearly, I and anti-opium activists can work together on this issue. IOW, there are many cases where activists are opposed to one another.
.
---
.
(2) Agreeing but still poles apart – Reasons !!
.
But there are cases where activists agree on causes, or “seem to agree” on causes. But disagree completely when it comes to campaign !!
.
-------
.
(2.1) Reason-1 – My cause should be kept first !!
.
And they may have valid reasons why their cause should be first. I will discuss some reasons I got.
.
.
Now in my activism method, given any cause, I either oppose a cause or I have given law-drafts that imo implements the cause. So for some 20 causes, there are some 100 law-drafts I have proposed. And for 100 causes there are some 300 law-drafts that I have proposed.
.
And activism method I propose to other activists is – he can publicize the drafts he supports on twitter, FB, pamphlets, newspaper advt, election contesting, election campaigning etc. And he may ignore the drafts that he things are not useful, and he may ignore the drafts / causes he isnt interested in or opposes.
.
Now some law-drafts are far important to me than others. eg DDMRCAM (aka MRCM) is most important to me. But to fellow activists, I never insist that they should promote DDMRCAM more than other law-drafts. Each activist decides which law-drafts he wishes to publicize
.
----
.
(2.2) Reason-2 ---– my cause or my proposed law-draft is more fundamental
.
But many activists insist that law-drafts that they propose are “more important” and “more fundamental” then others’ law-drafts.
.
And there is one more situation --- many activists insist that law-draft-B is good, but must be passed ONLY after cause-A has been fulfilled !! eg one statement I get is “RTR-law-drafts are good, but RTR-law-drafts need educated citizens and so RTR-law-drafts should be passed ONLY after education casue has been fulfilled and till then RTR-law-drafts should NOT be passed !!” IOW, not only his cause of education is ahead of RTR, but till his education cause has been fulfilled, RTR should be opposed !!!
.
In my campaign method, all this doesn’t exist. If I support a law-draft-B, I never put condition that law-draft-A must be passed BEFORE or cause-A must be fulfilled before and so on. IMO, if an activist likes law-draft-A, then he need not wait till casue-B is met or law-draft-B is passed. Each law-draft can do good on its own.
.
---
.
(2.3) My law-draft / cause subsumed your law-draft / cause !!
.
Say activist-A supports cause-A and law-draft-A and activist-B supports cause-B and law-draft-B. Now activist-A may make statement that “I support law-draft-B, but activism on law-draft-B is not needed !! Because once law-draft-A is passed, law-draft-B will NOT be needed or law-draft-B will get passed without activism !!” IOW, as per activist-A, activist-B “should” end his activism on law-draft-B, and should focus only on activism for law-draft-A.
.
The argument of subsuming may or may not be correct.
.
Eg1. consider law-draft-A that “all landownership records should be made public”. And consider law-draft-B that “land ownership of trusts should be made public”. The law-draft-A covers law-draft-B, but from activism point of view – it does not. Because there can be many pro-privacy activists who will support law-draft-B and NOT law-draft-A !! So getting law-draft-A printed is more difficult, and so some activist may campaign for law-draft-B. So activist-B has valid reason not to agree with activist-A.
.
Eg2. consider law-draft-A which reduces poverty and law-draft-B which improve ration card system. Activist-A may feel that “once poverty reduces, ration card system isnt needed and so activism for law-draft-B isnt needed” !! But activist-B may feel different. Because getting law-draft-A passed may take longer, and even if it passes, there is some possibility that poverty may not reduce for months. And above all, person who wants to improve ration card system may not have so much faith in activist-A that he will agree to spend time in explaining law-draft-A to 1000s of supporters of law-draft-B !! So even if law-draft-A may be subsuming law-draft-B, the activism for law-draft-A imo does NOT subsume activism for law-draft-B. The activist-A and activist-B both have valid reason for not working on same campaign steps.
.
---
.
(3) Proposed solution
.
The two solutions I propose are
.
(3a) that each activist-A should put a status post in his FB profile which explains steps he wants other activists to take. And the status should be kept in a way that activist-B who is visiting profile of activist-A can reach by following links from his cover page. Eg the steps I ask other activists to take are at - fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154841368981922 . The link is in coverpage description. Other activist can read the writeup and decide how much to follow, and how much not to.
.
(3b) Each activists should make list of law-drafts he supports (and/or causes) in his FB statuses, and these statuses should be reachable from his FB cover picture. And every other activist can take a look, and work on law-drafts A is proposing or ignore his law-drafts. i.e. no pressing need for a common minimum program or common maximum program.
.
----
.
(4) Conclusion
.
My point here is --- when activists oppose each other’s cause, disagreement is visible.
.
But many times, when activist-A supports cause-B and viceversa, then it would NOT mean that they can agree on one campaign method. And some of the reasons can be
.
(4a) activist-A feels that if cause-B or law-draft-B is implemented BEFORE law-draft-A or cause-A, then it will cause harm !! And so cause-B or law-draft-B, is good, but should be postponed till cause-A or law-draft-A is implemented
.
(4b) activist-A feels that if law-draft-A is implemented, then cause-B gets fulfilled or will get fulfilled easily, whereas activist-B doesn’t think same or doesn’t want to spend time in explaining law-draft-A to 100 other activists working on law-draft-B implemented
.
And many more valid or valid looking reasons !!
.
And solution I propose is – each activist can put steps he proposes other activists to take in an FB status and he can put law-drafts he supports in FB statuses. And the statuses should be reachable by links starting from cover picture. That way, one activist can decide how many of law-drafts given by other activist does he wish to support / oppose / ignore.
