#PartialRefundOfElectionDeposit - complete refund of election deposit at 5% of polled votes and partial refund for lesser votes (eg 20% deposit will be refunded if a candidates gets 1% votes)
.
Today, one needs 15% of votes for election deposit refund. And deposit is refunded completely or completely forefitted. Today, there is no partial refund.
.
I am proposing law for Partial Refund of deposits as follows
.
5% of polled votes - complete deposit refund . So I am proposing lowering of threshold from 15% to 5%.
.
And for lesser - partial pro-rated refund
.
So if a candidate gets 4% of polled votes, then deposit refund he will get will be 80% of deposit he paid

(My reason : This will enable one to justify increase in MLA deposit as follows
.
(1) candidate wealth above Rs 2 crore, then proposed deposit = Rs 200,000
(2) candidate below Rs 25 lakhs = Rs 25000
(3) candidate wealth in between = 1% of wealth )
.
---
