WHY such rule? Why ask ALL to have one and ONLY one profile on FB?
.
Say an activist has 4 profiles, and on one profile, he praises RTR, on second profile he praises SoGa / congress. on third profile he praises NaMo / RSS and on forth profile he praises ArKe / AAP. Then how do I or anyone knows what he stands for? He can tell RRP workers that "see, I am recallist, and other 3 profiles are only to fool congress / rss / aap" workers. And he can tell RSS worker "see, my second profile is main profile, and I am pro-rss and I use other three profiles ONLY too fool recallists and congress/aap workers". And he can go to congress workers and say "see, my 3rd profile is main profile, see I am pro-congress and my other 3 profiles are only to fool recallists and rss / aap workers". And so on.
.
Now FB allows a person to have dozens of profiles. But if we as activists start encouraging people to have multiple profiles, each profile appearing as main profile, then we are allowing people to make different statements to different people.
.
And to ensure that one person is making SAME statements to all persons, it is necessary that we ask ALL to have one profile only.
.
Now many people want 2-3 profiles so that if FB blocks one profile, then they can use other profile. They also use other profile to keep important post as backup. This is fine - because FB does block profiles.
.
