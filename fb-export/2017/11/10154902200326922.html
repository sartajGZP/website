#JSHSM Act – Jury System for High / Supreme Courts for Murder and other violent crimes
---------------------
.
[ comment : This JSHSM law-draft needs to be passed in Loksabha and Rajyasabha with simple majority, and passing this law needs no amendment in Constitution.
.
This draft has 2 parts -
.
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
.
Part - II : Other instructions for Officers and citizens
.
Important clauses are – (7) , (11) , (12) ]
.
The “comments” listed here are not binding on any Officer / Minister / judges. The Jurors , Citizens and all can use it as guidelines. But they are not binding. ]
.
-----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : summary of the law
.
(1.1) comment : The reader is requested to read “newindia dot in/causes/djsm” i.e. “District Jury System for Murder and serious violent crimes” law-draft. The DJSM law creates Jury System in Magistrates’ Courts and District Courts, for crimes such as murder, beatings, threat, rape, abduction and all crimes where physical force or threat of using physical violence is used.
.
(1.1.1) comment : this law-draft creates a system where appeals, filed against judgments given by Juries in District Court, will be decided by Juries in High Court and Supreme Court.
.
(1.2) comment : once this law passes, you (you means registered voter in India) can be called for Jury Duty in your State High Court or India’s Supreme Court. If you are called for Jury Duty, then you will have to listen to facts and arguments as presented by accused, victim, witnesses and lawyers of both sides and decide the punishment/fines or acquittal.
.
(1.3) comment : The jury system in High Court will be supervised by an officer called as State Jury Administrator. SJA will be appointed by CM and you (you means voters of State) can replace him using (RTR) clauses given in this law.
.
(1.3.1) comment : The jury system in Supreme Court will be supervised by an officer called as National Jury Administrator or NJA. NJA will be appointed by PM and you (you means voters of State) can replace him using (RTR) clauses given in this law.
.
(1.3.2) comment : The appeal in High Court will be decided 15 to 1500 Jurors called in High Court OR case will be sent to 3 to 5 District in the State Juries and majority judgment will be taken as High Court judgment
.
(1.3.3) comment : The appeal in Supreme Court will be decided 15 to 1500 Jurors called in Supreme Court OR case will be sent to 5 to 11 District Juries across India in different States and majority judgment will be taken as Supreme Court judgment
.

Part - II : Instructions for Citizens, Officers and comments
------------------------------------
.
(2) This law will cover only appeals against verdicts of District Courts and Lower Courts, and only in following cases, regardless of age of accused and victims
.
(2.1) murder, attempt to murder, any human death due to accident or negligence or any unnatural human death
.
(2.2) any crime involving violence, such as beating, or threat of violence or accident or negligence that caused bodily harm or could have caused serious injury
.
(2.3) abduction / rape / molestation / harassment / stalking of woman of any age, including harassment at workplace.
.
(2.4) any type of crime or any type of civil dispute mentioned by PM or CM using Gazette Notification in this list
.
(2.5) any crime or any type of civil dispute mentioned by majority voters via using “(CV) Citizen’s Voice” clauses, and approved by PM / CM.
.
(3) , (3.1), (3.2) [ same as clauses (3) , (3.1) , (3.2) of DJSM]
.
(4) Temporary suspension of Jury System in a State
.
(4.1) With approval of over 51% of ALL citizen voters in India, PM can suspend all above clauses for any districts in India and appoint DJA for 5 years in those districts. PM may change SJA anytime during these 5 years. And PM may move a case of that State to another State selected at random
.
(5) The CM will appoint an officer as SJA, which citizens can replace using RTR clauses given later. The PM will appoint an officer as NJA, which citizens can replace using RTR clauses given later
.
(6) Formation of First State Grand Jury and First National Grand Jury
.
(6.1) Using the voter list of the State, SJA will, in a public meeting, randomly select 60 citizens from the voter-list between age of 25 years and 55 years. From that, he can exclude any 30 after interview, so that finally there are 30 State Grand Jurors. If SJA is appointed by PM under clause-(4), then SJA may select up to 100 citizens from State / India and exclude 70.

(6.2) Using the voter list of whole India, NJA will, in a public meeting, randomly select 100 citizens from the voter-list of India between age of 25 years and 55 years. From that, he can exclude any 70 after interview, so that finally there are 30 National Grand Jurors.
.
(7) Formation of subsequent Grand Juries - In the first set of Grand Jurors, SJA and NJA will retire the first 10 Grand Jurors every 10 days. And after first month, term of each State / National Grand Juror will be 3 months, and thus 10 Grand Jurors will retire every month and 10 more will be selected. For selection of 10 Grand Jurors, JA will select 30 voters at random from voter list of State or India, as in clause above, and select any 10 after interview.
.
(8) Method to select voters at random – same as DJSM
.
[ (8.1) to (8.3) , SJA and NJA will use procedures similar to clauses (8.1) to (8.3) in DJSM ]
.
(8.4) The State Grand Jurors and National Grand Jurors will meet on every Saturday and Sunday. They may meet on more days if over 15 Grand Jurors approve. The number must be over 15, even when less than 30 Grand Jurors are present. The meetings, if happen, must start at 11 am and last till at least 5 pm. The Grand Juror will get Rs. 500 per day he attends plus travel expense. The CM / PM may change the compensation as per inflation or other conditions such as travel distance. The money will be give 60 days after his term ends.
.
(8.5) If a Grand Juror is absent in a meeting, he will not his daily payment. And may loose up to thrice his amount to be paid and maximum fine of 0.05% of his wealth and 1% of his annual income may be imposed. The individuals who are Grand Jurors 60 days later will decide the fine.
.
(9) If any person or company or District / State / National Prosecutor or any entity, has complaint against any other person or entity, or appeal against a judgment, and crime is covered under clause-2 or GN issued under clause-2, then he can write to some or all Grand Jurors or ask Grand Jurors to give hearing or he may upload the complain on website using (CV) clause. The complainer may specify the remedy he wishes. The remedy can be obtaining possession of a property or obtaining monetary compensation from the accused or imprisoning the accused for certain number of years/months or his execution. The remedy asked must be legal, as per the law interpreted by Jurors and Grand Jurors.
.
(10) If over 15 Grand Jurors, in a meeting, issue an invitation to a witness or complainer or accused, then that person may appear. The Grand Jurors may or need not invite the accused or complainer. They may allow lawyers and experts to speak or not to speak. The hearing can be over video conferencing via District court or Tahsil Court or any designated place or can be in person.
.
(11) If over 15 State / National Grand Jurors declare that there is some merit in the complaint, then appeal will be admitted. The appeal will be conducted as follows :
.
(11.1) For State High Courts, Grand Jurors using majority will decide one of the two following options :
.
(11.1.1) Grand Jurors can ask SJA select 15 to 1500 voters at random from voter list of State or India and call them at State High Court and decide the appeal
.
(11.1.2) Grand Jurors can select 6 or 10 districts at random, and pick 3 or 5 districts using discretion, and ask each DJA to call District Juries of 15 to 1500 voters to decide the appeal. The verdict of majority District Juries will be taken as verdict of High Court
.
(11.1.3) in each case, Grand Jurors will decide the number of Jurors who will decide appeal, and it will be decided by median
.
(11.2) For National High Courts, Grand Jurors using majority will decide one of the two following options :
.
(11.2.1) Grand Jurors can ask NJA select 15 to 1500 voters at random from voter list of India and call them at Supreme Court and decide the appeal
.
(11.2.2) Grand Jurors can select 10 to 22 districts at random from India, each in separate State, and pick 5 to 11 districts, using discretion, and ask each DJA to call District Juries of 15 to 1500 voters to decide the appeal. Here, verdict of majority districts will be taken as verdict of Supreme Court
.
(11.2.3) in each case, Grand Jurors will decide the number of Jurors who will decide appeal, and it will be decided by median
.
(12) if majority Grand Jurors think that the complaint has no merit, then they can dismiss the complaint. And if majority think that complaint was absolutely frivolous then they may impose a fine of upto Rs 20000 per hour they spent in hearing and deliberation, within one hour minimum, and no more. Each Grand Juror will declare a fine, and the median fine will be imposed and executed by DJA. The Jurors will also decide how much of that fine should be given to wrongfully accused as compensation. The accused may file a separate case to recover additional compensation for damages such as loss of time, reputation and other damages he suffered.
.
(13) Deciding number of Jurors for a case – [ same as in DJSM]
.
(14) Jury selection – SJA and NJA will put a procedure similar to DJSM.
.
(15) SJA will ask HCCJ = High Court Chief Judge to appoint one or at most 3 Judges to advise the Jurors and administer the case. The Chief Judge's decision on number of judges will be final. SJA will appoint Trial Administrator , TA henceforth, to run the trial. NJA will follow similar rule for Supreme Court.
.
(16) to (19) TA, i.e. Trial administrator will use procedure similar to DJSM
.
(20) to (23) [ same as in DJSM]
.
(24) to (27) [same as in DJSM]
.
(28) Appeal : Either party will have 30 days to appeal against High Court’s verdict in Supreme Court. The verdict of Supreme Court cannot be appealed in any Court, but can be reserved by Parliament or citizens using (CV)-clauses.
.
(29) (a) If citizens of State instruct HCCJ to cancel the verdict of State High Court Jury using CV clauses at State level, then HCCJ may cancel the verdict of High Court Jury (b) If citizens of India instruct SCCJ to cancel the verdict of Supreme Court Jury using CV clauses at all India level, then SCCJ may cancel the verdict of Supreme Court Jury
.
(30) The Police Officers across India will execute the fine or imprisonments as written by the High / Supreme Judges and approved by the Jurors in High/Supreme Courts.
.
(31) In cases listed in clause-2 or GN issued under clause-2, no Govt employee will impose any fine or prison sentence without consent of over 2/3rd of Jurors of the Lower Courts, unless approved High Courts or Supreme Court or majority voters of District / State / India as decided by (CV) clauses. No Govt employee will imprison any citizen for more than 48 hours without approval of over 2/3rd of the Jurors or 50% of the Grand Jurors or majority voters of District / State / India using (CV) clauses
.
(32) The Jurors will decide the facts as well as intensions, and must also interpret the laws as well the Constitution, and natural justice. If Jurors think that the law is unjust or inapplicable, then the Jurors can give “not guilty” verdict even against the law. The Jurors can also give “guilty” verdict even against the law.
.
(RTR) Right to Recall State/National Jury administrator – PM shall print RTR clauses for State / National Jury Administrators. The threshold for change for SJA will be “over 51% of all voters of state must approve and over 5% of all voters more than existing SJA must approve”. The threshold for change for SJA will be “over 45 crore approvals in India, and over 1 crore more than existing NJA”.
.
