#DJSM - District Jury System for Murder and other violent crimes
.
(full text of the proposed law-draft - fb.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154878885916922 )
.
Once this act is printed, punishments/fines/acquittals, in cases such as murder, abduction, rape, beatings, death due to negligence and all cases which involve force and violence, will be decided a Jury of 15 voters to 1500 voters chosen at RANDOM from District/State or whole India. The age of Jurors will be between 25 years and 55 years. The cases will be decided in Lower Courts. And appeal can be filed in High/Supreme Court. A separate law create Jury System in High/Supreme Court.
.
And later, PM/CM/voters may add several more types of crimes and many types of civil disputes in this law. And so those cases thereafter will be decided by Jurors and not judges. PM/CM can add types of crimes etc using legislation. Voters can add types of crimes using procedure stated in newindia.in\TCP2 or any such procedure
.
Why have “Jury System for murder” first? And why then add 100 types of crimes later using next 2-10 law-drafts? Why not have Jury Trial for all cases in one go?
.
Because nyayamurtipujak (i.e. intellectuals i.e. buddheejeevies of India who believe that judges are Gods and so they worship judges as Gods) give one argument against Jury System that “Jury System is worse than judge system because people of India don’t know laws”. The nyayamurtipujak use this argument to create FUD i.e. create fear, uncertainty and doubts in the minds of activists. And thus nyayamurtipujaks try to create opinion and atmosphere against Jury System. So to thwart this argument and this FUD strategy, I have proposed “DJSM - Jury System in Lower Court for Murder and other violent crimes”. So when this SPECIFIC Jury System is being discussed, most activists will agree that commons of India very much do understand laws wrt murder and violent crimes. And so intellectuals’ argument that “people don’t know laws about murder and violent crime” will NOT appeal to activists. And so intellectuals’ attempt to create FUD i.e. fear, uncertainty, doubt against DJSM will fail. And so support for DJSM in activists will grow. And thru later law-drafts, many more types of criminal offences and civil disputes can be added. So we can have full Jury System in about 2 to 10 steps.
