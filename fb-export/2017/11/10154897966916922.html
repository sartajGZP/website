#RtrSCCj - Right to Recall Supreme Court Chief judge
.
Once this law is passed, citizens will be able to change Supreme Court Chief judge. Each voter can approve one or more candidates via SMS / APP for the position of Nationally Recognized Jurists. and can change approvals anyday. If approval of most approved NRJ crosses 45 crore, then PM and existing SCCj may appoint most approved person as new SCCJ.
