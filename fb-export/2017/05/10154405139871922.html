The ONLY difficulty EVM adds is 20 second delay. So to add say 500 votes, a criminal will have to wait for 500*20 - 10000 seconds = 3 hrs and so policemen can catch him with probability more than paper ballot
.
But if franking ballot paper is made compulsory, and franking machine can have delay of 20 seconds, then we have same 20 second delay.
.
OR we can have ballot paper dispensing machine (just as we have a currency note dispensing machine in ATMs). with 20 second delay.
.
OR we can have adhesive label dispending machine with 20 second delay, and ballot paper without adhesive label can be voided
.
And booth capturing didnt stop due to EVMs, It reduced because starting 1998, election commission started using CRPF and Military to gaurd booths. Before that ECI used to have just 1-2 LOCAL policemen with no guns to gaurd cluster of 1 to 10 booths !!! IOW, EVMs didnt reduce booth capturing. It was better policing.
.
Today, ECI can put CCTV camera (which are cheaper than EVMs) in booths and sensitive booths can have 2 camera, with live feeds to police stations. So booth capturing is passe, All in all, EVM lobby is using fake arguments to rationalize EVMs
