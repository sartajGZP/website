Pls ORGANIZE coverpage, coverpage description and posts on your FB profile, so that if and when a TRUE activist visits your FB profile,
.
(a) chances that he reads your posts increases
.
(b) chances -- that he gets introduced to the law-drafts and campaign method of "send important instructions to @PmoIndia over twitter" -- increases
.
(c) he sees links to PDFs etc as soon as possible, and chances that he downloads increases
.
(d) he becomes capable of spreading the information on drafts and campaign methods as soon as possible
.
(e) all this should happen with MINIMAL phone talks or personal meetings and by maximal readings on his part
.
The last part is important. The posts, links to videos, links to PDFs, the contents in videos, the contents in PDF etc should be so that he can become "recallist" with minimal phone / meeting talks. Because you will never get time to do meetings and phone talks to explain to 100s of activists in your life
.
----
.
So pls see a post on your profile. Pls see whether it will increase the chances that a TRUE ACTIVIST will read next post or walk away because he feels that that post was waste of his time. Imo, a non political post will make a true activist feel that he wasted his time in seeing and reading your post. And imo a political post which was just an echo or slogan may also make him feel that he wasted his time. All this is subjective. Likewise, if an activist is supporter of a leader and you wrote against that leader, he may walk away from your profile. So should that stop you from writing posts against that leader? I wont. because in this case, imo, it is better to lose that activist that not write the post !! So you need to decide whether post is worth writing or not , and one factor is whether an activist will find that post relevant or timewaste. But that should NOT be the only factor.
.
You can also make some posts "friends only" and rest public. And you can make non friends as "restricted friend" on FB. Pls google on what restricted friend means.
..
Next, most IMPORTANT imo is to keep a FB status post called as "INDEX to important post" . This status post should have links to ALL important posts in your profile. (see index post of MY profile in first comment of this post). And link to this index MUST be in description of the cover page. (I am yet to add that).
.
And coverpage imo should have clear sentence that mentions the reader that cover page description has law-drafts you support. And coverpage should be so that any user can download and upload it as his coverpage. Because not many will find time to make a coverpage using paint software etc.
.
Likewise, each step you tale on your profile should be so that chances a TRUE activist reads more posts and he becomes more productive.
.\
----
,.
Finally, is match fixing cheating? Well, movies are always fixed. We all know that dialogues in movies are pre-scripted and pre-rehearsed. Does that make movies unethical? NO.
.
Match fixing is same as movie scripting and nothing else. So I dont see ANYTHING unethical in match fixing
.
The only sad part I see is when outcomes of match fixing get Padma Shri , Bharat Ratna etc. Solution is that we should remove ALL sports , cricket as well as other sports, from Govt awards. The sportsman get medals for winning matches and get modeling contracts. And they can keep money they get from match fixers. Thats good and that should be all.
.
So there is NOTHING immoral in match fixing, It should be fully legalized.
.
If anyone wants to stop match fixing, then he can demand a law that Jury can order narcotest in public in case there is a complaint of match fixing. But if I am in Jury, I will oppose narcotest and dismiss the case, because I see NOTHING wrong in two players fixing the game before they start.
.
Another law one can propose to end match fixing is -- loser must die. I oppose the law - but this law is MUST to end fixing !! Romans made this UNPRINTED DEFACTO law in all gladiator sports, that the winning gladiator must kill loser or else the stadium guards will kill the winner !!! They had to make this unprinted law. Thats becuase gladiators used to fix matches !! And romans saw that unless "loser must die" law is made, there is NO way to end this match fixing. Now, I oppose this ":loser must die" law. And instead, I support match fixing.
