Why pictures with political messages are useless. And why tweets are BETTER options
.
Many activists suggest that we should ask supporters of a cause to keep some postor / pamphlet in hand and take their pictures, and post them in profile of activist, as a proof that that citizen has supported the cause.
.
Because thanks to photoshop, such pictures will have NO trust. eg I can make picture of Donald Trump with any message say "India should bring Right to Recall Supreme Court judge law in India". How much trust will that picture generate?
.
So alternative I propose is that --- we should ask citizen-supporter to put a tweet with appropriate link and hashtag on HIS twitter account, abd the activist should keep LINKS to that voter's tweet in a list. The tweet on account of citizen's own account is a proof that he is supporter.
.
So pictures are now useless. What "counts" is only tweets on citizens' own accounts.
