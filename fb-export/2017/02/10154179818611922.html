,
Let me state this FACT --- it was Sant Sri Asaram Bapuji who had started this about 6 years ago !!!
.
I dont at all mind Ahmedabad colleges / schools and Missionary schools copying this idea. But least they could have done is given credit to the man who started it.
.
It is shame that colleges / schools officers did NOT even mention name of Asaram Bapuji !!!
