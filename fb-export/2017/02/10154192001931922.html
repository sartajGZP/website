Instead of asking "why powerful are powerful", ask "why powerless are powerless" !! And better, ask "why a segment of population LESS powesrless in one country is MORE powerless in another country" .
.
There must be countless quotes \ videos on what is power and why powerful are powerful. From serious looking writings of bikau political science professors (*) to 2 bit script writers of serials like Games of Thrones, net is full of quotes and vidoes of what is power and why powerful are powerful.
.
I always found all definitions or power and writings on "sources of power" and "what makes powerful people powerful" useless and boring. None really pointed to how even trivial ecopol (*) problems, whose technical solutions already existed, can be solved in ecopol. ,
.
An activist , instead of focusing on why powerful are powerful, should rather focus on why powerless are powerless and how can they be made LESS powerless !! And one way is to find answers to question "why a segment of population LESS POWERLESS in one country is MORE POWERLESS in other country" !! Answering that question will give REALIZED options to reduce powerlessness. And then original analysis can give more options. Thats what many TRUE-activists aka non-80G-activists do. But "interesting": videos and quotations on power , sources of power, what makes powerful person powerful etc will never help in answering these questions.
