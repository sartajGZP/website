RBI collected data 3000 private non-financial companies Between nov-jun. Their survey shows --- small companies suffered MUCH MUCH more large companies due to demo and pre-GST transition !!! And some very large companies profitted heavily !! And sum total went down.
.
The analysis doesnt cover imports, But foreign companies benefited MOST , because local manufacturing was disrupted and so many had to use imports more
.
Thats what I had said on nov-2016 , that smaller a company / person, more damage he will face due to Demo. And pre-GST transition cause same effect
.
Transition part's losses are inevitable and will vanish in long run. But question is --- once GST stabilizes by dec-2017, whom will it favor? My analysis says --- smaller a company, more it will suffer and reason is NOT technical inefficiency or economic inefficiency , but GST law's missing trader problem and double taxation on small traders problem makes it worse for small firms.
.
Pls read the whole article. EVERY line is raw data from RBI and worth reading.
.
And real music will only now start. Right now, GSTN has only started taking invoice details and only 10% of July invoices are uploaded. By 30-nov-2017, companies will finish uploading all july invoices, and GSTN will generate missing trader notices !!! By 31-dec-2017 end, there will LAKHS of missing trader notices all over !!
.
