And bank had to also hire temp peons and pay them some Rs 400 per day. Plus staff had to work, and banks had to compensate them indirectly.
.

And banks got flooded with deposits, and there were NO loan takers. So interest burden shot up.

So TOTAL costs that banks to bear was far more
