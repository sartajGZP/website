Who are "usuk-elitemen" that I often mention? And how do they control Indian leaders / judges?
.
(1) Who are "usuk-elitemen" that I often mention?
.
Eliteman means a person who is extremely wealthy or earning lot, and yet manages to pay much lesser TOTAL taxes , as % of his wealth / income, than tax rate that middle class men pay.
.
So a doctor earning Rs 10 crore a year, and paying Rs 2 crore taxes is NOT an eliteman, But someone who has land worth Rs 500,000 crores in Mumbai alone, and not paying even Rs 10000 crore as TOTAL income and all taxes is an eliteman.
.
The elitemen has stashed their wealth in mineral mines, foundations aka charitable trusts, foreign companies, foreign accounts, bank holding companies, banks shares etc and so defacto tax rate on their income/wealth is much much below middle class and even lower class.
.
Each entity they own, the tax rate is less !! eg if you own an emmpty unused home in USA, then normal tax rate applies. But if a bank-owner owns home via bank he owns, the tax rate is almost zero !! In addition bank owners via bank can create unlimited money and loan it to companies they own, and so defacto interest they have to pay is low. And the foundations owners stash the wealth in foundations, and so inheritance tax rate is zero, income tax rate is near zero, many wealth tax exemptions apply and so on !!!
.
So who are these usuk-elitemen at INDIVIDUAL levels? What are their names, addresses, social security numbers etc?
.
To get complete answer, one will will need to publish ALL data on who owns which mineral mines, who owns which plots of land, who owns which foundations, and how much wealth those foundations have, who owns which bank holding companies and so on. Once this data is all put on public website, all names can be churned out
.
Who are usuk-elitemen? Some families are Rothschilde, Rockefellar etc. Some estimate that some 200 families and some 2000 individuals own over 90% of bank holding companies and foundation assets.
.
India too has its share of wealth individuals whom total tax rate is near much much lower and defacto mineral royalty they pay is much lower. eg Tata owns a coal mine on which royalty he pays is (hold your breadth) Re 1 per YEAR per ACRE !! Yes, per acre, not even ton. Dig as much coal as you want and royalty is princely sum of Re 1 per year per acre only !! He got this mine in 1946 for 999 year lease !!! Condition is that coal can be used only for Tata's steel mills. But who is watching the gates? Tata's own security !!! And likewise, Godrej via trusts owns some 3400 acres of land in mumbai alone !! At throw away price of Rs 500,000 per sqmt, it would be about Rs 500,000 lakh crores !! And how much wealth tax he payd? zero !! because it is "charitable" trust.
.
What makes them stronger inside their own country? The strength comes from LOWER TAX RATE they pay and lower royalty rates and land rents (or land prices) on govt plots they pay. Then other ways are bribing forcing ministers / ias / judges to twist laws and judgments that will make the field unlevel and so on.
.
---
.
(2) Why usuk-elitemen are stronger than Indian-elitemen?
.
Thats because USA / UK are far far stronger than India. USA has RTR, JurySys, referendum, multi-election, no service tax, no GST, less sales tax, no excise, has less convoluted income tax, has wealth tax etc and so on. India has no RTR, no JurySys, no referendum, no multi-eelections, has huge GST etc and no wealth tax. And so factory owners in USA are LESS INEFFICIENT than factory owners in India. And so USA's manufacturing ability is more and weapon manufacturing ability is more.
.
So Indian elitemen are now dead scared of usuk-elitemen. If Indian elitemen defy the order of usuk-elitemen, then they know that usuk-elitemen wll give weapons to pakistan and run havoc in India (eg kargil war).
.
In short, the strength of usuk-elitemen comes from weapons US Govt owns
.
Now inside USA, there is RTR / Jury / referendum etc to by which commons control us-elitemen, But when it comes to us-elitemen's power in FOREIGN country such as Iraq or Libya or India, the ONLY control is weapons that these countries own, which are next to zero. So us-elitemen have effectively NO control in Iraq, Libya, India etc
.
-----
.
(3) Do elitemen add wealth of subtract wealth from commons?
.
The elitemen pay less tax rate and less royalties and less land price (from govt land). So they subtract wealth from commons. BUT, there is a big BUT here --- usuk-elitemen also get gigatons of wealth from foreign countries into their own countries. eg us-govt and us-elitemen got gigatons of crude oil from iraq and libya for free !! So this adds wealth to commons. Thats why activists in USA are not juch against us-elitemen,. Because they do all additons and all subtractions, they see that commons of usa are not losing
.
The commons in country like iraq, libya , china etc end up facing twice the burden --- they have support their own elitemen and also usuk-elitemen. Solution? Long list
.

(4) So summing up, usuk-elitemen are NOT wealthy men in USA who are paying huge taxes too. They are people who have huge incomes / wealth via their companies / trusts and defacto tax rate they pay in usa or foreign country where they are earning is much much lower than tax rate on middle class respective country. Their strength in their country mainly comes from lower tax rate and in foreign country, it comes from weapons they and their own govts own.
