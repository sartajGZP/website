EIAR Act – Expelling Infiltrators and allowing in Refugees from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar etc
.
(a) to enable Govt officers to trace infiltrators and refugees for several countries, such as Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar etc
.
(b) to enable Govt officers and Jurors to prove whether a person is an infiltrator or a refugee or a bonafide Indian citizen
.
(c) to expel infiltrators and allow in refugee
.
------------------------------------------
.
[ comment : This EBIAR law-draft can be printed as Gazette Notification by PM (but not by CMs). It does need approval of Loksabha or Rajyasabha and needs no amendment in Constitution.
.
This draft has 2 parts -
.
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
.
Part - II : Other instructions for Officers, citizens and comments
.
Important clauses are – (1.2) , (1.3) , (5.4) , (6.6) , (6.8) , (6.8.1) to (6.8.5) , (8.1) , (8.2)
.
The comments listed are not binding on any Officer / Minister / judges. The Jurors , Citizens and all can use it as guidelines. But they are not binding. ]
.
-----------------------------------
Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) comment : summary of the law
.
(1.1) comment : This law sets a procedure by which Central govt officers can trace Bangladeshi infiltrators as well as refugees and gather evidences to prove / disapprove whether he is Bangladeshi or a bonafide Indian citizen, and whether he is infiltrator or a refugee.
.
(1.2) comment : The officers will gather phone call logs, blood group details and DNA details of accused and their blood relatives in Bangladesh. The officers will also gather whatever documents they can gather from Bangladesh that can prove that accused is Bangladeshi or not.
.
(1.3) comment : The officers will place all facts before Jurors. The Jurors using these facts, testimonies and using narcotest in public, the Jurors will decide whether the accused is Bangladeshi or bonafide Indian citizen, and whether he is refugee or infiltrator. And if he is infiltrator, then Jury will decide the punishment.
.
(2) NR and Right to Recall NR
.
(2.1) PM will appoint National Registrar for Infiltrators and Refugees, also referred as NRIR, or NR for short. NR will appoint State Registrars and District Registrars, and other officers to carry out the tasks
.
.(2.2) If you (you means, every registered voter of India) are dissatisfied with will be able to approve upto 5 alternate candidates. If any candidate gets approved by over 45 crore voters of India, then PM may appoint the most approved candidate as NR. Thus, you i.e. voters of India, will have Right to Recall NR. The details of the procedure are in “(RTR) Right to recall NR”: clauses.
.
-----------------------------------
Part - II : Instructions for Citizens, Officers and comments
------------------------------------
.
(3) PM will appoint an officer titled as National Registrar for Infiltrators and Refugees, henceforth referred as NRIR or simply NR. The voters may replace NR using “{RTR) Right to Recall NR”
.
(4) National Registrar will appoint State Registrars i.e. SRs and District Registrars i.e. DRs, and NR will also print RTR procedure code for SR and DR NR / SR / DR will hire other officers on at most 2 year contract or take officers from State / Central Govt on deputation
.
(5) Making first list of Bangladeshi using phone call logs and other details
.
(5.1) PM will order Department of Telecom to provide list of all calls to and from Bangladesh to NR in past 2 years. Using this list, NR will make a list of Bangladeshi suspects
.
(5.2) Any citizen of India can publicly or privately provide a list of Bangladeshi suspects, and he must order the list in descending order of suspicion. If the list has less than 20% errors, then NR will reward him in private pr public, as the citizen wishes
.
(5.3) NR, based on his discretion and to the best of his information, will give approximate number of Bangladeshi in each District in India
.
(5.4) NR will appoints agents in Bangladesh. NR will agents to get blood group details and DNA samples of suspects’ relatives living in Bangladesh. NR will also ask agents to get documents that may prove Bangladeshi origin of suspects.
.
(5.5) NR may put names, pictures and other details of the suspects on the public website, requesting all to obtain names etc of his relatives in Bangladesh.
.
(5.6) NR will prepare a list of suspects that NR is convinced are Bangladeshi.
.
(6) Jury Trials to decide if suspects are infiltrators or refugee
.
(6.1) For each suspect, NR will select 100 voters between age of 30 years and 55 years from 5 different districts. The first district will be district closest to where suspect was found, and has Bangladeshi population below 10% (best to the information of NR). The other 4 districts will be four closest districts to the first district and each district should also have Bangladeshi population below 10%.
.
(6.2) NR will select 20 voters at random from each District.
.
(6.3) The NR may increase number of districts to 10, and select 20 more Jurors from each Districts using above method. NR’s decision on number of Districts to be chosen will be final.
.
(6.4) The accused will inform Jurors about whether he was born in India or in which country was he born in. And if he was borne in India, the in which city / village he was borne, whereabouts of his parents, brothers, relatives and where he did schooling etc. And if he was borne outside India, then why he has emigrated to India.
.
(6.5) NR’s officers will explain whether the person is borne in India or borne outside India, and whether he is an infiltrator or a refugee.
.
(6.6) If over 50 Jurors suggest that polygraph in public, brainmapping in public and narcotest in public should be taken, then these tests will be taken. Each Juror will suggest questions and each Jurors will give 0-100 Points to each question and questions with highest marks will be asked. NR will select doctor for narotest and brainmapping. But of over 67 Jurors approve another doctor, then that doctor will be taken. The doctor chosen must have degrees required by NR.
.
(6.7) After hearing all sides, each Juror will cite whether he thinks that accused is bonafide Indian citizen or refugee or infiltrator. And if infiltrator, then for how many months should he be imprisoned for wrongful entry inside India, and what should be fine on him. And if the accused was refugee and bonafide citizen, then what should be his compensation between Rs 0 and Rs 100,000
.
(6.8) Decision whether he is citizen, refugee or infiltrator
.
(6.8.1) If over 67% Jurors declare than accused is infiltrator, then NR will imprison him for median number of months, and then deport him to the country where he came from
.
(6.8.2) if over 50% but below 67% Jurors cite that he is infiltrator, then he will be allowed to stay in India with no imprisonment, but in one of the districts decided by NR
.
(6.8.3) if 50% or less Jurors decide that he is infiltrator, then he will not be punished.
.
(6.8.4) if over 50% Jurors declare him as citizen, then there will be no action against and no restrictions against him. And NR will give median amount as compensation
.
(6.8.5) if over 50% have declared him as non-citizen, but a refugee, then he can stay in India as resident, in a district designated by NR ; and later, he may apply for citizenship
.
(7) Appeals
.
(7.1) Either NR or convict may appeal the decision of Jurors before Jury chosen from twice the number of districts. From each district, NR will choose 20 Jurors at random.
.
(7.2) The decision of second Jury can be appealed in High Court or Supreme Court.

(8) Reduction in prison sentence
.
(8.1) NR’s officer will ask convict to provide names and addresses of blood relatives For every name and address of blood relatives or other infiltrators he gives, and proofs he provides, NR will reduce sentence by 1 month.
.
(8.2) NR’s officers can narcotest convict in public to obtain more names of infiltrators that he knows of.
.
(9) Districts for refugees – to divide burden of refugees across India
.
(9.1) NR will make a list of districts where population of refugees is less. (comment : so all districts of Asam, West Bengal, North East, Mumbai, Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida etc will not come in this list, as they have already taken more than fair share of refugees)
.
(9.2) NR will remove border districts and districts where economic opportunities are low. (comment : border districts have security problems, and districts where economic opportunities are low will not be able to support refugees)
.
(9.3) The refugees will have to live in one of the districts in the list.
.
(RTR) Right to Recall National Registrar for Infiltrators and Refugees
.
(RTR.1) If any voter of India wishes to become NR, then he can appear in person before DC (DC = District Collector of District where he is voter). DC would accept his candidacy for NR. DC will take filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election. And DC will issue a serial number unique across India.
.
(RTR 2) Any voter can visit Talati office in person. pay Rs 3 fee, and approve at most five persons for the NR position of NR. The Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on PM's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences
.
(RTR.3) If a the voter comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee
.
(RTR.4) If a candidate gets approval of over 43 crore voters in India, and has 1 crore more approvals than existing NR, then PM may or need not expel the existing NR and may or need not appoint the person with highest approval count as NR. The decision of PM will be final
.
(RTR.5) If existing NR has approval of less than 35 crore voters, then PM may replace him.
.
(RTR.6) PM may create a system whereby citizen can file\cancel approvals using SMS or mobile phone apps or via ATMs.
.
(RTR.7) comment : Later, PM may create a system where voter can give score of (-100 to 100) to any candidate. If a voter doesn’t give any score, it will be assumed as zero. The voter will have choice --- he can give score of simply approve / not-approve, and in that case not-approving will mean score of zero and approving will mean score of 100. If a candidate has positive score from over 33% o ALL voters, then negative scores will be ignored. The candidate with highest score, and who has over 51% of highest possible score will be appointed as NR
.
(CV) Citizens’ Voice:
.
(CV.1) If any citizen residing anywhere in India wants any change in this law or has any complaint under this law, then he may submit an affidavit to DC’s designated clerk and clerk will scan the affidavit on put on PM’s website along with photo and details of the citizen. The clerk will charge Rs 30 per page. The clerk will issue serial number for the affidavit.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen residing in the area covered by the Patwari wants to register his YES / NO on the affidavit submitted in above clause, then he can go to Talati’s office and provide the serial number of the affidavit and his YES/NO on a form, and Patwari will enter his YES/NO for a fee for Rs 3 . The YES/NO shall come on PM’s website along with citizen’s voter-id, name, date etc The YES / NO count will not be binding on PM or any officer etc.
.
