One day , Raju Srivastava was asked to read a deshbhakti poem. Since it was deshbhakti poem, he decided to take shower first (he hadnt taken bath since he was 7 years old).
.
So took bath, and recited the deshbhakti poem. Audience was impressed , and asked "sir, you recite poem so well, whats you name? !! Apparently, since he had taken shower and he was doing non-comedy act, the audience could NOT recognize him !!
.
So Ravi Srivastava though "my image is as comedian, and if audience finds out that I am comedian, then patriotic effect that poem reciting generated will decrease" !!
.
So he said "my name is Kumar Vishavas" !!
.
Point is --- just as shaktiman and gagadhar are same, KV and RS are same. Just as gangadhar after dress change becomes shaktimaan, "RS after taking shower and on reciting patriotic peome" becomes KV !!
.
They are actually one and same person. Their aadhar card number is also same.
.
Thats why, when you see speeches of Kumar Vishvaaas on politics, they are as comic as talks of Raju Srivastava !! Kumar Vishvas's speeches on politicas are very very entertaining. But after speech , when one thinks "what solutions did he provided?", one realizes that whole speech was just a comedy. The reason why it is comedy is, at core, KV is just RS.
