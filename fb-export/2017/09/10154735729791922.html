Proposed SSDMT Act – Sanatan Sampradaya Devalaya Management Trust Act
.
(a) to free Hindu \ Sanatan Devalayas i.e. temples from Govt control ; and
.
(b) to create an SGPC like management structure over Hindu Devalayas , if and only if , the existing trustees \ owners wish to create such a management structure.
.

Part - I : Basic instructions for citizens
------------------------------------
.
(1) This law applies only for Devalaya of Sanatan Sampradaya across India, and Trusts (or bodies) which own these Devalayas. The word “Sanatan” in this law includes all followers of all Hindu Sampradayas and other Sanatan Sampradaya \ Panth which originated in Indian subcontinent. The law does not put label of Hindu on those sects or citizens, who do not wish to be called as Hindu. They may apply this law on their Devalaya without applying label of Hindu on themselves and their Sampraday.
.
(1.1) This law will NOT put any restriction on followers and shrines of Islam, Christianity, Zoroasterism and religions which originated outside Indian subcontinent. They will not be covered under this law. And since Sikhs already have SGPC Act, this law will also not apply on Sikh Gurudwara as well.
.
(1.2) comment : This law will initiate a Jury Trials, to decide ownership of all Devalayas under Government control. The Jurors will consist of 12 to 1500 Hindu voters between the age of 30 years and 55 years , chosen at random from the voter list of District / State / India as may be the case. The Jurors will decide which claimant has what % ownership / trusteeship over the Devalaya. And after deciding, will handover the Devalaya to the owners and free it from Govt control. The trial in most cases will be over within 6 months.
.
(1.3) comment : This law will enable the owners / trustees of Devalayas to create an SGPC (SGPC means Sikh Gurudrwara Prabadhank Committee) like structure to manage Devalayas. All voters are requested to read and study SGPC Act to know its details. The owners / trustees of the trust may decide NOT to create such SGPC like structure over their Devalayas. But owners decide to create such SGPC like structure to manage their Devalayas, then the Bhakts (followers) of the Sampraday will elect the heads and Juries will chosen from Bhakts will decide the disputes. And if the owners / trustees decide to create such a structure, then they cannot get old management structure back.
.
(2) PM shall appoint a National Registrar. National Registrar will keep list of owners, trustees and Bhakts (followers) all Devalaya and Hindu \ Sanatan Religious Trusts in India. The National Registrar will appoint State Registrars and District Registrars to manage the task at State \ District Level.
.
(2.1) National Registrar, State Registrar and District Registrar are referred as NR, SR and DR in this act.
.
(2.2) You (you here means a Hindu \ Sanatan voter including Sikh, Jain, Buddhist voter, residing anywhere in India) can replace NR using procedure described in “(RTR) Right to Recall NR” clause given later. NR will print RTR procedures for State / District Registrars.
.
(3) In case there is a dispute between Devalaya Trustees, the DR / SR / NR , will randomly select 12 to 1500 Hindu \ Sanatan voters between age of 30 years and 55 years from District / State or India, to resolve the disputes between Devalaya owners / trustees. And DR / SR / NR will hand over all Devalaya under Govt control back to trustees and owners. The Jurors will also decide the weight of the votes of each owner / trustee, using a procedure given in later clauses.
.
(4) This law will give two options to Devalaya trustees / owners to manage the Devalaya in two ways (4a) the owners or trustees themselves using their votes can appoint / expel Devalaya Managers and staff, who will manage the Devalaya , and disputes will decided by Managers or Trustees / owners (4b) owners / trustees can create a structure in which each Bhakt will be a voting member, and Bhakts elect / recall the Devalaya Chairman, and Jurors selected from Bhakts will decide the disputes amongst staff and Bhakts.
.
(4.1) It will be optional for Devalaya trustees / owners to create such democratic structure. It is not all required at all by this law that trustees / owners must create such a democratic structure. If the trustee / owners want, then they can continue to run the Devalaya on their own, without giving electoral or management powers to the Bhakts.
.
(4.2) But if the trustees / owners decide to form a democratic structure, then they cannot take this control back. So formation of the democratic structure is a “one way street”.
.
-----------------------------------
Part - II : Instructions for Citizens, Officers and comments
------------------------------------

(5) Appointment \ Recalls of Registrars :
.
(5.1) The PM will appoint an officer titled as National Registrar for Sanatan Sampradaya Trusts, henceforth referred as NRSST , or National Registrar or simply NR. NR will be recallable by voters of all Bharatiya Sampraday across India such as Hinduism, all sects with roots in India, Jains, Buddhists, Sikhs etc.
.
(5.1.1) comment : The Right to Recall over National Registrar procedure is detailed in later clauses.
.
(5.1.2) comment : The Sikhs have been visiting Hindu Devalayas over and above Gurudwara since centauries, and have also been giving donations to Devalayas, and have worked to protect the Devalayas in past. And so they will have voting rights in RTR-NR)
.
(5.2) NR will appoint State Registrars (SR) and District Registrars (DR). NR will also print procedures by which Hindu \ Sanatan voters of State can replace SR and Hindu \ Sanatan voters of a District can replace DR. If Hindu voters find these procedures weak, then they may add procedure using (CV) clauses given later or replace NR using RTR-NR clauses given later.
.
(6) Registration of Devalaya, Devalaya owning Trust, and Trustees
.
(6.1) For rest of the act, the word “trustee” will include “owner” if the Devalaya owning body is a private body and not a trust. And the word trust here will include that “private body”, if that private body is owning the Devalaya.
.
(6.2) If Devalaya is directly owned by one or more, but less than 20 persons, then Devalaya owners will be free not to register themselves and not to register their Devalayas with DR. They may or may not register. But if Devalaya is owned by a registered private body or a trust or registered association or any non-person entity, or owned by 20 or more persons, then Devalaya and owning body must register themselves with DR.
.
(6.2.1) They will register within 30 days after DR issue notifications. After that, registration can be there, but Jurors may impose a fine for being late.
.
(6.3) DR will post the name and location of each Devalaya on his website. DR will also post the names, addresses etc of the Trust that owns the Devalaya. DR will issue a serial number for the Devalaya as well as the Trust. DR will also publish the Trust Deed (or partnership deed or document that shows formation of the private body that owns the Devalaya, or simply name of owners if that Devalaya is owned by one or more persons without any body). SR and NR will combine this data across all districts of India and put on their websites.
.
(6.4) DR will post names and Aadhar number of every Trustee, or every person who claims to be Trustee.
.
(6.4.1) If later, Jurors decide that he had knowingly made a false claim that he is Trustee, and he knew that he had no basis to make such claim, then Jurors may imprison him for 3 years.
.
(6.4.2) His claim will be accepted or rejected later using Jury Trial. But DR must register all claims.
.
(6.4.3) If trustee doesn’t have Aadhar number, then DR will use his PAN or Passport Number or Voter Number or number issued by DR or number issued by SR / NR. And DR will also put names / numbers of persons who were trustees in past along with when they were appointed and when they left, as far as records become available.
.
(6.5) SR / NR will combine and put information for State / India.
.
(6.6) For all official work, the Trustee will have to come to the DR or offices designated by DR to manage the Trusts. If the Trustee is living in same district in which the registered office of the Trust is located, then the Trustee will have to visit that DR office. If the Trustee is living in some other District, then the Trustee may visit DR where he lives in or DR where the Trust is registered. DR may work to delegate some work to Talati.
.
(6.7) DR will register all trustees’ claims, that they are trustees, without asking for proofs. Later, if there is dispute, on whether a person who claims to be trustee is not trustee, then Jurors will decide if he is trustee or not. And Jurors will decide the fine for making false claims. But DR will admit all claims and post them on website.
.
(6.8) If a person who claims that he is Trustee of a Trust, must Register his name within 30 days after DR issues notification for Registration. The fee after 30 days will be Rs 1000 higher for delay of each month. No claim will be admitted 10 years after this law is passed..
.
(7) Registration and annual fees
.
(7.1) DR will charge a fee of Rs 2000 one time, and Rs 1000 per year from each Devalaya in the district where Trust is registered. If Trust owns Devalaya in District other than District where Trust is registered, then fees will be twice.
.
(7.2) If the Trustee resides in District other than District where Trust is registered, then DR will charge Rs 2000 one time and Rs 1000 per year. If Trustee resides in District other than District where Trust is registered, then fee will be twice.
.
(7.3) The fee for Chairman will be twice the trustee.
.
(8) Displaying assets owned by Trusts
.
(8.1) Each trustee will submit the list of assets that trust owns, best of his information, to DR. The list will include the locations of the plots it owns, area of constructions, cash, FDs, shares, bonds, gold, silver, furniture, loans given, loans taken and all assets , along with circle rates (aka Jantri) in the area where plot/constructions is located. DR will display all the information on website. One or more trustee may sign a list of asset prepared by one Trustee.
.
(8.2) DR will obtain the circle rates of each property listed in the list and display the information on DR’s website.
.
(9) Resolving disputes of Trusteeship and deciding each Trustee’s weight
.
(9.1) Any Trustee (i.e. person who claims to be Trustee) can prepare a list of persons whom he thinks are Trustees with their Aadhar number or other ID numbers, as per the Trust Deed and agreement of Trustees and subsequent Trust Resolutions etc, best to his information. The Trustee must give this list to DR within 3 months after this law is passed or Jury may levy a late fine. The list must also have weight of each Trustee’s vote, as per Trust deed and / or agreement of Trustees and best to his information.
.
(9.1.1) Votes of Trustees, in Trusts registered under this act, may have same weights or different weights, as decided by Trustee. All trustees must agree. If there is dispute, it will be decided by Jurors
.
(9.2) A trustee can prepare the list himself or approve the list prepared by another Trustee. But each Trustee must prepare one list himself or approve exactly one list. If he doesn’t prepare or approve any list, DR will summon him and remove that Trustee’s name within 30 days.
.
(9.3) The Trustee who is giving list must also specify weights of the votes of the members. And if the weights are specified, then they must add up to 100 or at least 99.99 . The weights must be decimal digits and not fractions , and can have at most 15 digits after decimal. If the weights do not add up to 100, then DR will re-scale the weights so that they add upto 100 or at least 99.99. If weights are not given then DR will post the list, and mark it as “useless”.
.
(9.4) A trustee can submit new list anyday and he can change list he approves anyday. But he cannot modify any list he has submitted.
.
(9.5) The DR will both charge following fees to the Trustee who is submitting list :
.
(a) Less than 10 Trustees - Rs 500 per Trustee
(b) 10 to 100 trustees --- Rs 5000 + Rs 50 per trustee
(c) 10 to 1000 trustees --- Rs 10000 + Rs 20 per trustee
.
The Trustee who is approving a list, and not submitting a new list, will only pay Rs 500. And if a Trustee who has submitted one list, is submitting a new list, then fees will be Rs 5000, plus difference between new fee based on trustee count and previously paid fee, only if the new fee is higher. If a trustee has approved a list, and he wants to approval a different list, then charge will be Rs 1000.
.
(9.6) DR will put each list submitted by Trustees on the website. (a) If the Trustees have presented only one list, and that list is approved by all the Trustees, OR all lists presented by all Trustees are identical wrt Trustees and their weights, then DR will take first submitted list as undisputed list. (b) But if the lists submitted by Trustees have different Trustees and/or have different weights, then DR will call for Jury Trial to decide the final list of Trustees and their weights.
.
(9.7) The DR will call a Jury of Hindu \ Sanatan 15-1500 citizens chosen at random from voter list of the district between the age of 40 years and 55 years to decide --- who are trustees and who are not , and what is the weight of each trustee’s vote. The size of Jury will depend on assets in the Trust, and will be 15 Jurors and 1 Juror more per Rs 1 crore of asset. If any Trustee wants to change the District to District which has largest Devalaya (largest by donation collection in past three years before the law is passed), then he may ask NR. NR will select 5 districts at random, and each DR will call a Jury of exactly 15 Hindu \ Sanatan to decide. If three or over three Jurors agree, then NR will move the case to that District.
.
(9.8) Each Trustee will present Member List with weights before the Jurors. And DR will give his list best as per his opinions and findings. In addition, one Juror may allow any one voter of India to present a list of Trustees for that trust with weights. The Jurors with majority vote will decide which Trustee or which List Preparer will speak for how many minutes.
.
(9.9) After all speeches, each Juror will either (a) approve a list OR (b) he will give 0-100 weight to each person who is claiming to be Trustee. .
.
(9.9.1) The list given by each Juror should be such that weights must add to over 99.99. If a Juror has not give any weight to a Trustee, then it will be presumed as zero weight. If weights don’t add to over 99.99, then DR will rescale the weights so that they add up to over 99.99.
.
(9.9.2) DR will take average of all weights that a Trustee claimant got from all Jurors, and display that as a final list.
.
(9.10) After the list is finalized, each Juror will cite a rupee number to be given to each List Preparer from the Trust. The rupee number must be between Rs 0 and 2% of the Trust Wealth. If the amount is higher than 2% of Trust Wealth, then DR will lower it to 2% of Trust wealth. And the sum of all amounts in the list made by Jurors too should be below 2% of Trust wealth. If the sum is above this limit, then DR will rescale them so that sum is 2% of the wealth. But if the sum is below this limit, then DR will not rescale it.
.
(9.10.1) After this, DR will add the numbers cited by each Juror for each List Preparer. The grand total of all payments given to all List Preparers. Again, all grand total must below 2% of the trust wealth. And if the amount is more, then all totals will be rescaled so that final sum of all sums if below 2% of the trust’s wealth. DR will take that amount from the Trust and will pay that amount to the List Preparers.
.
(9.11) A Juror may also announce a fine between Rs 0 and 2% of wealth of the person who had claimed to be Trustee, if the Juror thinks that that person had willfully prepared or approved a false list. The Juror can also cite a number between 0 months and 36 months of imprisonment, if the Juror thinks that that person had criminal intent in giving such false information. DR will take median fine and median imprisonment as final fine and imprisonment.
.
(9.12) If anyone is not satisfied with the final list of Trustees and weights , then they can appeal before SR. SR will randomly pick three districts in the State and send the complain to DR of those 3 Districts. Each DR will call a Jury of same size or larger size as the size of the Jury called in the first District within 30 days. Each Jurors in each Jury will give 0-100 points to the list presented in the first trial. No new list can be presented in the appeal. SR will declare the list with largest points across 3 Juries as the final list. The Jurors can also announce fines and imprisonment length in months. And SR will impose median fine and median imprisonment sentence.
.
(9.13) If anyone is not satisfied with verdict of SR, then they can appeal before NR. NR will randomly pick five districts across India, in the States other than where Trial was held. in the State and send the complain to DR of those 5 Districts. Each DR will call a Jury of same size or larger size as the size of the Jury called in the first District within 30 days. Each Jurors in each Jury will give 0-100 points to the list presented in the first trial. No new list can be presented in the appeal. NR will declare the list with largest points across 5 Juries as the final list. The Jurors can also announce fines and imprisonment length in months. And NR will impose median fine and median imprisonment sentence.
.
(10) Procedures by which Trustees can appoint / recall Trust Chairman and Trust Committee Members
.
(10.1) Each Trust will have one Chairman and at most 20 committee members. Each Trustee will give number of committee members trust should have to DR. DR will take median number, taking the weights of Trustees into account.
.
(10.2) Appointment and recall of Chairman by Trustees, in Trust with Non-Democratic Structure
.
(10.2.1) Any person who wishes to be Chairman for a Trust can register his name. If he is Trustee, then he doesn’t need any person to second his name. If the person is NOT a Trustee, then he will need a Trustee to second his name. The fees will be Rs 2000 for Chairman candidate and Rs 1000 for Committee member other than Chairman.
.
(10.2.2) A Trustee can give (-100 to 100) Points to a any Chairman candidate. The Points will come on DR’s website and thus it will be open voting. The vote filing fee for trustee will be Rs 50 per score he is giving (so if he is giving scores to say 8 candidates, then fee will be Rs 400). The Trustee can change score anyday. The fee will be Rs 50 per change. (so if Trustee changes Points of say 10 candidates, then fee will be Rs 500). If a Trustee has not given score to any candidate, then it will be taken as -100.
.
(10.2.3) A trustee can transfer his voting rights to a Trustee or non-Trustee for a fee of Rs 500. And he can revoke the voting rights anyday for no fee. If a person has voting rights for more than one Trustee, then he may file same vote for all trustees or file differently. If he is filing differently, then separate filing fees will apply. If he is filing together, then fee will be fee for one, plus Rs 50 for each Trustee.
.
(10.2.4) DR will multiply the points given by a trustee with weight that that Trustee has and add the Points received. If a person has obtained positive points from majority of Trustee, counting weights, then DR will ignore negative Points given to him. The DR will appoint the Chairman candidate who has highest total of weighted Points as the Chairman of the Trust
.
(10.2.5) Every 2 years, the candidate with highest Points will be appointed as Chairman by DR
.
(10.2.6) Recall of Chairman before 2 years by Trustees : any day, if a Chairman candidate as over 33% of highest possible Points, and he has over 2% of the Points that current Chairman has, and the difference stays for more than 30 says, then DR will remove the existing Chairman and replace him with Chairman with highest Points
.
(10.3) Appointment and Recall of Committee Members
.
(10.3.1) Any person who wishes to be Committee Member for a Trust can register his name. Fee will be same as fee for Chairman.
.
(10.3.2) A Trustee can register whom he prefers as Committee Members in order of 1 to as many he wishes. The fee will be Rs 100 per preference he gives. A trustee can change his order of preference anyday. Fee will same as per first filing.
.
(10.3.3) A Trustee can give his voting rights to any Trustee or non Trustee for a fee of Rs 500 and revoke the rights anyday for no fee. If the person has voting rights of another Trustees, then he can vote for all Trustees together or separately. If he is voting together, then fee will be
.
(10.3.4) The votes of a Trustee will be multiplied by the weight of his votes
.
(10.3.5) DR will use system will similar to Rajyasabha members are elected (as in year 2016) with a difference that each vote has a different weight.
.
(a) Say number of committee members to be appointed are N and say sum of all weights is 100. Then a committee member will need X = (100/N + 0.001) or highest votes to become member
.
(b) DR will add first preference votes for each candidate. If one candidate has obtained over X votes, then he will be declared as winner, and the X votes he got will be marked as “consumed”, And the remaining votes will be given to second preference. This step will be repeated, till all votes are consumed, and N candidates are selected.
.
(c) NR will print detailed instructions. The system should be identical to system where Rajyasabha MPs are elected.
.
(d) The voting will be open. All preferences etc cited by Trustees will come on DR’s public website.
.
(10.3.6) DR will change committee members every two years using above procedure.
.
(10.3.6) During those two years, if a candidate’s approval is at least X and 2% higher than approval of the least approved committee member, then DR will remove the least approved committee member and appoint the candidate with highest approval
.
(11) Nominee : A trustee can appoint one or more nominees for his Trusteeship.
.
(11.1) Transfer or sale : A trustee may permanently transfer part or whole of his Trusteeship. A trustee may legally accept money or other consideration for permanent transfer. But a trustee cannot mortgage or give power of Attorney on his Trusteeship, or transfer for a duration, and make it part of any contract.
.
(12) Dissolution of Trust :
.
(12.1) If over 75% Trustees, counted by weights, inform DR (by personally appearing at DR’s officer where Trust is registered) that they want to dissolve the Trust, the DR will conduct the auction of all Trust wealth and distribute the money as per the weights of the Trustees, after paying all taxes, loans, pending salaries of employees etc in that order.
.
(12.2) The amount received by Trustees will be taxed at 1.25 times the highest income tax rate on other normal income of individual (not corporate) . DR will first subtract the auctions fees as decided by NR. Two thirds of the tax received will be equally distributed amongst all Hindu \ Sanatan (including Sikhs, Jains, Buddhists).
.
(12.3) comment : example - so if highest income tax rate is 30%, then tax on the amount received by Trustees will be 1.25 * 30% = 37.5%.
.
(12.4) If 75% of Trustees announce that they want to sell whole or part of the Trusts’ assets, then DR will conduct auctions on those assets and each Trustee will get as per his weights. The tax applicable will be same as above clauses.
.
(13) Transformation to Democratic Structure
.
(13.1) if over 75% of Trustees by weighted votes, agree and cite at DR’s office, that they want “democratic structure” over their Trust, then 90 days after they have applied in person, DR will announce that Hindu / Sanatan Sampradaya Trust as Trust with Democratic Structure. During these 90 days, the Trustees may withdraw the application.
.
(13.2) At the time of application, the Trustees must provide list of all Voting Members and Non Voting Members. The Voting Members must be voters of India or PIO or OCI. The Non Voting Members can be minors and citizens of any country. The list must be approved by over 75% of Trustees. The minors will automatically become Voting Member when they becomes major.
.
(13.3) Once a Trust adopts democratic structure, its voting members will elect the Chairman using open voting via DR’s website. And Chairman will be recallable. The Trust will have 4 to 20 Committee Members depending on the assets of Trust. And 65% Committee Members will be elected by voting members and 35% committee members will be elected as well as non-voting members. The Committee Members elected by Non-Voting Members will not have voting rights inside committee. All committee members will be recallable.
.
(13.4) NR will print the procedure to elect and recall Chairman and Committee Members for the trust via Trusts’ voting members. If citizens of India don’t like that procedure, citizens can replace NR using RTR NR procedure.
.
(13.5) Once a Trust takes Democratic Structure, the officers employed by the Trust can will be first tried by Jurors and then their guilt or innocence and punishment will be decided by the Jurors chosen from the Voting Members. The maximum punishment can be 2 months of salary and expulsion, but no imprisonment, If Trust’s Chairman or Voting Members want more punishment, then they will need to file case against the officer in civil or criminal or other applicable court.
.
(14) Membership in Hindu \ Sanatan Trusts with Democratic Structure
,
(14.1) NR, with help of SR and DR, will maintain all lists of Voting Members and Non-voting Members in all Hindu \ Sanatan Religious Trusts which have opted for Democratic Structure under this act,
.
(14.2) Initial list will be given by Trustees as per clause-13.2, or will be made when the Trust is formed.
,
(14.3) A Voting or Non Voting Member can leave membership anyday. DR will put a public notice and after 6 months, DR will remove his membership. During 6 months, he may cancel his decision to retain Membership
.
(14.4) A Trust can remove Voting Member or Non voting Member only after an internal Jury Trial which will be conducted by DR. DR will select 15 to 1500 Voting Members of the Trust between the age of 25 years and 55 years and ask if the Voting Member should be expelled. The Jurors will hear complainer and the Member. The size of Jury will depend on assets owned by the member and will be minimum 15 and 1 Juror more for every Rs 1 crore.
.
(14.5) If and only if over 75% Jurors approve expulsion, then DR will put his name for expulsion on website. The decision of District Jury can cancelled by State Jury of Trust and decision of State Jury of the Trust can be cancelled by National Jury of the Trust. The decision of National Jury can be cancelled only by Jury of formed by Hindu \ Sanatan voters across State or Hindu \ Sanatan voters across India.
.
(14.6) NR will print the detailed code to run the Jury Trials. The Hindu \ Sanatan voters may replace NR if the code he has printed is weak, using RTR-NR clauses.
.
(14.7) If a person wants to join another Hindu \ Sanatan Religious Trust, with Democratic Structure, registered under this act, then he can submit his application at DR. DR will post his wish on the website. The Trust he wishes to join
.
(14.8) DR will choose 15 to 1500 Voting Members across India, of the Trust at random. The number of Jurors selected will depend on Wealth of the member who is applying. The Jurors needed not physically come to DR office. Each Voting Member will register YES / NO using SMS or AAP or by personally visiting DR office at any District or personally visiting Talati office, within 30 days after application is posted. If below 75% Voting Members register YES, then DR will cancel the application. And if over 75% of Voting Members register YES, then DR will wait for 60 days. During these 60 days, if over 51% of all Voting Members register NO, then DR will cancel application or else, DR will list him as Non-Voting Member in Trust. And DR will repeat same process after 2 years to decide whether he should be made Voting Member or not.
.
(14.9) Son / Daughter of a male or female Voting Member will automatically become a Non-Voting Member at birth, and will become Voting Member when he reaches 18 years of age, unless he decides not to be. If a male Voting Member marries a female who is not a Voting Member, then she will become Voting Member after 2 years. But if a female who is a Voting Member marries a male who is not a Voting Member, then female will loose Membership after 2 years and male will need not automatically get Voting Membership. Both may later apply for Membership.
.
(14.10) A member may lose membership if he converts to any other religion. The Jury selected from the Trust will have final decision
.
(14.11) Membership cannot be sold or transferred by contract or by inheritance
.
(15) Division of a Trust
.
(15.1) If over 25% Trustees or 25% of Voting Members want a division, then any Trustee / Member can submit an Asset Division Proposal. DR will put all Asset Division Proposals on net for a fee decided by NR
.
(15.2) DR (or SR or NR) will summon of Jury of 15 to 1500 Hindu \ Sanatan voters across District or State or India, between the age of 30 years to 55 years. If Trust has over 75% of assets in District, then DR will select all Jurors from District. If no such District is there, but over 75% of assets are in State, then SR will select Jurors from State. And if assets are across India, then NR will choose Jurors from Districts across India. The numbers of Jurors chosen from District will depend on % of assets the Trust has in a given district.
.
(15.3) Each Juror will give (-100 to 100) Points to each Asset Division Proposal. If a proposal has positive Points from over 33% of Jurors, then all its negative Points will be ignored. The proposal with highest Points will be accepted as judgment for Asset Division.
.
(15.4) DR / SR / NR will divide the assets as per the proposal approved by Jurors
.
(15.5) DR / SR / NR will collect expenses from both parties in ratio of assets they got
.
(16) Merger of two Trusts
.
(16.1) For merger, both Trust must have democratic structure or both must have non democratic structure
.
(16.2) If over 75% of Trustees or 75% of Voting Members of two Trusts agree for a Merger Proposal submitted by any Trustee or any Member, then and then only DR or SR or NR will execute their Merger. (RTR) Right to Recall National Registrar
.
(RTR.1) If any Hindu \ Sanatan voter of India wishes to become NR, then he can appear in person before DC (DC = District Collector of District where he is voter). DC would accept his candidacy for NR. DC will take filing fee same as deposit amount for MP election. And DC will issue a serial number unique across India.
.
(RTR 2) Any Hindu \ Sanatan voter can visit Talati office in person. pay Rs 3 fee, and approve at most five persons for the NR position of NR. The Talati would enter his approvals in the computer and would give him a receipt with his voter-id#, date/time and the persons he approved. The Talati will put the preferences of the citizen on PM's website with citizen's voter-ID number and his preferences
.
(RTR.3) If a the voter comes to cancel his Approvals, the Talati will cancel one of more of his approvals without any fee
.
(RTR.4) If a candidate gets approval of over 35 crore Hindu \ Sanatan voters in India, and 1 crore more approvals then existing NR, then PM may or need not expel the existing NR and may or need not appoint the person with highest approval count as NR. The decision of PM will be final
.
(RTR.5) If existing NR has approval of less than 35 crore Hindu \ Sanatan voters, then PM may replace him.
.
(RTR.6) PM may create a system whereby citizen can file\cancel approvals using SMS or mobile phone apps or via ATMs.
.
(RTR.7) comment : Later, PM may create a system where voter can give score of (-100 to 100) to any candidate. If a voter doesn’t give any score, it will be assumed as zero. The voter will have choice --- he can give score of simply approve / not-approve, and in that case not-approving will mean score of zero and approving will mean score of 100. If a candidate has positive score from over 33% o ALL voters, then negative scores will be ignored. The candidate with highest score, and who has over 51% of highest possible score will be appointed as NR
.
(CV) Citizens’ Voice:
.
(CV.1) If any citizen residing anywhere in India wants any change in this law or has any complaint under this law, then he may submit an affidavit to DC’s designated clerk and clerk will scan the affidavit on put on PM’s website along with photo and details of the citizen. The clerk will charge Rs 30 per page. The clerk will issue serial number for the affidavit.
.
(CV.2) If any citizen residing in the area covered by the Patwari wants to register his YES / NO on the affidavit submitted in above clause, then he can go to Talati’s office and provide the serial number of the affidavit and his YES/NO on a form, and Patwari will enter his YES/NO for a fee for Rs 3 . The YES/NO shall come on PM’s website along with citizen’s voter-id, name, date etc The YES / NO count will not be binding on PM or any officer etc.
.
