Sat Sep 30 2017 15:11:34 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

Since ages and ages, I and my fellow recallists have said that --- we should scrap taxes such as GST , excise, VAT , service tax, CST, entry taxes etc and instead have wealth tax on land and streamlines income tax.
.
And all congress / rss / aap workers including their leaders opposed it, and instead supported GST , and opposed wealth tax.
.
Well, now thanks to congress / rss / aap workers and their apex leaders like SoMoKe, India is now in deep mess and mess will worsen day by day
.
The only "improvements" now we will see is more and more FDI in India, which will enable foreigners to take over bigger and bigger share of indian economy. And as weeks pass, the contribution of small / medium companies in production as well as trade will decrease and decrease and the share of large companies (mostly FDI companies) and IMPORTS will further and further increase. The TOTAL will decrease and then will slowly increase, but ratio of India / USA as well as ratio of India / China will worsen and worsen.
.
Thats the current trendline.
.
Can it be reseversed? IMO yes. HOW? By abolishing useless laws like GST and instead levying wealth tax, and streamlining income by abolishing Mauritius / Fiji / Singapore etc tax treaties and so on and removing plethora of exemptions such as SEZ etc. Next, we need to publicize ALL land ownership so that we can see how much land charity people have hoarded in the name of charities and so on.
.
And several such law-drafts - see my post "Fixing economy within NINETY days" at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10154778548161922 for more.
.
So what can activists do?
.
IMO, activists should publicize the above law-drafts and activists should work to convince voters across India to support these law-drafts. The activists should send tweet @PmoIndia to print these laws, and must share this tweet on ghis FB profile along with EXPLANATION. Next, activists should actively inform voters across India, the FACT, that congress / rss / aap workers have opposed these laws such as wealth tax law etc and that their leaders such as SoMoKe have also opposed these laws. And activists should assist those election candidates, who have worked to promote these law-drafts.
.
These are someone of the steps --- the activists scan surely take more steps
.
But otherwise, the existing trendline is clear --- India / USA and India / China strenngth ratios will only worsen and worsen because of the GST like law drafts that SoMoKe and congress / rss / app workers promote and wealth tax like law-drafts they oppose.
