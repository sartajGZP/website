As per proposed law, crimes such as murder, attempt to murder, death due to negligence, rape, abduction, harassment , stalking etc will be tried by Jurors and not judges,. For such cases, 15 voters to 1500 voters between age of 25 years and 55 years will be chosen from voter list of District / State or whole India. These voters aka Jurors will listen to both sides, their lawyers, technical experts and decide the punishment. The punishment taken will be "median" of punishments decided by Jurors/
.
This proposed law creates Jury System ONLY in lower courts,. A separate proposed law will create Jury System in State High Courts and also Supreme Court
