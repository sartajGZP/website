How can you use FB productively (for political campaigning)
.
(1) click on EACH friend's profile. If you dont personally know him, and if his posts are not relevent to your goals, then UNFOLLOW him. This will clean your newsfeed.
.
(2) If his posts are VERY relevant, then pls click "see first" in "follow" option.
.
(3) do activism on PC. The mobile phone FB isnt good for activism etc
..
(4) make 3-more lists of friends - daily , weekly, montyhly etc. Put friends in these lists. And visit the profile of "daily" people once a day, visit profile of "weekly" people once a week and so on. And unfollow weekly / monthly people, but sure to visit their profile once a week / month. That way, you will know their political views etc. But their posts wont appear in your newsfeed everyday
.
(5) Pls request people you personally dont know to put you in "Restricted" list so that you see ONLY their PUBLIC posts. That will also reduce loan on your newsfeed.
