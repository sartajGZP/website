Central Government to Lease National Highways
.
The Central Government has planned to lease its nearly 100 operational National Highways to monetize the state-owned National Highways, work will start next month. This could earn around Rs. 75,000 crores. The government is starting lease of its projects under the investment plan of operational funding, to raise fund for the new infrastructure projects through monetization.Central Government to Lease National Highways
.
These highways will be auctioned by the Road Transport and Highways Ministry of Toll Operate and Transfer (TOT) model. .
.
In the first phase of the pilot project, the government will call the bid for 11 highways. By leasing them, the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways is expected to get an amount of Rs. 6500 crores.Central Government to Lease National HighwaysOut of these 11 highways, 4 are in Gujarat and 7 are in Andhra Pradesh and their total length is a little less than 700 km. In T.O.T model by making a one-time payment by pension funds will give the opportunity to bring government national highways at lease for 30 years. During the concession period, the fund and its operations and maintenance partners will be allowed to charge and share the toll on the leased highway.Central Government to Lease National Highways
.
These companies are showing interest in investment: According to sources, Temasek Holdings in Borders and GIC of Singapore and many other International Funds, such as JP Morgan Asset Management and the Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA) can invest. The bid will start in mid-September and this process will be completed in 2 months.
