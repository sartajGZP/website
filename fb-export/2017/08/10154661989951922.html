The first entity that forced people to use prefix "Honorable" was East India Company --- so word "Honorable" is quite "appropriate" for SCjs and HCjs
.
The kings of India, be Hindu or Muslims, never forced people to use word "Honorable" !! The people were required to bow down and obey, but Kings never said "you must honor us" !! Because "honor" comes from heart and not sword. It is person's right to say whether he respects the ruler or not, If he doesnt respect, he may not say "I dont respect you". But he doesnt respect, he will not be forced to say "I do respect you" !!
.
But East India Company went one step ahead, The Company's apex officers said to all Indian subjects --- you must all must say that we deserve and you must all say that you consider us honorable !!!
.
So in THIS context and THIS context ALONE, it may be appropriate to say that ALL Supreme and High judges are indeed "Honorable" !!
.
