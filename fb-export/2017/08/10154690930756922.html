.
electricity generation numbers ( million units , Giga Watt Hours, 1 unit is consumed when a fan runs some 15 hours)
.

july-2014 --- 89499 (about 11% higher than prev year)
july-2015 --- 92880 (about 4% higher than prev year)
july-2016 --- 94590 (about 3% higher than prev year)
