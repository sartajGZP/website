.
Say economy as on jun-2014 was 100

Say growth rate was 4% each year, but reported growth rate was 6%. Even rss;s own MP subramanian swamy has been complaining that growth rate is fake and over reported
.

.

.

.

.
So lockdown will enable to escape from several years of data fudging.
.
