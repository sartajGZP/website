How serious is NOVEL corona virus and how effective lockdown will be ? Imo, NO one knows
.
There is a chance that this novel corona virus can be more lethal than spanish flue which killed 2 crore to 3 crores in USA/Europe alone.
.
But consider this ---- singer Kunika Kappor was tested positive, but no one she came in contact with has been tested positive !!! And almost all who passed away in Italy was very old and also had prior heart / lung problem.
.
So there is also a chance that novel corona virus may be just only as strong as dengue etc
.
And this a case, where best informed person also do not know how potent the virus on general working population
.
And will lockdown vastly reduce the spread? Again, no one knows
.
So imo, we should NOT blame anyone here. We are all grossly uniformed on this new virus --- be scientists or doctors or PM or laymen. Of course, mediamen know all ...; but that is aside.
.
So on THIS issue, given that NONE have any good information, there is no point in blaming anyone
.
There are general issues --- like not enough hospitals, not enough bed, not enough equipment --- but those are general medical issues. Not specuific to this crisis
