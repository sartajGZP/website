For people that need to go out for food or medicines , the government need to publish a schedule based in last name in aadhar card.
.
For example, all last names starting with A: Monday 10-12; B - 12-2pm, C 2-4pm, and so on... This is better at controlling infection than people going out at random when they need to. And then can go only to stores close within 2 km of home.
.
Or a person with mobile can send sms to central number and get a 2 hour per week permit where he can go only to shops he has mentioned and travel only on roads mentioned.
.
In cities , those who don't have mobile can pair up with one person who does.
.
Some system to let 1.5% people out for 1 hr a week in shops 1 km around his home will be needed so that he can buy food and Medicines.
