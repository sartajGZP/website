Effect of reducing interest rate -- how value of savings reduces
.
RBI has reduced interest rates by 0.75% .
.
This will increase loans and so increase rupee3 supply aka money supply (M3)
.
And when volume of Rupee3 aka M3 increases, without same increase in material production, the value of rupee may fall. And so value of those who have cash or bank deposits will decrease. The biggest loss will be faced by senior citizens who have deposits, and those who saved
.
And same way,material burden of those who have taken loans will decrease
.
What was needed most was direct cash doles to daily wage workers, who depend on daily incomes
.
But instead of that, Modiji decided to give benefits to loan takers (and soga / arke has supported it) , most of whom are big loan takers. and all this in the name of fight against corona !!!
