How meat eating will increases chances of corona infection. And pls tweet @PmoIndia to ban meat till Corona crisis ends.
.
Because virus decomposes in 12 hours if it falls on any non living surface or vegetable, but will live for several hours if it falls on meat
.
Say a person is infected with corona and he sneezes. Then droplets containing virus will be all over room. Say the droplet falls on a non living surface. Then virus will decompose in about 12 hours. And if virus falls on a vegetable or grain, then also it will decompose in same number of hours because this virus cannot grow inside cells of vegetables or grains or pulses.
.
But if that virus falls on animal cells (like meat), then it may be able to infect those cells and multiply.
.
This virus has infected many animals such as bat, chicken, rats, cattle and also dogs.
.
But the virus is simply unable to grow or even survive in any vegetable cells. And so if it comes in contact with vegetable, it will decompose within a few hours
.
Now say a cow or buffalo or chicken was infected with corona virus. Then that animal will infect many animals. And also, when the animal dies,. many cells in that meat are "alive" enough for hundreds of hours and so virus inside them will grow.
.
Now what if a person is eating meat and gets corona from meat, and he is near a vegetarian. Then that vegetarian can indeed get corona from any human - be vegetarian or meat eater. The virus infects both with high probabiluit
.
So for now, govt should ban all meat, fish and also eggs for at least a few months
.
