Why bank run on SBI will NOT create ANY loss to SBI depositor or SBI shut down
.
Many people, who still live in year 1900 say that if every SBI depositor runs to SBI and asks for currency notes for his deposits, then SBI will also fail just like YES bank. Because SBI has bank notes only equal to 5% to 10% of deposits it has.
,.
IOW, they want to blame YES bank failure on "BANK RUN phenomenon" and not on the fund siphoning done by bank owners with help of SoMoKe / CoRaAp .
.
Well, imo, these people are LIARS.
.
As today's law-drafts, PM via RBI has power to print as many currency notes it wants, and give to SBI or YES bank or any bank as loan. The gold base ended in year 1942 and was completely removed in year 1951
.
So in case EVERY SBI depositor and every bank depositor goes to bank, then PM via RBI can print as many paper cash notes as needed and give to SBI any or all banks as loans.
.
So it may cause a delay of about 2 months to 3 months --- time needed to print that many Rs 500 notes or Rs 2000 notes or Rs 100 notes. But this will NOT cause any bank shut down or any loss to SBI depositor
.
And will this currency printing cause inflation?
.
NOT NECESSARILY
.
Because Inflation depends on R3 = Rupee Supply Three (called as M3 in RBI document). R3 is sum of currency notes and all bank deposits. If people want cash for their deposits, then cash note volume increases, but deposit amount DECREASES by same amount.
.
So there is no net increase in R3 or M3 when a bank depositor takes cashnotes for his deposits.
.
So there will NOT be any new inflation
.
Now what when these cashnotes are deposited in bank again? Wont this create inflation?
.
NOT NECESSARILY
.
Because the so called CRR (cash reservse ratio) of 5% can be changed by PM via RBI any day to 100% or 1%. So in case, cash notes amount increases inside, then CRR can be increased
.
So bank runs are now non issue for PSU banks
.
Bank runs may be issue for PRIVATE banks, because PM giving unlimited cash to private banks will get exposed easily,
.
So problem of BANK RUN doesnt apply on PSU banks.
