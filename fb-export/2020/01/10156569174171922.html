Standard method to take financial contribution
.
Small chai paani type contributions ( below Rs 1000) can be taken by cash. For large contributions, following method is must
.
(1) new current or savings bank account must be opened in PSU bank. Private bank is now allowed. And bank account must be opened after you 01-apr-2019
.
(2) contribution can be taken only into that account directly and not indirectly. I.e. giver must be told about bank account number, bank name , and account owner name ..
.
(3) recallists must put all transactions in that account on fb.
.
The giver must be told that his identity will become public.
