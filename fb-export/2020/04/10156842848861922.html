why referendum / jury system is more and more important
.
In corona, govt has printed laws that a person can be forcibly admitted into hospital against his wish, and he can be given medicine and treatment and doctor against his wish. Any good reason? I dont know OFFICIAL reason given by govt,. But only reason I can think of here is --- if one isnt treated, then chances that one will infect others will increase !!!
.
The other possible reason, that without treatment , one may die doesnt apply.Because Govt never forced cancer patient to take medicine.
.
Forcing an infected person to stay home is fine. But now not just that person is stopped from moving out of his home, as per existing laws printed, he can be forced by policemen to get admitted in hospital and get treatment against his will and wish
.
And thats why, imo referendum / Jury system becomes more and more necessary
.
Imo, govt offices should NOT be allowed to use force against non criminals, in the name of helping society or helping themy, without a referendum / jury procedure.
.
An ethical question still remains whether after referendum/jury,. an individual can be forced or not to take treatment against his will. But in absence of referndum/jury,. allowing Ministers/ias/ips/judges to treat patient against will open invitation to political suppression of all types.
.
My request to all activists is ti publicize referendum/jury law-drafts (follow my wall , I will soon post latest drafts. For old drafts, and concept of referendum / Jury, pls sdown PDF in comment )
.
