How REACHABLE are your important FB posts on important topics?
.
Consider your imp posts on imp topics.
.
(a) Is there a good possibility that a NEWCOMER who comes to your wall will see that post?
.
(b) How easy is it for you to search that post when you need
.
(c) Say a person who knows you comes to wall again, How easy is it for him to search that post?
.
===============
.
Imo, organizing posts in following way increases reach
----------------------------------------------------------------
.
(1) Making one-more albums per TOPIC : pls make ONE OR TWO or more albums per TOPIC that is important to you. (why more than one?)
.
So if there 20 topics important to you, there will be 20 to 40 or more albums, one to more per topic.
.
In second and third album, put all posts on that topic.
.
(2) Putting imp posts on one topic in one album
.
And in first album, you put only 50 to 100 important posts on that topic. So when that album has too many posts, you can copy-paste that post text to another album and delete, And downside is --- you loose all comments. But they will be old comments, which may not be now very relevent,
.
(2) Now pls put link to all imp albums in one post -- lets call this post as "List of all albums" post. Optionally, you can also make one post with links to ALL your albums.
.
(3) put link to that "list of all album" post in cover picture and profile picture description,
.
(4) And also make sure that a post which contains link to the "List of all albums" post is in top 10 posts in the wall. You can do that copying that post again, and may delete old post
.
============
.
Now I will answer questions (a) , (b) and (c) I had asked
.
(a) When a newcomer comes, chances that he will see the link "List of all albums" are high because this post is always in top 10 in timeline. And when he sees the list of TOPICS increases. And inside album with imp posts, there are only 50 to 100 posts. So chances that he will see THAT imp post increases
.
(b) When you yourself want to search the post --- you can go to the album and see the post
.
(c) When a person who is familiar with your profile, will come to profile, click profile picture oir cover picture, and go to post which has links to all albums,. And from there, he can see 20-30 posts on THAT TOPIC, and see the posts he wanted.
.
=======
.
So chances that a person will see your IMPORTANT posts on a TOPIC increases when important posts on a topic are in ONE album, and links to all albums are in one post, and link to THAT post is in cover picture or top 10 posts in time
.
This how TEXTBOOKS are organized. Each textbook has list chapters in front and has page number for that chapter, But FB doesnt allow you to make list of chapters and list of posts !!! (why not?) , So you have to make it yourself. .....
