(a) committed to cause or just using cause for fame/business/cause?
.
A junior activist will come across many many political and "apolitical", who simply use cause to get fame, and use fame for votes/money or sometimes fame itself. And many, who are truly committed to their cause, but dont want to spend a minute to help poor and sometime they hate poor and hate idea of helping poor.
.
eg1 . I have come across many whose claimed that they want to save cows,And they never promoted any law drafts to end gohatya. And when I looked at their posts, my coclusion was that --- all he wanted was .donations for his own goshala !!! And I consider goshala and good business, and we need lakhs of good businessmen across India. After all, activists can ONLY fix laws. Once laws are fixed, good businessmen are needed to produce goods. But if one's goal is to end gohatya, then soending time with person whose goal to collect funds fo his goshala is waste of time. And in long run, a person who wants to make goshala, but doesnt want to spend time in making laws to end gohatya, will only end up serving rich, neither country nor poor nor cows, because till laws are fixed, honest cow milk business is a hige loss maker only. So if a true activists links with such poerson , it will be total waste of time
.
eg2. One can find dime a dozen "education" activists who want to improve education, And you may want to explain the Right to Recall District Eductauion Law Draft. They will not spend even 1 second tweeting to CM that they want this law !! And when you analyse their activituies, you will see that they are busy building a giant education NGO !! Imo, such persons will only again end up serving rich, and neither poor nor education or nor nation. Because education if poor because of poor laws
.
.eg3. Same for organic faming, helping poor, helping dalits, helping women etc. There is long list of causes, and in each cause, you will meet 100s of poeple who show zero interest in fixing laws that will serve their stated cause, but want to own a giant NGO which serves that cause.
.
IMO, a junior activist should stay away from them
