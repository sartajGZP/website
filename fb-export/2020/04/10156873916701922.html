When you post something inside your FB album, a viewer who doesnt like that specific post has two choices --- he can unfollow that album alone or unfollow your profile.
.
But when you post something on wall outside all albums, then the viewer who does not like that specific post now has only one choice --- unfollow your profile !!!
.
So imo putting all posts in albums imo decreases chances that a person will unfollow your profile.
.
So imo, making an album "(U) Unclassified posts" , where you can put all your out of album posts, and may later copy-paste them into an album --- decreases the chances that one will unfollow you.
