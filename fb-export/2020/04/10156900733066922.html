One way to estimate corona deaths --- we need deathcount between 01-apr-2019 and 23-apr-2019 i.e. PREVIOUS YEAR and compare with deathcount between same days THIS year
.
Hospital data is nonsense. Because one can say both ways --- that hospitals counted deaths with corona as corona deaths. And many can say that many corona patients died outside hospital
.
So one way to get corona deathcount is to get EXCESS deaths in this mar-april. What is excess deaths? It is deaths this feb-mar-apr minus deaths in feb-mar-apr of past 5 years
.
And this analysis must be done AGEWISE. Because Italy has some 5% of its population above 80 years, while India has below 1%. So only after obtaining excess deaths agewise, in Italy , Spain, UK, USA etc, one can estimate deaths in no lockdown condition in India.
.
All in all, one MUST get AGEWISE deathcounts data of PREVIOUS months in years and compare with THIS years
.
Depending on hospital data is waste of time
.
Estimating deaths and health loss due to lockdown is very subjective and complicated.
