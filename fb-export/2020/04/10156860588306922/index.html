.
Does a 45 day lockdown reduce END TOTAL corona deaths? And how many deaths lockdown may cause?
.
(1) an analogy
(2) summary
(3) conditions
(4) my analysis - not much difference, and lockdown also kills
(5) my conclusion --- all voters must send sms/tweet to PM to Continue of End Lockdown, and my tweet is @PmoIndia #EndLockDown
.
=================
.
(1) Analogy
-----------
.
analogy --- every month, 12000 people pass away in traffic fatalities in India. So say we stop traffic in India for 60 days and resume only after 60 days. So we certainly did "save" those 24000 lives !! But after 60 days, traffic death rate may be same.
.
Now only difference between traffic analogy and actual corona lockdown is --- in 45 day lockdown, hospital beds were added
.
=================
.
(2) Summary
-------------
.
lets label immunity strength as level Level-A i.e. person with immunity above Level-A will almost survive corona-19 virus exposure and vice versa. Now when 60 day lockdown ends, the virus will start spreading in all 140 crore citizens. Immunity level of population as a whole does not increase within those 60 days.
.
So imo, there will be no net effect on end fatalaties, except shift of 60 days.
.
( In fact, 5 crore to 7 crore people had to walk 500 km to 1500 km on feet in rain, heat and cold. And there is general economic collapse due to lockdown. So immunity level will DECREASE due to lockdowns. But I will take that aside. )
.
=================
.
(3) Background
----------------
.
And pls accept LABELS like herd immunity after analysing those labels. Because a common argument thrown is --- herd immunity reduces total deaths, and during lockdown, heard immunity will come, and so after lockdown deaths will be fewer. Well, pls analyse herd immunity. Heard immunity comes with vaccine or only after a huge % of population comes in contact with virus. If no vaccine comes, then a certain small % will pass away before herd immunity comes. Lockdown only reduced deaths per day, and increases number of days, and so doenst reduce total. And lockdown also delays herd immunity.
.
In comparing analyising lockdown vs 45 day lockdown, I am taking following approach
.
(3.1) 30 day or even 90 day is too less a time to assume that vaccine will come --- HIV vaccine didnt come even after 30 years, and rabies vaccine took years and years. So we cant expect miracle in 30 days. I call it "NO VACCINE in 60 DAYS" condition.
.
(3.2) Virus may mutate to less strong forms, but may also become stronger. And 30 day to 60 day is also a small time for mutation. So this is "NO VIRUS MUTATION IN 60 DAYS CONDITION"
.
================
.
(4) MY ANALYSIS
------------------
.
(4.1) Immunity level of person is important in understanding fight against corona. But sadly, there is NO test to measure immunity level and give an exact score. But it is known that different individuals have different immunity strength.
.
(eg some 200 years ago, we had no way to measure amount of water that flowed in river. That doesnt mean that one cant say that some river is bigger and another is smallet. Many have objected, that since immunity level can be measured as per existing science, we should NOT talk about levels. I oppose them)
.
There are 140 crore people in India, with different immunity levels.
.
If a person has immunity level of zero, then even simplest cold will be now fatal. eg every year, lakhs of people across in USA / Europe pass away due to flu that seldom kills youth. These fatalities happen, because now immunity is too low to fight even the flu virus.
.
Now say if a person has immunity of Level-A or more, then he will survive corona-19 attack. I am taking Level-A as that threshold level of immunity.
.
And other germs may cause him fatality at such Level-A or higher or lower levels.
.
Sp pls note last statement --- other germs stronger than corona-19 may cause him fatality as well,.
.
Now say on 20-march-2020, there were say 1000 corona-19 cases.
.
Say NO lockdown was done. And say 30 days later, i,e, on 21-april-2020, there will be still some cases --- say 8000 cases, we assuming doubling every 10 days.
..
Now say second scenario where lock down was imposed on 20-mar-2020 for about 62 days and is lifted on 22-may-2020. Say there are 500 cases
.
Now in next 40 days, there will be again 8000 cases !!!
.
IOW, we only got about 60 days to 70 days of delay. This is assuming that number of cases decreased during Lockdown, which will NOT be the case, The number of cases will increase at slower rate. That is doubling days in no lockdown case are 10 days, then during lockdown, it will be 30 days,
.
So imo,.except for N+10 day shift, N day lockdown gets nothing., And that too assuming that lockdown will result into DECREASE in number of cases, which wont be case,
.
==================
.
(5) my conclusion
.
Now one has to do his own analysis to decide number lives Lockdown will take away, and number of days it subtracts from each human's life. Surely, lockdown is adding lifedays in some cases. But it may be also subtracting. eg Lockdown will vastly reduce immunity level of many due to shortage of fruits in food, increased tension and so on. And So lockdown may increase number of corona deaths.
.
We dont know how many lives corona may take and hoe many life-days it will subtract. And we dont know how many lives lockdown will save from that, and how many other lives it will take
.
So at the end of the day, my request to all 90 crore voters is --- pls decide whether lockdown should continue or end, and pls tweet "@PmoIndia # ContinueLockDown " (without space between # and word ContinueLockDown or tweet "@PmoIndia #EndLockDown " depending on your decision. And you must consult a doctor you know before you tweet. And pls ask all voters you know to do the same.
