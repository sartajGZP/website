1500 coconuts, artificially ripened using sulphur, seized
in Punalur
.
21-nov-2019
.
Using sulphur to ripen coconut in Punalur market
.
Punalur (Kollam): The food safety officers found out, a place in Punalur market here, where coconut is artificially ripened using sulphur. The centre has been functioning here for over six months.
.
More than 1500 coconuts found from here were destroyed. .
.
Tender coconuts are piled up with sulphur in order to change their colour to look like ripe ones. The unsuspecting buyers will pick them without knowing the trick.
.
The artificially ripened coconut is harmful for health and causes serious hazards, the food safety officials said. They added that a report will be submitted to the assistant commissioner in this regard and fine will be charged from the guilty.
.
