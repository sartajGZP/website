post# 12 --- Proof that over 70 crore voters had opposed lockdown on 29-mar-2020 --- see zero lockdown in Bihar due to elections due on oct-2020
.
There are about 90 crore voters in India (in population of 136 crore). And imo, some 70 crore voters were opposed to lockdown on 29-mar-2020 . some one week after lockdown started
.
Here are some proofs
.
Now Bihar has State Assembly Elections in oct-2020.
.
Now consider Bihar since 20-may-2020.
.
There has been zero defacto lockdown since 20-may-2020. On paper, there may be strict lockdown. But in reality, there is none.
.
Now when elections come, Govts always works to enforce things that people rightly or wrongly support and never enforces things that people oppoose
.
So if lockdown is widely popular in large section of society, then why is Bihar State Govt not enforcing lockdown?
.
The fact that Bihar State govt is showing zero interest in enforcing any lockdown proves ---a large majority of voters are against lockdown or even restrictions.
.
Now say whole India was to go for loksabha elections on oct-2020.;
.
Then how much would have been level of restrictions across India?
.
Extrapolation from Bihar says --- almost zero lockdown
.
IOW, experience of Bihar since 20-may-2020 shows that a large majority of India is against lockdown etc
