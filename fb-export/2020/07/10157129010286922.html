Why we face sometimes shortage of beds and sometimes have to turn away patients?
.
A person has been tested corona positive. He isnt some criminal etc, He has just been tested corona positive. And policemen and hospital staff are dragging him to hospital, due to laws passed by NaMo, Soga, ArKe etc and workers of congress / rss / aap
.
This is why we have shortage of beds. First, we drag completely healthy asymptomatioc patients to hospitals or quarantine centers etc. Thats takes up space. And then there is no space for patients with symptioms and they are turned away
.
And instead of wasting manpower , money and energy in dragging them to hospitals, if energy is applied in adding beds in hospitals, then there will be more beds, and no need to turn down patients with symptoms
.
