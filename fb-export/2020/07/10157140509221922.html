"solution" to permanent problem of unclear things in proposed law-drafts--- what if an RRP draft with perhaps unclear statement is printed in Gazzette?
.
We have low budgets and only 2-4 activists are working to write proposed drafts. And so drafts will have many problems such unclear statements.
.
now once a draft is give, A may say "its clear and meaning is X". And B may say "draft is clear and meaning is Y", And C may say "draft is unclear"
.
So what if draft which has some unclear things is passed? Or, things which are clear to some and unclear to others and clear but still have different meanings
.
eg say draft says "citizen can register YES NO at Patwari and Patwari shall put YES NO on District website"
.
But draft writer may forget or deliberately not put word "public" before website.
.
So now will YES NO be visible to all?
.
One cay say "other clauses make genera meaning clear that YES / NO should be visible to all". One may say "draft is clear that YES NO need not be visivble to all". And third person may say "draft is unclear".
.
There will be many many such unclear or potentially unclear statements in all drafts
.
-------------------------
.
So what may happen if such drafts get printed in Gazzette?
.
------------------------------
.
(1) In all such cases, first PM / CM will decide on unclear things. eg PM / CM may make approvals public or may not/
.
(2) Next, any voter can ask PM / CM to act in certain way on unclear things
.
(3) The Jurors can instruct PM / CM to keep approvals open or confidential. And make them open instantkly or within a few days
.
(4) PM / CM may obey Jurors or may not
.
(6) Via TCP or Citizens' Voice clauses, majority citizens may ask PM / CM to act in certain way
.
(7) PM / CM may obey or disobey TCP counts
.
(8) Finally, voters may or may not replace PM / CM for decisions he took or refused to take
.
IOW, there is CHAIN OF ACTIONS by PM , officers, Jurors, TCP, Recall to give clarity to drafts when clarity is missing,.
.
---------------------
.
In general, one purpose of Jury / Recall is to reduce lengths of drafts, All drafts have a general meanings. And 100s of words have to be added only because officers deliberately act against general meaning. And one solution is to add more and more words in drafts. And second solution I propose is that --- citizens should use fines by Jury or recall against officers who act against general meaning.
.
So once recall procedures comes, the chances that officers / PM / CM etc will act against general meaning (i.e. meaning decided by majority) will decrease,
.
So imo, Jury / TCP will clarify the drafts when needed. And recall will make sure that CM / PM go as per these decisions.
.
So all our drafts will have problems of some missing clarity at some point,. Because we have only 2 to 4 people writing drafts and each is unpaid and has 50 things to do. And solution is Jury / tcp / recall itself .
.
So, imo, activists need not give too much thoughts to hair splitting (baal ki khal nikalana). Because in all such cases, the DRAFTS' execution will be decided by PM / CM, but citizens' will have far more effect than today because of Jury / Recall / TCP.
.
So Jury / TCP / Recall is main answer to possibly unclear things in existing proposed RRP drafts
