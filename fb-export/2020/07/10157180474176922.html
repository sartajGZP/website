A general answer to objections/suggestions to proposed RRP drafts
.
Anyone can take any proposed draft, change some sentences, and show that the new draft is 0.01% better than old one. And there can be 1000 such addition, and together, they will not add to 1000 * 0.01% = 10% , but less than 0.1%
.
Its not possible for any draft write to accommodate suggestions after suggestions
.
So I welcome only following flaws / suggestions
.
(1) a flaw that shows that if draft is printed, then work wont proceed on very first day.
.
eg proposed RTR-MP-law-draft (old draft in 301 . pdf as well as latest draft) imo has a SERIOUS administrative flaw. Serious enough that collector will not be able to work on day-01 itself !! (sadly, none except myself has been able notice this flaw !!! And even I noticed after over 12 years !!) And yet, imo this flaw is conceptually non-serious enough that once this administrative flaw is shown, solution clauses can drafted within a day. But still, pointing out such administrative flaw, is welcome.
.
now one may claim that a draft has flaw that if printed in Gazette, the officer may not be able to provide some services in the draft for say 1-2 days. (because say draft doesnt say where officer will get funds from). This imo is non serious. There is a whole heirarcy of PM / CM and officers and recall over them to handle such operational issues.
.
So if anyone points to a SERIOUS flaw that makes functioning impossible on day-01, such flaw pointing is welcome.
.
(2) Many suggestions are NOT flaws, but suggestions that may improve efficiency. if adding 5 to 10 lines can increase efficiency on first day or early period SIGNIFICANTLY, then that addition is welcome.
.
Otherwise, all drafts has so called TCP-clauses, to makes changes in that draft, And all drafts also have Jury-clauses to clear disputes that come or meaning or incompleteness when draft is printed,
.
