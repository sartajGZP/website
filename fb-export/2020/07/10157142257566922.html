What may happen if an RRP proposed drafts with perhaps unclear clause gets printed in Gazzette?
.
Given a clause, A may say that "clause is clear and meaning is X", and B may say that "clause is clear and meaning is Y, different from X" and C may say that "clause isnt clear". Such disagreements about full detailed meaning of clauses are common.
.
So what may happen if such RRP draft gets printed in Gazzette with such perhaps unclear clauses?
.
(1) In all such cases, first PM / CM will decide on that clause
.
(2) Next, any voter can ask PM / CM to act in certain way on unclear things. And other voters can register their YES NO on that proposal. Almost all our RRP drafts have TCP clauses. And there is general TCP draft too,
.
(3) The Jurors can instruct PM / CM to act in certain way .
.
(4) PM / CM may obey Jurors or may not
.
(6) Via TCP or Citizens' Voice clauses, majority citizens may ask PM / CM to act in certain way. And voters using TCP can also propose an amendment to the draft.
.
(7) PM / CM may obey or disobey the TCP counts
.
(8) Finally, voters may or need not replace PM / CM for decisions he took or decisions he refused to take
.
IOW, there is CHAIN OF ACTIONS by PM / CM , Jurors, TCP, Recall to give clarity to RRP drafts in case one says that clarity is missing,
.
The effect of all RRP drafts depend on PM / CM , Jury , TCP and CM-Recall / PM-Recall in that order. This is true for all clauses.
.
So imo, activists need not worry much if some clause has small error in it.
.
