A general answer to objections/suggestions to proposed RRP drafts
.
Anyone can take any proposed draft, change some sentences, and show that the new draft is 0.01% better than old one. And there can be 1000 such addition, and together, they will not add to 1000 * 0.01% = 10% , but less than 0.1%
.
Its not possible for any draft write to accommodate suggestions after suggestions
.
So I welcome only following flaws / suggestions
.
(1) a flaw that shows that if draft is printed, then work wont proceed on very first day.
.
eg proposed RTR-MP-law-draft (old draft in 301 . pdf as well as latest draft) imo has a SERIOUS administrative flaw. Serious enough that collector will not be able to work on day-01 itself !! (sadly, none except myself has been able notice this flaw !!! And even I noticed after over 12 years !!) And yet, imo this flaw is conceptually non-serious enough that once this administrative flaw is shown, solution clauses can drafted within a day. But still, pointing out such administrative flaw, is welcome.
.
now one may claim that a draft has flaw that if printed in Gazette, the officer may not be able to provide some services in the draft for say 1-2 days. (because say draft doesnt say where officer will get funds from). This imo is non serious. There is a whole heirarcy of PM / CM and officers and recall over them to handle such operational issues.
.
So if anyone points to a SERIOUS flaw that makes functioning impossible on day-01, such flaw pointing is welcome.
.
(2) Many suggestions are NOT flaws, but suggestions that may improve efficiency. if adding 5 to 10 lines can increase efficiency on first day or early period SIGNIFICANTLY, then that addition is welcome.
.
Otherwise, all drafts has so called TCP-clauses, to makes changes in that draft, And all drafts also have Jury-clauses to clear disputes that come or meaning or incompleteness when draft is printed,
.
--------
.
Now following are 10s of RR movement work I have
.
(01) running campaign to start mass movement end lockdown
.
(02) campaign to end EVMs, make cheaper EVM equipment ; assist 10s of engineers in making EVM equipment
.
(03) writing more drafts - even after writing 25 drafts, 10s of imp issues are left, eg
.
(03.a) several laws on health are yet to be drafted (replacing subsidies to sugar / rice mills by direct deposit)
.
(03.b) preferential voting system for loksabha / assembly
.
(03.c) direct election of Rajsabha MPs and RTR of Rajsabha MPs
.
(03,d) laws related to farming and farmer protections
.
(03.e) reforms in election procedure
,
And so on.
.
So there are 10s of issues on which drafts are to be writtem
.
(04) making pamphlets on drafts and campaign steps
.
(05) printing , distributing pamphlets
.
(06) making vdo on drafts
.
(07) election works
..
(08) party administration (for past 2 days, I have been running from pillar ti posts to open party bank account and file NIL income tax returns)
.
So you may make any flaw / suggestion on your FB profile. But before messaging to me or Pawan Sharma or any draft writer --- pls make sure --- is the change you are proposing making a HUGE difference or PETTY difference,
.
If it is making small difference, then pls only post on your wall and pls dont send / tag draft writers. We will read from your newsfeed when we get time
.
------------------------
.
I request all recallists to follow above guideline while evaluating a proposed CHANGE in draft.
.
A huge amount of work is pending ----- informing lakhs of activists and crores of voters about drafts, informing about importance of election contesting to over 50000 activists, removing EVM etc,
.
So if you see any FB post on improvement in the draft then pls do pay attention ONLY IF flaw in your opinion is serious enough that officer / citizens cant function on day-01, and suggestion vastly improved efficiency of new draft over existing draft. RTR itself ensures that officer will implement all minor suggestions with asking due to fear of recall. And TCP / Jury clauses in drafts can remove minor flaws. So imo, it is better to spend TIME on other tasks I mentioned above and not answering inor flaws./ suggestions
.
