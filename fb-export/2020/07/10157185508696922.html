Why we created labels like JuryCourt , VoteVapasi
.
We had proposed JuryTrial-law-draft
.
Now anyone can propose a DIFFERENT draft and call it JuryTrial-draft. And that will confuse new coming activists.
.
And we have no moral right to accuse him of being immoral, because JuryTrial label is over 800 year old and has been used across world
.
So we have created our own labels.
.
That creates a loss. Because RightToRecall is know, while votevapasi is not known. And Jury Trial is known while JuryCourt isnt known
.
But these NEW labels are created , so that of someone uses these labels for his DIFFERENT proposal , we can at least show to all that he is acting immorally.
.
So to those who wish to act morally, I have following request --- please use NEW labels for your new proposals. Or, you may use age old labels (like Right to recall, Jury Trial, Jury System, Mineral Royalties etc etc).
.
But if you are using our NEW LABELS to name YOUR proposals, then many activists may decide that you have taken an immoral step. It will be their decision
.
