Ethical way of proposing MODIFIED drafts - and what should be done if an activist used UNETHICAL ways to promote MODIFIED drafts
.
I have copied many concepts like Jury System, RTR and so on. Some are 100% original such DhanvapasiPassbook , TCP, Wealth Tax Credit System in Wealth tax draft, and so on
.
But all drafts are original i.e. I didnt copy / modify any draft (except Right to Recall Janlokpal clauses , which were / are not part of main proposals)
.
Now many activists claim that drafts they have made by modifying my drafts are better than my drafts., and so they promote those drafts
.
Now here are some points of ethics
.
(01) draft modifier must use completely separate label
(02) his pamphlet / FB post must first mention that it is MODIFIED from other draft
(03) his pamphlet / FB post must first contain FULL original draft as is and them mention modification he made
(04) If an activist is not taking step-01 to step-03, then other activists must brand his action as UNETHICAL and shun him.
.
Presenting a modified draft without above steps is UNETHICAL. It is also illegal, but I will take law part separately.
.
------
.
DETAILS and REASONS
-----------------------------------
.
(01) use different label --- The reason I (and Pawan Kumar ) took label like JuryCourt and gave up JuryTrial and JurySystem was --- anyone can write a new JuryTrial draft and claim as his own. We didnt create the labels and we cant have any moral claims. So if anyone is proposing a new draft, he must NOT use labels we have used or even modified labels such as JuryCourt2 or NewJuryCourt or BetterJuryCourt. He is welcome to use labels like JuryTrial or JuryTrial2 etc etc. We request him to use totally separate labels to avoid confusion
.
(02) His pahpmlet and FB post must mention right in the begining that its a modified draft and give name of original draft
,
(03) if one is presenting MODIFIED draft, then the pamphlet must first have FULL ORIGINAL draft , and modification. The reader must be able to see original draft and modification SEPARATELY so that he can decide whether modification was worth making before draft was printed in Gazette, This is necessary, because each DRAFT weakens the mass movement for original draft. And this may be welcome, if modification adds a huge difference in efficiency. But reader must have FULL information to decide whether modification makes trivial difference or major difference
.
(03.01) So draft MODIFIER must present the full original draft as is, and mention that he is NOT author of original draft and then mention the changes / additions / sutractions he is putting. And may explain about need of those changes
.
(03.02) if draft modifier is HIDING the fact that he is not the author of full draft, then a reader may think that draft writer has written the whole draft, And when he sees original draft, he may think that
.
------
.
If draft modifier doesnt take above steps, then I request all activist to take him as unethical and shun him
.
And pls note --- Modifying a draft and campaigning for modifying draft and opposing original draft are NOT unethical. But hiding the fact that draft is modified and not original is unethical. And such unethical behavior will ruin activism and so imo activists should ask such draft modifier to edit the posts containing modified drafts.
