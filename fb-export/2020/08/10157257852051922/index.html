Right to recall Sarpanch draft proposed by Right to recall party will improves administration, but Right to Recall Sarpanch Haryana RSS + JJP Govt's is DANGEROUS, and only bring instability .
.
A short summary is given here.
.
Today is 28-aug-2020 , friday.
.
Now exact draft by Haryana's RSS Govt is not yet published. .
.
But some details have come in media, and they are similar to Right to recall MP / MLA draft presented by RSS MP Sri Varun Gandhiji.
.
So I will rewrite this article after full draft is published by Haryana Govt.
.
( see first comment for
.
(a) Full comparison )
.
(b) Link to RRP's Right to Recall Sarpanch draft
.
(c) Haryana RSS's Right to Recall Sarpanch draft
.
(d) RRS MP Sri Varun Gandhiji's RTR MP draft )
.
Why RRP draft is good and Haryana Govt is dangerous
-------------------------------------
.
(1) RRP draft uses POSITIVE recall procedure. Whereas Haryana Govt draft will use NEGATIVE recall procedure , which brings near zero improvement and huge instability
.
So what is positive recall procedure and what is negative recall procdure?
.
(1.1) Positive recall procedure --- a voter can specify whom he wants as new Sarpanch , and voter can also specify that he wants existing Sarpanch to continue. And only when a candidate gets more approvals than existing sarpanch, recall poll is announced.
.
(1.2) Negative recall procedure --- a voter cannot specify whom he wants to be new Sarpanch, and he also cannot specify that he wants existing sarpanch to stay. A voter may may only specify that existing sarpanch should be expelled
.
Positive recall improves administration, and causes ZERO instability
.
Negative recall only brings instability and no improvement
.
RRP's draft uses positive recall procedure. Whereas Haryana Govt's draft. to be published, but from details I saw on media, will use negative recall procedure only
.
(2) in our RRP's proposed draft, the voter has to PERSONALLY appear at Patwari's office to file his approvals and thus indirectly ask for recall poll. RRP draft has no signature gathering and needs no physical gathering of all 5000 voters at one place on one day and one time. Wherseas Haryana Govt draft used signature gathering OR Gram Sabha meeting where all 5000 voters of Gram Panchayat may have to be present. Both, signature gathering and physically grouping on one day and one time as serious problems such as (1.1) more than half the adult voters dont know how to sign (1.2) Tahsildaar has no counter copy of 1000s of voters. (1.3) And anyone can make fake signatures. (1.4) One voter may sign several times !! Whareas, in RRP's RTR sarpanch proedure, voter comes to Patwari ofice anyday anytime and register's his approvals.
.
(3) RTR RTR Sarpanch draft has feature of VOTEVAPASI PASSBOOK i.e. voter has record in his hand on whole approvals he filed and cancelled. Whereas Haryana Draft has no provision for such passbook. Now imagine a bank account where person is given no passbook or statement. This can cause 10s of problems.
.
(4) In RRP's RTR Sarpanch proposed draft, the voter's approvals become public and come on website of State Election Commission (SEC) and each voter gets feedback via SMS when he puts his approvals at Patwari office. In Haryana Govt's proposal, (details awaited), signatures are gathered on paper and may not come on website !! The voter will never get SMS if his signature is present on that recall petition paper !!! So its possible that someone make fake signature of voter-A, and voter-A will never know !! This flaw is present in Varun Gandhiji's RTR MP proposal, and may get repeated in Haryana Govt proposal.
.
(5) In RRP's RTR Sarpanch proposed draft, a poll for recall and actual recall will NOT happen if sitting Sarpanch is still most popular of all candidates. So RRP's RTR sarpanch draft has zero possibility of instability. Whereas in Haryana Govt proposal, there is very high possibility that a poll for recall will happen even if sitting Sarpanch is still most popular, and an actual recall also may happen even if sitting MP is most popular. So RSS' RRP draft can cause instability.
.
(6) In RRP's procedure, when a replacement candidate has over 10% more support than existing Sarpanch had and existing Sarpanch has, then only recall / replacement are likely to happen. And an actual recall is also very likely to happen when a candidate is more popular than existing Sarpanch. But in Haryana Govt's procedure, it is possible that recall will NOT happen even if a candidate is now far far more popular than existing Sarpanch
.
(7) The only "advantage" of Haryana Govt's draft proposed procedure is that it is "simple" !! But if dangerous procedure is simple, then a complex but useful procedure is better. So this advantage of simplicity is imo pointless.
