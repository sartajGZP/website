health card is same as addhar number added to all treatments. But instead of adhar, a new ID was created, so that patient doesnt need to disclose his aadhar to doctor
.
plus points of health card ---
.
(1) It is now impossible to run health subisidies without this card i.e. this card VASTLY reduces FALSE govt health expenditure and may also reduce false expenses that patients have to pay. How will false govt expenditure reduce.?
.
Say a Govt subsidized hospital reports that it was was giving 30 tablets per month to say 10000 patients, and collects money for 300,000 tablets from Govt, Today, hospital puts any 10000 names. And that hospital may be giving tablets to only 5000 patients or even less. But with health ID-card, hospital has to give health card numbers of patients. So say a person didnt get those 30 tablets and he is put in the list,. Then that person will see in his mobile app linked with health ID card number that 30 tablets were issued in his name !!! And so some patients may complaint. So hospitals' ability to report ungiven treatment reduces. So govt's FALSE expenses will fall.
.
(2) say a hospital treated 5000 patient who all needed some bandage,. Noe bandage comes in roll of 10 meters. Its possible that hospital bought only 2500 bandages because each patient needs only 5 meters of bandage. And hospital charged one bandage cost to ALL 5000 patients !!! With health card, ALL data comes to one place. And so such theft can be easily detected.
.
(3) A hospital gave medicine which costs Rs 10000. But same medicine with different brandname was available at say Rs 1000. Today, it is had to detect and prove this. But with health card, hospital will have to eneter the medicine name, brand name, price etc in system;. So at any later date, system can tell patient and govt , with proof, that medicine-X costing Rs 10000 was given while Rs 1000 medicine Y with SAME components but different company brand was also available. Such record keeping is needed to control costs
.
So we need system where medicine / treatment / test data is recorded, but govt officers cant access without permission of patient. Such system can be made. This can be done by issuing multiple IDs to same person. So when person gives one or more of his IDs, then only doc or govt can see all his data, And govt officers who are in cross checking business (to ensure that there has been no leakages) will get only IDs, but no names.
