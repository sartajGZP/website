We all know that, when temperature drops below 100 degree centigrades, water molecules in gaseous water vapor form will become liquid water drops .
.
But here is a counter intuitive REALITY !!!
.
Lets say we have 3000 kg (= 1000 * crore * crore * crore molecules ) of 100% 100% 100% pure water. i.e. there is NOT a single molecule other than water molecules.
.
Then no matter how low the temperature is (except near zero kelvin) --- water molecules will remain in "gas" form and not form liquid water !!!
.
And add 0.00001 mg of dust to this 3000 kg of 100% pure water , and it will become water within minutes (at temperature below 100 centigrade ) !!!
.
How? WHY?
.
Water molecules form liquid "droplet" when crores of molecules are very close. Two or more molecules will come close due to random motion and may combine to form a cluster. But they may also get separated when a third molecule collides with either of them.
.
So say 1000 water molecules are in a cluster. Now say a new 1001st water molecule collides with a molecule outermost in the cluster. Then the impact of collision will tend to make that outermost molecule to separate out. And attraction force with existing 999 water molecule makes that outermost water molecule to stay attached with cluster.
.
And the force that makes it remain combined depends on number of water molecules in that cluster. If cluster has smaller number of molecules, then chances are high that a collision with a new molecule will knock off a rim molecule.
.
Now It turns out that if a cluster has less than 150 million molecules , then inward force on outermost water molecule, that keeps it attached to cluster, is LESSER than impact force created by colliding water molecule !!!
.
So if a cluster has less than 150 million water molecules, then chances that it will split are more than chances that it will grow !!
.
Now at above 100 degrees, , all molecules are isolated. Sav temperature drops below 100 degrees, Now some molecules will form cluster of 2 , 3 or 4. Now chances that a solo molecule will hit those clusters and break them, are much much higher than chances that cluster of 5 will be formed !!! One can show that molecules will keep colliding , but nor form cluster for billions of years !!!
.
Even if there is water ball of size bigger than earth, and its 100.000% pure water i.e. all water molecule and no other molecule at all, then even if temperature drops to from 100 degrees to zero degrees,m it will remain in dense gas form and not become liquid water !! Thats because clsuter will grow to size of 2 , 3 and 4 and may be 5, but soon fall apart, and even cluster of 150 water molecules will not come, forget 150 million !!
.
Then how does rain happen?
.
Its because of impurities like dust !!!
.
Some water molecules will collide with dust and immidiaty settle on dust. Now these billions of water molecules for enough force that a colliding molecuing will stay rather than depart or split. And thus water droplets are formed
