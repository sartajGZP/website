For Panchayats, Haryana Govt to table ‘right to recall’ Bill in Assembly
.
Haryana Deputy Chief Minister Dushyant Chautala on Tuesday said a bill will be tabled in the monsoon session of the state assembly, beginning Wednesday, empowering people to recall elected representatives of panchayat bodies if they lose their confidence.
.
Addressing a news conference, he said his Government will make efforts to have a discussion on the bill in the House before it is passed.
.
Chautala, who is also the State panchayats minister, said former Deputy PM Devi Lal had a dream that there should be a “right to recall” in the panchayati raj system if elected representatives lose people’s confidence and fail to come up to their minimum expectations.
