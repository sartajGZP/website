A general answer to many questions about drafts we have proposed
.
I and other RRP-activists have written several proposed-law-drafts , and redrafted many. Some 25 drafts are at tinyurl .com / RrpAllDrafts .
.
Now there will many questions about incompleteness of drafts, clarity of clauses, dispute about exact meanings etc This general answer imo answers most of those questions
.
(01) Draft writers' role ends after writing drafts. once the drafts are passed the meaning and execution will be decided by PM / CM etc
.
(02) If there is incompleteness in draft, then PM / CM will make rules to address the cases of incompleteness. And citizens and Jurors can suggest / instruct PM / CM on what those rules should be. PM / CM may take notice of those suggestions or instructions or may ignore them. And he may follow them or may not.
.
(03) citizens may use Citizens' Voice clauses aka TCP clauses to send suggestions / instrictions to PM / CM . Again, PM / CM may take notice or may ignore. And PM/CM may follow them or may not
.
(04) citizens may or need not replace PM / CM via RTR-PM , RTR-CM law-drafts
.
(05) Also, even a draft says that "PM / CM shall implement xyz system" nothing in drafts except RTR-PM , RTR-CM drafts guarantee that PM / CM will actually implement that xyz system properly.
.
(06) And what if an officer plays mischief? ef say draft says "officer shall put data on websirte": and officer mkay put data on website in a way none except him and PM/CM can see !!! And officer may claim "see, draft only says that I should put data on website. It doesnt say that I should put in a way public can see". And if even word public is added in draft, the officer can put 50 captcha before anyone can see any data, and data can be put in extremely poor way. eg . Officer can literralyy put all data in greek and latin and say "where does the draft say that I must put data in English or local language"
.
(07) so imo, PM / CM can handle such mischiefs. And what if PM / CM dont? Then ONLY recourse I have offered is -- citizens can use RTR PM and RTR CM. Once one CM loses job, all CMs and even PM will learn to behave. And there are other drafts such as financial fines by majority voting, imprisonment by majority voting and execution by majority voting which will soon be added, As a draft writer, I DO NOT want to write 100s of lines to handle 100s of ways in which officers can play mischief.
.
(08) what if there is error in draft? Then my proposal is ---the draft should be first printed as is, with errors , and next minute PM / CM can correct errors using GN. And what if PM / CM deliberately dont fix the errors or fix in a poor way? Then solution I propose to citizens is --- use RTR PM and RTR CM law-drafts,.
.
In short --- Jury / CV-clauses / RTR-CM / RTR-PM are the answers to almost all questions you may have on errors, incompleteness and confusion on interpretation related questions about drafts we have proposed
