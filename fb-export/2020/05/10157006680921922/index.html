.
Summary -- I have been working to raise mass movement to end lockdown since 27-mar-2020 , because imo, over 70 crore Indian voters (out of total of 90 crore) have been against lockdown since 27-mar-2020.
.
Topics ---
.
(1) How many crore voters support / oppose LockDown as of today? And how many supported / opposed Lockdown as on 27-mar-2020?
.
(2) Dealing with the egalitarian view that "doesnt matter that 70 crore oppose lockdown ... lockdown must go on ..."
.
-----
.
(1) How many crore voters support / oppose LockDown as of today?
.
on 27-mar-2020 lakhs and lakhs of laborer from Delhi and many cities started started walking to their villages about 500 km to 1500 km away on feet. Heat , cold and it also rained during those days. On an average, they walked for 10 days to 40 days .
.
Did these lakhs / crores of labor supported lockdown or opposed lockdown?
.
I am sure they all opposed lockdown.
,
Now, my guess is that out of 90 crore voters across, about 70 crore voters oppose lockdown as of today (22-may-2020).
.
And over 70 crore opposed lockdown even on 27-mar-2020.
.
But anyone is free to believe otherwise.
.
If one wishes to believe that 70 crore voters supported lockdown on 27-mar-2020 and still support lockdown today, then fine. Each one, one belief.
.
My belief is that since 27-mar-202, over 70 crore voters have been anti-lockdown
.
------------------------
.
(2) Dealing with egalitarian view that "doesnt matter that 70 crore oppose lockdown ... lockdown must go on ..."
.
Now there are many, who believe that "it does not matter whether 70 crore voters oppose lockdown or 70 crore support lockdown !!! Lockdown was good and so it should be imposed on all 90 crore voters without worrying about how many support / oppose lockdown, and lockdown should continue for as many days as needed".
.
This egalitarian view (opposite of democratic view) has been on this planet since men arrived. The egalitarian people, have always believed that they know, while commons dont know, what is good for commons. And commons count should be manipulated or suppressed, but need not be considered./.
.
As per these egalitarian people , lockdown was good, because without lockdown, crores of lives will be lost and lockdown will save crores of lives. And since commons will simply never understand this complex data and complex analysis, the commons' opinion should be manipulated or suppressed and lockdown should go on as long as needed.
.
"The Truth should suppress the majority opinion", as they claimed !!
.
So I asked many of these egalitarian people --- can you give me age (in years) of all those who died in Italy between 01-feb-2020 and 20-mar-2020. I asked these egalitarian people to tweet Govt of India to get Italy's agewise death data. None tweeted Govt to of India to provide this data !!!
,
Next I asked --- can you ask Govt of India to release all age and co-illness of data of those are passing away in India, be corona deaths or other deaths?
.
Again, none of these egalitarian people asked !! They were all somehow sure that all deaths were due to corona and not due to co-illnesses !!!
.
IOW, they are damn sure about facts and what is right !!
.
They want truth to suppress majority opinion
.
But they do NOT want to publicize the data which may prove that they are right or they are wrong !!!
.
So much for their devotion to truth , on basis of which they want to suppress majority opinion.
.
Now stand since 27-mar-2020 has been
.
(a) lockdown will cause many many non-corona deaths
.
(b) lockdown lockdown does NOT reduce corona deaths even by 1% --- it does add a few days in lives of those who are likely to die by corona, but thats all.
.
(c)--- based on agewise UK's death data, my analysis says that corona deaths would NOT have exceeded 5 lakhs, i.e. about 4 times traffic deaths, about 10 times UK , even though population is 23 times UK. Thats because people in India die early and are younger, and so population has more immunity. And person at age = N years in India ha smore immunity than person of age = N years in UK and so death will be much below 500,000, less than 50000
.
But PRIMARY reason I have been working to raise mass movement to end lockdown since 27-mar-2020 is --- because imo, over 70 crore voters (out of total of 90 crore voters) in India since 27-mar-2020 have been anti-lockdown and want lockdown to end asap
.
And one should also note that pro-lockdown people, while they claim that "truth should suppress majority opinion" , do not want to give agewise deaths of countries such as Italy, UK, Sweden etc and do not want to give co-illness data of India !!!
.
This album is about what steps activists can take to mobilize the 70 crore voters, and what steps 70 crore voters can take, so that PM / CMs. have to end lockdown.
