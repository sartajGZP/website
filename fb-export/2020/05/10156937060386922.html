Why death risk on Indian of age k years is lower than British of same age? Most reasons are NOT good easons, but they happen to be there !!!
.
One thing that can be noted is that there is no medicine for corona. So only defense is immunity system and other external physical factors that decide rate at which virus will spread and how fast virus's fat layer degenerates
.
The medicines only cure secondary infections and at best and they slow down virus growth to half, but not more. Oxygen therapy and ventilators at best add a few days. During these few added days, if immunity beats corona virus, then person lives, or else he passes away. So finally immunity level as on 01-feb-2020 is the main decision maker.
.
So if immunity system, is strong, then the person will survive or else, he will pass away, Medication and treatment only help those whose immunity is borderline.
.
Now I will explain why corona-19 death risk for Indian of age k years is much lower than English person of same age. Why?
.
Imo, due to three broad reasons
.
(1) immunity of Indian is higher for good or bad reasons
(2) effective spread rate will be lower in India
(3) efficiency of virus is lower !!!
.
------------------
.
(1) Why due to right/wrong reasons, k-year old Indian has more immunity than same age English
.
(1.01) The child death rate is higher in India. So persons who have genetically poor immunity pass away earlier. This is NOT a good reason. But as far as corona analysis goes, the reason is important
.
(1.02) In India, 20 in 1000 lives to age of k = 80 years. In UK, 80 in 1000 live to age of k = 80 years. So a person with lower immunity in India is more likely NOT to live to 80 years. Again, this is NOT a good reason. But due to this, immunity levels in K years old in India is higher and so there will be fewer % deaths in K years segment, for all K.
.
(1.03) A large population in India which had low immunity say in year 2019, got finished by 10s of pathogens like TB, malaria etc. Whereas in UK, these pathogens are less fatal due to better treatment and less numerous due better hygiene. So when corona came to UK, there were many whose immunity was weak in 2019 itself. While most of such persons in India would have passed away in year 2019 itself. IOW, before corona gets chance to kill such low immunity people in India, other 10s of pathogens combined with poor health care may have finished him !!! So corona will not find many people left !! This reason, to some extent, is restatement of above
.
(1.04) Smoking and alcoholism in India , particularly in those above 60 years is still less. Smoking and alcoholism has certainly increase after 1990s. but it started becoming trend only in youth back then. So those who are 60+ today still take much much less alcohol and smoking. Corona-19 does more damage to smoking and alcohol addict,
..
(1.05) In India, the population has been perpetually fighting against malaria, TB and 10s of pathogens due to worse hygiene compared to UK. So immunity in those who have survived is higher than persons in UK of same age
.
(1.07) In India, people still eat some jaggery and less sugar compared to USA / UK. Jaggery ( = gud = gur ) does less damage to immunity system. Also, sugar intake vastly increased in UK in 1960s, while it increased in India only AFTER 1990s and didnt become a trend in those above 35 years back then. So elderly population has suffered less "sugar damage" to their immunity. This factor will reduce corona deaths in India.
.
(1.08) In India, people eat spices and spices are heated in edible oil (cooking process called tadaka or vaghar), eat raw vegetables. Tadaka or vaghar increase NET vitamin intake, because vitamins present in spices get dissolved in fats present in ghee or edible oil used for tadaka or vaghar. And all this increases immunity. In USUK, vegetable eating has vastly reduced, and fruits are taken via bottled drinks and less eaten raw. Also, milk is consumed in form of cheese, which does add fats to body but less vitamins. Because vitamins in cheese are not in soluble form and so absorption by body is less
.
(1.09) Less sunshine : Exposing body to sun increases immunity.
.
(1.10) Form of milk intake : UK takes more per capita milk, but takes in form of Cheese. Where as Indians take milk in form of milk, tea, curd, buttermilk and ghee. Cheese has vitamin-D but it is NOT in soluble form and so absorption is poor. (asdie : - this is one reason why in West, despite large consumption of cheese (via cheeseburgers, pizza, pasta etc), So milk taken by Indians, in form of ghee or cured or milk etc boosts immunity while milk taken in form of cheese doesnt add much to immunity..

(1.11) Egg eaters in India seldom throw away yolk. Whereas more and more egg eaters ij UK throw away yolk. Egg yolk has vitamins which boost immunity.
.
(1.12) In India, non vegetarians eat animal liver or fish liver as delicacy, while in US/UK, in burger, steak etc or fish fries in large chain restaurants, there is near zero component of animal/fish liver. So vitamins intake of meat/fish eater in India is much higher than usuk
.
(1.13) Consider amount of medicines taken by all persons of same age k years of UK and India. The medicines taken by the person in India were much much less.
.
(1.14) Many youth in since 1970s USUK take vast amount of sports dopes which decreases immunity after a few years. In India, despite promotion of sports dope by movies like Amir Khan’s Dangal, consumption of sports dopes is very low.
.
(1.15) An average Indian of age = k years , does more physical labor than person of same age in UK. Physical labor and exercises has vastly reduced across India in past 20 years. But still it is much higher than UK. Physical exercises are most imp factors in immunity across ages

(2) Effective spread rate of virus is lower in India
.
(2.1) Old age homes increase spread rate of virus. And India has much less population in old age homes.
.
(2.2) Air conditioners increase spread rate. And India has much less air conditioners, and much less air conditioners in old age homes.
.
-------------------------
.
(3) Why efficiency of virus will be lower in India
.
(3.1) The virus has lipid cover, If this cover melts or gets damaged, then potency of virus decreases. So now, following factors factors may look small, but they add up. All these factors increase heat in throat, respiratory track etc compared to UK, and so potency of virus decreases
.
(3.2) In India, people drink less cold water (because of habit of matka i.e mud picter water or because they cant afford). Many working class will take cold water in plastic bottle, and use it for next 2 to 4 hours, and after 10-20 minutes, water is not as cold as it came out of water cooler. In take of less cooler water lowers potency of virus
.
(3.3) In India, the temperatures are much higher than UK. So strength of lipid cover weakens.
.
(3.4) In India, use of air conditioner is less. So air in mouth and wind pipe is less cooler compared to UK and so lipid cover weakens.
.
(3.5) People have habit of drinking tea more than cold drinks
.
(3.6) Again, all these factors may look small, but they add up. All these factors increase heat in throat, respiratory track etc compared to UK, and so potency of virus decreases
.
So due to 10s of reasons related with temperature, potency of corona virus is lower in India, and due to 10s other good or bad reasons, immunity in Indians, of same age as British, is higher. And potency of virus is lower. And when a person is exposed to virus in small doses, chances that he will gain immunity are more than death.
.
So death disk, as assuming virus potency in India will be same as UK, and agewise immunity levels in India is same as UK is 221,000.

But if one puts factors that virus potency will be much lower due to temperature, lesser use of cold water drinking, lesser air conditioners and immunity levels ate same age are higher, then death risk was much lower.
