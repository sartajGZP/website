Importance of religious custom of "donating" or "offering" coconut copra at temple
.
Brown coconut water has good health benefits (such as raising immunity). And brown coconut has THICK white copra , which is also good for health but it has too much calories and oil. And so too much corpra is NOT good for a man who is doing not much physical labor
.
Now many sects have this practice, that the devotee will go to temple, pay some Rs 20 to Rs 30 and buy brown coconut, and then the devotee may drink coconut water , and the copra is offered to Temple. So that saves him from excess calories.
.
Now Temple can either give copra away to children or poor or may sell to sweet shops or sell to oil mills. And depending on management, temple will use the money for good or not good
.
So the practice has advantages to both --- the devotee gets coconut water and NOT copra. So he gets nutrients etc needed for health and he doesnt get excess calories in copra, And if the temple is managing this donation of copra for community use, then community also benefits.
