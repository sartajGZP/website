Differences between lockdown of USA and lockdown of India
.
Paidmediamen and soga / namo / arke and workers have hidden information about differences between lockdown in India and USA. Here are some difference. Here are some points they have been hiding.
.
------
.
(1) No compulsory medication in USA
.
(1.01) No forced testing in USA --- a person in USA can refuse testing in which case he will be served with stay home order, And case will be filed against him only if he goes out of home. In India, police force was authorized by soga namo arke etc and workers of congress rss aap etc for compulsory testing
.
(1.02) No compulsory hospitalization in USA --- if a person is found corona positive in USA, he had option of stay home or hospitalization. in India, as per laws printed by soga namo arke etc, the positive tested person must walk to hospital or he will be dragged by policemen to hospital, and case will be filed against him. Or person has option of going to private corona hospital, where charged start from Rs 10000 per day !!!
.
(1.03) No compulsory / forced medication --- in USA, there was no forced medication. In India, once a person is hospitalized, with or against his will, he has to take whatever medicines doctor decides. And policemen were told to force patients to take medicine. And police case can be filed if he doesnt take medicines. And medicines like HCQ were given even to asymptomatic patients !!!
.
(1.04) Dead body of the person was always returned back to relative in USA, and they had option of post mortem. In India, dead body of relative was not given back to them !! And no option of post mortem.
.
-----------------
.
(2) lockdown was very mild in USA --- nothing compared to India !!!
.
(2.01) City metro and intra-city buses were ON for ALL days during lockdown. NYC metro train remain closed only for 1 day (maintenance and mass fumigation etc) but on that day, intra-city buses were ON
.
(2.02) inter-city buses and inter-city trains were also all on, with reduced frewuency
.
(2.03) Domestic flights across USA were on
.
(2.04) No restriction on private car movements. Number of passangers in car = 3 in some states, but can be more if all are from same family
.
(2.05) Further , in USA, hotels / motels were all open. And all private yellow taxis, uber etc were also open ALL the days. In India, almost all hotels were shut and taxis were also shut !!!
.
(2.06) Rule to wear mask is there in NYC, but not enforced yet. And even as per rule, mask is needed only in places where distance cant be maintained. So one isnt legally required to wear mask in park or while walking on street
.
(2.07) USA hasnt made any law to make it compulsory for passangers on flights / trains etc to install govt APP (like Arogya Setu) is compulsory
.
(2.08) Essential services (food, liquor, medicine, auto repairs, all repairs) were not clased even for one day. The stores only implemented limited number of customers inside store. And so long queue outside stores were there for first few days, but not after first few days.
.
(2.09) Take out and home delivery from Restaurants were NOT closed in NYC and most of USA even for one day. Many restaurants remained closed due to lack of business.

(2.10) No compulsory app installation :in India, soga namo arke and workers of congress rss aap have printed law that anyone travelling in train and flight must install Arogra Setu app on his mobile. There is no such compulsory APP instaltion in passengers in USA

So there was no India like lockdown anywhere in USA.
