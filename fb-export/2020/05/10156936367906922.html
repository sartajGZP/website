Why such variation from Rs 154 to Rs 1674 , almost 11 times
.
One valid reason is vast variation oin quality of coal. But thats not only reason. In many cases, nexuses and maipulation work so that royalty amount is low.
.
Most imp reason is -- coruption in Coal India . Otherwise, If Coal India is run efficienctly and without corruption, then they can outbid all.
..
To understand another reason, pls see second picture.. It says -- anyone can bid, but end use has to be steel, cement or captive plant.
.
IOW, each open auction has many many conditions attached Due to these conditions, only few will be able to bid. They form cartel and keep prices low. Then how come we get prices like Rs 1600 per tonne? Because . sometime cartel breaks and conditions are weak, and too many players rush in
.
Also, the bidders have to meet lot of conditions. And plot size is kept so high that small players dont qualify.
.
SOLUTION?
.
I have proposed Dhanvapasi Passbook law draft which will improve auction mechanism and ensure that all mineral royalty is equally distributed amongst all commons,. This will end this mineral royalty loot.
.
And another proposal is Right to recall Coal Minister and Jury System over Coal Ministry Staff. This will improve Coal India, and so coal royalties will further improve.
.
