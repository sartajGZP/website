chap-03 : why sudden increase in deaths after week-13 in UK?
---------------------------------------------------------------
.
Here, imo, reasons are related to immunity MEMORY and ability to make NEW immunity
.
Say a person young and healthy once. He had faced 100s of disease causing viruses and bacteria etc. So back then, his body developed antibodies to fight against these viruses etc. Now most animal and plant bodies somehow remembers that immunity for decades and decades. So almost till death, that body is immune to those specific bacteria or viruses, unless at very very old age or very ill condition.
.
Now with age, and other factors, like diabetes, high bp, cancer medicines, heart medicines etc ability to make NEW antibody to fight against NEW bacteria or virus weakens,
.
===== IMPORTANT ====> So at any given point of time, there is some old/ill section in population, whose immunity MEMORY is still strong enough to deal with all seen bacteria/viruses ; but ability to make NEW immunity to fight NEW virus is now very very weak.
.
Since this is IMPORTANT, I will repeat -----------
.
=> At any given point of time, there is some old or very ill section in population, whose immunity MEMORY is still strong enough to deal with all seen bacteria/virus ; but his ability to make NEW immunity to fight NEW virus or bacteria is very very weak.
,.
So if his TOTAL immunity was weak, then 100s of pathogens would have killed him way before 01-mar-2020. But his IMMUNITY MEMORY was STRONG. But ability to make new immunity was very weak. So his body successfully defended against all "old" seen viruses. But the same body's ability to make NEW IMMUNITY was gone long back. So since no new virus namely corona-19 came in 01-mar-2020, body could not defend.
