Why policemen are unable to implement lockdown in many areas --- because there are only 185 policemen and 75 soldiers per lakh population.
.
So unless heavy use of firearm is allowed, it isnt possible for them to implement lockdown with lathies alone if too many people decide to defy lockdown.
.
There are 15 lakh non reserve policemen, plus 10 lakh reserves (CRPF , SRP etc) and 10 lakh military soldiers,. And population is 136 crore. So we have 25 lakh / 136 crore = 185 policemen per lakh of population and 75 soldiers per lakh of population
.
And not all can be tasked with lockdown implementation,. They have other tasks.
.
So when a section of population defies an order, with lathi charge alone, there is not much policemen and soldiers can do.
.
With firearms, very small number of policemen can implement any order on any number of population. eg with firearms, 10000 policemen are sufficient to implement order on 136 crore of population, in round one.
.
But thats a political decision with political unknown political outcomes in round two.
.
So we see that in many areas, lockdown implementation is very weak.
