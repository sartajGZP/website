Infant death rate in haryana increases by 46% in apr-jun-2020 , compared to same months 2019 !!! - Tribune newspaper says
.

.
https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/health-services-restricted-haryana-infant-deaths-rise-46-in-lockdown-122246
.
A whopping 46 per cent rise has been witnessed in the number of deaths of infants in Haryana between April and June this year compared to the corresponding period in 2019, information accessed under the Right to Information has revealed. Experts have blamed the increase on restricted access to health services during the initial phases of lockdown.

46% increase in infant death is UNBELIEVABLE , Because infant deaths are high in out-state labor, and much of the labor had left Haryana by april end. So while nationwide infant deaths must have increased, infant deaths in haryana should have decreased,.
.
Also, infants death registration is lax. Not all infant deaths get registered. And % of "didnt register cases" would be higher in apr-jun-2020 than in apr-jun-2019, across India.
