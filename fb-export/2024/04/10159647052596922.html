1. *Bolta Hindustan* YouTube channel has been deleted. It’s interesting to note that they covered the EVM demonstration on 6th January and had published other videos critical of EVMs.

2. *Janta Live* was forced to delete the video of the 6th January EVM demonstration.

3. *Times Express* was forced to make the EVM Jantar Mantar video from 2019 private (now deleted). During a call, a person from there mentioned that “he can’t divulge reasons for this” and hung up the call.

4. When we requested *Forum4* to cover the PCI event on 9th April, they messaged us saying, “most of the channels reporting on EVM are getting demonetized, so we won’t be able to cover your event.”

5. *Article 19* and *National Dastak* (with 90L followers) have also regularly reported on EVM demonstrations and other anti-EVM content. These channels have been given notice for shutdown. The reasons could be many, but EVM criticism is surely one of them.
