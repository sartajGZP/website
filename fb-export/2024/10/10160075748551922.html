Right to Recall Party is visible on ECI Subvudha website ie https://suvidha.eci.gov.in/ac/public/affidavit/candidatedetails

To see RRP, click on "Name of Political Party" selection box. And speedily type on keyboard "right to" keys, all lower case. You see right to recall party

Basically, the party names in selection box are not sorted. So if you scrol, Righ to recall Party will not come in list of parties starting with R. It is placed at much bottom !!! So click on selection box and type keys "right to"
