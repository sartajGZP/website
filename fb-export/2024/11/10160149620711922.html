Nifty Sensex are used to lure in new investors and then brokers use small new company shares to rob investors. Solution?
.
This is how share market people rob new investors :
.
(1) new investors are lured in using SENSEX , NIFTY etc.
.
(2) once investors open account, the brokers etc call them and ask investors to invests into small new company shares.
.
(3) The brokers get hefty commissions for convincing ( = fooling? ) investors to buy small new company shares.
.
(4) And in 9 out of 10 cases, investors who invest in small new companies end up losing !!!
.
Solution : solution I propose is - each investor can ask SEBI to put his entire transaction list on net for others' research purposes. So if investors who have lost agree to upload their transaction list, then others can use them to show that on an average, an average investor loses more than gains.
