**some of the chemicals used in making sugar , and PM CMs give subsidies to sugar mill to use these chemicals !!!**

Jggery is made by merely heating sugarcane juice so that much of the water evaporates and solid remains. And a very small quantity of salt is added to reduce bacteria/fungus growth and also give some salty taste, because too sweet taste sometimes reduces appeal. **No chemicals, except some samudri salt or senda salt are added in raw jaggery. **Whereas factory sugar is made by adding 10s of chemicals in this jaggery water. And even after removal of these chemicals, traces of these chemicals are present. In addition, there is a huge investment in machinery and labor. Despite this, sugar is cheaper because of subsidies given by PM CMs to these sugar mills.

Anyway, here is some of the chemicals that PM CMs have legally allowed sugar factory owners to use to. There are many more.

.

**(01) Sulfur Dioxide** : This gas is used as a bleaching agent and preservative to improve the color and shelf life of sugar.

**(02) Phosphoric Acid **: This acid is used to adjust the pH of the sugar solution and to precipitate impurities.

**(03) Formaldehyde** : Sanitation Chemical

**(04) Sodium dodecyl benzene sulfonate** : Viscosity Reducer

**(05) Polyacrylamide (PAM) **: This is a polymer flocculant that aids in the aggregation of suspended particles. It is one of the most widely used flocculants in sugar processing because of its effectiveness and relatively **low toxicity **when properly used.

**(06) Polyaluminum Chloride (PAC) **: Though more commonly used in water treatment, PAC can sometimes be used in sugar clarification as a coagulant and flocculant due to its high efficacy in settling suspended particles.

(07) Lime: Used to adjust the pH of the sugar solution and to precipitate impurities.

(08) Poly DADMAC (Polydimethyldiallylammonium chloride) : Colour Precipitants

(09) Poly AMINE polymers : Colour Precipitants

(10) Phosphonic acid based antiscalants (e.g., HEDP, ATMP) : Evaporator Antiscalant

(11) Polymeric antiscalants : Evaporator Antiscalants

(12) Polyacrylamide : Mud Settling Agent

(13) Ferric chloride : Mud Settling Agent

(14) Polymeric dispersants : Viscosity Reducer

(15) Chlorine : Sanitation Chemical

(16) Quaternary ammonium compounds : Sanitation Chemical

(17) Hydrochloric Acid : Used to adjust the pH of the sugar solution.

(18) Sodium Hydroxide : Used to neutralize the acid in the sugar solution.

(19) Carbon Dioxide : Used to carbonate the sugar solution, which helps to remove impurities.

(20) Vegetable Oils : Natural oils like canola oil, soybean oil, and sunflower oil can act as mild antifoaming agents, especially in food production where chemical-based agents might be restricted.

(21) Polypropylene Glycol (PPG) : Used in certain food processing applications, PPG is a non-silicone defoamer that is effective in sugar processing. It is generally food-safe at specific concentrations.

(22) Polyethylene Glycol Esters : These are also used in the food industry as antifoaming agents and are compatible with most food processing systems.

(23) Aluminum Sulfate (Alum) : Alum is occasionally used as a flocculant in the initial stages of sugar clarification. It helps in binding suspended particles, which can then be filtered out.

(24) Ferric Chloride : Though less common, ferric chloride is sometimes used as a flocculant in sugar refining for its effectiveness in coagulating impurities.
