How to "compare" RRP lawdrafts with other proposed lawdarfts?
.
Activists will face 10s or 100s of competing lawdrafts. So how to compare RRP lawdrafts with other PROPOSED lawdrafts?
.
In some cases, the other proposals are physically UNFEASIBLE . eg ULM says all will get any amount of good superfood they want. This is UNFEASIBLE as even short amount of superfood cant be made available to even 10% population.
.
But in many casesm proposals are feasible.
.
One method I use is -- TWO COUNTRY MODEL
.
So consider two or more countries. Say one country-A follows RRP lawdraft and other countries say country-B, country-C etc follow other proposed lawdrafts. Then which country will have better technology, economy and military in short run or long run?
.
Imo, this method is one good way to compare lawdrafts
.
In one country model, anything will work. Even theory of FATE and slavery worked and may work again in countryA !!! But when such countryA faces a countryB which has less slavery or no slavery and people who believe less in fate, then CountryA may get badly defeated.
.
So imo two country analysis is one efficient way to COMPARE two proposed lawdraft sets.
