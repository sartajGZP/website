RSS-apex timepass in name of education ---- in order to show "RSS is doing something, RSS is doing something", RSS-apex holding 100s of seminars etc on "how to improve history / moral education" !!! And it is royal timepass ONLY
.
DETAILS
.
Some 19 months have passed since NaMo became PM. Things are surely less bad than Congress-raaj. But much of it is same as congress raaj. Not even 5% of what RSS-workers had expected and were promised by NaMo has happened. Excuses that BJP doesnt have majority in Rajsabha isnt working as more and more RSS-activists come to know about procedure of the joint session.
.
So in order to fool RSS-workers as well as general RSS-supporting voters, RSS-apex is now holding 10s and 100s of seminars across India in name of "improving education" !!!
.
As per RSS-apex, RSS-experts will create policy suggestions for NaMo-govt for Education dept. And Education dept will implement policies made by RSS-apex and RSS's "experts" in education !!! And so Ram-raajya will come in India !!!
.
Lets see what a ROYAL timewaste this is. Basically, it is an exercise to fool RSS-workers and their supporters.
.
1. RSS's education experts are giving policy suggestions for education. RSS' education-experts refuse to put them on net. I came to know via the workers who have attended the meetings.
.
2. These RSS-experts go on and on and on on "samasya varnan' i.e. "describing the problem". The problem as per them is "Indian education system is based on West, and so it is harming India. Next , they do is "siddhant sthapana" i.e. "establishing principles" such as " our education system should depend on vedic system " etc etc.
.
3. The RSS-experts refuse to give law-drafts to fix education system !! When my friend asked them to give law-drafts, they said "Only now we have BJP govt who is willing to listen. So now we are working on policy. Once policy is finalized, we will prepare law-drafts,". IOW, law-drafts will be given in next life time !!
.
4. Even policy statements are all wrt to history education and moral value education ONLY !! There was NOTHING on improving Maths education, science education etc. English education was opposed !!! Many insisted that English should be taught ONLY after 6th class !! And there was NO mention of computer literacy. IOW, skills are all ignored and focus is ONLY on history and moral values.
.
5. The RSS-experts completely oppose law-education and weapon-use-education in schools !!
.
6. No mention of how they intend to fix problem of corruption in education. As per them, "the new education system will improve moral values and so corruption problem will go away " !!!!
.
And I will repeat ---- they refuse to give law-drafts to implement their own policies !!! And as per them, policy isnt yet ready and law-drafts will become available after policies become ready !!!
.
Now please see what a giant timepass this is.
.
RSS-apex has been assistsing BJP-apex in winning elections since 1950s. So they should have had written policy drafts WITH law-drafts needed to implement these policies several years back. And on 25-may-2014 when NaMo became PM, they should have asked NaMo to print these law-drafts in Gazette. But all these 60 - 65 years, they didnt write even one page of law-draft !!! And now when RSS-apex has their own govt, they start "policy" writing after 19 long months. And the task of writing law-drafts will start AFTER policy drafting ends !!!
.
All in all, this is a drama to ensure that RSS-workers dont lose hope and RSS-workers keep thinking that "something dramtaic will happen, something great will happen". RSS-apex is only giving fake hopes and NOTHING ELSE
.
Solution?
.
Solution I propose is that activists should demp RSS-apex, RSS-workers etc and work to FORCE PM, CMs, MPs, MLAs to print law-drafts that can fix the fiend of education and other areas such courts, police etc. The law-drafts are propose for to imrove education is "Right to Recall District Education Officer : at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10152255030191922 ". If you support this law-draft , then pls send this link to MP via SMS , , and pls share this link on YOUR wall. RTR-DEO draft will force DEO to introduce law education and weapon use education as well. This will strength all citizens of India, and India as well.
.

As long as there are High Court judges like Honorable Justice A R Joshi, who took bribe of Rs 15.31 crore (rupees fifteen crore and thirty one lakh only) from Bhaijan Salman Khan and acquit him, it is next to impossible to put moral values using moral education.
