London water consumption = 200 liter per person per day (pls google "london water daily usage")
.
Ahmedabad water consumption = 150 liter per person per day provided by Municipality and if you include private bores, it would be well above 200 liter per person per day.
.
So water consumption is almost same. But London has 24 hr water supply in almost all parts. Where as Ahmedabad , except those who have private bore, gets water mere 1-2 hours a day.
.
WHY?
.
Simple. No water meters !!!
.
Ahmedabad doesnt have water meters !!! Whereas London installed water meters in almost all connections by 1930s !!! Same for several cities across globe such as NY, Singapore etc.

IOW, moral values and bhashans to save water are useless. What is needed is water meter
