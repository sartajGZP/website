How NaMo's proposal of "reservation for poor upper caste in govt collages" will benefit self finance collage owners.
.
NaMo has ordered Gujarat CM Mrs. Anandiben Patel to reserve x% of seats for poor general students.
.
Consider say LDCE = LD Engineering college , which is low fee govt college. Say there are 1000 seats for students. Say 500 are for SC / ST / OBC and 500 for General.
.
Now say a general person, whose parents income is above say Rs 600,000 . Say in general merit list, say he rank is 401. He would have taken in LDCE
.
Now if 100 out of 500 seats of LD are reserved for Economically Worseoff Upper Caste students, then that well off general will NOT get admission in LDCE.
.
So now he will have to go to a private college.
.
IOW, by "reserving" govt college seats for economically worse off in govt collages, the economically well off will be forced to move to private colleges
.
=======
.
No wonder why owners of self finance collages were all supporting this "anti-reservation" movement !! A large number of private college engineering seats were remaining vacant due to high fees and fact that many well off students got seats in govt colleges
.
And whats the definition of well off and worseoff ? Combined income of parents should be Rs 600,000 !! (Or is it Rs 450,000 ?) IOW, a large class of professions will get labled as "economicall well off": and will be forced to send kids to private colleges.
.
