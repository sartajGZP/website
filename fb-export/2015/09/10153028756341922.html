The video which says that SC / ST / OBC can get admission in IIM by "very low %" seems to be more hoax than reality
.
Pls see IIMA data for year 2014
.
http://insideiim.com/what-it-takes-to-get-into-iim-ahmedabad-rti-query-class-of-2014-stats/
.
Category ---- Maximum ---- Mean ---- Minimum
----
General --- 100 ----- 99.635 --- 97.95
OBC-NCL---- 99.99 ----- 95.508 ---- 86.51
SC --- 99.83 ---- 90.806 ---- 66.2
ST --- 98.6 ---- 84.357 --- 60.93
.
So average scores are
.
General --- 99.635
OBC-NCL--- 95.508
SC --- 90.806
ST --- 84.357
.
====
.
And minimum CAT scores for SC / ST / OBC goes low because IIM takes 10th class % , 12th class% , etc into account. So many SC / ST etc got higher CAT score than the ones who got selected, but they had poorer 10th class% and 12th class%
.
If CAT written was only criteria, then CAT scores of SC / ST / OBC selection would have been higher/
.
So once again "50% wala OBC getting admission is all hoax".
.
