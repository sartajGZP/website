A system I propose to reduce FALSE heartburn caused by reservation
.
First, what is FALSE heartburn?
.
Let me give an example
.
==== examples of real and false heartburn ====
.
Say you have Rs 1000 and someone steals it. You feel loss. It is real heartburn
.
Say you didnt buy a plot in Delhi in year 2004 for Rs 10 lakhs. That plot now sells for Rs 1 crore. You feel loss of Rs 90 lakhs. This FALSE heartburn !!!
.
===== end of example ====
.
Now here is how it applies to caste based reservation.
.
Say there are 100 medical seats. Say 51 General, 27 OBC , 15 SC and 7 ST
.
Say marks are out of 1000
.
Say there are 10,000 applicants
.
Say score of 100th student in JOINT merit list is 800/1000
.
Say in JOINT merit list of first 100, there are 95 General, and 5 OBC, SC , ST
.
Say General closes at say 820/1000 and 51 General get selected
,
Say OBC closes at say 750/1000
.
Say SC closes at say 700/1000
.
Say ST closes at say 600/1000
.
Say there are 3000 General students over 600 / 1000
.
So all these 3000 students will think --- if there was no reservation, I would have got admission !!!
.
Now of these 3000, heartburn of 45 is real and heartburn of 2955 is FALSE.
.
Because even if there was NO reservation, ONLY 45 more General candidates would have got admission.
.
====
.
Now can FALSE heartburn be reduced
.
Each time admissions end. each student should get a copy of FULL sheet by email and a summary by letter.
,
And the letter should clearly mention whether he lost admission due to reservation OR due to lack of seats !!
.
So 45 students whose score was over ST closing marks of 600, would get explicit letter saying that they lost admission due to reservation.
.
And 2955 students whose score was over ST closing marks of 600, would get explicit letter saying that they lost admission , NOT due to reservation but simpky because of lack of seats. And that even if therwere were no reservation, they would NOT have got admission.
.
IOW, the FALSE heartburn will reduce
.

.
