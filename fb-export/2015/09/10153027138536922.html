We at Right to Recall group have been demanding since 2008 that a candidate in SC / ST / OBC should get seat in his quota and not in General quota. Today, Gujarat High Court has given judgement along same line
.
We dont take ANY credit for court case --- someone else filed the case did did the bulk work. We only circulated the proposal since 2008. More and more people came to know. I am not sure if litigant came to know via our efforts --- because people have been working on THIS demand since 1990s. In fact, Tamil Nadu had implemented this rule in 1990 !!
.

section-36.3 " Stand on issued linked with reservation" , line item-5 says
.
5. The person in reservation caste will get seat in reservation quota first, and will be considered in general list only after his reservation quota is full..
.
====
.
So when his quota is full, the next person's score will be far far less than closing rank of general, and so he wont get general seat anyway.
.
If activists had paid attention to this demand, and instead of SoMoKe-andhbhakti, had taken up this demand, then Govt would have issued GN to implement this demand anyway. But instead, activists decided to confine to SoMoKe-andhbhakti. And so this demand didnt go far.
.
Over 8 people died , some Rs 200 crore of property got gutted, and population of 6 crore ended up wasting 3-4 days of productive time. And worse may come. And all this would have been LESS , if activists had paid attention to a simple fact, that "law-drafts are important , not vyakti-puja". .
.
Anyway, I will request all activist focus on following law-drafts I have proposed to reduce the reservation mess
.
(A.1) I have proposed a law-draft that will reduce reservation from 49% to 5% of may be less , at cost of Rs 1000 per YEAR per Dalit / ST / OBC i.e. about Rs 84000 crores per year, which will increase existing taxes by about 5% . The draft is at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10152992759916922 . If you like the draft , pls follow instructions in the first five comments
.
(A.2) Another proposed law-draft will further reduce caste based reservation by eliminated double options i,e. SC , ST and OBC can opt in General as well as their own quota . The draft is at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10152990537221922
.
(A.3) Another proposed law-draft is --- if % of job holders belonging to a sub-caste in a Govt, including its PSUs , is more than their % population of that sub-caste , then reservation for that sub-caste will be suspended for a few years. And Total % reservation will decrease by % population of that caste. The law-draft is at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10152993013736922
