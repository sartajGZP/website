We at Right to Recall group also opposed Govt control over temples. The solution we proposed was to print law-drafts that would enable Hindus to either

(a) continue with existing oligarchic ownership of temples OR
.
(b) enable to setup SGPC like structures over the temples.

Myself and most of recallist support (b) over (a), but the law-drafts we proposed does NOT enforce (b) over (a). The law-drafts we proposed enables existing temple owners to setup (a) OR (b). And the bhakts aka followers in the sect can form a new sect with SAME beliefs which has structure (b) OR then can pressurize / incentivize existing temple owners to change structure of existing temple management from (a) to (b)..

As always, RSS-apex, BJP-leaders opposed these law-drafts. They have oppose the proposal of printing law-drafts that will enable Hindu temples' followers to ceate an SGPC like management structure over temples.
.
What RSS-apex and BJP-apex are now planning is to create the management boards over temples , and these boards will be filled by men appointed by RSS-apex and BJP-apex.

I will repeat and please note ----- What RSS-apex and BJP-apex are now planning is to create the management boards over temples , and these boards will be filled by men appointed by RSS-apex and BJP-apex.

This is similar to what Papacy did in Italy / Rome some 1700 -1800 years ago. Papacy grew because most Pagan temple owners had become self seeks and followed inheritance over temples. So most temple owners in Rome had become self-seekers and were losing faith in Pagan temples. Papacy created a structure where Priests have to remain unmarried. Though a formal rule, that "Priest must NOT marry" came much later, but informal rule that "only unmarried men or men who have given uo their marriage and family will be allowed to be become Priest": came in around 300 AD. And promotions were given only to unmarried priests. And Pagan priests back then had business / jobs etc. While Papacy allowed priests only to do jobs which were given or approved by Pope and Clergy as a whole. And Papacy forbade priests from owning their own property and doing their own business or taking jobs. Particularly, jobs in Govt were forbidden.
.
This made Catholic Priests more community serving , more committed to organization work and less self serving compared to the priests of the Pagan Temples of Rome , Italy and across Roman Empire, The Roman Emperors and Governors also saw that Papacy / Catholic Priests are more dependable than Pagan Priests and common citizens are having more attachment with Catholic Priests than Pagan Priests. And so influence of Pagan Priests declined and that of Catholic Priests grew. Gradually, Papacy Priests took over Pagan Temples and converted them into their churches, and in most cases, they were supported by Roman elite as well as commons.
.
In short, 1700 years ago, Pagan Temple owners in Roman Empire were married, had businesses of their own, and so they practiced inheritance over temple wealth, and so became self serving and less community serving. In contrast, Papacy enforced "priest will not marry, not have kids, not have job, not have business" rule and so Papacy as a whole was more community serving compared to owners of Pagan Temples.
.
And so Papacy's influence grew, and they started taking over Pagan temples and converted them into Churches, Many many Churches in Rome are former Pagan temples !!!
.
Almost same happened in large parts of India, East Asia, China etc. The Buddhist Monastary implemented the rule that "priests must not marry, not have kids, not have job, not have a business". And so they became more committed organization work, community serving and less self serving. So several followers of other sects left their sects and became Buddhists. So Buddhism, across Afghanistan, Pakistan, India, Myanmar = Burmma, Cambodia, Thailland, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, China, Japan grew and displaced their respective Pagan religions. Hinduism recovered only after several sects also adopted the rule that "priests will not marry, will not have kids, will not have job and will not have business".
.
However, around year 1500 AD Sikhism formulated , IMO, a far far better way of managing Temples i.e. Gurudwara. Sikhs created a system where the local Temple head was directly elected , formally or informally, thru voting by every adult Sikh citizen in that area !! And the temple head would have a term of 2 years to 5 years, and repetitions were discouraged. In my links given above, I have explained why / how this methods is far far superior than all other three methods ---- control via inheritance, control by community elders and "control by organization of unmarried priests". So in a short span of 200 years, Sikhism became so powerful, that they gained rulership over Afghanistan, Pakistan, Baluch, Sindh, Punjan and even Kashmir. At that time, Sikhs were hardly 5% of the total population of the area under their command. They could NOT replicate the structure over entire administration, and so administration outside temple was run in same inheritance based way (see how Sikh Kingdowm was managed after death of Maharaja Ranjit Singh). And so British could defeat them. (pls google on two "Anglo Sikh wars"). But till date, their temple management method, i.e. direct election of temple heads, has been, IMO, the best, or say, least bad method to run temples.
