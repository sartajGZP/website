How removing income limit for OBC-quota, with condition that OBC candidate must apply first in OBC quota , and can apply in General quota ONLY if OBC is full --- will INCREASE seats available for GENERAL candidates !!!
.
Let me explain why income limit on OBC is hurting General candidates.
.
Say there are 125 seats. Some 25 will be for NRI / Managament.. We take them out. So we have now 100 seats.
.
Say 7 for SC, 15 for ST, 27 for OBC-NCL and 51 General
.
(OBC are divided into two groups --- CL =- creamy layer = those who parents' income is equal to above Rs 600,000 per year, and NCL =- Non Creamy layer = those who parents' income is below Rs 600,000 per year. Reservation is ONLY for OBC-NCL)

Say there are 10000 applicants
.
Say there are 5000 ST, 10000 ST, 5000 OBC-CL, 20000 OBC-NCL and 60000 General applicants.
.
So 7 + 15 = 22 seats go for SC / ST
.
We have 27 for OBC-NCL and 51 for General
.
Now say 46th General candidate's score is 800/1000
.
Say there are 5 OBC-CL with score above 800/100
.
Then as per existing law, OBC-CL cannot even apply in OBC-NCL quota of 27 seats.
.
So all 5 will have to apply in general quota , and will get into general quota as their score is above 46th person in General list !!
.
So total number of OBCs who get selected = 27 into OBC-NCL quota + 5 OBC-CL in General quota = 32
.
And numebr of General who will get selected = 51 - 5 = 46 !!
.
Now say we print rule that
,.
1. OBC-CL can apply in OBC quota
.
2. OBC candidate can apply in General ONLY after his quota is full
,
Then all 5 OBC-CL will get absorbed in OBC quota;
.
So number of general will increase to 51 !!
.
====
.
IOW, putting income ceiling on OBC quota may hurt some OBC-CL candidate, but overall,. it causes more damage to GENERAL candidates. And it lowers the average score of seat holders
.
=====
.
