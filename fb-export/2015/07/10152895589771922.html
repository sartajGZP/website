Being better than Congress is no surity of "growth" . To grow, we need to make less "mistakes" than Chinese / USA makes.
.
A classic wrong notion many activists seem to be having is --- existing regime is less bad than Congress regime and hence growth is guaranteed.
.
Existing regime is surely far less bad than congress regime. But that would NOT take us much far. Because till our goods are CHEAPER than Chinese / Western goods, they wont even sell in India , forget international market. And till our goods sell, growth is pipe dream.
.
And only way out goods can become CHEAPER and have at least same quality as western / chinese is when we make lesser "mistakes" than they do.
.
And are we making "lesse" mistakes then they are doing? NO.
.
We are still using same run down judge system for ALL trials, while west uses Jury Trials in many many cases, and CHina is also pushing towards Jury System. West such as USA is using RTR in many posts , while we conitinue with mistake of NOT using RTR in even one place.
.
So efficiency of India will remain lower than China / USA. And so goods we make will remain more and more expensive.
.
So chances to REAL growth are remote.
.
The ONLY growth we will see will be FDI-based growth , which will take India closer to Greece
..
