Many say that if polls were conducted in jun-2014 , then BJP would have won. WRONG !! BJP would have lost even then, though not so badly. But BJP wanted to gain CM-ship and so took unprincipled stand of delaying election, and so now lost badly !!!
.
Lets compare votes in assembly-dec-2013 and LS-may-2014 .
.
The votes of AAP had increased from 29% to 32% !! But they lost all seven LS seats because votes of BJP increased even more --- from 33% to 46%. . But that was because people hated Congress and wanted NaMo as PM.
.
Even if Delhi assembly elections were to be held in aug-2014 , NaMo was not going to become CM. So NaMo factor would have played no role, and BJP would have been back to 33%. And these voters werent going to go back to Congress. Some would have gone to BJP and rest to AAP. So AAP would have won with 40-42 seats.
.
Then why didnt BJP conduct polls in aug-2014? Because BJP thought that in few months, they can convince a large number of Delhi voters to vote for BJP and get Delhi CM chair !!!
.
So hope to get Delhi CM position created delay. And so not only BJP lost CM chair, but suffered a worse defeat.
