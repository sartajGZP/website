How vegetarian is LESS violent than non-vegetarian food.
.

.
But one issue that comes is that nonveg food is violence. And the nonvegitarians often make counter-argument --- that plants also have life and also vegism is also violent. This post is to refute this SPECIFIC counter-argument only.
.
1. To produce 1 kg of meat, some 10 kg to 20 kg wheat equivalent grains/pulse/vegetables are needed. So 1 kg of meat will involve MORE violence than 1 kg of grain/pulse/vegetable. So even if killing plan is violent, then also killing animals is 10-20-more times MORE VIOLENT.
.
2. The plants have lesser nuerons and have only name sake nervous system. (please google on nervous system in plants). The nervous system is only to guide the plant to make its limited movement towards sunlight , water etc. Experiments show that plants make attempt to move towards sunlight, move towards water, move away from toxins, but plants make no attempt to move away from grazing animals or even fruit plucking animals or insects. IOW, plants consider getting eaten as their destiny, Whereas animals make explicit attempts to move away from grazers. IOW, plants perhaps consider being eaten as their destiny.
.
3. PAIN AND VIOLENCE CAUSE BY ASSOCIATION : The plants have no association, while animals have. eg when a human is killed, the OTHER humans around him feel pain. Same way, when an animal is killed, the other animals around him feel fear, pain and also miss him. When one plant is taken away from field, the other plants in field feel no pains. At least, thats what experiments show. May be , future experiments will show association between plants. But so far , there is none.
.
4. Plants have no parent-child emotional relation . eg in almost all mammals , parents sacrifice for their kids, Where as in plants , when a seed falls and tries to become plant, the parent plant will actually end up killing its growth by absorbing away all sunlight, water and minerals !!! IOW, there is no known emotional bonding amongst plants. So killing one plant doesnt increase fear, pain etc in other plants. So killing plants is LESS violent than animals.
.
5. DISPERSAL OF SEEDS---- plants cant survive without dispersal of seeds (pls google on dispersal of seeds). And for dispersal, they depend on animals. And so food the incentive plants offer to animals for dispersal. I am NOT joking or kidding. Please google on dispersal of seeds and you will see that animals play important role for plants by eating plants !!
.
If every fruit of of a tree falls below and doesnt get moved elsewhere, then that fruit will simply dont become a tree because the existing tree will keep soaking water / minerals etc. So seeds need dispersal. And thats the job of animals --- they disperse the seeds by eating plants / fruits. So plants benefit from animals' eating of plants. But when we eat animals, the animals dont get anything in return.
.
So in all cases , nonvegeism is more violent than vegism.
