We at Right to Recall Group need to give our own version of land acquisition draft. And show that it is better than drafts given by SoMoke. And we also need drafts for other issues such as slum issue and other issue. I request all recallist to start writing drafts , and later we can combine them.
.
1. LA is needed for industrialization.
.
2. LS isnt about acquiring land for highway or railways, For that , laws already exist and land acquired is so small, that there is rarely much controversy. It is also not about land needed to make airports, train station etc as they are small in number.
.
3. LA is about acquiring land to setup giant industrial estates, making dams and so forth.
.
Without LA, setting up industrial estates will become difficult may also become impossible, And rampant misuse of LA can cause huge damage to farmers..
.
As of now only 2.65% of land in India is used for (industrial + mining + commercial + residential + govt offices + Military establishments + roads + railways + warehouses ) !!! Rest 97.35% is agricultural , water bodies, fallow land and forests.
.
So in next 10 years, industries simply can even consume 1% of land of India !!
.
And some 50% of land is used for agricultural. So how can we take 2% of agricultural land without creating a mess? we need a law-draft for this.
.
