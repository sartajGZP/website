On facebook, pls divide all your fbfriends into LISTS , and when you put status posts, please select which FRIEND LIST should see that specific status post.
.
Or else, all your fbfriends will see all your posts and so your fbfriends will simply unfollow you to keep their newsfeed relevent and clean, and save their time.
.
So when you make important post which you want ALL to see, that post will have less audience.
.
eg I saw a status post from one fbfriend of mine, who is NOT a close real life friend, " had a great breakfast at .... ". Now if that post was from my reallife friend, I would have liked-commented-ignored it. But since it was from a non-real-life fbfriend, I clicked "unfollow" that person , because I dont want my newsfeed to get cluttered from non-political non-serious posts from non-real-life fbfriend !! So next time, when that person posts a serious political post, fb will NOT show it to me. It is my loss, and also his loss.
.
Instead, if that person had made friend lists such as "real life close friends" , "real life not close friends" , "political colleagues" etc etc and shown that "had great breakfast" status ONLY to "real life close friends" , then I would not have seen that status, and I would NOT have unfollowed him !!
.
So please classify your fbfriends into friend lists , and please dont make all non-serious posts public . Or else, more and more will only unfollow you.
