2. SoMoKe are supporting Mumbai High Court's decision that will effectively ensure that foreign companies dont have to pay any capital gains tax !! How? Say company X is registered in India. So when A sells share of X to B, A has to pay capital gains tax. But lets say a foreign company Y owns share of X. And A and B are foreign companies. Then A sells Y to B, and capital gains tax will be zero even though effective ownership of X did change !!! This is contrary to nexus rule aka section-9 of Indian Income Tax act . But SoMoKe have supported this High Court ruling.
.
Solution I propose : if shares are owned by a foreign entity, then every 5 year , a tax should be applies on it which should be 30% of increase in its value i.e. all share owned by foreign entities should be subject to deemed capital gains tax.
.
Otherwise, as per SoMoKe's stand, the defactor rule will be "gora shall pay no capital gains taxes ; desi must pay capital gains taxes" !! SoMoke andbhagats are also supporting this defacto stand.
