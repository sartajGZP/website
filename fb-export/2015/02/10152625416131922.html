Dear all,
.
When you post a status with link, please remove the box containing picture or link !!
.
Why?
.
Because when you keep that box and if someone shares your status, then ONLY link gets pasted on his wall and your text isnt copied !!
.
But if you remove the picture and remove the box containing link before you click "post" , and then if somone shares your post , then your text gets copied .
