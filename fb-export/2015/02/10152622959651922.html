How SoMoKe's coming GST may reduce income of small businesses
.
The GST will be 25% (*) . And there will be two types of businesses --- one which will pay 2% on lumpsum revenue . The revenue must be below Rs 50 lakhs or some such limit.
.
The small trader doesnt get GST he paid as credit. And the GST of 2% he charged cannot be taken as input credit. .
.
So business of small traders will decrease. Let me explain how.
.
Say manufacturer M is selling air conditioner at Rs 20000 . His bill will be
.
Price = Rs 20000
GST = Rs 5000 (25%)
Total = Rs 25000
.
Say a large retailer LR sells same ac as
.
Price = Rs 21000
GST = Rs 5250
Total = Rs 26250
.
Now consider a small retailer SR.. Say he decides to sell ac at zero profit (I want to show that even at zero profit, he will lose customers to big retailer)
.
Price he paid = Rs 25000 (because he cant deduct GST of Rs 5000)
GST = 2% = Rs 500
Total = Rs 25500
.
So his price is lesser than LR = Large retailer.
,
But a large business LB will buy from LR and NOT SR. Why?
.
WHY ???
.
