The most important reason why NaMo had decided to put his name on the suit was to take the focus of people AWAY from the Indo-US nuclear deal. Basically, he reduced the damage on himself by taking focus away from something more damaging to something less damaging
.
Indk-US nuclear deal says that GoI or people of India, will bear the cost of damage in case euipment supplied or plants run by US companies create a mega-nuclear-disaster. This was a complete surrender of Indian interest before US interests. If this deal had got public focus , then it would have created a huge damage to reputation of NaMo.
.
So NaMo decided to wear suit with his name all over. He knew that this would create a huge controversy and eclipse and completely cover Indo-US nuclear deal. and paidmedia was also paid to cover the suit and hide the details of Indo-US nuclear deal.
.
As an analogy, consider an organism trying to escape from predator which is chasing it. That organism will drop its tail and does damage to itself. But the predator will get occpuied with tail, and the organism will walk away . Basically, act of taking small damage is to evade the act of larger damage.
.
Same way, NaMo created "name on the suit" controversy to cover the Indo-US-nuclear-deal-surrender controversy.
.
And later Virani story and Rs 4.31 cr auction were placed to furthet reduce the damages.
.
All in India, the total surrender of Indian interest got defocused and that was huge loss to India.
