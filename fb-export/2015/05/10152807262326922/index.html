Why many rich "RIGHTLY" feel they should own Aston Martin --- its question of safety , a basic necessity
.
If Nobel Prize winner John Nash was in Aston Martin , very likely, he would have been alive !!! I am not sure if he was wealthy enough to buy Aston Martin and also afford a chauffeur . But if he was so wealthy , then he made a poor economic choice by NOT buying Aston Martin. The good economists fell for common "it cant happen with me so soon" fallacy common with common men across world across time.
.
( to see how robust Aston Martin is, pls see http://www.forbes.com/sites/naazneenkarmali/2014/01/02/the-curious-incident-of-mukesh-ambanis-aston-martin-in-the-night-time/ )
.
Aston Martin is much safer when two cars collide.
.
The pecking order on street is ---
.
BWM beats normal car
.
Audi beats BMW
.
Aston Martin beats Audi
.
Hummer beats Aston Martin
.
So as road rage and rampant rash driving on Indian road increases , more and more rich see Aston Martin as "basic necessity" necessary for survival. I am NOT kidding. When I came from USA in 1999, my parents used to tell me "take car , dont buy two wheelers because roads are very unsafe". And now a days, many rich tell their kids "dont drive Honda , take BMW , roads are very unsafe" !! And soon they will upgrade to "dont drive BMW , take Aston Martin , roads are very unsafe".
.
In India, if you own a car, and are rich enough to buy a judge, then you dont need to press brakes when you see a pedestarian or bicycle or two wheeler. He is supposed to run / drive faster. But if you have normal car and opponent has BMW, you better press brakes or else BMW will smash you.
.
And if you have BMW , and have money to buy judge, and you see normal car, then you dont need to press brakes. The normal car owner is supported to stay away or get out fast.
.
But if you have BMW and opponent has Audi, then you have to press brakes and let him pass.
.
And if you have Aston Martin and opponent as Audi , and you have money to buy judge, then you dont need to press brakes. The opponent has to worry.
.
So Aston Martin increases safety, as well enables you to drive recklessly. So many rich in India RIGHTLY see Aston Martin as basic necessity.
.
