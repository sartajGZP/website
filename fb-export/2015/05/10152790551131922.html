Its time we voters of India order MPs via SMS , to shift capital of India to some central place in Madhya Pradesh
.
Delhi was "central" place when Afghanistan and Pakistan were part of India. That is no longer the case. So Delhi isnt "central" anymore.
.
Voters of India should rent 200 sqkm of land from MP , and rant equal to 3% of AVERAGE market value of urban land in top-5 metro cities should be paid to voters of MP every year. And the rent should be colletected by taxes on all voters of India. .
(eg say 1 sqm of land in top-5 metro city is worth Rs 100,000 , then 1 sqkm would be worth (1000 * 1000) sqm * Rs 100,000 /sqm = Rs 10,000 crore, And 200 sqkm would be worth Rs 200,000 core. And 3% would be Rs 6000 crore per year. That much tax will be collected from people of India, and distributed equally amongst people of MP. Of course , with wealth tax etc, land rent will fall to below Rs 10000 sqm even in urban areas and so rent will NOT even cross Rs 1000 crore / year).
.
Is 200 sqkm enough? Washington DC is 180 sqkm. So if USA can manage its Union Govt in 176 sqkm , so can we.
.
The Union Govt should use land ONLY for staffing of employees , and lease it to those who provide facilities. And no one can purchase land there. And upon retirement , the staff will have to leave.
.
After capital moves, Delhi can remain as a state or better be divided into two parts --- western part can be merged with haryana and Eastern part can be merged with UP.
