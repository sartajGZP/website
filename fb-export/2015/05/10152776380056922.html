Re : My views on collection of contributions for spreading RTR information
.
Other than newspaper advt , I oppose gathering contributions for any activity to spread information on RTR law-drafts
.
Newspaper advt is huge money. And so one person may not be able to contribute. But all other activities, that I can think of , funding is small. And 2-5 persons who personally know each other and meet each other often can contribute and fully fund that activity.
.
eg consider distributing say 5000 12 page pampglets. The of printing wll be cost will be around Rs 10000 . This can be done by 1-5 persons and so doenst need large scale contribution gathering.
.
Likewise, all activities I could think of, except newspaper advt, can be done in groups of 1-5 or at most 10 persons. Only newspaper advt is huge.
.
