Gyaani tota (knowledgeble parrot ) and Importance of war
.
Once a highly knowledgeable person had a parrot. The knowledgeable person used to give sermons on philosophy and parrot used to listen.
.
One day, the cage was left open, and the parrot flew away. Other parrots included him in their herd. The parrot recited the sermons on philosophy and other parrots were greatly impressed. Soon, the parrot became known as "gyaani parrot" and became most respectable member amongst parrots. The gyaani parrot would recite sermons and other parrots would bring the best food they could. Days and weeks went on.
.
One day a cat strolled , and parrots started flying away. The gyaani parrot said "see , this cat is all maya and all mithya . so dont be worried and dont fly away". The other parrots screamed loudly but gyaani parrot refused to fly away.
.
The cat ate the gyaani parrot.
.
Moral of the story is ------ all gyaan is finally tested when real war starts

And there will always be real war.

No mercy. No exception.

And other than war,world knows NO other test to know which gyaan is less bad. Intuition can help, But finally grading will be done by war..
