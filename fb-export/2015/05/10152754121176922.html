My take --- less than 2%
.
I looked at some 100 friends of mine chosen at random from 4500+ friends and looked at their first 3-4 status. So I looked at some 300-400 status and less than 10 were related to national problems.
.
IOW, of all LEISURE hours one has, even in that lesiure hours, over 98% goes for self publicity , entertainment etc and less than 2% goes for society.
.
And this is true for people of ALL countries, not just India.
.
The reason is rational. Yes, the reason is rational.
.
Entertainment and self publicity is short term gain and all of it goes to oneself. While in spending time on national causes, there is no short term gain, and long term gain, if any gets divided over millions and billions.

Lets say 10000 voters in India or anywhere are chosen at random. And they are given 10 hours of time and 1000 hours of video on national cuases, entertainment etc . Because if each viewer is asked to decide which video others should watch, then he will give higher rating to videos of national causes and lesser to entertainment.

IOW, while most persons want to maximize their gains by spending more time on entertainment and less on national causes, they want to further maximize gains by trying to ensure that others spend higher time on national causes and lesser on entertainment !!
