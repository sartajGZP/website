A key task is to inform 85 crore voters of India about law-drafts such as RTR / Jury etc . Newspaper advertisements and pamphlets, and later TV-advertisemens are MUST to reach voters. The costs to inform one voter , not counting activist's time --- by my estimate, is at least about Rs 100 per voter.
.
And if activist's time is added, it would cross Rs 300 per voter. And this does NOT count voter's time.
.
Morally speaking, the activists too need to be paid for their time. And even that is ignored, we still need Rs 100 / per voter * 85 crore voters = Rs 8500 to inform all voters about these law-draft.
.
Lets say some 10 lakh activists agree to bear this cost. Then cost per activist is coming as Rs 8500 crore / 10 lakh = Rs 85000 per activist. This is quite high. Plus cost of their time will make it much higher.
.
Say we are able to create 1 crore supporters who cant spare time, but are wiling to spend money. Then cost per supporter is Rs 8500 , which isnt low, but still high.
.
But question is --- how collect money from supporters. And bigger question is ---- what to they get , if RTR etc does come and does benefit all 85 crore voters.
.
Some one crore supporters paying Rs 8500 each , and all get equal benefit, and the one crore who paid get nothing more ---- is unethical situation.
.
See if you can find any solution to ensure some LEGAL / CONTRACTUAL way that those who pay Rs 8500 crores for RTR advertisements etc do get paid back , if RTR comes and benefits all 85 crore voters. Otherwise, convincing 1 crore people to contribute wikll be difficult.
