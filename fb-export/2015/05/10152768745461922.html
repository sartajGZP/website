How law-draft like Preferential voting system reduce mess like Jayalalitha
.
Number of fanatic supporters of JL are less than 1%, which is high. And number of non-fanatic supporters are very high --- say 35% to 55% of TN voter population. Why do such corrupt leaders have such following?
.
The reason is --- many rightly or wrongly see other WINNABLE alternative as worse. And they see better alternatives as NON-WINNABLE
.
Solution?
.
Solution I propose is Preferential Voting System
.
Say voter has 5 preferences to give.
.
In preferential voting system, the voter can give first 4 preferences to the alternatives he thinks are least bad --- be winnable or non-winnable.
.
And he can give last preference to the winnable alternative he thinks is least bad.
,
So when results comes, the NON-WINNABLE alternative will get highlighted first few rounds, though they may lose in final round.
.
But in next election, they will get more votes and eventually become winnable and thus may get ,more votes, and may win.
.
IOW, Preferential Voting System reduce the problem of oligopoly of the leaders.
.
