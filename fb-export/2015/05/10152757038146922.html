‘News Traders Night’ at Jaitley’s residence, Modi joins secret party to manage top guns
.
... Union Information Minister Arun Jaitley throws a lavish party for top journalists at his residence. ... The star attraction at the party was Prime Minister Narendra Modi, .... At the Press Club of India and National Media Centre Jaitley’s hush hush party was dubbed as ‘News Traders Night’ as Modi often terms New Delhi media as ‘news traders’. A list of select journalist which included Arnab Goswami and several editors from print media were invited by Jaitley. Interestingly some of the editors who were not part of Jaitley’s coterie were cautioned not to publish or report any discussion emerging out of the secret meeting.
.
Prime Minister Narendra Modi often uses the term ‘News Traders’ for journalists who neither have any morals nor any journalistic principles.
