Tcp-India law-draft
.
[ The statements inside square brackets, such as this one, are guildelines to citizens, officers, Jurors but they are not part of procedure.]
.
[ This TCP-India law-draft has 3 clauses. Each clause is an instruction to officers / citizens. Each clause has 3 parts (a) clause-number or clause-label (b) list of officers etc inside round brackets , who are supposed to execute the instruction OR list of persons for whom declaration is stated (c) the instruction / declaration itself ]
.
---
.
Tcp.India-1. ( instruction to DC = District Collector or Tahasildar he deputes )
.
The President hereby orders DC that : if a woman voter or dalit voter or senior citizen voter or poor voter or farmer voter or any citizen-voter in his district submits a Right to Information application or complaint against corruption or any change proposed to this law-draft or any new law-draft or any affidavit to the Collector and requests that submitted papers be put on the website of Prime Minister, then the Collector or his designated Tahasildar will issue a serial number and put that affidavit on the website of the Prime Minister. The fee will be Rs 20 per page.
.
---
.
TCP-India-2. ( instructions to Talati aka Patwari aka Village Officer or his clerk , and guidelines to Collector / PM )
.
(TCP-India-2.1) if any citizen-voter comes with voter ID, and specifies Yes-No on an RTI application, complaint or any affidavit submitted in the above cluase-1, then the Patwari will enter citizen's Yes-No on the PM's website with his voter-ID and give a printed receipt to the citizen. The fee will be Rs 3 .
.
(TCP-India-2.2) The Patwari will also allow citizen to change his Yes-No for Rs 3 fee.
.
(TCP-India-2.3) The fees will be Re 1 for BPL card holder
.
(TCP-India-2.4) The DC may create system of sending SMS feedback to voters
.
(TCP-India-2.5) The DC may create a system of taking finger-print and picture of the voter and putting it on the receipt.
.
(TCP-India-2.6) The PM may create a system where by citizens can register YES/NO via ATM using ATM-cards for a harge 50 paise
.
(TCP-India-2.7) The PM may enable citizens to register YES/NO via SMS for 5 paise
.
(TCP-India-2.8) Further, the Collector may design and propose a system to collect Yes-No in clause-2 over SMS, and implement after approval of the PM.
.
---
.
TCP-India-3. ( guideline to all Citizens, Officers, Ministers , judges )
.
This TcpIndia law-draft is not a referendum procedure. The Yes-No counts will not be binding on PM, Ministers, officers, judges etc. However, if over 43 crore women voters, dalit voters, senior citizen voters, poor voters, farmer voters or ANY 43 crore citizen-voters register Yes on a given affidavit, then the PM may or need not give reply and may or need not take necessary action on the affidavit. If over 43 crore citizen-voters register YES, then the PM may or need not print the law-draft proposed in the affidavit in Gazette or the PM may or need not resign. The PM's decision will be final.
.
