Simplified TCP
.
I propose following GN draft. It has only following clauses
.
(1) If any voter comes to office of Collector or Tahsildaar / Talati / clerks designated by the Collector , and wishes to register his public key i.e. string of length 256 characters or less, the deputed officer will register the key and give receipt. The fee will be Rs 5
,
(2) The voter can change his public key any day for fee of Rs 5
.
(3) The collector will publish (voterid , name, publickey) list
.
This is sufficient to implement TCP and also ALL law-drafts we have proposed without any further help from Govt. IOW, using above, activists will be able to create a mass movement to force leaders to print TCP-draft, RTR-drafts and all drafts
.
