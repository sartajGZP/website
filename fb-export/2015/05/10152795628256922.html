Thieves stole jewellery and destroyed all land ownership records papers and FD papers etc !!!
.
A distant neighbor of mine suffered a theft some 2 months back. Today , I came to know, that thieves broke it, stole some jewellry cash worth some Rs 2 lakhs. But the bigger damage the thieves did was that the thieves destroyed ALL papers they found in home !! They destroyed all FD papers , property papers, bank passbooks, chequebooks etc. They also broke his PC and destroyed his hard disks !!!
.
I suggest to all to keep copy of ALL papers you have in bank locker , so at least you have a copy. And keep ORIGINAL papers at back lockers OR if you keep at home, then keep in place where thieves wont suspect.
.
The solution at society level is to keep ALL property records in electronic form on torrents. Torrents are practically indestructible.
.
As per the victim, there is no easy solution now.
