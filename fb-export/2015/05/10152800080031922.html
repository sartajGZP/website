Too late , too too late --- but better late then never ---- we should order MPs via SMS to start Rs 2000 / mo pension to those who were imprisoned under MISA during Emergency. Devi Indira Amma was forced to lift emergency due to their sacrifice.
.
If they all had worn bengals (chudia) and remained with those who didnt risk imprisonment, emeregency would have lasted for many many more years and Indian may have become another Pakistan.
.
Some 5 lakh persons risked imprisonment in 1975-1977 . They were imprisoned because they opposed Devi Indira Amma or opposed local govt officers for their corruption etc. Many were even forced to go under vasectomies.
.
We all benefitted due to them. The final decision of Devi Indira Amma to lift Emergency came because of those who risked the imprisonment.
.
We all benefitted due to their sacrifice.
.
