Victory of "order your MP via SMS" campaign ---- govt releases census-2011 religion data, but delay shows that data could have been tempered .
.
Those who are reading my wall know that we at Right to Recall Group started "order your MP via SMS to release census-2011 religion / language data " campaign in apr-2012 . SoMoKe-andhbhagats as well as CoBhApSwaRa-workers opposes this proposal. And back then, almost all BJP leaders supported Sonia's proposal to suppress the religion data.
.
Preliminary data was available in mar-2012. But Congress, BJP and AAP (then IAC) leaders decied to suppress it. Then full data was ready by jan-2014 - some one year ago. But all leaders decided to supress it. There is NO record of any BJP leader including NaMo giving newspaper advt across India asking voters to demand MPs to demand this data. There were some token statements made in Parliament to fool activists and andhbhagats. But that was all.
.
We started "order you MP via SMS to release census-2011 religion \language data " campaign on FB, pamphlets etc. Some 20000 voters across India sent SMS . And finally, the data is PARTIALLY out !!!!
.
But the delay of over 2 years shows that data could have been tempered.
.
