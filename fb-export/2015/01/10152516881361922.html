BST-workers refuse to file PIL / FIR against movie PK , refuse to order MP to ban PK, and then demand arrest of AmKh on streers !! Well, what do you call a lawyer who talks on street and remains silent in court ?
.
Most BST-workers are NaMo-andbhagats, and NaMo has been staunch fan of AmKh. So when PK came, BST workers , like all NaMo-andhbhagats were supporting PK and asking all to see PK ASAP. However , as "order your MP via SMS to ban PK" campaign picked up, BST-workers got shocked. Then some 10 days after PK was released BST-workers asked Swami Ramdevji to oppose PK.
.
But as always, BST-workers misguided Swami Ramdevji. BST-workers refused to file FIR or PIL against PK. BST-workers also refused to order MPs via SMS to ban PK . BST-workers are also opposing Right to Recall Censor Board Chairman and not even one BST worker has ordered his MP via SMS to print RTR censor board chairman draft in Gazette !!!
.
So all in all, BST-workers only gave silent support to the movie PK. The BST-workers asked Swami Ramdevji to demand arrest of AmKh only too fool activists and create a show that BST is indeed doing something to stop PK and AmKh !!!
.
All in all, my point to all activists ---- BST-workers are like lawyers who would give elegant arguments on streets and keep quite in courtroom !!!
Please dont waste time with BST-workers.
