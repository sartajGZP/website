Chandra Kumar Bose:

How can the Government of India & the State Governments suppress & withhold documents pertaining to Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose- the Chief architect of India's freedom movement? If Netaji's INA's final onslaught at the Imphal frontier & subsequently the revolt in the British Armed Forces due to the INA Trials had not taken place- India would have still been under British domination- & there would not have been any Government in independent India. Therefore, the present Government both at the Centre & the States must realise it is due to the INA & Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose they are in Government in free India. Little they can do to honour Netaji & the INA warriors is to #declassifynetajifiles & unravel the mystery of his disappearance. Jai Hind!
