Why are Sakshi Maharaj ,Yogi Adityanath and ALL BJP-MPs opposing referendum / voting on 2-child-law-draft? Why do BJP-MPs oppose disclosure of census-2011 religion data?
.
In order to hide the FACT that BJP-MPs dont want to print 2 child law-draft, BJP-apex ordered Sakshi Maharaj to make nonsensical statements like "Hindu women should have 4 kids". And what we need is 2-child-law draft (3 for tribals, and an extra child for those who have 2 daughters). We dont need nonsense like 4 kids and 5 kids.
.
=======
.
We all ignored population / demographic problem till 1980. It was Devi Indira Amma who first focused on this problem in 1975, but sadly, Sanjay Gundaa ruined it due to whimsical ham handed forceful vasectomies. In 1980 , the focus on problem came back.
.
But due to Saudi Arabian bribes, Congress-MPs opposed 2 child law-draft. But the question is --- why have BJP-MPs too been opposing 2 child-law draft since 1980? Why didnt BJP MPs ever demanded referendum on 2-child law-draft ? The only excuse that came from BJP-MPs was --- we dont have enough MPa to pass this law. Well, to demand referendum, one doesnt need even one MP.
.
And today, AAP is also opposing 2-child-law-draft and also opposing referendum on 2-child-law-draft.
.
The reason was NEVER vote bank. Those who have 2 kids are in majority and those who have more than 2 kids are far far below 50%. Any referendum on 2-child-law will fetch over 65% to 75% votes. So votes are non-issue.
.
The reason why most Congress-MPs opposed 2-child-law was bribes coming from Saudi Arabia and NOT muslim votes. And the ditto same reason holds true for BJP-MPs as well AAP-MPs.
.
For decades , BJP-leaders fooled its voters and its activists by saying "see, we dont want to pass 2 child law , but we dont have majority in Parliament". Now BJP MPs have majority in Parliament join session. So this excuse will NOT apply. So to divert the whole agenda, the BJP-apex is ordering Sakshi Maharaj et al to make useless staments like "every Hindu women should have 4 children". If every women or every Hindu owmen start having even 3 children, then population growth will take to extreme scarcity of water, food, fuel and everything. We will be left with no money to make any industrial goods , weapons etc and will be ruined by foreign Militaries.
.
The new fashion of 1 child is deplortable, and solution I propose is 5% tax break to those who have 2 child , with cap of Rs 30 lakh per year with total cap of Rs 2 crore.
.
And same Shakshi Maharj supports supression of census-2011 relegion / language data !!
.
So dear all, please wake and smell coffee. These CoBhAp-MPs are thugging us left , right and centre. All these MPs , including AAP-MPs are opposing 2 child law-draft and they are also opposing release of census-2011 religion data. And cover their failures, we have demagogy like "have 4 children" and "have 5 children ".
.
Solution I propose is that --- please order YOUR MP via SMS to print 2 child-law-draft in GAzette. Please ensure that SMS has #TwoChildLaw hashtag. The SMS should have URL, but I havent been able to finish the draft and so I am unable to put the URL. So please use hashtag for now.

All in all CoBhAp-MPs like Sakshi Maharaj, by supporting suppression of religion data census-2011 . Pls dont waste time on them.
