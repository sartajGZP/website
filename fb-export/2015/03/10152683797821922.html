International coal prices ( pls google on "international coal prices" ) = Rs 4000 per tonne to Rs 6000 /tonne (prices vary day to day). This is price at the post of source country
,
In India, same price can be obtained at the mine.
.
But most auction have fetched between Rs 800 / tonne and Rs 2000 / tonne. There are some outliers like Rs 3500 / tonne , but there are also some outliers like Rs 108 / tonne.
.
Why were bids so low compared to international prices?
.
(a) Small companies were not allowed to bit by making qualifying conditions cumbersome and also putting restrictions on capital the company must have.
.
(b) In many cases, restrictions were put that company must be power company or steel company !!!
.
(c) In many cases, restrictions were put that company must have capaibility and experience in mining.
.
(d) open auctions were NOT held ; e-auction were held which enables server owners to rigg bidding process and yet public cant see. If auction is made in public (like cricketers are auctions) , then manipulation still may happen, but whole world will see !!
.
Solution :-
.
1. Right to Recall coal minister --- so that we can get a coal minister who conductions auction in open and doesnt put useless conditions.
.

.

Create uniform coal market across India i.e. Railways should take coal from railway station X to railway station Y at neat flat rate IRRESPECTIVE of distance , i.e distance should pay minor role in frieght of coal. 3. The bidding should be on cost of mining, and lowest cost giving contractor should be given mine
