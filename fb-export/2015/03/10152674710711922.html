How Go-Raksha reduced / eliminated slavery in ancient India --- bullocks provided plentiful cheaper labor
.
India was first country in world to eliminate slavery. And one reason was --- bullock labor became plentiful due to go-raksha that was susdized by rich via donations.
.
In ancient Greece \ Rome , some 60% to 80% population was slave and slavery lasted till 300 AD. Arab / Turk / Moguls had slavery for long long time. China too had slavery till 1600 AD. But slavery ended in ancient India loooong back - way before villages started. It appears that large scale slavery never came in Indian sub-continent. One reason was go-raksha which was subsidized by donations from rich.
.
Agriculture required huge amount of labor. And slave is intelligent than a bullock. And a slave is fed by his parents till he is 14 to 16 years old, and then he is enslaved in slave raids and then sold. All this makes a slave cheaper than bullock !! Which is why most societies outside India preferred to keep bullocks for meat rather then use them for labor.

But in India, go-pooja and related "daan" subsidized cows as well as bullocks. And slaughter became sinful. So bullocks became plentiful. And so there was almost no need for human slaves !!
