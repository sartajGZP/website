Cow slaughter question was more than settled in 1947. All those, be Hindus or non-Hindus, who wanted to eat cow meat were given separate land, by those who wanted to protect cows. This was only so that those who want to eat cow meat can eat all the cow meat they want.
.
Those who didnt want cows to be slaughtered in their land gave them that piece of land for this reason only.
.
So those who want to slaughter cows are welcome to go westward , as per terms and conditions of 1947.
