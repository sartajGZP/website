In which case identity theft / identity tempering will be easier --- when all identity data is made public or when it is semi-confidential?
.
100% confidentiality isnt possible in identity data because Govt staff will always have information .
.
Idenity data means --- name, parents' name, pan-id, aadhar-id, voter-id, address, parents' names. past addresses , photo, finger print etc
.
identity theft means thief-X managing to pretend that he is A, and managed to steal assets etc belonging to A
.
identity tempering means some hacker or insider changing the data like finger print or photo etc in A's record inside System or Govt , so that now A is no longer to claim his own asset using his finger print. This can be sabotage or theft.

An extreme case is --- some outsiders or some insiders change the data of lakhs of individuals inside system to cause massive sabotage.
.
So we have following threats
.
1. A thief with no access to system's internal working as imposter
.
2. A hacker gets access to system's internal and causes sabotage or steals identity data
.
3. An insider sabotage or steals identity data
.
4. Outsides , hackers or non-hackers, or insiders due a massive sabotage.
.
In which case will it be easier --- when all idenity data is public or when it is semi-confidential?
