CBSE board exam 2015: Board question papers directly from the book
.
Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) conducted Mathematics board exam for Class X students on March 16. The students claimed that the exam was very tough and lengthy. Some students said that they could not even finish the examination on time as the paper was very lengthy.

CBSE affirmed that the Mathematics question paper was directly from the book. The students say that the CBSE board exams are tougher than the former years, while many teachers said that board exam question papers are easier than the mock tests and practice papers.
