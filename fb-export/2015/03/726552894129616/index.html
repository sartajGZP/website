But slavery ended in ancient India loooong back - way before villages started. It appears that large scale slavery never came in Indian sub-continent. One reason was go-raksha which was subsidized by donations from rich.
.
Agriculture required huge amount of labor. And slave is intelligent than a bullock. Which is why most societies outside India preferred to keep bullocks for meat rather then use them for labor.
