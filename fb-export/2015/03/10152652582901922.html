Next mine will have rider attached that miner must have steel company !! A steel company which far away will not be able to bid, as it has now way to move coal from that mine to its steel factory. So a steel factory near by will bid pittance, and still get the mine !!

Suffices to say that it will get coal only from first mine and will leave second mine unused !!! Or worse, it will get coal from second mine and show on paper that coal was obtained from first mine !! So all revenue forecast that are being made are useless.

.

Solution?
.
The first solution is Right to Recall Coal Minister. This is must to ensure that apex person doesnt do cheating the way we saw in this auction.
.
Next , the mining should be delinked from users. The miners should have been asked to give "tell the cost that you would need to mine X tonne of coal and take it to nearest railway station". And the miner which gives lower cost should be given mine. Each buyer should be required to buy at same market price, depending on quality of coal only. And the difference should be equally divided amongst citizens of India. IOW, each buyer gets coal at almost same price , except that bulk buyer may get at say 10% to 20% lower price because transport costs for bulk buyer per tonne is lesser.
