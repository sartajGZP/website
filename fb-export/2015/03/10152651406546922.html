Distancing from useless criticism against wrong persons
.
Many times , "moral" issues raised against wrong persons are spurious . In such case, one should focus ONLY on relevent issues against the wrong persons and ignore the spurious issue.
.
ArKe is fundemantally dishonest. That is evident from the fact that it has been 4 years since he talked about RTR-draft in apr-2011 and no draft is on his website. We should focus on that relevant issue only.
.
And we should not let spurious issue like cutting deals with Congress MLAs take much space.
.
Without cutting deals with MLAs / MPs of other parties , running govt is impossible under existing laws for any PM / CM , unless he has complete majority. Solution is law-drafts like direct election of PM/CM , RTR-PM which serves as de-facto direct election of PM, RTR-CM , which also serves as de-facto direct election of PM, RTR-MLA so that deal cutting MLA can be expelled, RTR-MP so that deal cutting MP can be expelled , and TCP in case PM/CM cant convince MLA/MP to pass a law etc etc.
.
And the REAL issue is that SoMoKe have always opposed RTR-PM, RTR-CM, RTR-MP, RTR-MLA and also TCP .
.
In absence of above law-draft, deal cutting with MLAs of other parties or so called horse trading is bound to happen.
.
So issue we should focus on is --- the FACT that SoMoKe oppose systemic solution to horse trading problem. Not specific instance of horse trading, because that is something SoMoKe and all have done and will keep doing till law-drafts needed to end horse trading are printed in gazette.
.
