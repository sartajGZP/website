Say you are in Jury. Then how many months of prison sentence would YOU impose in each case. 0 months means = no imprisonment = acquittal
.
1. B = Borrower has borrowed Rs 1,00,000 from L = Lender on say 1-feb-2014 . And he had promised to return in writing on 1-feb-2015 i.e. 12 months later along with say 15% per year interest. And all search show that B has no money to pay back. L demands prison sentence of upto 24 months.
.
And say you are in Jury. Then how many months of prison sentence would you impose? (0 --- means no prison sentence = acquittal )
.
My answer --- 0
.
=====
.
2. B = Borrower has borrowed Rs 1,00,000 from L = Lender on say 1-feb-2014 . And he had promised in writing to return the money on 1-feb-2015 i.e. 12 months later along with say 15% per year interest.

The cheque bounces. And all search show that B has no money to pay L . And say you are in Jury. Then how many months of prison sentence would you impose? (0 --- means no prison sentence = acquittal )

My answer --- 0 months
