Please dont allow your friend to tag you.
.
When X tags you, his post will go on newsfeed of all your friends. And if your friends dont like that post, chances are high that they might unfriend you !!
.
So please dont allow people to tag you.
.
You MUST activate tag filtering on fb.
.
You can unfriend people who tag you, which is what I do.
