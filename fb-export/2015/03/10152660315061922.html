A useful historical fact --- by 1870 , London School Board members and Chairman were all elected , and elected by citizens across London. Back then, MPs were elected by only 10% citizens i.e. only 10% had voting rights in election of MPs. But for school board members, voting was with almost 100% citizens.
.
Also " Firstly, the board's election of 1870 was polled by secret ballot, being the first large-scale election to use this approach in Britain. Secondly, the cumulative voting system gave electors a number of votes equal to the number of seats in the division in which they were voting. The elector could use up as many of their votes on a single candidate as they wished, which meant that minority interests often found representation."
.
see wiki for http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_Board for more
.
This vastly improved education.
.
USA went one step further and also enacted Right to recall over District Education Officer. And so education in USA became better than education than UK.
