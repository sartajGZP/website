Dear Recallists , dear all,
.
There is near zero activism against FDI and corruption in judges issues and huge amount of "activism" on land acquisition issue, goraksha issue, crimes issue , organic farming issue , black money, lokpal issue etc etc. This is HUGE inversion of priroity !!

.
So while we need to write about law-draft on all issues, it is necessary that we work to make all focus on the the two MOST important issus --- rising dominance of foreigners due to FDI etc and rising and rising nepotism / corruption in judges. And first issue is more important than second.
.
Also, we need to convince all that focus should on law-drafts and NOT on tall leaders, the parties, their adhoc policies , their so called ideologies, problem descriptions etc not of much value.
