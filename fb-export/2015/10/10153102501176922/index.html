How bank-owners across world rob the citizens of their respective countries
.
Almost all countries have law-drafts that if a non-banker owns a property, then he has to pay wealth tax, municipal taxes, inheritance taxes etc. But if a bank-owner owns property via bank, then these taxes are practically nil. So bank owners can hoard property for free. This increases their ability to gain from property price rises, at the cost of non-banker i.e. commons.
.
Such law-drafts exist because bank-owners have bribed the apex law-makers to print such laws.
.
Now in USA, there are over 2000 counties (districts) and each county has its own administration. Bank owners cant bribe so many country law-makers. So in USA, the bank owners FORCED county law-makers to print such law-drafts, How did they manage to force county law-makers?
.
Bankers printed megatons of dollars from thin air. So prices of everything went up. So salary had to be raised. So counties balance sheet went into deep deficit. It will be country specific as each country has too much variation in their tax law-drafts.

So bank-owners will come at par with non-bank-owners. The full wealth tax law-draft is at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/posts/10152686291831922 .
