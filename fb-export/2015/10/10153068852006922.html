Dadri violence --- how corruption / inefficency of policenen / judges promote private violence. Solution? print law-drafts to reduce corruption / inefficiency in policemen / judges. Otherwise, all rona-dhona is just timepass
.
Whether it is suspected case of cow slaughter or whether it is traffic violence, there is a high propensity for private violence in India. Whereas in USA etc, people would wait for policemen / courts to act.
.
The desire to punish guilty is natural. And so is desire NOT to punish innocent.
.
The reason why people in India resort to private violence is because too many people RIGHTLY suspect that policemen / judges will take bribes and let the person walk away. In USA, most people have faith in policemen / judges. And so they dont resort to private violence. Exceptions exist --- there are times when section of people who didnt have faith in policemen / courts did resort to private violence. But its rare compared to India.
.
Solution?
.
IMO, solution is that activists should focus on publicizing law-drafts that can reduce corruption / inefficiency in policemen / courts. The law-drafts I propose are RTR-judges, RTR-police-chiefs, JurySys, narcotest in public if Jurors deem necessary etc. Pls send links to these drafts via SMS to MP as an order. The links are in first comment of this post. .
