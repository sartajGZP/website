When elections are held in 2-3 states / region , the election commission puts different voting dates , but counting date of all regions is AFTER voting is over in ALL regions.
.
But Gujarat State Election Commission will declare results of Municipal Corporation election on 26-nov-2015 , and then hold voting in Gujarat small cities and villages on 29-nov-2015 !!! .
.
The reason why normally results are declared after voting ends in all regions is to ensure that results of one place dont impact voter preferences of other places.
.
But Gujarat State Election Commissioner wants results of 6 cities to have MAXIMAL impact on voters' decisions in small cities and villages. Which is why results of 6 cities will be declared on 26-nov-2015 , and then voting will be held on 29-nov-2015
.
