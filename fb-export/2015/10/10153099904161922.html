1. Gujarat State Election Commission put voting date of villages 3 days AFTER result date of big cities. WHY?
.
Normally , when central election commission holds election in say two or three or four states together, then each state will have separate voting date, but vote counting date for all sates will be same. The vote counting will start ONLY after voting has ended in all states. This is to ensure that results of one state doesnt effect voters' preference in other states.
.
-----------
.
Example : consider election held in nov-2013 and dec 2013 in following states. (see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_elections_in_India )
.
Chhattisgadh assembly polls --- voting date -11-nov-2013 , count date - 8-dec-2013
.
Madhya Pradesh poll --- voting date - 25-nov-2013 , count date - 8-dec-2013
.
Rajasthan assembly polls --- voting date - 1-dec-2013 , count date -- 8-dec-2013
.
Delhi assembly elections --- voting date - 4-dec-2013 , count date - 8-dec-2013
.
IOW, counting started ONLY after voting ended in all states.
.
Likewise, one can look any election schedules across past several years. Vote counting is held ONLY after polling is over in all states / regions.
