Instructions to MP ,
.
If you received link to this post via SMS from your voter , then it is an order from your voter , not from the author of this post. The order is to print following resolution in Parliament.
.
==== draft of legislation ===
.
1. The Parliament hereby declares Ahimsamurti Mahatma Subhashchandra Bose as Rashtrapita i.e Father of Nation.
.
2. All Central Govt and State Govt documents printed after this date, and all Central Govt and State Govt officers henceforth will refer to only Mahatma Subhashchandra Bose as Rashtrapita , and not refer anyone else as Rashtrapita.
.
3. All textbooks aided by Centralor State Govt or in boards recognised by Central or State Govt must henceforth mention Mahatma Subhashchandra Bose as Rashtrapita , and not refer anyone else as Rashtrapita.
.
4. If any Central Govt and State Govt Govt officer deliberately refers to anyone else as Rashtrapita , then he may be fined by fine less than or equal to his monthly salary for every offence. And for repeated deliberate references for over 12 times, he will be expelled.
.
5. The resolution will not apply on private citizens who receive no salary or grants from Central or State Govt. The resolution will also not apply on NGOs,.
.
=== end of resolution ==-===
