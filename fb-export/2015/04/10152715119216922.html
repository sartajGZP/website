See http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_knowledge_(logic) and it is DIFFERENT from http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_knowledge .

Problem-1 :
.
(1A) There were 10 mathematicians. A king placed white or black cap on their head. Each can see color of others' caps but not his own. Exactly 4 of them had black cap and 6 had white cap.
.
(1B) The king said to all --- "Exactly one of you have black cap. Those who have black cap should raise their hands at the stroke of the bell". .
.
(1C) The king rang bell once. The king rang bell again. None raised his hands. Then king rang bell 3rd time and still no one raised hands. And then king rang bell 4th time, and all and only those who had black caps raised their hands.
.
So questions are --- (q1a) how did those with black caps concluded that that they have black caps (q1b) How did those with white caps decided that they have white caps? (q1c) why did king had to ask question 4 times, and not 3 or 5 times. Please note --- if your replace numbers 10 and 4 my N and m, then also problem and solution word.

The problems will explains why "everyone knows" is FAR FAR LESS POWERFUL than "everyone knows that everyone knows".
.
And "everyone knows that everyone knows that everyone knows" is more powerful than "everyone knows that everyone knows" .
.
And "everyone knows that everyone knows .... infinite times " with N persons more powerful than "everyone knows that everyone knows .... M times " for any large finite M .
.
=============
.
How does this apply in politics / activism?
.
Many times , there are certain actions that will happen only if "everyone knows that everyone knows" and those actions will NOT happen even if "everyone knows".
.
Example .
.
1. say everyone knows everyone knows that everyone knows .... that "everyone thinks that party-A is corrupt"
.
2. say everyone thinks that "party-B is only slightly less corrupt". But everyone doesnt know that everyone knows that "party-B is only slightly less corrupt".
.
In such case, if paidmedia favors party-B , then paidmedia may manage to convince everyone that "everyone else thinks that party-B is non-corrupt" !!!
.
And if paidmedia opposes party-B , then paidmedia may manage to convince everyone that "everyone thinks that party-B is non-corrupt" !!!
.
IOW, paidmedia cant write against common knowledge. But paidmedia can create illusion that "everyone else thinks A is true" even when "everyone thinks that A is false".
.
In addition, the concept of common knowledge has many many applications.
.
