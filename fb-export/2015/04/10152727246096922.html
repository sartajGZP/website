To avert the plan of Jeev Dayaavaadies , who had voted for BJP, to illegally vandalie meat market in Una , Junagadh, the owners of meat market asked and convinced BJP Minister to inaugurate it !! And BJP Minister did it !!
.
Why did BJP Minister inaugerate meat market building? Couldnt he have just politely declined? Well, here are the reasons.
.
Due to speeches against pink revolution, many jeev dayavaadies were thinking that NaMo will work to reduce slaughter / export of ALL animals. Little did they know that it was just another "Rs 15 lakh" type chunavi jumla.
.
Now new meat market was all ready to go in Una in Junagadh district in Gujarat. The Jeev Dayavaadies there had made plans to vandalize the whole complex to a point that it become unusable, The jeev-dayaa-vaadies were thinking that since they voted for BJP / RSS / VHP en-masse, the BJP / VHP / RSS will elt them vandalize the meat market building and take no police action against them.
.
Thats why, meat market owners asked BJP Minister to inaugurate it --- to give clear signals to jeev daya vaadies that BJP is supports animal slaughter (except cows) and so any attempt to damage the building will be met with strict police action.
.
The Minister could have simply politely refused to inaugurate the building. But that would give mixed signals to jeev dayaa vaadi, and they may think that BJP is opposed to slaughter and so vandalizing building will NOT create any police action against them. So meat market owners did NOT want anyone else but BJP Ministers to inaugurate the building.
.
