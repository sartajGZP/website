.
Talk about ignoring Rs 500 notes and Rs 1000 notes, and then counting every small coins.
.
NaMo's promotes FDI everywhere including paidmedia. And then NaMo talks about checking Fraud Foundation and foreign religious funding etc. It is similar to ignoring Rs 500 notes and Rs 1000 notes, and then counting every small coins.
.
The damage that FDI using their CSR , control over economy , control over employment and paidmedia, will do will be several times damage Fraud Foundation etc do. The Fraud Foundation is more of a nodal agency -- it doesnt have much funds, FF only does surveying / research etc and then gives guidelines using which MNC-owners use their CSR funds, paidmedia and other resources.
.
By allowing FDI, NaMo is handing over one after another sectors of economy to foreigners. And NaMo is also promoting FDI in paidmedia. (if NOT , pls show me laws NaMo printed to end FDI in paidmediamen). And at the same time,. NaMo is opposing Jury System, RTR, wealth tax etc law-drafts that can improve efficiencies of the local manufacturers and businesses. So locals wont be able to reduce their inefficiencies. So one after another sectors will move into the hands of foreigners.
.
In all this pro-FDI scenario, supervising Fraud Foundation etc is nothing but to create an illusion before Hinduvaadies , Nationalists etc that "see, NaMo is doing something, NaMo is doing something" !!

Solution I propose is that activists should work on law-drafts that will end FDI in paidmedia business, other businesses and also reduce inefficiencies of the locals. Which law-drafts can achieve that?
