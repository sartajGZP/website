To dear citizens of India ,
.
This proposed law-draft is to reduce the miseries of farmers who grow sugarcane.
.
The sugarcane growers have to sell sugarcane to sugarmills who often dont pay them for years. Why is this so? The reason is --- oligopoly in sugarmill ownership.. And the oligopoly is because there are licensing requirements imposed by state / central govt Ministers , which stops NEW sugar and gur mills from coming up and forcing existing one to behave well.
.
If all licensing requirements are removed, then more sugar and gur mills will come and competition between sugar mills will grow. And so they will make immediate payments to sugarcane growers
.
The restrictions that govt imposes on creation of sugar mills can be removed , if we citizens by FORCE CMs \ PMs to print a gazette notification to do the same. The draft is given here. As more sugarmills come, the farmers will get better treatments.
.

section-1 : De licensing of Sugar Mills and Gur Mills
.
1.1 Any citizen \ company of India, if it wants to start a gur or sugar mill or wants to trade in sugar or gur, will NOT require to take any permission from any State \ Central authority. He will ONLY be required to register his name , his aadhar , voter of Panid and address at local Tahsildaar's office.
.
1.2. Every person except farmer will be required to disclose the stock of sugar / gur he has at local collector's office. CM may depute officer to make stock disclosure online.
.
1.3 If any voter in that tahsil / district has objection against that sugar mill due to pollution or other reasons, then he may submit application at Collector's office for fee of Rs 20 per page.
.
1.4 If any person wants to support / oppose the objection , then he may file YES / NO by visiting Patwari's office , pay Rs 3 fee . The CM or PM may depute officers to ensure that citizen can take this step via SMS for fee of 50 paise.
.
1.5 If the sugar mill is causing pollution beyond limit or causing other problems, then the collector may take necessary legal action using Jury trial or other legal ways. However, if subsequent notification show Jury Trial as only way, then action will be taken only via Jurors.
.
