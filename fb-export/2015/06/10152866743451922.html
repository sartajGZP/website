As field of medicine progresses , money is becoming bigger and bigger life extender as well as good health provider
.
Heart replacement is possible, and can extend the life by a few months to decade, and can greatly improve quality if life !!! Cost can be several lakhs rupees to crore.
.
Liver / kidney transplant can also also extend life and improve quality if life. Again, costs can go in several lakhs to crores of rupees.
.
And there are cancer treatments that can cure even 3rd stage cancer completely. Again, costs apply.
.
And medical expenses for these advanced treatment is high because many intelligent persons have to work day and night to make the treatment successful. Not just doctors, but engineers have to work to make machines, researchers have to work to make medicines, biochem and biomed people have to work to make medicines and so forth.
.
Moral of the story is --- money is coming close to becoming synonym of "longer better life". Not a a perfect synonym of life --- because even those with 100s or crores rupees will still die.
.
So moral of the status post is --- earn as much as you can, and also protect your earning from getting stolen by black or white thieves. Every penny earned and every penny saved can add a few seconds to your life.
