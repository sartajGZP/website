A friend of mine was living with his parents. He forced his parents to give up gas subsidy . He recently bought his own flat and move there. He applied for a new gas connection without subsidy. .
So least NaMo can do now is that --- he can make rule that gas agencies CANNOT sell gas stoves. And he should also ask traffic policemen to give some discounts to those who have given up gas subsidy. Otherwise, it is double whammy all the the way.
