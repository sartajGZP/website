Dear all,
.
If you are earning say above Rs 300,000 pa , then pls give up Rs 3600 / pa LPG gas subsidy you are entitled for.
.
I am also a bit late , but better later than never.
.
As such, I had inquired 10 years back on how I cna give up gas subsidy on my connection. But back then , there was NO option !! Only option was to buy a commercial connection which is almost 50% more expensive on per kg basis than non-subsidy LPG !!!
.
Also, its time GoI publishes list of "poor need citizens" and "list of non-poor citizens". The first list should have names of ALL those who are getting LPG subsidies and second list should have names of non-poor like myself, and others, who have given up LPG subsidies.
.
Btw , has NaMo ordered all BJP members earning say above Rs 500,000 pa to give up LPG subsidy?
.
WHY NOT?
.
