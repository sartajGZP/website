OBCs who earn over Rs 6 lakhs per year (income of wife plus husband) , their children DO NOT get OBC reservation benefits in education or jobs.
.
Please google on "creamy layer OBC" for more information.
.
There is no such creamy layer restrictions for SC / ST. But for OBCs , it is there.
.
Now many wealthy non-salaried OBCs do manage to get non-creamy layer certificate by
.
(a) confining to income in cash
.
(b) taking cheque income in the names of trusted relatives
.
And same two problems will come if reservation is implemented for economically poor !! Those who have cash incomes in lakhs and crores will easily manage to get "poor" certificates
