======start of proposed draft=====
.
Following lines shall be inserted after section-309 Indian Penal Code
.
Exception to section 309 ----
.
If a person
.
(a) with sane mind, decides not to intake food for the rest of his life
.
(b) and his decision is in accordance with his religious traditions or customs, and
.
(c) his action has no possibility or intention of causing political or social disruption,
.
then his action will not be taken as attempt to suicide and will not be punishable under this section
.
=== end of the proposed draft ===
.
For importance of the 3 points, please see first comment.
