How multi-elections reduce the problem of voter buyout i.e. giving money to voters
.
But another issue is --- Multi-election itself reduces the problem of paying money to voters . HOW?
.
In India, we vote for just 3 posts --- City Corporator , MLA and MP
.
These three posts indirectly decide 100s of appointments --- Mayor, SP, Collector, District Supply Officer, CM, PM, several Ministers etc
.
Multi-elections means means several officers at district / state / national levels are directly elected.
.
eg voters in USA elect City Councilor, Mayor, District Police Chief, District Education Officer, District Public Prosecutor , District Judges and 2-3 more district level officers. They elect MLA , CM, State High Court Judges, and in some states they also elect State Public Prosecutor. At national level, USA has lesser multi-elections. 4 officials are elected --- Senator , Assemblymen, President and VP.
.
So in India, Corporator, MLA, MP can afford to pay Rs 500 to Rs 1000 per voter for every 5 years. Because he stands to make Rs 5000 per voter in that 5 year period by getting power to appoint dozens of officials at District / State / Central levels.
.
So Corporator / MLA / MP can afford to pay such bribes to voters
.
But if each position has direct election, then money gets divided and so bribe to voters will become so small that it wont pay much role.
.
Now my proposal is NOT multi-election. But my proposal is RTR-drafts as in rahulmehta.com / 301.htm (remove spaces of see comment for link). in RTR-drafts I have proposed , each oficer is appointed by CM / PM / Mayor , but can be replaced by voters. So some 50 district level officers / judges, 50-100 state level officers / judges / Ministers and some 50-100 national level officers / mibisters / judges become replaceable by citizens.
.
So control Corporator / MLA / MP have over officers reduces and control voters have increases. So Corporator / MLA / MP simply will NOT be able to collect that much bribes and so will no longer be in position to pay even Rs 50 to voters.
.
This how MULTI-ELECTIONS reduce problem of giving bribe sto voters.
.
