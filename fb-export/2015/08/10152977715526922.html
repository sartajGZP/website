Why I dont put ANY pictures , such as mine or Rajeev Dixitjee or Mahatma Bhagat Singh or Rastrapita Boseji etc on pamphlets of RTR-drafts etc
.
And finally, each one has to decide what he wishes to write.
.
If you notice, ALL my pamphlets are void of pictures of anyone. My election pamphlets have my one passport size simple picture, because people need to see candidate face. But other than that, I dont put my picture.
.
I also dont put pictures of Mahatma Bhagat Singh or Rashtrapita Bose etc. Why not? Or rajeev Dixitjee. Why not?
.
Because many people might think that we are trying to encash on their popularity. Too many fake people, such as as tall leaders across political parties and many activists have misused their pictures.
.
So putting pictures often creates an image that "you guys dont have anything worth saying , and so you are using pictures of great men to grab attention !!".
.
So I dont put any pictures on my pamphlets
.
And finally , it is author's choice.
