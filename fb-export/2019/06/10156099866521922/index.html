Activist, active citizens and freeloaders, and COLLABORATORS !!!
.
During freedom movement. there were 40 crore Indians (India + Pakistan + Bangldesh etc ) , and one may classify them in following groups
.
(1) Collaborators - who were helping British such as Tata1 ( the word Tata1 includes British puppets such as Tata , Birla, Dalmia, Sarabhai, Bajaj, Scindhiam Gaekwads etc of BEFORE year 1947) , Mahaduratma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Ghazi, Lal Bahadur Shastri , Hegadewar, Shyama Prasad Mukherjee etc, IAS, IPS, apex Military officers who were HELPING British. Tata1 etc started as KHABAIES (informers) who were giving information to British which enabled them to SLAUGHTER (yes physically slaughter) the then freedom fighters. And some of these khabaries were promoted and made contractors and then allowed to start factories because importing everything from UK was not possible. And later, all were instructed by British to start SOCIAL SERVICES as coverup.
.
(2) activists -- like Rashtrapita mahatma Subhashchandra Bose, Mahatma Bhagat Singh , Mahatma Udham singh, Mahatma Savarkar etc who eventually forced British to hand over India to Collaborators . and also many grassroot workers of congress/rss/cpi etc. They were probably some 0.2% of population.
.
(3) active citizens - about 2% to 3% i.e. say about 1 crore citizens who were not as active as activists but still assisted activists in many ways by rallies or giving money or giving shelter etc
.
(4) freeloader citizens - the remaining 39 crore were deliberate or unwilling freeloaders. Some 30 crore were so poor and remote and uniformed that they had no way to become active citizens or activists. And remaining 9 crore were DELIBERATE freeloaders, i.e.. they were very much in position and to contribute but they decided to spend time and efforts on career, business, self development, hobbies, entertainment etc. They were not as bad as collberators and many would have never become collaberiots. But they were never willing to become active citiens either
.
And when freedom come, the biggest beneficieries were Collaborators like Tata1, Mahaduratma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Ghazi, Vallabhbhai Patel, Lal Bahadur Shastri , Shyama Prasad Mukherjee
.
And next set of beneficieries were Freeloaders Citizens. , who benefitted more than active citizens
.
And activists were biggest losers ----- Rashtrapita Bose was facing tortures and rotted in cold dark damp prison cell of Siberia Russia, where he was later murdered due to British pressure on Stalin. Many have valid reasons to claim that Mahatma Bhagat singh etc were NOT hanged but were smuggled out of India to UK< where they were tortured for many decades !! And Mahatma Batukeshwar Dutt spent all his life in poverty and so on and on and on.
.
What was even worse is ---- rank and file of journalists and bikau textbook writers gave credit of freedom to collaborators like Tata1, Mahaduratma Gandhi etc. Only after arrival of internet, could most come to know that Tata1, Mahaduratma Gandhi, Hegdewar, Shyama Prasad Mukherjee were just British puppets and they worked more to get freedom fighters slaughtered (literally !!) and delay freedom. Bit all along from 1947 to till 2015, these collberators were given credit of freedom !!!
.
The collaborators like Tata1, Mahaduratma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Ghazi, Vallabhbhai Patel, Lal Bahadur Shastri , Shyama Prasad Mukherjee etc all opposed giving even pittance of freedom fighter pension to those who had lost life or limb or mental stability or become victims of diseases like TB in british prisons. These collaborators even refused returning back of property (or equivalent value) that British had confiscated from freedom fighters. And even a documentary list mentiong all freedom fighter was NOT published !! (Freedom Fighter pension started in year 1972) And most citizens in 1950s also didnt work to give any economic support to freedom fighters.
.
This is what I call as Betrayal By Beneficiaries. or BBB for short. Take it or not, this has been observed in many countries, not just India. Less in some countries, more in others. But BBB is almost a universal phenomemon.
