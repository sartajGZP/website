Some important point new candidates (not existing ones) need to keep in mind is ---
.
(1) To me, and many, Right to Recall Party is a PART of Right to Recall MOVEMENT (aandolan). And my goal to start this MOVEMENT (aandolan) is bring some 200 law-drafts.
.
(2) The MOST important goal of the movement to convince voters of India, to send postcard to PM, CM, ECI etc to print these law-drafts, and also send FULL DRAFTS of some laws to PM / CM etc..
.
(2a) So my goal is to CONNECT that voter with DRAFTS, And then ONLY connect him with aandolan. And ONLY after that , connect that voter
.
(3) And I have formed RRP and I ask people to contest elections in this party, ONLY so that speed and intensity of movement increases
.
(4) New candidates will need to give AFFIDAVIT that they will ask Speaker to bring these 200 law-drafts and will vote YES on them WITHOUT amendments and debating in Parliament / Assembly. The affidavit will also mention AMUS's role.
.
(5) Candidates and their DONORS will have FULL and FINAL say in their CAMPAIGN METHODS inside their constituencies. But the activists / members may decide NOT to campaign for them and campaign for someone else , if they do NOT like candidate's pamphlets , campaign method etc. But candidate will NOT be required to obey anyone when it comes to campaigning.
.
======
.
Interface between candidates / activists / contributors
.
As my own thoughts, I welcome any candidate who is willing to contest elections via RRP. To me, election candidate contesting at his own money , OR (candidate + financial contributor) combinations are VIP. But I would like to inform all newcomers candidates that activists and members are coming to RRP due to DRAFTS. So if you do not campaign for DRAFTS, then members and activists will campaign for someone else !! And there is NOTHING I or even God can do to stop that.
.
Also, candidates are FIRMLY requested to either deny financial contributions and non-financial contributions, or accept the will of the contributors. Many candidates often take contributors from contributors who want to promote DRAFTS. And then candidates use the money to print pamphlets that promote candidates and NO DRAFTS !!!
.
At the same time, I would like to inform ALL activists that --- always respect TIME and HONEST MONEY. Candidates have to put 10s and 100s of hours in filling forms, expense reports etc . And they and their close aides have to put 100s of hours in campaigning. And candidates and their contributors spend 1000s of sometimes a few lakhs of rupees in campaign. And no movement in world can move, if members decide NOT to respect time and money. Many activists have tendency to IGNORE or sometimes even disrespect financial contributors and candidates who are spending their own money. Eventually, such members will ONLY slowdown the movement. My goal will be explain to candidates and allto completely IGNORE such members, who choose not to respect others' time and money.
.
All in all, this is NOT issue for this album. This album is about ADMINISTRATIVE DECISIONS. But many ADMINISTRATIVE DECISIONS will be only to address this issue.
