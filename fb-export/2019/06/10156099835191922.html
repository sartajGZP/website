Are you contesting election to win election or lose elections? my ans: utterly useless question
.
Many YES/NO questions have an answer better than both YES or NO --- that question is useless. eg many ask me, "will PM read letters sent by 15 crore voters to him to print XYZ law such as #CancelEvm or #MRCM or #LevyEltCancelGst ". To this my answer is "I dismiss your question as question as useless Q, and wont reply". And same is my answer to this Q "are you contesting election to win election or lose elections? ". To all who asked me this question since year 2008, my answer has been "I dismiss your Q as useless Q".
.
I will give my reasons behind my answer
.
Almost all who are contesting election are serious. No one hs Rs 25000 deposit money to throw away. Now one may be able to contest seriously or not (eg in may-2014, I distributed 25000 pamphlets while may-2019 I could not distribute even 2000 pamphlets) because of other work . eg this time, my anti-EVM campaign took 4 days and then notices thrown by Election Commission took away 8 more days, totaling 12 days !!! And I was assisting 10 other Loksabha candidates of my Right to Recall Party, which took away a lot of time
.
So question can only be --- are you serious about election contesting or not? And yes, some 5% candidates may ne non serious, but everyone else is dead serious when he contesting elections. Now it may happen, then some other serious issues come up and so candidate may be unable to give full time.
.
So again, "are you serious about election contesting" is a valid question, but still a pointless question, because in 95% cases, answer is always YES.
.
Now winning of not winning of candidate-A depends on voters, and many other factors and NOT just candidate alone. Many voters have valid reasons for voting some other candidate-B , who may be better than candidate-A or worse than candidate-B. And likewise, voters have VALID reasons for voting for a Party, or voting for a CM-candidate or PM-candidate , in which case he ignores the Loksabha candidate or Vidhansabha candidate completely.
.
Similarly, a candidate too has limitations. eg one line that will become EXPLICIT in my campaign will be "all non-doctor voters who have NOT written letter to PM CM etc or not voted on newindia dot in to print law-drafts they voters support are requested NOT to vote for me and my fellow Right to Recall Party candidates" !!! Of course, a non-doctor voter may have NOT written letter and may not have voted on newindia dot in, and still may vote for us and there is no way to know or stop him. But still, making this guideline is necessary for me. Because if a voter has NOT written letter to PM/CM and also has NOT voted on newindia, then that voter's vote is a liability to me and my Party, and NOT and asset.
.
So candidate has VALID limitations, His manifesto may exclude many voters whom he himself doesnt want.
.
Amongst 1000 VALID or invalid issues that govern voters and also candidate , imo a question "are you contesting election to win election or lose election" is useless. So imo, all candidates will face this question. And each can decide his answer. I have given my answer.
