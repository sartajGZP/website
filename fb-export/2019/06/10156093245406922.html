From Letter Register Method to
"100% autonomous rallies"
.
In Letter Register System, voter-A will take voter-B as supporter of of a law-draft ONLY if voter-B has sent postcard to PM / CM / etc demanding law-draft and the copy of the postcard has been pasted in voter-B's register. Otherwise, imo, voter-A should ignore voter-B's claimed support for the law-draft
.
Now voter-A should note down in his own register , on page on that specific law-draft , that B is supporter of law-draft. Of course, this should happen only if voter-A personally knows voter-B OR voter-B is some political senior such as local congress/rss/aap activist, local congress/rss/aap leaders etc. But they must all be local people, eg may be corporator, but definitely not MLA or MP or Ministers
.
So for each law-draft, I need to write which persons have sent letter to PM / CM etc demanding that law-draft and also pasted copy of that law-draft in his register and also has a "supporter list" in his register
.
So each voter / activists has to build REAL social connections with supporters, Each voter-activist should try to build REAL social connection with at least LOCAL 10-20 activists-voters . By local, I mean - living as close as possible, Searching becomes possible by asking and telling people you personally know.
.
Now this LOCAL group of people, who are connected due to a cause an for a cause, can meet in garden etc,
.
In meeting, a struct condition should be met --- any person is welcome if he wishes to know about MRCM, redo, Rego etc. But after 2-3 meetings, of the person refuses to keep a register, then he should NOT be allowed to attend meetings. Because meeting is ONLY to promote letter register method and promote rallies of those who are maintaining register and also sending letters.
.
The rally if to be taken, should be taken out ONLY on Sunday 10 am and no other time. And route, person in charge of taking permission etc should be ALL decided by LOCAL group who have met
.
---------------
.
The plus point of such meetings and rallies is --- TOTAL LOCAL AUTONOMY. There is none at state / national level with remote control or direct control. There is law-draft such as MRCM or Rego or Redo etc .And rest is all done at individual level, and individuals who got connected by their own letters and registers.
.
So basically , the "List of supporters" page , one such page for each law-draft that you are serious about, will lead to LOCAL meetings and local rallies.
