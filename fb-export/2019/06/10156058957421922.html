post 001 , A201.001

(1) Letters are letters including postcards sent to PM / CM / ECI / SCjs etc for law-drafts and key changes that a voter / activists wants in Govt. The law-draft or proposal MUST have a HASHTAG. The LETTERS / POSTCARDS you send must have date, your voterid , name and city name. Full address is optional.
.
(2) Register is a notebook , fullscape size or any size, where voter is keeping XEROX COPY of postcard / letter he has sent.
.
(3) whenever you send letter to PM etc, pls keep xerox of FIRST page of that letter pasted in register, If letter is imp to you, you may keep ALL pages pasted. And whenever you send postcard, pls keep COPY of postcard pasted in register
.
(4) Register must also have one page for each HASHTAG i.e. change you want in Govt . And in that page voter or activist must write one line showing name of people he personally knows (except relatives, work colleagues , school friends, college friends etc). It should be all people whom he knows and those who claim as political / moral activists, and all political seniors, starting from PM to local corporator. And the line must have YES / NO / UNKNOWN depending on whether THAT person has sent letter / postcard to PM / CM / ECI etc with THAT specific HASHTAG or not. eg say your local MP has NOT kept copy of paostcard he has sent in his register, to the best of your knowledge, Then pls write NO after his name.There must also me a line for CANDIDATES he voted for and may vote for, unless he wants to keep his vote confidential.

(5) The register must have first page as INDEX page, which should have one line for each CODE of the law-draft or change you demanded. And page number which has copy of the letters / postcard of law-draft you sent. And list of names of all people who support
