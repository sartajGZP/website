Aandolan and election contesting : why in absence of SERIOUS election contesting, aandolan alone, will imo not work to bring SERIOUS law-drafts !!!
.
Mainly, because aandolan has risk of chaos, and is lot of work for voters. And so crores of voters will ask activists to contest and win elections. And these voters will not join aandolan, except only if procedure of election is suspended or cancelled.
.

=======================
.
SUMMARY
-------------
.
Across world and India, and across times, true activists have successfully convinced citizens to join aandolan. But imo this happened only when election procedure was NOT an option for crores of citizens. So now a question before those activists who wish to bring serious structural changes in govt like MRCM is ----- can activists convince citizens to join aandolan when citizens have power to vote?
.
IMO, no !! why not?
.
imo, reasons are (a) many citizens rightly believe that aandolan has risk of chaos and instability, which they cant afford (b) aandolan is too time consuming and also expensive for citizens
.
So many citizens, who do support a proposal, but are averse to chaos, will ask activists to get it implemented via election contesting !!
.
summarizing in one line ---- PROCEDURE of election has weakened the preference for aandolan in VOTERS. And imo, activists should give preference to what voters want and not what they personally prefer, except when question is of ethics. So imo, activists better take election contesting very very seriously.
.
=========================
.
DETAILS
-----------
.
During freedom movement. in phases from 1760s to 1940s, activists started covert / overt Aandolans. Britishers did sabotage the process via psuedos like Mahaduratma Gandhi and Hegadewar. But aandolans did happen, and crores of Indian citizens joined activists in many ways.
.
But back then, less than 5% citizens had power to vote till 1952 and MPs / MLAs were under Viceroy. So imo , since citizens didnt have power of voting, and so citizens joined aandolan
.
And aandolan for land reforms in Gujarat in late 1940s was successful. But that too, because back then 95% had no power to vote. When bottom 95% has no power to vote, then aandolan is ONLY option for them. And in such case activists promoting aandolan will get support from citizens.
.
Aandolan to remove emergency succeeded. But imo, that was because election process was suspended during emergency.
.
Now lets look at some successful examples of aandolan. .
Thru aandolan, citizens have been able to force a CM or even PM (VP Singh) to resign. But that doesnt count much when goal is to get law-drafts like MRCM, Wealth Tax (aka Empty Land tax) passed. Resignation of CM or even PM is non issue to elitemen compared to MRCM, wealth tax etc
..
But when election contesting is option, imo, voters will ask activists to SERIOUSLY CONTEST elections, win elections and bring those changes.
.
Election contesting also is test for activists -- can they work in a team, can they listen to each other and resolve differences and work ahead, or they are just a bunch of speakers who speak and dont listen to even their own group members, forget citizens? Can they communicate to crores of voters and work to convince them? And so on. All these skills and needs are also there is aandolan. But election contesting needs all these skills and also works as proofs of these attempts (assuming EVMs are faithful).
..
------
.
So if activists are serious about bringing mega changes like MRCM, WealthTax = ELT = Empty Land Tax, etc, then they better take election contesting and bring laws via election winning very very seriously. Because a large number of voters will prefer procedure of election, and will look at aandolan ONLY if election procedure is suspended or cancelled.
.
==============
.
CONCLUSIONS AND CLAIMS
------------------------------
.
So my conclusions and claims are that
.
(1) citizens will join andolan if voting power is suspended or cancelled
.
(2) but if citizens have voting power, they will directly or indirectly ask activists to contest elections and bring laws via election winning, and not aandolan, because of risk of chaos / instability in aandolan
.
(3) and so all activists (who want SERIOUS structural change in govt such as MRCM, ending GST, levying Empty Land Tax, etc) better take election contesting SERIOUSLY, because many voters prefer election to aandolan because aandolan has possibility of chaos
.
=======
.
Andolan vs election --- citizens will decide, not activists
---------------------------------------
.
Many activists may say "I prefer election winning to aandolan" and some activists may say opposite.
.
But imo, finally, it is CITIZENS who will decide. Imo, the statements like "I am not contesting elections to win elections" are SPURIOUS and useless, because candidate can only decide whether he is SERIOUS about election contesting or not. The decision of his winning or losing will be taken by voters and NOT candidate.
.
And same way, an activists may say whether he is serious about aandolan or not. But finally, it will be citizens who will decide whether they want to go for andolan or election route to bring the change. Imo, it is best if activists try BOTH routes seriously and with full commitment.
.

======
.
My opinions on important andolans - case by case basis
-------------------------------------------------
.
Now lets see some cases of aandolan. Lets see in which aandolans, citizens joined or not, and to what extent.
.
(1) Freedom movement - citizens of India joined aandolan, because imo 95% didnt have power to vote
.
(2) Land reform aandolan in Gujarat in late 1940s and many other states - citizens joined, but till 1952, 95% didnt have power to vote
.
(3) Gujarat 1972 navnirman aandolan - citizens joined, but demand was resignation of CM, but that is too small an issue compared to MRCM, Empty Land tax, etc . So success of 1972 aandolan doesnt count much.
.
(4) Aandolan to end emergency of 1975 - 1977 --- citizens joined, but imo that was because voting power was suspended, and so citizens had only one option.
.
(5) Aandolan against PM V P Singh in 1989 - 1990 ----- anti-reservation citizens joined, and that was key reason why eventually VPS govt ended. But again, reservation issue is peanuts compared to MRCM. And anti-reservation citizens joined because they knew that voting procedure cannot end caste based reservation.
.
(6) Andolan for Janlokpal Nonsense --- Janlokpal Nonsense was a nonsense proposal. And Sarkari Lokpal was also a nonsense proposal. Both were nonsense proposals. And andolan imo was more of a paidmedia hype, and anti-congress voters joined only because they would support anything that is anti-congress.
.
IOW, raw data very much fits with my conclusions and claims, that procedure of election has vastly reduced the possibility that voters will join aandolan. So activists who want SERIOUS structural changes imo better start taking election contesting very very seriously. If you are NOT serious about election contesting, then chances are high that voters will NOT join you.
.
And most difficult part in election contesting is -- team work One needs a large team to cover 2.50 lakh voters per Vidhansabha or 20 lakh voters per Loksabha, and cover 300 booths per vidhansabha and 2000 booths per Loksabha. And even covering all 20 counting tables per Vidhansabha and 150 counting tables per Loksabha is a big task.
.
Election contesting needs co-ordination between activists and candidates , and many campaign workers, Imo, all activists better take ALL election related issues seriously
