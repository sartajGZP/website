Understanding the concept of Common Knowledge --- set-1 --- Also, this exercise imo explains an imp difference amongst islam / hinduism / sikhism/ christianity
.
pls do this experiment - need 8 or more players. You can certainly do with less players, but results will be clearer if you have 8 or more player.
.
There are 4 games in this set-1 here, Following are the steps for 7 player version of this game-1 to game-4 .
.
Game - 1
---------------
.
(1.1) One player will become Host H and remaining will take labels of P1 to P7
.
(1.2) You need 7 white caps and 7 black caps. Or, instead of caps, you take colored HAIRPINS. . Or you can take 14 white caps , in which 7 caps have black dot at top and 7 caps have white dot at top,
.
(1.3) IMPORTANT : Host must put caps on heads of players in a way that one doesnt know what the color of his own cap. .
.
(1.3.A) IMPORTANT --- Now a player may see the color of his own cap --- due to mistake or some mirror or by chance. But players must NOT conclude from that observation. The players must cite the color of the cap later, AFTER they have logically deduced. Not because they have seen color when host was putting the can due to error of host
.
(1.4) Host will put 2 black caps on heads of two player P1 and P2 and 5 white caps on heads of other five players. In next rounds of the game, put 2 black caps on different players. And in next round of the game, take 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 and then 0 black caps, and remaining white caps.
.
(1.5) After putting caps, host will ask once --- he who knows he has black cap or white cap or dont know color, will say "black , while or dont know" . Then host will ask same question again, and wait for 1 minute. Like that, host will ask this question 8-9 times.
.
(1.6) Each player must say --- black, white or "I dont know the color of my cap" everytime host asks the question
.
======
.
Game - 2
---------------
.
(2.1) to (2.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(2.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will now say - at least one of you have a black cap..
.
IMPORTANT QUESTION -- Now pls note that each player with black cap knew that at least one player has black cap. And each player with white cap also knew that 2 players have black cap. So has host added any new information?
.
(2.5) Host will ask once --- he who knows he has black cap or white cap or dont know color, will say "black , while or dont know" . Then host will ask same question again, and wait for 1 minute. Like that, host will ask this question 8-9 times.
.
(1.6) Each player must say --- black, white or "I dont know the color of my cap" everytime host asks the question
.
=========
.
Game - 3
---------------
.
(3.1) to (3.4) - same as in (1.1) to (1.4)
.
(3.4.1) IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE --- Host will ask one player with black cap to leave room for 2 minutes. That player, say P1 must NOT hear what host has said.
.
(3.4.2) After he has left room, the host will say "at least one of you have a black cap"..And all players must note that statement made by host is NOT heard by that P1. Host should explain these points to all players - that existing player has not heard the statement. Each player must note that P1 had left the room and P1 should note that all players saw him leaving the room.
.
(3.4.3) IMPORTANT QUESTION -- Now pls note that each player with black cap knew that at least one player has black cap. And each player with white cap also knew that 2 players have black cap. So has host added any new information? And the player P1 who had not heard this statement also had this information. So did he miss anything?
.
(3.4.4) Now the player with black cap who had left the room will be brought back
.
(3.5) same as (1.5.)
