How VVPATS can managed? - by replacing print in VVPAT by a printer which prints on both sides, in a way that print on front side evaporates after a few hours
.
The six minute video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlCOj1dElDY shows how non-VVPAT EVMs can be tempered at mass scale.
.
VVPAT can be tempered as follows
.
The printer's head can be replaced by DUAL PRINTING head. It isnt available in off the shelf market, but can be made. That head will print ob both sides, but one side is printed in a way that ink will evaporate in few minutes. Also, it will do dual printing ONLY after first 100 votes are cast. So during test, it will be 100% honest !!
.
Let me give more details.
.
Say EVM manipulator wants to favor candidate-A. Then printer will act as follows
.
(1) for 100 votes, the printer will act fairly - i.e. it will print only on one side and the candidate for whom the vote was cast
.
(2) after 100 votes, or when it gets command to start tempering, vote manipulation will start
.
(3) Say 20% extra votes are to be given. Then excery 5th vote's paper recipet will have candidate-A on BACK SIDE and front side will have candidate that voter voted for
.
(4) The front side ink will evaporate after a few minutes or hours.
.
So when VVPAT print recipet are counted , candidate-A will have 20% extra votes and other candidates votes would be reduced.
.
IOW, VVPAT needs replacement of printer head with a printer head that can print on both sides, and using ink on one side that evaporates after a few minutes
