activist A says "I have proposed law-draft-X in my video, but I dont have time to put draft on FB ". Should activist-B "support" that draft?
.
Well, I ignore all such proposals.
.
For me, "supporting" is a serious business. This means I have to send tweet to PmoIndia, vote on that draft on newindia, put links to tweets and newindia link on my FB profile in appropriate album and so on. And if the draft is in top 50 drafts of my list, then I also need to send a postcard to PM to print that draft.
.
Also, unless crores of voters tale above steps, and parhaps more time consuming steps, that draft is unlikely to get passed
.
IOW, anyone who is proposing a either knowingly or unknowingly is asking crores of voters to spend several minutes and hours of their lives.
.
And same person says "I dont have time to write, and I will make only video" !!
.
Now whatever you wish to communicate --- a video takes LESS time than writing, but for audience it is other way. For audience, reading takes LESS time than watching audio. Also when a proposal is in text form, it is easy for a a person to copy-paste a section of portion and comment on that portion. It isnt easy to cut-copy-paste video and comment on portion
.
There are indeed some people who prefer video watching to draft reading, But there are many, who prefer DRAFT READING than video watching, And evebtually, an activist will definitely need a draft in text format.
.
So if an activist is serious about getting a draft printed in Gazette, , then he MUST give the deraft in text form, not just a video description.
.
So my request to all activists is --- pls give more importance to text of the proposed draft, and not the video/
