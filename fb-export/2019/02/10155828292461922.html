chap-1: why election contesting is MUST
---------------------------------------------------
.
Imo, some campaign methods have "MUSTNESS" i.e. no amount of other campaign methods will give certain UNIQUE and MUST factors that that campaign method gives.
.
And election contesting is mo one such method.It gives some UNIQUE additions to RTR-draft-movement which NO
.
There are 543 Loksabha seats, about 4200 MLA seats and some 200,000 Municipal Corportation, Panchayat etc seats.
.
Contesting elections on MP / MLA seats add a necessary factor to RTR campaign, which no amount of other campaign methods added provide. What are those additions that election contesting provides?
.
(1.1) Making activist familiar about problem of reaching 80 crore voters :
.
Every activist who contests MLA/MP realizes SIZE of the problem !! This problem isnt visible in contesting Mayoral or Panchayat seats where constituency is small.
.
One RTR goal is to inform over 60 crore voters about RTR / JurySys / MRCM / TCP / etc law-drafts, And election contesting is imo BEST way to inform an activist what "60 crore" means. Because each MLA candidate realizes how difficult it is to reach 200,000 voters and each MP candidate realized how difficult it is to reach 15,00,000 voters.
.
Of them, those who wish to solve the problem of reaching 90 crore voters, will realize how important it is to form a NETWORK of committed activists.
.
(1.2) Second, to voters, election contesting imo makes a huge difference.
.
Many voters are not "investors" but want ready made goods. .
.
eg say you decide to start a restaurant, which iyo will be better than others. Some may show interest in investing, but many may say "if your food is good, I will buy, but I dont want to invest in your making a restaurant".
.
Same way, many voters are investors i.e. they will be willing to vote for a non-winner with hope that one day non winner will win, and bring change OR voting for non-winner will force winners to change.
.
But many many voters say to candidate that "I will consider you only after you have obtained 20% votes and till then, I dont even want to know your name" !!
.
So many voters never take any agenda seriously till the activists who are promoting that agenda get at least 5% voters nationwide. Till then, they will confine to congress/rss/aap. So unless a party puts 500 candidates, and gets 5% votes nationwide, many voters will NOT take that party seriously.
.
So unless group of activists who wish to get some law-drafts passed actually start put 10s of MP candidates, they will never reach near 500. And they will never reach near 5% or even 2%

(1.3) Only election contestants understand SERIOUSNESS of problem of reaching 60 crore voters
.
Now for me, election contestants are very important. Because problem of reaching 60 crore voters is serious, and I am unable to spend time in devising means to reach voters. Because most of my time goes in draft writing, and a huge amount of time and energy in past 6 months got drained in party registration, and lot for time goes in designing campaign methods such as postcard to PM , voting on newindia, tweets to PM, putting links in FB albums etc. So I am unable to take actions needed to actually reach crores of voters.
.
And to devise and implement means to reach crores of voters, I need activists.
.
And my experience is that --- the activist who has actually contested election is far far more effective in working on "problem of informing 60 crore voters".
.

chap-2: why election contesting or even election winning is NOT sufficient to bring RTR-law-drafts
---------------------------------------------------
.
But at the same time, imo, election contesting and even winning wont bring RTR-Jury-etc-law-drafts in absence of DIRECT ACTION BASED MASS MOVEMENT.
.
Imo, the law-drafts will come ONLY after crores of voters are DIRECTLY "REQUESTING" elitemen to put up with RTR-law-drafts or else. The MPs who will print these laws in Gazette are mere willing or unwilling postmen. On their own, they are no match against strength of elitemen,.
.
No matter which 500 Indian citizens are picked and made MPs, there are not even 0.001% of strength that desi / videshi elitemen have, given that most judges / IAS / IPS etc support elitemen and oppose RTR-Jury-law-drafts..
.
No matter which 500 Indian citizens are picked and made MPs, almost all will get bribed or liquidated if they decide to print RTR-law-drafts.
.
The only way elitemen , judges, IAS, IPS etc can be convinced to put up with RTR-law-drafts is --- the commons need to convince them by DIRECT COMMUNICATION to PM that "see, if these law-drafts dont come, then ....."
.
So PRIMARY steps are --- those who want RTR-law-drafts to come MUST convince crores of voters to DIRECTLY communicate to PM, CM , judges, IAS, IPS and elitemen that voters want these law-drafts. How is such communication possible? which physical channels should be used? Imo, physical channels can be postcard to PM, voting on PM's website newindia dot in and sending tweet to @PmoIndia .
.
So imo, activists need to convince crores of voters to DIRECTLY communicate to PM, CM etc that "we the voters of India want these laws". And citizens should also ask voters to DIRECTLY communicate to SCjs and HCjs not to throw stay order when these laws are printed. And eventually, activists need to convince over 50 crore voters of India to DIRECTLY communicate to all SCjs and HCjs, and all judges and all magistrates to resign and not enter courts premises.
.

chap-3 : and so whats more important than election contesting within election contesting framework ?
-----------------------------------------------
.
So imo RTR-activists should campaign for election candidates. But they should also see -- is candidate working to convince voters to DIRECTLY communicate to PM via available physical communication channels such as postcard, newindia dot in, twitter? If not, and he is ONLY RTR candidate, then activist has no choice. But if there are two RTR candidates A and B, and A is working to convince voters to directly communicate to PM to print RTR-law-drafts, and B is not doing so, then imo activists should campaign for A and not B, Of course, this is ONE FACTOR, and there are many many other factors to consider. So directly or indirectly, activists can prompt / force candidate to convince voters to DIRECTLY communicate with PM to print RTR-law-draft, or shun that candidate and work for a candidate who is asking voters to directly communicate to PM to print RTR-law-drafts.
.
The behavior of activists will impact the candidates.
