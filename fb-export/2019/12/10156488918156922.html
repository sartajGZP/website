Proof that leaderless medialess mass movements work ---- Gujarat govt forced to end Helmet law !!!
.
Helmet law was a huge bribe income generator for Gujarat govt. And so no congress rss aap leader opposed it. But a leaderless medialess mass movement against helmet law started in Gujarat . And due to that leadeless medialess mass movement, Gujarat govt position holders had to let go the bribe income helmet law was generating. .
.
So imo leaderless medialess mass movement work. And that should be our focus.
.

In case of helmet removing, alternative was obvious . But if one wishes to have made movement to end GST the alternative like Empty Land Tax is needed. But then one also need activists who are willing to contest election and print that law If they get elected.
.
