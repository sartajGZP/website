How lakhs of VVPAT-EVM can be rigged with just 2 men !!
.
It is possible to transfer crores of votes in lakhs of EVM-VVPATs across India with just 2 persons at head offices !!!
.
One person needed for this task is the coder who wrote the code of VVPAT or microcode of VVPAT’s CPU (which is made in USA). And another person needed is the person in ECI who makes bitmaps.
.
Also, voters will see the symbol they voted !! eg say there are 1000 voters and say 500 vote for candidate-1 and 500 vote for candidate-2. Then 500 who voted for candidate-1 will see "1 , name of 1, symbol of 1" and the other 500 who voted for candidate-2 will see "2, name of 2 and symbol of 2".
.
But when counting is done on EVM, say candidate-1 will get 700 votes and candidate-2 will get only 300 votes. And if paper slips are counted, then they will match with EVM i.e. there will 700 slips for candidate-1 and 300 slips for candidate-2. IOW, even though 500 voters saw symbols of candidate-2, the printer output will be different !! i.e. some 200 votes will get stolen or transferred.
.
And crores of votes across lakhs of EVMs can be transferred using just 2 persons., using my method !!!
.
Rigging can be done using following steps in existing VVPAT
.
(1) As of now ,the connections are as follows –
.
(1a) BU i.e. Ballot Unit is connected to VVPAT
.
(1b) VVPAT is connected to CU i.e. control unit.
.
(1c) the bitmaps of the symbols associated with candidate numbers are downloaded by BEL\ECIL technicians in VVPAT about 15 days before polling. BEL and ECIL are two PSUs which make EVMs.
.
(1d) Each bitmap file of any symbol in computer consists of about hundred to a few thousand integers from 0 to 65535. Elections symbols have file size of about 2000 such integers.
.
(2) The voting, printing and vote storage happens as follows
.
(2a) The voter presses candidate number button on BU, and BU sends signal to VVPAT
.
(2b) VVPAT prints the symbol, candidate number and candidate name on thermal paper roll, then roller revolves, the paper moves ahead, a light turns on so that voter can see the paper, then light turns off, then the paper is cut by an internal cutter, and paper slip falls in a bin which is locked and sealed.
.
(2c) VVPAT sends candidate number of CU. The CU stores the votes of each candidate in its memory
.
(3) mow say there are four candidates 1 to 4. Say their symbols are Apple, Banana, Coconut, and Date (khajur). So a technician in ECI will create bitmap files for Apple, Banana, Coconut and Date. These bitmap files will be given to some 1000 technicians of BEL. And some 15 days before polling, the BEL technician will enter these 4 bitmap files in the EVMs in that specific constituency. And the BEL technician will also enter codes that button-1 is associated with symbol Apple and so on.
.
(3a) Now to rig votes, the chip programmer will put following code in chip. Say candidate with symbol Banana has to be favored by atmost 20% i.e. all candidates 20% votes should be deducted and transferred to Banana symbol
.
(4) The coder will put code that, when Bitmap file’s individual integers are added, and when last 10 digits of the sum are say S = 3,050,080,765 (for sake of example), then and then only that symbol will be favored by VVPAT.
.
(5) The ECI artist who is making bitmap files will make the file in such a way that only Banana bitmap’s number adds to that S = 3,050,080,765
.
Now actual rigging on polling day will happen as follow

(6) Say first voter casts his votes to “candidate-2 Banana. Then VVPAT will show “candidate-2 , Banana” to voter, it will cut the paper, the slip will fall in the bin and VVPAT will send “candidate number 2” to CU. So it was honest vote counting.
.
(7) Say second voter casts his vote to “candidate-1 Apple”. Then VVPAT will show “candidate-1 , Apple” to voter, but it will NOT cut the paper slip, will keep as it is, turn the light off, and send “candidate 1” to CU
.
(8) say third voter casts his vote to “candidate-1 Apple”. Then VVPAT will show the SAME slip of Apple to third voter, that it had shown to second voter, and then send “candidate-2” to CU !! So third voter’s vote was for candidate-1, but this third vote got allocated to candidate-2 and not candidate-1. And VVPAT will then cut “candidate-1 Apple” slip, and the print “candidate-2 Banana” slip and cut that slip as well
.
(9) So now two voters voted for “candidate-1 Apple”, and both saw Apple slip !! And one voter voted for “candidate-2 Banana” he saw “candidate-2 Banana”.
.
(10) But VVPAT printed two slips of “candidate-2 Banana” and only one slip of “candidate-1 Apple”
.
(11) And CU too has 2 votes for candidate-2 and one vote for candidate-1 !! So VVPAT results will tally with CU
.
(12) so manipulation method works as follows –
.
(12a) say candidate-2 has to be favored
.
(12b) when two CONSECUTIVE voters vote for candidate-1 (or when two consecutive voters vote for candidate-3), the VVPAT will show SAME one slip of candidate-1 to BOTH voters
.
(12c) and then VVPAT will print 1 slip of candidate-1 and one slip for candidate-2
.
(12d) and VVPAT will send one “candidate-1” vote to CU and
one “candidate-2” vote to CU
.
(12e) the code in VVPAT decides whether candidate should be favored or not depending on “secretly embedded information” in the bitmap file of the symbols.
.
(13) The manipulation method works because vote has no way to know whether slip he is seeing is same slip that printed for previous voter or the slip he is seeing is the slip that just printed for him !! The voter merely sees a paper slip which has same candidate and same symbol that he pressed button of.
.
(14) The above method of vote transfer has only one limitation – that if voters vote as “Apple Banana Apple Banana Apple Banana …” then VVPAT will fail to transfer votes. i.e. if every vote is DIFFERENT from previous vote, then VVPAT will not be able to manipulate. This manipulation method works when a vote is same as previous vote.

(15) Now in real life, there are about 700 voters per booth and one EVM / VVPAT per booth. So its more than likely, in many cases, two CONSECUTIVE voters will be casting votes to the same candidate.
