Rahul Mehta Gandhinagar LsCandidate

Wed May 08 2019 14:56:12 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

proposal - polling and counting on same day, at same place across entire India , using PAPER ballots and PERMAMENT polling stations.
.
My proposal is as follows
.
(1) Every locality of 5000 to 10000 voters in India, should have a govt owned hall (with pillars or without pillars, as local body decides) where 1000 to 2000 people can sit on chairs.
.
(2) On voting days, this hall will be used for voting only. On other days, whole or parts can be rented for other purposes.
.
(3) Each voter will get Rs 10 per km of distance between his him and hall, if he votes. So yes, if a voter is 50 km away, he will get Rs 500 to cover cost of travel both ways.
.
(4) Voting will be between 8 am and 3pm across India (yes, whole of India), and counting will be on SAME day between 3pm and 9pm. By 10pm, all results will be known to all. Or voting can be 9am first day to 3pm NEXT DAY, and open during night time,. So voting duration will be 24 hours + 6 hours = 30 hours.
.
(5) The hall will have 10 to 20 voting boxes and all votes will be using paper ballots
.
(6) 50 to 100 voters will be chosen at random, and will have to stay for at least 4 hours in the hall as witness / supervisor. They will get Rs 200 for the day.
.
(7) All halls will have CCTV etc which will livecast on a govt servers. The halls will also have ac , drinking water etc
.
(8) Deposit amount for Loksabha should be Rs 50000 (2019 prices, and will increase with inflation) and voters can contribute in election deposit account and will get refunded if deposit comes back, So number of non serious candidates will reduce.
.
So now polling frauds and counting frauds will be minimal.
.
Costwise, it will be CHEAPER than existing system, even after voters are paid to come to center and vote.
.
Today, ECI is passing the costs on govt bodies and nation as a whole without ECI paying those costs,. eg ECI pays Rs 1000 per day for 4 days of bank deputy manager working as presiding officer (2 days of training + 2 days of work) . His actual cost to bank is Rs 2500 per day. So bank bears remaining Rs 1500 * 4 = Rs 6000. And customers face costs because their work is delayed when bank staff is deputed.
.
When booth size is large (5000 to 1000) and CCTV camera are installed, chances of law-order problems are minimal. And paper ballot needs less literate staff, So TOTAL policing costs reduces and so does cost of manning the booth and counting.
.
The booth of 5000 to 10000 voters can have 20 ballot boxes. And with just 4 persons, 10000 paper votes can be counted within 2-3 hours
.
The one time cost of making such large hall (with or without pillars) is non issue. Because hall can be rented for other purposes on non voting days. Halls without pillars are very very expensive., But halls with pillars are mere Rs 800 per sqft. So a 5000 sqft hall will be just Rs 40,00,000 = Rs 40 lakhs (plus land cost) or Rs 400 per voter and this is ONE TIME cost. And hall rents during other days will fetch huge incomes too.
