.
Imo, using one THIN CLIENT or LAPTOP, one can make a mock EVM VVPAT and demonstrate how "double expense fraud" can be implemented in existing VVPATs.
.
The overall design is as follows
.
(1) FIRST DESIGN ---- working parts, working code, NO PHYSICAL design , LAPTOP or PC, and not thin client
.
This design will have only actual working parts, and NO new physical plastic boxes etc. Physical plastic boxes will be in second design.
.
(1.1) Pls take following parts
.
(1.1.1) one PC or Laptop
.
(1.1.2) one thermal printer with cutter like https://www.amazon.in/Everycom-80mm-Thermal-Printer-Cutter/dp/B07NQPR125 or like https://www.amazon.in/MATZ-High-Speed-Printing-Cartridge-Required/dp/B07L3S78PS
.
(1.1.3) one keypad with LED similar to
.
(1a)
DESIGN - 1 : This will have three plastic boxes, and one
--------------
.
All three boxes can be combined into one box. So one box will have 8 buttons, a printer with cutter , under DARK BLACK ONE SIDE MIRROR ONE SIDE TRANSPARENT glass, a white LED bulb inside. And an LED showing voter counts
.
So here one thin client will drive all the hardware -- 8 candidate buttons, printer, cutter, LED bulb inside VVPAT chamber etc .
.
The THIN CLIENT will cost say Rs 3500 . And printer may be Rs 1000. and other parts will cost Rs 1000 and box not more than Rs 1000. So whole one piece demo can be made in less than Rs 10000
.
==============
.
DESIGN - 2 : This will have ONE plastic box
--------------
.
(1) Three plastic acrylic boxes will be needed - one box looking like Control Unit CU, another Ballot Unit BU and third VVPAT.
.
(2) The VVPAT looking box will be complicated PHYSICAL DESIGN. It would need fittings to hold a printer cum cutter and should also have a dark black glass which is one side mirror and one side transparent. The glass should be same as in VVPAT. It will also need a while LED bulb inside.
.
(3) The third box will be BU with 16 buttons , 16 LEDs etc etc
.
(4) Each box should have one THIN CLIENT inside. The Thin client will implement the LOGIC of that box
.
(5) Thin Clients cost Rs 3500 to Rs 5000. They have windows or lynux operating system and code can be written in VB.NET or JAVA. The boxes' material costs is below Rs 5000. So imo one should be able to make this whole assembly in less than Rs 25000
.
(6) The BU's code will be honest - whatever button# is pressed, it will transmit that to VVPAT
.
(7) VVPAT will have code that will work in honest mode or dishonest mode. In honest mode, it will print what it gets from BU and send same to CU. And in dishonest mode, it will implement "double expense fraud"
.
(8) CU will store the counts it gets from VVPAT and then later, it will show the counts for each candidate
.
======================
.
So now using a demo, one can demonstrate how VVPATs with dark black one side mirror type glasses can indeed implement "double expense fraud"
.
