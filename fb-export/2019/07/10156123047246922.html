#NewPaperballotSystem --- proposed new ballot paper system
.
(1) Ballot box design I propose - the voter is given a ballot paper, and after stamping, he puts the ballot paper n ENVELOPE (why?) and then drops the envelop in box, which transparent (glass or acrylic) from all sides.
.
(2) In my proposed POLLING system, voters will be paid Rs 12 per kilometer distance between home and polling station each way
.
(3) There will one polling station for at least 10000 to at most 50000 voters.
.
(4) polling time will be 9am to 3pm next to next day i.e. 1 days and 6 hours or 30 hours, including night times. There will complete webcasting of ALL polling stations across India.
.
(5) there will be 20 to 50 ballot boxes in one hall of size 25 meters * 25 meters with pillars but no walls.
.
(6) counting will be done on SAME table right after 3pm when polling ends. And whole counting will be webcasted.to all.
.
(7) whole India will go polling on SAME day, same time
.
(8) Compulsory recounting by Jurors i.e. randomly chosen citizens. And Jurors i.e. randomly chosen citizens will also have watch duty during polling and counting
.
Costs of tis system, including cost of making such halls, are LESS than existing EVM based polling with one booth per 900 voters to at most 1200 voters.
.
The existing system of making small booths was made because back in 1919, transportation was simply unviable not just expensive. Today, transportation is cheap and plenty.
