Wrong answers and right answers to useless questions against paper-ballots
.
(1) paper ballots increase enviroment damage as so many papers are used
.
WRONG ANSWER : VVPAT also uses paper. This answer is wrong because paper used by VVPAT is 1/50th of ballot paper in size
.
Imo Right Answer : One EVM is used per booth. One booth has 1000 voters. One ballot is A3 size paper at most - twice the ordinary computer printer paper. Weight of one paper will be 12 grams. Weight of 1000 such papers will be 12 kg. One EVM is used for 10 years and will serve at most 3 loksabha general elections and 3 assembly general elections and may be 1-2 byelections. So say it serves 10 elections. A different EVM is used for Municipal elections. So paper it replaces is at most 120 kg. Now EVM has 3-4 parts togather they weigh more than 12 kg excluding suitcases of which some 10 kg is electoronic weight and some 2 kg is plastic weight. So EVM causes 10 kg of electronic waste and 2 kg of plastic waste every 10 years as opposed to ballot paper which cause paper loss of 120 kg papers at most every 10 years. Electronic waste and plastic cause ORDERS of magnitude more damage than paper. So overall, EVM causes more burden on environment than paper ballots
.
Also, lets compare ballot paper used with newspapers. One newspaper of 24 pages has paper equal to 48 ballot papers. But ballot paper is thicker. So lets take it 24 ballot papers. During one election, there are 90 crore voters. So say we have to print 120 crore ballot papers Thats equal to 120 / 24 = 5 crore newspapers. This is LESS than daily newspaper circulation !! IOW, ballot papers consume less paper than what newspapers consume EVERYDAY !! So much for paper saving
.
And this question is pointless. Because when quesion is ballot paper vs EVM, main issue is -- which is suspectible to mass rigging and rigging. value of vote is more than paper or machine, So when EVM is compared with paper, paper is cheaper and less damaging to environment, but the issue itself is poitnless.
