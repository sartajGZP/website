(A039) Right to Recall Physical Meetings
.
Less than 2% of Indian voters uses Facebook EFFECTIVELY. About 40% uses Whatsapp, but WhatsApp is not too incomplete a tool to transfer BODY of political information and opinions.
.
So even today, the 50000 years old method of PHYSICAL meetings is as needed as it was in past. May be, Facebook live will later reduce this need. But NOT today
.
So I request ALL recallists to hold one garden meeting (or any office meeting) every Sunday Morning 10 am to 3 pm, where each person should bring his own water, tea, biscuits, food, lunch etc , unless he is comfortable from eating from near by stalls. This way, meeting organizers wont be burdened with arranging for water, food etc.
.
I held 100s of Sunday Morning meetings , but for past 2 years, I have been too busy with DRAFT WRITING and RE-WRITING, and wrinting many articles etc on campaign methods and election contesting.
.
So if you are doing MORE important things than meetings, such as draft writing, election contesting, then you may or may not attend these meetings.
.
But if you are not a draft writer and not an election contestant, then I request you to attend at least 2 sunday morning meetings a month --- meeting on FIRST SUNDAY and any other sunday.
