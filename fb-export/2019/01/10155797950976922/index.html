Important year 1972 old news -- the first High Court judge who gave judgement against Devi Indira Amma was a BRITISH named William Broome , who had become Indian Citizen in year 1948 !!!
.
Quite a shame that an Englishman who was appointed as judge by colonial masters was allowed to work as High Court judge by Jawaharlal Ghazi. And he remained loyal to British , and gave verdict against Devi Indira Amma when she went against usuk-elitemen !!
.
William Broome was Englishman born in London in 1910 and came to India as officer of Imperial British Govt. He was later appointed as District judge by Viceroy and later promoted as High Court judge in Allahabad High Court (now Prayagraj High Court).
.
He decided to stay in India as High Court judge in 1948, and so he applied for Indian Citizenship and Jawaharlal Ghazi and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel BOTH approved his citizenship as well as his continuation as High Court judge !!! And no congressman such as Lal Bahadur Shashtri etc ever demanded his expulsion !!!
.
And when Devi Indira Amma raised head against usuk-elitemen, judge William Brooke worked to damage her.
