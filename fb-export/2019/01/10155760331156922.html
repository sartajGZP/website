(A039) Right to Recall Physical Meetings
.
Less than 2% of Indian voters uses Facebook EFFECTIVELY. About 40% use Whatsapp, but WhatsApp is too incomplete a tool to transfer BODY of political information and political opinions. Also, WhatsApp enables activists to communicate ONLY to voters known to him, and not NEW voters
.
So even today, the 50000 years old method of PHYSICAL meetings is as needed as it was in past. May be, Facebook live will later reduce this of physical meetings. But NOT today. Today, physical meetings are must.
.
So I request ALL recallists to hold one garden meeting (or any office meeting) every Sunday Morning 10 am to as long as he has time. And each person should be encouraged to bring his own water, tea, biscuits, food, lunch etc , unless he is comfortable from eating from near by stalls. This way, meeting organizers wont be burdened with arranging for water, food etc.
.
I held 100s of Sunday Morning meetings in past.
.
Imo, only thing more important than meetings is election contesting. Because proper election contesting consumes away so much time that after that, one may not have time to attend meetings.
.
So if you are doing MORE important things than meetings, then you may or may not attend these meetings.
.
But if you are not a draft writer and not an election contestant, then I request you to attend at least 2 sunday morning meetings a month --- meeting on FIRST SUNDAY and any other sunday.
.
Meeting on FIRST Sunday of the month is more important that other Sundays, And all should try to attend that meeting, And meetings on other sundays can be made optional.
