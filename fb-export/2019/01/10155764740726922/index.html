Tue Jan 08 2019 13:16:58 GMT+0000 (Coordinated Universal Time)

I support new proposed Economic Class Based Reservation - it will not do much good, but doesnt do much harm
.
CBR (CBR = caste based reservation) did have a major effect -- it drastically reduced conversion of SC / ST and also OBCs. Without CBR, a large number of SC / ST / OBC would have landed in laps of missionaries. Imo, reducing conversion was a GOOD thing, and so I have always supported caste based reservation since my first year college days. In addition, caste based reservation (and procedure of election and vote bank politics) has drastically reduced caste discrimination which was rampant some 70 years back.
.
How rampant was caste based reservation in 1940s? My mother told me her school day incident. She was 10 years old in 1952 and got admitted in first class in the local govt school in Muli village near Surendranagar town in Gujarat. And Dalit students were NOT allowed to enter the school !!! Some communist and gandhians activists came and heated debate with school principal and village sarpanch. They were beaten up and forced to flee. But after few days, they came back with court orders, some policemen and court had ordered policemen to protect them and ensure that Dalit students were admitted !! Finally, school principal and sarpanch agreed, with condition that the Dalit students can enter and study in school, but will have to sit outside the classroom in corridor !! And only some 10 years later in around 1960, they got "right" to sit in the classroom !!
.
Such discrimination is now in less than 5% of India, and main reasons for reduction is procedure of election and also caste based reservation. If this discrimination had not reduced at this pace, then many many SC / ST / OBCs would have left Hinduism
.
So imo, CBR played important role, and imo it was POSITIVE role.
.
But EBR (Economic Based reservation) will NOT play any positive major role. But it will also not play any major physical role.
.
All in EBR does play one major POSITIVE role --- now upper caste cant complaint against caste based reservation !!! Because 27% OBC reservation is also for only those who earn below Rs 8 lakhs per year etc. And same criteria is also now for upper caste EBR . So all in all, now we have EQUAL EQUAL scenario. So opposition against caste based reservation now loses some steam. So in activist and politics, it will have positive effect. Now one headache that activist faced is less.
.
So all in all, I dont oppose this EBR , and I support it. No party will dare to oppose it.. So Modiji can get CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT passed within a week and also get the law passed in 2 weeks. Whatever votes who-so-ever gets --- its all upto them
