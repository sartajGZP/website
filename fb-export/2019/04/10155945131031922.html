VVPAT code can also move the paper roll REVERSE BACK. IOW, a variation in method I proposed improves vote transfer. Here is the new SIMILAR method
.
Now not all thermal printers support this command of moving the motor in reverse. One needs to see whether VVPAT printer supports this command or not. If VVPAT printer doesnt support this command, then only method left is keeping the paper where it is
.
Say VVPAT coder wants to add votes to APPLE
.
Summary : say voter-1 presses BANANA. Then after showing BANANA to voter-1 and, VVPAT code can roll the printer BACK. Now if voter-2 also votes for BANANA, then same BANANA can be shown to voter-2 !!
.
DETAILS
-----------
.
Say VVPAT wants to favor APPLE
.
(1) say voter-1 votes for BANANA
(2) VVPAT prints BANANA on paper
(3) VVPAT turns light ON
(4) voter-1 sees BANANA for 7 seconds
(5) VVPAT turns light OFF
----> (6) VVPAT rolls back BANANA paper slip
.
(7) say voter-2 votes for BANANA
(8) VVPAT rolls down same paper slip
(9) VVPAT turns light ON
---->(10) voter-2 sees same BANANA for 7 seconds.
(11) VVPAT turns light OFF
(12) VVPAT cuts BANANA slip
(13) VVPAT prints APPLE and cuts the slip
so one BANANA vote was transferred to APPLE
.
Now say second apple voted for some other symbol - other than BANANA , say GRAPES. In that case
(A7) VVPAT will keep light OFF
(A8) VVPAT will roll down the BANANA slip and cut it
(A9) VVPAT will print GRAPES
(A10) VVPAT turns light ON
(A11) voter-2 sees same GRAPES
(A12) VVPAT cuts GRAPES and turns light OFF
No vote stealing happened
.
So vote stealing can happen only when two CONSECUTIVE voters both vote for same symbols.
