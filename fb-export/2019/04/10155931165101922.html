Why is glass on VVPAT pitch black and not transparent colorless?
.
Glass on VVPAT is so dark black that nothing inside is visible even if someone throws a torch light on it. And paper slip becomes visible only when a strong light is shed from inside

So why is VVPAT glass pitch black and not transparent colorless?

When voter comes , he has no way to know that previous voter's symbol is still there or a blank paper is there !!! And that's because VVPAT glass is pitch dark black and not transparent white or colorless.
