what is SUBSTITUTE candidate --- why we should also work to file SUBSTITUTE candidates as well
.
A political party gives formA and formB to candidate , which is also called as TICKET or MANDATE in common language. In formB, the political party can give name of one and only one first candidate name and one (*) SUBSTITUTE candidate nae.
.
The SUBSTITUTE candidate becomes party candidate in case and only if main candidate doesnt fill form or his form is cancelled.
.
The SUBSTITUTE candidate needs to file a separate form, separate affidavit and his 10 proposers MUST BE DIFFERENT. And he too needs to pay deposit. And if main candidate is approved, then he can continue as independent or he can withdraw. If he withdraws, then the deposit is REFUNDED. But if he decides to contest as independent, then he wont get deposit back
.
So far, we at RRP didnt have even one substitute candidate
.
But now onwards, we should also work to file SUBSTITUTE candidates as well, in case main candidate's form is rejected
