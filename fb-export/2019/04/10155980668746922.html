(1) Bring Wealth tax ; immediately end GST and eWayBill in totality
.
(video - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K06hucHflfk )
.
we were candidates in assembly elections in year 2017. At that time, my advertisement was - "print wealth tax law draft, and end GST, or else GST will finish small manufacturers".
.
And how sadly this has come true
.
Due to GST, small traders / industrialists are getting finished. .
.
Why?
.
Because in GST, buyer has to pay GST to seller. And CGST clause-16.2.c says that if seller doesnt deposit that GST amount in govt, (and if buyer is regular registered) then buyer will have to pay that amount again.
.
This problem is called as "invoice mismatch":
.
In addition, in supply chain, if there is any unregistered or composite, then GST on final customer is double.
.
Due to these problems, small industrialists and small traders will be finished.
.
So unemployment will rise.
.
And so govt will start handing over economy to foreigners via FDI
.
Solution?
.
Solution I propose is - completely end GST, and on over 1500 sqft feet land , levy 1% of value per year as wealth tax
.
And from that, deduce, income tax paid, and 15% of salaries paid to employees.
.
This will reduce land prices. So new factories will start, new shops and offices will start. So unemployment will reduce. Incomes will increase and income tax will increase.
.
Also, end Mauritius Treaty, SEZ and tax exemptions given to Trusts and Political Parties
.
And as income tax increases, income tax rate can be decreased.
.
I request all that please dont demand only removal of GST. But pls also demand alternative (wealth tax or any other alternative), or else citizens will not take you seriously.
.
Also, pls note that certain political party workers are trying to create image that traders are tax evaders.
.
