Manhattanize street layout of all Indian cities. WHAT? HOW?
.
This is streetmap of New York City, USA. The city is also known as Manhattan
.
All roads made after 1860 are parallel or cut each other at 90 degrees, And all streets have wide footpaths. Such SQUARE GRID structure and wide footpaths reduces traffic mess.
.
We need to Manhattanize all roads and streets in India. This will need city to acquire a lot of land to straighten the roads. And all land must be acquiresd at twice the market price. How is this possible?
.
Law-drafts such as MRCM, CancelGST, EmptyLandTax will reduce land prices to 1/0th of what they are now in cities,. With decrease in land prices, it will become possible for city govt to acquire land needed to Manhattanize the street layout
