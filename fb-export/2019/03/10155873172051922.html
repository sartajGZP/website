X using Y as a camel to break the gate -- meaning of expression and how that expression came up
.
There is a saying in Gujarati --- X using Y as a camel to break gate of Z's castle. It can be be used to say "USA used Pakistan as camel to break gates of India and also USA is using India to break into gates of Pakistan / China" and so on.
.
I will explain how this expression came into existence
.
Hundreds of years ago, invaders would use elephants to break the caste's thick strong wooden gate door. An armor will be put on elephant's head, and elephant will start pushing against the door. The door would break.
.
So caste makers started putting 2 feet long iron nails on the door. So if elephant pushes against the door, the nail would kill the elephant.
.
So invaders should put a camel across the door. The camel would held with ropes so it cam run. Now the elephant would push against the camel's body. The nails will penetrate camel's body, but wont touch the elephant. But force imparted by elephant will get transmitted on to the gate's think wooden door and gate would crack.
.
So defenders came with more additions. When elephants came close to caste door, they would start throwing acid and fireballs, damahing elephant and mahavat.
.
So invaders came with another method.
.
Now camel will be "trained". Camel will kept hungry for days, and given some liquor. And then a thin door would be kept, and camel would be pushed to hit the door. The camel would hit the door and food will be served. But door would be so thin - just made of card board, that came would not be hurt
.
Then camel is trainned to run and hit the door.
.
So camel is trained to thibnk that if he runs fast, hits the door, then nothing will happen to him, and he would get great food
.
On actual war day, many such hungry drunk camels will be left loose before the castle door. The camels would run thinking that after hitting the door, they will be safe, and will get food. On the war day, camel would be dead.
.
These two methods were used by invaders, which used camels to break the castle door.s. And thats how Gujarati expression "using him as a camel to break door" came.
.
The expression is applied when A uses B to hurt C, where B loses more , gains nothing and gets used like a dumb pawn
.
----------
.
one example can be Kargil War. India under ABV govt in 1997 did Pokharan-2 against wish of USA and Russia. Next India was blocking FDI in insurance and paidmedia. And ABV was being forced to make serious moves to leave WTO and remove usuk-elitemen's companies from India.
.
To teach a lesson to India, USA (using France , Belgium etc gave huge amount of weapons and goods) to Pakistan needed for high altitude warfare and support. And Pakistan's general was promised huge gains if they unleash Kargil war on India. And USA also pulled strings so that its high ranking agents in IAS worked to shut down satellite servellience over Kargil.
.
During Kargil war, India was loosing and lossing with no end in sight. India lost 4 aircrafts, 1000s of bofors shells, over 400 soldiers, but no end was in sight. Finally, ABV agreed with all conditions of USA -- to end nuclear warhead testing, allow FDI in insurance and banking, allow FDI in paidmedia and so on. And so USA ordered Pakistan to withdraw. Pakistan didnt obey. And so USA asked France to give laser guided bombs to India. And using LGB, India won the won within 2-3 weeks.
.
IOW, Pakistan got used as camel by USA to break India's gates !!
.
Whether USA is now using India as camel to break China / Pakistan needs to be seen.
