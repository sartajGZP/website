Procedures I have proposed to simplify income tax
.
(1) Each person/business will have current account and savings account. And he already has cashbook
.
(2) if X pays cheque to Y, he will need to write "pay to Y savings" or "pay to Y current". If it is not written, it will be presumed as "pay to Y current"
.
(3) if X pays "Y current" then it will NOT become taxable income of Y and X can NOT claim a deductible expense
.
(4) if X pays "Y savings" then it will become taxable income of Y and X can claim a deductible expense, provide item/service bought is a deductible expense
.
(5) All money paid to/from a Govt body will be deductible expense if it qualifies as deductible expense, And all payments from Govt will be deposited into savings only
.
(6) if X pays cash to Y, then Y will give receipt with marked as "tax deductible expense" or "not deductible expense". If it is not marked, then it will be taken as "no deductible expense". If Y has given "tax deductible expense" receipt, then that will be taxable income of Y. Also, if Y has accepted cash and given a receipt with label "tax deductible expense", then Y must submit enter amount, payer name/id and bill number within 90 days. Or else, X will not be able to claim deduction.
.
(7) X can transfer money from his current account to his savings account. In that case, it will be taxable income. But as long as he keeps all money in current account, it will not be taxable income
.
(8A) land, construction, car and several designated items can be bought only from savings account cheques and not cash and not current account cheques. And money paid must be paid into savings account only.
.
(8B) Also, if A lends money to B , then A can lend only from his savings account to B's savings account. If A pays from his current account to B, then it will be taken as NOT loan, but payment for services or goods.
.
So all money received in current account will not be taken as taxable income, till one wants to buy property (in which case, he will need to move that amount into savings, and then it will be taken as taxable income).
.
And if a person has received cash by giving only "not tax deductible" receipts, then also all his cash will not be taxable. He can spend money and buy many goods / service, but he wont be able to buy property. To buy property, he will need to first move that cash into savings account and only then he will be able to buy property. And at the time of moving money into savings, income tax will have to be paid
.
So in the law I am proposing, since all receipts in current account are NOT taxable , there will be minimal reason to keep cash. All can keep account in current accounts and pay/recieve money in current accounts. So problem of fake currency will reduce, theft, currency counting costs and note replacement costs will reduce.
.
And there will be no need to hide transactions, because transactions taken in current account are NOT taxes.
.
This will simplify book keeping for all persons and businesses
