RSS MP ABV had supported Right to Recall in 1974 in Loksabha , only to fool activists and people of India
.
1974 में कम्युनिस्ट सी. के. चंद्रप्पन ने लोकसभा में राईट टू रिकॉल सांसद का बिल पेश किया था। तब वाजपेयी ने इस बिल का समर्थन करते हुए कहा था कि --
.
"महोदय, मैं यहाँ राईट टू रिकॉल के समर्थन में बोलने के लिए खड़ा हूँ, और सरकार से पूछना चाहता हूँ कि, जब सांसद सेवा करने का वादा करके सत्ता में आकर मेवा खाने लगे, और काम करने की जगह दाम कमाने लगे, तो क्या भारत की जनता अगले 5 वर्षो तक सोती रहेगी ? और फिर 5 साल में एक दिन के लिए वोट डालने के लिए उठेगी ? क्या भारतीयों के पास अपने भ्रष्ट प्रतिनिधियों को रिकॉल करने का अधिकार नहीं होना चाहिए। आप कहते है स्विट्जर्लेंड छोटा है, इसीलिए इसका उदाहरण यहाँ लागू नहीं हो सकता, सोवियत रूस बड़ा है इसीलिए उसका उदाहरण यहाँ लागू नहीं हो सकता, और वह देश साम्यवादी भी है !! तो लोकतंत्र में आप किसी नए फार्मूले का अविष्कार क्यों नहीं करते !! "

" Sir, I stand to support Right to recall bill. Should the people sleep for 4 years and 364 days and wake up for one day for exercise their vote ? what should be done if they elected for seva but acquire meva ? should the people watch in silence if they get elected for kaam but make daam instead for work ? switzerland is a small country so its example cannot be applied here. soviet russia is a big country so its example cannot be applied here. it is a communist country too, so its example cannot be applied here. so why dont you introduce something new in democracy ?"
.
पर जब वाजपेयी सत्ता में आये तो उन्होंने राईट टू रिकॉल क़ानून गेजेट में छापने से इंकार कर दिया !!
.

my views
-----------
.
RSS MP ABV was always against Right to Recall. This isnt new --- even in 1925 when Mahatma Chandrashekhar Azad had proposed RTR, the then RSS-leaders had opposed it. As per them, RSS via shakha training will create good moral character in MPs, and so MPs will be so good that recall will NOT be needed !!! And RSS had always maintained that RTR will increase instability, and RSS ( = BJP ) President LKA had repeated that view in year 2011.
.
So why did ABV supported RTR in 1974?
.
ONLY TO FOOL VOTERS AND ACTIVISTS !!!
.
ABV made statement supporting RTR so that pro-RTR activists get glued to ABV and they end up wasting their time and energy !!
.
And today, we see same, We see RSS MP Varun Gandhi deliberately opposing positive / appearance based RTR-MP procedure and instead he proposed USELESS negative recall procedure that too based on signature gathering !!! And today we also see fake RTR supporters like Duratma Anna and Mahatma Arvind Gandhi making pro-RTR-statements to fool voters
.
Solution?
.
Solution I propose is that activists should oppose all "RTR supporters" who do not give RTR-procedure drafts. After getting drafts, the activists/voters should either read drafts themselves or ask activists they trust to PUBLICLY give opinion about these drafts on twitter / FB / postcard to PM. The person may later change the opinion.
.
The RTR-MP-draft I have proposed are at http://facebook.com/10154352176181922 .
.
You may go thru draft or ask opinion of trusted activists to give opinion about this draft in public on twitter / FB etc. And decide whether you want to support above draft, or useless draft proposed by RSS MP shri Varun Gandhi.
