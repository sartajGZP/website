Georgia has voting machines in which voter votes using touch screen, A4 size paper is printed that voter verifies, and then that paper is deposited inside scanner by voter. The scanner gives almost instant counting. And in case there is doubt in resuklts given by scanner, papers can be counted . The sorter inside sorts the paper as needed
