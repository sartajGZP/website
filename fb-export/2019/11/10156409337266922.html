Old VVPATs had ordinary colorless both side transparent glass to see the paper vote.
.
(thanks to Rakesh Suri at https://www.facebook.com/rakeshsuri.rtr for digging these pictures out)
.
I dont know when, but may be sometime in middle of year 2017, the "ordinary colorless both side transparent glass" was replaced by "dark black defacto front side mirror only back side transparent" glass, so that NOTHING is visible inside when inner light is OFF
.
reason?
.
official reason given is so laughable, that I wont bother to write.
.
But changing the glass does enable malacious program to implement a papervote stealing trick described at https://www.facebook.com/mehtarahulc/videos/10156284004731922/
.
Was such paper vote stealing trick actually implemented? Well, I can say that ONLY if I see code in VVPAT ROM.
.
