Why direct deposit of mineral profits in KhaMBa imo will NOT cause laziness?
.
If labor supply decreases, then labor wages incresse. So now factory owner will give less for minerals.

So mineral sale price decreases and so mineral profits decrease. So money from KhaMBa decreases. So laziness will decrease.

Also, say laziness incteses. Then labor supply decreases. Then, labor costs will increase. And so mining labor costs will increase. And so profits from mineral will decrease. And so KhaMBa money will decrease. So laziness will decrease.

So there is a negative feedback loop.

And security increases innovation, not in all people, but in all people, who do want to innovate. So innovations increase outputs.

So imo KhaMBa will increase total labor output ( labor * productivity increase due to innovation).
