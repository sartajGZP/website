In a dark night, near a lamp post, in an almost empty parking lot, a wise man was searching for something. A (dumb) common was passing by and asked "o wise man, what are you looking for?" The wise man replied "I am searching for my car keys. I dropped them while I was trying to open my car door".
.
So the common man said "you car is paked all the way there. So isnt it more likely that keys are somewhere near the car and no here?.

So the wise man replied "yes, But there is more light here and so I will search for my keys here !!"
.
So wise men talk on and confine to TOPICS for which there are citations, evidences, proofs and all that. Whereas a (dumb) common man speaks on TOPICS which are necessary and important.
