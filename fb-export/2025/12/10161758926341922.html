As per mugal british records of 1856, delhi police commisioner (kotwal) name was Syed Mubarak Khan Shah. This is 100% confirmed. Some less verified accounts also say that his nephew was deputy Kotwal named Gyasuddin Ghazi. There was no one named Gangadhar Nehru.
.
So motilal was either motilal saiyed khan Shah or motilal ghazi. Nehru or kaul etc are fictions.
.
What happened back then is that apex british commanders had asked soldiers to arrest or kill all apex moguls. And Sikh soldiers in british armies were also hunting mogul officers. So many moguls took fake hindu names for survival. But they left delhi as too many people in delhi would identify them.

At same time, british were not hurting hindu mogul officers, because they needed officers to run administration. So if anyone named Gangadhar Nehru actually existed, there was no reason for him to flee to Agra.

And no archive before 1870 has word Nehru

So real last name motilal , jawahar , devi indira Amma (before marriage ) was saiyed khan shah or ghazi.
