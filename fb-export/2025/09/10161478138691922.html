Activists working to reduce co2 levels and UNO and world govts will need to decide whether base should be 'per capita' or 'per 1951 capita' or 'per 1971 capita' or 'per 1991 capita'.

Just because a country increases population, its co2 quota should not increase. So imo, Per capita is simply wrong. Whether base year should be 1951 or 1971 or 1991 or 2001 needs to decided. But imo it should not be later than 1991. Because by 1991, all knew well about co2 levels and people across world had legal means to stop co2 increase.
