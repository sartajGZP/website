If 3 gm of water is added to liter of E20 petrol, then it will dissolve well. It can be labeled as accidental natural moisture absorption and so its impossible to frame criminal charges.

Net profits of pump owner increase by 10% to 30% !! Vehicle damage, spare part revenue and vehicle sales also vastly increase.

How?

Gross profit of pump owner is Rs 4 per rs 100 of sale. Net profit is Rs 1 to rs 3.

3gm of water per liter increases revenue by 30 paise per Rs 100. So net profits increase by 30% to 10%.

Vehicle spare part making companies and vehicle manufacturers may ask inspectors to go easy on 3gm per liter water addition. Because it increases vehicle spare part sales and increase vehicle sales.
