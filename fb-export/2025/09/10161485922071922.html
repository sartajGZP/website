Focus where the fraud is and not all points.

Adani got 1000 acre land for rent of Re 1 per year. But scam is not in land rent. Its in deciding terms and conditions of contracts.

Anyone who were to get land would add land rent to power price and so he would increase the electricity sale price. So all tender submitter asked - what will be rent of land , and we would add that to per unit electricity we sell. All quoted electricity unit price and Adani quote was lowest.
.
So say bihar govt had set rent to say rs 10 crore per year, then all would have added say 10 paise to unit electricity.

But scam was in setting terms and conditions of contracts which makes it impossible for small players to bid

And bigger scam is in opposing RTR over electricity Minister.
