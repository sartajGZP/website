Ballot access rules i propose

Main problem : if we have ballot access limit, then supporters of large parties can fill the ballot paper with dummies to push out independents or small party candidates.

So following scheme looks workable but has a major glitch!!!

(1) max slots : 12 + writein + .= 14 District panchayat can pay extra paper costs and add upto 6 more slots.

(2) political parties or independents which were in top 4 in previous elections are automatically given ballot access. If anyone is top 4 isnt contesting, then 5th or 6th or next become eligible.

(3) voter can propose one candidate. Via sms or app or personal appearance at Talati office for fee of rs 3. Approvals can be changed by fee. Approvals will be public.

(4) the candidate with highest approvals will get spot on ballot paper.

What is major glitch ?
