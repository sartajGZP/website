My proposal wrt patent laws is - provisional and complete patent applications BOTH must get PUBLISHED asap with or without fees, without separate form. Imo its must to reduce problem of "hidden prior art voiding novelty" issue.
.
by Rahul Chimanbhai Mehta , 05-mar-2025
.
SUMMARY : (a) as per existing Patent laws, a provisional application is NOT published at all ; and a complete application is published only after applicant submits publication request and pays fees. (b) a new patent applicant must ensure that his application doesnt conflict with even UNPUBLISHED applications aka hidden prior art !!! (c) and this condition is logically impossible to meet (d) solution I propose is that all provisional and complete application must be published asap.
.
DETAILS

(01) In existing Patent laws in India and across world, imo there is serious flaw imo. As per law, the provisional patent applications are not published at all, even if applicant is willing to pay fees. And a complete patent application is published only after the applicant pays fees or after 36 months.
.
(02) an unpublished provisional application or complete application is also known as so called HIDDEN prior art.
.
(03) Now "novelty" criteria in patent law says that - ideas in a patent application must NOT be covered wholly or even partly by any prior provisional or complete published or unpublished patent application.
.
(04) So an applicant has to search amongst all existing patent application and reseqarch publications and ensure that his new patent application doesnt clash with existing applications filed anywhere in world, whether published or unpublished
.
(05) now imo it is LOGICALLY impossible to fulfil condition that a new application must not conflict with UNPUBLISHED provisional and complete patent applications.
.
(06) oppurtunity to play a criminal mischief : also, we need to consider possibility that Patent office staff can do a criminal fraud by a provisional or complete application with false previous date. See example below.

(07) Example of crminal fraud made easy : say an applicant A say files complete application X say on 01-mar-2025 Tue. And say he gives publication request and examination request on same date. And say his application gets published on say 04-mar-2025 Fri. Now say that no published application conflicts. But say patent office staff inserts a FAKE provisional application with fake date say 01-dec-2024 !!! Now outside world has no record to prove that this application never existed !!!
.
(08) SOLUTION : My proposed change in patent is that all provisional and complete applications must be published asap without any extra form or request or charge. So publication fee is added to application fees. To keep costs low,, first application for all applicant can be made half the price or lesser ( for natural persons only). And if there are more than one applicants, and one of them is first applicant and natural person, then also half price benefit may be given
.
(09) given that all publications are now digital, imo, publicant costs are low. In addition, parent register must be blockchained, but thats a separate issue.
.
summary : all provisional and complete applications must be published asap, say within 2-3 days, with or without costs., but without any additional forms or requests. Imo, this is must to reduce problem of "hidden piror art conflicting with novelty in new application". This will need amendment in Bharatiya Patent Laws. And also amendments across world.
