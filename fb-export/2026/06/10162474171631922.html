Say vidhansabha term is 2 years. Then how can Cm have 4 year term, under existing framework of 'MLA elect CM"?
.
One way is - say in 2024 MLA elect CM with over 50% majority. Then that CM will go on till MLA elect new CM with over 50% MLA votes. And CM need not be MLA.
.
So MLA term can be 2 years. And CM term can be 4 years.
