A heartbreaking scene from Uttar Pradesh’s Farrukhabad has left many people emotional and questioning the system meant to support the elderly.

A young man was forced to take his 72-year-old grandmother, who suffers from a fractured hip and is unable to walk, to the bank on a handcart just to collect her pension. Despite her age and condition, the family was reportedly told that the pension could only be released if the account holder appeared in person.

With no other choice, the grandson carefully pushed his grandmother through the scorching heat, holding an umbrella over her to protect her from the sun. The painful journey was not for treatment or an emergency—it was simply to access money that already belonged to her.

The video of the elderly woman sitting helplessly on the handcart quickly went viral, touching hearts across the country and sparking widespread outrage. Many questioned why a frail senior citizen had to endure such hardship just to receive her pension.

Following public criticism, the bank manager reportedly visited the woman’s home and ensured that her pension was delivered.

The incident serves as a powerful reminder that systems created to help the elderly should never make them suffer. Compassion, dignity, and accessibility should come before paperwork. ❤️
