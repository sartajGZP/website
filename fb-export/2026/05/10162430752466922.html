Imp info for all selenium users. ( related to coding software only, not politics , not economics ) .
.
Selenium makes too many folders named 'scoped_dirxxxx_yyyy' in not just one, but 10 places in cdrive or ddrive. So search across cdrive and ddrive on 'scoped_dir*' and delete these folders.
.
I use selenium on 3 machines. Today , i found scoped_dirxxxx_yyyy occupying 25GB , 15GB and 27GB space !!! GB, not MB. I wasted huge huge my manhours because two machines were aws machines and just had 60GB disk.
.
So if you are selenium , pls search all your drives for 'scoped_dir*'.
