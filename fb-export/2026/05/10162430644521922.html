F'd Up Facts:

Give someone a home first—no conditions, no waiting. That's Finland's secret. 🏠✨

Since 2008, Housing First has cut long-term homelessness by over 35% and saved €15,000 per person annually in emergency services. Shelters were replaced with real homes. Addiction and mental health support followed.

The result? Finland is the only EU country where homelessness is falling. Not because they fixed people first—because they gave them a key first. 🔑💙
