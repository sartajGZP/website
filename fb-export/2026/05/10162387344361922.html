A team of researchers ran an experiment. They invented a fake medical condition called "Bixonimania" and seeded it lightly online. Within weeks, large language models including Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini were confidently telling users it was real. Copilot even called it "intriguing and relatively rare." Gemini said it was caused by blue light exposure. The point of the experiment: showing how LLMs hallucinate confident-sounding misinformation. Quick question: have you ever caught an AI making something up that sounded completely real? 🤖
