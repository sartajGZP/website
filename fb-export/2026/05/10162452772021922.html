Optional open vote for any national position N or state position S or district position D
.
I haven't proposed any draft yet. Following is draft i will make sometime in future
.
(01l anyone can register his name for any national state district position by paying deposit equal to MP MLA Corporation election
.
(02) a citizen can register whether he wants open vote or confidential vote. If wants confidential vote, he need not do anything. If he wants open vote for a position, he can register by paying Rs 5. So if he wants open vote for 100 positions, it will be rs 500
.
(03) open vote will be range vote or approval vote and can be changed any day.
.
(04) if a candidate has obtained approval of 25% of ALL voters or obtained score of 1 or more from 25% voters, then in next general elections, a ballot paper for that position will be put.

(05) The ballot paper will have names of all candidates who have approval of over 10% voters or those who pay deposit in each constituency where he wants his name on ballot paper. The name of existing position holder will be automatically on ballot paper.
.
(06) The voters who have registered for open vote cannot vote as confidential voter. The voters who have opted for confidential vote for that position can vote for any of the candidates
.
(07) winner must get more than 51% of votes.
.
(08) if a candidate has obtained open approval of 51% of all voters or has obtained open range score of 1 or more from over 51% voters, then no confidential vote will be held for that position. He will be declared winner for that position
.
(09) PM CM may or need not appoint winner for that position on that position.
