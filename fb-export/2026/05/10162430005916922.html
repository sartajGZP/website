Massive residential complexes like this have become increasingly common in densely populated parts of China, where rapid urban growth has forced cities to build upward instead of outward.

One of the most famous examples is Regent International in Hangzhou, a gigantic apartment building designed to function almost like a self-contained community.

Reports claim that more than 20,000 to 30,000 people may live there depending on occupancy levels. Many residents rarely need to leave the building because nearly everything they need is available inside.

To outsiders, these buildings can seem futuristic or even dystopian, with endless rows of balconies and windows stretching across the skyline.
