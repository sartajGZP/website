Before 2001, it was easy for municipal staff to catch , relocate or kill a stray dog. So did they kill all dogs? No. Only when a dog was reported as aggressive, then only municipal staff or authorized contractors would catch and relocate the dog to remote areas or kill if dog was rabid.
.
So pre-2001 status was sufficient. There was no widespread cruelty against dogs before 2001.
.
ABV and later PM namely MMS Modi used 2001 law to make dog catching relocation or even killing aggressive dogs almost impossible.
