A sentence spoken against a fake like yes_ramdev or ravishankerji or modi or raga or kejrival etc must meet two conditions
.
(1) it should make some sense to his 100 supporters chosen at random from all his crores of supporters
.
(2) it must sound sensible to 100 votets chosen at random from population of 95 crore votets.
.
So sentence like "he doesnt support jury system" doesnt make valid expose-sentence against anyone because neither his supporters nor general population know what jurysys is and they do not have opinion on whether jurysys is good or bad !!!

Also, sentence like "prashant tripathy doesnt support votevapsi" is inapplicable because his supporters, partly rightly, believe that global warming us far more imp issue than votevapsi.

But sentence like "prashant kishore is fake RTR supporter and in reality he opposes RTR" is applicable.
.
So in general, when proving that a person is fake, in one post, write only about one person, or take one specific issue and write about many. But putting many issues and many persons in same post will create too many "inapplicable" sentences.
