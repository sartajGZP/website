Kaler Barta:

Professor Dr. FM Siddiqui, a member of former Prime Minister Khaleda Zia’s personal medical team, has called for all medical records related to her treatment at Bangladesh Medical University to be legally seized and for travel bans to be imposed on relevant individuals if necessary. He said that after Khaleda Zia was admitted to Evercare Hospital on April 27, 2021, due to COVID-19 complications, their board took responsibility for her care and discovered that she was suffering from liver cirrhosis.

Dr. Siddiqui stated, “Methotrexate was the drug that accelerated her fatty liver disease into liver cirrhosis. In this context, it acted as a 'slow poison' on her liver.” He also said that the negligence in her treatment should be considered ‘deliberate negligence’ and warrants investigation.

He demanded a detailed inquiry into three areas: “Who were the members of the government-appointed medical board and on what basis were they responsible for her care? Which doctors were involved in her treatment during hospitalization, and is there evidence of negligence? When Khaleda Zia attempted to include her personal doctors through her lawyer during her treatment at the medical university, why was this prevented and by whom?”#KhaledaZia #BNP#BangladeshPolitics #HealthControversy#MedicalClaim #SlowPoison#Kaler Barta
