When you buy fruits vegetable from ORGANIC food shop, pls ensure bill has word organic before item name.
.
Eg say shop name us Xyz Organic Food. Say bill has this shop name. But item name you bought is listed as 'mango' and not 'organic mango'. And if that mango is NOT organic, then sale is legal !!
.
And you ended up buying non organic mango while you thought it was organic.
.
So pls ensure item name has organic in front of item name in the bill !!!
