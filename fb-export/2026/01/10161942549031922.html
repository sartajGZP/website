Are muslims less likely to become victim in digital arrest frauds, because of better social connectivity?

I studied 10s and 10s of cases of digital arrest frauds. I just noticed one thing - in almost all cases above rs 10 lakhs, except one case, all VICTIMS, were hindus and none except one case were muslim !!! In one case, muslim lady was retired Aligarh Muslim univ Prof aged 75 years.
.
I asked one bank employee who deals exclusively in digital payment fraud cases for past 2 years. Of 100s ( yes 100s ) cases, he didnt came across even just 1 or 2 muslim victims !!!
.
The sample size is not large to draw any valid conclusion
.
But imo community life is playing imp role. Those who have good social community daily weekly meetings with close relatives or close friends are far less likely to become victim of digital arrest nonsense. Elder hindu are living alone and also not having any close friends they depend on. And so when digital arrest hits them, they dont share information on such digital arrest phone with anyone and so they become victim
