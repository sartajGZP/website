Karnataka Times:

China has rolled out a new social media regulation under which only certified influencers can create or share content related to medicine, financial advice, and education. Influencers who are not officially certified or qualified may face penalties, content removal, or legal action. The decision aims to curb misinformation and ensure that sensitive topics are discussed only by verified professionals.
