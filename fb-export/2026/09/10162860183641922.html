Stephen Petro:

One high school in Budapest helped produce John von Neumann, Eugene Wigner, Edward Teller, George Pólya, and other extraordinary thinkers.

One key to its approach was simple: students confronted difficult problems before being shown the method.

They had to struggle, experiment, and begin constructing solutions for themselves.
Don’t always study the answer first.

Attempt the problem before you think you’re ready.
