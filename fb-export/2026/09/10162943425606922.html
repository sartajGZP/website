Why vvpat vidoes are now unreliable
.
Vvpat video showing paper slip not getting cut have zero value. Because

(1) when paper slip was getting cut , the light was dim. Now object in dim light is visible to eyes but can become almost invisible in camera recording. Most camera need more light than human eye. Thats why cameramen carry strong lights. So if paper slip fall is not in video, it may mean that light was insufficient. It doesn't prove that voter didnt see

(2) these video can be AI fakes

(3) vdo may be real , but portion showing paper slip getting cut and falling was edited out

(4) so one's own experience and observation is real. But video have less value now.
