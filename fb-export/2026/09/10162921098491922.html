The coal mining cost as per our data is rs 600 per tonne and coal open market value is over rs 8000 per tonne. Our suggestion to citizens of Bharat is - citizens should take back coal mine from Tata , reauction the coal mine in open market , give it bidder for 3 years , who quotes lowest mining costs.
