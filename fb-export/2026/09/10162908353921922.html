Contrast sensor can be used to know if voter is standing or has walked away.
.
Say votet is standing and say light intensity is 50 lux and say CR is 35

( CR = contrast ratio )
.
Now for same paper , say voter moves away , and say light intensity becomes 200 lux. So for same paper print, light intensity will decrease and may become 20
.
So contrast sensor may be used by vvpat software to know if voter is still standing or has walked away.
