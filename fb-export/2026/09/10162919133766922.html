Dr. Emmett Blahnik:

If you’re over 50, you’ve probably been told to take a statin.

It might be one of the most dangerous medications on the market.

Statins can demyelinate the brain and nerves, increase symptoms of Alzheimer’s and dementia, raise cancer risk dramatically, and actually increase the risk of heart failure, the very thing they’re supposed to help prevent.

They lower cholesterol by pulling it from your brain, nerves, liver, and glands… the exact places your body needs cholesterol to maintain a healthy nervous system.

There’s a lot of downside.
