Swatantra Bhardwaj has claimed in a podcast that he assaulted the father of CJP protester Nishu Azad during the Jantar Mantar protests and that political contacts helped him avoid being jailed. He specifically mentioned BJP leader Kapil Mishra and Chirag Paswan, while also referring to Narendra Modi as an “elder brother.” These are Bhardwaj’s claims and should not be treated as established facts.

Delhi Police has said the June 23 incident involved a head injury caused by a kada and that the medical examination described the injury as minor; police also said further legal action would follow. 0
