A major plus point of QA on FB over QA on voice chats
.
Each one can decide what he likes. But following is one imo HUGE plus point in FB - you can see full profile of question asker and decide whether that person is just a question asker passivist or actually an activist.
.

.
So QA on FB saves time imo.
