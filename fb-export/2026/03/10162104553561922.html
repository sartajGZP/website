Result of Marx's focusing on factories and removing focus away from land, minerals - it protected mineral oligopoly of his relatives
.
Mahatma Thomas Paine wrote Agrarian Jutsice booklet in 1797. The main theme was - plots / mines should be rented and all rents should be divided equally amongst all citizens. I label this proposal as Thomaism, though as Mahatma Thomas himself had said that this thought is 1000s of years old. Till 1870, this thought remained main topic amongst poorists.
.
Thomasism would have caused serious damage to land and mineral oligopolists. Karl Marx relatives were almost all big time land bank mineral owners
.
Karl Marx in 1860s onwards said that focus should be put on nationalization of factories and factory equipments. This effectively removed focus away from distribution of land mineral profits.
.
The Thomasism got blacked out and 'nationalize factories' ie marxism got huge funding needed for promotion.
