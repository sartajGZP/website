A trivial solution to reduce fake currency note problem was available by year 2005 - currency serial number scanners.
.
So each branch can be given serial number scanners. So when a note depositor comes, machine will scan serial numbers and send data to central server immediately or after some time.
.
A fake note will either use existing real serial number or a serial number which has never been issued. In either case, system will flag.
.
And then manual investigation can start
.
So many fake notes will get detected at early stage. And manual policing , using public narcotests if person has large number of fake notes, can lead to person who is making fake notes or person who is bringing fake notes inside country
