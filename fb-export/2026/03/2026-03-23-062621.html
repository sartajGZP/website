Read Message to Young Political Workers (1931)
http://www.shahidbhagatsingh.org/index.asp?link=political_workers

Bhagat Singh explained the true meaning of revolution and political struggle, warning that India’s freedom movement could end in compromise and that political independence alone is not enough. He emphasized that real revolution requires organized and systematic effort, not emotional or spontaneous actions. He highlighted that the true strength of the nation lies in workers and peasants, urging young people to work among them, educate them, and organize them, as no revolution can succeed without mass support. He also made it clear that merely replacing British rulers with Indian elites does not bring real freedom; instead, the goal must be a complete transformation of society, ending exploitation and establishing a just and equal system based on socialist ideals.
