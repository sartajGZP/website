Gun ownership in Iceland is through the roof, yet gun deaths are almost nonexistent.

With about 32 guns per 100 people, Iceland ranks among the highest worldwide in gun ownership. Most firearms are hunting rifles or shotguns, deeply tied to tradition and sport. Semi-automatic rifles are banned, and handguns are rare.

Strict laws require owners to be 20+, pass medical checks, have clean records, and complete training courses. Over 25 years, only five people have died from shootings—a stunning contrast to other nations with similar gun rates.

Iceland proves that responsible ownership and strong regulations can coexist.
