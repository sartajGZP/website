Indian Startup News:

Scientists at the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research’s National Institute for Interdisciplinary Science and Technology (CSIR-NIIST) have developed a new variety of high-protein “designer rice” aimed at tackling both diabetes risk and malnutrition in India.

Rice, a staple for millions in the country, is largely composed of starch and has low protein and fibre content, often leading to rapid spikes in blood sugar levels. At the same time, many rice-based diets contribute to “hidden hunger,” where people consume enough calories but lack essential nutrients.

The newly developed rice contains more than 20% protein, compared with about 6–8% in conventional rice, and has a low glycaemic index below 55, enabling slower energy release and better blood sugar control. The grains are also fortified with micronutrients such as iron, folic acid and vitamin B12 to address deficiencies like anaemia.

Researchers said the innovation could significantly improve the nutritional value of a widely consumed food while supporting diabetes management.

To enable commercial rollout, CSIR-NIIST has partnered with Tata Consumer Products, which will take the technology forward for large-scale production and market distribution.
