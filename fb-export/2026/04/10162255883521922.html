In usa, on most streets, say a car is going on street and crossroad traffic light is red. Then before turning passanger side ie right, the car has to fully stop. Full stop. And then make passanger side turn ie right in usa. ( usa has drive right as opposed to drive left in bharat ).
.
And all traffic light in city in usa are no turn on red.
.
So either there is no turn on red or full stop before turning on red.
.
In Bharat, say a car is going on street and crossroad traffic light is red. Then before turning passanger side ie left, the car is NOT legally required to stop fully !!! Its only required to slow down. And without full stop, the car can legally make passanger side turn ( ie left in bharat) on red
.
And almost all traffic joints in bharat even in crowded city allow free turn on red !! There is NO "no turn on red" !!!
.
Will making law in bharat that car must stop on red before turning left reduce fuel consumption? Imo yes. How?
.
In any case, i propose law that car must stop before turning right. And i propose to make all crowded joints no turn on red.
