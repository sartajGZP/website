Fact Point:

Tennessee has made history by becoming the first US state to require gun safety courses in all public schools from kindergarten through twelfth grade. The law was passed in 2025 and will take effect during the 2025 to 2026 school year, making firearm safety education mandatory for all students regardless of age. The curriculum focuses on teaching students how to distinguish toy guns from real ones, the importance of safe storage and what to do if they encounter a firearm. Importantly the program will not involve live ammunition or shooting practice as it is purely focused on awareness and responsible behavior. Schools are permitted to partner with law enforcement and community organizations to deliver the lessons in a practical and credible way.
