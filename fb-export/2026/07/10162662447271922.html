A simple vending machine in Staten Island is now part of New York City's public health system. 💙 On June 29, 2026, city officials opened new health vending machines in Port Richmond, offering New Yorkers free access to wellness supplies and life saving overdose reversal medication, 24 hours a day. According to the NYC Health Department, residents can access the machines by entering a New York City zip code.

This effort builds on a citywide program that began in Brooklyn and Queens in 2023. Officials report those machines have been used more than 70000 times and have distributed thousands of naloxone kits, a medication that can reverse opioid overdoses. Health officials also note a meaningful decline in overdose deaths on Staten Island since 2023. 🩺 Public health experts say small tools like this can make a real difference. Could simple access like this help save lives in more communities?
