.

.
In year 2026 , about 2.2 crore people crossed age of 17 years. No of MBBS seats = 167000. About 1 in say 160 !!
.
And more than 50% are self financed
.
Same way , engineering seats increased by several times.
.
And as UP Bihar add more self financed medical engineering college , numbers will increase.
.
So upper class and middle class gets mbbs engineering admission by merit or money. So they dont spend time in anti reservation
.
