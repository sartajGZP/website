Press Note 3 (2020) is a critical Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) regulation in India that mandates prior government approval for all investments originating from countries that share a land border with India. Originally introduced to prevent opportunistic acquisitions of Indian companies during the pandemic, the policy was significantly amended in March 2026. [1, 2, 3]
Key Regulations

• Prior Government Approval: Any direct or indirect investment from land-bordering countries (China, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Bhutan, Nepal, Myanmar, and Afghanistan) must go through the Government Route, bypassing the Automatic Route.
• Transfer of Ownership: Any transfer of ownership of an Indian entity that results in beneficial ownership shifting to a land-bordering country also requires prior government approval. [1]

2026 Policy Amendments
In March 2026, the Union Cabinet introduced a calibrated relaxation to the strict rules to encourage capital flows into specific manufacturing sectors:

• 10% Safe Harbor: Investors from land-bordering countries with non-controlling beneficial ownership of up to 10% can invest through the automatic route, subject to sectoral caps.
• Fast-Track Timelines: Investment proposals in specific focus sectors (manufacturing in capital goods, electronic components, polysilicon, etc.) must be processed and decided within 60 days.
• Control Clause: These relaxations only apply to Indian companies where majority shareholding and control remain with resident Indian citizens. [2, 4]

For the complete official policy text and notifications, refer to the DPIIT FDI Policy Circulars .
