The Vyom India :

A video circulating on social media appears to show a police officer warning two young men to leave the area and go straight home. During the exchange, the officer allegedly says he could falsely implicate them by planting 50 grams of an illicit substance, claiming it would lead to a non-bailable case.

The two young men repeatedly apologize and assure the officer they will head home immediately. The clip has triggered widespread discussion online, with many questioning the officer's remarks. The authenticity, context, and location of the video have not been independently verified.
