General knowledge:

If you buy or sell items on Craigslist or Facebook Marketplace, meeting a stranger can be risky. That's why many police departments across the U.S. have created safe exchange zones. These are usually in the police station parking lot, lobby, or a special area that is well-lit and monitored by video surveillance 24/7.

Police generally won't watch the actual exchange, but the location and cameras can scare off anyone with bad intentions. These zones are free and open to the public, with some departments even allowing their use for child custody exchanges. If the person you're dealing with refuses to meet you there, it's a big "red flag".
