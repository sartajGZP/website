QA on proposed modifications to drafts : if a person is proposing a modification to draft, as far as possible, do QA on FB and not voice calls or voice meetings.

step-1 : create a group to discuss draft modification

step-2 : person who is proposing modification has to first cite on twitter fb whether he supports existing draft without modification or opposes existing draft without his modification

step-3 : ask him to post his proposed modification in post in FB in that group

Then question : how much damage draft without modification make on date one? DAY ONE . ie why should modification be made now and not after draft is passed? All drafts have CV clauses using which modification can be made after law is passed. So one who is peoposed modification needs to answer first - Why is it necessary to make modification NOW and not later?
.
Without reading answers to above, immediately answering on proposed modification will consume too much time.
