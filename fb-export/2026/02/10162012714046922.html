Wealth Inheritance tax vs Annual wealth tax
.
Mahatma Thomas Paine proposed in year 1797 in the most imp work in pre-1954 history 'Agrarian Justice' that --- '10% of inheritance tax should be levied on land at the time of death of owner, and amount collected should be divided equally amongst all citizens'.
.
He proposed inheritance tax and not annual wealth tax. He never wrote against Annual wealth tax and never supported it. He himself mentioned in 1797 article that due to ill health, he is unable to think out all details
.
When one writes down detailed procedure to collect taxes, one may see that inheritance tax is too easy to evade.
.
Eg1. A person can gift away his wealth before death. So at the time of death, he has near zero wealth. So inheritance tax will be zero. So now gift tax has to be implemented. So tax law becomes clumsy. Annual wealth tax doesnt have this problem.
.
Eg2. A person can give an amount to his heirs every year as gift. So a big portion of wealth will get inherited and avoid inheritance tax
.
Eg3. Before death, person can buy goods at overpriced invoices from heir and so wealth passed as income. So again, inheritance got implemented using income. So income tax laws are needed to plug this loophole
.
Eg4. A person can transfer wealth in company or trust or foreign company and then only transfer shares or seat of that trust or company to heirs. Now keeping track of land and shares owned trusts and companies and foreign companies is a huge problem.
.
So imo, inheritance tax is easy to avoid or evade using gifts to hiers and by buying overpriced goods from heirs or using trusts and companies.
.
But Annual wealth tax doesnt have these problems. Who so ever owns lands, pays tax every month or year.
.
So I am proposing Annual wealth tax on land and small tax on construction. And not inheritance tax.
.
As per distribution, in my proposal land rent on govt plots is redistribute equally amongst all citizens, and tax on private land is NOT distributed, but used only for courts, police, military. Thats a separate point.
