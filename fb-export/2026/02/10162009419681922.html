Over 80% of Epstien files are redacted.

C-SPAN:

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) on Tuesday read on the House floor the names of six “wealthy, powerful men” whose identities were originally redacted in the Epstein files released to the public.
 
The names include Leslie Wexner, the billionaire former owner of Victoria’s Secret, and Sultan Ahmed bin Sulayem, the CEO of Dubai-based logistics company DP World. The others were Salvatore Nuara, Zurab Mikeladze, Leonid Leonov and Nicola Caputo.
 
The move came after he and Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY) viewed unredacted files at the Justice Department and found that the identities of at least six individuals had been shielded in violation of a federal law mandating their public release.
 
The Justice Department unredacted the names after the issue was raised by the congressmen who led the legislative effort to force the department to publicly release the files.
 
“Why did it take Thomas Massie and me going to the Justice Department to get these six men’s identities to become public?” Rep. Khanna asked. “And if we found six men that they were hiding in two hours, imagine how many men they are covering up for in those 3 million files.”
