A cautionary tale shared by Chartered Accountant Meenal Goel on February 16, 2026, has highlighted the harsh reality of home ownership in India. Her neighbor, a Bengaluru professional who lost his job in October, saw his ₹1.2 crore flat auctioned by the bank after he missed just three consecutive EMIs. Under the SARFAESI Act, which allows banks to recover dues without a court order, the property was classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) and sold for a "distress price" of ₹95 lakh—well below its market value. After the bank recovered its outstanding ₹80 lakh loan, the resident was left with a mere ₹15 lakh, effectively losing eight years of hard-earned equity and his home in just 60 days. The story has sparked a massive debate on social media about the importance of emergency funds and the "illusion of ownership" until the very last loan installment is paid.
