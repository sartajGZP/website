F'd Up Facts:

Elvis Presley had a famously shrewd manager, Colonel Tom Parker, who reportedly sold “I Hate Elvis” badges at the height of Elvis mania. The logic was simple: if someone wouldn’t buy pro-Elvis merchandise, they might still pay to show their dislike. In the end, even critics became customers—turning backlash into business.
