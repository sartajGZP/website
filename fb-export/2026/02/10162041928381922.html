Imo, all poorists must understand difference between marx and mahatma thomas paine
.
Central main proposal of Marx 1870 ad is : society should confiscate all machines andball equipment and make it public property, except may be personal hand held tools or something that 1 or 2 humans may carry or keep with them. So home or office PC can be private. But data center which has 1000s of PCs should be public. This would also apply on software. So all software except say software inside one's own PC should be public property only. One may have car or tractor, but tractor making factiry must be public. Also, incomes should not be distributed, but govt shall decide what is public welfare and spend on public welfare.
.
Mahatma Thomas Paine 1797 ad said, 70 years before marx : all nature and only nature should be public. So if iron coal etc is found, it should be mined and profits should be equally distributed in all citizens. But afterwards, if anyone buys iron etc, makes steel and from steel makes a car manufacturing factory, then that factory as per Paine, will remain private.
.
Oligarchs say that all mineral etc should belong to a few and they alone should keep all profits and may spend on charity.
.
I am Paineist. And most antimarx are prp-oligarchy and not paineist.
