A disturbing investigation has revealed claims that wealthy tourists paid up to $91,000 to shoot innocent civilians during the Siege of Sarajevo, turning parts of the city into what witnesses called a “human safari.”⁠
⁠
The killings allegedly took place along the stretch later nicknamed Sniper Alley, where Bosnian Serb forces targeted anyone who tried to cross.⁠
⁠
Italian authorities are now working with Bosnian officials to identify those responsible nearly three decades later.
