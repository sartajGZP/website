Imo, all (useless) abstract concepts were created only to supress Agrarian Justice like thoughts which may have come as early as 10000 bc
.
About 10000 bc onwards, land around ponds and rivers became precious. Say all that land was private. And there are villages or small towns with head, police, tax officers, road makers etc.
.
Now say river chanhes course. This is important point. Say river changes course.
.
Then all existing land loses value new land near new river banks become valuable.
.
Imo, there must have been many Mahatma like Mahatma Thomas Paine, who would have proposed that new valuable land, which was earlier zero value, should not be sold permanently but should be rented only and annual rent must be divided amongst all citizens equally.
.
And 100s of (useless) abstract philosophical spiritual economic sociological concepts were created, mainly to fill the conversation space so that this Paineist proposal doesnt get time in public conversation. And over time, all land must be private and no rent from land should go to citizen thought, was put as some eternal truth
.
In around year 1997, I framed this pond question. The land question is - every year , rain gives 1 crore liter of water to a pond and some 1000 people live near pond. So who should get how much water. My answer is - each should get 10000 liters a year which he may use or sell. ( i came to know about Agrarian Justice after 1997 ). And same for coal, crude oil etc. Later I noticed, that all apex intellectuals work day and night to evade this 'distribution of income from nature' concept.
.
As far as I know, no well known respected book, be religious book or social book or philosophical book says this Paineist proposal. Except Agrain Justice 1797 and a few related books like Progress and Poverty 1870 by Mahatma Henry George.
.
So imo, The Paineism is main proposal. And now i dismiss all other economic proposals not related Paineism as alternate proposals meant only to reduce information flow.
