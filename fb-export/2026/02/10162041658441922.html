Jawaharlal Saiyyed Khan and Saradar Patel, using bharatiya soldiers took back lands from small farmers, and gave it back to Nizam and his landlords. That land was taken from Nizam and his landlords by poorists marxists between 01-jun-1946 and 15-sep-1948 and given to small farmers
.
Poorists marxists started armed rebellion against Nizam and his landlords in hyderabad state on around 01-jun-1946. Between 01-jun-1946 and 15-sep-1948, about 20% to 35% land area of Hyderabd state was under poorists marxists. On 01-sep-1948, they were only 30 km away from hyderabad city border and moving closer. In about 35% area, poorists marxists had expelled all Nizam landlords soldiers policemen and officers. The poorists marxists were running police stations, courts and collecting taxes.
.
They had expelled all landlords and distributed lands amongst small farmers
.
Between 16-sep-1948 and 31-dec-1952, second PM jawahar Sayyid khan and Sardara Vallabhbhai Patel, using Bharatiya soldiers, took back almost all those lands and gave it back to Nizam and his landlords. Many poorists, marxists freedom fighters were killed in the process.
