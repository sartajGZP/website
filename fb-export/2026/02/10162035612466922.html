In Bangladesh , vote couting starts right after polling ends, and counting is done in same counting station as polling station. There is no movement of ballot boxes - except for may be one room to another in same building
.
There are about 42777 polling stations in Bangladesh. A polling station may have 1 to 10 booths. And there are 42777 counting stations. There is 1 polling station per 3000 voters
