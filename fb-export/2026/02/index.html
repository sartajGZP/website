Today marks the 144th birth anniversary of Mahatma Vijay Singh Pathik ji🙏
To my knowledge, they were

1) not only among the greatest revolutionaries of India but perhaps the first in the world to truly understand the importance of "procedure" (drafting) in governance & systematically documented & published it in their book “Chunavi Paddhati evam Jansatta” (Electoral System and People’s Rule), written in 1923.

To write this book, Pathik ji studied books worth ₹300 at that time (equivalent to nearly ₹24,00,000 in 2026). In that era, Indian books were available for ₹1, and international books ranged between ₹5 to ₹15. By that calculation, one can estimate that Mahatma Pathik ji must have studied such a humongous number of books just to write Chunavi Paddhati evam Jansatta.

24 years before India attained independence, in 1923 itself, he had already described the very processes on which governance ought to function. From referendums to the recall of judges, officials, and politicias, to the system of preferential voting—he documented every practical aspect of these mechanisms. He also exposed how such processes became the foundation of development and good governance in Switzerland, the United States, Australia, and other European nations.

During his lifetime, he authored more than 30 books.

The greatest curse of political streams is the endless “analysis of ideas and ideologies.” For centuries, sponsors of paid media have deliberately kept serious political activists across the world entangled in abstract debates over ideas and doctrines—while pushing activists into hollow slogans and superficial statements. After 1916, Mahatma Vijay Singh Pathik ji also successfully led the Bijolia Peasant Movement to its decisive phase—recognized as the world’s first successful non-violent Satyagraha movement.

2) Alongside his intellectual depth, he also successfully executed a powerful revolutionary movement. In the second major armed revolutionary attempt for India’s independence in 1915, led by Mahatma Rash Behari Bose ji, Rash Behari ji entrusted Mahatma Vijay Singh Pathik ji with the command of Rajasthan and Central India.

One does not ordinarily expect a field-oriented personality—someone capable of mobilizing, energizing, and electrifying the masses, of raising adrenaline and igniting movements—to descend into such rigorous, painstaking, and seemingly depths of procedural details of governance. It is the meeting of opposite poles North & South.

Mahatma Vijay Singh Pathik ji were a rarest of rare convergence of this both
