In most states of the USA, the public has the right to elect and recall police chiefs and judges. There, citizens sit with judges to deliver verdicts. This is why corruption is very low in America. We are also working to bring the right to recall for the people of India regarding SPs, Judges, MPs, and MLAs, as well as introduce the jury system.
