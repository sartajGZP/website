# Right to Recall Party 
Right to recall party was founded by Rahul chimanbhai Mehta and Pawan Juri Ji. 

## Goals of Right to Recall Party 
In most states of the USA, the public has the right to elect and recall police chiefs and judges. There, citizens sit with judges to deliver verdicts. This is why corruption is very low in America.
We are working to bring the law of right to recall SP, Right to Recall Judge and bring Jury Court in india.
Please take a look at law drafts of [Jury Court](/drafts/en/jury-court) as words can not convey the full meaning of these proposals
